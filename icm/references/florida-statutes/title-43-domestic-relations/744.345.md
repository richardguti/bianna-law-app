# Fla. Stat. § 744.345 — Letters of guardianship.F.S. 744.3426744.342

**Title:** XLIII — DOMESTIC RELATIONS
**Chapter:** 744 — GUARDIANSHIP
**Part:** V — ADJUDICATION OF INCAPACITY AND APPOINTMENT OF GUARDIANS
**Section:** 744.345
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Upon petition, the court may appoint a guardian for a minor without appointing an examining committee or conducting an adjudicatory hearing pursuant to s. 744.331.

**History:** s. 71, ch. 90-271.
