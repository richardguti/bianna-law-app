# Fla. Stat. § 744.653 — Annual guardianship report.

**Title:** XLIII — DOMESTIC RELATIONS
**Chapter:** 747 — CONSERVATORSHIP
**Part:** VIII — VETERANS’ GUARDIANSHIP
**Section:** 744.653
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Guardians appointed under the Veterans' Guardianship Law shall not be required to comply with the provisions of s. 744.367.

**History:** s. 6, ch. 91-306.
