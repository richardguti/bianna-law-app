# Fla. Stat. § 742.06 — Jurisdiction retained for future orders.

**Title:** XLIII — DOMESTIC RELATIONS
**Chapter:** 742 — DETERMINATION OF PARENTAGE
**Part:** XV — COMMUNITY PROPERTY TRUST ACT
**Section:** 742.06
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The court shall retain jurisdiction of the cause for the purpose of entering such other and further orders as changing circumstances of the parties may in justice and equity require. Modifications and enforcement of child support, time-sharing, and support for a dependent adult child are determined under chapter 61.

**History:** s. 5, ch. 26949, 1951; s. 8, ch. 2023-213.
