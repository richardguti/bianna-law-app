# Fla. Stat. § 741.327 — Certification and monitoring of batterers' intervention programs; rules.

**Title:** XLIII — DOMESTIC RELATIONS
**Chapter:** 741 — MARRIAGE; DOMESTIC VIOLENCE
**Part:** XV — COMMUNITY PROPERTY TRUST ACT
**Section:** 741.327
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
Pursuant to s. 741.32, the Department of Children and Families shall certify and monitor batterers' intervention programs.

(2)  
The department shall adopt by rule procedures to administer this section, including, but not limited to, procedures related to the development of criteria for the approval, suspension, or rejection of certification of batterers' intervention programs.

**History:** s. 38, ch. 96-312; s. 6, ch. 98-388; s. 5, ch. 2001-183; s. 14, ch. 2012-147; s. 5, ch. 2021-152.
