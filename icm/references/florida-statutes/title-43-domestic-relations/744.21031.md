# Fla. Stat. § 744.21031 — Public records exemption.

**Title:** XLIII — DOMESTIC RELATIONS
**Chapter:** 744 — GUARDIANSHIP
**Part:** II — PUBLIC AND PROFESSIONAL GUARDIANS
**Section:** 744.21031
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

——

(1)  
For purposes of this section, the term:
(a)  
“Agency” has the same meaning as provided in s. 119.011.

(b)  
“Employee with fiduciary responsibility” means an employee of a public guardian who has the ability to direct any transactions of a ward's funds, assets, or property; who, under the supervision of a guardian, manages the care of a ward; or who makes any health care decision as defined in s. 765.101 on behalf of a ward.

(c)  
“Telephone numbers” has the same meaning as provided in s. 119.071(4)(d)1.c.
