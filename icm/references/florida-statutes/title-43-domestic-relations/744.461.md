# Fla. Stat. § 744.461 — Purchasers and lenders protected.

**Title:** XLIII — DOMESTIC RELATIONS
**Chapter:** 744 — GUARDIANSHIP
**Part:** VI — POWERS AND DUTIES
**Section:** 744.461
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

No person purchasing or leasing from, or taking a mortgage, pledge, or other lien from, a guardian shall be bound to see that the money or other things of value paid to the guardian are actually needed or properly applied. The person is not otherwise bound as to the proprieties or expediencies of the acts of the guardian.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 74-106; ss. 24, 26, ch. 75-222; s. 80, ch. 89-96.

**Note:** Nothing in this section shall prohibit the court in its discretion from appointing a sole guardian to serve as guardian for both spouses.
