# Fla. Stat. § 744.109 — Records.

**Title:** XLIII — DOMESTIC RELATIONS
**Chapter:** 744 — GUARDIANSHIP
**Part:** I — GENERAL PROVISIONS
**Section:** 744.109
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
All hearings on appointment of a guardian; adjudication of incapacity; modification, termination, or revocation of the adjudication of incapacity; or restoration of capacity must be electronically or stenographically recorded.

(2)  
If an appeal is taken from any of these proceedings, a transcript must be furnished to an indigent ward at public expense.

**History:** ss. 18, 26, ch. 75-222; s. 11, ch. 89-96; s. 5, ch. 90-271; s. 2, ch. 96-354; s. 7, ch. 2003-57; s. 4, ch. 2015-83.
