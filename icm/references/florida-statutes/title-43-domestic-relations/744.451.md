# Fla. Stat. § 744.451 — Order.

**Title:** XLIII — DOMESTIC RELATIONS
**Chapter:** 744 — GUARDIANSHIP
**Part:** VI — POWERS AND DUTIES
**Section:** 744.451
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
If a sale or mortgage is authorized, the order shall describe the property, and
(a)  
If the property is authorized for sale at private sale, the order shall fix the price and the terms of sale.

(b)  
If the sale is to be public, the order shall state that the sale shall be made to the highest bidder and the court reserves the right to reject all bids.

(2)  
An order for any other act permitted under s. 744.441 or s. 744.446 shall describe the permitted act and authorize the guardian to perform it.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 74-106; ss. 24, 26, ch. 75-222; s. 77, ch. 89-96.

**Note:** Created from former s. 745.09.
