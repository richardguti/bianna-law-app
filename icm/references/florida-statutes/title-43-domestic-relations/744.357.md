# Fla. Stat. § 744.357 — Liability of surety.

**Title:** XLIII — DOMESTIC RELATIONS
**Chapter:** 744 — GUARDIANSHIP
**Part:** V — ADJUDICATION OF INCAPACITY AND APPOINTMENT OF GUARDIANS
**Section:** 744.357
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

No surety for a guardian shall be charged beyond the property of the ward.
