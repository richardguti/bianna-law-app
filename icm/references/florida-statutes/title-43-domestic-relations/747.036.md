# Fla. Stat. § 747.036 — Resignation or removal of conservator.

**Title:** XLIII — DOMESTIC RELATIONS
**Chapter:** 747 — CONSERVATORSHIP
**Part:** VIII — VETERANS’ GUARDIANSHIP
**Section:** 747.036
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The provision for resignation and removal of a guardian of the property in [1]chapter 746 shall apply in the circuit court to resignation and removal of a conservator.

**History:** s. 11, ch. 71-103.

**Note:** [1]Note.
—
All sections in chapter 746 were repealed by s. 3, ch. 74-106.
