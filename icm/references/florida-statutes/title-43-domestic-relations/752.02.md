# Fla. Stat. § 752.02 — Persons who must be served notice of petition; manner of service.

**Title:** XLIII — DOMESTIC RELATIONS
**Chapter:** 753 — SUPERVISED VISITATION
**Part:** VIII — VETERANS’ GUARDIANSHIP
**Section:** 752.02
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Notice of the filing of, and a copy of, the petition for grandparental visitation rights shall be served on the parents of the minor child in the manner prescribed by chapter 48.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 84-64; s. 64, ch. 85-62.
