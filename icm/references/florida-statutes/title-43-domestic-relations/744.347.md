# Fla. Stat. § 744.347 — Oath of guardian.

**Title:** XLIII — DOMESTIC RELATIONS
**Chapter:** 744 — GUARDIANSHIP
**Part:** V — ADJUDICATION OF INCAPACITY AND APPOINTMENT OF GUARDIANS
**Section:** 744.347
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Before exercising his or her authority as guardian, every guardian shall take an oath that he or she will faithfully perform his or her duties as guardian. This oath is not jurisdictional.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 74-106; ss. 19, 26, ch. 75-222; s. 40, ch. 89-96; s. 1082, ch. 97-102.

**Note:** Created from former s. 744.36.
