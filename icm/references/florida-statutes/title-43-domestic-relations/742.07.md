# Fla. Stat. § 742.07 — Effect of adoption.

**Title:** XLIII — DOMESTIC RELATIONS
**Chapter:** 742 — DETERMINATION OF PARENTAGE
**Part:** XV — COMMUNITY PROPERTY TRUST ACT
**Section:** 742.07
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Upon the adoption of a child, for whom support has been ordered, by some person other than the father, the liability of the father for the support of the child shall be terminated.

**History:** s. 5, ch. 26949, 1951; s. 8, ch. 2023-213.
