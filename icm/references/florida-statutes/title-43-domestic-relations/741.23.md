# Fla. Stat. § 741.23 — Husband not liable for wife's torts.

**Title:** XLIII — DOMESTIC RELATIONS
**Chapter:** 741 — MARRIAGE; DOMESTIC VIOLENCE
**Part:** XV — COMMUNITY PROPERTY TRUST ACT
**Section:** 741.23
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The common-law rule whereby a husband is liable for the torts of his wife is hereby abrogated.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 26829, 1951.
