# Fla. Stat. § 744.105 — Costs.

**Title:** XLIII — DOMESTIC RELATIONS
**Chapter:** 744 — GUARDIANSHIP
**Part:** I — GENERAL PROVISIONS
**Section:** 744.105
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

In all guardianship proceedings, costs may be awarded. When the costs are to be paid out of the property of the ward, the court may direct from what part of the property the costs shall be paid.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 74-106; s. 8, ch. 89-96; s. 3, ch. 90-271.

**Note:** F.S. 744.1046744.104  
Verification of documents.
—

When verification of a document is required in this chapter or by rule, the document filed shall include an oath or affirmation or the following statement: “Under penalties of perjury, I declare that I have read the foregoing, and the facts alleged are true to the best of my knowledge and belief.” Any person who shall willfully include a false statement in the document shall be guilty of perjury and upon conviction shall be punished accordingly.
