# Fla. Stat. § 765.112 — Recognition of advance directive executed in another state.

**Title:** XLIV — CIVIL RIGHTS
**Chapter:** 765 — HEALTH CARE ADVANCE DIRECTIVES
**Part:** I — GENERAL PROVISIONS
**Section:** 765.112
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

An advance directive executed in another state in compliance with the law of that state or of this state is validly executed for the purposes of this chapter.

**History:** s. 2, ch. 92-199.
