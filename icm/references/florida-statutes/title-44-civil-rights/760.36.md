# Fla. Stat. § 760.36 — Conciliation agreements.

**Title:** XLIV — CIVIL RIGHTS
**Chapter:** 760 — DISCRIMINATION IN THE TREATMENT OF PERSONS; MINORITY REPRESENTATION
**Part:** II — FAIR HOUSING ACT
**Section:** 760.36
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

——

Any conciliation agreement arising out of conciliation efforts by the Florida Commission on Human Relations pursuant to the Fair Housing Act must be agreed to by the respondent and the complainant and is subject to the approval of the commission.
