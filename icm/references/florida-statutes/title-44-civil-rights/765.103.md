# Fla. Stat. § 765.103 — Existing advance directives.

**Title:** XLIV — CIVIL RIGHTS
**Chapter:** 765 — HEALTH CARE ADVANCE DIRECTIVES
**Part:** I — GENERAL PROVISIONS
**Section:** 765.103
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Any advance directive made prior to October 1, 1999, shall be given effect as executed, provided such directive was legally effective when written.

**History:** s. 2, ch. 92or Health Care Administration, and the Department of Health shall jointly create a campaign on end-of-life care for purposes of educating the public. This campaign should include culturally sensitive programs to improve understanding of end-of-life care issues in minority communities.
