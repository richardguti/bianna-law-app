# Fla. Stat. § 765.513 — Donees; purposes for which anatomical gifts may be made.

**Title:** XLIV — CIVIL RIGHTS
**Chapter:** 765 — HEALTH CARE ADVANCE DIRECTIVES
**Part:** V — ANATOMICAL GIFTS
**Section:** 765.513
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

——

(1)  
The following persons or entities may become donees of anatomical gifts of bodies or parts of them for the purposes stated:
(a)  
Any procurement organization or accredited medical or dental school, college, or university for education, research, therapy, or transplantation.

(b)  
2009-218.

**Note:** Created from former s. 736.23; s. 732.912.
