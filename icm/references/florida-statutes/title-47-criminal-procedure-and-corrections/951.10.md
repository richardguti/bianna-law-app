# Fla. Stat. § 951.10 — Leasing prisoners to work for private interests prohibited.

**Title:** XLVII — CRIMINAL PROCEDURE AND CORRECTIONS
**Chapter:** 951 — COUNTY AND MUNICIPAL PRISONERS
**Part:** II — LEASED OR MANAGED WORK PROGRAMS
**Section:** 951.10
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

County prisoners may not be leased to work for any private interests. This section does not prohibit county inmates from working in nonprofit and private sector jobs pursuant to s. 951.24(2) and consistent with federal law.

**History:** s. 3, ch. 9203, 1923; CGL 8551; s. 1, ch. 61-488; s. 17, ch. 2001-110.
