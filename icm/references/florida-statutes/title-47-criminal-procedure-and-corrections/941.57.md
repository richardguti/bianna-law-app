# Fla. Stat. § 941.57 — Powers.

**Title:** XLVII — CRIMINAL PROCEDURE AND CORRECTIONS
**Chapter:** 942 — INTERSTATE EXTRADITION OF WITNESSES
**Part:** III — INTERSTATE CORRECTIONS COMPACT
**Section:** 941.57
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The Department of Corrections is hereby authorized and directed to do all things necessary or incidental to the carrying out of the compact in every particular.

**History:** s. 3, ch. 73-288; s. 20, ch. 77-120; s. 33, ch. 79-3.
