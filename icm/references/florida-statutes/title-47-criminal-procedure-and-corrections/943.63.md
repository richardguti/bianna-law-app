# Fla. Stat. § 943.63 — Arrest by the Capitol Police.

**Title:** XLVII — CRIMINAL PROCEDURE AND CORRECTIONS
**Chapter:** 943 — DEPARTMENT OF LAW ENFORCEMENT
**Part:** III — INTERSTATE CORRECTIONS COMPACT
**Section:** 943.63
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

A person arrested by an officer of the Capitol Police shall be delivered to the sheriff of the county in which the arrest takes place unless a notice to appear is issued.

**History:** s. 16, ch. 2002-21; s. 3, ch. 2005-120.
