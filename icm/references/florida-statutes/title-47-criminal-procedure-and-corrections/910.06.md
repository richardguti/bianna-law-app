# Fla. Stat. § 910.06 — Where person in one county commits offense in another.

**Title:** XLVII — CRIMINAL PROCEDURE AND CORRECTIONS
**Chapter:** 910 — JURISDICTION AND VENUE
**Part:** IV — CREDIT COUNSELING SERVICES
**Section:** 910.06
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

If a person in one county commits an offense in another county, the offender may be tried in either county.

**History:** s. 165, ch. 19554, 1939; CGL 1940 Supp. 8663(172); s. 78, ch. 70-339.
