# Fla. Stat. § 905.02 — Who may challenge.

**Title:** XLVII — CRIMINAL PROCEDURE AND CORRECTIONS
**Chapter:** 905 — GRAND JURY
**Part:** IV — CREDIT COUNSELING SERVICES
**Section:** 905.02
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The state or a person who has been held to answer may challenge the panel or individual grand jurors.

**History:** s. 81, ch. 19554, 1939; CGL 1940 Supp. 8663(81); s. 42, ch. 70-339.
