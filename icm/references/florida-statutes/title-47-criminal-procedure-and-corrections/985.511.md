# Fla. Stat. § 985.511 — Costs of representation.

**Title:** XLVII — CRIMINAL PROCEDURE AND CORRECTIONS
**Chapter:** 985 — JUVENILE JUSTICE; INTERSTATE COMPACT ON JUVENILES
**Part:** VII — DISPOSITION; POSTDISPOSITION
**Section:** 985.511
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The responsibilities of the parents or legal guardian of the child to pay costs associated with the representation of the child are prescribed under s. 985.033.

**History:** s. 63, ch. 2006-120.
