# Fla. Stat. § 905.03 — Ground for challenge to panel.

**Title:** XLVII — CRIMINAL PROCEDURE AND CORRECTIONS
**Chapter:** 905 — GRAND JURY
**Part:** IV — CREDIT COUNSELING SERVICES
**Section:** 905.03
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

A challenge to the panel may be made only on the ground that the grand jurors were not selected according to law.

**History:** s. 82, ch. 19554, 1939; CGL 1940 Supp. 8663(82); s. 42, ch. 70-339.
