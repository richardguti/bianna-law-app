# Fla. Stat. § 944.596 — Transfer of convicted foreign citizens or nationals under treaty.

**Title:** XLVII — CRIMINAL PROCEDURE AND CORRECTIONS
**Chapter:** 944 — STATE CORRECTIONAL SYSTEM
**Part:** III — INTERSTATE CORRECTIONS COMPACT
**Section:** 944.596
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

When a treaty is in effect between the United States and a foreign country providing for the transfer of a convicted offender who is a citizen or national of a foreign country to the foreign country of which she or he is a citizen or national, the Governor or the Governor's designee is authorized, subject to the terms of such treaty, to consent to the transfer of such convicted offender.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 79-75; s. 1655, ch. 97-102.
