# Fla. Stat. § 943.02 — Definitions.

**Title:** XLVII — CRIMINAL PROCEDURE AND CORRECTIONS
**Chapter:** 943 — DEPARTMENT OF LAW ENFORCEMENT
**Part:** III — INTERSTATE CORRECTIONS COMPACT
**Section:** 943.02
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

For the purpose of this chapter:
(1)  
“Department” means the Department of Law Enforcement.

(2)  
“Executive director” means the executive director of the Department of Law Enforcement.

**History:** s. 2, ch. 74-386; s. 2, ch. 78-347.
