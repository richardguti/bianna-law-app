# Fla. Stat. § 914.07 — Competency of evidence.

**Title:** XLVII — CRIMINAL PROCEDURE AND CORRECTIONS
**Chapter:** 914 — WITNESSES; CRIMINAL PROCEEDINGS
**Part:** IV — CREDIT COUNSELING SERVICES
**Section:** 914.07
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Except as otherwise provided, the law regarding competency of evidence and witnesses in civil cases shall apply in criminal cases.

**History:** RS 2863; GS 3919; RGS 6018; CGL 8312; s. 100, ch. 70-339.

**Note:** Former s. 932.31.
