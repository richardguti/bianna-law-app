# Fla. Stat. § 936.003 — Procedure.

**Title:** XLVII — CRIMINAL PROCEDURE AND CORRECTIONS

**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

“Inquest” defined.
——

As used in this chapter, the term “inquest” means a formal, nonadversary, nonjury presentation of evidence concerning a death, discovered by the medical examiner, state attorney, and law enforcement agency during their respective examinations and investigations into the death.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 77-294.
