# Fla. Stat. § 945.093 — Requirements for work assignments and programs.

**Title:** XLVII — CRIMINAL PROCEDURE AND CORRECTIONS
**Chapter:** 945 — DEPARTMENT OF CORRECTIONS
**Part:** III — INTERSTATE CORRECTIONS COMPACT
**Section:** 945.093
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The department shall evaluate, at a minimum, the physical and mental health of each inmate eligible for a work assignment or correctional work program and shall document approval of eligibility before the inmate receives orders for the assignment or program. The department may use discretion in determining whether an inmate is appropriate for an assignment.

**History:** s. 7, ch. 2025-180.
