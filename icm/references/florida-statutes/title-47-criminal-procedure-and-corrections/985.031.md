# Fla. Stat. § 985.031 — Age limitation; exception.

**Title:** XLVII — CRIMINAL PROCEDURE AND CORRECTIONS
**Chapter:** 985 — JUVENILE JUSTICE; INTERSTATE COMPACT ON JUVENILES
**Part:** I — GENERAL PROVISIONS
**Section:** 985.031
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
This section may be cited as the “Kaia Rolle Act.”

(2)  
A child younger than 7 years of age may not be arrested, charged, or adjudicated delinquent for a delinquent act or violation of law based on an act occurring before he or she reaches 7 years of age, unless the violation of law is a forcible felony as defined in s. 776.08.

**History:** s. 8, ch. 2021-241.
