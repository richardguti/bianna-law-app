# Fla. Stat. § 941.50 — Designation of officer.

**Title:** XLVII — CRIMINAL PROCEDURE AND CORRECTIONS
**Chapter:** 941 — CORRECTIONS: INTERSTATE COOPERATION
**Part:** III — INTERSTATE CORRECTIONS COMPACT
**Section:** 941.50
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The officer who will serve as central administrator of, and information agent for, the Agreement on Detainers shall be designated by the secretary of the Department of Corrections.

**History:** s. 6, ch. 73-287; s. 180, ch. 83-216.
