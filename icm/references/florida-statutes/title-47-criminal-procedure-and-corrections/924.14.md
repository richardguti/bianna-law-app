# Fla. Stat. § 924.14 — Stay of execution when defendant appeals.

**Title:** XLVII — CRIMINAL PROCEDURE AND CORRECTIONS
**Chapter:** 924 — CRIMINAL APPEALS AND COLLATERAL REVIEW
**Part:** III — FORENSIC SERVICES FOR PERSONS WHO ARE INTELLECTUALLY DISABLED OR AUTISTIC
**Section:** 924.14
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

An appeal by a defendant from either the judgment or sentence shall stay execution of the sentence, subject to the provisions of s. 924.065.

**History:** s. 293a, ch. 19554, 1939; F.S. 924.096924.09
