# Fla. Stat. § 944.52 — Legal adviser.

**Title:** XLVII — CRIMINAL PROCEDURE AND CORRECTIONS
**Chapter:** 944 — STATE CORRECTIONAL SYSTEM
**Part:** III — INTERSTATE CORRECTIONS COMPACT
**Section:** 944.52
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The Department of Legal Affairs shall be the legal adviser of the Department of Corrections.

**History:** s. 42, ch. 57-121; s. 18, ch. 61-530; ss. 11, 35, ch. 69-106; s. 63, ch. 77-120; s. 74, ch. 79-3.
