# Fla. Stat. § 941.42 — Extradition of persons alleged to be of unsound mind; purpose of law.

**Title:** XLVII — CRIMINAL PROCEDURE AND CORRECTIONS
**Chapter:** 941 — CORRECTIONS: INTERSTATE COOPERATION
**Part:** II — INTERSTATE AGREEMENT ON DETAINERS
**Section:** 941.42
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

This law is remedial and shall be in addition and as a supplement to any and all existing methods of procedure, including reciprocal agreements between this state and any other state for the transfer of persons of unsound mind; and shall be so interpreted and construed as to effectuate its general purpose to make uniform the law of those states which enact it.

**History:** ss. 8, 9, ch. 29686, 1955.
