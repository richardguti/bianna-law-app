# Fla. Stat. § 933.25 — Duration of inspection warrant.

**Title:** XLVII — CRIMINAL PROCEDURE AND CORRECTIONS
**Chapter:** 933 — SEARCH AND INSPECTION WARRANTS
**Part:** III — FORENSIC SERVICES FOR PERSONS WHO ARE INTELLECTUALLY DISABLED OR AUTISTIC
**Section:** 933.25
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

F.S. 933.246933.24  
Issuance of inspection warrant; contents.
——

If the judge is satisfied that cause for the inspection exists, he or she may issue the warrant particularly describing the place, dwelling, structure, or premises to be inspected and designating on the warrant the purpose and limitations of the inspection, including the limitations required by this act.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 82-8; s. 1579, ch. 97-102.
