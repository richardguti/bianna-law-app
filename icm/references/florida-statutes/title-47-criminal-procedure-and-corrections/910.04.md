# Fla. Stat. § 910.04 — Where aider in one county and offense committed in another.

**Title:** XLVII — CRIMINAL PROCEDURE AND CORRECTIONS
**Chapter:** 910 — JURISDICTION AND VENUE
**Part:** IV — CREDIT COUNSELING SERVICES
**Section:** 910.04
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

If a person in one county aids, abets, or procures the commission of an offense in another county, the person may be tried in either county.

**History:** s. 163, ch. 19554, 1939; CGL 1940 Supp. 8663(170); s. 76, ch. 70-339; s. 1515, ch. 97-102.
