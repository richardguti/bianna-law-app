# Fla. Stat. § 958.046 — Placement in county-operated boot camp programs for youthful offenders.

**Title:** XLVII — CRIMINAL PROCEDURE AND CORRECTIONS
**Chapter:** 958 — YOUTHFUL OFFENDERS
**Part:** II — LEASED OR MANAGED WORK PROGRAMS
**Section:** 958.046
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

——

In counties where there are county-operated youthful offender boot camp programs, other than boot camps described in s. 958.T basic training facility is necessary to aid in alleviating an emergency situation.
