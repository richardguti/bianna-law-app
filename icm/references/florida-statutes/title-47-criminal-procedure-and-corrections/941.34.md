# Fla. Stat. § 941.34 — Definition of “state.”

**Title:** XLVII — CRIMINAL PROCEDURE AND CORRECTIONS
**Chapter:** 941 — CORRECTIONS: INTERSTATE COOPERATION
**Part:** I — UNIFORM INTERSTATE EXTRADITION
**Section:** 941.34
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

For the purpose of this law the word “state” shall include the District of Columbia.

**History:** s. 4, ch. 20461, 1941.
