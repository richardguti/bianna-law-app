# Fla. Stat. § 941.33 — Fresh pursuit; validity of arrest.

**Title:** XLVII — CRIMINAL PROCEDURE AND CORRECTIONS
**Chapter:** 941 — CORRECTIONS: INTERSTATE COOPERATION
**Part:** I — UNIFORM INTERSTATE EXTRADITION
**Section:** 941.33
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Section 941.31 shall not be construed so as to make unlawful any arrest in this state which would otherwise be lawful.

**History:** s. 2, ch. 20461, 1941; s. 44, ch. 73-334; s. 1619, ch. 97-102.
