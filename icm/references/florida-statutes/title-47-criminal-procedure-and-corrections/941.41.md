# Fla. Stat. § 941.41 — Extradition of persons alleged to be of unsound mind; Governor to demand.

**Title:** XLVII — CRIMINAL PROCEDURE AND CORRECTIONS
**Chapter:** 941 — CORRECTIONS: INTERSTATE COOPERATION
**Part:** I — UNIFORM INTERSTATE EXTRADITION
**Section:** 941.41
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The Governor is vested with the power, on the application of any person interested, to demand the return to this state of any fugitive within the purview of this statute.

**History:** s. 7, ch. 29686, 1955.
