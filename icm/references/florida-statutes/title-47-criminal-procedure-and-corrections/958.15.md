# Fla. Stat. § 958.15 — Mutual participation agreements.

**Title:** XLVII — CRIMINAL PROCEDURE AND CORRECTIONS
**Chapter:** 958 — YOUTHFUL OFFENDERS
**Part:** II — LEASED OR MANAGED WORK PROGRAMS
**Section:** 958.15
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The provisions of this act shall not restrict the participation of youthful offenders in a mutual participation agreement adopted pursuant to s. 947.135.

**History:** s. 15, ch. 78-84.
