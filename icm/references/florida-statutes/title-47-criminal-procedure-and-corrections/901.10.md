# Fla. Stat. § 901.10 — How summons served.

**Title:** XLVII — CRIMINAL PROCEDURE AND CORRECTIONS
**Chapter:** 901 — ARRESTS AND TEMPORARY DETENTIONS
**Part:** IV — CREDIT COUNSELING SERVICES
**Section:** 901.10
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

A summons shall be served in the same manner as a summons in a civil action.

**History:** s. 10, ch. 19554, 1939; CGL 1940 Supp. 8663(10); s. 6, ch. 70-339.
