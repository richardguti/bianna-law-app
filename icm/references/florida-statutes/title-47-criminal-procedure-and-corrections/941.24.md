# Fla. Stat. § 941.24 — Costs and expenses.

**Title:** XLVII — CRIMINAL PROCEDURE AND CORRECTIONS
**Chapter:** 941 — CORRECTIONS: INTERSTATE COOPERATION
**Part:** I — UNIFORM INTERSTATE EXTRADITION
**Section:** 941.24
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The costs and expenses of confinement of persons convicted in this state after extradition shall be paid as now or hereafter provided by law.

**History:** s. 24, ch. 20460, 1941.
