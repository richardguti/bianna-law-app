# Fla. Stat. § 949.04 — Law to be liberally construed.

**Title:** XLVII — CRIMINAL PROCEDURE AND CORRECTIONS
**Chapter:** 949 — PAROLE AND PROBATION: GENERAL PROVISIONS
**Part:** II — LEASED OR MANAGED WORK PROGRAMS
**Section:** 949.04
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Chapters 947-949 shall be liberally construed that its objects may be achieved.

**History:** s. 33, ch. 20519, 1941.
