# Fla. Stat. § 985.6885 — Persons authorized to visit juvenile facilities.

**Title:** XLVII — CRIMINAL PROCEDURE AND CORRECTIONS
**Chapter:** 985 — JUVENILE JUSTICE; INTERSTATE COMPACT ON JUVENILES
**Part:** XI — DEPARTMENT OF JUVENILE JUSTICE
**Section:** 985.6885
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

——

(1)  
The following persons may visit at their pleasure between the hours of 6 a.m. and 11 p.m. all facilities housing juveniles which are operated or overseen by the department or a county:
(a)  
The Governor.

(b)  
A Cabinet member.

(c)  
A member of the Legislature.

4.  
The facility is inspected annually and meets the Florida Model Jail Standards.
