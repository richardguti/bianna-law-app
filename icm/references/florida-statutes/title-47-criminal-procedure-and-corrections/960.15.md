# Fla. Stat. § 960.15 — Records.

**Title:** XLVII — CRIMINAL PROCEDURE AND CORRECTIONS
**Chapter:** 960 — VICTIM ASSISTANCE
**Part:** II — LEASED OR MANAGED WORK PROGRAMS
**Section:** 960.15
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Any record or report obtained by the department or a hearing officer that is confidential or exempt from the provisions of s. 119.07(1) shall retain that status and shall not be subject to public disclosure.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 77-452; s. 13, ch. 80-146; s. 10, ch. 91-23; s. 26, ch. 91-46; s. 1, ch. 94-93; s. 454, ch. 96-406.
