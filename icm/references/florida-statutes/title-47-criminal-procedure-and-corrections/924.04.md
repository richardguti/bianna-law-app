# Fla. Stat. § 924.04 — Appeal by one of several defendants.

**Title:** XLVII — CRIMINAL PROCEDURE AND CORRECTIONS
**Chapter:** 924 — CRIMINAL APPEALS AND COLLATERAL REVIEW
**Part:** III — FORENSIC SERVICES FOR PERSONS WHO ARE INTELLECTUALLY DISABLED OR AUTISTIC
**Section:** 924.04
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

One or more defendants who are tried jointly may appeal, but those who do not join shall not be affected by the appeal except by express provision of the appellate court.

**History:** s. 283, ch. 19554, 1939; CGL 1940 Supp. 8663(293); s. 145, ch. 70-339.
