# Fla. Stat. § 905.12 — Retirement of grand jurors.

**Title:** XLVII — CRIMINAL PROCEDURE AND CORRECTIONS
**Chapter:** 905 — GRAND JURY
**Part:** IV — CREDIT COUNSELING SERVICES
**Section:** 905.12
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

After being charged by the court, the grand jury shall retire to a private place and perform their duties.

**History:** s. 91, ch. 19554, 1939; CGL 1940 Supp. 8663(91); s. 50, ch. 70-339.
