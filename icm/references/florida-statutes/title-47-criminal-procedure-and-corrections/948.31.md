# Fla. Stat. § 948.31 — Evaluation and treatment of sexual predators and offenders on probation or community control.

**Title:** XLVII — CRIMINAL PROCEDURE AND CORRECTIONS
**Chapter:** 948 — PROBATION AND COMMUNITY CONTROL
**Part:** II — LEASED OR MANAGED WORK PROGRAMS
**Section:** 948.31
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The court may require any probationer or community controllee who is required to register as a sexual predator under s. 775.21 or sexual offender under s.  xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml">F.S. 948.3016948.301  
Electronic monitoring as a condition of probation or community control for certain offenders.
——

(1)  
As used in this section, the term “school” means the grounds or facility of any early learning, prekindergarten, kindergarten, elementary school, middle school, junior high school, secondary school, career center, or postsecondary school, whether public or private.

(2)  
Effective for any probationer or community controllee whose crime was committed on or after October 1, 2023, and who is placed under supervision for a violation of s. 790.115, s. 790.161, s. 790.1615, s. 790.162, s. 790.163, s. 790.164, s. 790.165, s. 790.166, s. 810.095, or s. 836.10, committed at or against a school or against a student while he or she is at school, the court must consider whether conditions of electronic monitoring and a prohibition from being within 1,000 feet of any school are appropriate for the offender.

**History:** s. 2, ch. 2023-225.

**Note:** Former s. 948.03(4).
