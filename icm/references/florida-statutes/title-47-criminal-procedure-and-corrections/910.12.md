# Fla. Stat. § 910.12 — Trial of aider.

**Title:** XLVII — CRIMINAL PROCEDURE AND CORRECTIONS
**Chapter:** 910 — JURISDICTION AND VENUE
**Part:** IV — CREDIT COUNSELING SERVICES
**Section:** 910.12
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

A person, within or outside this state, who counsels, hires, or procures a felony to be committed may be tried in the same county in which the principal felon might be tried.

**History:** s. 170, ch. 19554, 1939; CGL 1940 Supp. 8663(177); s. 81, ch. 70-339; s. 1517, ch. 97-102.
