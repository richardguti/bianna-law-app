# Fla. Stat. § 985.465 — Maximum-risk residential facilities.

**Title:** XLVII — CRIMINAL PROCEDURE AND CORRECTIONS
**Chapter:** 985 — JUVENILE JUSTICE; INTERSTATE COMPACT ON JUVENILES
**Part:** VII — DISPOSITION; POSTDISPOSITION
**Section:** 985.465
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

A maximum-risk residential facility is a physically secure residential commitment program with a designated length of stay from 18 months to 36 months, primarily serving children 13 years of age to 19 years of age or until the jurisdiction of the court expires. Each child committed to this level must meet one of the following criteria:
(1)  
The child is at least 13 years of age at the time of the disposition for the current offense and has been adjudicated on the current offense for:
(a)  
Arson;

(b)  
Sexual battery;

(c)  
Robbery;

(d)  
Kidnapping;

(e)  
Aggravated child abuse;

(f)  
Aggravated assault;

(g)  
Aggravated stalking;

(h)  
Murder;

(i)  
Manslaughter;
