# Fla. Stat. § 945.74 — Implementation.

**Title:** XLVII — CRIMINAL PROCEDURE AND CORRECTIONS
**Chapter:** 945 — DEPARTMENT OF CORRECTIONS
**Part:** III — INTERSTATE CORRECTIONS COMPACT
**Section:** 945.74
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The department shall implement the inmate training program to the fullest extent feasible within the parameters of ss. 945.71-945.74.

**History:** s. 27, ch. 92-310; s. 89, ch. 2000-158.
