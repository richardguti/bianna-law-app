# Fla. Stat. § 943.2562 — Advisory boards.

**Title:** XLVII — CRIMINAL PROCEDURE AND CORRECTIONS
**Chapter:** 943 — DEPARTMENT OF LAW ENFORCEMENT
**Part:** III — INTERSTATE CORRECTIONS COMPACT
**Section:** 943.2562
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Each center's policies shall be established by an advisory board comprised of not more than 11 members, the composition of which shall be determined by the participating agencies.

**History:** s. 3, ch. 91-205.
