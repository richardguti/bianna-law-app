# Fla. Stat. § 960.22 — Application for federal funds.

**Title:** XLVII — CRIMINAL PROCEDURE AND CORRECTIONS
**Chapter:** 960 — VICTIM ASSISTANCE
**Part:** II — LEASED OR MANAGED WORK PROGRAMS
**Section:** 960.22
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The department is authorized to apply for funds from, and to submit all necessary forms to, any federal agency participating in a cooperative program to compensate victims of crime.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 77-452; s. 17, ch. 80-146; s. 13, ch. 91-23.
