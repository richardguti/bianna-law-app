# Fla. Stat. § 905.07 — Effect of sustaining challenge to panel.

**Title:** XLVII — CRIMINAL PROCEDURE AND CORRECTIONS
**Chapter:** 905 — GRAND JURY
**Part:** IV — CREDIT COUNSELING SERVICES
**Section:** 905.07
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

If a challenge to the panel is sustained, the grand jury shall be discharged.

**History:** s. 86, ch. 19554, 1939; CGL 1940 Supp. 8663(86).
