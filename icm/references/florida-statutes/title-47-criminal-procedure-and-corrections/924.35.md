# Fla. Stat. § 924.35 — Enforcement of judgment on affirmance.

**Title:** XLVII — CRIMINAL PROCEDURE AND CORRECTIONS
**Chapter:** 924 — CRIMINAL APPEALS AND COLLATERAL REVIEW
**Part:** III — FORENSIC SERVICES FOR PERSONS WHO ARE INTELLECTUALLY DISABLED OR AUTISTIC
**Section:** 924.35
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

When the judgment against the defendant is affirmed, the judgment shall be enforced by the trial court.

**History:** s. 311, ch. 19554, 1939; CGL 1940 Supp. 8663(324); s. 162, ch. 70-339.
