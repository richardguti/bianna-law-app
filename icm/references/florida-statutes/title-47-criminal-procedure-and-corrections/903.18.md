# Fla. Stat. § 903.18 — Substitution of cash bail for other bail.

**Title:** XLVII — CRIMINAL PROCEDURE AND CORRECTIONS
**Chapter:** 903 — BAIL
**Part:** IV — CREDIT COUNSELING SERVICES
**Section:** 903.18
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

When bail other than a deposit of money or bonds has been given, the defendant or the surety may deposit money or bonds as provided in s. 903.16 and have the original bond canceled.

**History:** s. 60, ch. 19554, 1939; CGL 1940 Supp. 8663(60); s. 27, ch. 70-339.
