# Fla. Stat. § 905.165 — Grand jury to make presentments.

**Title:** XLVII — CRIMINAL PROCEDURE AND CORRECTIONS
**Chapter:** 905 — GRAND JURY
**Part:** IV — CREDIT COUNSELING SERVICES
**Section:** 905.165
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The grand jury may make presentments for offenses against the criminal laws, whether or not specific punishment is provided for the offense.

**History:** s. 16, Nov. 19, 1828; RS 2805; GS 3854; RGS 5949; CGL 8215; s. 53, ch. 70-339.

**Note:** Former s. 932.15.
