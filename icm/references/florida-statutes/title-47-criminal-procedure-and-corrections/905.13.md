# Fla. Stat. § 905.13 — Appointment of clerk.

**Title:** XLVII — CRIMINAL PROCEDURE AND CORRECTIONS
**Chapter:** 905 — GRAND JURY
**Part:** IV — CREDIT COUNSELING SERVICES
**Section:** 905.13
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The foreperson shall appoint one of the grand jurors as clerk to keep minutes of the proceedings.

**History:** s. 92, ch. 19554, 1939; CGL 1940 Supp. 8663(92); s. 50, ch. 70-339; s. 1495, ch. 97-102.
