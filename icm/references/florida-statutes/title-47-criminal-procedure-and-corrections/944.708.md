# Fla. Stat. § 944.708 — Rules.

**Title:** XLVII — CRIMINAL PROCEDURE AND CORRECTIONS
**Chapter:** 944 — STATE CORRECTIONAL SYSTEM
**Part:** III — INTERSTATE CORRECTIONS COMPACT
**Section:** 944.708
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The Department of Corrections shall adopt rules to implement the provisions of ss. 944.701-944.707.

**History:** ss. 11, 12, ch. 87-298; s. 13, ch. 88-122; s. 41, ch. 2010-117; s. 34, ch. 2011-213.
