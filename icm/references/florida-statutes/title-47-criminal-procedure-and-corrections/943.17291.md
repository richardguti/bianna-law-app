# Fla. Stat. § 943.17291 — Basic skills training in juvenile sexual offender investigation.

**Title:** XLVII — CRIMINAL PROCEDURE AND CORRECTIONS
**Chapter:** 943 — DEPARTMENT OF LAW ENFORCEMENT
**Part:** III — INTERSTATE CORRECTIONS COMPACT
**Section:** 943.17291
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The commission shall incorporate juvenile sexual offender investigation instruction into the course curriculum required for a law enforcement officer to obtain initial certification.

**History:** s. 6, ch. 98-158.
