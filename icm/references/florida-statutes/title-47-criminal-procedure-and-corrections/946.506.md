# Fla. Stat. § 946.506 — Modification or termination of correctional work program by the corporation.

**Title:** XLVII — CRIMINAL PROCEDURE AND CORRECTIONS
**Chapter:** 945 — DEPARTMENT OF CORRECTIONS
**Part:** II — LEASED OR MANAGED WORK PROGRAMS
**Section:** 946.506
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

This part does not prevent the corporation from modifying, altering, or terminating any correctional work program, once assumed, so long as the corporation is otherwise carrying out the provisions of this part.

**History:** s. 2, ch. 83-209; ss. 4, 9, ch. 87-286; s. 5, ch. 96-270; s. 5, ch. 2001-242.

**Note:** Former s. 946.041.
