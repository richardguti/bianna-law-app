# Fla. Stat. § 924.15 — Approval of appeal bonds.

**Title:** XLVII — CRIMINAL PROCEDURE AND CORRECTIONS
**Chapter:** 924 — CRIMINAL APPEALS AND COLLATERAL REVIEW
**Part:** III — FORENSIC SERVICES FOR PERSONS WHO ARE INTELLECTUALLY DISABLED OR AUTISTIC
**Section:** 924.15
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Appeal bonds shall be approved by the court which originally determined the action and shall be filed with the clerk of that court.

**History:** s. 293b, ch. 19554, 1939; CGL 1940 Supp. 8663(304); s. 152, ch. 70-339.
