# Fla. Stat. § 941.21 — Governor may recall warrant or issue alias.

**Title:** XLVII — CRIMINAL PROCEDURE AND CORRECTIONS
**Chapter:** 941 — CORRECTIONS: INTERSTATE COOPERATION
**Part:** I — UNIFORM INTERSTATE EXTRADITION
**Section:** 941.21
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The Governor may recall his or her warrant or warrants of arrest or may issue another warrant whenever he or she deems proper.

**History:** s. 21, ch. 20460, 1941; s. 1612, ch. 97-102.
