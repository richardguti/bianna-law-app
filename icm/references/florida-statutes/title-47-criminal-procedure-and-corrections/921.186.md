# Fla. Stat. § 921.186 — Substantial assistance.

**Title:** XLVII — CRIMINAL PROCEDURE AND CORRECTIONS
**Chapter:** 921 — SENTENCE
**Part:** III — FORENSIC SERVICES FOR PERSONS WHO ARE INTELLECTUALLY DISABLED OR AUTISTIC
**Section:** 921.186
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Notwithstanding any other law, the state attorney may move the sentencing court to reduce or suspend the sentence of any person who is convicted of violating any felony offense and who provides substantial assistance in the identification, arrest, or conviction of any of that person's accomplices, accessories, coconspirators, or principals or of any other person engaged in criminal activity that would constitute a felony. Sentence; restitution a mitigation in certain crimes.
——

In the imposition of a sentence for any felony or misdemeanor involving property, but not injury or opportunity for injury to persons, the court, in its discretion, shall consider any degree of restitution a mitigation of the severity of an otherwise appropriate sentence.
