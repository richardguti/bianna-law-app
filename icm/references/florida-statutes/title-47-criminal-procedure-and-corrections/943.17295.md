# Fla. Stat. § 943.17295 — Continued employment training relating to juvenile sexual offender investigation.

**Title:** XLVII — CRIMINAL PROCEDURE AND CORRECTIONS
**Chapter:** 943 — DEPARTMENT OF LAW ENFORCEMENT
**Part:** III — INTERSTATE CORRECTIONS COMPACT
**Section:** 943.17295
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The commission shall incorporate the subject of sexual abuse and assault investigation, with an emphasis on cases involving child victims or juvenile offenders, into the curriculum required for continuous employment or appointment as a law enforcement officer.

**History:** s. 7, ch. 98-158.
