# Fla. Stat. § 902.20 — Contempts before committing trial court judge.

**Title:** XLVII — CRIMINAL PROCEDURE AND CORRECTIONS
**Chapter:** 902 — PRELIMINARY HEARINGS
**Part:** IV — CREDIT COUNSELING SERVICES
**Section:** 902.20
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

A committing trial court judge holding a preliminary hearing shall have the same power to punish for contempts that she or he has while presiding at the trial of criminal cases.

**History:** RS 2795; GS 3845; RGS 5940; CGL 8206; s. 19, ch. 70-339; s. 1473, ch. 97-102; s. 28, ch. 2004-11.

**Note:** Former s. 932.04.
