# Fla. Stat. § 901.09 — When summons shall be issued.

**Title:** XLVII — CRIMINAL PROCEDURE AND CORRECTIONS
**Chapter:** 901 — ARRESTS AND TEMPORARY DETENTIONS
**Part:** IV — CREDIT COUNSELING SERVICES
**Section:** 901.09
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
When the complaint is for an offense that the trial court judge is empowered to try summarily, the trial court judge shall issue a summons instead of a warrant, unless she or he reasonably believes that the person against whom the complaint was made will not appear upon a summons, in which event the trial court judge shall issue a warrant.

(2)  
When the complaint is for a misdemeanor that the trial court judge is not empowered to try summarily, the trial court judge shall issue a summons instead of a warrant if she or he reasonably believes that the person against whom the complaint was made will appear upon a summons.

(3)  
The summons shall set forth substantially the nature of the offense and shall command the person against whom the complaint was made to appear before the trial court judge at a stated time and place.

**History:** s. 8, ch. 19554, 1939; CGL 1940 Supp. 8663(8); s. 6, ch. 70-339; s. 1454, ch. 97-102; s. 21, ch. 2004-11.
