# Fla. Stat. § 913.15 — Special jurors.

**Title:** XLVII — CRIMINAL PROCEDURE AND CORRECTIONS
**Chapter:** 913 — TRIAL JURY
**Part:** IV — CREDIT COUNSELING SERVICES
**Section:** 913.15
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The court may summon jurors in addition to the regular panel.

**History:** s. 12, ch. 1637, 1868; RS 2850; GS 3906; RGS 6004; CGL 8298; s. 89, ch. 70-339; s. 1521, ch. 97-102.

**Note:** Former s. 932.20.
