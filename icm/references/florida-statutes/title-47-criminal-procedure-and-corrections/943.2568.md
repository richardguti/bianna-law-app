# Fla. Stat. § 943.2568 — Advisory boards; expense reimbursement.

**Title:** XLVII — CRIMINAL PROCEDURE AND CORRECTIONS
**Chapter:** 943 — DEPARTMENT OF LAW ENFORCEMENT
**Part:** III — INTERSTATE CORRECTIONS COMPACT
**Section:** 943.2568
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Members of advisory boards shall not receive compensation for their services but shall receive reimbursement for expenses as provided in s. 112.061.

**History:** s. 9, ch. 91-205.
