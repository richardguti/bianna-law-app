# Fla. Stat. § 908.108 — Education records.

**Title:** XLVII — CRIMINAL PROCEDURE AND CORRECTIONS
**Chapter:** 908 — FEDERAL IMMIGRATION ENFORCEMENT
**Part:** IV — CREDIT COUNSELING SERVICES
**Section:** 908.108
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

This chapter does not apply to the release of information contained in education records of an educational agency or institution, except in conformity with the Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act of 1974, 20 U.S.C. s. 1232g.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 2019-102.
