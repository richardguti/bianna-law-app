# Fla. Stat. § 946.512 — Inmate compensation plan.

**Title:** XLVII — CRIMINAL PROCEDURE AND CORRECTIONS
**Chapter:** 945 — DEPARTMENT OF CORRECTIONS
**Part:** II — LEASED OR MANAGED WORK PROGRAMS
**Section:** 946.512
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The corporation shall establish a compensation plan that provides for a specific amount to be paid to the department to be credited to an account for an inmate performing labor and a portion to be used to make any court-ordered payments, including restitution to the victim, and a specific amount to be paid to the Prison Industries Trust Fund to be used as provided in s. 946.522. Such funds, excluding victim restitution payments, court-ordered payments, and the amount credited to the account of the inmate, shall be deposited in the Prison Industries Trust Fund to be used as provided in s. 946.522.

**History:** s. 2, ch. 83-209; s. 4, ch. 87-286; s. 4, ch. 91-298; s. 7, ch. 97-227; s. 2, ch. 2000-278.

**Note:** Former s. 946.10.
