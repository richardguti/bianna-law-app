# Fla. Stat. § 943.403 — Rulemaking; criminal justice program.

**Title:** XLVII — CRIMINAL PROCEDURE AND CORRECTIONS
**Chapter:** 943 — DEPARTMENT OF LAW ENFORCEMENT
**Part:** III — INTERSTATE CORRECTIONS COMPACT
**Section:** 943.403
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The Department of Law Enforcement may adopt rules necessary for the operation of the criminal justice program.

**History:** s. 6, ch. 2001-232.
