An application for an administrative expunction for a record containing immigration detainer information as described in this section must include the date and time when the person was detained, the person's name, the offender-based tracking system number, and information relating to the immigration detainer. An application or endorsement under this section is not admissible as evidence in any judicial or administrative proceeding and may not be construed in any way as an admission of liability in connection with a detention.
