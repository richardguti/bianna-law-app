# Fla. Stat. § 947.05 — Seal.

**Title:** XLVII — CRIMINAL PROCEDURE AND CORRECTIONS
**Chapter:** 947 — FLORIDA COMMISSION ON OFFENDER REVIEW; CONDITIONAL RELEASE; CONTROL RELEASE; PAROLE
**Part:** II — LEASED OR MANAGED WORK PROGRAMS
**Section:** 947.05
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The commission shall adopt an official seal of which the courts shall take judicial notice.

**History:** s. 3, ch. 20519, 1941; s. 34, ch. 83-131; s. 37, ch. 86-183; s. 67, ch. 88-122; s. 17, ch. 89-531; s. 20, ch. 90-337; s. 1, ch. 93-2.
