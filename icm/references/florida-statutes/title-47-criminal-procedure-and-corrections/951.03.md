# Fla. Stat. § 951.03 — County commissioners to provide food, etc.

**Title:** XLVII — CRIMINAL PROCEDURE AND CORRECTIONS
**Chapter:** 951 — COUNTY AND MUNICIPAL PRISONERS
**Part:** II — LEASED OR MANAGED WORK PROGRAMS
**Section:** 951.03
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Boards of county commissioners, when working county prisoners on the public works of the counties shall provide, or cause to be provided, substantial food, clothes, shoes, medical attention, etc., for said prisoners as are required for state prisoners in the state.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 2090, 1877; RS 3032; GS 4109; s. 1, ch. 5705, 1907; s. 1, ch. 5963, 1909; RGS 6217; s. 1, ch. 7323, 1917; s. 1, ch. 9203, 1923; CGL 8549; s. 1, ch. 61-488.
