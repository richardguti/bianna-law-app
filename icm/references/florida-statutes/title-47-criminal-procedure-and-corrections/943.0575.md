# Fla. Stat. § 943.0575 — Public access to records.

**Title:** XLVII — CRIMINAL PROCEDURE AND CORRECTIONS
**Chapter:** 943 — DEPARTMENT OF LAW ENFORCEMENT
**Part:** III — INTERSTATE CORRECTIONS COMPACT
**Section:** 943.0575
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Nothing in this act shall be construed to restrict or condition public access to records as provided by s. 119.07.

**History:** s. 16, ch. 80-409.
