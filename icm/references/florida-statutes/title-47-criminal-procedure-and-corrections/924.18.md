# Fla. Stat. § 924.18 — Bail when state appeals.

**Title:** XLVII — CRIMINAL PROCEDURE AND CORRECTIONS
**Chapter:** 924 — CRIMINAL APPEALS AND COLLATERAL REVIEW
**Part:** III — FORENSIC SERVICES FOR PERSONS WHO ARE INTELLECTUALLY DISABLED OR AUTISTIC
**Section:** 924.18
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

If the state appeals after a conviction of the defendant, a justice or judge of the appellate or trial court may in his or her discretion admit the defendant to bail.

**History:** s. 294, ch. 19554, 1939; CGL 1940 Supp. 8663(307); s. 155, ch. 70-339; s. 1556, ch. 97-102.
