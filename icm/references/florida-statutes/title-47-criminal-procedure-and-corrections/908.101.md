# Fla. Stat. § 908.101 — Legislative findings and intent.

**Title:** XLVII — CRIMINAL PROCEDURE AND CORRECTIONS
**Chapter:** 908 — FEDERAL IMMIGRATION ENFORCEMENT
**Part:** IV — CREDIT COUNSELING SERVICES
**Section:** 908.101
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The Legislature finds that it is an important state interest to cooperate and assist the Federal Government in the enforcement of federal immigration laws within this state.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 2019-102.
