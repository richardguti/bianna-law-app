# Fla. Stat. § 934.28 — Exclusivity of remedies and sanctions.

**Title:** XLVII — CRIMINAL PROCEDURE AND CORRECTIONS
**Chapter:** 934 — SECURITY OF COMMUNICATIONS; SURVEILLANCE
**Part:** III — FORENSIC SERVICES FOR PERSONS WHO ARE INTELLECTUALLY DISABLED OR AUTISTIC
**Section:** 934.28
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The remedies and sanctions described in ss. 934.21-934.27 are the only judicial remedies and sanctions for violation of those sections.

**History:** s. 9, ch. 88-184.
