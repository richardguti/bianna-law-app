# Fla. Stat. § 941.46 — Definition.

**Title:** XLVII — CRIMINAL PROCEDURE AND CORRECTIONS
**Chapter:** 941 — CORRECTIONS: INTERSTATE COOPERATION
**Part:** II — INTERSTATE AGREEMENT ON DETAINERS
**Section:** 941.46
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

As used in this part, “appropriate court” means, with reference to the courts of this state, the circuit court possessing the proper venue.

**History:** s. 2, ch. 73-287.
