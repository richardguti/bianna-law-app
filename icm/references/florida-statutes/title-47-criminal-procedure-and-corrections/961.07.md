# Fla. Stat. § 961.07 — Continuing appropriation.

**Title:** XLVII — CRIMINAL PROCEDURE AND CORRECTIONS
**Chapter:** 961 — VICTIMS OF WRONGFUL INCARCERATION COMPENSATION
**Part:** II — LEASED OR MANAGED WORK PROGRAMS
**Section:** 961.07
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
Beginning in the 2025-2026 fiscal year and continuing each fiscal year thereafter, a sum sufficient to pay the approved payments under s. 961.03(1)(b)1. is appropriated from the General Revenue Fund to the Chief Financial Officer, which sum is further appropriated for expenditure pursuant to this act.

(2)  
Payments for petitions filed pursuant to s. 961.03(1)(b)2. are subject to specific appropriation.

**History:** s. 7, ch. 2008-39; s. 5, ch. 2025-194.
