# Fla. Stat. § 960.298 — Priority of liens.

**Title:** XLVII — CRIMINAL PROCEDURE AND CORRECTIONS
**Chapter:** 961 — VICTIMS OF WRONGFUL INCARCERATION COMPENSATION
**Part:** II — LEASED OR MANAGED WORK PROGRAMS
**Section:** 960.298
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Any liens already in existence at the time the restitution lien is placed shall take precedence over the restitution lien.

**History:** s. 23, ch. 94-342.
