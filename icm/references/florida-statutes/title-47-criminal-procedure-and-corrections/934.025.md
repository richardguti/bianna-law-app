# Fla. Stat. § 934.025 — Search warrants and court orders; remote appearance authorized.

**Title:** XLVII — CRIMINAL PROCEDURE AND CORRECTIONS
**Chapter:** 934 — SECURITY OF COMMUNICATIONS; SURVEILLANCE
**Part:** III — FORENSIC SERVICES FOR PERSONS WHO ARE INTELLECTUALLY DISABLED OR AUTISTIC
**Section:** 934.025
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

A judge may authorize a law enforcement officer applying for a search warrant or court order pursuant to this chapter to appear remotely using audio-video communication technology. As used in this section, the term “audio-video communication technology” has the same meaning as in s. 117.201.

**History:** s. 4, ch. 2026-177.
