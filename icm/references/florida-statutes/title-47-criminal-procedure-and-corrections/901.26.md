# Fla. Stat. § 901.26 — Arrest and detention of foreign nationals.

**Title:** XLVII — CRIMINAL PROCEDURE AND CORRECTIONS
**Chapter:** 901 — ARRESTS AND TEMPORARY DETENTIONS
**Part:** IV — CREDIT COUNSELING SERVICES
**Section:** 901.26
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Failure to provide consular notification under the Vienna Convention on Consular Relations or other bilateral consular conventions shall not be a defense in any criminal proceeding against any foreign national and shall not be cause for the foreign national's discharge from custody.

**History:** ss. 1, 2, 3, ch. 65-523; ss. 10, 35, ch. 69-106; s. 15, ch. 70-339; s. 1469, ch. 97-102; s. 5, ch. 2001-200.
