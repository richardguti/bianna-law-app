# Fla. Stat. § 957.02 — Definition.

**Title:** XLVII — CRIMINAL PROCEDURE AND CORRECTIONS
**Chapter:** 957 — CORRECTIONAL PRIVATIZATION
**Part:** II — LEASED OR MANAGED WORK PROGRAMS
**Section:** 957.02
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

As used in this chapter, “department” means the Department of Corrections.

**History:** s. 40, ch. 93-406; s. 11, ch. 2004-248.
