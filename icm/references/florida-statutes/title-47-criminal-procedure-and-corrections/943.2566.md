# Fla. Stat. § 943.2566 — Promotions; feasibility study to establish pool of evaluators to assess qualifications.

**Title:** XLVII — CRIMINAL PROCEDURE AND CORRECTIONS
**Chapter:** 943 — DEPARTMENT OF LAW ENFORCEMENT
**Part:** III — INTERSTATE CORRECTIONS COMPACT
**Section:** 943.2566
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Upon the request of any participating criminal justice agency in its region, the center shall engage in a feasibility study to establish a qualified pool of evaluators to assess qualifications of inservice officers for purposes of determining promotions.

**History:** s. 7, ch. 91-205.
