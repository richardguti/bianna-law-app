# Fla. Stat. § 908.103 — Sanctuary policies prohibited.

**Title:** XLVII — CRIMINAL PROCEDURE AND CORRECTIONS
**Chapter:** 908 — FEDERAL IMMIGRATION ENFORCEMENT
**Part:** IV — CREDIT COUNSELING SERVICES
**Section:** 908.103
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

A state entity, law enforcement agency, or local governmental entity may not adopt or have in effect a sanctuary policy.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 2019-102.
