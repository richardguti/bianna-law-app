# Fla. Stat. § 900.03 — Courts vested with criminal jurisdiction; process.

**Title:** XLVII — CRIMINAL PROCEDURE AND CORRECTIONS
**Chapter:** 900 — GENERAL PROVISIONS
**Part:** IV — CREDIT COUNSELING SERVICES
**Section:** 900.03
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
Original jurisdiction in criminal cases is vested in the circuit courts and county courts.

(2)  
Courts having criminal jurisdiction may issue writs and process necessary to the exercise of the criminal jurisdiction and the writs and process shall have effect through the state.

**History:** s. 321, ch. 19554, 1939; CGL 1940 Supp. 8663(334); s. 1, ch. 70-339.

**Note:** Former s. 925.02.
