# Fla. Stat. § 945.6032 — Quality management program requirements.

**Title:** XLVII — CRIMINAL PROCEDURE AND CORRECTIONS
**Chapter:** 945 — DEPARTMENT OF CORRECTIONS
**Part:** III — INTERSTATE CORRECTIONS COMPACT
**Section:** 945.6032
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
The authority shall appoint a medical review committee pursuant to s. 766.101 to provide oversight for the Department of Corrections' inmate health care quality management program. The authority shall also designate one of its members to serve on the Department of Corrections' medical review committee in order to ensure coordination between the department and the authority with regard to issues of quality management and to enhance the authority's oversight of the Department of Corrections' quality management system.

(2)  
The authority's medical review committee shall review amendments to the orrections to file a corrective action plan or to timely implement the provisions of a corrective action plan correcting identified deficiencies may result in the initiation of the dispute resolution procedures by the authority pursuant to s. 945.6035.
