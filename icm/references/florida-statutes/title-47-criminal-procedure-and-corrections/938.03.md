# Fla. Stat. § 938.03 — Crimes Compensation Trust Fund.

**Title:** XLVII — CRIMINAL PROCEDURE AND CORRECTIONS
**Chapter:** 938 — COURT COSTS
**Part:** I — MANDATORY COSTS IN ALL CASES
**Section:** 938.03
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

——

(1)  
Any person pleading guilty or nolo contendere to, or being convicted of or adjudicated delinquent for, any felony, misdemeanor, delinquent act, or criminal traffic offense under the laws of this state or the violation of any municipal or county ordinance which adopts by reference any misdemeanor under state law, shall pay as an additional cost in the case, in addition and prior to any other cost required to be imposed by law, the sum of $50. Any person whose adjudication is withheld shall also be assessed such cost.

(2)  
These costs shall not be waived by the court.
