# Fla. Stat. § 940.05 — Restoration of civil rights.

**Title:** XLVII — CRIMINAL PROCEDURE AND CORRECTIONS
**Chapter:** 940 — EXECUTIVE CLEMENCY
**Part:** V — MISCELLANEOUS PROVISIONS
**Section:** 940.05
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Any person who has been convicted of a felony may be entitled to the restoration of all the rights of citizenship enjoyed by him or her before conviction if the person has:
(1)  
Received a full pardon from the Board of Executive Clemency;

(2)  
Served the maximum term of the sentence imposed upon him or her; or

(3)  
Been granted his or her final release by the Florida Commission on Offender Review.
