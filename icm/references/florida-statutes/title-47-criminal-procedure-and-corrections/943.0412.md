# Fla. Stat. § 943.0412 — Identity Theft and Fraud Grant Program.

**Title:** XLVII — CRIMINAL PROCEDURE AND CORRECTIONS
**Chapter:** 943 — DEPARTMENT OF LAW ENFORCEMENT
**Part:** III — INTERSTATE CORRECTIONS COMPACT
**Section:** 943.0412
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

——

(1)  
There is created the Identity Theft and Fraud Grant Program within the department to award grants to support local law enforcement agencies in the investigation and enforcement of personal identification information theft and fraud. Grants shall be provided if funds are appropriated for that purpose by law.
