# Fla. Stat. § 921.161 — Sentence not to run until imposed; credit for county jail time after sentence; certificate of custodian of jail.

**Title:** XLVII — CRIMINAL PROCEDURE AND CORRECTIONS
**Chapter:** 921 — SENTENCE
**Part:** III — FORENSIC SERVICES FOR PERSONS WHO ARE INTELLECTUALLY DISABLED OR AUTISTIC
**Section:** 921.161
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
A sentence of imprisonment shall not begin to run before the date it is imposed, but the court imposing a sentence shall allow a defendant credit for all of the time she or he spent in the county jail before sentence. The credit must be for a specified period of time and shall be provided for in the sentence.

(2)   imposed by another court of this state or imposed by a court of another state.
