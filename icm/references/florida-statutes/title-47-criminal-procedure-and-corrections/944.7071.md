# Fla. Stat. § 944.7071 — Operation New Hope.

**Title:** XLVII — CRIMINAL PROCEDURE AND CORRECTIONS
**Chapter:** 944 — STATE CORRECTIONAL SYSTEM
**Part:** III — INTERSTATE CORRECTIONS COMPACT
**Section:** 944.7071
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

As authorized by and consistent with funding appropriated in the General Appropriations Act, the department may contract, in accordance with s. 944.706, with Operation New Hope, a nonprofit organization exempt from taxation pursuant to s. 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code, to provide reentry services, including, but not limited to, counseling; providing information on housing and job placement; money management assistance; and programs that address substance abuse, mental health, and co-occurring conditions.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 2023-276.
