# Fla. Stat. § 947.21 — Violations of parole.

**Title:** XLVII — CRIMINAL PROCEDURE AND CORRECTIONS
**Chapter:** 947 — FLORIDA COMMISSION ON OFFENDER REVIEW; CONDITIONAL RELEASE; CONTROL RELEASE; PAROLE
**Part:** II — LEASED OR MANAGED WORK PROGRAMS
**Section:** 947.21
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
A violation of the terms of parole may render the parolee liable to arrest and a return to prison to serve out the term for which the parolee was sentenced.

(2)  
An offender whose parole is revoked may, at the discretion of the commission, be credited with any portion of the time the offender has satisfactorily served on parole.
