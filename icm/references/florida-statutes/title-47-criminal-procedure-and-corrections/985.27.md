# Fla. Stat. § 985.27 — Postdisposition detention while awaiting residential commitment placement.

**Title:** XLVII — CRIMINAL PROCEDURE AND CORRECTIONS
**Chapter:** 985 — JUVENILE JUSTICE; INTERSTATE COMPACT ON JUVENILES
**Part:** V — DETENTION
**Section:** 985.27
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The court must place all children who are adjudicated and awaiting placement in a moderate-risk, high-risk, or maximum-risk residential commitment program in secure detention care until the placement or commitment is accomplished.
