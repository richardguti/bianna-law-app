# Fla. Stat. § 913.10 — Number of jurors.

**Title:** XLVII — CRIMINAL PROCEDURE AND CORRECTIONS
**Chapter:** 913 — TRIAL JURY
**Part:** IV — CREDIT COUNSELING SERVICES
**Section:** 913.10
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Twelve persons shall constitute a jury to try all capital cases, and six persons shall constitute a jury to try all other criminal cases.

**History:** s. 191, ch. 19554, 1939; CGL 1940 Supp. 8663(198); s. 87, ch. 70-339.
