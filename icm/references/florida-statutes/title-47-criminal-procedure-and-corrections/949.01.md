# Fla. Stat. § 949.01 — Juvenile matters unaffected.

**Title:** XLVII — CRIMINAL PROCEDURE AND CORRECTIONS
**Chapter:** 948 — PROBATION AND COMMUNITY CONTROL
**Part:** II — LEASED OR MANAGED WORK PROGRAMS
**Section:** 949.01
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Nothing in chapters 947-949 shall be construed to change or modify the law respecting parole and probation as administered by a circuit court exercising juvenile jurisdiction.

**History:** s. 30, ch. 20519, 1941; s. 44, ch. 73-334.
