# Fla. Stat. § 958.06 — Suspension of sentence by court.

**Title:** XLVII — CRIMINAL PROCEDURE AND CORRECTIONS
**Chapter:** 958 — YOUTHFUL OFFENDERS
**Part:** II — LEASED OR MANAGED WORK PROGRAMS
**Section:** 958.06
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The court, upon motion of the defendant, or upon its own motion, may within 60 days after imposition of sentence suspend the further execution of the sentence and place the defendant on probation in a community control program upon such terms as the court may require. The department shall forward to the court, not later than 3 working days prior to the hearing on the motion, all relevant material on the youthful offender's progress while in custody.

**History:** s. 7, ch. 78-84.
