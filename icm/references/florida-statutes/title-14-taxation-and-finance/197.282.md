# Fla. Stat. § 197.282 — Distribution of payments.

**Title:** XIV — TAXATION AND FINANCE
**Chapter:** 197 — TAX COLLECTIONS, SALES, AND LIENS
**Part:** III — ASSESSMENT: PRESUMPTION OF CORRECTNESS
**Section:** 197.282
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

When any deferred taxes, assessments, or interest is collected, the tax collector shall maintain a record of the payment. The tax collector shall distribute payments received in accordance with the procedures for distribution of ad valorem taxes, non-ad valorem assessments, or redemption moneys as prescribed in this chapter.

**History:** s. 9, ch. 77-301; s. 6, ch. 78-161; s. 151, ch. 85-342; s. 22, ch. 2011-151.

**Note:** Former s. 197.0171.
