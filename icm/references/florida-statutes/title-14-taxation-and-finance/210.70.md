# Fla. Stat. § 210.70 — Disposition of funds.

**Title:** XIV — TAXATION AND FINANCE
**Chapter:** 210 — TAX ON TOBACCO PRODUCTS
**Part:** II — TAX ON TOBACCO PRODUCTS OTHER THAN CIGARETTES OR CIGARS
**Section:** 210.70
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

As collections from the taxes imposed under this part are received by the division, it shall pay the same into the General Revenue Fund.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 85-141; s. 20, ch. 86-269; s. 1, ch. 86-286; s. 4, ch. 91-429.
