# Fla. Stat. § 218.79 — Repeal of conflicting laws.

**Title:** XIV — TAXATION AND FINANCE
**Chapter:** 219 — COUNTY PUBLIC MONEY, HANDLING BY STATE AND COUNTY
**Part:** VII — LOCAL GOVERNMENT PROMPT PAYMENT ACT
**Section:** 218.79
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

All laws and parts of laws in conflict with this part are repealed.

**History:** s. 4, ch. 89-297.
