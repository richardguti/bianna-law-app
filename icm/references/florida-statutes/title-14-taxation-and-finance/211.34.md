# Fla. Stat. § 211.34 — Local ordinances not preempted.

**Title:** XIV — TAXATION AND FINANCE
**Chapter:** 211 — TAX ON PRODUCTION OF OIL AND GAS AND SEVERANCE OF SOLID MINERALS
**Part:** II — TAX ON SEVERANCE OF SOLID MINERALS
**Section:** 211.34
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Nothing in this act shall be deemed to preempt local ordinances that impose stricter land reclamation standards.

**History:** s. 5, ch. 75-40.
