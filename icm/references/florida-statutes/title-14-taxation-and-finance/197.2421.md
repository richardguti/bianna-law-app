# Fla. Stat. § 197.2421 — Property tax deferral.

**Title:** XIV — TAXATION AND FINANCE
**Chapter:** 197 — TAX COLLECTIONS, SALES, AND LIENS
**Part:** III — ASSESSMENT: PRESUMPTION OF CORRECTNESS
**Section:** 197.2421
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

——

<SECB pro rata to the taxing authorities in the proportion that each authority's tax levy for the prior tax year bears to the total ad valorem tax levy for the prior tax year; however, a taxing authority which has requested and received an emergency distribution of estimated taxes shall not receive this distribution.
