# Fla. Stat. § 218.412 — Rulemaking authority.

**Title:** XIV — TAXATION AND FINANCE
**Chapter:** 218 — FINANCIAL MATTERS PERTAINING TO POLITICAL SUBDIVISIONS
**Part:** IV — INVESTMENT OF LOCAL GOVERNMENT SURPLUS FUNDS
**Section:** 218.412
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The board may adopt rules as it deems necessary to carry out the provisions of this part for the administration of the trust fund.

**History:** s. 12, ch. 98-47; s. 7, ch. 2008-59.
