# Fla. Stat. § 207.001 — Short title.

**Title:** XIV — TAXATION AND FINANCE
**Chapter:** 206 — MOTOR AND OTHER FUEL TAXES
**Part:** IV — NATURAL GAS FUEL
**Section:** 207.001
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

This chapter shall be known as the “Florida Motor Fuel Use Tax Act,” and the taxes levied under this chapter shall be in addition to all other taxes imposed by law.
