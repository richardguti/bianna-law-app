# Fla. Stat. § 216.0113 — Preferred pricing clauses in state contracts; compliance required.

**Title:** XIV — TAXATION AND FINANCE
**Chapter:** 216 — PLANNING AND BUDGETING
**Part:** II — TAX ON SEVERANCE OF SOLID MINERALS
**Section:** 216.0113
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

——

(1)  
Each state agency, as defined in s. 216.011, shall review its contracts and, for any contract with a preferred-pricing clause, the agency shall ensure that the contractor complies with such clause.

s. 46, ch. 2010-151.
