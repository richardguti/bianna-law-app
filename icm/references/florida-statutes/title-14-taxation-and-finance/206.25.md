# Fla. Stat. § 206.25 — Method for collection of tax cumulative.

**Title:** XIV — TAXATION AND FINANCE
**Chapter:** 206 — MOTOR AND OTHER FUEL TAXES
**Part:** I — MOTOR FUELS
**Section:** 206.25
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The methods and means of effecting and enforcing the collection of fuel taxes as set out in this part shall be in addition to, and not in lieu of, the methods and means of effecting and enforcing collection set out in the fuel tax laws of Florida.

**History:** s. 28, ch. 16082, 1933; CGL 1936 Supp. 1167(87); s. 1, ch. 70-995; s. 36, ch. 95-417.

**Note:** (1)  
The department and its deputies, agents, and employees may make arrests without warrants for any violation of the provisions of this part. Any person arrested for violation of any provision of this part shall be surrendered without delay to the sheriff of the county in which the arrest was made and formal complaint made against him or her, in accordance with law.
