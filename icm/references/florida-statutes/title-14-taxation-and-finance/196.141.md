# Fla. Stat. § 196.141 — Homestead exemptions; duty of property appraiser.

**Title:** XIV — TAXATION AND FINANCE
**Chapter:** 196 — EXEMPTION
**Part:** III — ASSESSMENT: PRESUMPTION OF CORRECTNESS
**Section:** 196.141
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The property appraiser shall examine each claim for exemption filed with or referred to him or her and shall allow the same, if found to be in accordance with law, by marking the same approved and by making the proper deductions on the tax books.

**History:** s. 6, ch. 17060, 1935; CGL 1936 Supp. 897(7); ss. 1, 2, ch. 69-55; s. 1, ch. 77-102; s. 6, ch. 79-332; s. 995, ch. 95-147; s. 38, ch. 98-129; s. 49, ch. 2005-278.

**Note:** (1)  
At the time each taxpayer files claim for homestead exemption, the property appraiser shall deliver to the taxpayer a receipt over his or her signature, or that of a duly authorized deputy, which shall appropriately identify the property covered in the application, shall bear date as of the day such application is received by the property appraiser, and shall include any serial number or other identifying data desired by said property appraiser. The possession of such receipt shall constitute conclusive proof of the timely filing of such application.
