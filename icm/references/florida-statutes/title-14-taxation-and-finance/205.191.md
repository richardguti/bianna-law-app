# Fla. Stat. § 205.191 — Religious tenets; exemption.

**Title:** XIV — TAXATION AND FINANCE
**Chapter:** 205 — LOCAL BUSINESS TAXES
**Part:** II — ADMINISTRATIVE, COLLECTION, AND ENFORCEMENT PROCEDURES
**Section:** 205.191
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

This chapter does not require a business tax receipt for practicing the religious tenets of any church.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 67-433; s. 21, ch. 2006-152.
