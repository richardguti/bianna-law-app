# Fla. Stat. § 207.003 — Privilege tax levied.

**Title:** XIV — TAXATION AND FINANCE
**Chapter:** 207 — MOTOR FUEL USE TAX
**Part:** IV — NATURAL GAS FUEL
**Section:** 207.003
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

——

A tax for the privilege of operating any qualified motor vehicle upon the public highways of this state shall be levied upon every motor carrier at a rate which includes the minimum rates provided in parts I-III of chapter 206 on each gallon of motor fuel used for the propulsion of a qualified motor vehicle by such motor carrier within this state.</spa companies, agencies, or associations, singular or plural.
