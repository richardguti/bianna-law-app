# Fla. Stat. § 215.199 — Audit and Warrant Clearing Trust Fund.

**Title:** XIV — TAXATION AND FINANCE
**Chapter:** 215 — FINANCIAL MATTERS: GENERAL PROVISIONS
**Part:** II — TAX ON SEVERANCE OF SOLID MINERALS
**Section:** 215.199
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
The Audit and Warrant Clearing Trust Fund is created within the Department of Revenue.

(2)  
The fund is established for use as a depository for audit receipts, warrant receipts, and governmental leasehold receipts and for subsequent distributions to appropriate entities and accounts.
