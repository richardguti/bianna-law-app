# Fla. Stat. § 193.132 — Prior assessments validated.

**Title:** XIV — TAXATION AND FINANCE
**Chapter:** 193 — ASSESSMENTS
**Part:** I — GENERAL PROVISIONS
**Section:** 193.132
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

——

Every assessment of taxes heretofore made on property of any kiong as is necessary to provide for the orderly collection of taxes. Such regulations shall also provide for the maintenance of the necessary permanent copies of such rolls.
