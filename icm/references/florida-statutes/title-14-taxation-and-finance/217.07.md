# Fla. Stat. § 217.07 — Transfer of surplus property assets to department.

**Title:** XIV — TAXATION AND FINANCE
**Chapter:** 217 — SURPLUS PROPERTY
**Part:** II — TAX ON SEVERANCE OF SOLID MINERALS
**Section:** 217.07
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The Chief Financial Officer is authorized to transfer to the department any funds unexpended in the Surplus Property Revolving Trust Fund account in the State Treasury.
