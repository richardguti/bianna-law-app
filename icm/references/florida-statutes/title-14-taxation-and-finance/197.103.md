# Fla. Stat. § 197.103 — Deputy tax collectors; appointment.

**Title:** XIV — TAXATION AND FINANCE
**Chapter:** 197 — TAX COLLECTIONS, SALES, AND LIENS
**Part:** III — ASSESSMENT: PRESUMPTION OF CORRECTNESS
**Section:** 197.103
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Tax collectors may appoint deputies to act in their behalf in carrying out the duties prescribed by law.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 80-366; s. 9, ch. 81-284; s. 128, ch. 85-342.

**Note:** Former s. 197.0122.
