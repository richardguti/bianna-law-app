# Fla. Stat. § 210.32 — Account; online system.

**Title:** XIV — TAXATION AND FINANCE
**Chapter:** 210 — TAX ON TOBACCO PRODUCTS
**Part:** II — TAX ON TOBACCO PRODUCTS OTHER THAN CIGARETTES OR CIGARS
**Section:** 210.32
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

A person or an entity licensed or permitted by the division, or applying for a license or a permit, must create and maintain an account with the division's online system and provide an e-mail address to the division to function as the primary means of contact for all communication by the division to the licensee, permittee, or applicant. The Secretary of Business and Professional Regulation may require a distributor who sells tobacco products within the state to remit by electronic funds transfer any tax imposed under s. 210.30 if the taxpayer is subject to the tax and if the total of such taxes the distributor paid in the prior year amounted to $50,000 or more.
