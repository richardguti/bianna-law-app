# Fla. Stat. § 218.20 — Short title.

**Title:** XIV — TAXATION AND FINANCE
**Chapter:** 218 — FINANCIAL MATTERS PERTAINING TO POLITICAL SUBDIVISIONS
**Part:** II — REVENUE SHARING ACT OF 1972
**Section:** 218.20
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

This part shall be known, and may be cited, as the “Florida Revenue Sharing Act of 1972.”

**History:** s. 1, ch. 72-360; s. 1, ch. 73-349; s. 1, ch. 74-194.
