# Fla. Stat. § 206.045 — Licensing period.

**Title:** XIV — TAXATION AND FINANCE
**Chapter:** 206 — MOTOR AND OTHER FUEL TAXES
**Part:** I — MOTOR FUELS
**Section:** 206.045
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Beginning January 1, 1998, the licensing period under this chapter shall be a calendar year, or any part thereof.

**History:** s. 29, ch. 96-323; s. 4, ch. 99-5; s. 13, ch. 2017-36.
