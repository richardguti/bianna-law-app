# Fla. Stat. § 215.321 — Regulatory Trust Fund.

**Title:** XIV — TAXATION AND FINANCE
**Chapter:** 215 — FINANCIAL MATTERS: GENERAL PROVISIONS
**Part:** II — TAX ON SEVERANCE OF SOLID MINERALS
**Section:** 215.321
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

All funds received pursuant to chapter 494, chapter 497, chapter 516, chapter 520, or part I of chapter 559 shall be deposited into the Regulatory Trust Fund.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 72-174; s. 1, ch. 72-222; s. 44, ch. 79-164; s. 121, ch. 81-259; s. 54, ch. 2006-122.
