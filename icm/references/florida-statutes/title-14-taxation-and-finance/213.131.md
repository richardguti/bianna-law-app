# Fla. Stat. § 213.131 — Clerks of the Court Trust Fund within the Department of Revenue.

**Title:** XIV — TAXATION AND FINANCE
**Chapter:** 213 — STATE REVENUE LAWS: GENERAL PROVISIONS
**Part:** II — TAX ON SEVERANCE OF SOLID MINERALS
**Section:** 213.131
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The Clerks of the Court Trust Fund is created within the Department of Revenue.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 2001-121; s. 2, ch. 2003-243; s. 7, ch. 2005-3; s. 14, ch. 2009-204; s. 13, ch. 2013-44.
