# Fla. Stat. § 202.21 — Effective dates; procedures for informing dealers of communications services of tax levies and rate changes.

**Title:** XIV — TAXATION AND FINANCE
**Chapter:** 202 — COMMUNICATIONS SERVICES TAX SIMPLIFICATION LAW
**Part:** II — ADMINISTRATIVE, COLLECTION, AND ENFORCEMENT PROCEDURES
**Section:** 202.21
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

Any adoption, repeal, or change in the rate of a local communications services tax imposed under s. 202.19 is effective with respect to taxable services included on bills that are dated on or after the January 1 subsequent to such adoption, repeal, or change. A municipality or county adopting, repealing, or changing the rate of such tax must notify the department of the adoption, repeal, or change by September 1 immediately preceding such January 1. Notification must be furnished on a form prescribed by the department and must specify the rate of tax; the effective date of the adoption, repeal, or change thereof; and the name, mailing address, and telephone number of a person designated by the municipality or county to respond to inquiries concerning the tax.
