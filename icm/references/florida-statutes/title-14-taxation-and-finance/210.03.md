# Fla. Stat. § 210.03 — Prohibition against levying of cigarette taxes by municipalities.

**Title:** XIV — TAXATION AND FINANCE
**Chapter:** 210 — TAX ON TOBACCO PRODUCTS
**Part:** I — TAX ON CIGARETTES
**Section:** 210.03
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

No municipality shall levy or collect any excise tax on cigarettes.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 26320, 1949; s. 1, ch. 63-486; ss. 16, 35, ch. 69-106; s. 10, ch. 72-360; s. 35, ch. 2019-3.
