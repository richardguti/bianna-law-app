# Fla. Stat. § 218.504 — Cessation of state action.

**Title:** XIV — TAXATION AND FINANCE
**Chapter:** 218 — FINANCIAL MATTERS PERTAINING TO POLITICAL SUBDIVISIONS
**Part:** V — LOCAL GOVERNMENTAL ENTITY AND DISTRICT SCHOOL BOARD FINANCIAL EMERGENCIES
**Section:** 218.504
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The Governor or the Commissioner of Education, as appropriate, has the authority to terminate all state actions pursuant to ss. 218.50-218.504. Cessation of state action must not occur until the Governor or the Commissioner of Education, as appropriate, has determined that:
(1)  
The local governmental entity, charter school, charter technical career center, or district school board:
(a)  
Has established and is operating an effective financial accounting and reporting system.

(b)  
Has resolved the conditions outlined in s. 218.503(1).

(2)  
None of the conditions outlined in s. 218.503(1) exists.

**History:** s. 8, ch. 79-183; s. 28, ch. 96-324; s. 36, ch. 2004-305; s. 6, ch. 218.5031
