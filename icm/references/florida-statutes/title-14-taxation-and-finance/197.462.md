# Fla. Stat. § 197.462 — Transfer of tax certificates held by individuals.

**Title:** XIV — TAXATION AND FINANCE
**Chapter:** 197 — TAX COLLECTIONS, SALES, AND LIENS
**Part:** III — ASSESSMENT: PRESUMPTION OF CORRECTNESS
**Section:** 197.462
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
All tax certificates issued to an individual may be transferred at any time before they are redeemed or a tax deed is executed.

(2)  
The tax collector shall record the transfer on the record of tax certificates sold.

(3)  
The tax collector shall receive $2.25 as a service charge for each transfer.

**History:** s. 182, ch. 85-342; s. 11, ch. 90-343; s. 57, ch. 94-353; s. 43, ch. 2011-151.
