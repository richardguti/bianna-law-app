# Fla. Stat. § 215.01 — Fiscal year.

**Title:** XIV — TAXATION AND FINANCE
**Chapter:** 215 — FINANCIAL MATTERS: GENERAL PROVISIONS
**Part:** II — TAX ON SEVERANCE OF SOLID MINERALS
**Section:** 215.01
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The fiscal year shall begin on July 1 and end on June 30 in each and every year.

**History:** s. 5, ch. 515, 1853; RS 405; GS 597; RGS 1032; s. 1, ch. 10124, 1925; s. 1, ch. 10130, 1925; CGL 1343.
