# Fla. Stat. § 218.50 — Short title.

**Title:** XIV — TAXATION AND FINANCE
**Chapter:** 218 — FINANCIAL MATTERS PERTAINING TO POLITICAL SUBDIVISIONS
**Part:** V — LOCAL GOVERNMENTAL ENTITY AND DISTRICT SCHOOL BOARD FINANCIAL EMERGENCIES
**Section:** 218.50
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

Sections 218.50-218.504 may be cited as the “Local Governmental Entity, Charter School, Charter Technical Career Center, and District School Board Financial Emergencies Act.”

**History:** s. 8, ch. 79-183; s. 32, ch. 2004-305; s. 3, ch. 2006-190; s. 3, ch. 2009-214.
