# Fla. Stat. § 217.001 — Permanent state plan of operation for receipt of federal surplus property.

**Title:** XIV — TAXATION AND FINANCE
**Chapter:** 217 — SURPLUS PROPERTY
**Part:** II — TAX ON SEVERANCE OF SOLID MINERALS
**Section:** 217.001
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The Plan of Operation of the Federal Surplus Property Donation Program, approved by the Governor in Executive Order Number 77-36 and by the General Services Administration on October 17, 1977, is adopted as the permanent state plan of operation.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 83-31.
