# Fla. Stat. § 206.626 — Refunds to ethanol dealers.

**Title:** XIV — TAXATION AND FINANCE
**Chapter:** 206 — MOTOR AND OTHER FUEL TAXES
**Part:** I — MOTOR FUELS
**Section:** 206.626
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Any ethanol dealer who has paid the tax imposed under this chapter on purchases of motor fuel used for denaturing from a duly licensed terminal supplier, importer, or wholesaler is entitled to a refund.

**History:** s. 115, ch. 85-342; s. 68, ch. 95-417.
