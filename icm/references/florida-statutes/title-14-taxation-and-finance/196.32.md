# Fla. Stat. § 196.32 — Executive Office of the Governor; consent required to certain assessments.

**Title:** XIV — TAXATION AND FINANCE
**Chapter:** 196 — EXEMPTION
**Part:** III — ASSESSMENT: PRESUMPTION OF CORRECTNESS
**Section:** 196.32
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

When, under any law of this state heretofore or hereafter enacted providing for the imposition of any tax, provision is made for the payment of any portion of the revenue derived from such tax by any state officer, officers, or board, to defray expenses incident to the enforcement and collection thereof, no such state officer, officers, or board may pay or agree to pay any of such funds without the express authorization and approval of the Executive Office of the Governor.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 21919, 1943; ss. 2, 3, ch. 67-371; ss. 1, 2, ch. 69-55; ss. 31, 35, ch. 69-106; s. 94, ch. 79-190.

**Note:** Former s. 192.51.
