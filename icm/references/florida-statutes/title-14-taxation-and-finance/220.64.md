# Fla. Stat. § 220.64 — Other provisions applicable to franchise tax.

**Title:** XIV — TAXATION AND FINANCE
**Chapter:** 220 — INCOME TAX CODE
**Part:** VII — SPECIAL RULES RELATING TO TAXATION OF BANKS AND SAVINGS ASSOCIATIONS
**Section:** 220.64
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

To the extent that they are not manifestly incompatible with the provisions of this part, parts I, III, IV, V, VI, VIII, IX, and X of this code and ss. 220.12, 220.13, 220.15, and 220.16 apply to the franchise tax imposed by this part. Under rules prescribed in s. 220.131, a consolidated return may be filed by any affiliated group of corporations composed of one or more banks or savings associations, its or their Florida parent corporation, and any nonbank or nonsavings subsidiaries of such parent corporation.
