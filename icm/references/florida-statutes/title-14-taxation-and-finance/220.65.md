# Fla. Stat. § 220.65 — Discharge of tax liability.

**Title:** XIV — TAXATION AND FINANCE
**Chapter:** 220 — INCOME TAX CODE
**Part:** VIII — ADMINISTRATIVE PROCEDURES AND JUDICIAL REVIEW
**Section:** 220.65
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The tax imposed by this part shall be in lieu of, and no bank or savings association shall be subject to, the tax imposed under part II.

**History:** s. 8, ch. 72-278; s. 4, ch. 73-152.
