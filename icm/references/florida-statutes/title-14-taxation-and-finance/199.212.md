# Fla. Stat. § 199.212 — All state agencies to cooperate in administration of law.

**Title:** XIV — TAXATION AND FINANCE
**Chapter:** 199 — INTANGIBLE PERSONAL PROPERTY TAXES
**Part:** II — ADMINISTRATIVE, COLLECTION, AND ENFORCEMENT PROCEDURES
**Section:** 199.212
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The department may call on any state, county, or municipal agency, department, bureau, or board for any information which may, in the department's judgment, assist it in administering this chapter. Such agency, department, bureau, or board shall promptly furnish such information.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 71-134; s. 18, ch. 85-342; s. 15, ch. 98-200.
