# Fla. Stat. § 215.556 — Exemption.

**Title:** XIV — TAXATION AND FINANCE
**Chapter:** 215 — FINANCIAL MATTERS: GENERAL PROVISIONS
**Part:** II — TAX ON SEVERANCE OF SOLID MINERALS
**Section:** 215.556
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The Florida Hurricane Catastrophe Fund created by s. 215.555 is exempt from the deduction required by s. 215.20(1).

**History:** s. 2, ch. 93-409.
