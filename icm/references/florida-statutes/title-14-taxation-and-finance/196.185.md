# Fla. Stat. § 196.185 — Exemption of inventory.

**Title:** XIV — TAXATION AND FINANCE
**Chapter:** 196 — EXEMPTION
**Part:** III — ASSESSMENT: PRESUMPTION OF CORRECTNESS
**Section:** 196.185
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

All items of inventory are exempt from ad valorem taxation.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 81-308.
