# Fla. Stat. § 215.441 — Board of Administration; appointment of executive director.

**Title:** XIV — TAXATION AND FINANCE
**Chapter:** 215 — FINANCIAL MATTERS: GENERAL PROVISIONS
**Part:** II — TAX ON SEVERANCE OF SOLID MINERALS
**Section:** 215.441
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

——

The appointment of the executive director of the State Board of Administration shall be subject to the approval by a majority vote of the Board of Trustees of the State Board of Administration, and the Governor must vote on the prevailing side. Such appointment must be reaffirmed in the same manner by the board of trustees on an annual basis.
