# Fla. Stat. § 198.11 — Appointment of special appraisers.

**Title:** XIV — TAXATION AND FINANCE
**Chapter:** 198 — ESTATE TAXES
**Part:** III — ASSESSMENT: PRESUMPTION OF CORRECTNESS
**Section:** 198.11
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The department may employ special appraisers for the purpose of determining the value of any property which is, or is believed by the department to be, subject to the tax imposed by this chapter, and such special appraisers shall be paid such compensation as said department shall deem proper.

**History:** s. 6, ch. 16015, 1933; CGL 1936 Supp. 1342(86); ss. 21, 35, ch. 69-106.
