# Fla. Stat. § 212.202 — Renaming and continuation of certain funds.

**Title:** XIV — TAXATION AND FINANCE
**Chapter:** 212 — TAX ON SALES, USE, AND OTHER TRANSACTIONS
**Part:** II — TAX ON SEVERANCE OF SOLID MINERALS
**Section:** 212.202
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The Local Government Infrastructure Tax Trust Fund is hereby retitled the Discretionary Sales Surtax Clearing Trust Fund. The Mail Order Sales Tax Clearing Trust Fund is retitled the Communications Services Tax Clearing Trust Fund. Notwithstanding the repeal of s. 212.237 by s. 45, chapter 89-356, the Solid Waste Management Trust Fund shall continue to exist.

**History:** s. 40, ch. 89-356; s. 33, ch. 2001-140.
