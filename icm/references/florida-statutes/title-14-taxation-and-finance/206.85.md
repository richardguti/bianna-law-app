# Fla. Stat. § 206.85 — Purpose.

**Title:** XIV — TAXATION AND FINANCE
**Chapter:** 206 — MOTOR AND OTHER FUEL TAXES
**Part:** II — DIESEL FUELS
**Section:** 206.85
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The tax imposed by this part II of this chapter is levied for the purpose of providing revenue to be used by this state to defray in whole or in part the cost of constructing, widening, reconstructing, maintaining, resurfacing, and repairing the public highways of this state and the cost and expense incurred in the administration and enforcement of this part and for no other purpose whatsoever.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 26718, 1951; s. 1, ch. 70-995.

**Note:** Former s. 209.001.
