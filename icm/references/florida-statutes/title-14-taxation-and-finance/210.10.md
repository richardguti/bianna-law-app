# Fla. Stat. § 210.10 — General powers of the Division of Alcoholic Beverages and Tobacco.

**Title:** XIV — TAXATION AND FINANCE
**Chapter:** 210 — TAX ON TOBACCO PRODUCTS

**Section:** 210.10

The Division of Alcoholic Beverages and Tobacco has authority to adopt rules pursuant to ss. 120.536(1) and 120.54 to implement the provisions of this part.
