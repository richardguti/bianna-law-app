# Fla. Stat. § 217.12 — Departmental authority relative to additional restrictions.

**Title:** XIV — TAXATION AND FINANCE
**Chapter:** 217 — SURPLUS PROPERTY
**Part:** II — TAX ON SEVERANCE OF SOLID MINERALS
**Section:** 217.12
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The department, when deemed necessary, may impose additional state restrictions on any equipment or surplus property other than the federal restrictions imposed under the Federal Property and Administrative Services Act of 1949.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 65-173; ss. 22, 35, ch. 69-106.
