# Fla. Stat. § 211.027 — Exemptions.

**Title:** XIV — TAXATION AND FINANCE
**Chapter:** 211 — TAX ON PRODUCTION OF OIL AND GAS AND SEVERANCE OF SOLID MINERALS
**Part:** I — TAX ON PRODUCTION OF OIL AND GAS
**Section:** 211.027
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

——

The following on-shore production is not subject to any tax imposed under this part:* a result which is reasonably consistent with the result which would have been obtained had the index not been substantially revised or the base year changed.
