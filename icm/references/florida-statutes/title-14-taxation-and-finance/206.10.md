# Fla. Stat. § 206.10 — Reports to be filed whether taxes due or not.

**Title:** XIV — TAXATION AND FINANCE
**Chapter:** 206 — MOTOR AND OTHER FUEL TAXES
**Part:** I — MOTOR FUELS
**Section:** 206.10
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

All statements or reports required by part I or part II of this chapter and the fuel tax laws of this state to be made to the department monthly shall be filed each month, regardless of whether or not a fuel tax is due under the provisions of the laws of Florida.

**History:** s. 17, ch. 16082, 1933; CGL 1936 Supp. 1167(78); s. 7, ch. 63-253; s. 5, ch. 65-371; s. 2, ch. 65-420; ss. 21, 35, ch. 69-106; s. 1, ch. 70-995; s. 91, ch. 81-259; s. 19, ch. 95-417.

**Note:** Former s. 207.14.
