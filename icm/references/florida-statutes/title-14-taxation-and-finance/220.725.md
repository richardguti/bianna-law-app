# Fla. Stat. § 220.725 — Overpayments; refunds.

**Title:** XIV — TAXATION AND FINANCE
**Chapter:** 220 — INCOME TAX CODE
**Part:** VIII — ADMINISTRATIVE PROCEDURES AND JUDICIAL REVIEW
**Section:** 220.725
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
Every claim for refund shall be filed with the department in writing, in such form as the department may by regulation prescribe, and shall state the amount claimed, the specific grounds upon which the claim is founded, and the taxable years or periods involved.

(2)  
As soon as practicable after a claim for refund is filed, the department shall examine the claim and either issue a notice of refund, abatement, or credit to the claimant or issue a notice of denial.

**History:** s. 19, ch. 71-359; s. 54, ch. 78-95; s. 54, ch. 91-112.

**Note:** 220.807 upon any overpayment of a tax imposed by this chapter. However, if any overpayment is refunded or credited within 3 months after the date upon which the taxpayer files written notice advising the department of such overpayment, no interest shall be allowed on such overpayment.
