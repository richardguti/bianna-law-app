# Fla. Stat. § 206.945 — Settlement or compromise of tax, penalty, or interest.

**Title:** XIV — TAXATION AND FINANCE
**Chapter:** 206 — MOTOR AND OTHER FUEL TAXES
**Part:** II — DIESEL FUELS
**Section:** 206.945
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The department may settle or compromise tax, penalty, or interest imposed by this part pursuant to s. 213.21.

**History:** s. 8, ch. 81-178; s. 65, ch. 83-217; s. 94, ch. 90-136; s. 6, ch. 90-351.
