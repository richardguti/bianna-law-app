# Fla. Stat. § 197.312 — Payment by mortgagee.

**Title:** XIV — TAXATION AND FINANCE
**Chapter:** 197 — TAX COLLECTIONS, SALES, AND LIENS
**Part:** III — ASSESSMENT: PRESUMPTION OF CORRECTNESS
**Section:** 197.312
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

If any mortgagee elects to pay the taxes when an applicant qualifies for tax deferral, such election does not give the mortgagee the right to foreclose.

**History:** s. 12, ch. 77-301; s. 154, ch. 85-342; s. 25, ch. 2011-151.

**Note:** Former s. 197.0174.
