# Fla. Stat. § 200.081 — Millage limitation; municipalities.

**Title:** XIV — TAXATION AND FINANCE
**Chapter:** 200 — DETERMINATION OF MILLAGE
**Part:** II — ADMINISTRATIVE, COLLECTION, AND ENFORCEMENT PROCEDURES
**Section:** 200.081
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

No municipality shall levy ad valorem taxes against real property and tangible personal property in excess of 10 mills, except for voted levies.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 67-396; ss. 1, 2, ch. 69-55; s. 17, ch. 82-154.

**Note:** Former s. 167.441.
