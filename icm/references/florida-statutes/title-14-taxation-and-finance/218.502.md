# Fla. Stat. § 218.502 — Definition.

**Title:** XIV — TAXATION AND FINANCE
**Chapter:** 218 — FINANCIAL MATTERS PERTAINING TO POLITICAL SUBDIVISIONS
**Part:** V — LOCAL GOVERNMENTAL ENTITY AND DISTRICT SCHOOL BOARD FINANCIAL EMERGENCIES
**Section:** 218.502
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

As used in ss. 218.50-218.504, the term “local governmental entity” means a county, municipality, or special district.

**History:** s. 8, ch. 79-183; s. 26, ch. 96-324; s. 24, ch. 99-333; s. 34, ch. 2004-305.
