# Fla. Stat. § 199.155 — Valuation.

**Title:** XIV — TAXATION AND FINANCE
**Chapter:** 199 — INTANGIBLE PERSONAL PROPERTY TAXES
**Part:** I — NONRECURRING TAX
**Section:** 199.155
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Subject to the provisions of s. 199.133(2), all notes, bonds, and other obligations for payment of money subject to the nonrecurring tax shall be valued at the principal amount of indebtedness evidenced by such obligation.
