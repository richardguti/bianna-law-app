# Fla. Stat. § 193.024 — Deputy property appraisers.

**Title:** XIV — TAXATION AND FINANCE
**Chapter:** 193 — ASSESSMENTS
**Part:** I — GENERAL PROVISIONS
**Section:** 193.024
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Property appraisers may appoint deputies to act in their behalf in carrying out the duties prescribed by law.

**History:** s. 2, ch. 80-366.
