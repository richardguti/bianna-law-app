# Fla. Stat. § 11.021 — Evidence of publication of notice.

**Title:** III — LEGISLATIVE BRANCH; COMMISSIONS
**Chapter:** 11 — LEGISLATIVE ORGANIZATION, PROCEDURES, AND STAFFING
**Section:** 11.021
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The evidence that such notice has been published shall be established in the Legislature before such bill shall be passed, and such evidence shall be filed or preserved with the bill in the Department of State in such manner as the Legislature shall provide.

**History:** Former s. 21, Art. III of the State Constitution of 1885, as amended; converted to statutory law by s. 10, Art. XII of the State Constitution as revised in 1968; ss. 10, 35, ch. 69-106; s. 3, ch. 96-318.
