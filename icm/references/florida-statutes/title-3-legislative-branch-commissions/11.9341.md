# Fla. Stat. § 11.9341 — Duties of alternate delegates.

**Title:** III — LEGISLATIVE BRANCH; COMMISSIONS
**Chapter:** 11 — LEGISLATIVE ORGANIZATION, PROCEDURES, AND STAFFING
**Section:** 11.9341
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

An alternate delegate:
(1)  
Shall act in the place of the paired delegate when the paired delegate is absent from the Article V convention.

(2)  
Replaces the paired delegate if the alternate delegate's paired delegate vacates the office.

**History:** s. 13, ch. 2014-52.
