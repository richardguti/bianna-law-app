# Fla. Stat. § 11.9336 — Oath.

**Title:** III — LEGISLATIVE BRANCH; COMMISSIONS
**Chapter:** 11 — LEGISLATIVE ORGANIZATION, PROCEDURES, AND STAFFING
**Section:** 11.9336
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Each delegate and alternate delegate shall, before exercising any function of the position, execute an oath in the state and in writing that the delegate or alternate delegate will:
(1)  
Support the Constitution of the United States and the State Constitution.

(2)  
Faithfully abide by and execute any instructions to delegates and alternate delegates adopted by the Legislature.

Reimbursement of per diem and travel expenses.
——

A delegate or alternate delegate shall serve without compensation but may be reimbursed for per diem and travel expenses pursuant to s. 112.061.
