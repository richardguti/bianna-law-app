# Fla. Stat. § 10.209 — Applicability.

**Title:** III — LEGISLATIVE BRANCH; COMMISSIONS
**Chapter:** 10 — SENATE AND HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES
**Section:** 10.209
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

This joint resolution applies with respect to the qualification, nomination, and election of members of the Legislature in the primary and general elections held in 2022 and thereafter.

**History:** s. 9, CS for SJR 100, 2022 Regular Session.
