# Fla. Stat. § 11.0451 — Requirements for reinstitution of lobbyist registration after felony conviction.

**Title:** III — LEGISLATIVE BRANCH; COMMISSIONS
**Chapter:** 11 — LEGISLATIVE ORGANIZATION, PROCEDURES, AND STAFFING
**Section:** 11.0451
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

A person convicted of a felony after January 1, 2006, may not be registered as a lobbyist pursuant to s. 11.045 until the person:
(1)  
Has been released from incarceration and any postconviction supervision, and has paid all court costs and court-ordered restitution; and

(2)  
Has had his or her civil rights restored.

**History:** s. 9, ch. 2005-359; s. 1, ch. 2007-5.
