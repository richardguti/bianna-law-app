# Fla. Stat. § 11.2421 — Florida Statutes 2026 adopted.

**Title:** III — LEGISLATIVE BRANCH; COMMISSIONS
**Chapter:** 11 — LEGISLATIVE ORGANIZATION, PROCEDURES, AND STAFFING
**Section:** 11.2421
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The accompanying revision, consolidation, and compilation of the public statutes of 2025 of a general and permanent nature, excepting tables, rules, indexes, and other related matter contained therein, prepared by the Office of Legislative Services under the provisions of s. 11.242, together with corrections, changes, and amendments to and repeals of provisions of Florida Statutes 2025 enacted in additional reviser's bill or bills by the 2026 Legislature, is adopted and enacted as the official statute law of the state under the title of “Florida Statutes 2026” and shall take effect immediately upon publication. Said statutes may be cited as “Florida Statutes 2026,” “Florida Statutes,” or “F.S. 2026.”
