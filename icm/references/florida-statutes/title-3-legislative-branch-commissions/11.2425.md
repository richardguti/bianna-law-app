# Fla. Stat. § 11.2425 — Rights reserved under repealed statutes.

**Title:** III — LEGISLATIVE BRANCH; COMMISSIONS
**Chapter:** 11 — LEGISLATIVE ORGANIZATION, PROCEDURES, AND STAFFING
**Section:** 11.2425
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The repeal of any statute by the adoption and enactment of Florida Statutes 2026, by s. 11.2421, as amended, shall not affect any right accrued before such repeal or any civil remedy where a suit is pending.
