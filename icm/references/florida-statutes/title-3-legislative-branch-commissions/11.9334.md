# Fla. Stat. § 11.9334 — Method of appointment and recall.

**Title:** III — LEGISLATIVE BRANCH; COMMISSIONS
**Chapter:** 11 — LEGISLATIVE ORGANIZATION, PROCEDURES, AND STAFFING
**Section:** 11.9334
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The Legislature shall appoint or recall delegates or alternate delegates by concurrent resolution.

**History:** s. 8, ch. 2014-52.
