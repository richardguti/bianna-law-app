# Fla. Stat. § 11.241 — Permanent statutory revision plan created.

**Title:** III — LEGISLATIVE BRANCH; COMMISSIONS
**Chapter:** 11 — LEGISLATIVE ORGANIZATION, PROCEDURES, AND STAFFING
**Section:** 11.241
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

There is created a permanent statutory revision plan to be implemented and maintained under the supervision of the Office of Legislative Services.

**History:** s. 2, ch. 67-472; s. 13, ch. 68-35; ss. 29, 30, ch. 69-52; s. 6, ch. 72-178; s. 13, ch. 98-136.
