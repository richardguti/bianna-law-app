# Fla. Stat. § 11.933 — Qualifications of delegates and alternate delegates.

**Title:** III — LEGISLATIVE BRANCH; COMMISSIONS
**Chapter:** 11 — LEGISLATIVE ORGANIZATION, PROCEDURES, AND STAFFING
**Section:** 11.933
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
To be appointed as a delegate or alternate delegate to an Article V convention, a person must:
(a)  
Reside in this state.

(b)  
Be a registered voter in this state.

(c)  
Not be registered or required to be registered as a lobbyist under the laws of this state.

(2)  
A person may not be appointed as a delegate i932  
Definitions.
——

As used in ss. 11.93-11.9352, the term:
(1)  
“Alternate delegate” means an individual who is appointed as an alternate delegate as provided by law.

(2)  
“Article V convention” means a convention called for by the states under Article V of the Constitution of the United States for the purpose of proposing amendments to the Constitution of the United States.

(3)  
“Chamber” means either the Senate or the House of Representatives.

(4)  
“Delegate” means an individual appointed to represent Florida at an Article V convention.

(5)  
“Paired delegate” means the delegate with whom an alternate delegate is paired.

**History:** s. 3, ch. 2014-52.
