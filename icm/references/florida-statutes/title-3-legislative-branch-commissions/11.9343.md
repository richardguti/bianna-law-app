# Fla. Stat. § 11.9343 — Vote cast outside the scope of instructions or limits; appointment forfeited.

**Title:** III — LEGISLATIVE BRANCH; COMMISSIONS
**Chapter:** 11 — LEGISLATIVE ORGANIZATION, PROCEDURES, AND STAFFING
**Section:** 11.9343
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
A delegate or alternate delegate forfeits his or her appointment by virtue of a vote or attempt to vote that is outside the scope of:
(a)  
The instructions established by a concurrent resolution adopted pursuant to ss. 11.93-11.9352; or

(b)  
The limits placed by the Legislature in a concurrent resolution or memorial that calls for an Article V convention for the purpose of proposing one or more amendments to the Constitution of the United States on the subjects and amendments that may be considered by the Article V convention.

(2)  
If a delegate forfeits an appointment under subsection (1), the paired alternate delegate of the delegate becomes the delegate at the time the forfeiture of the appointment occurs.

**History:** s. 15, ch. 2014-52.
