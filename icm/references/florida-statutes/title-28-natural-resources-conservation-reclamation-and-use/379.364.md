# Fla. Stat. § 379.364 — License required for fur and hide dealers.

**Title:** XXVIII — NATURAL RESOURCES; CONSERVATION, RECLAMATION, AND USE
**Chapter:** 379 — FISH AND WILDLIFE CONSERVATION
**Part:** VII — NONRECREATIONAL LICENSES
**Section:** 379.364
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

——

(1)  
A person may not engage in the business of a dealer or buyer in green or dried alligator hides or green or dried furs in the state or purchase such hides or furs within the state until the person has been licensed as provided in this section.

(2)  
A person who solicits business through the mail, or by advertising, or who travels to buy or employs or has other agents or buyers, shall be deemed a dealer.
