# Fla. Stat. § 379.33 — Enforcement of commission rules.

**Title:** XXVIII — NATURAL RESOURCES; CONSERVATION, RECLAMATION, AND USE
**Chapter:** 379 — FISH AND WILDLIFE CONSERVATION
**Part:** V — LAW ENFORCEMENT
**Section:** 379.33
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Rules of the commission shall be enforced by any law enforcement officer certified pursuant to s. 943.13.

**History:** ss. 2, 5, ch. 83-134; ss. 3, 17, ch. 85-234; ss. 16, 17, 18, ch. 93-213; ss. 1, 2, 3, ch. 94-247; s. 8, ch. 98-203; s. 96, ch. 99-245; s. 3, ch. 2006-304; s. 113, ch. 2008-247; s. 7, ch. 2016-107.

**Note:** Former s. 370.028.
