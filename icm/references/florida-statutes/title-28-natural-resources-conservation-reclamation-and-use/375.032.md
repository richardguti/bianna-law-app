# Fla. Stat. § 375.032 — Recreation; required purpose for purchase.

**Title:** XXVIII — NATURAL RESOURCES; CONSERVATION, RECLAMATION, AND USE
**Chapter:** 375 — OUTDOOR RECREATION AND CONSERVATION LANDS
**Part:** II — FLORIDA INLAND NAVIGATION DISTRICT LAW
**Section:** 375.032
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

No land shall be purchased under this act or any funds expended for any project unless a finding is made that recreation is the prime purpose of the purchase or of the project.

**History:** s. 6, ch. 67-351.
