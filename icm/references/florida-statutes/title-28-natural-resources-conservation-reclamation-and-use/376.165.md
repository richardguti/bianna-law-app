# Fla. Stat. § 376.165 — “Hold-harmless” agreements prohibited.

**Title:** XXVIII — NATURAL RESOURCES; CONSERVATION, RECLAMATION, AND USE
**Chapter:** 376 — POLLUTANT DISCHARGE PREVENTION AND REMOVAL
**Part:** II — FLORIDA INLAND NAVIGATION DISTRICT LAW
**Section:** 376.165
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

——

Any agreement entered into after July 1, 1974, to “hold-harmless” a vessel or terminal facility from liability for the occurrence of a discharge prohibited by ss. 376.011-7eeding, $15,000 for each subsequent discharge of pollutants other than gasoline or diesel within a 12-month period.
