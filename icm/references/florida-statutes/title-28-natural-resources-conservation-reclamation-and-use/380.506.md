# Fla. Stat. § 380.506 — Support services.

**Title:** XXVIII — NATURAL RESOURCES; CONSERVATION, RECLAMATION, AND USE
**Chapter:** 380 — LAND AND WATER MANAGEMENT
**Part:** III — FLORIDA COMMUNITIES TRUST
**Section:** 380.506
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The department shall furnish administrative, personnel, and other support services necessary for the trust to accomplish the purposes of this part.

**History:** sF.S. 380.5056380.505
