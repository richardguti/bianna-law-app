# Fla. Stat. § 379.4115 — Florida or wild panther; killing prohibited; penalty.

**Title:** XXVIII — NATURAL RESOURCES; CONSERVATION, RECLAMATION, AND USE
**Chapter:** 379 — FISH AND WILDLIFE CONSERVATION
**Part:** VIII — PENALTIES
**Section:** 379.4115
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
It is unlawful for a person to kill a member of the Florida “endangered species,” as defined in s. 379.2291(3), known as the Florida panther (Felis concolor coryi
).

(2)  
It is unlawful for a person to kill any member of the species of panther (Felis concolor
) occurring in the wild.

(3)  
A person who violates this section commits a Level Four violation under s. 379.401.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 78-173; s. 1, ch. 84-99; s. 179, ch. 2008-247; s. 24, ch. 2016-107.

**Note:** Former s. 372.671.
