# Fla. Stat. § 379.356 — Fish pond license.

**Title:** XXVIII — NATURAL RESOURCES; CONSERVATION, RECLAMATION, AND USE
**Chapter:** 379 — FISH AND WILDLIFE CONSERVATION
**Part:** VI — LICENSES FOR RECREATIONAL ACTIVITIES
**Section:** 379.356
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The owner of a fish pond of more than 20 acres which is located entirely within her or his property may obtain a license from the commission for such pond at a fee of $3 per surface acre, and no fishing license shall be required of any person fishing in such licensed pond.

**History:** s. 4, ch. 85-235; s. 575, ch. 95-148; s. 141, ch. 2008-247.

**Note:** Former s. 372.5705.
