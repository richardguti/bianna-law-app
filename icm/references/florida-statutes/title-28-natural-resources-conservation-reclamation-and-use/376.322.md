# Fla. Stat. § 376.322 — Powers and duties of the department.

**Title:** XXVIII — NATURAL RESOURCES; CONSERVATION, RECLAMATION, AND USE
**Chapter:** 376 — POLLUTANT DISCHARGE PREVENTION AND REMOVAL
**Part:** II — FLORIDA INLAND NAVIGATION DISTRICT LAW
**Section:** 376.322
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

——

The department shall have the power and duty to:
(1)  
Contract with local governments as needed to perform any of its duties under ss. 376.320-376.326.

(2)  
Establish a program to register tanks subject to the provisions of ss. 376.320-376.326.

(3)  
Adopt rules to implement ss. 376.320-376.326.

; s. 304, ch. 94-356.
