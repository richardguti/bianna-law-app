# Fla. Stat. § 379.35 — Review of fees for licenses and permits; review of exemptions.

**Title:** XXVIII — NATURAL RESOURCES; CONSERVATION, RECLAMATION, AND USE
**Chapter:** 379 — FISH AND WILDLIFE CONSERVATION
**Part:** VI — LICENSES FOR RECREATIONAL ACTIVITIES
**Section:** 379.35
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The fees for licenses and permits established under this chapter, and exemptions thereto, shall be reviewed by the Legislature during its regular session every 5 years beginning in 2000.

**History:** s. 15, ch. 99-353; s. 130, ch. 2008-247.

**Note:** Former s. 372.5711.
