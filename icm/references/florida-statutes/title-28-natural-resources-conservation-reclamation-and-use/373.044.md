# Fla. Stat. § 373.044 — Rules; enforcement; availability of personnel rules.

**Title:** XXVIII — NATURAL RESOURCES; CONSERVATION, RECLAMATION, AND USE
**Chapter:** 373 — WATER RESOURCES
**Part:** I — STATE WATER RESOURCE PLAN
**Section:** 373.044
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The governing board of the district is authorized to adopt rules pursuant to ss. F.S. 373.0436373.043  
Adoption and enforcement of rules by the department.
——

The department has authority to adopt rules pursuant to ss. 120.536(1) and 120.54 to implement the provisions of this chapter.

**History:** s. 8, part I, ch. 72-299; s. 5, ch. 74-114; s. 81, ch. 98-200.
