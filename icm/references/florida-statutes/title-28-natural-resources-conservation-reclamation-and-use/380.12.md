# Fla. Stat. § 380.12 — Rights unaffected by ch. 75-22.

**Title:** XXVIII — NATURAL RESOURCES; CONSERVATION, RECLAMATION, AND USE
**Chapter:** 380 — LAND AND WATER MANAGEMENT
**Part:** II — COASTAL PLANNING AND MANAGEMENT
**Section:** 380.12
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Nothing in chapter 75-22, Laws of Florida, shall alter or affect rights previously vested under this chapter.

**History:** s. 23, ch. 75-22.
