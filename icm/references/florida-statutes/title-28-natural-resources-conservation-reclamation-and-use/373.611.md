# Fla. Stat. § 373.611 — Modification or limitation of remedy.

**Title:** XXVIII — NATURAL RESOURCES; CONSERVATION, RECLAMATION, AND USE
**Chapter:** 373 — WATER RESOURCES
**Part:** VI — MISCELLANEOUS PROVISIONS
**Section:** 373.611
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

In order to promoteF.S. 373.6106373.610  
Defaulting contractors.
——

The district may suspend a contractor on a temporary or permanent basis from doing work with the district if such contractor has materially breached its contract with the district. The district shall adopt rules to administer the provisions of this section to specify the circumstances and conditions that constitute a materially breached contract and conditions that constitute the period for temporary or permanent suspension and for reinstatement.

**History:** s. 5, ch. 2001-256; s. 32, ch. 2002-207.

**Note:** Former s. 373.211.
