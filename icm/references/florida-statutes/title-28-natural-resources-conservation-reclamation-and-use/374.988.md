# Fla. Stat. § 374.988 — Preservation of taxing authority.

**Title:** XXVIII — NATURAL RESOURCES; CONSERVATION, RECLAMATION, AND USE
**Chapter:** 374 — NAVIGATION DISTRICTS; WATERWAYS DEVELOPMENT
**Part:** II — FLORIDA INLAND NAVIGATION DISTRICT LAW
**Section:** 374.988
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The enactment of this act shall not affect the taxing authority granted to the district or any contract or obligation of such district entered into prior to July 1, 1996. The district shall continue to exercise the taxing power previously authorized by the Laws of Florida, until such power shall be specifically revoked or modified by the Legislature.

**History:** s. 2, ch. 96-425.
