# Fla. Stat. § 373.566

**Title:** XXVIII — NATURAL RESOURCES; CONSERVATION, RECLAMATION, AND USE
**Chapter:** 373 — WATER RESOURCES
**Part:** V — FINANCE AND TAXATION
**Section:** 373.566
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

373.566  
All bonds and coupons not paid at maturity shall bear interest at a rate not to exceed 7.5 percent per annum from maturity until paid, or until sufficient funds have been deposited at the place of payment.
