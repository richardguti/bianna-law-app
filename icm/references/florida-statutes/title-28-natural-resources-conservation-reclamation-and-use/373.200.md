# Fla. Stat. § 373.200 — Seminole Tribe Water Rights Compact.

**Title:** XXVIII — NATURAL RESOURCES; CONSERVATION, RECLAMATION, AND USE
**Chapter:** 373 — WATER RESOURCES
**Part:** I — STATE WATER RESOURCE PLAN
**Section:** 373.200
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Pursuant to the provisions of s. 285.165, the South Florida Water Management District is authorized to act in accordance with the Seminole Tribe Water Rights Compact incorporated by reference therein.

**History:** s. 6, ch. 2000-133.
