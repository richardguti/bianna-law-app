# Fla. Stat. § 377.31 — Evidence of rules and orders.

**Title:** XXVIII — NATURAL RESOURCES; CONSERVATION, RECLAMATION, AND USE
**Chapter:** 377 — ENERGY RESOURCES
**Part:** I — REGULATION OF OIL AND GAS RESOURCES
**Section:** 377.31
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

A certified copy of any division rule, regulation, or order shall be received in evidence in all courts of this state with the same effect as the original thereof.

**History:** s. 25, ch. 22819, 1945; ss. 25, 35, ch. 69-106; s. 23, ch. 78-95.
