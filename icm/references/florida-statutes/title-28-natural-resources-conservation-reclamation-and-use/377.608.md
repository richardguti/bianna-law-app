# Fla. Stat. § 377.608 — Prosecution of cases by state attorney.

**Title:** XXVIII — NATURAL RESOURCES; CONSERVATION, RECLAMATION, AND USE
**Chapter:** 377 — ENERGY RESOURCES
**Part:** II — PLANNING AND DEVELOPMENT
**Section:** 377.608
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

F.S. 377.6076377.607  
Violations; penalties.
——

Any person who willfully fails to submit information as required by ss. 377.601-377.608, or submits false information, is guilty of a misdemeanor of the first degree, punishable as provided in ss. 775.082 and 775.083.

**History:** s. 9, ch. 74-186; s. 2, ch. 78-25; s. 52, ch. 91-221.
