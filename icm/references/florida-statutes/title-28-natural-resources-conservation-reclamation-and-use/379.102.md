# Fla. Stat. § 379.102 — Fish and Wildlife Conservation Commission.

**Title:** XXVIII — NATURAL RESOURCES; CONSERVATION, RECLAMATION, AND USE
**Chapter:** 379 — FISH AND WILDLIFE CONSERVATION
**Part:** I — GENERAL PROVISIONS
**Section:** 379.102
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

——

(1)  
The Fish and Wildlife Conservation Commission shall consist of seven members who shall be appointed by the Governor, subject to confirmation by the Senate, for staggered terms of 5 years.

(2)  
Members so appointed shall annually select one of their members as chair.
