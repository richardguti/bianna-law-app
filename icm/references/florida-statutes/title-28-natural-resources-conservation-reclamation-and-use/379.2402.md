# Fla. Stat. § 379.2402 — Marine information system.

**Title:** XXVIII — NATURAL RESOURCES; CONSERVATION, RECLAMATION, AND USE
**Chapter:** 379 — FISH AND WILDLIFE CONSERVATION
**Part:** II — MARINE LIFE
**Section:** 379.2402
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The Fish and Wildlife Conservation Commission shall establish a marine information system in conjunction with the licensing program to gather marine fisheries data.

**History:** s. 6, ch. 89-270; s. 211, ch. 94-356; s. 239, ch. 99-245; s. 64, ch. 2008-247; s. 199 decisions shall be fair and equitable to all the people of this state and carried out in such a manner that no individual, corporation, or entity acquires an excessive share of such privileges.
