# Fla. Stat. § 379.2511 — Lease of state-owned water bottoms for growing oysters and clams.

**Title:** XXVIII — NATURAL RESOURCES; CONSERVATION, RECLAMATION, AND USE
**Chapter:** 379 — FISH AND WILDLIFE CONSERVATION
**Part:** II — MARINE LIFE
**Section:** 379.2511
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Effective July 1, 1988, persons wishing to lease state-owned water bottoms for the purpose of growing oysters and clams shall no longer be required to apply under the provisions of s. 379.2525; such leases shall be issued pursuant to the provisions of ss. 253.67-253.75.

**History:** s.F.S. 379.256379.25

**Note:** Former s. 370.23.
