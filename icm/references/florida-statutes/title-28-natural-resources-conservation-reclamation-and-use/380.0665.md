# Fla. Stat. § 380.0665 — Executive director; agents and employees.

**Title:** XXVIII — NATURAL RESOURCES; CONSERVATION, RECLAMATION, AND USE
**Chapter:** 380 — LAND AND WATER MANAGEMENT
**Part:** I — ENVIRONMENTAL LAND AND WATER MANAGEMENT
**Section:** 380.0665
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The appointment and removal of an executive director shall be by the members of the land authority. The executive director shall subsequently employ legal and technical experts and such other agents and employees, permanent and temporary, as the land authority may require.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 86-170; s. 643, ch. 95-148.
