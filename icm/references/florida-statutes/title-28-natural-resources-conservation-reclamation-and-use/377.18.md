# Fla. Stat. § 377.18 — Common sources of oil and gas.

**Title:** XXVIII — NATURAL RESOURCES; CONSERVATION, RECLAMATION, AND USE
**Chapter:** 377 — ENERGY RESOURCES
**Part:** I — REGULATION OF OIL AND GAS RESOURCES
**Section:** 377.18
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

All common sources of supply of oil or native gas shall have the production controlled or regulated in accordance with the provisions of this law.

**History:** s. 13, ch. 22819, 1945; s. 6, ch. 2013-205.
