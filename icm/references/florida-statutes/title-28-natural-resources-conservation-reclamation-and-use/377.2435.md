# Fla. Stat. § 377.2435 — Rule adoption relating to natural gas storage.

**Title:** XXVIII — NATURAL RESOURCES; CONSERVATION, RECLAMATION, AND USE
**Chapter:** 377 — ENERGY RESOURCES
**Part:** I — REGULATION OF OIL AND GAS RESOURCES
**Section:** 377.2435
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The Department of Environmental Protection shall adopt rules relating to natural gas storage before issuing a natural gas storage facility permit.

**History:** s. 25, ch. 2013-205.
