# Fla. Stat. § 380.515 — Construction.

**Title:** XXVIII — NATURAL RESOURCES; CONSERVATION, RECLAMATION, AND USE
**Chapter:** 380 — LAND AND WATER MANAGEMENT
**Part:** III — FLORIDA COMMUNITIES TRUST
**Section:** 380.515
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The provisions of this part shall be liberally construed in a manner to accomplish its purposes.

**History:** s. 28, ch. 89-175; s. 2, ch. 90-192; s. 18, ch. 91-192; s. 5, ch. 91-429.
