# Fla. Stat. § 373.614 — Unlawful damage to district property or works; penalty.

**Title:** XXVIII — NATURAL RESOURCES; CONSERVATION, RECLAMATION, AND USE
**Chapter:** 373 — WATER RESOURCES
**Part:** VI — MISCELLANEOUS PROVISIONS
**Section:** 373.614
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The governing board of the district shall have the power, and is authorized, to offer and pay rewards of up to $1,000 to any person furnishing information leading to the arrest and conviction of any person who has committed an unlawful act or acts upon the rights-of-way, land, or land interests of the district or has destroyed or damaged district properties or works.

**History:** s. 18, ch. 57-380; s. 325, ch. 71-136; s. 25, ch. 73-190.

**Note:** Former s. 373.241.
