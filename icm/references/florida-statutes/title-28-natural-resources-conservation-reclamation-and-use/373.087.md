# Fla. Stat. § 373.087 — District works using aquifer for storage and supply.

**Title:** XXVIII — NATURAL RESOURCES; CONSERVATION, RECLAMATION, AND USE
**Chapter:** 373 — WATER RESOURCES
**Part:** I — STATE WATER RESOURCE PLAN
**Section:** 373.087
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The governing board may establish works of the district for the purpose of introducing water into, or drawing water from, the underlying aquifer for storage or supply. However, only water of a compatible quality shall be introduced directly into such aquifer.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 7A5oryTitle">History.

**Note:** Former s. 378.16.
