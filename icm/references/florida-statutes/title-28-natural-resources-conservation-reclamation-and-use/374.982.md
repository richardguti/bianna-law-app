# Fla. Stat. § 374.982 — District.

**Title:** XXVIII — NATURAL RESOURCES; CONSERVATION, RECLAMATION, AND USE
**Chapter:** 374 — NAVIGATION DISTRICTS; WATERWAYS DEVELOPMENT
**Part:** II — FLORIDA INLAND NAVIGATION DISTRICT LAW
**Section:** 374.982
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

An independent special taxing district to be known as the “Florida Inland Navigation District” is hereby created. The territorial boundaries of the district shall be the counties of Nassau, Duval, St. Johns, Flagler, Volusia, Brevard, St. Lucie, Martin, Indian River, Palm Beach, Broward, and Miami-Dade. For purposes of this chapter, the term “district” means the Florida Inland Navigation District and the term “board” means the Board of Commissioners of the Florida Inland Navigation District.

**History:** s. 2, ch. 96-425; s. 1, ch. 2004-15.
