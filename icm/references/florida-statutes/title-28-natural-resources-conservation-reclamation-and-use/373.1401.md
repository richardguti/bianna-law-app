# Fla. Stat. § 373.1401 — Management of lands of water management districts.

**Title:** XXVIII — NATURAL RESOURCES; CONSERVATION, RECLAMATION, AND USE
**Chapter:** 373 — WATER RESOURCES
**Part:** I — STATE WATER RESOURCE PLAN
**Section:** 373.1401
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

In addition to provisions contained in s. 373.1391(1) for soil and water conservation districts, the governing board of each water management district may contract with a nongovernmental person or entity, any federal or state agency, a county, a municipality, or any other governmental entity, or environmental nonprofit organization to provide for the improvement, management, or maintenance of any real property owned by or under the control of the district.

**History:** s. 6, ch. 91-288; s. 14, ch. 2001-256.
