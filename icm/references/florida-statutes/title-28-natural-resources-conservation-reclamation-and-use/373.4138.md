# Fla. Stat. § 373.4138 — High Speed Rail Project; determination of mitigation requirements and costs.

**Title:** XXVIII — NATURAL RESOURCES; CONSERVATION, RECLAMATION, AND USE
**Chapter:** 373 — WATER RESOURCES
**Part:** IV — MANAGEMENT AND STORAGE OF SURFACE WATERS
**Section:** 373.4138
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

With respect to the High Speed Rail Project, any mitigation requirements and associated costs shall be determined by negotiation between the Department of Environmental Protection and the Department of Transportation, but if agreement on mitigation costs cannot be reached, the project may proceed at the rates determined under s. 373.4137(3).

**History:** <HIS5 of Environmental Protection authorizes the activities proposed in the mitigation plan, and no other state, regional, or local permit or approval is necessary.
