# Fla. Stat. § 373.5071 — Audit report; furnishing to governing board and clerks of circuit courts.

**Title:** XXVIII — NATURAL RESOURCES; CONSERVATION, RECLAMATION, AND USE
**Chapter:** 373 — WATER RESOURCES
**Part:** V — FINANCE AND TAXATION
**Section:** 373.5071
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The audit report required in s. 218.39 shall be furnished to the governing board of the district and the clerks of the circuit courts of each county within or partly within the district.

**History:** s. 109, ch. 2001-266.
