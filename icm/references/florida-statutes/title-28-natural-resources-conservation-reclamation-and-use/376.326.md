# Fla. Stat. § 376.326 — Application of s. 376.317.

**Title:** XXVIII — NATURAL RESOURCES; CONSERVATION, RECLAMATION, AND USE
**Chapter:** 376 — POLLUTANT DISCHARGE PREVENTION AND REMOVAL
**Part:** II — FLORIDA INLAND NAVIGATION DISTRICT LAW
**Section:** 376.326
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Nothing in ss. 376.320-376.326 shall be construed to exclude aboveground storage tanks from the application of s. 376.317.

**History:** s. 7, ch. 90-98; s. 22, ch. 92-30.
