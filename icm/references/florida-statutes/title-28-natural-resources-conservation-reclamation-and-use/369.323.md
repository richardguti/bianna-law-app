# Fla. Stat. § 369.323 — Compliance.

**Title:** XXVIII — NATURAL RESOURCES; CONSERVATION, RECLAMATION, AND USE
**Chapter:** 369 — CONSERVATION
**Part:** III — WEKIVA PARKWAY AND PROTECTION ACT
**Section:** 369.323
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Comprehensive plans and plan amendments adopted by the local governments within the Wekiva Study Area to implement this act shall be reviewed for compliance by the Department of Commerce.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 2004-384; s. 246, ch. 2011-142; s. 110, ch. 2024-6.
