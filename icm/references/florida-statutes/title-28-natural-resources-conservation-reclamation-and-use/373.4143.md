# Fla. Stat. § 373.4143 — Declaration of policy.

**Title:** XXVIII — NATURAL RESOURCES; CONSERVATION, RECLAMATION, AND USE
**Chapter:** 373 — WATER RESOURCES
**Part:** IV — MANAGEMENT AND STORAGE OF SURFACE WATERS
**Section:** 373.4143
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

It is the policy of the Legislature that the state provide efficient government services by consolidating, to the maximum extent practicable, federal and state permitting associated with wetlands and navigable waters within the state.

**History:** s. 2, ch. 2005-273.
