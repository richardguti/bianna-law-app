# Fla. Stat. § 373.0698 — Creation and operation of basin boards; other laws superseded.

**Title:** XXVIII — NATURAL RESOURCES; CONSERVATION, RECLAMATION, AND USE
**Chapter:** 373 — WATER RESOURCES
**Part:** I — STATE WATER RESOURCE PLAN
**Section:** 373.0698
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The provisions of ss. 373.0693-373.0697 shall govern the creation and operation of basin boards within any water management district, the provisions of any other general or special law to the contrary notwithstanding.

**History:** s. 5, ch. 84-341.
