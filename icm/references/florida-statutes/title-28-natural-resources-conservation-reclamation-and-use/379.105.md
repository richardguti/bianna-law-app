# Fla. Stat. § 379.105 — Harassment of hunters, trappers, or fishers.

**Title:** XXVIII — NATURAL RESOURCES; CONSERVATION, RECLAMATION, AND USE
**Chapter:** 379 — FISH AND WILDLIFE CONSERVATION
**Part:** I — GENERAL PROVISIONS
**Section:** 379.105
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
A person may not intentionally, within or on any public lands or publicly or privately owned wildlife management and fish management areas, or in or on any public waters:
(a)  
Interfere with or attempt to prevent the lawful taking of fish, game, or nongame animals by another within or on such lands or areas, or in or on such waters.

(b)  
Attempt to disturb fish, game, or nongame animals or attempt to affect their behavior with the intent to prevent their lawful taking by another within or on such lands or areas, or in or on such waters.

(2)  
Any person who violates this section commits a Level Two violation under s. 379.401.

**History:** s. 2, ch. 90-170; s. 27, ch. Right to hunt and fish.

**Note:** Former s. 372.002.
