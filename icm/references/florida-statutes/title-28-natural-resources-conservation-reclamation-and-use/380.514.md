# Fla. Stat. § 380.514 — Inconsistent provisions of other laws superseded.

**Title:** XXVIII — NATURAL RESOURCES; CONSERVATION, RECLAMATION, AND USE
**Chapter:** 380 — LAND AND WATER MANAGEMENT
**Part:** III — FLORIDA COMMUNITIES TRUST
**Section:** 380.514
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

If the provisions of this part are inconsistent with the provisions of any other law, general, special, or local, the provisions of this part shall be controlling.

**History:** s. 28, ch. 89-175; s. 2, ch. 90-192; s. 17, ch. 91-192; s. 5, ch. 91-429.
