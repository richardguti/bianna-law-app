# Fla. Stat. § 378.409 — Civil liability.

**Title:** XXVIII — NATURAL RESOURCES; CONSERVATION, RECLAMATION, AND USE
**Chapter:** 378 — LAND RECLAMATION
**Part:** III — RESOURCE EXTRACTION RECLAMATION
**Section:** 378.409
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
Any operator who begins resource extraction without meeting the requirements of this part is liable to the state for any damages caused to the water or property, including F.S. 378.4086378.408  
Injunctive relief.
——

The secretary may institute civil action in a court of competent jurisdiction to seek injunctive relief to enforce compliance with the requirements of this part.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 86-294; s. 329, ch. 94-356.
