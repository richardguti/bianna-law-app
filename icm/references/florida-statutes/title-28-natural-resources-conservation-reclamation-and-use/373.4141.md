# Fla. Stat. § 373.4141 — Permits; processing.

**Title:** XXVIII — NATURAL RESOURCES; CONSERVATION, RECLAMATION, AND USE
**Chapter:** 373 — WATER RESOURCES
**Part:** IV — MANAGEMENT AND STORAGE OF SURFACE WATERS
**Section:** 373.4141
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

——

(1)  
Within 30 days after receipt of an application for a permit under this part, the department or the water management district shall review the application and shall request submittal of all additional information the department or the water management district is permitted by law to require. If the applicant believes any request for additional information is not authorized by law or rule, the applicant may request a hearing pursuant to s. 120.57. Within 30 days after receipt of such additional information, the department or water management district shall review it and may request only that information needed to clarify such additional information or to answer new questions raised by or directly related to such additional information.
