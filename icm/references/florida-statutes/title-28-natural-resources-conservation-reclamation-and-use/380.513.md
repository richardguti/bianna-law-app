# Fla. Stat. § 380.513 — Corporate existence.

**Title:** XXVIII — NATURAL RESOURCES; CONSERVATION, RECLAMATION, AND USE
**Chapter:** 380 — LAND AND WATER MANAGEMENT
**Part:** III — FLORIDA COMMUNITIES TRUST
**Section:** 380.513
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The trust and its corporate existence shall continue until terminated by law. Upon termination of the agency, all its rights and properties in excess of its obligations shall pass to and be vested in the state.
