# Fla. Stat. § 379.3711 — License fee for private game preserves and farms.

**Title:** XXVIII — NATURAL RESOURCES; CONSERVATION, RECLAMATION, AND USE
**Chapter:** 379 — FISH AND WILDLIFE CONSERVATION
**Part:** VII — NONRECREATIONAL LICENSES
**Section:** 379.3711
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The license fee for establishing, maintaining, and operating a private preserve and farm pursuant to s. 379.302 is $50 per year. The fee is payable to the commission and shall be deposited in the State Game Trust Fund.

**History:** s. 157, ch. 2008-247.
