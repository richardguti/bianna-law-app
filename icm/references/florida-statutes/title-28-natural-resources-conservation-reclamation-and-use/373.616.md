# Fla. Stat. § 373.616 — Liberal construction.

**Title:** XXVIII — NATURAL RESOURCES; CONSERVATION, RECLAMATION, AND USE
**Chapter:** 373 — WATER RESOURCES
**Part:** VI — MISCELLANEOUS PROVISIONS
**Section:** 373.616
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The provisions of this chapter shall be liberally construed in order to effectively carry out its purposes.

**History:** s. 4, part VI, ch. 72-299.
