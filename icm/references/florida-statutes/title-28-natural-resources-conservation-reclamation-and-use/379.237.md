# Fla. Stat. § 379.237 — Courts of equity may enjoin.

**Title:** XXVIII — NATURAL RESOURCES; CONSERVATION, RECLAMATION, AND USE
**Chapter:** 379 — FISH AND WILDLIFE CONSERVATION
**Part:** I — GENERAL PROVISIONS
**Section:** 379.237
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Courts of equity in this state have jurisdiction to enforce the conservation laws of this state by injunction.

**History:** s. 2, ch. 61-231; s. 2, ch. 98-227; s. 95, ch. 99-245; s. 1, ch. 2002-264; s. 60, ch. 2008-247.

**Note:** Former s. 370.021(10).
