# Fla. Stat. § 373.619 — Recognition of water and sewer-saving devices.

**Title:** XXVIII — NATURAL RESOURCES; CONSERVATION, RECLAMATION, AND USE
**Chapter:** 373 — WATER RESOURCES
**Part:** VI — MISCELLANEOUS PROVISIONS
**Section:** 373.619
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The Legislature urges all public-owned or investor-owned water and sewerage systems to reduce connection fees and regular service charges for customers who utilize water or sewer-saving devices, including, but not limited to, individual graywater disposal systems.

**History:** s. 2, ch. 82-10.
