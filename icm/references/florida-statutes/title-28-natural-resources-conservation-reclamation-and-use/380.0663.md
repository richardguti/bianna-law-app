# Fla. Stat. § 380.0663 — Land authority; creation, membership, expenses.

**Title:** XXVIII — NATURAL RESOURCES; CONSERVATION, RECLAMATION, AND USE
**Chapter:** 380 — LAND AND WATER MANAGEMENT
**Part:** I — ENVIRONMENTAL LAND AND WATER MANAGEMENT
**Section:** 380.0663
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

——

(1)  
Each county in which one or more areas of critical state concern are located is authorized to create, by ordinance, a public body corporate and politic, to be known as a land authority, which may be renamed by the governing board of the county. The governing body of the land authority shall be the governing board of the county. For the purposes of this act, the governing body of the land authority shall be referred to individually or collectively as the members or membership of the land authority, whichever is appropriate.

(2)
