# Fla. Stat. § 379.3504 — Entering false information on licenses or permits.

**Title:** XXVIII — NATURAL RESOURCES; CONSERVATION, RECLAMATION, AND USE
**Chapter:** 379 — FISH AND WILDLIFE CONSERVATION
**Part:** VI — LICENSES FOR RECREATIONAL ACTIVITIES
**Section:** 379.3504
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Whoever knowingly and willfully enters false information on, or allows or causes false information to be entered on or shown upon, any license or permit issued under this chapter in order to avoid prosecution or assist another in avoiding prosecution, or for any other wrongful purpose, commits a Level Two violation under s. 379.401.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 65-159; s. 11, ch. 85-235; s. 7, ch. 91-134; s. 19, ch. 96-300; s. 134, ch. 2008-247; s. 10, ch. 2016-107.

**Note:** Former s. 372.581.
