# Fla. Stat. § 447.209 — Public employer's rights.

**Title:** XXXI — LABOR
**Chapter:** 447 — LABOR ORGANIZATIONS
**Part:** II — PUBLIC EMPLOYEES
**Section:** 447.209
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

It is the right of the public employer to determine unilaterally the purpose of each of its constituent agencies, set standards of services to be offered to the public, and exercise control and discretion over its organization and operations.
