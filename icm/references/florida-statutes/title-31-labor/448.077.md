# Fla. Stat. § 448.077 — Preemption of employee scheduling regulation.

**Title:** XXXI — LABOR
**Chapter:** 448 — GENERAL LABOR REGULATIONS
**Part:** I — TERMS AND CONDITIONS OF EMPLOYMENT
**Section:** 448.077
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

A local government may not adopt or enforce any ordinance, resolution, order, rule, policy, or contract requirement regulating scheduling, including predictive scheduling, by a private employer except as expressly authorized or required by state or federal law, rule, or regulation or pursuant to federal grant requirements.

**History:** s. 4, ch. 2024-80.
