# Fla. Stat. § 447.05 — Initiation fees; limitation.

**Title:** XXXI — LABOR
**Chapter:** 447 — LABOR ORGANIZATIONS
**Part:** I — GENERAL PROVISIONS
**Section:** 447.05
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Labor unions or labor organizations shall not charge an initiation fee in excess of the sum of $15; provided, that initiation fees in effect on January 1, 1940, may be continued.

**History:** s. 5, ch. 21968, 1943.

**Note:** Former s. 481.05.
