# Fla. Stat. § 435.10 — Sharing of personnel information among employers.

**Title:** XXXI — LABOR
**Chapter:** 435 — EMPLOYMENT SCREENING
**Part:** III — ADULT DAY CARE CENTERS
**Section:** 435.10
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Every employer of employees covered by this chapter shall furnish copies of personnel records for employees or former employees to any other employer requesting this information pursuant to this section. Information contained in the records may include, but is not limited to, disciplinary matters and any reason for termination. Any employer releasing such records pursuant to this chapter shall be considered to be acting in good faith and may not be held liable for information contained in such records, absent a showing that the employer maliciously falsified such records.

**History:** s. 47, ch. 95-228.
