# Fla. Stat. § 450.095 — Waivers.

**Title:** XXXI — LABOR
**Chapter:** 450 — MINORITY LABOR GROUPS
**Part:** I — CHILD LABOR
**Section:** 450.095
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

In extenuating circumstances when it clearly appears to be in the best interest of the child, the department may grant a waiver of the restrictions imposed by the Child Labor Law on the employment of a child. Such waivers shall be granted upon a case-by-case basis and shall be based upon such factors as the department, by rule, establishes as determinative of whether such waiver is in the best interest of a child.

**History:** s. 7, ch. 81-192; s. 138, ch. 2000-165.
