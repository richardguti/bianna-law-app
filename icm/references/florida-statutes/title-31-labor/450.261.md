# Fla. Stat. § 450.261 — Interstate Migrant Labor Commission; Florida membership.

**Title:** XXXI — LABOR
**Chapter:** 450 — MINORITY LABOR GROUPS
**Part:** III — FARM LABOR CONTRACTOR REGISTRATION
**Section:** 450.261
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

In selecting the Florida membership of the Interstate Migrant Labor Commission, the Governor may designate the Secretary of Commerce as his or her representative.

**History:** s. 7, ch. 70-131; s. 75, ch. 81-167; s. 78, ch. 83-55; s. 174, ch. 97-103; s. 35, ch. 2011-34; s. 394, ch. 2011-142; s. 75, ch. 2012-96; s. 52, ch. 2021-25; s. 223, ch. 2024-6.
