# Fla. Stat. § 447.51 — Violations not a ground for municipal recall.

**Title:** XXXI — LABOR
**Chapter:** 447 — LABOR ORGANIZATIONS
**Part:** II — PUBLIC EMPLOYEES
**Section:** 447.51
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Any violation of this part (Labor Organizations; Public Employees) shall not subject any person to municipal recall and shall not be considered as grounds for municipal recall.

**History:** s. 2, ch. 77-279.
