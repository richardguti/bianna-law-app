# Fla. Stat. § 448.104 — Attorney's fees and costs.

**Title:** XXXI — LABOR
**Chapter:** 448 — GENERAL LABOR REGULATIONS
**Part:** I — TERMS AND CONDITIONS OF EMPLOYMENT
**Section:** 448.104
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

A court may award reasonable attorney's fees, court costs, and expenses to the prevailing party.

**History:** s. 7, ch. 91-285.
