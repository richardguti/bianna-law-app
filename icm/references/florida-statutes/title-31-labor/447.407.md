# Fla. Stat. § 447.407 — Compensation of mediator and special magistrate; expenses.

**Title:** XXXI — LABOR
**Chapter:** 447 — LABOR ORGANIZATIONS
**Part:** II — PUBLIC EMPLOYEES
**Section:** 447.407
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The compensation of the mediator and special magistrate, and all stenographic and other expenses, shall be borne equally by the parties.

**History:** s. 3, ch. 74-100; s. 17, ch. 77-343; s. 83, ch. 2004-11.
