# Fla. Stat. § 443.1317 — Rulemaking authority; enforcement of rules.

**Title:** XXXI — LABOR
**Chapter:** 443 — REEMPLOYMENT ASSISTANCE
**Part:** III — ADULT DAY CARE CENTERS
**Section:** 443.1317
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
DEPARTMENT OF COMMERCE.——

(a)  
Except as otherwise provided in s. 443.012, the Department of Commerce has ultimate authority over the administration of the Reemployment Assistance Program.

(b)  
The department may adopt rules under ss.LID=FS20260213.69 --#">213.69; 213.692; 213.73; 213.733; 213.74; and 213.757 apply to the collection of reemployment assistance contributions and reimbursements by the Department of Revenue unless prohibited by federal law.
