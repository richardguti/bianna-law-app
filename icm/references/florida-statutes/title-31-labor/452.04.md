# Fla. Stat. § 452.04 — Bonds violating chapter void.

**Title:** XXXI — LABOR
**Chapter:** 452 — BONDS OF EMPLOYEES OF COMMON CARRIERS
**Part:** III — FARM LABOR CONTRACTOR REGISTRATION
**Section:** 452.04
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Any bond, contract, or undertaking made in violation of the provisions of this chapter shall be void.

**History:** s. 4, ch. 6519, 1913; RGS 4044; CGL 5966.
