# Fla. Stat. § 448.22 — Definitions.

**Title:** XXXI — LABOR
**Chapter:** 448 — GENERAL LABOR REGULATIONS
**Part:** II — LABOR POOL ACT
**Section:** 448.22
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

For the purposes of this part:
(1)  
“Labor pool” means a business entity that operates a labor hall by one or more of the following methods:
(a)  
Contracting with third-party users to supply day laborers to them on a temporary basis.

(b)  
Hiring, employing, recruiting, or contracting with workers to fulfill these temporary labor contracts for day labor.

(c)  
Fulfilling any contracts for day labor in accordance with this subsection, even if the entity also conducts other business.
