# Fla. Stat. § 447.505 — Strikes prohibited.

**Title:** XXXI — LABOR
**Chapter:** 447 — LABOR ORGANIZATIONS
**Part:** II — PUBLIC EMPLOYEES
**Section:** 447.505
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

No public employee or employee organization may participate in a strike against a public employer by instigating or supporting, in any manner, a strike. Any violation of this section shall subject the violator to the penalties provided in this part.

**History:** s. 3, ch. 74-100.
