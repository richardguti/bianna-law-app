# Fla. Stat. § 448.04 — Penalty for officer or agent violating s. 448.03.

**Title:** XXXI — LABOR
**Chapter:** 448 — GENERAL LABOR REGULATIONS
**Part:** I — TERMS AND CONDITIONS OF EMPLOYMENT
**Section:** 448.04
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Any person acting as an officer or agent of any firm, joint stock company, association or corporation of the kind and character as described in s. 448.03 or for any one of them, who makes or executes any notice, order or threat of the kind therein mentioned and forbidden, shall be guilty of a misdemeanor of the second degree, punishable as provided in s. 775.082 or s. 775.083.

**History:** s. 2, ch. 5015, 1901; GS 3234; RGS 5067; CGL 7169; s. 375, ch. 71-136.
