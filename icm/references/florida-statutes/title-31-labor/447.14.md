# Fla. Stat. § 447.14 — Penalties.

**Title:** XXXI — LABOR
**Chapter:** 447 — LABOR ORGANIZATIONS
**Part:** I — GENERAL PROVISIONS
**Section:** 447.14
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Any person or labor organization who shall violate any of the provisions of this part shall be guilty of a misdemeanor of the second degree, punishable as provided in s. 775.082 or s. 775.083.

**History:** s. 14, ch. 21968, 1943; s. 373, ch. 71-136; s. 3, ch. 77-343; s. 81, ch. 91-224.

**Note:** Former s. 481.14.
