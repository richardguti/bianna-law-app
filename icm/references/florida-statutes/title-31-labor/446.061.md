# Fla. Stat. § 446.061 — Expenditures.

**Title:** XXXI — LABOR
**Chapter:** 446 — JOB TRAINING
**Part:** III — ADULT DAY CARE CENTERS
**Section:** 446.061
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The department shall make necessary expenditures from the appropriation provided by law for personal services, travel, printing, equipment, office space, and supplies as provided by law.

**History:** s. 6, ch. 23934, 1947; s. 24, ch. 57-1; s. 1, ch. 63-153; ss. 17, 35, ch. 69-106; s. 1, ch. 73-283; s. 1, ch. 77-174; s. 24, ch. 83-174; s. 13, ch. 95-345; s. 118, ch. 2000-165; s. 7, ch. 2004-322.

**Note:** Former s. 446.11.
