# Fla. Stat. § 452.05 — Violation of regulations as to employment bonds; penalty.

**Title:** XXXI — LABOR
**Chapter:** 452 — BONDS OF EMPLOYEES OF COMMON CARRIERS
**Part:** III — FARM LABOR CONTRACTOR REGISTRATION
**Section:** 452.05
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Any person who shall violate any of the provisions of this chapter shall be guilty of a misdemeanor of the second degree, punishable as provided in s. 775.082 or s. 775.083.

**History:** s. 4, ch. 6519, 1913; RGS 4044; CGL 5966.
