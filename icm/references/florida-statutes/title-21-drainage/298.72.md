# Fla. Stat. § 298.72 — Department may use proceeds of drainage tax to pay loans.

**Title:** XXI — DRAINAGE
**Chapter:** 298 — DRAINAGE AND WATER CONTROL
**Part:** III — VETERANS’ ADULT DAY HEALTH CARE OF FLORIDA ACT
**Section:** 298.72
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Any drainage tax provided by law to be assessed on the lands embraced in the district shall be available, and be used by the Department of Environmental Protection for the repayment of any loan or loans obtained by said department under the provisions of this chapter.

**History:** s. 3, ch. 6454, 1913; RGS 115F.S. 298.716298.71
