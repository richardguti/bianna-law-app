# Fla. Stat. § 298.21 — Supervisors may remove officers and employees.

**Title:** XXI — DRAINAGE
**Chapter:** 298 — DRAINAGE AND WATER CONTROL
**Part:** III — VETERANS’ ADULT DAY HEALTH CARE OF FLORIDA ACT
**Section:** 298.21
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The board of supervisors may at any time remove any officer, attorney, chief engineer or other employee appointed or employed by said board.

**History:** s. 47, ch. 6458, 1913; RGS 1144; CGL 1501.
