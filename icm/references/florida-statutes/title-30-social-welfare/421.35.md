# Fla. Stat. § 421.35 — Supplemental nature of sections.

**Title:** XXX — SOCIAL WELFARE
**Chapter:** 420 — HOUSING
**Part:** I — HOUSING AUTHORITIES
**Section:** 421.35
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The powers conferred by ss. 421.27-421.34 shall be in addition and supplemental to the powers conferred by any other law.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 20220, 1941; s. 5, ch. 65-223.
