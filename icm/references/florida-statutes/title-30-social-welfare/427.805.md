# Fla. Stat. § 427.805 — Waiver.

**Title:** XXX — SOCIAL WELFARE
**Chapter:** 427 — SPECIAL TRANSPORTATION AND COMMUNICATIONS SERVICES
**Part:** III — ASSISTIVE TECHNOLOGY DEVICE WARRANTY ACT
**Section:** 427.805
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Any waiver by a consumer of rights under this part is void.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 97-47.
