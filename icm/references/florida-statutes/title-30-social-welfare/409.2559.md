# Fla. Stat. § 409.2559 — State disbursement unit.

**Title:** XXX — SOCIAL WELFARE
**Chapter:** 409 — SOCIAL AND ECONOMIC ASSISTANCE
**Part:** I — SOCIAL AND ECONOMIC ASSISTANCE
**Section:** 409.2559
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The department shall establish and operate a state disbursement unit by October 1, 1999, as required by 42 U.S.C. s. 654(27).

**History:** s. 22, ch. 98-397.
