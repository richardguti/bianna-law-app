# Fla. Stat. § 420.516 — Discrimination prohibited.

**Title:** XXX — SOCIAL WELFARE
**Chapter:** 420 — HOUSING
**Part:** V — FLORIDA HOUSING FINANCE CORPORATION
**Section:** 420.516
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

It is an unlawful practice for a sponsor, while bonds are outstanding for the purpose of funding or financing the sponsor's project, to discriminate against any person or family because of race, color, religion, sex, national origin, or marital status.

**History:** s. 4, ch. 83-238.
