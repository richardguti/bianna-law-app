# Fla. Stat. § 413.015 — Workers' compensation coverage for program participants.

**Title:** XXX — SOCIAL WELFARE
**Chapter:** 413 — EMPLOYMENT AND RELATED SERVICES FOR PERSONS WITH DISABILITIES
**Part:** I — BLIND SERVICES PROGRAM
**Section:** 413.015
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

A participant in an adult or youth work experience activity administered under this part shall be deemed an employee of the state for purposes of workers' compensation coverage.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 2018-72.
