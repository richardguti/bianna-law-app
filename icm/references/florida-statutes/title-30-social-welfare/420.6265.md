# Fla. Stat. § 420.6265 — Rapid ReHousing.

**Title:** XXX — SOCIAL WELFARE
**Chapter:** 420 — HOUSING
**Part:** VI — AFFORDABLE HOUSING; COALITIONS FOR HOMELESS; FAMILY EMERGENCY ASSISTANCE
**Section:** 420.6265
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
The Rapid ReHousing response to homelessness differs from traditional approaches to addressing homelessness by focusing on each individual's or family's barriers to housing. By using this approach, communities can significantly reduce the amount of time that individuals and families are homeless and prevent further episodes of homelessness.
