# Fla. Stat. § 409.25641 — Procedures for processing interstate enforcement requests.

**Title:** XXX — SOCIAL WELFARE
**Chapter:** 409 — SOCIAL AND ECONOMIC ASSISTANCE
**Part:** I — SOCIAL AND ECONOMIC ASSISTANCE
**Section:** 409.25641
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The department shall use automated administrative enforcement as provided in 42 U.S.C. s. 666(a)(14)(A) to respond to a request from another state to enforce a support order and shall promptly report the results of the enforcement action to the requesting state.

**History:** s. 49, ch. 97-170; s. 25, ch. 98-397; s. 14, ch. 99-375; s. 5, ch. 2007-85.
