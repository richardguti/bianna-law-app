# Fla. Stat. § 410.606 — Rulemaking.

**Title:** XXX — SOCIAL WELFARE
**Chapter:** 411 — HANDICAP OR HIGH-RISK CONDITION PREVENTION AND EARLY CHILDHOOD ASSISTANCE
**Part:** V — COMMUNITY-BASED CHILD WELFARE
**Section:** 410.606
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The department shall adopt rules to implement ss. 410.601-410.606. The rules shall define each community service, establish minimum standards for the delivery of services, establish a fee schedule, and make other provisions necessary to implement ss. 410.601-410.606.

**History:** s. 10, ch. 88-350.
