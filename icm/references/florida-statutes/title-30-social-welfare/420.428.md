# Fla. Stat. § 420.428 — Eligible activities.

**Title:** XXX — SOCIAL WELFARE
**Chapter:** 420 — HOUSING
**Part:** IV — NEIGHBORHOOD HOUSING REHABILITATION PROGRAMS
**Section:** 420.428
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Grant recipients may use grant funds made available pursuant to ss. 420.421-420.429 to:
(1)  
Pay the Neighborhood Reinvestment Corporation for the fee charged to develop a neighborhood housing services corporation, which fee, if refunded, may be used for any eligible activity;

(2)  
Fund the operation of a neighborhood housing services corporation by payment of salaries and other administrative expenses, as approved by the department; and

(3)  
Fund a revolving high-risk loan fund which is administered by a neighborhood housing services corporation to finance the rehabilitation of property in the corporation's targeted area.

**History:** s. 7, ch. 79-259; s. 6, ch. 85-265.
