# Fla. Stat. § 410.034 — Department determination of fitness to provide home care.

**Title:** XXX — SOCIAL WELFARE
**Chapter:** 410 — AGING AND ADULT SERVICES
**Part:** V — COMMUNITY-BASED CHILD WELFARE
**Section:** 410.034
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

In accordance with s. 429.02, a person caring for an adult who is related to such person by blood or marriage is not subject to the Assisted Living Facilities Act. If, however, the person who plans to provide home care under this act is found by the department to be unable to provide this care, the department shall notify the person wishing to provide home care of this determination, and the person shall not be eligible for subsidy payments under ss. 410.031-410.036.

**History:** s. 16, ch. 77-336; s. 18, ch. 78-433; s. 168, ch. 79-400; s. 33, ch. 95-210; s. 74, ch. 95-418; s. 85, ch. 2006-197.

Home care for disabled adults; rules.
—

The department shall by rule establish minimum standards and procedures for the provision of home care for disabled adults, and for the approval of persons wishing to provide such care. Priority shall be given to provision of care to disabled adults who are not eligible for comparable services in programs of and funded by the department. Any person who is approved by the department to provide such care for a disabled adult is eligible for the subsidy payments described in s. 410.035. However, the home care for disabled adults program must operate within the funds appropriated by the Legislature.
