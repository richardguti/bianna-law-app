# Fla. Stat. § 430.604 — Department determination of inability to provide home care.

**Title:** XXX — SOCIAL WELFARE
**Chapter:** 430 — ELDER AFFAIRS
**Part:** III — ADULT DAY CARE CENTERS
**Section:** 430.604
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

If a person who plans to provide home care under ss. 430.601-430.606 is found by the department, or its designee, to be unable to provide this care, the department must notify the person seeking to provide home care of this determination, and the person is not eligible for subsidy payments under ss. 430.601-430.606.

**History:** s. 83, ch. 95-418.
