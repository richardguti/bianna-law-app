# Fla. Stat. § 429.178 — Special care for persons with Alzheimer's disease or other related disorders.

**Title:** XXX — SOCIAL WELFARE
**Chapter:** 429 — ASSISTED CARE COMMUNITIES
**Part:** I — ASSISTED LIVING FACILITIES
**Section:** 429.178
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

A facility which advertises that it provides special care for persons with Alzheimer's disease or other related disorders must meet the following standards of operation:
(1)(a)  
If the facility has 17 or more residents, have an awake staff member on duty at all hours of the day and night; or

(b)  
If the facility has fewer than 17 residents, have an awake staff member on duty at all hours of the day and night or have mechanisms in place to monitor and ensure the safety of the facility's residents.——

A facility licensed under this part which claims that it provides special care for persons who have Alzheimer's disease or other related disorders must disclose in its advertisements or in a separate document those services that distinguish the care as being especially applicable to, or suitable for, such persons. The facility must give a copy of all such advertisements or a copy of the document to each person who requests information about programs and services for persons with Alzheimer's disease or other related disorders offered by the facility and must maintain a copy of all such advertisements and documents in its records. The agency shall examine all such advertisements and documents in the facility's records as part of the license renewal procedure.

**History:** s. 2, ch. 93-105; s. 2, ch. 2006-197; s. 5, ch. 2026-102.
