# Fla. Stat. § 409.2581 — Use of clearing accounts and revolving funds.

**Title:** XXX — SOCIAL WELFARE
**Chapter:** 409 — SOCIAL AND ECONOMIC ASSISTANCE
**Part:** I — SOCIAL AND ECONOMIC ASSISTANCE
**Section:** 409.2581
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

To facilitate the cash flow and administration of child support enforcement under this act, the department may use clearing accounts and revolving funds.

**History:** s. 10, ch. 76-220.
