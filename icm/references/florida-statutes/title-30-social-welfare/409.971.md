# Fla. Stat. § 409.971 — Managed medical assistance program.

**Title:** XXX — SOCIAL WELFARE
**Chapter:** 409 — SOCIAL AND ECONOMIC ASSISTANCE
**Part:** IV — MEDICAID MANAGED CARE
**Section:** 409.971
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The agency shall make payments for primary and acute medical assistance and related services using a managed care model.

**History:** s. 12, ch. 2011-134; s. 102, ch. 2020-2.
