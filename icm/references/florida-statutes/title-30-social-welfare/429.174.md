# Fla. Stat. § 429.174 — Background screening.

**Title:** XXX — SOCIAL WELFARE
**Chapter:** 429 — ASSISTED CARE COMMUNITIES
**Part:** I — ASSISTED LIVING FACILITIES
**Section:** 429.174
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The agency shall require level 2 background screening for personnel as required in s. 408.809(1)(e) pursuant to chapter 435 and s. 408.809.
