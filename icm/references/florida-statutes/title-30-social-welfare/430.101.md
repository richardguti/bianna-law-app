# Fla. Stat. § 430.101 — Administration of federal aging programs.

**Title:** XXX — SOCIAL WELFARE
**Chapter:** 430 — ELDER AFFAIRS

**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

The Department of Elderly Affairs is designated the state unit on aging under the federal Older Americans Act of 1965, as amended, and shall exercise all responsibilities under that act.
