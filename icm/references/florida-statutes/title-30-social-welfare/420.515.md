# Fla. Stat. § 420.515 — Inconsistent provisions of other laws superseded.

**Title:** XXX — SOCIAL WELFARE
**Chapter:** 420 — HOUSING
**Part:** V — FLORIDA HOUSING FINANCE CORPORATION
**Section:** 420.515
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Insofar as the provisions of this part are inconsistent with the provisions of any other law, general, special, or local, the provisions of this part shall be controlling.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 80-161; s. 24, ch. 97-167.
