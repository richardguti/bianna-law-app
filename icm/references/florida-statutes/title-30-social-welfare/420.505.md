# Fla. Stat. § 420.505 — Meetings; quorum; voting.

**Title:** XXX — SOCIAL WELFARE
**Chapter:** 420 — HOUSING
**Part:** V — FLORIDA HOUSING FINANCE CORPORATION
**Section:** 420.505
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The powers of the corporation shall be vested in the members of its board of directors in office from time to time. Five members of the board shall constitute a quorum for the purpose of conducting its business and exercising its powers and for all other purposes. Action may be taken by the board upon an affirmative vote of a majority of the members present, provided that no action shall be taken by an affirmative vote of less than four members.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 80-161; s. 8, ch. 97-167.
