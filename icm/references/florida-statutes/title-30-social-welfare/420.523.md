# Fla. Stat. § 420.523 — Purpose.

**Title:** XXX — SOCIAL WELFARE
**Chapter:** 420 — HOUSING
**Part:** V — FLORIDA HOUSING FINANCE CORPORATION
**Section:** 420.523
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The purpose of the Predevelopment Loan Program is to:
(1)  
Provide for financial and technical assistance to local governments, housing authorities, and not-for-profit organizations who will provide for the sponsorship of housing for the target population in the state, such housing to be financed by federal, state, local, or private resources.

(2)  
F.S. 420.5226420.522  
Legislative findings.
——

It is hereby found by the Legislature that:
(1)  
There continues to exist a serious shortage of affordable housing for farmworkers, very-low-income persons and families, and low-income persons and families throughout the state.

(2)  
Existing private, local, state, and federal resources have not been adequate to remedy this shortage.

(3)  
The provision of housing predevelopment funds is necessary to leverage currently available resources for housing development.

**History:** s. 13, ch. 88-376; s. 17, ch. 93-181.

**Note:** Former s. 420.304.
