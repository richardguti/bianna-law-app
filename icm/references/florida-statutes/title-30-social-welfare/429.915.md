# Fla. Stat. § 429.915 — Conditional license.

**Title:** XXX — SOCIAL WELFARE
**Chapter:** 429 — ASSISTED CARE COMMUNITIES
**Part:** III — ADULT DAY CARE CENTERS
**Section:** 429.915
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

——

In addition to the license categories available in part II of chapter 408, the agency may issue a conditional license to an applicant for license renewal or change of ownership if the applicant fails to meet all standards and requirements for licensure.
