# Fla. Stat. § 423.001 — Supplemental nature of chapter.

**Title:** XXX — SOCIAL WELFARE
**Chapter:** 422 — HOUSING COOPERATION LAW
**Part:** II — MISCELLANEOUS PROVISIONS
**Section:** 423.001
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The powers conferred by this chapter shall be in addition and supplemental to the powers conferred by any other law.

**History:** s. 8, ch. 17982, 1937; CGL 1940 Supp. 7100(3-vv).
