# Fla. Stat. § 409.031 — State agency for administering social service funds.

**Title:** XXX — SOCIAL WELFARE
**Chapter:** 409 — SOCIAL AND ECONOMIC ASSISTANCE
**Part:** I — SOCIAL AND ECONOMIC ASSISTANCE
**Section:** 409.031
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The department is designated as the state agency responsible for the administration of social service funds under Title XX of the Social Security Act.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 78-433.
