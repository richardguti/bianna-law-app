# Fla. Stat. § 409.965 — Mandatory enrollment.

**Title:** XXX — SOCIAL WELFARE
**Chapter:** 409 — SOCIAL AND ECONOMIC ASSISTANCE
**Part:** IV — MEDICAID MANAGED CARE
**Section:** 409.965
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

All Medicaid recipients shall receive covered services through the statewide managed care program, except as provided by this part pursuant to an approved federal waiver. The following Medicaid recipients are exempt from participation in the statewide managed care program:
(1)  
Women who are eligible only for family planning services.

(2)  
Women who are eligible only for breast and cervical cancer services.

(3)  
Persons who are eligible for emergency Medicaid for aliens.

**History:** s. 6, ch. 2011-134; s. 4, ch. 2014-57.
