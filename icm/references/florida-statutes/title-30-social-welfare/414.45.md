# Fla. Stat. § 414.45 — F.S. 414.426414.42

**Title:** XXX — SOCIAL WELFARE
**Chapter:** 414 — FAMILY SELF-SUFFICIENCY
**Part:** III — EMPLOYMENT FIRST; UNIQUE ABILITIES PARTNER PROGRAM
**Section:** 414.45
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

It is cause for dismissal of an employee of the Department of Children and Families if the employee knowingly and willfully allows an ineligible person to obtain public assistance.

**History:** s. 9, ch. 92-125; s. 45, ch. 96-175; s. 220, ch. 97-101; s. 33, ch. 97-173; s. 233, ch. 2014-19.

**Note:** Former s. 409.3282.
