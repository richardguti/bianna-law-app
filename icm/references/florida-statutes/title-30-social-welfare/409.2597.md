# Fla. Stat. § 409.2597 — Retention of actions.

**Title:** XXX — SOCIAL WELFARE
**Chapter:** 409 — SOCIAL AND ECONOMIC ASSISTANCE
**Part:** I — SOCIAL AND ECONOMIC ASSISTANCE
**Section:** 409.2597
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

All actions pending under the authority of those statutes repealed by this act shall not abate but shall continue pursuant to the provisions of this act.

**History:** s. 16, ch. 76-220.
