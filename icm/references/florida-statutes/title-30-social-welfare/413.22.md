# Fla. Stat. § 413.22 — Division rules.

**Title:** XXX — SOCIAL WELFARE
**Chapter:** 413 — EMPLOYMENT AND RELATED SERVICES FOR PERSONS WITH DISABILITIES
**Part:** II — GENERAL VOCATIONAL REHABILITATION PROGRAMS
**Section:** 413.22
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The division shall prepare rules as it finds necessary to carry out the purposes of this part.
