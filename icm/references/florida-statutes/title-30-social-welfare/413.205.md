# Fla. Stat. § 413.205 — Payments not treated as collateral payments.

**Title:** XXX — SOCIAL WELFARE
**Chapter:** 413 — EMPLOYMENT AND RELATED SERVICES FOR PERSONS WITH DISABILITIES
**Part:** II — GENERAL VOCATIONAL REHABILITATION PROGRAMS
**Section:** 413.205
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Notwithstanding any other law to the contrary, payments made under programs covered by this part are neither collateral payments nor collateral sources within the meaning of chapter 86-160, Laws of Florida, or chapter 88-1, Laws of Florida.

**History:** s. 50, ch. 88-277; s. 5, ch. 94-324.
