# Fla. Stat. § 409.90201 — Recipient address update process.

**Title:** XXX — SOCIAL WELFARE
**Chapter:** 409 — SOCIAL AND ECONOMIC ASSISTANCE
**Part:** III — MEDICAID
**Section:** 409.90201
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The Agency for Health Care Administration and the Department of Children and Families, in consultation with hospitals and nursing homes that serve Medicaid recipients, shall develop a process to update a recipient's address in the Medicaid eligibility system at the time a recipient is admitted to a hospital or nursing home. If a recipient's address information in the Medicaid eligibility system needs to be updated, the update shall be completed within 10 days after the recipient's admission to a hospital or nursing home.

**History:** s. 13, ch. 2012-33; s. 203, ch. 2014-19.
