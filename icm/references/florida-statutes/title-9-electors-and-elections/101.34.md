# Fla. Stat. § 101.34 — Custody of voting system.

**Title:** IX — ELECTORS AND ELECTIONS
**Chapter:** 101 — VOTING METHODS AND PROCEDURE
**Part:** II — FLORIDA VOTER REGISTRATION ACT
**Section:** 101.34
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The supervisor of elections shall be the custodian of the voting system in the county, and he or she shall appoint deputies necessary to prepare and supervise the voting system prior to and during elections. The compensation for such deputies shall be paid by the supervisor of elections.

**History:** s. 3-A, ch. 22018, 1943; s. 4, ch. 24089, 1947; s. 5, ch. 26870, 1951; s. 16, ch. 65-380; s. 18, ch. 77-175; s. 3, ch. 80-20; s. 567, ch. 95-147; s. 11, ch. 2001-40.

**Note:** Former s. 100.42.
