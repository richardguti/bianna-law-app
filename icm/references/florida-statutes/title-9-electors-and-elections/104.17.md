# Fla. Stat. § 104.17 — Voting in person after casting vote-by-mail ballot.

**Title:** IX — ELECTORS AND ELECTIONS
**Chapter:** 104 — ELECTION CODE: VIOLATIONS; PENALTIES
**Part:** II — FLORIDA VOTER REGISTRATION ACT
**Section:** 104.17
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Any person who willfully votes or attempts to vote both in person and by vote-by-mail ballot at any election is guilty of a felony of the third degree, punishable as provided in s. 775.082, s. 775.083, or s. 775.084.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 22014, 1943; s. 1, ch. 25385, 1949; s. 8, ch. 26870, 1951; s. 7, ch. 65-379; s. 36, ch. 71-136; s. 35, ch. 77-175; s. 39, ch. 2016-37.

**Note:** Former s. 101.11.
