# Fla. Stat. § 106.28 — Limitation of actions.

**Title:** IX — ELECTORS AND ELECTIONS
**Chapter:** 106 — CAMPAIGN FINANCING
**Part:** II — FLORIDA VOTER REGISTRATION ACT
**Section:** 106.28
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Actions for violation of this chapter must be commenced before 2 years have elapsed from the date of the violation.
