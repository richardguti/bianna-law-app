# Fla. Stat. § 101.56064 — Application for federal funds under ch. 2002-281.

**Title:** IX — ELECTORS AND ELECTIONS
**Chapter:** 101 — VOTING METHODS AND PROCEDURE
**Part:** II — FLORIDA VOTER REGISTRATION ACT
**Section:** 101.56064
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The state may apply for all available federal funds to be used to pay for the costs associated with this act.

**History:** s. 21, ch. 2002-281.
