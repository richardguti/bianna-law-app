# Fla. Stat. § 100.341 — Bond referendum ballot.

**Title:** IX — ELECTORS AND ELECTIONS
**Chapter:** 100 — GENERAL, PRIMARY, SPECIAL, BOND, AND REFERENDUM ELECTIONS
**Part:** II — FLORIDA VOTER REGISTRATION ACT
**Section:** 100.341
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The ballots used in bond referenda shall include a printed description of the issuance of bonds to be voted on as prescribed by the authority calling the referendum. If any bond referendum is called and held for approving the issuance of bonds for a particular purpose and such referendum does not result in the approval of the bonds, then no other referendum for the approval of bonds for the same purpose shall be called for at least 6 months.
