# Fla. Stat. § 104.22 — Stealing and destroying records, etc., of election.

**Title:** IX — ELECTORS AND ELECTIONS
**Chapter:** 104 — ELECTION CODE: VIOLATIONS; PENALTIES
**Part:** II — FLORIDA VOTER REGISTRATION ACT
**Section:** 104.22
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Any person who is guilty of stealing, willfully and wrongfully breaking, destroying, mutilating, defacing, or unlawfully moving or securing and detaining the whole or any part of any ballot box or any record tally sheet or copy thereof, returns, or any other paper or document provided for, or who fraudulently makes aF.S. 104.216104.21  
Changing electors' ballots.
——

Whoever fraudulently changes or attempts to change the vote or ballot of any elector, by which actions such elector is prevented from voting such ballot or from voting such ballot as the elector intended, is guilty of a felony of the third degree, punishable as provided in s. 775.082, s. 775.083, or s. 775.084.
