# Fla. Stat. § 104.041 — Fraud in connection with casting vote.F.S. 104.0316104.031

**Title:** IX — ELECTORS AND ELECTIONS
**Chapter:** 104 — ELECTION CODE: VIOLATIONS; PENALTIES
**Part:** II — FLORIDA VOTER REGISTRATION ACT
**Section:** 104.041
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Any person who makes a false declaration for assistance in voting, or in the preparation of his or her ballot, in any election is guilty of a misdemeanor of the first degree, punishable as provided in s. 775.082 or s. 775.083.

**History:** s. 49, ch. 4328, 1895; GS 3829; RGS 5892; CGL 8156; s. 8, ch. 26870, 1951; s. 22, ch. 71-136; s. 35, ch. 77-175; s. 4, ch. 91-224; s. 613, ch. 95-147.

**Note:** Former s. 99.31.
