# Fla. Stat. § 105.10 — Applicability of election code.

**Title:** IX — ELECTORS AND ELECTIONS
**Chapter:** 105 — NONPARTISAN ELECTIONS
**Part:** II — FLORIDA VOTER REGISTRATION ACT
**Section:** 105.10
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

If any provision of this chapter is in conflict with any other provision of this code, the provision of this chapter shall prevail.

**History:** s. 10, ch. 71-49; s. 38, ch. 77-175.
