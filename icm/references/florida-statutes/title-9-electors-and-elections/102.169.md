# Fla. Stat. § 102.169 — Quo warranto not abridged.

**Title:** IX — ELECTORS AND ELECTIONS
**Chapter:** 102 — CONDUCTING ELECTIONS AND ASCERTAINING THE RESULTS
**Part:** II — FLORIDA VOTER REGISTRATION ACT
**Section:** 102.169
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Nothing in this code shall be construed to abrogate or abridge any remedy that may now exist by quo warranto, but in such case the proceeding prescribed in s. 102.168 shall be an alternative or cumulative remedy.

**History:** RS 203; GS 287; RGS 383; CGL 448; s. 3, ch. 26870, 1951; s. 19, ch. 65-378; s. 31, ch. 77-175.

**Note:** Former s. 104.10; s. 99.221; s. 102.164.
