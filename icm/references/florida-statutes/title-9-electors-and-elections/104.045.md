# Fla. Stat. § 104.045 — Vote selling.

**Title:** IX — ELECTORS AND ELECTIONS
**Chapter:** 104 — ELECTION CODE: VIOLATIONS; PENALTIES
**Part:** II — FLORIDA VOTER REGISTRATION ACT
**Section:** 104.045
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Any person who:
(1)  
Corruptly offers to vote for or against, or to refrain from voting for or against, any candidate in any election in return for pecuniary or other benefit; or

(2)  
F.S. 104.0426104.042  
Time limitation; election fraud.
——

A prosecution for a felony violation under the Florida Election Code must be commenced within 5 years after the date the violation is committed.

**History:** s. 25, ch. 2026-26.
