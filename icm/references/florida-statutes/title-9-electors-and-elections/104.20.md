# Fla. Stat. § 104.20 — Ballot not to be seen, and other offenses.

**Title:** IX — ELECTORS AND ELECTIONS
**Chapter:** 104 — ELECTION CODE: VIOLATIONS; PENALTIES
**Part:** II — FLORIDA VOTER REGISTRATION ACT
**Section:** 104.20
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Any elector who, except as provided by law, allows his or her ballot to be seen by any person; takes or removes, or attempts to take or remove, any ballot from the polling place before the close of the polls; places any mark on his or her ballot by which it may be identified; endeavors to induce any elector to show how he or she voted; aids or attempts to aid any elector unlawfully; or prints or procures to be printed, or has in his or her possession, any copies of any ballot prepared to be voted is guilty of a misdemeanor of the first degree, punishable as provided in s. 104.19  
Using stickers or rubber stamps or carrying certain items in voting booth; penalty.
——

(1)(a)  
It is unlawful for any person casting a ballot at any election to use stickers or rubber stamps or to carry into a voting booth any mechanical device, paper, or memorandum which might be used to affect adversely the normal election process.

(b)  
In casting a write-in ballot, the elector shall cast the same in his or her own handwriting or in the handwriting of an authorized person aiding him or her.

(2)  
Any person who violates the provisions of this section is guilty of a misdemeanor of the second degree, punishable as provided in s. 775.082 or s. 775.083.
