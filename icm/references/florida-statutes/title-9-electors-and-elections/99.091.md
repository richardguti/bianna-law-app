# Fla. Stat. § 99.091 — Representatives to Congress.

**Title:** IX — ELECTORS AND ELECTIONS
**Chapter:** 99 — CANDIDATES
**Part:** II — FLORIDA VOTER REGISTRATION ACT
**Section:** 99.091
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
A Representative to Congress shall be elected in and for each congressional district at each general election.

(2)  
When Florida is entitled to additional representatives according to the last census, representatives shall be elected from the state at large and at large thereafter until the state is redistricted by the Legislature.

**History:** ss. 2, 3, ch. 3879, 1889; RS 157; s. 4, ch. 4328, 1895; s. 3, ch. 4537, 1897; GS 174; RGS 218; CGL 253; s. 2, ch. 25383, 1949; s. 3, ch. 26870, 1951; s. 6, ch. 77-175.

**Note:** Former s. 98.07.
