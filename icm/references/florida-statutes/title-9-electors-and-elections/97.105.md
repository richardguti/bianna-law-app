# Fla. Stat. § 97.105 — Permanent single registration system established.

**Title:** IX — ELECTORS AND ELECTIONS
**Chapter:** 97 — QUALIFICATION AND REGISTRATION OF ELECTORS
**Part:** II — FLORIDA VOTER REGISTRATION ACT
**Section:** 97.105
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

——

A permanent single registration system for the registration of electors to qualify them to vote in all elections is provided for the several counties and municipalities. This system shall be put into use by all municipalities and shall be in lieu of any other system of municipal registration. El2005-286; s. 8, ch. 2011-40; s. 9, ch. 2021-11.
