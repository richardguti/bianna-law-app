# Fla. Stat. § 102.072 — Vote-by-mail count reporting.

**Title:** IX — ELECTORS AND ELECTIONS
**Chapter:** 102 — CONDUCTING ELECTIONS AND ASCERTAINING THE RESULTS
**Part:** II — FLORIDA VOTER REGISTRATION ACT
**Section:** 102.072
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Beginning at 7 p.m. on election day, the supervisor must, at least once every hour while actively counting, post on his or her website the number of vote-by-mail ballots that have been received and the number of vote-by-mail ballots that remain uncounted.

**History:** s. 30, ch. 2021-11; s. 6, ch. 2022-4.
