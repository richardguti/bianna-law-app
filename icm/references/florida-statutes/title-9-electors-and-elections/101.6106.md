# Fla. Stat. § 101.6106 — Application of other election laws.

**Title:** IX — ELECTORS AND ELECTIONS
**Chapter:** 101 — VOTING METHODS AND PROCEDURE
**Part:** II — FLORIDA VOTER REGISTRATION ACT
**Section:** 101.6106
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

All laws that are applicable to general elections are applicable to mail ballot elections to the extent applicable.

**History:** s. 1. ch. 87-364.
