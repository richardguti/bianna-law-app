# Fla. Stat. § 101.6107 — Department of State to adopt rules.

**Title:** IX — ELECTORS AND ELECTIONS
**Chapter:** 101 — VOTING METHODS AND PROCEDURE
**Part:** II — FLORIDA VOTER REGISTRATION ACT
**Section:** 101.6107
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The Department of State shall adopt rules governing the procedures and forms necessary to implement ss. 101.6101-101.6107.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 87-364.
