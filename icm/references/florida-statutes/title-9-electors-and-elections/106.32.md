# Fla. Stat. § 106.32 — [1]Election Campaign Financing Trust Fund.

**Title:** IX — ELECTORS AND ELECTIONS
**Chapter:** 106 — CAMPAIGN FINANCING
**Part:** II — FLORIDA VOTER REGISTRATION ACT
**Section:** 106.32
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
There is hereby established in the State Treasury an [1]Election Campaign Financing Trust Fund to be utilized by the Department of State as provided in ss. 106.30-106.36. If necessary, each year in which a general election is to be held for the election of the Governor and Cabinet, additional funds shall be transferred to the [1]Election Campaign Financing Trust Fund from general revenue in an amount sufficient to fund qualifying candidates pursuant to the provisions of ss. 106.30-106.36.

(2)  
Proceeds from filing fees pursuant to ss. 99.092, 99.093, and 105.031 shall be deposited into the [1]Election Campaign Financing Trust Fund as designated in those sections.

(3)  
Proceeds from assessments pursuant to ss. 106.07 and 106.29 shall be deposited into the [1]Election Campaign Financing Trust Fund as designated in those sections.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 86-276; s. 19, ch. 91-107; s. 26, ch. 2013-37; ss. 1, 6, ch. 2024-116.

**Note:** [1]Note.
—
The trust fund expired, effective November 4, 1996, by operation of s. 19(f), Art. III of the State Constitution.
