# Fla. Stat. § 104.15 — Unqualified electors willfully voting.

**Title:** IX — ELECTORS AND ELECTIONS
**Chapter:** 104 — ELECTION CODE: VIOLATIONS; PENALTIES
**Part:** II — FLORIDA VOTER REGISTRATION ACT
**Section:** 104.15
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Whoever, knowing he or she is not a qualified elector, willfully votes at any election is guilty of a felony of the third degree, punishable as provided in s. 775.082, s. 775.083, or s. 775.084.

**History:** s. 8, ch. 26870, 1951; s. 5, ch. 65-379; s. 34, ch. 71-136; s. 35, ch. 77-175; s. 621, ch. F.S. 104.136104.13

**Note:** Former s. 101.11.
