# Fla. Stat. § 35.043 — Fifth Appellate District.

**Title:** V — JUDICIAL BRANCH
**Chapter:** 35 — DISTRICT COURTS OF APPEAL
**Part:** IV — CAPITAL COLLATERAL REPRESENTATION
**Section:** 35.043
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The Fifth Appellate District is composed of the Fourth, Fifth, Seventh, and Eighteenth Judicial Circuits.

**History:** s. 2, ch. 79-413; s. 7, ch. 2022-163.
