# Fla. Stat. § 28.231 — Service charges by clerks of courts.

**Title:** V — JUDICIAL BRANCH
**Chapter:** 28 — CLERKS OF THE CIRCUIT COURTS
**Part:** IV — CAPITAL COLLATERAL REPRESENTATION
**Section:** 28.231
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The clerk of any state appellate or county or state trial court shall receive as compensation for similar services the same charges as provided in this chapter for the clerk of the circuit court.

**History:** s. 39, ch. 70-134.
