# Fla. Stat. § 25.211 — Location of clerk's office.

**Title:** V — JUDICIAL BRANCH
**Chapter:** 25 — SUPREME COURT
**Part:** III — CUSTOMER SERVICE STANDARDS
**Section:** 25.211
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The clerk shall have an office in the Supreme Court Building.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 57-274; s. 101, ch. 95-147.
