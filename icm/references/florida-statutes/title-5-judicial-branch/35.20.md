# Fla. Stat. § 35.20 — Retirement of district court of appeal judge.

**Title:** V — JUDICIAL BRANCH
**Chapter:** 35 — DISTRICT COURTS OF APPEAL
**Part:** IV — CAPITAL COLLATERAL REPRESENTATION
**Section:** 35.20
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Retirement of a district court of appeal judge shall be as provided by law.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 57-248; s. 200, ch. 95-147; s. 5, ch. 2021-230.
