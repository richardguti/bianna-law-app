# Fla. Stat. § 28.212 — Minutes of court proceedings.

**Title:** V — JUDICIAL BRANCH
**Chapter:** 28 — CLERKS OF THE CIRCUIT COURTS
**Part:** IV — CAPITAL COLLATERAL REPRESENTATION
**Section:** 28.212
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The clerk may keep minutes of court proceedings. The action of the court shall be noted in the minutes, but orders and judgments shall not be recorded in the minutes.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 72-320.
