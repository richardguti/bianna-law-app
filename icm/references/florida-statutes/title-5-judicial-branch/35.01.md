# Fla. Stat. § 35.01 — District courts of appeal; districts.

**Title:** V — JUDICIAL BRANCH
**Chapter:** 34 — COUNTY COURTS
**Part:** IV — CAPITAL COLLATERAL REPRESENTATION
**Section:** 35.01
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Six district courts of appeal are created, and the state is divided into six appellate districts of contiguous circuits.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 57-248; s. 1, ch. 65-294; s. 1, ch. 79-413; s. 4, ch. 2022-163.
