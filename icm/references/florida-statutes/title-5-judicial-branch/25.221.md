# Fla. Stat. § 25.221 — Maintenance of books, records, and other materials.

**Title:** V — JUDICIAL BRANCH
**Chapter:** 25 — SUPREME COURT
**Part:** III — CUSTOMER SERVICE STANDARDS
**Section:** 25.221
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

All books, papers, records, files, and the seal of the Supreme Court must be maintained by the clerk and be in the clerk's control, as prescribed by the Supreme Court.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 57-274; s. 102, ch. 95-147; s. 1, ch. 2021-230.
