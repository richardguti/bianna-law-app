# Fla. Stat. § 28.215 — Pro se assistance.

**Title:** V — JUDICIAL BRANCH
**Chapter:** 28 — CLERKS OF THE CIRCUIT COURTS
**Part:** IV — CAPITAL COLLATERAL REPRESENTATION
**Section:** 28.215
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The clerk of the circuit court shall provide ministerial assistance to pro se litigants. Assistance shall not include the provision of legal advice.

**History:** s. 27, ch. 2003-402.
