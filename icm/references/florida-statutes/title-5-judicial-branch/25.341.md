# Fla. Stat. § 25.341 — Library of Supreme Court, custodian.

**Title:** V — JUDICIAL BRANCH
**Chapter:** 25 — SUPREME COURT
**Part:** III — CUSTOMER SERVICE STANDARDS
**Section:** 25.341
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The library of the Supreme Court shall be in custody of the librarian appointed by the court, who shall be subject to its direction.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 57-274.
