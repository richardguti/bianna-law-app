# Fla. Stat. § 35.24 — Location of clerk's office.

**Title:** V — JUDICIAL BRANCH
**Chapter:** 35 — DISTRICT COURTS OF APPEAL
**Part:** IV — CAPITAL COLLATERAL REPRESENTATION
**Section:** 35.24
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Each clerk shall have an office at the headquarters of the district court of appeal.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 57-248; s. 203, ch. 95-147; s. 6, ch. 2021-230.
