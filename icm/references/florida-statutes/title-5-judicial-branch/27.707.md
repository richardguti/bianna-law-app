# Fla. Stat. § 27.707 — Investigators; service of process.

**Title:** V — JUDICIAL BRANCH
**Chapter:** 27 — STATE ATTORNEYS; PUBLIC DEFENDERS; RELATED OFFICES
**Part:** IV — CAPITAL COLLATERAL REPRESENTATION
**Section:** 27.707
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Each investigator employed by the capital collateral regional counsel has full authority to serve any subpoena or court order issued by any court or judge in any case for which the office has responsibility for providing representation.

**History:** s. 3, ch. 85-332; s. 7, ch. F.S. 27.706627.706
