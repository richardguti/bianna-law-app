# Fla. Stat. § 27.35 — Salaries of state attorneys.

**Title:** V — JUDICIAL BRANCH
**Chapter:** 27 — STATE ATTORNEYS; PUBLIC DEFENDERS; RELATED OFFICES
**Part:** II — STATE ATTORNEYS
**Section:** 27.35
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Each state attorney shall receive as salary the amount provided in the General Appropriations Act.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 89-307; s. 4, ch. 95-280; s. 17, ch. 2001-56.
