# Fla. Stat. § 28.34 — Salary discrimination based on gender or race; review within the county and circuit courts.

**Title:** V — JUDICIAL BRANCH
**Chapter:** 28 — CLERKS OF THE CIRCUIT COURTS
**Part:** IV — CAPITAL COLLATERAL REPRESENTATION
**Section:** 28.34
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Each clerk of the court shall undertake an annual review of compensation, race, and gender employment policies for all persons employed or appointed by the clerk. Within the context of comparable positions, skills, experience, and responsibility, any inequities found to exist on the basis of gender or race shall be eliminated.

**History:** s. 6, ch. 91-74; s. 14, ch. 94-348.
