# Fla. Stat. § 27.715 — Capital Collateral Regional Counsel Trust Fund.

**Title:** V — JUDICIAL BRANCH
**Chapter:** 27 — STATE ATTORNEYS; PUBLIC DEFENDERS; RELATED OFFICES
**Part:** IV — CAPITAL COLLATERAL REPRESENTATION
**Section:** 27.715
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The Capital Collateral Regional Counsel Trust Fund is created within the Justice Administrative Commission. Moneys credited to the trust fund shall be used for the purpose of funding the activities of the capital collateral regional counsel.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 2009-62; s. 2, ch. 2013-12.
