# Fla. Stat. § 35.065 — Review of judgment or order certified by county court to be of great public importance.

**Title:** V — JUDICIAL BRANCH
**Chapter:** 35 — DISTRICT COURTS OF APPEAL
**Part:** IV — CAPITAL COLLATERAL REPRESENTATION
**Section:** 35.065
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Pursuant to s. 34.017, a district court of appeal may review any order or judgment of a county court which is certified by the county court to be of great public importance.

**History:** s. 4, ch. 84-303; s. 7, ch. 2020-61.
