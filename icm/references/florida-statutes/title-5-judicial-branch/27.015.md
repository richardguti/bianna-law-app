# Fla. Stat. § 27.015 — Private practice prohibited.

**Title:** V — JUDICIAL BRANCH
**Chapter:** 27 — STATE ATTORNEYS; PUBLIC DEFENDERS; RELATED OFFICES
**Part:** II — STATE ATTORNEYS
**Section:** 27.015
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

All state attorneys elected to said office shall be so elected on a full-time basis and shall be prohibited from the private practice of law while holding said office.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 70-79; s. 11, ch. 2002-1.
