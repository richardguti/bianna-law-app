# Fla. Stat. § 34.031 — Clerk.

**Title:** V — JUDICIAL BRANCH
**Chapter:** 34 — COUNTY COURTS
**Part:** IV — CAPITAL COLLATERAL REPRESENTATION
**Section:** 34.031
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The clerk of the circuit court shall be clerk of the county court unless otherwise provided by law.

**History:** s. 11, ch. 72-404.
