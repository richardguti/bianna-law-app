# Fla. Stat. § 39.813 — Continuing jurisdiction.

**Title:** V — JUDICIAL BRANCH
**Chapter:** 39 — PROCEEDINGS RELATING TO CHILDREN
**Part:** X — TERMINATION OF PARENTAL RIGHTS
**Section:** 39.813
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The court which terminates the parental rights of a child who is the subject of termination proceedings pursuant to this chapter shall retain exclusive jurisdiction in all matters pertaining to the child's adoption pursuant to chapter 63.

**Note:** Former s. 39.47.
