# Fla. Stat. § 27.03 — Duties before grand jury.

**Title:** V — JUDICIAL BRANCH
**Chapter:** 27 — STATE ATTORNEYS; PUBLIC DEFENDERS; RELATED OFFICES
**Part:** II — STATE ATTORNEYS
**Section:** 27.03
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Whenever required by the grand jury, the state attorney shall attend them for the purpose of examining witnesses in their presence, or of giving legal advice in any matter before them; and he or she shall prepare bills of indictment.
