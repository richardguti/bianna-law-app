# Fla. Stat. § 34.08 — Compensation of sheriff.

**Title:** V — JUDICIAL BRANCH
**Chapter:** 34 — COUNTY COURTS
**Part:** IV — CAPITAL COLLATERAL REPRESENTATION
**Section:** 34.08
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The compensation of the sheriff for serving processes in cases in the county court, and for other services in connection therewith, shall be the same as that for like services in the circuit court.

**History:** RS 2839; GS 3897; RGS 5994; CGL 8288.
