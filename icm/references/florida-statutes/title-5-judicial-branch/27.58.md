# Fla. Stat. § 27.58 — Administration of public defender services.

**Title:** V — JUDICIAL BRANCH
**Chapter:** 27 — STATE ATTORNEYS; PUBLIC DEFENDERS; RELATED OFFICES
**Part:** IV — CAPITAL COLLATERAL REPRESENTATION
**Section:** 27.58
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The public defender of each judicial circuit of the state shall be the chief administrator of all public defender services authorized under s. 27.51 within the circuit.

**History:** s. 7, ch. 63-409; s. 3, ch. 2000-343; s. 23, ch. 2003-402.
