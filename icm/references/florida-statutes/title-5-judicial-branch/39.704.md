# Fla. Stat. § 39.704 — Exemptions from judicial review.

**Title:** V — JUDICIAL BRANCH
**Chapter:** 39 — PROCEEDINGS RELATING TO CHILDREN
**Part:** IX — JUDICIAL REVIEWS
**Section:** 39.704
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Judicial review does not apply to:
(1)  
Minors who have been placed in adoptive homes by a licensed child-placing agency; or

(2)  
Minors who are refugees or entrants to whom federal regulations apply and who are in the care of a social service agency.

**History:** s. 9, ch. 87-289; s. 14, ch. 90-306; s. 81, ch. 98-403; s. 41, ch. 99-193.

**Note:** Former s. 39.456.
