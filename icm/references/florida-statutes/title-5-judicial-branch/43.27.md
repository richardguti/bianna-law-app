# Fla. Stat. § 43.27 — Office hours of clerks of court.

**Title:** V — JUDICIAL BRANCH
**Chapter:** 43 — COURTS: GENERAL PROVISIONS
**Part:** XII — DOMESTIC VIOLENCE
**Section:** 43.27
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

There may be a trial court administrator who shall perform such duties as the chief judge may direct.
