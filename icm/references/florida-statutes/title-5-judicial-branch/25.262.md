# Fla. Stat. § 25.262 — Duties of marshal, process.

**Title:** V — JUDICIAL BRANCH
**Chapter:** 25 — SUPREME COURT
**Part:** III — CUSTOMER SERVICE STANDARDS
**Section:** 25.262
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The marshal shall have the power to execute the process of the court throughout the state, and in any county he or she may deputize the sheriff or a deputy sheriff for such purpose.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 57-274; s. 105, ch. 95-147.
