# Fla. Stat. § 27.5302 — Salary discrimination based on gender or race; review within the office of public defender.

**Title:** V — JUDICIAL BRANCH
**Chapter:** 27 — STATE ATTORNEYS; PUBLIC DEFENDERS; RELATED OFFICES
**Part:** III — PUBLIC DEFENDERS AND OTHER COURT-APPOINTED COUNSEL
**Section:** 27.5302
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Each public defender shall undertake an annual review of compensation policies for the position of assistant public defender. Within the context of comparable skills, experience, and responsibility, any inequities found to exist on the basis of gender or race shall be eliminated.

**History:** s. 8, ch. 91-74.
