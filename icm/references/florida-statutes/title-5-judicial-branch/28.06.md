# Fla. Stat. § 28.06 — Power of clerk to appoint deputies.

**Title:** V — JUDICIAL BRANCH
**Chapter:** 28 — CLERKS OF THE CIRCUIT COURTS
**Part:** IV — CAPITAL COLLATERAL REPRESENTATION
**Section:** 28.06
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The clerk of the circuit court may appoint a deputy or deputies, for whose acts the clerk shall be liable, F.S. 28.05628.05
