# Fla. Stat. § 39.0135 — Operations and Maintenance Trust Fund.

**Title:** V — JUDICIAL BRANCH
**Chapter:** 39 — PROCEEDINGS RELATING TO CHILDREN
**Part:** I — GENERAL PROVISIONS
**Section:** 39.0135
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The department shall deposit all child support payments made to the department pursuant to this chapter into the Operations and Maintenance Trust Fund. The purpose of this funding is to care for children who are committed to the temporary legal custody of the department.

**History:** s. 87, ch. 86-220; s. 10, ch. 90-306; s. 16, ch. 16-010 --#">2016-10.

**Note:** Former ss. 39.415, 39.474.
