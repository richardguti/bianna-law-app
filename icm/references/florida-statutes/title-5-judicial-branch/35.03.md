# Fla. Stat. § 35.03 — Second Appellate District.

**Title:** V — JUDICIAL BRANCH
**Chapter:** 35 — DISTRICT COURTS OF APPEAL
**Part:** IV — CAPITAL COLLATERAL REPRESENTATION
**Section:** 35.03
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The Second Appellate District is composed of the Sixth, Twelfth, and Thirteenth Judicial Circuits.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 57-248; s. 1, ch. 65-294; s. 1, ch. 79-413; s. 6, ch. 2022-163.
