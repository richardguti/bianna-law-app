# Fla. Stat. § 35.08 — Power to execute its judgments.

**Title:** V — JUDICIAL BRANCH
**Chapter:** 35 — DISTRICT COURTS OF APPEAL
**Part:** IV — CAPITAL COLLATERAL REPRESENTATION
**Section:** 35.08
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Each district court of appeal is vested with all the power and authority necessary for carrying into complete execution all of its judgments, decrees, orders, and determinations in the matters before it agreeable to the usage and principles of law.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 57-248.
