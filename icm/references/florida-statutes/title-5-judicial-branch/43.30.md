# Fla. Stat. § 43.30 — Divisions of court.

**Title:** V — JUDICIAL BRANCH
**Chapter:** 43 — COURTS: GENERAL PROVISIONS
**Part:** XII — DOMESTIC VIOLENCE
**Section:** 43.30
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

All courts except the Supreme Court may sit in divisions as may be established by local rule approved by the Supreme Court.

**History:** s. 25, ch. 72-404.
