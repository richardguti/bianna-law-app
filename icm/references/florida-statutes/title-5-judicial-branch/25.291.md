# Fla. Stat. § 25.291 — Fines for contempt.

**Title:** V — JUDICIAL BRANCH
**Chapter:** 25 — SUPREME COURT
**Part:** III — CUSTOMER SERVICE STANDARDS
**Section:** 25.291
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Moneys derived from the imposition of fines for contempt of the Supreme Court of the state shall be deposited in the State Treasury into the General Revenue Fund unallocated.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 57-274; s. 1, ch. 80-145; s. 106, ch. 95-147; s. 2, ch. 2013-86.
