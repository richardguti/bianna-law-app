# Fla. Stat. § 34.171 — Salaries of bailiffs.

**Title:** V — JUDICIAL BRANCH
**Chapter:** 34 — COUNTY COURTS
**Part:** IV — CAPITAL COLLATERAL REPRESENTATION
**Section:** 34.171
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The county shall pay all reasonable salaries of bailiffs.

**History:** s. 15, ch. 72-404; s. 54, ch. 2003-402.
