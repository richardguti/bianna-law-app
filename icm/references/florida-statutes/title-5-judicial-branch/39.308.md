# Fla. Stat. § 39.308 — Guidelines for onsite child protective investigation.

**Title:** V — JUDICIAL BRANCH
**Chapter:** 39 — PROCEEDINGS RELATING TO CHILDREN
**Part:** IV — TAKING CHILDREN INTO CUSTODY AND SHELTER HEARINGS
**Section:** 39.308
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The Department of Children and Families shall develop guidelines for conducting an onsite child protective investigation that specifically does not require the additional activities required by the department and for conducting an enhanced child protective investigation, including determining whether compelling evidence exists that no maltreatment occurred, conducting collateral contacts, contacting the reporter, updating the risk assessment, and providing for differential levels of documentation between an onsite and an enhanced onsite child protective investigation.

**History:** s. 11, ch. 2003-127; s. 18, ch. 2014-19; s. 7, ch. 2023-77.
