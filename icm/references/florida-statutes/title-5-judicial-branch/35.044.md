# Fla. Stat. § 35.044 — Sixth Appellate District.

**Title:** V — JUDICIAL BRANCH
**Chapter:** 35 — DISTRICT COURTS OF APPEAL
**Part:** IV — CAPITAL COLLATERAL REPRESENTATION
**Section:** 35.044
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The Sixth Appellate District is composed of the Ninth, Tenth, and Twentieth Judicial Circuits.

**History:** s. 8, ch. 2022-163.
