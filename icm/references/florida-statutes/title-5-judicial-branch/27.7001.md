# Fla. Stat. § 27.7001 — Legislative intent and findings.

**Title:** V — JUDICIAL BRANCH
**Chapter:** 27 — STATE ATTORNEYS; PUBLIC DEFENDERS; RELATED OFFICES
**Part:** IV — CAPITAL COLLATERAL REPRESENTATION
**Section:** 27.7001
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

——

It is the intent of the Legislature to create part IV of this chapter, consisting of ss. Limitation on collateral representation; lawyer disqualification; use of state funds for excess fees not authorized.
