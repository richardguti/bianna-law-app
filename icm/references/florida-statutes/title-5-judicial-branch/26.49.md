# Fla. Stat. § 26.49 — Executive officer of circuit court.

**Title:** V — JUDICIAL BRANCH
**Chapter:** 26 — CIRCUIT COURTS
**Part:** III — CUSTOMER SERVICE STANDARDS
**Section:** 26.49
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The sheriff of the county shall be the executive officer of the circuit court of the county.

**History:** s. 14, ch. 4, 1845; RS 1396; GS 1841; RGS 3086; CGL 4869.
