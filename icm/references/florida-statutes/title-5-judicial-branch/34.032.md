# Fla. Stat. § 34.032 — Power of clerk to appoint deputies.

**Title:** V — JUDICIAL BRANCH
**Chapter:** 34 — COUNTY COURTS
**Part:** IV — CAPITAL COLLATERAL REPRESENTATION
**Section:** 34.032
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
With the concurrence of the chief circuit judge of the circuit, the clerk of the circuit court, in his or her capacity as clerk of the county court, may appoint a deputy clerk or clerks of the county court, for whose acts the clerk shall be liable, and the said deputies shall have and exercise each and every power of whatsoever nature and kind as the clerk may exercise as clerk of the county court, except the power to appoint a deputy or deputies.
