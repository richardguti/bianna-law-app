# Fla. Stat. § 30.61 — Establishment of civilian oversight boards.

**Title:** V — JUDICIAL BRANCH
**Chapter:** 30 — SHERIFFS
**Part:** IV — CAPITAL COLLATERAL REPRESENTATION
**Section:** 30.61
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
A county sheriff may establish a civilian oversight board to review the policies and procedures of his or her office and its subdivisions.

(2)  
The board must be composed of at least three and up to seven members appointed by the sheriff, one of whom shall be a retired law enforcement officer.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 2024-86; s. 2, ch. 2025-6.
