# Fla. Stat. § 38.04 — Sworn statement by judge holding himself or herself qualified.

**Title:** V — JUDICIAL BRANCH
**Chapter:** 38 — JUDGES: GENERAL PROVISIONS
**Part:** IV — CAPITAL COLLATERAL REPRESENTATION
**Section:** 38.04
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Whenever any judge shall enter an order under s. 38.02 declaring qualification to act in said cause, he or she shall contemporaneously therewith file therein a sworn statement that to the best of his or her knowledge and belief the ground or grounds of the disqualification named in the suggestion do not exist.

**History:** s. 5, ch. 16053, 1933; CGL 1936 Supp. 4155(4); s. 208, ch. 95-147.
