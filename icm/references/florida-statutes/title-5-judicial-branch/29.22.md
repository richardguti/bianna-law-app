# Fla. Stat. § 29.22 — State Courts Revenue Trust Fund.

**Title:** V — JUDICIAL BRANCH
**Chapter:** 29 — COURT SYSTEM FUNDING
**Part:** IV — CAPITAL COLLATERAL REPRESENTATION
**Section:** 29.22
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The State Courts Revenue Trust Fund is created within the state courts system. Moneys credited to the trust fund shall be used for the purpose of funding the activities of the state courts system.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 2009-7; s. 2, ch. 2011-19.F.S. 29.21629.21
