# Fla. Stat. § 30.077 — Conduct of hearing.

**Title:** V — JUDICIAL BRANCH
**Chapter:** 30 — SHERIFFS
**Part:** IV — CAPITAL COLLATERAL REPRESENTATION
**Section:** 30.077
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

A review board shall determine the truth while maintaining an atmosphere of fundamental fairness.

**History:** s. 8, ch. 94-143.
