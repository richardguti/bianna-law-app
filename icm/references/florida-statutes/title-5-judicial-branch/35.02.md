# Fla. Stat. § 35.02 — First Appellate District.

**Title:** V — JUDICIAL BRANCH
**Chapter:** 34 — COUNTY COURTS
**Part:** IV — CAPITAL COLLATERAL REPRESENTATION
**Section:** 35.02
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The First Appellate District is composed of the First, Second, Third, Eighth, and Fourteenth Judicial Circuits.
