# Fla. Stat. § 332.13 — Multicounty airport authorities created as independent special districts; noise-mitigation-project fund.

**Title:** XXV — AVIATION
**Chapter:** 332 — AIRPORTS AND OTHER AIR NAVIGATION FACILITIES
**Part:** III — SPACEFLIGHT
**Section:** 332.13
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Any multicounty airport authority created as an independent special district which is subject to a development-of-regional-impact development order and which has conducted a noise study in accordance with 14 C.F.R. part 150 shall, in fiscal year 2002, establish a noise-mitigation-project fund in an amount of $7.5 million, which shall be increased by another $2.5 million in fiscal year 2004. The moneys in the project fund shall be segregated and expended by the airport authority by December 31, 2006, to the extent necessary to comply with development order commitments to acquire property from or otherwise mitigate property owners adversely affected by the development of regional impact.
