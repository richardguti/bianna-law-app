# Fla. Stat. § 332.005 — Restrictions on authority of Department of Transportation.

**Title:** XXV — AVIATION
**Chapter:** 332 — AIRPORTS AND OTHER AIR NAVIGATION FACILITIES
**Part:** III — SPACEFLIGHT
**Section:** 332.005
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(15)  
“Sponsor” means any eligible agency which, either individually or jointly with one or more eligible agencies, submits to the department an application for financial assistance for an airport development project in accordance with this act.
