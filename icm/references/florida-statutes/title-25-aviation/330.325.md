# Fla. Stat. § 330.325 — Injunctive relief.

**Title:** XXV — AVIATION
**Chapter:** 330 — REGULATION OF AIRCRAFT, PILOTS, AND AIRPORTS
**Part:** II — VESSEL REGISTRATION
**Section:** 330.325
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The department may institute a civil action for injunctive relief in the appropriate circuit court to prevent the violation of any provision of this chapter.

**History:** s. 5, ch. 84-205.
