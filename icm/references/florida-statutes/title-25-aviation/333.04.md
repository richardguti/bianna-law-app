# Fla. Stat. § 333.04 — Comprehensive zoning regulations; most stringent to prevail where conflicts occur.

**Title:** XXV — AVIATION
**Chapter:** 333 — AIRPORT ZONING
**Part:** III — SPACEFLIGHT
**Section:** 333.04
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

——

(1)  
INCORPORATION.——
In the event that a political subdivision has adopted, or hereafter adopts, a comprehensive plan or policy regulating, among other things, the height of buildings, structures, and natural objects, and uses of property, any airport zoning regulations applicable to the same area or portion thereof may be incorporated in and made a part of such comprehensive plan or policy, and be administered and enforced in connection therewith.
