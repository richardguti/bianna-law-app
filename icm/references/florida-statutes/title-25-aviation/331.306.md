# Fla. Stat. § 331.306 — Federal airspace notification.

**Title:** XXV — AVIATION
**Chapter:** 331 — AVIATION AND AEROSPACE FACILITIES AND COMMERCE
**Part:** II — SPACE FLORIDA
**Section:** 331.306
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

In accordance with Federal Aviation Administration procedures, Space Florida shall file the appropriate federal notification to activate special use airspace in support of its launch operations.

**History:** ss. 6, 61, ch. 89-300; ss. 7, 8, ch. 90-192; s. 7, ch. 2006-60.
