# Fla. Stat. § 331.327 — Foreign trade zone.

**Title:** XXV — AVIATION
**Chapter:** 331 — AVIATION AND AEROSPACE FACILITIES AND COMMERCE
**Part:** II — SPACE FLORIDA
**Section:** 331.327
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Space Florida may apply to the Federal Government for a grant allowing the designation of any spaceport territory as a foreign trade zone pursuant to ss. 288.36 and 288.37. However, the designation of any spaceport territory as a foreign trade zone does not authorize an exemption from any tax imposed by the state or by any political subdivision, agency, or instrumentality thereof.

**History:** ss. 31, 61, ch. 89-300; ss. 7, 8, ch. 90-192; s. 26, ch. 2006-60.
