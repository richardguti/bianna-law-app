# Fla. Stat. § 330.29 — Administration and enforcement; rules; requirements for airport sites and airports.

**Title:** XXV — AVIATION
**Chapter:** 330 — REGULATION OF AIRCRAFT, PILOTS, AND AIRPORTS
**Part:** II — VESSEL REGISTRATION
**Section:** 330.29
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

It is the duty of the department to:
(1)  
Administer and enforce the provisions of this chapter.

(2)  
Establish requirements for airport site approval, licensure, and registration.

(3)  
Establish and maintain a state aviation facility data system to facilitate licensing and registration of all airports.

(4)  
Adopt rules pursuant to ss. 120.536(1) and 120.54 to implement the provisions of this chapter.
