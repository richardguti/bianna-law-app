# Fla. Stat. § 330.355 — Prohibition on landing fees for certain aircraft operations.

**Title:** XXV — AVIATION
**Chapter:** 330 — REGULATION OF AIRCRAFT, PILOTS, AND AIRPORTS
**Part:** II — VESSEL REGISTRATION
**Section:** 330.355
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

A publicly owned airport in this state may not charge a landing fee established on or after January 1, 2025, for aircraft operations conducted by an accredited nonprofit institution located in this state which offers a 4-year collegiate aviation program, if such aircraft operations are for flight training necessary for pilot certification and proficiency.

**History:** s. 12, ch. 2025-149; s. 14, ch. 2025-155.
