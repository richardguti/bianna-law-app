# Fla. Stat. § 159.816 — Certification as to state volume limitation.

**Title:** XI — COUNTY ORGANIZATION AND INTERGOVERNMENTAL RELATIONS
**Chapter:** 159 — BOND FINANCING
**Part:** VII — TAXABLE BONDS
**Section:** 159.816
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Following the timely filing of an issuance report, the director of the division shall execute a final certification of allocation.

**History:** s. 11, ch. 87-222; s. 29, ch. 2024-220.
