# Fla. Stat. § 142.16 — Change of venue.

**Title:** XI — COUNTY ORGANIZATION AND INTERGOVERNMENTAL RELATIONS
**Chapter:** 145 — COMPENSATION OF COUNTY OFFICIALS
**Part:** VI — VOLUNTEERS
**Section:** 142.16
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

In case of change of venue in any case, all fines and forfeitures in such case go to the clerk in the county in which the case was adjudicated.

**History:** s. 10, ch. 4323, 1895; GS 978; RGS 1789; CGL 2840; s. 85, ch. 2003-402.
