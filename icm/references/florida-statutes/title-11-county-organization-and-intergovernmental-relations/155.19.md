# Fla. Stat. § 155.19 — County hospitals; training school for nurses.

**Title:** XI — COUNTY ORGANIZATION AND INTERGOVERNMENTAL RELATIONS
**Chapter:** 155 — HOSPITALS
**Part:** V — PRIMARY CARE FOR CHILDREN AND FAMILIES
**Section:** 155.19
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The board of trustees may establish and maintain in connection with such hospital and as part thereof, a training school for nurses, and upon completion of the prescribed course of training shall give to such nurses who satisfactorily complete the said course, a diploma.

**History:** s. 16, ch. 20905, 1941.
