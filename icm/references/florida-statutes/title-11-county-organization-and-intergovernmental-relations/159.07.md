# Fla. Stat. § 159.07 — Construction of projects.

**Title:** XI — COUNTY ORGANIZATION AND INTERGOVERNMENTAL RELATIONS
**Chapter:** 159 — BOND FINANCING
**Part:** I — REVENUE BOND ACT OF 1953
**Section:** 159.07
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The governing body of any unit is hereby authorized and empowered to construct, whenever it shall deem such construction expedient, any self-liquidating project as hereinabove defined.

**History:** s. 7, ch. 28045, 1953.
