# Fla. Stat. § 159.7095 — Issuance of bonds.

**Title:** XI — COUNTY ORGANIZATION AND INTERGOVERNMENTAL RELATIONS
**Chapter:** 159 — BOND FINANCING
**Part:** VI — PRIVATE ACTIVITY BONDS
**Section:** 159.7095
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The bonds issued under ss. 159.701-159.7095 may be validated in the manner prescribed by chapter 75.

**History:** s. 4, ch. 79-101.
