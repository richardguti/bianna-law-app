# Fla. Stat. § 125.0107 — Ordinances relating to possession or sale of ammunition.

**Title:** XI — COUNTY ORGANIZATION AND INTERGOVERNMENTAL RELATIONS
**Chapter:** 125 — COUNTY GOVERNMENT
**Part:** I — COUNTY COMMISSIONERS: POWERS AND DUTIES
**Section:** 125.0107
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

No county may adopt any ordinance relating to the possession or sale of ammunition. Any such ordinance in effect on June 24, 1983, is void.

**History:** s. 2, ch. 83-253.
