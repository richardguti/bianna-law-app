# Fla. Stat. § 159.53 — Construction.

**Title:** XI — COUNTY ORGANIZATION AND INTERGOVERNMENTAL RELATIONS
**Chapter:** 159 — BOND FINANCING
**Part:** III — INDUSTRIAL DEVELOPMENT AUTHORITIES
**Section:** 159.53
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Sections 159.44-159.53, being necessary for the prosperity and welfare of the state and its inhabitants, shall be liberally construed to effect the purposes thereof.

**History:** s. 10, ch. 70-229.
