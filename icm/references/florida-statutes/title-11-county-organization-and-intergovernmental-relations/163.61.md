# Fla. Stat. § 163.61 — “Agency” defined.

**Title:** XI — COUNTY ORGANIZATION AND INTERGOVERNMENTAL RELATIONS
**Chapter:** 163 — INTERGOVERNMENTAL PROGRAMS
**Part:** VI — COLLABORATIVE CLIENT INFORMATION SYSTEMS
**Section:** 163.61
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

For the purposes of ss. 163.61-163.65, the word “agency” has the meaning ascribed in s. 119.011.

**History:** s. 43, ch. 97-286.
