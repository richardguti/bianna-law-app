# Fla. Stat. § 159.835 — Rules.

**Title:** XI — COUNTY ORGANIZATION AND INTERGOVERNMENTAL RELATIONS
**Chapter:** 159 — BOND FINANCING
**Part:** IX — QUALIFIED SCHOOL CONSTRUCTION BONDS
**Section:** 159.835
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The board and the department shall adopt any rules necessary to ensure the orderly implementation and administration of this act.

**History:** s. 49, ch. 2003-391.
