# Fla. Stat. § 132.09 — Sale of bonds.

**Title:** XI — COUNTY ORGANIZATION AND INTERGOVERNMENTAL RELATIONS
**Chapter:** 132 — GENERAL REFUNDING LAW
**Part:** VI — VOLUNTEERS
**Section:** 132.09
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

When sold, the refunding bonds (except as otherwise expressly provided) shall be sold in the manner provided in s. 218.385.

**History:** s. 8, ch. 15772, 1931; CGL 1936 Supp. 2383(8).
