# Fla. Stat. § 159.345 — Local agency reporting requirement.

**Title:** XI — COUNTY ORGANIZATION AND INTERGOVERNMENTAL RELATIONS
**Chapter:** 159 — BOND FINANCING
**Part:** II — FLORIDA INDUSTRIAL DEVELOPMENT FINANCING ACT
**Section:** 159.345
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Any local agency which issues any revenue bonds pursuant to this part shall supply the Division of Bond Finance of the State Board of Administration with a copy of the report required in s. 103 of the Internal Revenue Code of 1954, as amended, at the times required pursuant to that section.

**History:** s. 21, ch. 83-271; s. 5, ch. 86-181; s. 144, ch. 92-279; s. 55, ch. 92-326; s. 12, ch. 95-196; s. 13, ch. 2000-158.
