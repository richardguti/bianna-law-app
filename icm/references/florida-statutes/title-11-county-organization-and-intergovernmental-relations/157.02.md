# Fla. Stat. § 157.02 — Action on petition by county commissioners.

**Title:** XI — COUNTY ORGANIZATION AND INTERGOVERNMENTAL RELATIONS
**Chapter:** 157 — DRAINAGE BY COUNTIES
**Part:** V — PRIMARY CARE FOR CHILDREN AND FAMILIES
**Section:** 157.02
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Should the county commissioners, when the petition mentioned in s. 157.01, has been considered, deem it improper to grant the same it shall then be denied, but should they deem it proper to grant the same, they shall then enter an order of record that the said petition be granted.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 5035, 1901; GS 950; s. 1, ch. 6457, 1913; s. 1, ch. 6958, 1915; s. 1, ch. 7307, 1917; RGS 1734; CGL 2785; s. 7, ch. 90-279.
