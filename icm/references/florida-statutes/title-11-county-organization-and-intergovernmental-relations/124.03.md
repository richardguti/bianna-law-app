# Fla. Stat. § 124.03 — Description of district boundaries to be furnished Department of State.

**Title:** XI — COUNTY ORGANIZATION AND INTERGOVERNMENTAL RELATIONS
**Chapter:** 125 — COUNTY GOVERNMENT
**Part:** III — FLORIDA RETIREMENT SYSTEM CONTRIBUTION RATES
**Section:** 124.03
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Whenever the boundaries of existing county commissioners' districts are, from time to time, changed by the board, it shall cause its clerk to forthwith furnish the Department of State with a certified copy of its minutes, reflecting the description of the boundaries of the district, as changed, which shall record a description of such boundaries in its office in a book kept for that purpose.

**History:** s. 6, ch. 3723, 1887; RS 576; GS 767; RGS 1471; CGL 2149; s. 1, ch. 24108, 1947; ss. 10, 35, ch. 69-106.
