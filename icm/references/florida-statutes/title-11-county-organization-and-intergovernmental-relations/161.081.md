# Fla. Stat. § 161.081 — Powers of Department of Legal Affairs.

**Title:** XI — COUNTY ORGANIZATION AND INTERGOVERNMENTAL RELATIONS
**Chapter:** 161 — BEACH AND SHORE PRESERVATION
**Part:** I — REGULATION OF CONSTRUCTION, RECONSTRUCTION, AND OTHER PHYSICAL ACTIVITY
**Section:** 161.081
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

When a permit is required under this part and has not been issued as provided herein, any such project or physical activity shall be considered a public nuisance and the Department of Legal Affairs may at the request of the Department of Environmental Protection institute proceedings to enjoin or abate such nuisance.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 65-408; ss. 11, 25, 35, ch. 69-106; s. 1, ch. 95-150.
