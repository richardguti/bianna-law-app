# Fla. Stat. § 161.131 — Construction of ss. 161.011-161.212.

**Title:** XI — COUNTY ORGANIZATION AND INTERGOVERNMENTAL RELATIONS
**Chapter:** 161 — BEACH AND SHORE PRESERVATION
**Part:** I — REGULATION OF CONSTRUCTION, RECONSTRUCTION, AND OTHER PHYSICAL ACTIVITY
**Section:** 161.131
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The provisions of ss. 161.011-161.212 shall be liberally construed by all concerned in a manner to best accomplish the beach and shore preservation purposes and programs.

**History:** s. 3, ch. 65-408; s. 6, ch. 86-138.
