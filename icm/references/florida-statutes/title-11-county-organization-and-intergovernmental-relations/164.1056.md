# Fla. Stat. § 164.1056 — Final resolution.

**Title:** XI — COUNTY ORGANIZATION AND INTERGOVERNMENTAL RELATIONS
**Chapter:** 164 — GOVERNMENTAL DISPUTES
**Part:** VI — COLLABORATIVE CLIENT INFORMATION SYSTEMS
**Section:** 164.1056
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

If there is failure to resolve a conflict between governmental entities through the procedures provided by ss. 164.1053 and 164.1055, the entities participating in the dispute resolution process may avail themselves of any otherwise available legal rights.

**History:** s. 9, ch. 99-279.
