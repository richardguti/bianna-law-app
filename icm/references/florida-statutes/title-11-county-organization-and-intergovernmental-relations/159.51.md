# Fla. Stat. § 159.51 — Powers of chapter supplemental.

**Title:** XI — COUNTY ORGANIZATION AND INTERGOVERNMENTAL RELATIONS
**Chapter:** 159 — BOND FINANCING
**Part:** III — INDUSTRIAL DEVELOPMENT AUTHORITIES
**Section:** 159.51
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The powers conferred by ss. 159.44-159.53 shall be in addition and supplementary to existing powers and statutes, and said sections shall not be construed as repealing any of the provisions of any other law, general or local.
