# Fla. Stat. § 161.111 — Shore erosion emergency.

**Title:** XI — COUNTY ORGANIZATION AND INTERGOVERNMENTAL RELATIONS
**Chapter:** 161 — BEACH AND SHORE PRESERVATION
**Part:** I — REGULATION OF CONSTRUCTION, RECONSTRUCTION, AND OTHER PHYSICAL ACTIVITY
**Section:** 161.111
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

If a shore erosion emergency is declared by the Governor, the state, acting through the department, may spend whatever state funds are available to alleviate shore erosion.
