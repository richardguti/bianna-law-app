# Fla. Stat. § 157.34 — Reassessments to have effect as original assessment.

**Title:** XI — COUNTY ORGANIZATION AND INTERGOVERNMENTAL RELATIONS
**Chapter:** 157 — DRAINAGE BY COUNTIES
**Part:** V — PRIMARY CARE FOR CHILDREN AND FAMILIES
**Section:** 157.34
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

All assessments made pursuant to the provisions of ss. 157.30-157.32, shall have the same force and effect as is provided in cases of original assessments, and payment thereof shall be provided for and be enforced in the same manner.

**History:** s. 5, ch. 6963, 1915; RGS 1772; CGL 2823.
