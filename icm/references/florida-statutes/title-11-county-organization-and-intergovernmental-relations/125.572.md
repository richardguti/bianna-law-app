# Fla. Stat. § 125.572 — Regulation of synthetic turf.

**Title:** XI — COUNTY ORGANIZATION AND INTERGOVERNMENTAL RELATIONS
**Chapter:** 125 — COUNTY GOVERNMENT
**Part:** I — COUNTY COMMISSIONERS: POWERS AND DUTIES
**Section:** 125.572
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

A local government may not require an owner of a residence to obtain a permit to paint such residence, regardless of whether the residence is owned by a limited liability company.

**History:** s. 15, ch. 2017-149.
