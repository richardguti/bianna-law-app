# Fla. Stat. § 159.842 — Purpose.

**Title:** XI — COUNTY ORGANIZATION AND INTERGOVERNMENTAL RELATIONS
**Chapter:** 159 — BOND FINANCING
**Part:** IX — QUALIFIED SCHOOL CONSTRUCTION BONDS
**Section:** 159.842
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The purpose of this part is to allocate the state volume limitation imposed by s. 54F(d) of the code on qualified school construction bonds to finance qualified school construction facilities. Any bond issued which uses a portion of the limitation imposed by s. 54F(d)(1) of the code, or uses a portion of the limitation reallocated to the state pursuant to s. 54F(d)(2)(D) of the code, may not be issued in this state unless a written confirmation therefor is issued pursuant to this part.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 2009-59.
