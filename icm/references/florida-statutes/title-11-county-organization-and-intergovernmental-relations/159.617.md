# Fla. Stat. § 159.617 — Remedies of an obligee of a housing finance authority.

**Title:** XI — COUNTY ORGANIZATION AND INTERGOVERNMENTAL RELATIONS
**Chapter:** 159 — BOND FINANCING
**Part:** IV — HOUSING FINANCE AUTHORITIES
**Section:** 159.617
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

An obligee of a housing finance authority shall have the right, in addition to all other rights which may be conferred on such obligee, subject only to any contractual restrictions binding upon such obligee:
(1)  
F.S. 159.6166159.616  
Actions to contest validity of bonds.
——

An action or proceeding to contest the validity of any bond issued under this act, other than a proceeding pursuant to s. 159.615, must be commenced within 30 days after notification in a newspaper of general circulation within the area of the passage by the housing finance authority of the resolution authorizing the issuance of such bond.

**History:** s. 16, ch. 78-89.
