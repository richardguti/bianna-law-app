# Fla. Stat. § 161.151 — Definitions; ss. 161.141-161.211.

**Title:** XI — COUNTY ORGANIZATION AND INTERGOVERNMENTAL RELATIONS
**Chapter:** 161 — BEACH AND SHORE PRESERVATION
**Part:** I — REGULATION OF CONSTRUCTION, RECONSTRUCTION, AND OTHER PHYSICAL ACTIVITY
**Section:** 161.151
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

As used in ss. 161.141-161.211:
(1)  
“Board of trustees” means the Board of Trustees of the Internal Improvement Trust Fund.

(2)  
“Requesting authority” means any coastal county, municipality, or beach erosion control district which requests a survey by the board of trustees under the provisions of ss. 161.141-161.211.
