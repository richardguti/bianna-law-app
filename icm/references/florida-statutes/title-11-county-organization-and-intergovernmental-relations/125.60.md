# Fla. Stat. § 125.60 — Adoption of county charter.

**Title:** XI — COUNTY ORGANIZATION AND INTERGOVERNMENTAL RELATIONS
**Chapter:** 125 — COUNTY GOVERNMENT
**Part:** I — COUNTY COMMISSIONERS: POWERS AND DUTIES
**Section:** 125.60
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Any county not having a chartered form of consolidated government may, pursuant to the provisions of ss. 125.60-125.64, locally initiate and adopt by a majority vote of the qualified electors of the county a county home rule charter.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 69-45.
