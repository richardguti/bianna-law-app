# Fla. Stat. § 164.1057 — Execution of resolution of conflict.

**Title:** XI — COUNTY ORGANIZATION AND INTERGOVERNMENTAL RELATIONS
**Chapter:** 164 — GOVERNMENTAL DISPUTES
**Part:** VI — COLLABORATIVE CLIENT INFORMATION SYSTEMS
**Section:** 164.1057
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Resolution of a conflict at any phase shall require passage of an ordinance, resolution, or interlocal agreement that reflects the terms or conditions of the resolution to the conflict.

**History:** s. 10, ch. 99-279.
