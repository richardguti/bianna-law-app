# Fla. Stat. § 159.14 — Alternative method.

**Title:** XI — COUNTY ORGANIZATION AND INTERGOVERNMENTAL RELATIONS
**Chapter:** 159 — BOND FINANCING
**Part:** I — REVENUE BOND ACT OF 1953
**Section:** 159.14
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

This part shall be deemed to provide an additional and alternative method for the doing of the things authorized hereby and shall be regarded as supplemental and additional to powers conferred by other laws, and shall not be regarded as in derogation of any powers now existing. This part, being necessary for the welfare of the inhabitants of the counties and municipalities of the state, shall be liberally construed to effect the purposes thereof.

**History:** s. 14, ch. 28045, 1953.
