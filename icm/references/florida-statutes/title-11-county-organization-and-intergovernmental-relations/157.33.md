# Fla. Stat. § 157.33 — Issuance of scrip to take up former scrip or bonds.

**Title:** XI — COUNTY ORGANIZATION AND INTERGOVERNMENTAL RELATIONS
**Chapter:** 157 — DRAINAGE BY COUNTIES
**Part:** V — PRIMARY CARE FOR CHILDREN AND FAMILIES
**Section:** 157.33
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

After the provisions of ss. 157.30-157.32 have been complied with, the board may issue scrip or bonds, as the case may be, to be delivered to the holder or holders of scrip or bonds issued pursuant to former proceedings upon surrender of the former scrip or bonds.

**History:** s. 4, ch. 6963, 1915; RGS 1771; CGL 2822; s. 43, ch. 77-104.
