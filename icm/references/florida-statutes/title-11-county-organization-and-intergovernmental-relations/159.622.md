# Fla. Stat. § 159.622 — Limitation on rates.

**Title:** XI — COUNTY ORGANIZATION AND INTERGOVERNMENTAL RELATIONS
**Chapter:** 159 — BOND FINANCING
**Part:** IV — HOUSING FINANCE AUTHORITIES
**Section:** 159.622
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The intent of this legislation is that consumers receive maximum possible benefits; therefore, no lending institution receiving proceeds of bond issues pursuant to this act may loan any of the proceeds of such bond issue at the rate violative of federal arbitrage regulations.

**History:** s. 24, ch. 78-89.
