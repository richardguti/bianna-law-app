# Fla. Stat. § 125.271 — Emergency medical services; county emergency medical service assessments.

**Title:** XI — COUNTY ORGANIZATION AND INTERGOVERNMENTAL RELATIONS
**Chapter:** 125 — COUNTY GOVERNMENT
**Part:** I — COUNTY COMMISSIONERS: POWERS AND DUTIES
**Section:** 125.271
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
As used in this section, the term “county” means:
(a)  
A county that is within a rural area of opportunity as designated by the Governor pursuant to s. 288.0656;

(b)  
A small county having a population of 75,000 or fewer on the effective date of this act which has levied at least 10 mills of ad valorem tax for the previous fiscal year; or

(c)  
A county that adopted an ordinance authorizing the imposition of an assessment for emergency medical services prior to January 1, 2002.

Once a county has qualified under this subsection, it always retains the qualification.

(2)  
A county may fund the costs of emergency medical services through the levy of a special assessment that apportions the cost among the property based on a reasonable methodology that charges a parcel in proportion to its benefits.

(3)  
The authorization provided in this section shall be construed to be general law authorization pursuant to ss. 1 and 9, Art. VII of the State Constitution.

(4)  
All special assessments for emergency medical services levied by a county prior to the effective date of this section are ratified and validated in all respects if they would haies that donate fire/rescue equipment, vehicles, or supplies directly to state, county, or local governmental entities having fire/rescue responsibilities shall not be held liable for civil damages resulting from use or possession of such items.
