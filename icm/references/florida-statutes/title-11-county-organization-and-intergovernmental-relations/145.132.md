# Fla. Stat. § 145.132 — Repeal of other laws relating to compensation of district school board members.

**Title:** XI — COUNTY ORGANIZATION AND INTERGOVERNMENTAL RELATIONS
**Chapter:** 145 — COMPENSATION OF COUNTY OFFICIALS
**Part:** VI — VOLUNTEERS
**Section:** 145.132
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

All local or special laws or general laws of local application enacted prior to July 1, 1993, which relate to compensation of district school board members are repealed.

**History:** s. 6, ch. 93-146.
