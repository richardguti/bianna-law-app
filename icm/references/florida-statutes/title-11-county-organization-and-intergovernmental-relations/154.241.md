# Fla. Stat. § 154.241 — Issuance of bonds.

**Title:** XI — COUNTY ORGANIZATION AND INTERGOVERNMENTAL RELATIONS
**Chapter:** 154 — PUBLIC HEALTH FACILITIES
**Part:** III — HEALTH FACILITIES AUTHORITIES
**Section:** 154.241
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Bonds issued under the provisions of this part shall be validated in the manner prescribed by chapter 75.

**History:** s. 21, ch. 74-323.
