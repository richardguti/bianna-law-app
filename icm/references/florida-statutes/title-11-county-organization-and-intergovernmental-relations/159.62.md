# Fla. Stat. § 159.62 — Liabilities of a housing finance authority.

**Title:** XI — COUNTY ORGANIZATION AND INTERGOVERNMENTAL RELATIONS
**Chapter:** 159 — BOND FINANCING
**Part:** IV — HOUSING FINANCE AUTHORITIES
**Section:** 159.62
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

In no event shall the liabilities, whether ex contractu or ex delicto, of a housing finance authority arising from the financing of any qualifying housing development be payable from any funds other than the revenues or receipts of such qualifying housing development.

**History:** s. 20, ch. 78-89.
