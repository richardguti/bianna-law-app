# Fla. Stat. § 161.41 — Construction of ss. 161.25-161.40.

**Title:** XI — COUNTY ORGANIZATION AND INTERGOVERNMENTAL RELATIONS
**Chapter:** 161 — BEACH AND SHORE PRESERVATION
**Part:** III — COASTAL ZONE PROTECTION
**Section:** 161.41
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The provisions of ss. 161.25-161.40 shall be liberally construed by all concerned in a manner to best accomplish the beach and shore preservation purposes and programs.

**History:** s. 3, ch. 65-408.
