# Fla. Stat. § 159.845 — Rules.

**Title:** XI — COUNTY ORGANIZATION AND INTERGOVERNMENTAL RELATIONS
**Chapter:** 159 — BOND FINANCING
**Part:** IX — QUALIFIED SCHOOL CONSTRUCTION BONDS
**Section:** 159.845
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The board and the department shall adopt any rules necessary to ensure the orderly implementation of this part.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 2009-59.
