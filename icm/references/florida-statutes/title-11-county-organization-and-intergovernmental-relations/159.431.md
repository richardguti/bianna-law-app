# Fla. Stat. § 159.431 — Applicability of ch. 80-287.

**Title:** XI — COUNTY ORGANIZATION AND INTERGOVERNMENTAL RELATIONS
**Chapter:** 159 — BOND FINANCING
**Part:** III — INDUSTRIAL DEVELOPMENT AUTHORITIES
**Section:** 159.431
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Chapter 80-287, Laws of Florida, shall not apply to any bonds for which a local agency has, prior to July 1, 1980, adopted a resolution or engaged in negotiations. Such bonds shall be governed by the provisions of chapter 159, as it existed immediately prior to July 1, 1980.

**History:** s. 18, ch. 80-287.
