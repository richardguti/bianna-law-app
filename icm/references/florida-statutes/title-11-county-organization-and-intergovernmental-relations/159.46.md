# Fla. Stat. § 159.46 — Purposes.

**Title:** XI — COUNTY ORGANIZATION AND INTERGOVERNMENTAL RELATIONS
**Chapter:** 159 — BOND FINANCING
**Part:** III — INDUSTRIAL DEVELOPMENT AUTHORITIES
**Section:** 159.46
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

——

Industrial development authorities, as authorized by ss. 159.44-159.53, are created for the purpose of financing and r, or willful neglect of duty. Each member of the authority, before entering upon his or her duties, shall take and subscribe the oath or affirmation required by the State Constitution. A record of each such oath shall be filed with the Department of State and with the clerk.
