# Fla. Stat. § 130.04 — Sale of bonds.

**Title:** XI — COUNTY ORGANIZATION AND INTERGOVERNMENTAL RELATIONS
**Chapter:** 130 — COUNTY BONDS
**Part:** VI — VOLUNTEERS
**Section:** 130.04
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

In case the issuing of bonds shall be authorized by the result of such election, the county commissioners shall sell the bonds in the manner provided in s. 218.385.

**History:** s. 6, ch. 2088, 1877; RS 596; s. 1, ch. 5200, 1903; GS 792; RGS 1537; s. 1, ch. 8551, 1921; CGL 2315; s. 1, ch. 61-113; s. 1, ch. 63-118; s. 9, ch. 2004-305.
