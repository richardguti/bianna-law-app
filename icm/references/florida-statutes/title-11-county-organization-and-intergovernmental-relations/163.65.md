# Fla. Stat. § 163.65 — Agencies receiving government funding encouraged to participate.

**Title:** XI — COUNTY ORGANIZATION AND INTERGOVERNMENTAL RELATIONS
**Chapter:** 164 — GOVERNMENTAL DISPUTES
**Part:** VI — COLLABORATIVE CLIENT INFORMATION SYSTEMS
**Section:** 163.65
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

An agency that receives moneys from a federal, state, or local agency is encouraged to participate in any collaborative client information system that is available within the service area of the agency.

**History:** s. 47, ch. 97-286.
