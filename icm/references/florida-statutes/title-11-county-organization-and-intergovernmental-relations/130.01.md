# Fla. Stat. § 130.01 — Purposes for which county bonds may issue.

**Title:** XI — COUNTY ORGANIZATION AND INTERGOVERNMENTAL RELATIONS
**Chapter:** 129 — COUNTY ANNUAL BUDGET
**Part:** VI — VOLUNTEERS
**Section:** 130.01
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

——

Whenever the board of county commissioners of any county shall deem it expedient, or to the best interests of such county, to issue the county bonds of their county, for the purpose of constructing paved, macadamized, or other hard-surfaced 4Time warrants in newly created counties.
