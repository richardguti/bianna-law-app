# Fla. Stat. § 124.02 — Notice of change of boundaries of district to be given by publication.

**Title:** XI — COUNTY ORGANIZATION AND INTERGOVERNMENTAL RELATIONS
**Chapter:** 124 — COMMISSIONERS’ DISTRICTS
**Part:** III — FLORIDA RETIREMENT SYSTEM CONTRIBUTION RATES
**Section:** 124.02
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

——

(1)  
Whenever the boundaries of existing county commissioners' districts are, from time to time, changed by the board of county commissioners, it shall cause an accurate description of the boundaries of such districts, as changed, to be entered upon its minutes and a certified copy thereof to be published at least once each week for 2 consecutive weeks (two publications being sufficient) in a newspaper published in said county.
