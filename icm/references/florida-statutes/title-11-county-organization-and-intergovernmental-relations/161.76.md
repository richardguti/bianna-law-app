# Fla. Stat. § 161.76 — Preservation of authority.

**Title:** XI — COUNTY ORGANIZATION AND INTERGOVERNMENTAL RELATIONS
**Chapter:** 162 — COUNTY OR MUNICIPAL CODE ENFORCEMENT
**Part:** I — LOCAL GOVERNMENT CODE ENFORCEMENT BOARDS
**Section:** 161.76
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

This part does not restrict or limit the authority otherwise granted to the commission, or other state agencies by law.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 2005-166.
