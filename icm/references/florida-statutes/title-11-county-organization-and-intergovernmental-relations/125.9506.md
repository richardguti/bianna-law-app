# Fla. Stat. § 125.9506 — Construction.

**Title:** XI — COUNTY ORGANIZATION AND INTERGOVERNMENTAL RELATIONS
**Chapter:** 125 — COUNTY GOVERNMENT
**Part:** VI — VOLUNTEERS
**Section:** 125.9506
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Nothing in ss. 125.9501-125.9506 shall operate or be construed to limit any rights or benefits to which a volunteer would otherwise be entitled under state or federal law.
