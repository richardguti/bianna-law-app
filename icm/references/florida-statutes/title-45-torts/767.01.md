# Fla. Stat. § 767.01 — Dog owner's liability for damages to persons, domestic animals, or livestock.

**Title:** XLV — TORTS
**Chapter:** 767 — DAMAGE BY DOGS; DANGEROUS DOGS
**Part:** V — ANATOMICAL GIFTS
**Section:** 767.01
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

——

(1)  
A dog owner is liable for any damage done by the owner's dog to a person or to any animal included in the definitions of “domestic animal” and “livestock” as provided by s. 585.01.

(2)  
If a dog owner has knowledge of the dog's dangerous propensities, the owner must securely confine the dog in a proper enclosure as defined in s. 767.11.
