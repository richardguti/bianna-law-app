# Fla. Stat. § 769.06 — Contracts limiting liability invalid.

**Title:** XLV — TORTS
**Chapter:** 770 — DEFAMATION
**Part:** II — DAMAGES
**Section:** 769.06
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Any contract, contrivance or device whatever, having the effect to relieve or exempt the persons mentioned in s. 769.01 from the liability prescribed by this chapter shall be illegal and void.

**History:** s. 6, ch. 6521, 1913; RGS 4976; CGL 7063.
