# Fla. Stat. § 769.05 — Proceeds of recovery for injuries exempt from garnishment and execution.

**Title:** XLV — TORTS
**Chapter:** 769 — HAZARDOUS OCCUPATIONS
**Part:** II — DAMAGES
**Section:** 769.05
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Writs of garnishment, execution or other processes, shall not issue out of any court to reach any money due or likely to become due as damages under the provisions of this chapter.

**History:** s. 4, ch. 6521, 1913; RGS 4974; CGL 7061; s. 1177, ch. 97-102.
