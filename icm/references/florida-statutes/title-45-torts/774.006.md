# Fla. Stat. § 774.006 — Adjustment.

**Title:** XLV — TORTS
**Chapter:** 774 — ASBESTOS-RELATED AND SILICA-RELATED CLAIMS
**Part:** I — ASBESTOS-RELATED CLAIMS
**Section:** 774.006
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

——

(1)  
Except as provided in subsections (2), (3), and (4), the fair market value of total gross assets at the time of a merger or consolidation shall increase annually at a rate equal to the sum of:
(a)  
The prime rate as listed in the first edition of the Wall Street Journal published for each calendar year since the merger or consolidation, unless the prime rate is not published in that edition of the Wall Street Journal, in which case any reasonable determination of the prime rate on the first day of the year may be used; and

(b)  
One percent.

(2)  
The rate in subsection (1) may not be compounded.

(3)  
The adjustment Arice attributable to or paid for the assets in an arm's length transaction; or

(b)  
In the absence of other readily available information from which fair market value can be determined, by reference to the value of the assets recorded on a balance sheet.
