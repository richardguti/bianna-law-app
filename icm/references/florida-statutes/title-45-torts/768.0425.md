# Fla. Stat. § 768.0425 — Damages in actions against contractors for injuries sustained from negligence, malfeasance, or misfeasance.

**Title:** XLV — TORTS
**Chapter:** 768 — NEGLIGENCE
**Part:** I — GENERAL PROVISIONS
**Section:** 768.0425
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
For purposes of this section only, the term “contractor” means any person who contracts to perform any construction or building service which is regulated by any state or F.S. 768.0426768.042  
Damages.
——

In any action brought in the circuit court to recover damages for personal injury or wrongful death, the amount of general damages shall not be stated in the complaint, but the amount of special damages, if any, may be specifically pleaded and the requisite jurisdictional amount established for filing in any court of competent jurisdiction.

**History:** ss. 8, 9, ch. 75-9; s. 161, ch. 2020-2.
