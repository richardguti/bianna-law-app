# Fla. Stat. § 769.04 — Doctrine of “assumption of risk” abrogated.

**Title:** XLV — TORTS
**Chapter:** 769 — HAZARDOUS OCCUPATIONS
**Part:** II — DAMAGES
**Section:** 769.04
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The doctrine of “assumption of risk” shall not obtain in any case arising under the provisions of this chapter, where the injury or death was attributable to the negligence of the employer, his or her agents or servants.

**History:** s. 4, ch. 6521, 1913; RGS 4974; CGL 7061; s. 1177, ch. 97-102.
