# Fla. Stat. § 768.22 — Form of verdict.

**Title:** XLV — TORTS
**Chapter:** 768 — NEGLIGENCE
**Part:** I — GENERAL PROVISIONS
**Section:** 768.22
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The amounts awarded to each survivor and to the estate shall be stated separately in the verdict.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 72-35.
