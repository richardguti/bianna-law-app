# Fla. Stat. § 772.185 — Attorney's fees taxed as costs.

**Title:** XLV — TORTS
**Chapter:** 772 — CIVIL REMEDIES FOR CRIMINAL PRACTICES
**Part:** II — DAMAGES
**Section:** 772.185
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Attorney's fees awarded under this chapter shall be taxed as costs.

**History:** s. 3, ch. 86-277.
