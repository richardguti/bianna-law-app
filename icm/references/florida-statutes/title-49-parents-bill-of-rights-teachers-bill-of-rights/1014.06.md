# Fla. Stat. § 1014.06 — Parental consent for health care services.

**Title:** XLIX — PARENTS’ BILL OF RIGHTS; TEACHERS’ BILL OF RIGHTS
**Chapter:** 1014 — PARENTS’ BILL OF RIGHTS
**Part:** IV — FUNDING FOR EDUCATIONAL FACILITIES
**Section:** 1014.06
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

——

(1)  
Except as otherwise provided by law, a health care practitioner, as defined in s. 456.001, or an individual employed by such health care practitioner may not provide or solicit or arrange to provide health care services or prescribe medicinal drugs to a minor child without first obtaining written parental consent.

11.  
In accordance with s. 1002.222(1)(a), the right of a parent to opt out of any district-level data collection relating to his or her minor child not required by law.
