# Fla. Stat. § 454.20 — Attorneys not to be sureties.

**Title:** XXXII — REGULATION OF PROFESSIONS AND OCCUPATIONS
**Chapter:** 454 — ATTORNEYS AT LAW
**Part:** III — FARM LABOR CONTRACTOR REGISTRATION
**Section:** 454.20
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

No attorney shall become surety on the official bond of any state, county, or municipal officer of this state, nor surety on any bond of a client in judicial proceedings.

**History:** s. 20, ch. 10175, 1925; CGL 4198.
