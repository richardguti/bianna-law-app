# Fla. Stat. § 488.08 — Disposition of revenues; budget.

**Title:** XXXII — REGULATION OF PROFESSIONS AND OCCUPATIONS
**Chapter:** 488 — COMMERCIAL DRIVING SCHOOLS
**Part:** II — FLORIDA AGRICULTURAL WORKER SAFETY ACT
**Section:** 488.08
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

All moneys received from the application for, or from the issuance of, licenses and certificates under this chapter shall be deposited into the General Revenue Fund. The Department of Highway Safety and Motor Vehicles shall include an amount in its legislative budget request sufficient for the proper administration of the provisions of this chapter.

**History:** ss. 9, 11, ch. 84-150; s. 4, ch. 91-429.
