# Fla. Stat. § 487.011 — Popular name; administration.

**Title:** XXXII — REGULATION OF PROFESSIONS AND OCCUPATIONS
**Chapter:** 487 — PESTICIDE REGULATION AND SAFETY
**Part:** I — FLORIDA PESTICIDE LAW
**Section:** 487.011
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

This part shall be known by the popular name the “Florida Pesticide Law” and shall be administered by the Department of Agriculture and Consumer Services.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 65-457; ss. 1, 37, ch. 92-115; s. 2, ch. 94-233; s. 8, ch. 2000-154; s. 21, ch. 2004-64.
