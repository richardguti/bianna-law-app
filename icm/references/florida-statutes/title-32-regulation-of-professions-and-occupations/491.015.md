# Fla. Stat. § 491.015 — Duties of the department as to certified master social workers.

**Title:** XXXII — REGULATION OF PROFESSIONS AND OCCUPATIONS
**Chapter:** 491 — CLINICAL, COUNSELING, AND PSYCHOTHERAPY SERVICES
**Part:** III — SEPTIC TANK CONTRACTING
**Section:** 491.015
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
All functions reserved to boards under chapter 456 shall be exercised by the department with respect to the regulation of certified master social workers and in a manner consistent with the exercise of its regulatory functions.

(2)  
The department shall adopt rules to implement and enforce provisions relating to certified master social workers.

**History:** ss. 15, 19, ch. 87-252; ss. 16, 19, 20, ch. 90-263; s. 4, ch. 91-429; s. 155, ch. 98-166; s. 216, ch. 2000-160.
