# Fla. Stat. § 477.014 — Qualifications for practice.

**Title:** XXXII — REGULATION OF PROFESSIONS AND OCCUPATIONS
**Chapter:** 477 — COSMETOLOGY
**Part:** IV — COMMERCIAL REAL ESTATE LEASING COMMISSION LIEN ACT
**Section:** 477.014
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

No person other than a duly licensed cosmetologist shall practice cosmetology or use the name or title of cosmetologist.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 78-253; s. 2, ch. 81-318; ss. 35, 36, ch. 85-297; s. 36, ch. 89-344; s. 4, ch. 91-429; s. 67, ch. 2018-110.
