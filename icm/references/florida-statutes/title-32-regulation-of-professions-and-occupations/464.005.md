# Fla. Stat. § 464.005 — Board headquarters.

**Title:** XXXII — REGULATION OF PROFESSIONS AND OCCUPATIONS
**Chapter:** 463 — OPTOMETRY
**Part:** I — NURSE PRACTICE ACT
**Section:** 464.005
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The board shall maintain its official headquarters in Tallahassee.
