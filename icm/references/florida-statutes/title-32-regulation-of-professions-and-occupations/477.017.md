# Fla. Stat. § 477.017 — Legal services.

**Title:** XXXII — REGULATION OF PROFESSIONS AND OCCUPATIONS
**Chapter:** 477 — COSMETOLOGY
**Part:** IV — COMMERCIAL REAL ESTATE LEASING COMMISSION LIEN ACT
**Section:** 477.017
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The department shall provide all legal services needed to carry out the provisions of this act.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 78-253; s. 2, ch. 81-318; ss. 35, 36, ch. 85-297; s. 4, ch. 91-429.
