# Fla. Stat. § 489.538 — Authority of licensed job scope.

**Title:** XXXII — REGULATION OF PROFESSIONS AND OCCUPATIONS
**Chapter:** 489 — CONTRACTING
**Part:** III — SEPTIC TANK CONTRACTING
**Section:** 489.538
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

A licensee under this part need not have a license under part I to perform work within the scope of his or her license under this part.

**History:** s. 37, ch. 90-228; s. 12, ch. 91-201; s. 4, ch. 91-429; s. 501, ch. 97-103.
