# Fla. Stat. § 468.351 — Purpose and intent; application.

**Title:** XXXII — REGULATION OF PROFESSIONS AND OCCUPATIONS
**Chapter:** 468 — MISCELLANEOUS PROFESSIONS AND OCCUPATIONS
**Part:** V — RESPIRATORY THERAPY
**Section:** 468.351
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)(a)  
The purpose in enacting this part is to provide for the licensure of persons who deliver respiratory care services and who meet certain requirements. The delivery of respiratory care services by persons licensed pursuant to this part shall not be construed to permit the practice of medicine.
