# Fla. Stat. § 468.507 — Authority to adopt rules.

**Title:** XXXII — REGULATION OF PROFESSIONS AND OCCUPATIONS
**Chapter:** 468 — MISCELLANEOUS PROFESSIONS AND OCCUPATIONS
**Part:** X — DIETETICS AND NUTRITION PRACTICE
**Section:** 468.507
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The board has authority to adopt rules pursuant to ss. 120.536(1) and 120.54 to implement the provisions of this part and chapter 456 conferring duties upon it. The powers and duties of the board as set forth in this part shall in no way limit or interfere with the powers and duties of the board as set forth in chapter 458. All powers and duties of the board set forth in this part shall be supplemental and additional powers and duties to those conferred upon the board by chapter 458.

**History:** ss. 7, 20, ch. 88-236; s. 4, ch. 91-429; s. 7, ch. 96-367; s. 92, ch. 98-166; s. 136, ch. 98-200; s. 151, ch. 2000-160.
