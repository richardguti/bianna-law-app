# Fla. Stat. § 487.2021 — Legislative intent.

**Title:** XXXII — REGULATION OF PROFESSIONS AND OCCUPATIONS
**Chapter:** 487 — PESTICIDE REGULATION AND SAFETY
**Part:** II — FLORIDA AGRICULTURAL WORKER SAFETY ACT
**Section:** 487.2021
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

It is the intent of the Legislature to ensure that agricultural workers employed in the state receive protection from agricultural pesticides. The Legislature intends to ensure that agricultural workers be given information concerning agricultural pesticides.

**History:** s. 48, ch. 2004-64.
