# Fla. Stat. § 468.312 — Fees; disposition.

**Title:** XXXII — REGULATION OF PROFESSIONS AND OCCUPATIONS
**Chapter:** 468 — MISCELLANEOUS PROFESSIONS AND OCCUPATIONS
**Part:** IV — RADIOLOGICAL PERSONNEL CERTIFICATION
**Section:** 468.312
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

All moneys derived from fees imposed pursuant to this part shall be placed in the Radiation Protection Trust Fund to be used by the department for the sole purpose of carrying out the provisions of this part.

**History:** s. 13, ch. 78-383; s. 2, ch. 81-318; sF.S. 468.31156468.3115
