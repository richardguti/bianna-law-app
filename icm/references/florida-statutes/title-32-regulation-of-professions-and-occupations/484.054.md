# Fla. Stat. § 484.054 — Sale or distribution of prescription hearing aids through mail; penalty.

**Title:** XXXII — REGULATION OF PROFESSIONS AND OCCUPATIONS
**Chapter:** 484 — DISPENSING OF OPTICAL DEVICES AND HEARING AIDS
**Part:** II — FITTING AND DISPENSING OF HEARING AIDS
**Section:** 484.054
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

It is unlawful for any person to sell or distribute prescription hearing aids through the mail to the ultimate consumer. Any violation of this section constitutes a misdemeanor of the second degree, punishable as provided in s. 775.082 or s. 775.083.
