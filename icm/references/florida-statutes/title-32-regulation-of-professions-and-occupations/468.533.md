# Fla. Stat. § 468.533 — Collected fees.

**Title:** XXXII — REGULATION OF PROFESSIONS AND OCCUPATIONS
**Chapter:** 468 — MISCELLANEOUS PROFESSIONS AND OCCUPATIONS
**Part:** XI — EMPLOYEE LEASING COMPANIES
**Section:** 468.533
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

All fees collected pursuant to this part shall be deposited into the Professional Regulation Trust Fund.

**History:** ss. 15, 17, ch. 91-93; s. 4, ch. 91-429; s. 46, ch. 94-119.
