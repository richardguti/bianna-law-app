# Fla. Stat. § 466.039 — Violations.

**Title:** XXXII — REGULATION OF PROFESSIONS AND OCCUPATIONS
**Chapter:** 466 — DENTISTRY, DENTAL HYGIENE, AND DENTAL LABORATORIES
**Part:** II — CERTIFIED NURSING ASSISTANTS
**Section:** 466.039
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

It shall be unlawful for any person, firm, or corporation to operate as a dental laboratory as defined in this chapter, except those registered as provided in s. 466.032. Violation shall constitute a misdemeanor of the second degree, punishable as provided in s. 775.082 or s. 775.083.

**History:** ss. 2, 3, ch. 79-330; ss. 2, 3, ch. 81-318; ss. 23, 24, ch. 86-291; s. 60, ch. 91-137; s. 7, ch. 91-156; s. 95, ch. 91-224; s. 4, ch. 91-429.
