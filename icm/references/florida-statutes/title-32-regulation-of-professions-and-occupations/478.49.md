# Fla. Stat. § 478.49 — License required.

**Title:** XXXII — REGULATION OF PROFESSIONS AND OCCUPATIONS
**Chapter:** 478 — ELECTROLYSIS
**Part:** IV — COMMERCIAL REAL ESTATE LEASING COMMISSION LIEN ACT
**Section:** 478.49
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
No person may practice electrology or hold herself or himself out as an electrologist in this state unless the person has been issued a license by the department and holds an active license pursuant to the requirements of this chapter.

(2)  
A licensee shall display her or his license in a conspicuous location in her or his place of practice and provide it to the department or the board upon request.

**History:** s. 10, ch. 92-172; s. 407, ch. 97-103.
