# Fla. Stat. § 490.015 — Duties of the department.

**Title:** XXXII — REGULATION OF PROFESSIONS AND OCCUPATIONS
**Chapter:** 490 — PSYCHOLOGICAL SERVICES
**Part:** III — SEPTIC TANK CONTRACTING
**Section:** 490.015
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
All functions reserved to boards under chapter 456 shall be exercised by the department with respect to the regulation of school psychologists and in a manner consistent with the exercise of its regulatory functions.

(2)  
The department shall adopt rules to implement the provisions of this chapter.
