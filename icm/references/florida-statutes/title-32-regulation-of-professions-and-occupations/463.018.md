# Fla. Stat. § 463.018 — Reciprocity.

**Title:** XXXII — REGULATION OF PROFESSIONS AND OCCUPATIONS
**Chapter:** 463 — OPTOMETRY
**Part:** III — FARM LABOR CONTRACTOR REGISTRATION
**Section:** 463.018
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

In order to ensure that licensed practitioners may be considered for licensure in other states, the board may enter into reciprocity agreements with other states.

**History:** ss. 1, 6, ch. 79-194; ss. 2, 3, ch. 81-318; ss. 18, 20, 21, ch. 86-289; s. 4, ch. 91-429.
