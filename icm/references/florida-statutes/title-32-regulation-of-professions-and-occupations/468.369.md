# Fla. Stat. § 468.369 — Payment or reimbursement by hospitals of costs of compliance with part.

**Title:** XXXII — REGULATION OF PROFESSIONS AND OCCUPATIONS
**Chapter:** 468 — MISCELLANEOUS PROFESSIONS AND OCCUPATIONS
**Part:** V — RESPIRATORY THERAPY
**Section:** 468.369
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

A hospital shall not be required to pay for or reimburse any person for the costs of compliance with any of the requirements of this part, including costs of continuing education.

**History:** ss. 1, 3, ch. 84-252; s. 4, ch. 91-429.
