# Fla. Stat. § 483.915 — Licensure renewal and continuing education requirements.

**Title:** XXXII — REGULATION OF PROFESSIONS AND OCCUPATIONS
**Chapter:** 483 — HEALTH TESTING SERVICES
**Part:** II — MEDICAL PHYSICISTS
**Section:** 483.915
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
The department shall renew a license upon receipt of a renewal application.

(2)  
The department shall adopt by rule continuing education requirements consistent with nationally accepted standards of the American Board of Genetic Counseling, Inc.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 2021-133.
