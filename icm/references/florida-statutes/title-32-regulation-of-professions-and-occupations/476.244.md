# Fla. Stat. § 476.244 — Administrative proceedings.

**Title:** XXXII — REGULATION OF PROFESSIONS AND OCCUPATIONS
**Chapter:** 476 — BARBERING
**Part:** IV — COMMERCIAL REAL ESTATE LEASING COMMISSION LIEN ACT
**Section:** 476.244
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

All hearings and other administrative proceedings shall be conducted pursuant to the provisions of chapter 120.

**History:** ss. 24, 28, ch. 78-155; ss. 2, 3, ch. 81-318; ss. 19, 20, ch. 85-297; s. 4, ch. 91-429.
