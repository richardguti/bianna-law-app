# Fla. Stat. § 468.457 — Rulemaking authority.

**Title:** XXXII — REGULATION OF PROFESSIONS AND OCCUPATIONS
**Chapter:** 468 — MISCELLANEOUS PROFESSIONS AND OCCUPATIONS
**Part:** IX — ATHLETE AGENTS
**Section:** 468.457
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The department shall adopt rules necessary to administer and enforce this part.
