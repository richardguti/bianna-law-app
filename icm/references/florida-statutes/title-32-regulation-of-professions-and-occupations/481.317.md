# Fla. Stat. § 481.317 — Temporary certificates.

**Title:** XXXII — REGULATION OF PROFESSIONS AND OCCUPATIONS
**Chapter:** 481 — ARCHITECTURE, INTERIOR DESIGN, AND LANDSCAPE ARCHITECTURE
**Part:** II — LANDSCAPE ARCHITECTURE
**Section:** 481.317
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
Upon the approval by the board and payment of the fee set in s. 481.307, the department shall grant a temporary certificate of registration for work on a specified project in this state for a period not to exceed 1 year to an applicant who is licensed in another state or territory to practice landscape architecture.

(2)  
The application for a temporary certificate shall constitute appointment of the Department of State as an agent of the applicant for service of process in any action or proceeding against the applicant arising out of any transaction or operation connected with or incidental to the practice of landscape architecture for which the temporary license was issued.

**History:** ss. 10, 18, ch. 79-407; ss. 2, 3, ch. 81-318; ss. 10, 18, 19, ch. 88-347; s. 4, ch. 91-429; s. 59, ch. 2020-160.
