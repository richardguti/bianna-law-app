# Fla. Stat. § 456.019 — Restriction on requirement of citizenship.

**Title:** XXXII — REGULATION OF PROFESSIONS AND OCCUPATIONS
**Chapter:** 456 — HEALTH PROFESSIONS AND OCCUPATIONS: GENERAL PROVISIONS
**Part:** III — FARM LABOR CONTRACTOR REGISTRATION
**Section:** 456.019
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

A person is not disqualified from practicing an occupation or profession regulated by the state solely because she or he is not a United States citizen.

**History:** s. 36, ch. 97-261; s. 20, ch. 99-7; s. 51, ch. 2000-160.

**Note:** Former s. 455.511.
