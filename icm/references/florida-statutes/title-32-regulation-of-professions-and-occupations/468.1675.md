# Fla. Stat. § 468.1675 — Board headquarters.

**Title:** XXXII — REGULATION OF PROFESSIONS AND OCCUPATIONS
**Chapter:** 468 — MISCELLANEOUS PROFESSIONS AND OCCUPATIONS
**Part:** II — NURSING HOME ADMINISTRATION
**Section:** 468.1675
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The board shall maintain its official headquarters in the City of Tallahassee.
