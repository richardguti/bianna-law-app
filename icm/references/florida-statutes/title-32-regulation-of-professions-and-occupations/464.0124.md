# Fla. Stat. § 464.0124 — Parkinson's disease registry; advanced practice registered nurse reporting requirement.

**Title:** XXXII — REGULATION OF PROFESSIONS AND OCCUPATIONS
**Chapter:** 463 — OPTOMETRY
**Part:** I — NURSE PRACTICE ACT
**Section:** 464.0124
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
Beginning January 1, 2027, each advanced practice registered nurse who diagnoses or treats a patient for Parkinson's disease or atypical parkinsonism shall report to the statewide Parkinson's disease registry, established pursuant to s. 1004.4352, information containing nationally recognized Parkinson's disease and atypical parkinsonism performance measures.

(2)  
A liability of any kind or character for damages or other relief may not arise or be enforced against an advanced practice registered nurse by reason of having provided such information to the statewide Parkinson's disease registry.

**History:** s. 3, ch. 2026-9.
