# Fla. Stat. § 458.336 — Drugs to treat obesity; rules establishing guidelines.

**Title:** XXXII — REGULATION OF PROFESSIONS AND OCCUPATIONS
**Chapter:** 458 — MEDICAL PRACTICE
**Part:** III — FARM LABOR CONTRACTOR REGISTRATION
**Section:** 458.336
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The Board of Medicine shall adopt rules to establish practice guidelines for physicians to safely prescribe phentermine, fenfluramine, and other drugs used to treat obesity.

**History:** s. 188, ch. 97-264.
