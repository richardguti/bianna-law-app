# Fla. Stat. § 488.07 — Penalties for violation.

**Title:** XXXII — REGULATION OF PROFESSIONS AND OCCUPATIONS
**Chapter:** 488 — COMMERCIAL DRIVING SCHOOLS
**Part:** II — FLORIDA AGRICULTURAL WORKER SAFETY ACT
**Section:** 488.07
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

A person who violates any of the provisions of this chapter or any of the rules or regulations promulgated hereunder is guilty of a misdemeanor of the first degree, punishable as provided in s. 775.082 or s. 775.083.

**History:** s. 7, ch. 28142, 1953; s. 441, ch. 71-136; s. 2, ch. 81-318; ss. 8, 10, 11, ch. 84-150; s. 4, ch. 91-429.
