# Fla. Stat. § 468.3851 — Renewal of license.

**Title:** XXXII — REGULATION OF PROFESSIONS AND OCCUPATIONS
**Chapter:** 468 — MISCELLANEOUS PROFESSIONS AND OCCUPATIONS
**Part:** VI — AUCTIONEERS
**Section:** 468.3851
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
The department shall renew a license upon receipt of the renewal application and fee.

(2)  
The department shall adopt a procedure for the biennial renewal of licenses.

**History:** ss. 4, 9, ch. 87-210; ss. 62, 65, ch. 91-137; ss. 11, 12, ch. 91-156; s. 4, ch. 91-429; s. 200, ch. 94-119; s. 40, ch. 2015-4.
