# Fla. Stat. § 476.154 — Biennial renewal of licenses.

**Title:** XXXII — REGULATION OF PROFESSIONS AND OCCUPATIONS
**Chapter:** 476 — BARBERING
**Part:** IV — COMMERCIAL REAL ESTATE LEASING COMMISSION LIEN ACT
**Section:** 476.154
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
Each licensed barber who continues in active practice or service shall renew her or his license biennially and pay the required fee.

(2)  
Any license or certificate of registration issued pursuant to this act for a period less than the established biennial issuance period may be issued for that lesser period of time, and the department shall adjust the required fee accordingly. The board shall adopt rules providing for such partial period fee adjustments.

(3)(a)  
The department shall renew a license upon receipt of the renewal application and fee.

(b)  
The department shall adopt rules establishing a procedure for the biennial renewal of licenses.
