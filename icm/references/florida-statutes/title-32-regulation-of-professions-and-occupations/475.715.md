# Fla. Stat. § 475.715 — Priority of recorded commission notice.

**Title:** XXXII — REGULATION OF PROFESSIONS AND OCCUPATIONS
**Chapter:** 475 — REAL ESTATE BROKERS, SALES ASSOCIATES, SCHOOLS, AND APPRAISERS
**Part:** III — COMMERCIAL REAL ESTATE SALES COMMISSION LIEN ACT
**Section:** 475.715
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

——

All statutory liens, consensual liens, mortgages, deeds of trust, assignments of rents, and other encumbrances, including all advances or charges made or accruing thereunder, whether voluntary or obligatory, and all modifications, extensions, renewals, and replacements thereof, recorded prior to the recording of a commission notice pursuant to the provisions of s. 475.707, have priority over the commission notice.
