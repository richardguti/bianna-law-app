# Fla. Stat. § 467.207 — Exceptions.

**Title:** XXXII — REGULATION OF PROFESSIONS AND OCCUPATIONS
**Chapter:** 467 — MIDWIFERY
**Part:** II — CERTIFIED NURSING ASSISTANTS
**Section:** 467.207
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

No provision of this chapter shall be construed to prohibit:
(1)  
The practice of midwifery by students enrolled in an approved midwifery training program.

(2)  
The establishment of an independent practice by one or more midwives for the purpose of rendering to patients midwifery services within the scope of the midwife license.

(3)  
Assistance by any person in the case of an emergency.

**History:** ss. 1, 3, ch. 82-99; s. 8, ch. 84-268; ss. 4, 5, ch. 91-429; s. 19, ch. 92-179; s. 79, ch. 2001-62.
