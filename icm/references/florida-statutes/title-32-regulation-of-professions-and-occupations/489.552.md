# Fla. Stat. § 489.552 — Registration required.

**Title:** XXXII — REGULATION OF PROFESSIONS AND OCCUPATIONS
**Chapter:** 489 — CONTRACTING
**Part:** III — SEPTIC TANK CONTRACTING
**Section:** 489.552
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

A person shall not hold himself or herself out as a septic tank contractor or a master septic tank contractor in this state unless he or she is registered by the department in accordance with the provisions of this part. However, nothing in this part prohibits any person licensed pursuant to s. 489.105(3)(m) in this state from engaging in the profession for which he or she is licensed.

**History:** ss. 2, 10, ch. 87-310; s. 4, ch. 91-429; s. 6, ch. 96-303.
