# Fla. Stat. § 472.0075 — Contacting board through department.

**Title:** XXXII — REGULATION OF PROFESSIONS AND OCCUPATIONS
**Chapter:** 472 — LAND SURVEYING AND MAPPING
**Part:** XVI — MOLD-RELATED SERVICES
**Section:** 472.0075
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The board may be contacted through the headquarters of the department in the City of Tallahassee.
