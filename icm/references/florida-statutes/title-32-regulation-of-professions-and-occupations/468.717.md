# Fla. Stat. § 468.717 — Violations and penalties.

**Title:** XXXII — REGULATION OF PROFESSIONS AND OCCUPATIONS
**Chapter:** 468 — MISCELLANEOUS PROFESSIONS AND OCCUPATIONS
**Part:** XIII — ATHLETIC TRAINERS
**Section:** 468.717
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Each of the following acts constitutes a misdemeanor of the first degree, punishable as provided in s. 775.082 or s. 775.083:
(1)  
PraF.S. 468.7156468.715  
Sexual misconduct.
——

The athletic trainer-patient relationship is founded on mutual trust. Sexual misconduct in the practice of athletic training is prohibited under s. 456.063.

**History:** s. 9, ch. 95-388; s. 9, ch. 2015-116.
