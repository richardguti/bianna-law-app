# Fla. Stat. § 471.009 — Board F.S. 471.0086471.008

**Title:** XXXII — REGULATION OF PROFESSIONS AND OCCUPATIONS
**Chapter:** 471 — ENGINEERING
**Part:** XVI — MOLD-RELATED SERVICES
**Section:** 471.009
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

The board has authority to adopt rules pursuant to ss. 120.536(1) and 120.54 to implement provisions of this chapter or chapter 455 conferring duties upon it.
