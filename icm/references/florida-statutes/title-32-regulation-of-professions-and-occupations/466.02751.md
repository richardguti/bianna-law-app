# Fla. Stat. § 466.02751 — Establishment of practitioner profile for designation as a controlled substance prescribing practitioner.

**Title:** XXXII — REGULATION OF PROFESSIONS AND OCCUPATIONS
**Chapter:** 466 — DENTISTRY, DENTAL HYGIENE, AND DENTAL LABORATORIES
**Part:** II — CERTIFIED NURSING ASSISTANTS
**Section:** 466.02751
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The Department of Health shall establish a practitioner profile for dentists licensed under this chapter for a practitioner's designation as a controlled substance prescribing practitioner as provided in s. 456.44.

**History:** s. 29, ch. 2011-141; s. 23, ch. 2016-224.
