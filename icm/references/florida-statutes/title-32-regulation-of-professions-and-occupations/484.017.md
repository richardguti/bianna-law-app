# Fla. Stat. § 484.017 — Reciprocity.

**Title:** XXXII — REGULATION OF PROFESSIONS AND OCCUPATIONS
**Chapter:** 484 — DISPENSING OF OPTICAL DEVICES AND HEARING AIDS
**Part:** I — PREPARING AND DISPENSING OF EYEGLASSES AND OTHER OPTICAL DEVICES
**Section:** 484.017
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

In order to ensure that opticians licensed in this state may be licensed in other states, the board may enter into reciprocity agreements with other states.

**History:** ss. 1, 5, ch. 79-275; ss. 2, 3, ch. 81-318; ss. 11, 12, ch. 86-254; s. 4, ch. 91-429.
