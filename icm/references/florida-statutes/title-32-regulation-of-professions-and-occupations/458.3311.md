# Fla. Stat. § 458.3311 — Emergency procedures for disciplinary action.

**Title:** XXXII — REGULATION OF PROFESSIONS AND OCCUPATIONS
**Chapter:** 458 — MEDICAL PRACTICE
**Part:** III — FARM LABOR CONTRACTOR REGISTRATION
**Section:** 458.3311
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

——

Notwithstanding any other provision of law to the contrary, no later than 30 days after a third report of a professional liability claim against a licensed physician has been submitted, within a 60-month period, as required by ss. ; s. 22, ch. 2016-224; s. 8, ch. 2017-41; ss. 1, 4, ch. 2017-232; s. 14, ch. 2018-13; s. 50, ch. 2018-106; s. 6, ch. 2019-112; s. 4, ch. 2019-130; s. 4, ch. 2020-31; s. 14, ch. 2022-35.
