# Fla. Stat. § 468.51 — Nutrition counselor; renewal of licensure.

**Title:** XXXII — REGULATION OF PROFESSIONS AND OCCUPATIONS
**Chapter:** 468 — MISCELLANEOUS PROFESSIONS AND OCCUPATIONS
**Part:** X — DIETETICS AND NUTRITION PRACTICE
**Section:** 468.51
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Any person previously certified as qualified by the board and holding a license to practice as a nutrition counselor in this state which was issued during the period from July 1, 1988, to March 30, 1997, based upon documentation that the person was employed as a practitioner of nutrition counseling previous to and on April 1, 1988, shall be eligible to renew his or her license pursuant to s. 468.514.

**History:** ss. 10, 20, ch. 88-236; s. 4, ch. 91-429; ss. 10, 11, ch. 96-367.
