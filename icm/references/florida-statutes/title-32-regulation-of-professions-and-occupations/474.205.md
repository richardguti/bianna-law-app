# Fla. Stat. § 474.205 — Headquarters.

**Title:** XXXII — REGULATION OF PROFESSIONS AND OCCUPATIONS
**Chapter:** 474 — VETERINARY MEDICAL PRACTICE
**Part:** XVI — MOLD-RELATED SERVICES
**Section:** 474.205
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The board shall maintain its official headquarters in the City of Tallahassee.

**History:** ss. 1, 2, ch. 79-228; ss. 2, 3, ch. 81-318; ss. 15, 16, ch. 85-291; s. 4, ch. 91-429.
