# Fla. Stat. § 475.255 — Determination of agency or transactional brokerage relationship.

**Title:** XXXII — REGULATION OF PROFESSIONS AND OCCUPATIONS
**Chapter:** 475 — REAL ESTATE BROKERS, SALES ASSOCIATES, SCHOOLS, AND APPRAISERS
**Part:** I — REAL ESTATE BROKERS, SALES ASSOCIATES, AND SCHOOLS
**Section:** 475.255
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Without consideration of the related facts and circumstances, the mere payment or promise to pay compensation to a licensee does not determine whether an agency or transactional brokerage relationship exists between the licensee and a seller, landlord, buyer, or tenant.

**History:** s. 142, ch. 94-119.
