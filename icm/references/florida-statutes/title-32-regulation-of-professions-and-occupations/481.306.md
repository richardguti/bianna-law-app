# Fla. Stat. § 481.306 — Authority to make rules.

**Title:** XXXII — REGULATION OF PROFESSIONS AND OCCUPATIONS
**Chapter:** 481 — ARCHITECTURE, INTERIOR DESIGN, AND LANDSCAPE ARCHITECTURE
**Part:** II — LANDSCAPE ARCHITECTURE
**Section:** 481.306
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The board has authority to adopt rules pursuant to ss. 120.536(1) and 120.54 to implement the provisions of this chapter and chapter 455 conferring duties upon it.

**History:** ss. 3, 18, ch. 79-407; ss. 2, 3, ch. 81-318; ss. 3, 18, 19, ch. 88-347; s. 4, ch. 91-429; s. 174, ch. 94-218.
