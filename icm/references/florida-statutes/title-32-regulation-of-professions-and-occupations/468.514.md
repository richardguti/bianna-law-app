# Fla. Stat. § 468.514 — Renewal of license.

**Title:** XXXII — REGULATION OF PROFESSIONS AND OCCUPATIONS
**Chapter:** 468 — MISCELLANEOUS PROFESSIONS AND OCCUPATIONS
**Part:** X — DIETETICS AND NUTRITION PRACTICE
**Section:** 468.514
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
The department shall renew a license under this part upon receipt of the renewal application, fee, and proof of the successful completion of continuing education requirements as determined by the board.

(2)  
The department shall adopt rules establishing a procedure for the biennial renewal of licenses under this part.F.S. 468.5136468.513  
Dietitian/nutritionist; licensure by endorsement.
——

The department shall issue a license to practice dietetics and nutrition by endorsement to any applicant who submits to background screening in accordance with s. 456.0135 and meets the requirements for licensure by endorsement under s. 456.0145, upon receipt of a completed application and the fee specified in s. 468.508.
