# Fla. Stat. § 456.045 — Practitioner profiles; maintenance of superseded information.

**Title:** XXXII — REGULATION OF PROFESSIONS AND OCCUPATIONS
**Chapter:** 456 — HEALTH PROFESSIONS AND OCCUPATIONS: GENERAL PROVISIONS
**Part:** III — FARM LABOR CONTRACTOR REGISTRATION
**Section:** 456.045
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Information in superseded practitioner profiles must be maintained by the Department of Health, in accordance with general law and the rules of the Department of State.

**History:** s. 132, ch. 97-237; s. 8, ch. 97-273; s. 71, ch. 2000-160.

**Note:** Former s. 455.5655.
