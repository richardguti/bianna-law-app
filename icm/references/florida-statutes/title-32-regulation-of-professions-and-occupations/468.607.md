# Fla. Stat. § 468.607 — Certification of building code administration and inspection personnel.

**Title:** XXXII — REGULATION OF PROFESSIONS AND OCCUPATIONS
**Chapter:** 468 — MISCELLANEOUS PROFESSIONS AND OCCUPATIONS
**Part:** XII — BUILDING CODE ADMINISTRATORS AND INSPECTORS
**Section:** 468.607
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The board shall issue a certificate to any individual whom the board determines to be qualified, within such class and level as provided in this part and with such limitations as the board may place upon it. No person may be employed by a state agency or local governmental authority to perform the duties of a building code administrator, plans examiner, or building code inspector after October 1, 1993, without possessing the proper valid certificate issued in accordance with the provisions of this part.
