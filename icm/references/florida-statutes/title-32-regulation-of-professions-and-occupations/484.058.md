# Fla. Stat. § 484.058 — Declaration of place of business; posting of license and notice.

**Title:** XXXII — REGULATION OF PROFESSIONS AND OCCUPATIONS
**Chapter:** 485 — HYPNOSIS
**Part:** II — FITTING AND DISPENSING OF HEARING AIDS
**Section:** 484.058
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Each licensee shall declare and establish a regular place of business, at which the licensee's license shall be conspicuously displayed.

**History:** ss. 17, 20, ch. 86-283; s. 22, ch. 90-341; s. 11, ch. 90-345; s. 4, ch. 91-429; s. 456, ch. 97-103.
