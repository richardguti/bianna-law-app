# Fla. Stat. § 479.08 — Denial or revocation of permit.

**Title:** XXXII — REGULATION OF PROFESSIONS AND OCCUPATIONS
**Chapter:** 479 — OUTDOOR ADVERTISING
**Part:** I — GENERAL PROVISIONS
**Section:** 479.08
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

——

The department may deny or revoke a permit requested or granted under this chapter in any case in which it determines that the application for the permit contains false or misleading information of material consequence. The department may revoke a permit granted under this chapter in any case in which the permittee has violated this chapter, unless such permittee, within 30 days after the receipt of notice by the department, complies with this chapter.
