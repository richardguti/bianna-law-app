# Fla. Stat. § 465.006 — Disposition of fees; expenditures.

**Title:** XXXII — REGULATION OF PROFESSIONS AND OCCUPATIONS
**Chapter:** 465 — PHARMACY
**Part:** II — CERTIFIED NURSING ASSISTANTS
**Section:** 465.006
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

All moneys received under this chapter shall be deposited and expended pursuant to the provisions of s. 456.025. All expenditures for duties of the board authorized by this chapter shall be paid upon presentation of vouchers approved by the executive director of the board.

**History:** ss. 1, 7, ch. 79-226; ss. 2, 3, ch. 81-318; ss. 26, 27, ch. 86-256; s. 59, ch. F.S. 465.0056465.005
