# Fla. Stat. § 478.41 — Purpose and intent.

**Title:** XXXII — REGULATION OF PROFESSIONS AND OCCUPATIONS
**Chapter:** 478 — ELECTROLYSIS
**Part:** IV — COMMERCIAL REAL ESTATE LEASING COMMISSION LIEN ACT
**Section:** 478.41
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The purpose of this chapter is to provide for the licensure of persons who deliver electrolysis services and to ensure that they meet certain requirements. It is the finding of the Legislature that the practice of electrology by unskilled or incompetent persons presents a danger to the public health and safety. Because it is difficult for the public to make informed choices related to electrolysis services and since the consequences of wrong choices can harm the public health and safety, it is the intent F.S. 478.406478.40

This chapter may be cited as the “Electrolysis Practice Act.”
