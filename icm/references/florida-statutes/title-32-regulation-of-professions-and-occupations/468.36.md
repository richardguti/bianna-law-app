# Fla. Stat. § 468.36 — Current address.

**Title:** XXXII — REGULATION OF PROFESSIONS AND OCCUPATIONS
**Chapter:** 468 — MISCELLANEOUS PROFESSIONS AND OCCUPATIONS
**Part:** V — RESPIRATORY THERAPY
**Section:** 468.36
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Every licensee shall file with the department the licensee's current residence address as defined by board rule.

**History:** ss. 1, 3, ch. 84-252; s. 4, ch. 91-429; s. 293, ch. 97-103; s. 137, ch. 97-264.
