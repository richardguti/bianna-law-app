Renewal of license; continuing education.
——

(1)  
Licenses issued under this part are subject to biennial renewal as provided in s. 456.004.

(2)  
The board may by rule prescribe continuing education requirements, not to exceed 30 contact hours biennially, as a condition for renewal of licensure. The program criteria for those requirements must be approved by the board.
