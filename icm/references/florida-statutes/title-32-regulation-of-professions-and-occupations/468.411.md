# Fla. Stat. § 468.411 — Labor disputes; statements required.

**Title:** XXXII — REGULATION OF PROFESSIONS AND OCCUPATIONS
**Chapter:** 468 — MISCELLANEOUS PROFESSIONS AND OCCUPATIONS
**Part:** VII — TALENT AGENCIES
**Section:** 468.411
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

No talent agency shall knowingly send any person who has received a motion picture or videotape engagement or any other engagement to any place where a strike, lockout, or other labor dispute is in active progress, without first notifying that person of such conditions.
