# Fla. Stat. § 472.009 — Board headquarters.

**Title:** XXXII — REGULATION OF PROFESSIONS AND OCCUPATIONS
**Chapter:** 472 — LAND SURVEYING AND MAPPING
**Part:** XVI — MOLD-RELATED SERVICES
**Section:** 472.009
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The headquarters of the board shall be in Tallahassee.

**History:** ss. 22, 42, ch. 79-243; ss. 2, 3, ch. 81-318; ss. 5, 7, ch. 89-137; s. 4, ch. 91-429.
