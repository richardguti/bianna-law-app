# Fla. Stat. § 456.038 — Renewal and cancellation notices.

(1)  
At least 90 days before the end of a licensure cycle, the department shall:
(a)  
Forward a licensure renewal notification to an active or inactive status licensee at the licensee's last known address of record with the department.

(b)  
Forward a notice of pending cancellation of licensure to a delinquent licensee at the licensee's last known address of record with the department.
