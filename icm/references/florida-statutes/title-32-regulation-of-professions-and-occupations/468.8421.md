# Fla. Stat. § 468.8421 — Insurance.

**Title:** XXXII — REGULATION OF PROFESSIONS AND OCCUPATIONS
**Chapter:** 468 — MISCELLANEOUS PROFESSIONS AND OCCUPATIONS
**Part:** XVI — MOLD-RELATED SERVICES
**Section:** 468.8421
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
A mold assessor shall maintain general liability and errors and omissions for both preliminary and postremediation mold assessment insurance coverage of at least $1 million.

(2)  
A mold remediator shall maintain a general liability insurance policy in an amount of not less than $1,000,000 that includes specific coverage for mold-related claims.

**History:** s. 3, ch. 2007-235; s. 31, ch. 2010-106; s. 23, ch. 2010-176.
