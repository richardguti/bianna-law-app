# Fla. Stat. § 468.522 — Rules of the board.

**Title:** XXXII — REGULATION OF PROFESSIONS AND OCCUPATIONS
**Chapter:** 468 — MISCELLANEOUS PROFESSIONS AND OCCUPATIONS
**Part:** XI — EMPLOYEE LEASING COMPANIES
**Section:** 468.522
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The board has authority to adopt rules pursuant to ss. 120.536(1) and 120.54 to implement the provisions of this part. Every licensee shall be governed and controlled by this part and the rules adopted by the board.

**History:** ss. 4, 17, ch. 91-93; s. 4, ch. Rs="Text Intro Justify">The Board of Employee Leasing Companies is created within the Department of Business and Professional Regulation and shall consist of seven members to be appointed by the Governor and confirmed by the Senate.
