# Fla. Stat. § 465.008 — Renewal of license.

**Title:** XXXII — REGULATION OF PROFESSIONS AND OCCUPATIONS
**Chapter:** 465 — PHARMACY
**Part:** II — CERTIFIED NURSING ASSISTANTS
**Section:** 465.008
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
The department shall renew a license upon receipt of the renewal application, verification of compliance with s. 465.009, and receipt of a fee set by the board not to exceed $250.

(2)  
The department shall adopt rules establishing a procedure for the biennial renewal of licenses.

(3)  
Any person licensed under this chapter for 50 years or more is exempt from the payment of the renewal or delinquent fee, and the department shall issue a lifetime license to such a person.
