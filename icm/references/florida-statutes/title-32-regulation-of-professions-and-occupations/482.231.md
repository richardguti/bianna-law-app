# Fla. Stat. § 482.231 — Use of fogging machines.

**Title:** XXXII — REGULATION OF PROFESSIONS AND OCCUPATIONS
**Chapter:** 482 — PEST CONTROL
**Part:** II — LANDSCAPE ARCHITECTURE
**Section:** 482.231
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Only a certified operator who is certified in the category of general household pest control, or an authorized employee of a licensee under the supervision of such an operator, may use a thermal-aerosol fogging machine in general household pest control.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 59-454; s. 1, ch. 65-295; s. 3, ch. 76-168; s. 1, ch. 77-457; s. 19, ch. 78-292; ss. 2, 3, ch. 81-318; ss. 14, 15, ch. 82-229; ss. 54, 59, ch. 92-203.
