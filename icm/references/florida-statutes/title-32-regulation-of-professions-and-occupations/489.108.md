# Fla. Stat. § 489.108 — Rulemaking authority.

**Title:** XXXII — REGULATION OF PROFESSIONS AND OCCUPATIONS
**Chapter:** 489 — CONTRACTING
**Part:** I — CONSTRUCTION CONTRACTING
**Section:** 489.108
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The board has authority to adopt rules pursuant to ss. 120.536(1) and 120.54 to implement the provisions of this chapter conferring duties upon it.
