# Fla. Stat. § 487.2061 — Prohibited acts.

**Title:** XXXII — REGULATION OF PROFESSIONS AND OCCUPATIONS
**Chapter:** 487 — PESTICIDE REGULATION AND SAFETY
**Part:** II — FLORIDA AGRICULTURAL WORKER SAFETY ACT
**Section:** 487.2061
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Any person covered by this part may not:
(1)  
Fail to provide agricultural pesticide information as required in this part; or

(2)  
Take retaliatory action.

**History:** s. 52, ch. 2004-64.
