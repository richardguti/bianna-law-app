# Fla. Stat. § 475.616 — Examination requirements.

**Title:** XXXII — REGULATION OF PROFESSIONS AND OCCUPATIONS
**Chapter:** 475 — REAL ESTATE BROKERS, SALES ASSOCIATES, SCHOOLS, AND APPRAISERS
**Part:** II — APPRAISERS
**Section:** 475.616
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

To be certified as an appraiser, the applicant must demonstrate, by passing a written examination, that she or he possesses:
(1)  
A knowledge of technical terms commonly used in real estate appraisal.

(7)  
No applicant seeking to become registered or certified under this part may be rejected solely by virtue of membership or lack of membership in any particular appraisal organization.
