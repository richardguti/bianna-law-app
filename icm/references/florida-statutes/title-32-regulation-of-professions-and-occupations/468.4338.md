# Fla. Stat. § 468.4338 — Reactivation; continuing education.

**Title:** XXXII — REGULATION OF PROFESSIONS AND OCCUPATIONS
**Chapter:** 468 — MISCELLANEOUS PROFESSIONS AND OCCUPATIONS
**Part:** VIII — COMMUNITY ASSOCIATION MANAGEMENT
**Section:** 468.4338
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The council shall prescribe by rule continuing education requirements for reactivating a license. The continuing education requirements for reactivating a license may not exceed one renewal cycle of continuing education.

**History:** s. 8, ch. 96-291; s. 5, ch. 2012-61.
