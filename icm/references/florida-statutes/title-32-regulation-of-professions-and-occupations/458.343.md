# Fla. Stat. § 458.343 — Subpoena of certain records.

**Title:** XXXII — REGULATION OF PROFESSIONS AND OCCUPATIONS
**Chapter:** 458 — MEDICAL PRACTICE
**Part:** III — FARM LABOR CONTRACTOR REGISTRATION
**Section:** 458.343
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Notwithstanding the provisions of s. 456.057, the department may issue subpoenas duces tecum requiring the names and addresses of some or all of the patients of a physician against whom a complaint has been filed pursuant to s. 456.073.

**History:** ss. 1, 8, ch. 79-302; s. 300, ch. 81-259; ss. 2, 3, ch. 81-318; ss. 25, 26, ch. 86-245; s. 4, ch. 91-429; s. 48, ch. 98-166; s. 106, ch. 2000-160.
