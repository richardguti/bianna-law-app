# Fla. Stat. § 468.4336 — Renewal of license.

**Title:** XXXII — REGULATION OF PROFESSIONS AND OCCUPATIONS
**Chapter:** 468 — MISCELLANEOUS PROFESSIONS AND OCCUPATIONS
**Part:** VIII — COMMUNITY ASSOCIATION MANAGEMENT
**Section:** 468.4336
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
The department shall renew a license upon receipt of the renewal application and fee and upon proof of compliance with the continuing education requirements of s. 468.4337.

(2)  
The department shall adopt rules establishing a procedure for the biennial renewal of licenses.

**History:** s. 6, ch. 96-291.
