# Fla. Stat. § 484.043 — Board headquarters.

**Title:** XXXII — REGULATION OF PROFESSIONS AND OCCUPATIONS
**Chapter:** 484 — DISPENSING OF OPTICAL DEVICES AND HEARING AIDS
**Part:** II — FITTING AND DISPENSING OF HEARING AIDS
**Section:** 484.043
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The board shall maintain its official headquarters in Tallahassee.

**History:** ss. 4, 18, ch. 83-153; ss. 1, 7, ch. 84-94; ss. 19, 20, ch. 86-283; s. 22, ch. 90-341; s. 11, ch. 90-345; s. 4, ch. 91-429.
