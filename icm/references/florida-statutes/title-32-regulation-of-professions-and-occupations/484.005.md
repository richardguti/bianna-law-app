# Fla. Stat. § 484.005 — Authority to make rules.

**Title:** XXXII — REGULATION OF PROFESSIONS AND OCCUPATIONS
**Chapter:** 484 — DISPENSING OF OPTICAL DEVICES AND HEARING AIDS
**Part:** I — PREPARING AND DISPENSING OF EYEGLASSES AND OTHER OPTICAL DEVICES
**Section:** 484.005
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The board has authority to adopt rules pursuant to ss. 120.536(1) and 120.54 to implement the provisions of this part conferring duties upon it. Such rules shall include, but not be limited to, rules relating to:
(1)  
A standard of practice for opticians licensed pursuant to this part.

(2)
