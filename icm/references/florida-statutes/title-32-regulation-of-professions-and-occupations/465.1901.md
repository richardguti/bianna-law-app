# Fla. Stat. § 465.1901 — Practice of orthotics and pedorthics.

**Title:** XXXII — REGULATION OF PROFESSIONS AND OCCUPATIONS
**Chapter:** 465 — PHARMACY
**Part:** II — CERTIFIED NURSING ASSISTANTS
**Section:** 465.1901
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

——

The provisions of chapter 468 relating to orthotics or pedorthics do not apply to any licensed pharmacist or to any person acting under the supervision of a licensed pharmacist.
