# Fla. Stat. § 482.152 — Duties of certified operator in charge of pest control activities of licensee.

**Title:** XXXII — REGULATION OF PROFESSIONS AND OCCUPATIONS
**Chapter:** 482 — PEST CONTROL
**Part:** II — LANDSCAPE ARCHITECTURE
**Section:** 482.152
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

(9)  
If a special identification cardholder becomes a member of the Armed Forces of the United States on active duty, the renewal fee and continuing education requirements are waived while the individual remains on active duty as a member of the Armed Forces.
