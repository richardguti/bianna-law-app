# Fla. Stat. § 468.4536 — Renewal of licensure.

**Title:** XXXII — REGULATION OF PROFESSIONS AND OCCUPATIONS
**Chapter:** 468 — MISCELLANEOUS PROFESSIONS AND OCCUPATIONS
**Part:** IX — ATHLETE AGENTS
**Section:** 468.4536
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The department shall renew a license pursuant to procedures provided for in s. 455.203.

**History:** s. 5, ch. 95-307.
