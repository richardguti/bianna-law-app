# Fla. Stat. § 468.8322 — Insurance.

**Title:** XXXII — REGULATION OF PROFESSIONS AND OCCUPATIONS
**Chapter:** 468 — MISCELLANEOUS PROFESSIONS AND OCCUPATIONS
**Part:** XV — HOME INSPECTORS
**Section:** 468.8322
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

A home inspector shall maintain a commercial general liability insurance policy in an amount of not less than $300,000.

**History:** s. 2, ch. 2007-235.
