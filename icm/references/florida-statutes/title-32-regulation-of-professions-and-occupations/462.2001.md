# Fla. Stat. § 462.2001 — Saving clause.

**Title:** XXXII — REGULATION OF PROFESSIONS AND OCCUPATIONS
**Chapter:** 462 — NATUROPATHY
**Part:** III — FARM LABOR CONTRACTOR REGISTRATION
**Section:** 462.2001
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

All licenses to practice naturopathy issued pursuant to this chapter and valid on October 1, 1985, shall remain in full force and effect.

**History:** s. 11, ch. 85-303.
