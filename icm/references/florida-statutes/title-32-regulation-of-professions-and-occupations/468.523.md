# Fla. Stat. § 468.523 — Applicability of s. 20.165 and ch. 455.

**Title:** XXXII — REGULATION OF PROFESSIONS AND OCCUPATIONS
**Chapter:** 468 — MISCELLANEOUS PROFESSIONS AND OCCUPATIONS
**Part:** XI — EMPLOYEE LEASING COMPANIES
**Section:** 468.523
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

All provisions of s. 20.165 and chapter 455 relating to activities of regulatory boards shall apply.

**History:** ss. 5, 17, ch. 91-93; s. 4, ch. 91-429; s. 34, ch. 94-119; s. 95, ch. 98-166; s. 153, ch. 2000-160.
