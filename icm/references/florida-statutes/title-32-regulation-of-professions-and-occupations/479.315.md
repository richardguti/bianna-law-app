# Fla. Stat. § 479.315 — Highway rights-of-way; cost of sign removal.

**Title:** XXXII — REGULATION OF PROFESSIONS AND OCCUPATIONS
**Chapter:** 480 — MASSAGE THERAPY PRACTICE
**Part:** III — SIGN REMOVAL
**Section:** 479.315
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

All cost incurred by the department in connection with the removal of a sign located within the right-of-way of the State Highway System, interstate highway system, or federal-aid primary highway system shall be assessed against and collected from the owner of the sign or the advertiser displayed on the sign.

**History:** s. 43, ch. 2010-225.
