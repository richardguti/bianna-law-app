# Fla. Stat. § 479.155 — Local outdoor advertising or sign ordinances.

**Title:** XXXII — REGULATION OF PROFESSIONS AND OCCUPATIONS
**Chapter:** 479 — OUTDOOR ADVERTISING
**Part:** I — GENERAL PROVISIONS
**Section:** 479.155
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The provisions of this chapter shall not be deemed to supersede the rights and powers of counties and municipalities to enact outdoor advertising or sign ordinances.

**History:** s. 4, ch. 78-138; s. 2, ch. 81-318; ss. 16, 25, 26, ch. 84-227; s. 4, ch. 91-429.
