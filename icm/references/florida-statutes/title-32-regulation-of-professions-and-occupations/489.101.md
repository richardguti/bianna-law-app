# Fla. Stat. § 489.101 — Purpose.

**Title:** XXXII — REGULATION OF PROFESSIONS AND OCCUPATIONS
**Chapter:** 489 — CONTRACTING
**Part:** I — CONSTRUCTION CONTRACTING
**Section:** 489.101
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The Legislature deems it necessary in the interest of the public health, safety, and welfare to regulate the construction industry.

**History:** ss. 1, 17, ch. 79-200; ss. 2, 3, ch. 81-318; ss. 1, 20, 21, ch. 88-156; s. 4, ch. 91-429; s. 20, ch. 2000-332.
