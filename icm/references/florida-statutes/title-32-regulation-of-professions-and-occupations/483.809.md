# Fla. Stat. § 483.809 — Licensure; examinations; registration of trainees; approval of curricula.

**Title:** XXXII — REGULATION OF PROFESSIONS AND OCCUPATIONS
**Chapter:** 483 — HEALTH TESTING SERVICES
**Part:** I — CLINICAL LABORATORY PERSONNEL
**Section:** 483.809
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
LICENSING.——
The department shall provide biennial licensure of all clinical laboratory personnel who the board certifies have met the requirements of this part. The license of any person who fails to pay~Us shall be established, collected, and deposited in accordance with s. 456.025.

**History:** s. 32, ch. 92-58; s. 243, ch. 94-119; s. 136, ch. 98-166; s. 146, ch. 99-397; s. 196, ch. 2000-160; s. 9, ch. 2001-277.
