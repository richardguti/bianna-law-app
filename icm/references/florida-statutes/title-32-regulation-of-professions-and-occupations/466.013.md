# Fla. Stat. § 466.013 — Renewal of license.

**Title:** XXXII — REGULATION OF PROFESSIONS AND OCCUPATIONS
**Chapter:** 466 — DENTISTRY, DENTAL HYGIENE, AND DENTAL LABORATORIES
**Part:** II — CERTIFIED NURSING ASSISTANTS
**Section:** 466.013
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
The department shall renew a license upon receipt of the renewal application and the fee set by the board not to exceed $300.

(2)  
The department shall adopt rules establishing a procedure for the biennial renewal of licenses.

**History:** ss. 1, 3, ch. 79-330; ss. 2, 3, ch. 81-318; ss. 23, 24, ch. 86-291; s. 36, ch. 89-162; s. 60, ch. 91-137; s. 7, ch. 91-156; s. 4, ch. 91-429; s. 180, ch. 94-119.
