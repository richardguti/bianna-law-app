# Fla. Stat. § 468.1175 — Examination.

**Title:** XXXII — REGULATION OF PROFESSIONS AND OCCUPATIONS
**Chapter:** 468 — MISCELLANEOUS PROFESSIONS AND OCCUPATIONS
**Part:** I — SPEECH-LANGUAGE PATHOLOGY AND AUDIOLOGY
**Section:** 468.1175
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

A person desiring to be licensed as a speech-language pathologist or an audiologist shall apply to the appropriate examining entity for examination. A person who fails an examination may make application for reexamination to the appropriate examining entity.

**History:** ss. 8, 31, ch. 90-134; s. 21, ch. 90-341; ss. 1, 10, ch. 90-345; s. 4, ch. 91-429; s. 103, ch. 92-149.
