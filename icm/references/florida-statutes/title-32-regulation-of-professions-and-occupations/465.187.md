# Fla. Stat. § 465.187 — Sale of medicinal drugs.

**Title:** XXXII — REGULATION OF PROFESSIONS AND OCCUPATIONS
**Chapter:** 465 — PHARMACY
**Part:** II — CERTIFIED NURSING ASSISTANTS
**Section:** 465.187
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The sale of medicinal drugs dispensed upon the order of a practitioner pursuant to this chapter shall be entitled to the exemption from sales tax provided for in s. 212.08.

**History:** ss. 21, 27, ch. 86-256; s. 59, ch. 91-137; s. 6, ch. 91-156; s. 4, ch. 91-429.
