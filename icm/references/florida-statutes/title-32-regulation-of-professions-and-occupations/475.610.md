# Fla. Stat. § 475.610 — Purpose.

**Title:** XXXII — REGULATION OF PROFESSIONS AND OCCUPATIONS
**Chapter:** 475 — REAL ESTATE BROKERS, SALES ASSOCIATES, SCHOOLS, AND APPRAISERS
**Part:** I — REAL ESTATE BROKERS, SALES ASSOCIATES, AND SCHOOLS
**Section:** 475.610
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The Legislature deems it necessary in the interest of the public welfare to regulate real estate appraisers in this state.
