# Fla. Stat. § 464.014 — Inactive status.

**Title:** XXXII — REGULATION OF PROFESSIONS AND OCCUPATIONS
**Chapter:** 463 — OPTOMETRY
**Part:** I — NURSE PRACTICE ACT
**Section:** 464.014
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
The board shall adopt rules relating to application procedures for inactive status, to the biennial renewal of inactive licenses, and to the reactivation of licenses. The board shall prescribe by rule an application fee for inactive status, a renewal fee for inactive status, a delinquency fee, and a fee for the reactivation of a license. None of these fees may exceed the biennial renewal fee established by the board for biennial renewal of an active license.

(2)  
The department may not reactivate a license unless the inactive or delinquent licensee has paid any applicable biennial renewal or delinquency fee, or both, and a reactivation fee.
