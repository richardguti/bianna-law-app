# Fla. Stat. § 487.156 — Governmental agencies.

**Title:** XXXII — REGULATION OF PROFESSIONS AND OCCUPATIONS
**Chapter:** 487 — PESTICIDE REGULATION AND SAFETY
**Part:** I — FLORIDA PESTICIDE LAW
**Section:** 487.156
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

All governmental agencies shall be subject to the provisions of this part and rules adopted under this part. Public applicators using or supervising the use of restricted-use pesticides shall be subject to examination as provided in s. 487.044.
