# Fla. Stat. § 468.386 — Fees; local licensing requirements.

**Title:** XXXII — REGULATION OF PROFESSIONS AND OCCUPATIONS
**Chapter:** 468 — MISCELLANEOUS PROFESSIONS AND OCCUPATIONS
**Part:** VI — AUCTIONEERS
**Section:** 468.386
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
The board by rule may establish application, examination, licensure, renewal, and other reasonable and necessary fees, based upon the department's estimate of the costs to the board in administering this act.

(2)  
An auctioneer shall obtain a local occupational license, if required, in the jurisdiction in which his or her permanent business or branch office is located. However, no local government or local agency may charge any other fee for the practice of auctioneering or require any auctioneer's license in addition to the license required by this part.

**History:** ss. 6, 14, ch. 86-119; s. 6, ch. 87-210; s. 62, ch. 91-137; s. 11, ch. 91-156; s. 4, ch. 91-429; s. 300, ch. 97-103.
