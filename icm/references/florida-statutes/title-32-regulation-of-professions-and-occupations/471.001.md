# Fla. Stat. § 471.001 — Purpose.

**Title:** XXXII — REGULATION OF PROFESSIONS AND OCCUPATIONS
**Chapter:** 471 — ENGINEERING
**Part:** XVI — MOLD-RELATED SERVICES
**Section:** 471.001
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The Legislature deems it necessary in the interest of public health and safety to regulate the practice of engineering in this state.

**History:** ss. 1, 42, ch. 79-243; ss. 2, 3, ch. 81-318; ss. 14, 15, ch. 89-30; s. 4, ch. 91-429; s. 5, ch. 2000-332.
