# Fla. Stat. § 458.3129 — Interstate Medical Licensure Compact; applicability.

**Title:** XXXII — REGULATION OF PROFESSIONS AND OCCUPATIONS
**Chapter:** 458 — MEDICAL PRACTICE
**Part:** III — FARM LABOR CONTRACTOR REGISTRATION
**Section:** 458.3129
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

A physician licensed to practice allopathic medicine under s. 456.4501 is deemed to also be licensed under this chapter.

**History:** s. 52, ch. 2024-15.
