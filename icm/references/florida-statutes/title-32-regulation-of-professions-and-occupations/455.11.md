# Fla. Stat. § 455.11 — Qualification of immigrants for examination to practice a licensed profession or occupation.

**Title:** XXXII — REGULATION OF PROFESSIONS AND OCCUPATIONS
**Chapter:** 455 — BUSINESS AND PROFESSIONAL REGULATION: GENERAL PROVISIONS
**Part:** III — FARM LABOR CONTRACTOR REGISTRATION
**Section:** 455.11
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

No person shall be disqualified from practicing an occupation or profession regulated by the state solely because he or she is not a United States citizen.

**History:** ss. 1, 2, 3, ch. 72-125; s. 1, ch. 74-37; s. 1, ch. 77-174; s. 5, ch. 79-36; s. 187, ch. 97-103.

**Note:** Former s. 455.012.
