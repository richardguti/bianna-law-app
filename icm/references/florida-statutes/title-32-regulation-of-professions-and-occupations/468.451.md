# Fla. Stat. § 468.451 — Legislative findings and intent.

**Title:** XXXII — REGULATION OF PROFESSIONS AND OCCUPATIONS
**Chapter:** 468 — MISCELLANEOUS PROFESSIONS AND OCCUPATIONS
**Part:** IX — ATHLETE AGENTS
**Section:** 468.451
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The Legislature finds that dishonest or unscrupulous practices by agents who solicit representation of student athletes can cause significant harm to student athletes and the academic institutions for which they play. It is the intent of the Legislature to protect the interests of student athletes and academic institutions by regulating the activities of athlete agents.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 88-229; s. 1, ch. 89-296; s. 4, ch. 91-429; s. 1, ch. 95-307.
