# Fla. Stat. § 468.8415 — Renewal of license.

**Title:** XXXII — REGULATION OF PROFESSIONS AND OCCUPATIONS
**Chapter:** 468 — MISCELLANEOUS PROFESSIONS AND OCCUPATIONS
**Part:** XVI — MOLD-RELATED SERVICES
**Section:** 468.8415
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
The department shall renew a license upon receipt of the renewal application and fee and upon certification by the department that the licensee has satisfactorily completed the continuing education requirements of s. 468.8416.

(2)S proceedings have been terminated.

**History:** s. 3, ch. 2007-235; s. 27, ch. 2010-106; s. 19, ch. 2010-176; s. 1, ch. 2023-211.
