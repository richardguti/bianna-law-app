# Fla. Stat. § 487.012 — Declaration of purpose.

**Title:** XXXII — REGULATION OF PROFESSIONS AND OCCUPATIONS
**Chapter:** 487 — PESTICIDE REGULATION AND SAFETY
**Part:** I — FLORIDA PESTICIDE LAW
**Section:** 487.012
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The purpose of this part is to regulate the distribution, sale, and use of pesticides, except as provided in chapters 388 and 482, and to protect people and the environment from the adverse effects of pesticides.

**History:** s. 3, ch. 74-247; s. 8, ch. 79-210; s. 2, ch. 81-318; ss. 14, 15, ch. 82-167; ss. 2, 37, ch. 92-115; s. 3, ch. 94-233; s. 9, ch. 2000-154; s. 22, ch. 2004-64.

**Note:** Former s. 487.152.
