# Fla. Stat. § 457.118 — Effect of chapter on other health care practices.

**Title:** XXXII — REGULATION OF PROFESSIONS AND OCCUPATIONS
**Chapter:** 457 — ACUPUNCTURE
**Part:** III — FARM LABOR CONTRACTOR REGISTRATION
**Section:** 457.118
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

This chapter shall not be construed to expand or limit the scope of practice authorized for any health care professional licensed under chapter 458, chapter 459, chapter 460, chapter 461, chapter 466, chapter 474, or chapter 486, as such scope of practice is defined by statute or rule.

**History:** ss. 1, 2, ch. 80-375; ss. 2, 3, ch. 81-318; ss. 10, 13, 14, ch. 86-265; s. 57, ch. 91-137; s. 4, ch. 91-156; s. 85, ch. 91-224; s. 4, ch. 91-429; s. 198, ch. 97-103; s. 13, ch. 97-264; s. 5, ch. 98-418.

**Note:** Former s. 468.328.
