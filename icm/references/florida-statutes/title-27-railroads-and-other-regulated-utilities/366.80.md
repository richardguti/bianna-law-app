# Fla. Stat. § 366.80 — Short title.

**Title:** XXVII — RAILROADS AND OTHER REGULATED UTILITIES
**Chapter:** 366 — PUBLIC UTILITIES
**Part:** II — JOINT ELECTRIC POWER SUPPLY PROJECTS
**Section:** 366.80
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Sections 366.80-366.83 and 403.519 shall be known and may be cited as the “Florida Energy Efficiency and Conservation Act.”

**History:** s. 5, ch. 80-65; s. 2, ch. 81-318; ss. 20, 22, ch. 89-292; s. 4, ch. 91-429; s. 68, ch. 2014-17.
