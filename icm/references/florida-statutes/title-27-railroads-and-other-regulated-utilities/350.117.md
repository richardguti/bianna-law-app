# Fla. Stat. § 350.117 — Reports; audits.

**Title:** XXVII — RAILROADS AND OTHER REGULATED UTILITIES
**Chapter:** 350 — FLORIDA PUBLIC SERVICE COMMISSION
**Part:** III — CENTRAL FLORIDA EXPRESSWAY AUTHORITY
**Section:** 350.117
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
The commission may require such regular or emergency reports, including, but not limited to, financial reports, as the commission deems necessary to fulfill its obligations under the law.

(2)  
The commission may perform management and operation audits of any regulated company. The commission may consider the results of such audits in establishing rates; however, the company shall not be denied due process as a result of the use of any such management or operation audit.F.S. 350.1156350.115  
Uniform systems and classifications of accounts.
——

The commission may prescribe by rule uniform systems and classifications of accounts for each type of regulated company and approve or establish adequate, fair, and reasonable depreciation rates and charges.
