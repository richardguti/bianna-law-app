# Fla. Stat. § 350.111 — “Regulated company” defined.

**Title:** XXVII — RAILROADS AND OTHER REGULATED UTILITIES
**Chapter:** 350 — FLORIDA PUBLIC SERVICE COMMISSION
**Part:** III — CENTRAL FLORIDA EXPRESSWAY AUTHORITY
**Section:** 350.111
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

As used in ss. 350.111-350.117 and ss. 350.121-350.128, “regulated company” means any public utility as defined in s. 366.02 or any person holding a valid and current certificate from the commission under chapter 351, chapter 364, chapter 365, or chapter 367.

**History:** ss. 3, 6, ch. 80-289; ss. 2, 3, ch. 81-318; s. 6, ch. 87-50; s. 57, ch. 95-143.
