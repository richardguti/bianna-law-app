# Fla. Stat. § 361.10 — Purpose.

**Title:** XXVII — RAILROADS AND OTHER REGULATED UTILITIES
**Chapter:** 361 — PUBLIC UTILITIES: SPECIAL POWERS
**Part:** II — JOINT ELECTRIC POWER SUPPLY PROJECTS
**Section:** 361.10
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The purpose of this act is to implement the provisions of s. 10(d), Art. VII of the State Constitution, as amended. This act may be known and cited as the “Joint Power Act.”

**History:** s. 1, ch. 75-200.
