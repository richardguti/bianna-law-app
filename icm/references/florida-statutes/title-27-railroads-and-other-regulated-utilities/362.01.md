# Fla. Stat. § 362.01 — Powers to occupy roads.

**Title:** XXVII — RAILROADS AND OTHER REGULATED UTILITIES
**Chapter:** 361 — PUBLIC UTILITIES: SPECIAL POWERS
**Part:** II — JOINT ELECTRIC POWER SUPPLY PROJECTS
**Section:** 362.01
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Any telegraph or telephone company chartered by this or another state, or any individual operating or desiring to operate a telegraph or telephone line, or lines, in this state, may erect posts, wires and other fixtures for telegraph or telephone purposes on or beside any public road or highway; provided, however, that the same shall not be set so as to obstruct or interfere with the common uses of said roads or highways. Permission to occupy the streets of an incorporated city or town must first be obtained from the city or town council.
