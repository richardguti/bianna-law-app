# Fla. Stat. § 366.01 — Legislative declaration.

**Title:** XXVII — RAILROADS AND OTHER REGULATED UTILITIES
**Chapter:** 365 — USE OF TELEPHONES AND FACSIMILE MACHINES
**Part:** II — JOINT ELECTRIC POWER SUPPLY PROJECTS
**Section:** 366.01
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The regulation of public utilities as defined herein is declared to be in the public interest and this chapter shall be deemed to be an exercise of the police power of the state for the protection of the public welfare and all the provisions hereof shall be liberally construed for the accomplishment of that purpose.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 26545, 1951; s. 3, ch. 76-168; s. 1, ch. 77-457; s. 16, ch. 80-35; s. 2, ch. 81-318; ss. 20, 22, ch. 89-292; s. 4, ch. 91-429.
