# Fla. Stat. § 354.05 — Term of office; removal.

**Title:** XXVII — RAILROADS AND OTHER REGULATED UTILITIES
**Chapter:** 354 — SPECIAL OFFICERS FOR CARRIERS
**Part:** III — CENTRAL FLORIDA EXPRESSWAY AUTHORITY
**Section:** 354.05
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The commission of a special officer shall continue so long as he or she is employed in such capacity by the railroad or other common carrier. However, a special officer may be removed at any time, in the manner and for the causes provided by law.
