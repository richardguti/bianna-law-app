# Fla. Stat. § 366.072 — Rate adjustment orders.

**Title:** XXVII — RAILROADS AND OTHER REGULATED UTILITIES
**Chapter:** 366 — PUBLIC UTILITIES
**Part:** II — JOINT ELECTRIC POWER SUPPLY PROJECTS
**Section:** 366.072
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

——

Any order issued by the commission adjusting general increases or reductions of the rates of an electric or gas company shall be reduced to writing including any dissenting or concurring opinions within 20 days of the official vote of the commission. Within said 20 days, the commission shall also mail a copy of the order to the clerk of the circuit court of each county in which customers are served who are affected by the rate adjustment, which copy shall be kept on file and made available to the public.
