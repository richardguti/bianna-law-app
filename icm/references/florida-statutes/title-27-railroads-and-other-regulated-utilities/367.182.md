# Fla. Stat. § 367.182 — Saving clause.

**Title:** XXVII — RAILROADS AND OTHER REGULATED UTILITIES
**Chapter:** 367 — WATER AND WASTEWATER SYSTEMS
**Part:** II — JOINT ELECTRIC POWER SUPPLY PROJECTS
**Section:** 367.182
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

——

All certificates and authorizations valid on the effective date of chapter 80-99, Laws of Florida, shall remain in full force and effect. Henceforth, all certif4d “commission” shall be read as “the governing body of such county” when the context implies or admits. The authorized rate of return shall be no less than the weighted cost of the capital of the utility, including debt and equity.
