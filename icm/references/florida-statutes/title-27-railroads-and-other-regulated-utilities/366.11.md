# Fla. Stat. § 366.11 — Certain exemptions.

**Title:** XXVII — RAILROADS AND OTHER REGULATED UTILITIES
**Chapter:** 366 — PUBLIC UTILITIES
**Part:** II — JOINT ELECTRIC POWER SUPPLY PROJECTS
**Section:** 366.11
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
No provision of this chapter shall apply in any manner, other than as specified in ss. 366.04, 366.05(7) and (8), 366.051, 366.055, 366.093, 366.095, 366.14, 366.80-366.83, and 366.91, to utilities owned and operated by municipalities, whether within or without any municipality, or by cooperatives organized and existing under the Rural Electric Cooperative Law of the state, or to the sale of electricity, manufactured gas, or natural gas at wholesale by any public utility to, and the purchase by, any municipality or cooperative under and pursuant to any contracts now in effect or which may be entered into in the future, when such municipality or cooperative is engaged in the sale and distribution of electricity or manufactured or natural gas, or to the rates provided for in such contracts.
