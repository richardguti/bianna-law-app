# Fla. Stat. § 367.1213 — Adequate land ownership; commission rulemaking authority.

**Title:** XXVII — RAILROADS AND OTHER REGULATED UTILITIES
**Chapter:** 367 — WATER AND WASTEWATER SYSTEMS
**Part:** II — JOINT ELECTRIC POWER SUPPLY PROJECTS
**Section:** 367.1213
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

A utility under the Water and Wastewater System Regulatory Law must own the land or possess the right to continued use of the land upon which treatment facilities are located. The commission shall adopt rules in accordance with this section.

**History:** s. 2, ch. 98-42.
