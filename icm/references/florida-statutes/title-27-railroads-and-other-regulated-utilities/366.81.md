# Fla. Stat. § 366.81 — Legislative findings and intent.

**Title:** XXVII — RAILROADS AND OTHER REGULATED UTILITIES
**Chapter:** 366 — PUBLIC UTILITIES
**Part:** II — JOINT ELECTRIC POWER SUPPLY PROJECTS
**Section:** 366.81
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The Legislature finds and declares that it is critical to utilize the most efficient and cost-effective demand-side renewable energy systems and conservation systems in order to protect the health, prosperity, and general welfare of the state and its citizens.
