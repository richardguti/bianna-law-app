# Fla. Stat. § 361.12 — Joint electric power supply project.

**Title:** XXVII — RAILROADS AND OTHER REGULATED UTILITIES
**Chapter:** 361 — PUBLIC UTILITIES: SPECIAL POWERS
**Part:** II — JOINT ELECTRIC POWER SUPPLY PROJECTS
**Section:** 361.12
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

In addition to its existing powers, any electric utility, or any organization, association, or separate legal entity whose membership consists only of electric utilities, by participating in an agreement to implement a project, is authorized and empowered to join with any one or more of the following:
(1)  
Any other electric utility;

(2)  
Any organization, association, or separate legal entity whose membership consists only of electric utilities;

(3)  
One or more foreign public utilities; or

(4)  
Any other person, if the right to full possession and to all of the use, services, output, and capacity of any such project during the original estimated useful life thereof is vested, subject to creditors' rights, in any one or more electric utilities, in any on3ctricity to retail or wholesale customers, or both, on a continuous, reliable, and dependable basis; or any affiliate or subsidiary of such person, the business of which is limited to the generation or transmission, or both, of electrical energy and activities reasonably incidental thereto.

**History:** s. 2, ch. 75-200; s. 3, ch. 82-53.
