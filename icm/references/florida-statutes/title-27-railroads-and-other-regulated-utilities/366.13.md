# Fla. Stat. § 366.13 — Taxes, not affected.

**Title:** XXVII — RAILROADS AND OTHER REGULATED UTILITIES
**Chapter:** 366 — PUBLIC UTILITIES
**Part:** II — JOINT ELECTRIC POWER SUPPLY PROJECTS
**Section:** 366.13
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

No provision of this chapter shall in any way affect any municipal tax or franchise tax in any manner whatsoever.
