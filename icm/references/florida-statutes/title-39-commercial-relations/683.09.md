# Fla. Stat. § 683.09 — DeSoto Day, legal holiday in Manatee County.

**Title:** XXXIX — COMMERCIAL RELATIONS
**Chapter:** 683 — LEGAL HOLIDAYS; SPECIAL OBSERVANCES
**Part:** VI — TRANSITIONAL PROVISIONS
**Section:** 683.09
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The last Friday of DeSoto Week every year shall be known as “DeSoto Day” and shall be a legal holiday in Manatee County, and all city, county, and state offices and banking institutions may remain closed on DeSoto Day.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 69-258; s. 1, ch. 69-289.
