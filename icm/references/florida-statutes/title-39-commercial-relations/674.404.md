# Fla. Stat. § 674.404 — Bank not obliged to pay check more than 6 months old.

**Title:** XXXIX — COMMERCIAL RELATIONS
**Chapter:** 674 — UNIFORM COMMERCIAL CODE: BANK DEPOSITS AND COLLECTIONS
**Part:** IV — RELATIONSHIP BETWEEN PAYOR BANK AND ITS CUSTOMER
**Section:** 674.404
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

A bank is under no obligation to a customer having a checking account to pay a check, other than a certified check, which is presented more than 6 months after its date; but it may charge its customer's account for a payment made thereafter in good faith.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 65-254; s. 41, ch. 92-82.

**Note:** s. 4-404, U.C.C.; supersedes s. 659.31.
