# Fla. Stat. § 677.306 — Altered bills of lading.

**Title:** XXXIX — COMMERCIAL RELATIONS
**Chapter:** 677 — UNIFORM COMMERCIAL CODE: DOCUMENTS OF TITLE
**Part:** III — BILLS OF LADING: SPECIAL PROVISIONS
**Section:** 677.306
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

An unauthorized alteration or filling in of a blank in a bill of lading leaves the bill enforceable according to its original tenor.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 65-254.

**Note:** s. 7-306, U.C.C.; supersedes s. 678.13.
