# Fla. Stat. § 672.515 — Preserving evidence of goods in dispute.

**Title:** XXXIX — COMMERCIAL RELATIONS
**Chapter:** 672 — UNIFORM COMMERCIAL CODE: SALES
**Part:** V — PERFORMANCE
**Section:** 672.515
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

In furtherance of the adjustment of any claim or dispute:
(1)  
Either party on reasonable notification to the other and for the purpose of ascertaining the facts and preserving evidence has the right to inspect, test and sample the goods including such of them as may be in the possession or control of the other; and

(2)

When documents deliverable on acceptance; when on payment.
——

Unless otherwise agreed documents against which a draft is drawn are to be delivered to the drawee on acceptance of the draft if it is payable more than 3 days after presentment; otherwise, only on payment.
