# Fla. Stat. § 679.339 — Priority subject to subordination.

**Title:** XXXIX — COMMERCIAL RELATIONS
**Chapter:** 679 — UNIFORM COMMERCIAL CODE: SECURED TRANSACTIONS
**Part:** III — PERFECTION AND PRIORITY
**Section:** 679.339
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

This chapter does not preclude subordination by agreement by a person entitled to priority.

**History:** s. 3, ch. 2001-198.
