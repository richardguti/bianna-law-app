# Fla. Stat. § 679.3031 — Law governing perfection and priority of security interests in goods covered by a certificate of title.F.S. 679.30216679.3021

**Title:** XXXIX — COMMERCIAL RELATIONS
**Chapter:** 679 — UNIFORM COMMERCIAL CODE: SECURED TRANSACTIONS
**Part:** III — PERFECTION AND PRIORITY
**Section:** 679.3031
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

While farm products are located in a jurisdiction, the local law of that jurisdiction governs perfection, the effect of perfection or nonperfection, and the priority of an agricultural lien on the farm products.

**History:** s. 3, ch. 2001-198.
