# Fla. Stat. § 683.25 — Bill of Rights Day.

**Title:** XXXIX — COMMERCIAL RELATIONS
**Chapter:** 683 — LEGAL HOLIDAYS; SPECIAL OBSERVANCES
**Part:** VI — TRANSITIONAL PROVISIONS
**Section:** 683.25
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
December 15 of each year is designated as “Bill of Rights Day.”

(2)  
The Governor may issue annually a proclamation designating December 15 as “Bill of Rights Day” and calling upon all citizens of the state to observe the occasion.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 2001-88.
