# Fla. Stat. § 682.25 — Disputes excluded.

**Title:** XXXIX — COMMERCIAL RELATIONS
**Chapter:** 682 — ARBITRATION CODE
**Part:** VI — TRANSITIONAL PROVISIONS
**Section:** 682.25
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

This chapter does not apply to any dispute involving child custody, visitation, or child support.

**History:** s. 36, ch. 2013-232.
