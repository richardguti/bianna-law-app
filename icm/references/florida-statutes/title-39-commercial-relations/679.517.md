# Fla. Stat. § 679.517 — Effect of indexing errors.

**Title:** XXXIX — COMMERCIAL RELATIONS
**Chapter:** 679 — UNIFORM COMMERCIAL CODE: SECURED TRANSACTIONS
**Part:** V — FILING
**Section:** 679.517
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The failure of the filing office to index a record correctly does not affect the effectiveness of the filed record.

**History:** s. 6, ch. 2001-198.
