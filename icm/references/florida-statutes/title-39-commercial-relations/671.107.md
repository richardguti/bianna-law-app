# Fla. Stat. § 671.107 — Waiver or renunciation of claim or right after breach.

**Title:** XXXIX — COMMERCIAL RELATIONS
**Chapter:** 671 — UNIFORM COMMERCIAL CODE: GENERAL PROVISIONS
**Part:** I — SHORT TITLE, CONSTRUCTION, APPLICATION, AND SUBJECT MATTER
**Section:** 671.107
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

A claim or right arising out of an alleged breach can be discharged in whole or in part without consideration by agreement of the aggrieved party in a signed record.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 65-254; s. 7, ch. 2007-134; s. 35, ch. 2025-92.

**Note:** s. 1-107, U.C.C.; supersedes ss. 675.28, 675.30.
