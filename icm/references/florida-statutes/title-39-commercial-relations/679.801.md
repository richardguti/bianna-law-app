# Fla. Stat. § 679.801 — Saving clause.

**Title:** XXXIX — COMMERCIAL RELATIONS
**Chapter:** 679 — UNIFORM COMMERCIAL CODE: SECURED TRANSACTIONS
**Part:** VII — TRANSITION
**Section:** 679.801
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
Except as otherwise provided in this part, this part applies to a transaction or lien within its scope, even if the transaction or lien was entered into or created before July 1, 2013.

(2)  
The amendments to this chapter by ch. 2012-59, Laws of Florida, do not affect an action, case, or proceeding commenced before July 1, 2013.

**History:** ss. 17, 19, ch. 2012-59.
