# Fla. Stat. § 679.601 — Rights after default; judicial enforcement; consignor or buyer of accounts, chattel paper, payment intangibles, or promissory notes.

**Title:** XXXIX — COMMERCIAL RELATIONS
**Chapter:** 679 — UNIFORM COMMERCIAL CODE: SECURED TRANSACTIONS
**Part:** VI — DEFAULT
**Section:** 679.601
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
After default, a secured party has the rights provided in this part and, except as otherwise provided in s. 679.602, those provided by agreement of the parties. A secured party:
(a)  
May reduce a claim to judgment, foreclose, or otherwise enforce the claim, security interest, or agricultural lien by any available judicial procedure; and

(b)  
If the collateral is documents, may proceed either as to the documents or as to the goods they cover.

(2)  
A secured party in possession of collateral or control of collateral under s. 679.1041, s. 679.1051, s. 679.1052, s. 679.1053, s. 679.1061, or s. 679.1071 has the rights and duties provided in s. 679.2071.

(3)  
The rights under subsections (1) and (2) are cumulative and may be exercised simultaneously.

(4)  
Except as otherwise provided in subsection (7) and s. 679.605, after default, a debtor and an obligor have the rights provided in this part and by agreement of the parties.

(5)

Agreement on standards concerning rights and duties.
——

(1)  
The parties may determine by agreement the standards measuring the fulfillment of the rights of a debtor or obligor and the duties of a secured party under a rule stated in s. 679.602 if the standards are not manifestly unreasonable.
