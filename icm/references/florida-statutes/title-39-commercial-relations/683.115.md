# Fla. Stat. § 683.115 — Law Enforcement Memorial Day.

**Title:** XXXIX — COMMERCIAL RELATIONS
**Chapter:** 683 — LEGAL HOLIDAYS; SPECIAL OBSERVANCES
**Part:** VI — TRANSITIONAL PROVISIONS
**Section:** 683.115
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
May 15 of each year is hereby designated as “Law Enforcement Memorial Day.”

(2)  
The Governor may issue annually a proclamation declaring May 15 to be a day of mourning throughout the state.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 78-46.
