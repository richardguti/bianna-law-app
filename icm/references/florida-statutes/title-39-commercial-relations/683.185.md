# Fla. Stat. § 683.185 — Everglades Day.

**Title:** XXXIX — COMMERCIAL RELATIONS
**Chapter:** 683 — LEGAL HOLIDAYS; SPECIAL OBSERVANCES
**Part:** VI — TRANSITIONAL PROVISIONS
**Section:** 683.185
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

April 7 of each year is designated as “Everglades Day.”

**History:** s. 1, ch. 2012-47.
