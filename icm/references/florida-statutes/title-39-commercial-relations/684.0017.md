# Fla. Stat. § 684.0017 — Competence of arbitral tribunal to rule on its jurisdiction.

**Title:** XXXIX — COMMERCIAL RELATIONS
**Chapter:** 684 — INTERNATIONAL COMMERCIAL ARBITRATION
**Part:** VI — TRANSITIONAL PROVISIONS
**Section:** 684.0017
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
The arbitral tribunal may rule on its own jurisdiction, including any objections with respect to the existence or validity of the arbitration agreement. For that purpose, an arbitration clause that forms part of a contract shall be treated as an agreement independent of the other terms of the contract.
