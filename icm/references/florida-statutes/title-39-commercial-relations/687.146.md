# Fla. Stat. § 687.146 — Criminal penalties.

**Title:** XXXIX — COMMERCIAL RELATIONS
**Chapter:** 687 — INTEREST AND USURY; LENDING PRACTICES
**Part:** VI — TRANSITIONAL PROVISIONS
**Section:** 687.146
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Whoever violates any provision of this act commits a felony of the third degree, punishable as provided in s. 775.082, s. 775.083, or s. 775.084.
