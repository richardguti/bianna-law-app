# Fla. Stat. § 679.329 — Priority of security interests in letter-of-credit right.

**Title:** XXXIX — COMMERCIAL RELATIONS
**Chapter:** 679 — UNIFORM COMMERCIAL CODE: SECURED TRANSACTIONS
**Part:** III — PERFECTION AND PRIORITY
**Section:** 679.329
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The following rules govern priority among conflicting security interests in the same letter-of-credit right:
(1)  
A security interest held by a secured party having control of the letter-of-credit right under s. 679.1071 has priority to the extent of its control over a conflicting security interest held by a secured party that does not have control.

(2)  
Security interests perfected by control under s. 679.3141 rank according to priority in time of obtaining control.

**History:** s. 3, ch. 2001-198; s. 161, ch. 2025-92.
