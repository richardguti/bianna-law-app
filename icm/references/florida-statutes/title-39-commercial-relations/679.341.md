# Fla. Stat. § 679.341 — Bank's rights and duties with respect to deposit account.

**Title:** XXXIX — COMMERCIAL RELATIONS
**Chapter:** 679 — UNIFORM COMMERCIAL CODE: SECURED TRANSACTIONS
**Part:** III — PERFECTION AND PRIORITY
**Section:** 679.341
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Except as otherwise provided in s. 679.340(3), and unless the bank otherwise agrees in a signed record, a bank's rights and duties with respect to a deposit account maintained with the bank are not terminated, suspended, or modified by:
(1)  
The creation, attachment, or perfection of a security interest in the deposit account;

(2)  
The bank's knowledge of the security interest; or

(3)  
The bank's receipt of instructions from the secured party.

**History:** s. 3, ch. 2001-198; s. 99, ch. 2025-92.
