# Fla. Stat. § 677.506 — Delivery without indorsement; right to compel indorsement.

**Title:** XXXIX — COMMERCIAL RELATIONS
**Chapter:** 677 — UNIFORM COMMERCIAL CODE: DOCUMENTS OF TITLE
**Part:** V — WAREHOUSE RECEIPTS AND BILLS OF LADING: NEGOTIATION AND TRANSFER
**Section:** 677.506
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The transferee of a negotiable tangible document of title has a specifically enforceable right to have its transferor supply any necessary indorsement but the transfer becomes a negotiation only as of the time the indorsement is supplied.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 65-254; s. 642, ch. 97-102; s. 50, ch. 2010-131.

**Note:** s. 7-506, U.C.C.; supersedes s. 678.45.
