# Fla. Stat. § 682.07 — Representation by attorney.

**Title:** XXXIX — COMMERCIAL RELATIONS
**Chapter:** 682 — ARBITRATION CODE
**Part:** VI — TRANSITIONAL PROVISIONS
**Section:** 682.07
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

A party has the right to be represented by an attorney at any arbitration proceeding or hearing under this law.

**History:** s. 6, ch. 57-402; s. 12, ch. 67-254; s. 17, ch. 2013-232.

**Note:** Former s. 57.15.
