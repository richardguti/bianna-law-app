# Fla. Stat. § 679.902 — Transitional provisions.

**Title:** XXXIX — COMMERCIAL RELATIONS
**Chapter:** 679 — UNIFORM COMMERCIAL CODE: SECURED TRANSACTIONS
**Part:** IX — TRANSITIONAL PROVISIONS
**Section:** 679.902
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Effective July 1, 2025, chapter 679 shall be amended by this act, including the transitional provisions for chapters 669 and 679, as amended by this act, as provided in part II of chapter 669.

**History:** s. 122, ch. 2025-92.
