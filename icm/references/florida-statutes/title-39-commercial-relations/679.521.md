# Fla. Stat. § 679.521 — Uniform form of written financing statement and amendment.

**Title:** XXXIX — COMMERCIAL RELATIONS
**Chapter:** 679 — UNIFORM COMMERCIAL CODE: SECURED TRANSACTIONS
**Part:** V — FILING
**Section:** 679.521
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The Secretary of State shall develop or approve acceptable forms for use in filing under this chapter. Such forms must be in accord with the requirements of Florida law, including s. 201.22. The secretary may, if he or she finds that such forms meet these requirements, approve the use of a standard national form for this purpose.

**History:** s. 6, ch. 2001-198.
