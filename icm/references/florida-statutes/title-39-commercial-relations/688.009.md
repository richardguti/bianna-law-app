# Fla. Stat. § 688.009 — Uniformity of application and construction.

**Title:** XXXIX — COMMERCIAL RELATIONS
**Chapter:** 688 — UNIFORM TRADE SECRETS ACT
**Part:** VI — TRANSITIONAL PROVISIONS
**Section:** 688.009
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Sections 688.001-688.009 shall be applied and construed to effectuate its general purpose to make uniform the law with respect to the subject of this act among states enacting it.

**History:** s. 9, ch. 88-254.
