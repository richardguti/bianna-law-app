# Fla. Stat. § 683.16 — Retired Teachers' Day.

**Title:** XXXIX — COMMERCIAL RELATIONS
**Chapter:** 683 — LEGAL HOLIDAYS; SPECIAL OBSERVANCES
**Part:** VI — TRANSITIONAL PROVISIONS
**Section:** 683.16
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
The Sunday commencing the third week of November of each year is hereby designated as “Retired Teachers' Day.”

F.S. 683.156683.15  
Teacher's Day.
——

(1)  
The third Friday in May of each year is designated as Teacher's Day.

(2)  
The Governor may issue annually a proclamation designating the third Friday in May as Teacher's Day and calling upon public schools and citizens of the state to observe the occasion.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 78-203.
