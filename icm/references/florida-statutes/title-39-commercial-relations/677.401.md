# Fla. Stat. § 677.401 — Irregularities in issue of receipt or bill or conduct of issuer.

**Title:** XXXIX — COMMERCIAL RELATIONS
**Chapter:** 677 — UNIFORM COMMERCIAL CODE: DOCUMENTS OF TITLE
**Part:** III — BILLS OF LADING: SPECIAL PROVISIONS
**Section:** 677.401
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The obligations imposed by this chapter on an issuer apply to a document of title even if:
(1)  
The document does not comply with the requirements of this chapter or of any other statute, rule of law, or regulation regarding its issuance, form, or content;

(2)  
The issuer violated laws regulating the conduct of its business;

(3)  
The goods covered by the document were owned by the bailee when the document was issued; or

(4)  
The person issuing the document is not a warehouse but the document purports to be a warehouse receipt.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 65-254; s. 634, ch. 97-102; s. 41, ch. 2010-131.

**Note:** s. 7-401, U.C.C.; supersedes s. 678.20.
