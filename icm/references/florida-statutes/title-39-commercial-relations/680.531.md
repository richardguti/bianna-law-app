# Fla. Stat. § 680.531 — Standing to sue third parties for injury to goods.

Lessor's incidental damages.
——

Incidental damages to an aggrieved lessor include any commercially reasonable charges, expenses, or commissions incurred in stopping delivery, in the transportation, care, and custody of goods after the lessee's default, or in connection with return or disposition of the goods, or otherwise resulting from the default.
