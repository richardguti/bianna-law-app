# Fla. Stat. § 678.5021 — Assertion of adverse claim against entitlement holder.

**Title:** XXXIX — COMMERCIAL RELATIONS
**Chapter:** 678 — UNIFORM COMMERCIAL CODE: INVESTMENT SECURITIES
**Part:** V — SECURITY ENTITLEMENTS
**Section:** 678.5021
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

An action based on an adverse claim to a financial asset, whether framed in conversion, replevin, constructive trust, equitable lien, or other theory, may not be asserted against a person who acquires a security entitlement under s. 678.5011 for value and without notice of the adverse claim.

**History:** s. 5, ch. 98-11.
