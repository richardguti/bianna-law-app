# Fla. Stat. § 683.195 — Florida Jewish History Month.

**Title:** XXXIX — COMMERCIAL RELATIONS
**Chapter:** 683 — LEGAL HOLIDAYS; SPECIAL OBSERVANCES
**Part:** VI — TRANSITIONAL PROVISIONS
**Section:** 683.195
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
The month of January of each year is designated as “Florida Jewish History Month.”

(2)  
The Governor may issue a proclamation annually designating the month of January as “Florida Jewish History Month” and calling upon the citizens of the state to observe the occasion.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 2003-7.
