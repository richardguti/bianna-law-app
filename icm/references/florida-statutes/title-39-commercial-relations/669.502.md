# Fla. Stat. § 669.502 — Definitions.

**Title:** XXXIX — COMMERCIAL RELATIONS
**Chapter:** 669 — UNIFORM COMMERCIAL CODE: CONTROLLABLE ELECTRONIC RECORDS AND TRANSITIONAL PROVISIONS
**Part:** II — TRANSITIONAL PROVISIONS
**Section:** 669.502
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

As used in this part:
(1)(a)  
“Adjustment date” means July 1, 2026.

(b)  
“Article 12” means Article 12 of the Uniform Commercial Code.

(c)  
“Article 12 property” means a controllable account, controllable electronic record, or controllable payment intangible.
