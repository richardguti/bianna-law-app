# Fla. Stat. § 674.204 — Effect of instructions.

**Title:** XXXIX — COMMERCIAL RELATIONS
**Chapter:** 674 — UNIFORM COMMERCIAL CODE: BANK DEPOSITS AND COLLECTIONS
**Part:** II — COLLECTION OF ITEMS: DEPOSITARY AND COLLECTING BANKS
**Section:** 674.204
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Subject to chapter 673 concerning conversion of instruments (s. 673.4201) and restrictive indorsements (s. 673.2061), only a collecting bank's transferor can give instructions that affect the bank or constitute notice to it, and a collecting bank is not liable to prior parties for any action taken pursuant to the instructions or in accordance with any agreement with its transferor.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 65-254; s. 21, ch. 92-82.

**Note:** s. 4-203, U.C.C.
