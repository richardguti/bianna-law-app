# Fla. Stat. § 679.526 — Filing-office rules.

**Title:** XXXIX — COMMERCIAL RELATIONS
**Chapter:** 679 — UNIFORM COMMERCIAL CODE: SECURED TRANSACTIONS
**Part:** V — FILING
**Section:** 679.526
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The Department of State may adopt and publish rules to administer this chapter. The filing-office rules must be:
(1)  
Consistent with this chapter.

(2)  
Adopted and published in accordance with the Administrative Procedure Act.

**History:** s. 6, ch. 2001-198|r to be cut under s. 679.5021(3). However, the recording and satisfaction fees that otherwise would be applicable to the mortgage apply.
