# Fla. Stat. § 675.105 — Consideration.

**Title:** XXXIX — COMMERCIAL RELATIONS
**Chapter:** 675 — UNIFORM COMMERCIAL CODE: LETTERS OF CREDIT
**Part:** V — COLLECTION OF DOCUMENTARY DRAFTS
**Section:** 675.105
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Consideration is not required to issue, amend, transfer, or cancel a letter of credit, advice, or confirmation.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 65-254; s. 1, ch. 99-137.

**Note:** s. 5-105, U.C.C.; supersedes ss. 674.27, 674.31.
