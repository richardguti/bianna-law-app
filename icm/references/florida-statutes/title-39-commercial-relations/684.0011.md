# Fla. Stat. § 684.0011 — Number of arbitrators.

**Title:** XXXIX — COMMERCIAL RELATIONS
**Chapter:** 684 — INTERNATIONAL COMMERCIAL ARBITRATION
**Part:** VI — TRANSITIONAL PROVISIONS
**Section:** 684.0011
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
The parties may determine the number of arbitrators.

(2)  
If the parties fail to determine the number of arbitrators, the number of arbitrators shall be three.

**History:** s. 11, ch. 2010-60.
