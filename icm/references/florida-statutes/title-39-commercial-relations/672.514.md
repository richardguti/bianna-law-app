# Fla. Stat. § 672.514 — When documents deliverable on acceptance; when on payment.

**Title:** XXXIX — COMMERCIAL RELATIONS
**Chapter:** 672 — UNIFORM COMMERCIAL CODE: SALES
**Part:** V — PERFORMANCE
**Section:** 672.514
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Unless otherwise agreed documents against which a draft is drawn are to be delivered to the drawee on acceptance of the draft if it is payable more than 3 days after presentment; otherwise, only on payment.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 65-254.

**Note:** s. 2-514, U.C.C.
