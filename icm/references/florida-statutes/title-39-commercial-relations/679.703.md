# Fla. Stat. § 679.703 — Security interest perfected before effective date.

**Title:** XXXIX — COMMERCIAL RELATIONS
**Chapter:** 679 — UNIFORM COMMERCIAL CODE: SECURED TRANSACTIONS
**Part:** VII — TRANSITION
**Section:** 679.703
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
A security interest that is enforceable immediately before this act takes effect and would have priority over the rights of a person who becomes a lien creditor at that time is a perfected security interest under this act if, when this act takes effect, the applicable requirements for enforceability and perfection under this act are satisfied without further action.

(2)  
Except as otherwise provided in s. 679.705, if, immediately before this act takes effect, a security interest is enforceable and would have priority over the rights of a person who becomes a lien creditor at that time, but the applicable requirements for enforceability or perfection under this act are not satisfied when this act takes effec|="Number">(1)  
Except as otherwise provided in this part, this act applies to a transaction or lien within its scope, even if the transaction or lien was entered into or created before this act takes effect.

(2)  
Except as otherwise provided in subsection (3) and ss. 679.703-679.709:
(a)  
Transactions and liens that were not governed by chapter 679, Florida Statutes 2000, were validly entered into or created before this act takes effect, and would be subject to this act if they had been entered into or created after this act takes effect, and the rights, duties, and interests flowing from those transactions and liens remain valid after this act takes effect; and

(b)  
The transactions and liens may be terminated, completed, consummated, and enforced as required or permitted by this act or by the law that otherwise would apply if this act had not taken effect.

(3)  
This act does not affect an action, case, or proceeding commenced before this act takes effect.

**History:** s. 8, ch. 2001-198.
