# Fla. Stat. § 687.142 — Responsibility of principals.

**Title:** XXXIX — COMMERCIAL RELATIONS
**Chapter:** 687 — INTEREST AND USURY; LENDING PRACTICES
**Part:** VI — TRANSITIONAL PROVISIONS
**Section:** 687.142
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Each principal of a loan broker may be sanctioned for the actions of the loan broker, including its agents or employees, in the course of business of the loan broker.

**History:** s. 3, ch. 91-87.
