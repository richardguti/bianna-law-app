# Fla. Stat. § 683.145 — I Am An American Day.

**Title:** XXXIX — COMMERCIAL RELATIONS
**Chapter:** 683 — LEGAL HOLIDAYS; SPECIAL OBSERVANCES
**Part:** VI — TRANSITIONAL PROVISIONS
**Section:** 683.145
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The third Sunday in October of each year is hereby designated as “I Am An American Day.”

**History:** s. 1, ch. 87-410.
