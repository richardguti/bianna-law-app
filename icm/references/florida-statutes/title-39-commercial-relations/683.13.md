# Fla. Stat. § 683.13 — State observance of national day of mourning.

**Title:** XXXIX — COMMERCIAL RELATIONS
**Chapter:** 683 — LEGAL HOLIDAYS; SPECIAL OBSERVANCES
**Part:** VI — TRANSITIONAL PROVISIONS
**Section:** 683.13
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Observance by the state of any day of mourning, as proclaimed by the Governor in response to the designation of a national day of mourning by the President of the United States, may be by declaring that the following Sunday shall be observed as the day of mourning.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 74-16.
