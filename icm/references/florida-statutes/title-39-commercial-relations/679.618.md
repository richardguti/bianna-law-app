# Fla. Stat. § 679.618 — Rights and duties of certain secondary obligors.

**Title:** XXXIX — COMMERCIAL RELATIONS
**Chapter:** 679 — UNIFORM COMMERCIAL CODE: SECURED TRANSACTIONS
**Part:** VI — DEFAULT
**Section:** 679.618
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
A secondary obligor acquires the rights and becomes obligated to perform the duties of the secured party after the secondary obligor:
(a)  
Receives an assignment of a secured obligation from the secured party;

(b)  
Receives a transfer of collateral from the secured party and agrees to accept the rights and assume the duties of the secured party; or

(c)  
Is subrogated to the rights of a secured party with respect to collateral.

(2)  
An assignment, transfer, or subrogation described in subsection (1):
(a)  
Is not a disposition of collateral under s. 679.610; and

(b)  
Relieves the secured party of further duties under this chapter.

**History:** s. 7, ch. 2001-198.
