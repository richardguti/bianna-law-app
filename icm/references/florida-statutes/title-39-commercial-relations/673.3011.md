# Fla. Stat. § 673.3011 — Person entitled to enforce instrument.

**Title:** XXXIX — COMMERCIAL RELATIONS
**Chapter:** 673 — UNIFORM COMMERCIAL CODE: NEGOTIABLE INSTRUMENTS
**Part:** III — ENFORCEMENT OF INSTRUMENTS
**Section:** 673.3011
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The term “person entitled to enforce” an instrument means:
(1)  
The holder of the instrument;

(2)  
A nonholder in possession of the instrument who has the rights of a holder; or

(3)  
A person not in possession of the instrument who is entitled to enforce the instrument pursuant to s. 673.3091 or s. 673.4181(4).

A person may be a person entitled to enforce the instrument even though the person is not the owner of the instrument or is in wrongful possession of the instrument.

**History:** s. 2, ch. 92-82.
