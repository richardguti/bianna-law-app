# Fla. Stat. § 674.206 — Transfer between banks.

**Title:** XXXIX — COMMERCIAL RELATIONS
**Chapter:** 674 — UNIFORM COMMERCIAL CODE: BANK DEPOSITS AND COLLECTIONS
**Part:** II — COLLECTION OF ITEMS: DEPOSITARY AND COLLECTING BANKS
**Section:** 674.206
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Any agreed method that identifies the transferor bank is sufficient for the item's further transfer to another bank.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 65-254; s. 24, ch. 92-82.

**Note:** s. 4-206, U.C.C.
