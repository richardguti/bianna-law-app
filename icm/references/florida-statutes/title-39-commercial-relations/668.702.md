# Fla. Stat. § 668.702 — Definitions.

**Title:** XXXIX — COMMERCIAL RELATIONS
**Chapter:** 668 — ELECTRONIC COMMERCE
**Part:** IV — FRAUDULENT USE OR POSSESSION OF IDENTIFYING INFORMATION
**Section:** 668.702
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

As used in this part, the term:
(1)  
“Department” means the Department of Legal Affairs.

(2)  
“Electronic mail address” has the same meaning as provided in s. 668.602.

(3)  
“Electronic mail message” has the same meaning as provided in s. 668.602.

(4)  
“Identifying information” has the same meaning as the term “personal identification information” as defined in s. 817.568(1).

(5)  
“Internet domain name” has the same meaning as provided in s. 668.602.

(6)  
“Web page” means a location that has a single uniform resource locator (URL) with respect to the World Wide Web or another location that can be accessed on the Internet.

**History:** s. 5, ch. 2006-232.
