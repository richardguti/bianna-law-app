# Fla. Stat. § 674.201 — Status of collecting bank as agent and provisional status of credits; applicability of chapter; item indorsed “pay any bank.”

**Title:** XXXIX — COMMERCIAL RELATIONS
**Chapter:** 674 — UNIFORM COMMERCIAL CODE: BANK DEPOSITS AND COLLECTIONS
**Part:** II — COLLECTION OF ITEMS: DEPOSITARY AND COLLECTING BANKS
**Section:** 674.201
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

——

(1)  
Unless a contrary intent clearly appears and before the time that a settlement given by a collecting bank for an item is or becomes final, the bank, with respect to the item, is an agent or subagent of the owner of the item and any settlement given for the item is provisional.
