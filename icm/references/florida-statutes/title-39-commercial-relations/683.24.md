# Fla. Stat. § 683.24 — Florida Alzheimer's Disease Day.

**Title:** XXXIX — COMMERCIAL RELATIONS
**Chapter:** 683 — LEGAL HOLIDAYS; SPECIAL OBSERVANCES
**Part:** VI — TRANSITIONAL PROVISIONS
**Section:** 683.24
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

February 6th of each year is designated Florida Alzheimer's Disease Day.

**History:** s. 25, ch. 2000-163; s. 66, ch. 2000-318; s. 28, ch. 2000-367.
