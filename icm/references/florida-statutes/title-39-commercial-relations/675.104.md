# Fla. Stat. § 675.104 — Formal requirements.

**Title:** XXXIX — COMMERCIAL RELATIONS
**Chapter:** 675 — UNIFORM COMMERCIAL CODE: LETTERS OF CREDIT
**Part:** V — COLLECTION OF DOCUMENTARY DRAFTS
**Section:** 675.104
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

A letter of credit, confirmation, advice, transfer, amendment, or cancellation may be issued in any form that is a signed record.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 65-254; s. 1, ch. 99-137; s. 55, ch. 2025-92.

**Note:** s. 5-104, U.C.C.
