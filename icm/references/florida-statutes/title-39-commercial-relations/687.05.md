# Fla. Stat. § 687.05 — Provisions for payment of attorney's fees.

**Title:** XXXIX — COMMERCIAL RELATIONS
**Chapter:** 687 — INTEREST AND USURY; LENDING PRACTICES
**Part:** VI — TRANSITIONAL PROVISIONS
**Section:** 687.05
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

No provision for the payment of attorney's fees, or charge for exchange or similar charge shall render such instrument subject to the terms of any statute of this state, limiting the amount of interest which shall be charged on such instrument.

**History:** s. 2, ch. 4374, 1895; GS 3107; RGS 4853; CGL 6940.
