# Fla. Stat. § 680.32 — Priority subject to subordination.

**Title:** XXXIX — COMMERCIAL RELATIONS
**Chapter:** 680 — UNIFORM COMMERCIAL CODE: LEASES
**Part:** III — EFFECT OF LEASE CONTRACT
**Section:** 680.32
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Nothing in this chapter prevents subordination by agreement by any person entitled to priority.

**History:** s. 32, ch. 98-11.
