# Fla. Stat. § 683.3342 — Revive Awareness Day.

**Title:** XXXIX — COMMERCIAL RELATIONS
**Chapter:** 683 — LEGAL HOLIDAYS; SPECIAL OBSERVANCES
**Part:** VI — TRANSITIONAL PROVISIONS
**Section:** 683.3342
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
June 6 of each year is designated as “Revive Awareness Day.”

(2)  
The Governor may issue an annual proclamation designating June 6 as “Revive Awareness Day.”

(3)  
The Department of Health is encouraged to hold events to raise awareness of the dangers of opioid overdose and the availability and safe use of opioid antagonists as an effective way to rapidly reverse the effects of opioid overdose.

**History:** s. 2, ch. 2024-67.
