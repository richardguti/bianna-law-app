# Fla. Stat. § 677.601 — Lost, stolen, or destroyed documents of title.

**Title:** XXXIX — COMMERCIAL RELATIONS
**Chapter:** 677 — UNIFORM COMMERCIAL CODE: DOCUMENTS OF TITLE
**Part:** V — WAREHOUSE RECEIPTS AND BILLS OF LADING: NEGOTIATION AND TRANSFER
**Section:** 677.601
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
If a document of title is lost, stolen, or destroyed, a court may order delivery of the goods or issuance of a substitute document and the bailee may without liability to any person comply with the order.
