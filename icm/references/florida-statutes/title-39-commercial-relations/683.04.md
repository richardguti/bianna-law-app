# Fla. Stat. § 683.04 — Arbor Day.

**Title:** XXXIX — COMMERCIAL RELATIONS
**Chapter:** 683 — LEGAL HOLIDAYS; SPECIAL OBSERVANCES
**Part:** VI — TRANSITIONAL PROVISIONS
**Section:** 683.04
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The third Friday in January of each year is hereby designated as “Arbor Day” in the state.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 22538, 1945.
