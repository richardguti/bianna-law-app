# Fla. Stat. § 669.703 — Security interest unperfected before effective date.

**Title:** XXXIX — COMMERCIAL RELATIONS
**Chapter:** 669 — UNIFORM COMMERCIAL CODE: CONTROLLABLE ELECTRONIC RECORDS AND TRANSITIONAL PROVISIONS
**Part:** II — TRANSITIONAL PROVISIONS
**Section:** 669.703
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

A security interest that is enforceable immediately before July 1, 2025, but is unperfected by that date:
(1)  
Remains an enforceable security interest until the adjustment date;

(2)  
Remains enforceable thereafter if the security interest becomes enforceable under s. 679.2031, as it existed on July 1, 2025, or before the adjustment date; and

(3)  
Becomes perfected:
(a)  
Without further action, by July 1, 2025, if the requirements for perfection under this act are satisfied on or before that date; or

(b)  
When the requirements for perfection are satisfied, if satisfaction occurs after July 1, 2025.

**History:** s. 16, ch. 2025-92.
