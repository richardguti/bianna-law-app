# Fla. Stat. § 672.308 — Absence of specified place for delivery.

**Title:** XXXIX — COMMERCIAL RELATIONS
**Chapter:** 672 — UNIFORM COMMERCIAL CODE: SALES
**Part:** III — GENERAL OBLIGATION AND CONSTRUCTION OF CONTRACT
**Section:** 672.308
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Unless otherwise agreed:
(1)  
The place for delivery of goods is the seller's place of business or if the seller has none his or her residence; but

(2)  
In a contract for sale of identified goods which to the knowledge of the parties at the time of contracting are F.S. 672.3076672.307  
Delivery in single lot or several lots.
——

Unless otherwise agreed all goods called for by a contract for sale must be tendered in a single delivery and payment is due only on such tender but where the circumstances give either party the right to make or demand delivery in lots the price if it can be apportioned may be demanded for each lot.
