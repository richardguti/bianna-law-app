# Fla. Stat. § 668.601 — Legislative intent.

**Title:** XXXIX — COMMERCIAL RELATIONS
**Chapter:** 668 — ELECTRONIC COMMERCE
**Part:** III — ELECTRONIC MAIL COMMUNICATIONS
**Section:** 668.601
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

This part is intended to promote the integrity of electronic commerce and shall be construed liberally in order to protect the public and legitimate businesses from deceptive and unsolicited commercial electronic mail.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 2004-233.
