# Fla. Stat. § 679.40111 — Alienability of debtor's rights.

**Title:** XXXIX — COMMERCIAL RELATIONS
**Chapter:** 679 — UNIFORM COMMERCIAL CODE: SECURED TRANSACTIONS
**Part:** IV — RIGHTS OF THIRD PARTIES
**Section:** 679.40111
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
Except as otherwise provided in subsection (2) and ss. 679.4061, 679.4071, 679.4081, and 679.409, whether a debtor's rights in collateral may be voluntarily or involuntarily transferred is governed by law other than this chapter.

(2)  
An agreement between the debtor and secured party which prohibits a transfer of the debtor's rights in collateral or makes the transfer a default does not prevent the transfer from taking effect.

**History:** s. 4, ch. 2001-198.
