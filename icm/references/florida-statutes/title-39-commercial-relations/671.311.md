# Fla. Stat. § 671.311 — Presumption that rule of law continues unchanged.

**Title:** XXXIX — COMMERCIAL RELATIONS
**Chapter:** 671 — UNIFORM COMMERCIAL CODE: GENERAL PROVISIONS
**Part:** III — EFFECTIVE DATE AND REPEALER
**Section:** 671.311
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Unless a change in law has clearly been made, the provisions of chapter 79-398, Laws of Florida, shall be deemed declaratory of the meaning of chapter 65-254, Laws of Florida, as amended.

**History:** s. 39, ch. 79-398; s. 6, ch. 90-278.

**Note:** Former s. 680.11.
