# Fla. Stat. § 687.148 — Duties and powers of the commission and office.

**Title:** XXXIX — COMMERCIAL RELATIONS
**Chapter:** 687 — INTEREST AND USURY; LENDING PRACTICES
**Part:** VI — TRANSITIONAL PROVISIONS
**Section:** 687.148
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
The office is responsible for the administration and enforcement of this act.

(2)  
The commission may adopt such rules as it may deem necessary in the administration of this act and not inconsistent therewith.

**History:** s. 8, ch. 91-87.
