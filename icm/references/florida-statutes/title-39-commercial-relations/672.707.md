# Fla. Stat. § 672.707 — “Person in the position of a seller.”

**Title:** XXXIX — COMMERCIAL RELATIONS
**Chapter:** 672 — UNIFORM COMMERCIAL CODE: SALES
**Part:** VII — REMEDIES
**Section:** 672.707
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
A “person in the position of a seller” includes as against a principal an agent who has paid or become responsible for the price of goods on behalf of his or her principal or anyone who otherwise holds a security interest or other right in goods similar to that of a seller.

(2)  
A person in the position of a seller may as provided in this chapter withhold or stop delivery (s. 672.705) and resell (s. 672.706) and recover incidental damages (s. 672.710).

**History:** s. 1, ch. 65-254; s. 605, ch. 97-102.

**Note:** The seller may buy.
