# Fla. Stat. § 679.333 — Priority of certain liens arising by operation of law.

**Title:** XXXIX — COMMERCIAL RELATIONS
**Chapter:** 679 — UNIFORM COMMERCIAL CODE: SECURED TRANSACTIONS
**Part:** III — PERFECTION AND PRIORITY
**Section:** 679.333
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
In this section, the term “possessory lien” means an interest, other than a security interest or an agricultural lien:
(a)  
Which secures payment or performance of an obligation for services or materials furnished with respect to goods by a person in the ordinary course of the person's business;

(b)  
Which is created by statute or rule of law in favor of the person; and

(c)  
The effectiveness of which depends on the person's possession of the goods.

(2)  
A possessory lien on goods has priority over a security interest in the goods unless the lien is created by a statute that expressly provides otherwise.
