# Fla. Stat. § 683.06 — Pascua Florida Day.

**Title:** XXXIX — COMMERCIAL RELATIONS
**Chapter:** 683 — LEGAL HOLIDAYS; SPECIAL OBSERVANCES
**Part:** VI — TRANSITIONAL PROVISIONS
**Section:** 683.06
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
April 2 of each year is hereby designated as “Florida State Day.” The day is to be known as “Pascua Florida Day.”

(2)  
The Governor may annually issue a proclamation designating April 2 as said State Day and designating the week of March 27 to April 2 as “Pascua Florida Week” and calling upon public schools and citizens of Florida to observe the same as a patriotic occasion.

**History:** ss. 1, 2, ch. 28063, 1953; s. 2, ch. 77-423; s. 77, ch. 2025-6.
