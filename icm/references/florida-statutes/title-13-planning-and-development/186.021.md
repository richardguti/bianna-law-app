# Fla. Stat. § 186.021 — Long-range program plans.

**Title:** XIII — PLANNING AND DEVELOPMENT
**Chapter:** 186 — STATE AND REGIONAL PLANNING
**Part:** III — RESTORATION OF CORNERS
**Section:** 186.021
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

——

Pursuant to s. 216.013, each state agency shall develop a long-range program plan on an annual basis. The plan shall provide the framework and context for designing and interpreting the agency budget request.
