# Fla. Stat. § 189.013 — Special districts; creation, dissolution, and reporting requirements.

**Title:** XIII — PLANNING AND DEVELOPMENT
**Chapter:** 189 — UNIFORM SPECIAL DISTRICT ACCOUNTABILITY ACT
**Part:** I — GENERAL PROVISIONS
**Section:** 189.013
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

All special districts, regardless of the existence of other, more specific provisions of applicable law, shall comply with the creation, dissolution, and reporting requirements set forth in this chapter.

**History:** s. 4, ch. 89-169; s. 5, ch. 97-255; s. 11, ch. 2014-22.

**Note:** Former s. 189.4031(1).
