# Fla. Stat. § 190.0485 — Notice of establishment.

**Title:** XIII — PLANNING AND DEVELOPMENT
**Chapter:** 190 — COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT DISTRICTS
**Part:** VIII — COMPREHENSIVE PLANNING
**Section:** 190.0485
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Within 30 days after the effective date of a rule or ordinance establishing a community development district under this act, the district shall cause to be recorded in the property records in the county in which it is located a “Notice of Establishment of the

Community Development District.” The notice shall, at a minimum, include the legal description of the district and a copy of the disclosure statement specified in s. 190.048.

**History:** s. 15, ch. 84-360; s. 3, ch. 90-46; s. 44, ch. 99-378.
