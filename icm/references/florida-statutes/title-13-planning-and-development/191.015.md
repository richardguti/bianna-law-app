# Fla. Stat. § 191.015 — Codification.

**Title:** XIII — PLANNING AND DEVELOPMENT
**Chapter:** 191 — INDEPENDENT SPECIAL FIRE CONTROL DISTRICTS
**Part:** VIII — COMPREHENSIVE PLANNING
**Section:** 191.015
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Each fire control district existing on the effective date of this section, by December 1, 2004, shall submit to the Legislature a draft codified charter, at its expense, so that its special acts may be codified into a single act for reenactment by the Legislature, if there is more than one special act for the district. The Legislature may adopt a schedule for individual district codification. Any codified act relating to a district, which act is submitted to the Legislature for reenactment, shall provide for the repeal of all prior special acts of the Legislature relating to the district. The codified act shall be filed with the Department of Commerce pursuant to s. F.S. 191.0146191.014  
District creation and expansion.
——

(1)  
New districts may be created only by the Legislature under s. 189.031.

(2)  
The boundaries of a district may be modified, extended, or enlarged upon approval or ratification by the Legislature.
