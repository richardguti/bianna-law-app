# Fla. Stat. § 189.073 — Legislative merger of independent special districts.

**Title:** XIII — PLANNING AND DEVELOPMENT
**Chapter:** 189 — UNIFORM SPECIAL DISTRICT ACCOUNTABILITY ACT
**Part:** VII — MERGER AND DISSOLUTION
**Section:** 189.073
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The Legislature, by special act, may merge independent special districts created and operating pursuant to special act.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 2012-16; s. 20, ch. 2014-22.

**Note:** Former s. 189.4042(4).
