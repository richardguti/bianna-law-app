# Fla. Stat. § 189.017 — Rulemaking authority.

**Title:** XIII — PLANNING AND DEVELOPMENT
**Chapter:** 189 — UNIFORM SPECIAL DISTRICT ACCOUNTABILITY ACT
**Part:** I — GENERAL PROVISIONS
**Section:** 189.017
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The department may adopt rules to implement the provisions of this chapter.

**History:** s. 59, ch. 89-169; s. 22, ch. 97-255; s. 67, ch. 2011-142; s. 46, ch. 2014-22.

**Note:** Former s. 189.425.
