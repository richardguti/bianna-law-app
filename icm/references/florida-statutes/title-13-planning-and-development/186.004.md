# Fla. Stat. § 186.004 — Governor; chief planning officer of the state.

**Title:** XIII — PLANNING AND DEVELOPMENT
**Chapter:** 186 — STATE AND REGIONAL PLANNING
**Part:** III — RESTORATION OF CORNERS
**Section:** 186.004
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The Governor is the chief planning officer of the state and shall conduct a biennial review and revision of the state comprehensive plan.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 67-157; s. 7, ch. 71-377; s. 4, ch. 72-295; s. 21, ch. 93-206.

**Note:** Former s. 23.011.
