# Fla. Stat. § 190.022 — Special assessments.

**Title:** XIII — PLANNING AND DEVELOPMENT
**Chapter:** 190 — COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT DISTRICTS
**Part:** VIII — COMPREHENSIVE PLANNING
**Section:** 190.022
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
The board may levy special assessments for the construction, reconstruction, acquisition, or maintenance of district facilities authorized under this chapter using the procedures for levy and collection provided in chapter 170 or chapter 197.

(2)  
Notwithstanding the provisions of s. 170.09, district assessments may be made payable in no more than 30 yearly installments.

**History:** s. 2, ch. 80-407; s. 12, ch. 84-360; s. 8, ch. 91-308; s. 41, ch. 99-378.
