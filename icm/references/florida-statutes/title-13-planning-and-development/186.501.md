# Fla. Stat. § 186.501 — Short title.

**Title:** XIII — PLANNING AND DEVELOPMENT
**Chapter:** 186 — STATE AND REGIONAL PLANNING
**Part:** III — RESTORATION OF CORNERS
**Section:** 186.501
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)
