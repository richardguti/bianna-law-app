# Fla. Stat. § 190.014 — Issuance of bond anticipation notes.

**Title:** XIII — PLANNING AND DEVELOPMENT
**Chapter:** 190 — COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT DISTRICTS
**Part:** VIII — COMPREHENSIVE PLANNING
**Section:** 190.014
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

——

In addition to the other powers provided for in this act, and not in limitation thereof, the district shall have the power, at any time, and from time to time after the issuance of any bonds of the district shall have been authorized, to borrow money for the purposes for which such bonds are to be issued in anticipation of the receipt of the proceeds of the sale of such bonds and to issue bond anticipation notes in a principal sum not in excess of the authorized maximum amount of such bond issue. Such notes shall be in such denomination or denominations, bear interest at such rate as the board may determine in compliance with s. 215.84, mature at such time or timeZithin 20 days after the final adoption of the plan by the board, the board shall proceed pursuant to s. 298.301.
