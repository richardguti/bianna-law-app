# Fla. Stat. § 189.0761 — Exemptions.

**Title:** XIII — PLANNING AND DEVELOPMENT
**Chapter:** 189 — UNIFORM SPECIAL DISTRICT ACCOUNTABILITY ACT
**Part:** VIII — COMPREHENSIVE PLANNING
**Section:** 189.0761
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

This part does not apply to community development districts implemented pursuant to chapter 190 or to water management districts created and operated pursuant to chapter 373.

**History:** s. 8, ch. 89-169; s. 8, ch. 97-255; s. 1, ch. 2012-16; s. 23, ch. 2014-22.

**Note:** Former s. 189.4042(7).
