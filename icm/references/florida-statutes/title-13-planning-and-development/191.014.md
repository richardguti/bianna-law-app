# Fla. Stat. § 191.014 — District creation and expansion.

**Title:** XIII — PLANNING AND DEVELOPMENT
**Chapter:** 191 — INDEPENDENT SPECIAL FIRE CONTROL DISTRICTS
**Part:** VIII — COMPREHENSIVE PLANNING
**Section:** 191.014
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
New districts may be created only by the Legislature under s. 189.031.

(2)  
The boundaries of a district may be modified, extended, or enlarged upon approval or ratification by the Legislature.

**History:** s. 13, ch. 97-256; s. 2, ch. 2012-16; s. 75, ch. 2014-22.
