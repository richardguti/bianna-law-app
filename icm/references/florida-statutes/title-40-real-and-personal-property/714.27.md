# Fla. Stat. § 714.27 — Relation to Electronic Signatures in Global and National Commerce Act.

**Title:** XL — REAL AND PERSONAL PROPERTY
**Chapter:** 714 — UNIFORM COMMERCIAL REAL ESTATE RECEIVERSHIP ACT
**Part:** IV — FLORIDA UNIFORM FEDERAL LIEN REGISTRATION ACT
**Section:** 714.27
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

This act modifies, limits, or supersedes the Electronic Signatures in Global and National Commerce Act, 15 U.S.C. ss. 7001 et seq., but does not modify, limit, or supersede s. 101(c) of that act, 15 U.S.C. s. 7001(c), or authorize electronic delivery of any of the notices described in s. 103(b) of that act, 15 U.S.C. s. 7003(b).

**History:** s. 1, ch. 2020-106.
