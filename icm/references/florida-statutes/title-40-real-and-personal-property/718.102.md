# Fla. Stat. § 718.102 — Purposes.

**Title:** XL — REAL AND PERSONAL PROPERTY
**Chapter:** 718 — CONDOMINIUMS
**Part:** I — GENERAL PROVISIONS
**Section:** 718.102
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The purpose of this chapter is:
(1)  
To give statutory recognition to the condominium form of ownership of real property.

(2)  
To establish procedures for the F.S. 718.1016718.101  
Short title.
——

This chapter shall be known and may be cited as the “Condominium Act.”

**History:** s. 1, ch. 76-222.
