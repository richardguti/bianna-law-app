# Fla. Stat. § 718.1045 — Timeshare estates; limitation on creation.

**Title:** XL — REAL AND PERSONAL PROPERTY
**Chapter:** 718 — CONDOMINIUMS
**Part:** I — GENERAL PROVISIONS
**Section:** 718.1045
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

No timeshare estates shall be created with respect to any condominium unit except pursuant to provisions in the declaration expressly permitting the creation of such estates.

**History:** s. 3, ch. 78-328.
