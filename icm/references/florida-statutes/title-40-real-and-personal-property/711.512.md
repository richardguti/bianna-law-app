# Fla. Stat. § 711.512 — Application of ss. 711.50-711.512.

**Title:** XL — REAL AND PERSONAL PROPERTY
**Chapter:** 712 — MARKETABLE RECORD TITLES TO REAL PROPERTY
**Part:** II — POWERS OF ATTORNEY
**Section:** 711.512
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Sections 711.50-711.512 apply to registrations of securities in beneficiary form made before, on, or after January 1, 1995, by decedents dying on or after January 1, 1995.

**History:** s. 3, ch. 94-216.
