# Fla. Stat. § 695.04 — Requirements of certificate.

**Title:** XL — REAL AND PERSONAL PROPERTY
**Chapter:** 695 — RECORD OF CONVEYANCES OF REAL ESTATE
**Part:** III — CONVEYANCES TO FOREIGN ENTITIES
**Section:** 695.04
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The certificate of the officer before whom the acknowledgment or proof is taken, except for a certificate legalizing or authenticating the signature of a person executing an instrument concerning real property pursuant to s. 695.03(3), shall contain and set forth substantially the matter required to be done or proved to make such acknowledgment or proof effectual as set forth in s. 117.05.

**History:** RS 1974; GS 2482; RGS 3824; CGL 5700; s. 2, ch. 84-97; s. 24, ch. 2019-71.
