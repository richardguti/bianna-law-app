# Fla. Stat. § 723.1255 — Alternative resolution of recall, election, and inspection and photocopying of official records disputes.

**Title:** XL — REAL AND PERSONAL PROPERTY
**Chapter:** 723 — MOBILE HOME PARK LOT TENANCIES
**Part:** IV — COMMISSIONER OF DEEDS
**Section:** 723.1255
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
A dispute between a mobile home owner and a homeowners' association regarding the election and recall of officers or directors under s. F.S. 723.08616723.0861  
Attorney's fees and costs.
——

The prevailing party in any action brought to enforce the provisions of [1]this section shall be entitled to reasonable attorney's fees and costs.

**History:** s. 19, ch. 92-148.

**Note:** [1]Note.
—
As enacted; the reference to “this section” is probably intended to refer to ss. 723.085, 723.086, and 723.0861. Section 12 of H.B. 2179 and s. 11 of C.S. for H.B. 2179 included in one section the provisions compiled as ss. 723.085, 723.086, and 723.0861. C.S. for H.B. 2179 was amended on the floor. See
Journal of the House of Representatives 1992, p. 1806. These provisions were separated in that amendment into ss. 17, 18, and 19 (see
pp. 1809-1810), and the amendment was adopted (see
p. 1812).
