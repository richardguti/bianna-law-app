# Fla. Stat. § 717.1261 — Death certificates.

**Title:** XL — REAL AND PERSONAL PROPERTY
**Chapter:** 717 — FLORIDA DISPOSITION OF ABANDONED PERSONAL PROPERTY ACT
**Part:** IV — FLORIDA UNIFORM FEDERAL LIEN REGISTRATION ACT
**Section:** 717.1261
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Any person who claims entitlement to abandoned property by means of the death of one or more persons shall file a copy of the death certificate of the decedent or decedents that has been certified as being authentic by the issuing governmental agency.
