# Fla. Stat. § 713.821 — Claim of lien.

**Title:** XL — REAL AND PERSONAL PROPERTY
**Chapter:** 713 — LIENS, GENERALLY
**Part:** III — OIL AND GAS LIENS
**Section:** 713.821
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The manner of perfecting a lien under this part shall be the same as that provided in s. 713.08.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 75-51.
