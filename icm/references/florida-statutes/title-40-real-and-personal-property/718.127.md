# Fla. Stat. § 718.127 — Receivership notification.

**Title:** XL — REAL AND PERSONAL PROPERTY
**Chapter:** 718 — CONDOMINIUMS
**Part:** I — GENERAL PROVISIONS
**Section:** 718.127
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

——

Upon the appointment of a receiver by a court for any reason relating to a condominium association, the court shall direct the receiver to provide to all unit owners written notice of his or her appointment as receiver. Such notice shall be mailed or delivered within 10 days after the appointment.
