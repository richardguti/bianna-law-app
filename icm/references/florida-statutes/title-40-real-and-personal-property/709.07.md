# Fla. Stat. § 709.07 — Power of appointment; effect of release on title to property.

**Title:** XL — REAL AND PERSONAL PROPERTY
**Chapter:** 709 — POWERS OF ATTORNEY AND SIMILAR INSTRUMENTS
**Part:** I — POWERS OF APPOINTMENT
**Section:** 709.07
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

No such release, in whole or in part, of a power of appointment shall affect the title to property of any bona fide purchaser for value who does not have notice or knowledge of such release.

**History:** s. 7, ch. 23007, 1945.
