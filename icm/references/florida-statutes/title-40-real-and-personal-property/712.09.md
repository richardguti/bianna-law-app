# Fla. Stat. § 712.09 — Extension of 30-year period.

**Title:** XL — REAL AND PERSONAL PROPERTY
**Chapter:** 712 — MARKETABLE RECORD TITLES TO REAL PROPERTY
**Part:** II — POWERS OF ATTORNEY
**Section:** 712.09
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

If the 30-year period for filing notice under s. 712.05 shall have expired prior to July 1, 1965, such period shall be extended to July 1, 1965.

**History:** s. 8, ch. 63-133; s. 799, ch. 97-102.
