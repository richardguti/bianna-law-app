# Fla. Stat. § 721.22 — Partition.

**Title:** XL — REAL AND PERSONAL PROPERTY
**Chapter:** 721 — VACATION AND TIMESHARE PLANS
**Part:** I — VACATION PLANS AND TIMESHARING
**Section:** 721.22
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
No action for partition of any timeshare unit shall lie, unless otherwise provided for in the contract between the seller and the purchaser.

(2)  
If a timeshare estate exists as an estate for years with a future interest, the estate for years shall not be deemed to have merged with the future interest, but neither the estate for years nor the corresponding future interest shall be conveyed or encumbered separately from the other.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 81-172; s. 21, ch. 83-264.
