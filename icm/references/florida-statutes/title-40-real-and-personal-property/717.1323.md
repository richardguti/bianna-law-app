# Fla. Stat. § 717.1323 — Prohibited practice.

**Title:** XL — REAL AND PERSONAL PROPERTY
**Chapter:** 717 — FLORIDA DISPOSITION OF ABANDONED PERSONAL PROPERTY ACT
**Part:** IV — FLORIDA UNIFORM FEDERAL LIEN REGISTRATION ACT
**Section:** 717.1323
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

A person may not knowingly enter false information onto the Internet website of the Division of Unclaimed Property.

**History:** s. 19, ch. 2005-163; s. 41, ch. 2016-165.
