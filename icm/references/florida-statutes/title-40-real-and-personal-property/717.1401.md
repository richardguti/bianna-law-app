# Fla. Stat. § 717.1401 — Repeal.

**Title:** XL — REAL AND PERSONAL PROPERTY
**Chapter:** 717 — FLORIDA DISPOSITION OF ABANDONED PERSONAL PROPERTY ACT
**Part:** IV — FLORIDA UNIFORM FEDERAL LIEN REGISTRATION ACT
**Section:** 717.1401
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

This chapter shall not repeal, but shall be additional and supplemental to the existing provisions of ss. 43.18 and 402.17 and chapter 716.

**History:** s. 41, ch. 87-105; s. 62, ch. 92-348; s. 11, ch. 2018-71.
