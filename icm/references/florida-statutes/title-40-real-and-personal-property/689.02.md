# Fla. Stat. § 689.02 — Form of warranty deed prescribed.

**Title:** XL — REAL AND PERSONAL PROPERTY
**Chapter:** 689 — CONVEYANCES OF LAND AND DECLARATIONS OF TRUST
**Part:** VI — TRANSITIONAL PROVISIONS
**Section:** 689.02
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

And the said party of the first part does hereby fully warrant the title to said land, and will defend the same against the lawful claims of all persons whomsoever.”

(2)  
The form for warranty deeds of conveyance to land shall include a blank space for the property appraiser's parcel identification number describing the property conveyed, which number, if available, shall be entered on the deed before it is presented for recording. The failure to include such blank space or the parcel identification number, or the inclusion of an incorrect parcel identification number, does not affect the validity of the conveyance or the recordability of the deed. Such parcel identification number is not a part of the legal description of the property otherwise set forth in the deed and may not be used as a substitute for the legal description of the property being conveyed.
