# Fla. Stat. § 721.98 — Powers of the division.

**Title:** XL — REAL AND PERSONAL PROPERTY
**Chapter:** 721 — VACATION AND TIMESHARE PLANS
**Part:** IV — COMMISSIONER OF DEEDS
**Section:** 721.98
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The division has no duty or authority to regulate, enforce, or ensure compliance with any provision of this part.

**History:** s. 14, ch. 98-36.
