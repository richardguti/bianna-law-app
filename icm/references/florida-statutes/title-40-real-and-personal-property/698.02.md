# Fla. Stat. § 698.02 — Acknowledgment.

**Title:** XL — REAL AND PERSONAL PROPERTY
**Chapter:** 698 — CHATTEL MORTGAGES
**Part:** III — CONVEYANCES TO FOREIGN ENTITIES
**Section:** 698.02
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

To entitle such mortgage to record, its execution must be acknowledged or proved in the manner provided for mortgages of real property.

**History:** RS 1984; GS 2497; RGS 3839; CGL 5727.
