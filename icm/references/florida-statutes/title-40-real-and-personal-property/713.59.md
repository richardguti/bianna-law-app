# Fla. Stat. § 713.59 — Liens for labor in raising crops.

**Title:** XL — REAL AND PERSONAL PROPERTY
**Chapter:** 713 — LIENS, GENERALLY
**Part:** II — MISCELLANEOUS LIENS
**Section:** 713.59
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

In favor of any person performing any labor in, or managing or overseeing, the cultivation or harvesting of crops; upon the crops cultivated or harvested.

**History:** ch. 1899, 1872; s. 9, ch. 3747, 1887; RS 1733; GS 2199; RGS 3506; CGL 5367; s. 36, ch. 67-254.

**Note:** Former s. 85.10.
