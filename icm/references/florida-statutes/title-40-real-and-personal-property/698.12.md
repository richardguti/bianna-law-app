# Fla. Stat. § 698.12 — Chapter not applicable to transactions under Uniform Commercial Code.

**Title:** XL — REAL AND PERSONAL PROPERTY
**Chapter:** 701 — ASSIGNMENT AND CANCELLATION OF MORTGAGES
**Part:** III — CONVEYANCES TO FOREIGN ENTITIES
**Section:** 698.12
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The provisions of this chapter shall not apply to transactions governed by any of the provisions of the Uniform Commercial Code, but shall remain applicable to transactions to which that code does not apply.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 65-254.
