# Fla. Stat. § 717.136 — Foreign transactions.

**Title:** XL — REAL AND PERSONAL PROPERTY
**Chapter:** 717 — FLORIDA DISPOSITION OF ABANDONED PERSONAL PROPERTY ACT
**Part:** IV — FLORIDA UNIFORM FEDERAL LIEN REGISTRATION ACT
**Section:** 717.136
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

This chapter does not apply to any property held, due, and owing in a foreign country and arising out of foreign transaction.
