# Fla. Stat. § 695.07 — Use of scrawl as seal.

**Title:** XL — REAL AND PERSONAL PROPERTY
**Chapter:** 695 — RECORD OF CONVEYANCES OF REAL ESTATE
**Part:** III — CONVEYANCES TO FOREIGN ENTITIES
**Section:** 695.07
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

A scrawl or scroll, printed or written, affixed as a seal to any written instrument shall be as effectual as a seal.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 4148, 1893; GS 2484; RGS 3826; CGL 5704.
