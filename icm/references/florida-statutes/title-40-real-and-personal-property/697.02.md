# Fla. Stat. § 697.02 — Nature of a mortgage.

**Title:** XL — REAL AND PERSONAL PROPERTY
**Chapter:** 697 — INSTRUMENTS DEEMED MORTGAGES AND THE NATURE OF A MORTGAGE
**Part:** III — CONVEYANCES TO FOREIGN ENTITIES
**Section:** 697.02
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

A mortgage shall be held to be a specific lien on the property therein described, and not a conveyance of the legal title or of the right of possession.

**History:** ss. 1, 2, ch. 525, 1853; RS 1982; GS 2495; RGS 3837; CGL 5725.
