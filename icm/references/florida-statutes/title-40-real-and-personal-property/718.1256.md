# Fla. Stat. § 718.1256 — Condominiums as residential property.

**Title:** XL — REAL AND PERSONAL PROPERTY
**Chapter:** 718 — CONDOMINIUMS
**Part:** I — GENERAL PROVISIONS
**Section:** 718.1256
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

For the purpose of property and casualty insurance risk classification, condominiums shall be classed as residential property.

**History:** s. 23, ch.
