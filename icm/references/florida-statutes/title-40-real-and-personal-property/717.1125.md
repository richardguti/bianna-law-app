# Fla. Stat. § 717.1125 — Property held by fiduciaries under trust instruments.

**Title:** XL — REAL AND PERSONAL PROPERTY
**Chapter:** 717 — FLORIDA DISPOSITION OF ABANDONED PERSONAL PROPERTY ACT
**Part:** IV — FLORIDA UNIFORM FEDERAL LIEN REGISTRATION ACT
**Section:** 717.1125
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

All intangible property and any income or increment thereon held in a fiduciary capacity for the benefit of another person under a trust instrument is presumed abandoned unless the apparent owner has, within 2 years after it has become payable or distributable, increased or decreased the principal, accepted payment of principal or income, communicated concerning the property, or otherwise indicated an interest as evidenced by a memorandum or other record on file with the fiduciary. This section does not relieve a fiduciary of his or her duties under the Florida Trust Code.

**History:** s. 4, ch. 2013-172; s. 45, ch. 2024-140; s. 51, ch. 2026-174.
