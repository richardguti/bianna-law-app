# Fla. Stat. § 713.63 — Liens for furnishing locomotives, machinery, etc.

**Title:** XL — REAL AND PERSONAL PROPERTY
**Chapter:** 713 — LIENS, GENERALLY
**Part:** II — MISCELLANEOUS LIENS
**Section:** 713.63
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

In favor of any person who shall furnish any locomotive or stationary engine, water engine, windmill, car or other machine or parts of machine or instrument for any railroad, telegraph or telephone line, mill, distillery, or other manufactory; upon the articles so furnished.

**History:** s. 9, ch. 3747, 1887; RS 1737; GS 2203; RGS 3510; CGL 5371; s. 36, ch. 67-254.

**Note:** F.S. 713.626713.62  
Liens for furnishing articles to be manufactured.
—

In favor of any person who shall furnish any logs, lumber, clay, sand, stone or other material whatsoever, crude or partially or wholly prepared for use, to any mill or other manufactory to be manufactured into any article of value; upon all such articles furnished and upon all articles manufactured therefrom.

History.
—
s. 7, ch. 3747, 1887; RS 1736; GS 2202; RGS 3509; CGL 5370; s. 36, ch. 67-254.
