# Fla. Stat. § 719.131 — Law enforcement vehicles.

**Title:** XL — REAL AND PERSONAL PROPERTY
**Chapter:** 719 — COOPERATIVES
**Part:** I — GENERAL PROVISIONS
**Section:** 719.131
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

——

An association may not prohibit a law enforcement officer, as defined in s. 943.10(1), who is a unit owner, or who is a tenant, guest, or invitee of a unit owner, from parking his or her assigned law enforcement vehicle in an area where the unit oote of the unit owners may not be taken on any issue other than the issues specifically identified in the electronic vote, when a quorum is established based on unit owners voting electronically pursuant to this section.
