# Fla. Stat. § 714.11 — Collection and turnover of receivership property.

**Title:** XL — REAL AND PERSONAL PROPERTY
**Chapter:** 714 — UNIFORM COMMERCIAL REAL ESTATE RECEIVERSHIP ACT
**Part:** IV — FLORIDA UNIFORM FEDERAL LIEN REGISTRATION ACT
**Section:** 714.11
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
Unless the court orders otherwise, on demand by a receiver:
(a)  
A person that owes a debt that is receivership property and is matured or payable on demand or on order shall pay the debt to or on the order of the receiver, except to the extent the debt is subject to setoff or recoupment; and

(b)  
F.S. 714.106714.10  
Security agreement covering after-acquired property.
——

Except as otherwise provided by law other than this chapter, property that a receiver or an owner acquires after appointment of the receiver is subject to a security agreement entered into before the appointment to the same extent as if the court had not appointed the receiver.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 2020-106.
