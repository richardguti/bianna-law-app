# Fla. Stat. § 713.823 — Release of lien by filing bond.

**Title:** XL — REAL AND PERSONAL PROPERTY
**Chapter:** 713 — LIENS, GENERALLY
**Part:** III — OIL AND GAS LIENS
**Section:** 713.823
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Any lienee may release her or his property from any lien under this part in the manner provided by s. 713.76.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 75-51; s. 836, ch. 97-102.
