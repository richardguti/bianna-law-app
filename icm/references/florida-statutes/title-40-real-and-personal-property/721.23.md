# Fla. Stat. § 721.23 — Securities.

**Title:** XL — REAL AND PERSONAL PROPERTY
**Chapter:** 721 — VACATION AND TIMESHARE PLANS
**Part:** I — VACATION PLANS AND TIMESHARING
**Section:** 721.23
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Timeshare plans are not securities under the provisions of chapter 517 or its successor.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 81-172; s. 57, ch. 85-62.
