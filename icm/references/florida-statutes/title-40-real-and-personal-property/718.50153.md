**Title:** XL — REAL AND PERSONAL PROPERTY
**Chapter:** 718 — CONDOMINIUMS
**Part:** V — REGULATION AND DISCLOSURE PRIOR TO SALE OF RESIDENTIAL CONDOMINIUMS
**Section:** 718.50153

(1)  
The executive offices of the division shall be established and maintained in Tallahassee.

(2)  
The division may establish and maintain branch offices.
