# Fla. Stat. § 709.2303 — Remedies under other law.

**Title:** XL — REAL AND PERSONAL PROPERTY
**Chapter:** 709 — POWERS OF ATTORNEY AND SIMILAR INSTRUMENTS
**Part:** II — POWERS OF ATTORNEY
**Section:** 709.2303
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The remedies under this part are not exclusive and do not abrogate any right or remedy under any other law other than this part.

**History:** s. 29, ch. 2011-210.
