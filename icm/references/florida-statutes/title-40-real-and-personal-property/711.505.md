# Fla. Stat. § 711.505 — Form of registration in beneficiary form.

**Title:** XL — REAL AND PERSONAL PROPERTY
**Chapter:** 711 — FLORIDA UNIFORM TRANSFER-ON-DEATH SECURITY REGISTRATION ACT
**Part:** II — POWERS OF ATTORNEY
**Section:** 711.505
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Registration in beneficiary form may be shown by the words “transfer on death” or the abbreviation “TOD,” or by the words “pay on death” or the abbreviation “POD,” after the name of the registered owner and before the name of a beneficiary.F.S. 711.5046711.504  
Origination of registration in beneficiary form.
——

A security, whether evidenced by certificate or account, is registered in beneficiary form when the registration includes a designation of a beneficiary to take the ownership at the death of the owner or the deaths of all multiple owners.

**History:** s. 3, ch. 94-216.
