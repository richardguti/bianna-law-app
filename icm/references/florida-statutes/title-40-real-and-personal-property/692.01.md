# Fla. Stat. § 692.01 — Conveyances executed by corporations.

**Title:** XL — REAL AND PERSONAL PROPERTY
**Chapter:** 689 — CONVEYANCES OF LAND AND DECLARATIONS OF TRUST
**Part:** VI — TRANSITIONAL PROVISIONS
**Section:** 692.01
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Any corporation may execute instruments conveying, mortgaging, or affecting any interest in lands by instruments sealed with the common or corporate seal and signed in its name by its president or any vice president or chief executive officer. Validation of conveyances.
——

Conveyances by corporations of lands in this state, heretofore executed, which have been sealed with the common or corporate seal of such corporation and signed in its name by a vice president or the chief executive officer thereof, shall be as valid and effective and shall bear the same presumptions as if signed in the name of such corporation by its president.
