# Fla. Stat. § 721.50 — Short title.

**Title:** XL — REAL AND PERSONAL PROPERTY
**Chapter:** 721 — VACATION AND TIMESHARE PLANS
**Part:** II — VACATION CLUBS
**Section:** 721.50
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

This part may be cited as the “McAllister Act” in recognition and appreciation for the years of extraordinary and insightful contributions by Mr. Bryan C. McAllister, Examinations Supervisor of the former Division of Florida Land Sales, Condominiums, and Mobile Homes.

**History:** s. 12, ch. 93-58; s. 33, ch. 95-274; s. 71, ch. 2008-240.
