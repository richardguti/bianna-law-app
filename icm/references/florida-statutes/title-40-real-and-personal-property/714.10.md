# Fla. Stat. § 714.10 — Security agreement covering after-acquired property.

**Title:** XL — REAL AND PERSONAL PROPERTY
**Chapter:** 714 — UNIFORM COMMERCIAL REAL ESTATE RECEIVERSHIP ACT
**Part:** IV — FLORIDA UNIFORM FEDERAL LIEN REGISTRATION ACT
**Section:** 714.10
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Except as otherwise provided by law other than this chapter, property that a receiver or an owner acquires after appointment of the receiver is subject to a security agreement entered into before the appointment to the same extent as if the court had not appointed the receiver.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 2020-106.
