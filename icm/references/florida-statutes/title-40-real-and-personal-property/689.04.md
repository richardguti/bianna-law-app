# Fla. Stat. § 689.04 — How executed.

**Title:** XL — REAL AND PERSONAL PROPERTY
**Chapter:** 689 — CONVEYANCES OF LAND AND DECLARATIONS OF TRUST
**Part:** VI — TRANSITIONAL PROVISIONS
**Section:** 689.04
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Such deeds shall be executed and acknowledged as is now or may hereafter be provided by the law regulating conveyances of realty by deed.

**History:** s. 3, ch. 4038, 1891; GS 2451; RGS 3790; CGL 5663.
