# Fla. Stat. § 713.64 — Liens for furnishing material for vessels.

**Title:** XL — REAL AND PERSONAL PROPERTY
**Chapter:** 713 — LIENS, GENERALLY
**Part:** II — MISCELLANEOUS LIENS
**Section:** 713.64
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

In favor of any ship chandler, storekeeper or dealer furnishing stores, provisions, rigging or other material to or for the use of any ship, vessel, steamboat or other watercraft; on such ship, vessel, steamboat or other watercraft.

**History:** s. 14, ch. 40, 1845; ss. 1-4, ch. 1128, 1861; RS 1738; GS 2204; RGS 3511; CGL 5372; s. 36, ch. 67-254.

**Note:** Former s. 85.15.
