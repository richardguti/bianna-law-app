# Fla. Stat. § 694.02 — Married women's conveyances validated.

**Title:** XL — REAL AND PERSONAL PROPERTY
**Chapter:** 694 — CERTAIN CONVEYANCES MADE VALID
**Part:** III — CONVEYANCES TO FOREIGN ENTITIES
**Section:** 694.02
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

All sales, conveyances, transfers, or mortgages made prior to February 14, 1835, by married women of their real estate of inheritance where the husbands of such married women have joined therein shall be as valid as if the same had been conveyed by fine as at common law.

**History:** s. 2, Feb. 4, 1835; RS 1969; GS 2475; RGS 3816; CGL 5689.
