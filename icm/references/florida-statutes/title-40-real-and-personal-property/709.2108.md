# Fla. Stat. § 709.2108 — When power of attorney is effective.

Meaning and effectiveness of power of attorney.
——

The meaning and effectiveness of a power of attorney is governed by this part if the power of attorney:
(1)  
Is used in this state; or

(2)  
States that it is to be governed by the laws of this state.

**History:** s. 9, ch. 2011-210.
