# Fla. Stat. § 708.05 — Husband not liable for antenuptial debts of wife.

**Title:** XL — REAL AND PERSONAL PROPERTY
**Chapter:** 708 — MARRIED WOMEN’S PROPERTY
**Part:** III — CONVEYANCES TO FOREIGN ENTITIES
**Section:** 708.05
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The husband shall not be liable to pay the debts of the wife contracted before marriage, but the property of the wife shall be subject to such debts.

**History:** s. 5, March 6, 1845; RS 2073; GS 2591; RGS 3950; CGL 5869.
