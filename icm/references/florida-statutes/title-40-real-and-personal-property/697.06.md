# Fla. Stat. § 697.06 — Prepayment of note.

**Title:** XL — REAL AND PERSONAL PROPERTY
**Chapter:** 697 — INSTRUMENTS DEEMED MORTGAGES AND THE NATURE OF A MORTGAGE
**Part:** III — CONVEYANCES TO FOREIGN ENTITIES
**Section:** 697.06
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Any note which is silent as to the right of the obligor to prepay the note in advance of the stated maturity date may be prepaid in full by the obligor or her or his successor in interest without penalty.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 77-318; s. 1, ch. 87-351; s. 1, ch. 88-7; s. 774, ch. 97-102.
