# Fla. Stat. § 713.61 — Liens for manufacturing and repairing articles.

**Title:** XL — REAL AND PERSONAL PROPERTY
**Chapter:** 713 — LIENS, GENERALLY
**Part:** II — MISCELLANEOUS LIENS
**Section:** 713.61
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

In favor of any person who shall manufacture, alter or repair any article or thing of value; upon such article or thing.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 3612, 1885; RS 1734; GS 2200; s. 10, ch. 7838, 1919; RGS 3507; CGL 5368; s. 36, ch. 67-254; s. 824, ch. 97-102.

**Note:** Former s. 85.11.
