# Fla. Stat. § 714.26 — Uniformity of application and construction.

**Title:** XL — REAL AND PERSONAL PROPERTY
**Chapter:** 714 — UNIFORM COMMERCIAL REAL ESTATE RECEIVERSHIP ACT
**Part:** IV — FLORIDA UNIFORM FEDERAL LIEN REGISTRATION ACT
**Section:** 714.26
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

Effect of enforcement by mortgagee.
——

A request by a mortgagee for the appointment of a receiver, the appointment of a receiver, or the application by a mortgagee of receivership property or proceeds to the secured obligation does not:
(1)  
Make the mortgagee a mortgagee in possession of the real property;

(2)  
Make the mortgagee an agent of the owner;

(3)  
Constitute an election of remedies which precludes a later action to enforce the secured obligation;

(4)  
Make the secured obligation unenforceable;

(5)  
Limit any right available to the mortgagee with respect to the secured obligation; or

(6)  
Constitute an action under chapter 702.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 2020-106.
