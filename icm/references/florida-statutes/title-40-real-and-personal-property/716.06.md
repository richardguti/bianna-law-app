# Fla. Stat. § 716.06 — Public records.

**Title:** XL — REAL AND PERSONAL PROPERTY
**Chapter:** 716 — ESCHEATS
**Part:** IV — FLORIDA UNIFORM FEDERAL LIEN REGISTRATION ACT
**Section:** 716.06
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

All records in the office of the Chief Financial Officer or the Department of Financial Services relating to federal funds, pursuant to this chapter, shall be public records.

**History:** s. 6, ch. 24333, 1947; ss. 12, 35, ch. 69-10F.S. 716.056716.05
