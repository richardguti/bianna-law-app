# Fla. Stat. § 721.25 — Zoning and building.

**Title:** XL — REAL AND PERSONAL PROPERTY
**Chapter:** 721 — VACATION AND TIMESHARE PLANS
**Part:** I — VACATION PLANS AND TIMESHARING
**Section:** 721.25
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

All laws, ordinances, and regulations concerning buildings or zoning shall be construed and applied with reference to the nature and use of the real estate timeshare plan property, without regard to the form of ownership.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 81-172; s. 159, ch. 83-216; s. 58, ch. 85-62.
