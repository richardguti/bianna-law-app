# Fla. Stat. § 718.1225 — Federal Condominium and Cooperative Abuse Relief Act of 1980; applicability.

**Title:** XL — REAL AND PERSONAL PROPERTY
**Chapter:** 718 — CONDOMINIUMS
**Part:** I — GENERAL PROVISIONS
**Section:** 718.1225
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

It is the intent of the Legislature that the provisions of Title VI of Pub. L. No. 96-399, other than the exceptions stated in s. 611 of that act, shall not apply in this state.

**History:** s. 6, ch. 82-199.
