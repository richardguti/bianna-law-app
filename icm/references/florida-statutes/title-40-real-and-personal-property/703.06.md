# Fla. Stat. § 703.06 — Board may purchase abstract books.F.S. 703.056703.05

**Title:** XL — REAL AND PERSONAL PROPERTY
**Chapter:** 703 — ABSTRACTS OF TITLE
**Part:** III — CONVEYANCES TO FOREIGN ENTITIES
**Section:** 703.06

When the records of the county have been abstracted, the service charge of the clerk for making an abstract shall be as provided in s. 28.24.

**History:** s. 5, ch. 5173, 1903; GS 2509; RGS 3852; CGL 5759; s. 33, ch. 70-134.
