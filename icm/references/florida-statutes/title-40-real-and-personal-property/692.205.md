# Fla. Stat. § 692.205 — Inapplicability of this part to real property for diplomatic purposes.

**Title:** XL — REAL AND PERSONAL PROPERTY
**Chapter:** 692 — CONVEYANCES BY OR TO PARTICULAR ENTITIES
**Part:** III — CONVEYANCES TO FOREIGN ENTITIES
**Section:** 692.205
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

This part does not apply to a foreign principal that acquires real property for a diplomatic purpose that is recognized, acknowledged, or allowed by the Federal Government.

**History:** s. 8, ch. 2023-33.
