# Fla. Stat. § 716.01 — Declaration of policy.

**Title:** XL — REAL AND PERSONAL PROPERTY
**Chapter:** 716 — ESCHEATS
**Part:** IV — FLORIDA UNIFORM FEDERAL LIEN REGISTRATION ACT
**Section:** 716.01
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

It is hereby declared to be the policy of the state, while protecting the interests of the owners thereof, to possess all unclaimed and abandoned money and property for the benefit of all the people of the state, and this law shall be liberally construed to accomplish such purpose.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 24333, 1947.
