# Fla. Stat. § 714.05 — Power of the court.

**Title:** XL — REAL AND PERSONAL PROPERTY
**Chapter:** 714 — UNIFORM COMMERCIAL REAL ESTATE RECEIVERSHIP ACT
**Part:** IV — FLORIDA UNIFORM FEDERAL LIEN REGISTRATION ACT
**Section:** 714.05
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The court that appoints a receiver under this chapter has exclusive jurisdiction to direct the receiver and determine any controversy related to the receivership or receivership property.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 2020-106.
