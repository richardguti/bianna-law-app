# Fla. Stat. § 689.08 — Fines and common recoveries.

**Title:** XL — REAL AND PERSONAL PROPERTY
**Chapter:** 689 — CONVEYANCES OF LAND AND DECLARATIONS OF TRUST
**Part:** VI — TRANSITIONAL PROVISIONS
**Section:** 689.08
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Conveyance by fine or by common recovery shall never be used in this state.

**History:** s. 2, Feb. 4, 1835; RS 1953; GS 2454; RGS 3794; CGL 5667.
