# Fla. Stat. § 663.14 — Foreign travel expenses.

**Title:** XXXVIII — BANKS AND BANKING
**Chapter:** 663 — INTERNATIONAL BANKING
**Part:** I — INTERNATIONAL BANKING CORPORATIONS
**Section:** 663.14
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

If domestic or foreign travel is deemed necessary by the office to effectuate the purposes of this part, representatives of the office shall be reimbursed for actual, reasonable, and necessary expenses incurred in such domestic or foreign travel.

**History:** ss. 95, 152, ch. 80-260; ss. 2, 3, ch. 81-318; ss. 26, 46, ch. 82-214; s. 1, ch. 91-307; ss. 1, 171, ch. 92-303; s. 17, ch. 2001-64; s. 1831, ch. 2003-261.
