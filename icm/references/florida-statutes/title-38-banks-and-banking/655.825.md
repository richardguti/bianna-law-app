# Fla. Stat. § 655.825 — Deposits in trust; applicability of s. 655.82 in place of former s. 655.81.

**Title:** XXXVIII — BANKS AND BANKING
**Chapter:** 655 — FINANCIAL INSTITUTIONS GENERALLY
**Part:** III — HEALTH CARE SERVICES
**Section:** 655.825
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
Because deposits in trust are also accounts with a pay-on-death designation as described in s. 655.82, it is the intent of the Legislature that the provisions of s. 655.82 shall apply to and govern deposits in trust. References to former s. 655.81 in any depository agreement shall be interpreted after the effective date of this act as references to s. 655.82.

(2)  
This section shall take effect July 1, 2001, and shall apply to deposits made to a depository account created after December 31, 1994.

**History:** s. 3, ch. 2001-243; s. 101, ch. 2019-3.
