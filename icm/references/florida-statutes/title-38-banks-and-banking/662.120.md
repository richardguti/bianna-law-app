# Fla. Stat. § 662.120 — Maximum number of designated relatives.

**Title:** XXXVIII — BANKS AND BANKING
**Chapter:** 662 — FAMILY TRUST COMPANIES
**Part:** III — HEALTH CARE SERVICES
**Section:** 662.120
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
A family trust company may not have more than one designated relative.

(2)  
A licensed family trust company may have up to two designated relatives. The designated relatives may not have a common ancestor within three generations.

**History:** s. 9, ch. 2014-97; s. 4, ch. 2016-35.
