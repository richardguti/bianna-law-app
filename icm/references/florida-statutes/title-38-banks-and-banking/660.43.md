# Fla. Stat. § 660.43 — Common trust fund investments.

**Title:** XXXVIII — BANKS AND BANKING
**Chapter:** 660 — TRUST BUSINESS
**Part:** III — HEALTH CARE SERVICES
**Section:** 660.43
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

No bank, association, or trust company shall mingle its own funds with any common trust fund managed by such bank, association, or trust company.

**History:** s. 3, ch. 28016, 1953; s. 1, ch. 67-336; s. 3, ch. 76-168; s. 1, ch. 77-457; ss. 141, 151, 152, ch. 80-260; ss. 2, 3, ch. 81-318; s. 1, ch. 91-307; s. 1, ch. 92-303; s. 5, ch. 93-257.

**Note:** Former s. 660.12.
