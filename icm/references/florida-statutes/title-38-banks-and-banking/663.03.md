# Fla. Stat. § 663.03 — Applicability of the Florida Business Corporation Act.

**Title:** XXXVIII — BANKS AND BANKING
**Chapter:** 663 — INTERNATIONAL BANKING
**Part:** I — INTERNATIONAL BANKING CORPORATIONS
**Section:** 663.03
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Notwithstanding s. 607.01401(36), the provisions of part I of chapter 607 not in conflict with the financial institutions codes which relate to foreign corporations apply to all international banking corporations and their offices doing business in this state.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 77-157; ss. 84, 151, 152, ch. 80-260; ss. 2, 3, ch. 81-318; s. 59, ch. 87-226; s. 184, ch. 90-179; s. 1, ch. 91-307; ss. 1, 152, ch. 92yCivil action subpoena enforcement.
