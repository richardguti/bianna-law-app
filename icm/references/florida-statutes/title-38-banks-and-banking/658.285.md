# Fla. Stat. § 658.285 — Acquisition or ownership of state banks by international banking corporations.

**Title:** XXXVIII — BANKS AND BANKING
**Chapter:** 658 — BANKS AND TRUST COMPANIES
**Part:** III — HEALTH CARE SERVICES
**Section:** 658.285
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

An international banking corporation may, with the approval of the office pursuant to s. 658.28, acquire control over or organize a state bank organized under the laws of this state. For the purposes of this section, the word “bank” shall have the meaning given in s. 2(c) of the Bank Holding Company Act of 1956, 12 U.S.C. s. 1841(c).

**History:** s. 162, ch. 92-303; s. 5, ch. 2000-155; s. 15, ch. 2001-243; s. 1774, ch. 2003-261.

**Note:** Former s. 663.066.
