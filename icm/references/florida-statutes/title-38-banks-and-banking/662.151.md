# Fla. Stat. § 662.151 — Registration of a foreign licensed family trust company to operate in this state.

**Title:** XXXVIII — BANKS AND BANKING
**Chapter:** 663 — INTERNATIONAL BANKING
**Part:** III — HEALTH CARE SERVICES
**Section:** 662.151
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

A foreign licensed family trust company lawfully organized and currently in good standing with the state regulatory agency in the jurisdiction under the law of which it is organized may qualify to begin operations in this state by:
(1)  
Filing an application with the Department of State to apply for a certificate of authority in accordance with and subject to s. 605.0902 or s. 607.1503.

(2)  
Filing an initial registration to begin operations as a foreign licensed family trust company in accordance with and subject to s. 662.122 and subject to the sections of this chapter which specifically state that they apply to a foreign licensed family trust company.

**History:** s. 36, ch. 2014-97; s. 17, ch. 2016-35.
