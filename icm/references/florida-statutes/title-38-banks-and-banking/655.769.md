# Fla. Stat. § 655.769 — Definitions of terms used in ss. 655.77-655.91.

**Title:** XXXVIII — BANKS AND BANKING
**Chapter:** 655 — FINANCIAL INSTITUTIONS GENERALLY
**Part:** III — HEALTH CARE SERVICES
**Section:** 655.769
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

As used in ss. F.S. 655.7626655.762  
Sale of assets.
——

A state financial institution may sell any asset in the ordinary course of business or with the approval of the office in any other circumstances.

**History:** s. 44, ch. 92-303; s. 1731, ch. 2003-261.
