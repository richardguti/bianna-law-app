# Fla. Stat. § 655.043 — Articles of incorporation; amendments; approval.

**Title:** XXXVIII — BANKS AND BANKING
**Chapter:** 655 — FINANCIAL INSTITUTIONS GENERALLY
**Part:** III — HEALTH CARE SERVICES
**Section:** 655.043
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

A bank, trust company, or association may not amend its articles of incorporation without the written approval of the office.

**History:** s. 7, ch. 84-216; s. 1, ch. 85-65; s. 2, ch. 85-82; s. 1, ch. 91-307; s. 1, ch. 92-303; s. 4, ch. 93-111; s. 2, ch. 2001-243; s. 1715, ch. 2003-261.
