# Fla. Stat. § 663.318 — Reserve requirements.

**Title:** XXXVIII — BANKS AND BANKING
**Chapter:** 663 — INTERNATIONAL BANKING
**Part:** III — INTERNATIONAL TRUST COMPANY REPRESENTATIVE OFFICES
**Section:** 663.318
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
An international development bank shall establish and maintain adequate loan loss reserves.

(2)  
An international development bank organized under chapter 617 as a corporation not for profit shall not be required to establish or maintain any reserves with respect to its liabilities.

**History:** ss. 1, 5, ch. 86-82; s. 9, ch. 89-110; s. 1, ch. 91-307; s. 1, ch. 92-303; s. 131, ch. 2005-2.
