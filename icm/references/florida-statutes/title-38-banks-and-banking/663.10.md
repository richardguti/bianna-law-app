# Fla. Stat. § 663.10 — Conversion of license.

**Title:** XXXVIII — BANKS AND BANKING
**Chapter:** 663 — INTERNATIONAL BANKING
**Part:** I — INTERNATIONAL BANKING CORPORATIONS
**Section:** 663.10
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

——

(1)  
An international banking corporation desiring to convert its existy and made available to the office, upon request, at any time during regular business hours of the office. Any failure to keep such records as aforesaid or any refusal to produce such records upon request by the office is grounds for suspension or revocation of any license issued under this part.
