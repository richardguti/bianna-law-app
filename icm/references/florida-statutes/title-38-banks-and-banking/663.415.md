# Fla. Stat. § 663.415 — Travel expenses.

**Title:** XXXVIII — BANKS AND BANKING
**Chapter:** 663 — INTERNATIONAL BANKING
**Part:** IV — QUALIFIED LIMITED SERVICE AFFILIATES OF INTERNATIONAL TRUST ENTITIES
**Section:** 663.415
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

If domestic or foreign travel is deemed necessary by the office to effectuate the purposes of this part, the office must be reimbursed for actual, reasonable, and necessary expenses incurred in such domestic or foreign travel by the international trust company representative office under examination.

**History:** s. 37, ch. 2017-83.
