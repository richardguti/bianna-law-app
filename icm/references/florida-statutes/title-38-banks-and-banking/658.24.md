# Fla. Stat. § 658.24 — Organizational procedures.

**Title:** XXXVIII — BANKS AND BANKING
**Chapter:** 658 — BANKS AND TRUST COMPANIES
**Part:** III — HEALTH CARE SERVICES
**Section:** 658.24
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

——

After the corporate existence of a bank or trust company corporation has commenced and the stock has been issued, but no less than 30 days prior to the intended opening date, a shareholders' meeting shall be held to elect directors already approved by the office, to approve organizational expenses, and to conduct such other business relating to the corporation as may be appropriate. Immediately after the board of directors has been elected by the shareholders, the board shall meet to adopt bylaws, elect officers, and conduct such other business relating to the corporation as may be appropriate.
