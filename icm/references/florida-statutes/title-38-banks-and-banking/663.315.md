# Fla. Stat. § 663.315 — Investment limits.

**Title:** XXXVIII — BANKS AND BANKING
**Chapter:** 663 — INTERNATIONAL BANKING
**Part:** II — INTERNATIONAL DEVELOPMENT BANKS
**Section:** 663.315
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

An international development bank shall be subject to the provisions of s. 658.67, relating to investments, in the same manner and to the same extent as a state bank.

**History:** ss. 1, 5, ch. 86-82; s. 1, ch. 91-307; s. 1, ch. 92-303.
