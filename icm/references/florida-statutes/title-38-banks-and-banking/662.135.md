# Fla. Stat. § 662.135 — Service as court-appointed fiduciary; bond requirement.

**Title:** XXXVIII — BANKS AND BANKING
**Chapter:** 662 — FAMILY TRUST COMPANIES
**Part:** III — HEALTH CARE SERVICES
**Section:** 662.135
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

A licensed family trust company is not required to provide or otherwise post a bond or other surety to serve as a court-appointed fiduciary in a proceeding brought or conducted in this state.

**History:** s. 26, ch. 2014-97.
