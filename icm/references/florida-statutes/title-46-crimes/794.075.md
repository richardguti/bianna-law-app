# Fla. Stat. § 794.075 — Sexual predators; erectile dysfunction drugs.

**Title:** XLVI — CRIMES
**Chapter:** 794 — SEXUAL BATTERY
**Part:** II — ASBESTOS AND SILICA COMPENSATION FAIRNESS ACT
**Section:** 794.075
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
A person may not possess a prescription drug, as defined in s. 499.003(40), for the purpose of treating erectile dysfunction if the person is designated as a sexual predator under s. 775.21.

(2)  
A person who violates a provision of this section for the first time commits a misdemeanor of the second degree, punishable as provided in s. 775.082 or s. 775.083. A person who violates a provision of this section a second or subsequent time commits a misdemeanor of the first degree, punishable as provided in s. 775.082 or s. 775.083.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 2006-150; s. 41, ch. 2008-207; s. 44, ch. 2010-161; s. 46, ch. 2016-24; s. 20, ch. 2016-212; s. 34, ch. 2025-156.
