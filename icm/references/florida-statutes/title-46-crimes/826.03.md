# Fla. Stat. § 826.03 — Knowingly marrying husband or wife of another.

**Title:** XLVI — CRIMES
**Chapter:** 826 — BIGAMY; INCEST
**Part:** IV — CREDIT COUNSELING SERVICES
**Section:** 826.03
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Whoever knowingly marries the husband or wife of another person, knowing him or her to be the spouse of another person, shall be guilty of a felony of the third degree, punishable as provided in s. 775.082, s. 775.083, or s. 775.084.

**History:** s. 39, Feb. 10, 1832; RS 2605; GS 3528; RGS 5418; CGL 7561; s. 776, ch. 71-136; s. 46, ch. 74-383; s. 30, ch. 75-298.

**Note:** Former s. 799.03.
