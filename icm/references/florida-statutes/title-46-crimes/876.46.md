# Fla. Stat. § 876.46 — Penalty for going upon closed or restricted highway.

**Title:** XLVI — CRIMES
**Chapter:** 876 — CRIMINAL ANARCHY, TREASON, AND OTHER CRIMES AGAINST PUBLIC ORDER
**Part:** IV — CREDIT COUNSELING SERVICES
**Section:** 876.46
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Whoever violates any order made under s. 876.45 shall be guilty of a misdemeanor of the second degree, punishable as provided in s. 775.082 or s. 775.083.

**History:** s. 10, ch. 20252, 1941; s. 710, ch. 71-136; s. 65, ch. 74-383; s. 246, ch. 77-104.

**Note:** Former s. 779.15.
