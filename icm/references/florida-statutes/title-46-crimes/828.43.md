# Fla. Stat. § 828.43 — Injunction.

**Title:** XLVI — CRIMES
**Chapter:** 828 — ANIMALS: CRUELTY; SALES; ANIMAL ENTERPRISE PROTECTION
**Part:** IV — CREDIT COUNSELING SERVICES
**Section:** 828.43
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

In a case of ongoing animal enterprise disruption, the aggrieved animal enterprise may obtain injunctive relief.

**History:** s. 12, ch. 93-13.
