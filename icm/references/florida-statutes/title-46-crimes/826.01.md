# Fla. Stat. § 826.01 — Bigamy; punishment.

**Title:** XLVI — CRIMES
**Chapter:** 826 — BIGAMY; INCEST
**Part:** IV — CREDIT COUNSELING SERVICES
**Section:** 826.01
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Whoever, having a husband or wife living, marries another person shall, except in the cases mentioned in s. 826.02, be guilty of a felony of the third degree, punishable as provided in s. 775.082, s. 775.083, or s. 775.084.

**History:** s. 4, sub-ch. 8, ch. 1637, 1868; RS 2603; GS 3526; RGS 5416; CGL 7559; s. 775, ch. 71-136; s. 44, ch. 74-383; s. 30, ch. 75-298.

**Note:** Former s. 799.01.
