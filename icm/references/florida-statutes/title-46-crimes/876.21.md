# Fla. Stat. § 876.21 — Sections 876.11-876.20; penalty.

**Title:** XLVI — CRIMES
**Chapter:** 876 — CRIMINAL ANARCHY, TREASON, AND OTHER CRIMES AGAINST PUBLIC ORDER
**Part:** IV — CREDIT COUNSELING SERVICES
**Section:** 876.21
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Any person or persons violating ss. 876.11-876.20, except as provided in s. 876.18, shall be guilty of a misdemeanor of the second degree, punishable as provided in s. 775.082 or s. 775.083.

**History:** s. 11, ch. 26542, 1951; s. 1142, ch. 71-136; s. 4, ch. 91-83.
