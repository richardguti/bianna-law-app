# Fla. Stat. § 828.252 — Nonambulatory animals.

**Title:** XLVI — CRIMES
**Chapter:** 828 — ANIMALS: CRUELTY; SALES; ANIMAL ENTERPRISE PROTECTION
**Part:** IV — CREDIT COUNSELING SERVICES
**Section:** 828.252
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

This section acknowledges that natural emergencies may arise and that, even under recognized best management practices, injury may occur. In all cases, nonambulatory animals must be dealt with in a humane manner.
(1)  
As used in this section, the term “nonambulatory animal” means any livestock that is unable to stand and walk unassisted.
