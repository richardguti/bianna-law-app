# Fla. Stat. § 812.0195 — Dealing in stolen property by use of the Internet.

**Title:** XLVI — CRIMES
**Chapter:** 812 — THEFT, ROBBERY, AND RELATED CRIMES
**Part:** II — ASBESTOS AND SILICA COMPENSATION FAIRNESS ACT
**Section:** 812.0195
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Any person in this state who uses the Internet to sell or offer for sale any merchandise or other property that the person knows, or has reasonable cause to believe, is stolen commits:
(1)  
A misdemeanor of the second degree, punishable as provided in s. 775.082 or s. 775.083, if the value of the properxsons, may be aggregated when determining the punishment for the offense.

(3)  
Any person who knowingly initiates, organizes, plans, finances, directs, manages, or supervises the obtaining of property paid for in whole or in part by the Medicaid program and who traffics in, or endeavors to traffic in, such property commits a felony of the first degree, punishable as provided in s. 775.082, s. 775.083, or s. 775.084.

**History:** s. 12, ch. 2004-344.
