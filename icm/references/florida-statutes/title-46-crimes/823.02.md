# Fla. Stat. § 823.02 — Building bonfires.

**Title:** XLVI — CRIMES
**Chapter:** 823 — PUBLIC NUISANCES
**Part:** IV — CREDIT COUNSELING SERVICES
**Section:** 823.02
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Whoever is concerned in causing or making a bonfire within 10 rods of any house or building shall be guilty of a misdemeanor of the second degree, punishable as provided in s. 775.082 or s. 775.083.

**History:** s. 12, ch. 1637, 1868; RS 2705; GS 3681; RGS 5625; CGL 7818; s. 933, ch. 71-136; s. 66, ch. 74-383; s. 1, ch. 75-24; s. 41, ch. 75-298.
