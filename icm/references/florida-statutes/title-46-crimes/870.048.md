# Fla. Stat. § 870.048 — Violations.

**Title:** XLVI — CRIMES
**Chapter:** 865 — VIOLATIONS OF CERTAIN COMMERCIAL RESTRICTIONS
**Part:** IV — CREDIT COUNSELING SERVICES
**Section:** 870.048
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Any violation of a provision of ss. 870.041-870.047 or of any emergency measure established pursuant thereto shall be a misdemeanor of the first degree, punishable as provided in s. 775.082 or s. 775.083.

**History:** s. 9, ch. 70-990; s. 1128, ch. 71-136.
