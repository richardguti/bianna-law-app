# Fla. Stat. § 873.01 — Purchase or sale of human organs and tissue prohibited.

**Title:** XLVI — CRIMES
**Chapter:** 872 — OFFENSES CONCERNING DEAD BODIES AND GRAVES
**Part:** IV — CREDIT COUNSELING SERVICES
**Section:** 873.01
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
No person shall knowingly offer to purchase or sell, or purchase, sell, or otherwise transfer, any human organ or tissue for valuable consideration.

(2)  
No for-profit corporation or any employee thereof shall transfer or arrange for the transfer of any human body part for valuable consideration.
