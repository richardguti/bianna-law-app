# Fla. Stat. § 836.01 — Punishment for libel.

**Title:** XLVI — CRIMES
**Chapter:** 836 — DEFAMATION; LIBEL; THREATENING LETTERS AND SIMILAR OFFENSES
**Part:** IV — CREDIT COUNSELING SERVICES
**Section:** 836.01
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Any person convicted of the publication of a libel shall be guilty of a misdemeanor of the first degree, punishable as provided in s. 775.082 or s. 775.083.

**History:** s. 15, sub-ch. 7, ch. 1637, 1868; RS 2418; GS 3256; RGS 5087; CGL 7189; s. 987, ch. 71-136.
