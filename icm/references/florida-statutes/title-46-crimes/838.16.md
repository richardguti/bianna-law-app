# Fla. Stat. § 838.16 — Commercial bribery.

**Title:** XLVI — CRIMES
**Chapter:** 838 — BRIBERY; MISUSE OF PUBLIC OFFICE
**Part:** IV — CREDIT COUNSELING SERVICES
**Section:** 838.16
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
A person commits the crime of commercial bribery if, knowing that another is subject to a duty described in s. 838.15(1) and with intent to influence the other person to violate that duty, the person confers, offers to confer, or agrees to confer a benefit on the other.

(2)  
Commercial bribery is a third degree felony, punishable as provided in s. 775.082, s. 775.083, or s. 775.084.

**History:** s. 2, ch. 90-301.
