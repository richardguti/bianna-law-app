# Fla. Stat. § 790.256 — Public service announcements.

**Title:** XLVI — CRIMES
**Chapter:** 790 — WEAPONS AND FIREARMS
**Part:** II — ASBESTOS AND SILICA COMPENSATION FAIRNESS ACT
**Section:** 790.256
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The Department of Health shall prepare public service announcements for dissemination to parents throughout the state, of the provisions of chapter 93-416, Laws of Florida.

**History:** s. 9, ch. 93-416; s. 295, ch. 99-8.
