# Fla. Stat. § 872.06 — Abuse of a dead human body; penalty.

**Title:** XLVI — CRIMES
**Chapter:** 872 — OFFENSES CONCERNING DEAD BODIES AND GRAVES
**Part:** IV — CREDIT COUNSELING SERVICES
**Section:** 872.06
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

——

(1)  
As used in this section, the term:
(a)  
“Female genitals” includes the labia minora, labia majora, clitoris, vulva, hymen, and vagina.

(b)  
“Sexual abuse” means:
1.  
Anal or female genital penetration of a dead human body by the sexual organ of a person or by any other object;

2.  
Contact or union of the penis, female genitals, or anus of a person with the mouth, penis, female genitals, or anus of a dead human body; or

3.  
Contact or union of a person's mouth with the penis, female genitals, or anus of a dead human body.

(2)  
A person who mutilates, commits sexual abuse (775.082 or s. 775.083.

(c)  
This subsection shall not apply to any person acting under the direction or authority of the division or to any person otherwise authorized by law to disturb, destroy, or remove an unmarked human burial.
