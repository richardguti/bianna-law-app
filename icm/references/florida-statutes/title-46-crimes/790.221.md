# Fla. Stat. § 790.221 — Possession of short-barreled rifle, short-barreled shotgun, or machine gun; penalty.

**Title:** XLVI — CRIMES
**Chapter:** 790 — WEAPONS AND FIREARMS
**Part:** II — ASBESTOS AND SILICA COMPENSATION FAIRNESS ACT
**Section:** 790.221
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

——

(1)  
It is unlawful for any person to own or to have in his or her care, custody, possession, or control any short-barreled rifle, short-barreled shotgun, or machine gun which is, or may readily be made, operable; but this section shall not apply to antique firearms.

(2)  
A person who violates this section commits a felony of the second degree, punishable as provided in s. 775.082, s. 775.083, or s. 24-133; s. 34, ch. 2025-153.
