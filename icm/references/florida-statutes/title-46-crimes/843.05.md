# Fla. Stat. § 843.05 — Resisting timber agent.

**Title:** XLVI — CRIMES
**Chapter:** 843 — OBSTRUCTING JUSTICE
**Part:** IV — CREDIT COUNSELING SERVICES
**Section:** 843.05
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Whoever obstructs, resists, or opposes a timber agent in the discharge of her or his duties, or attempts so to do, shall be guilty of a misdemeanor of the second degree, punishable as provided in s. 775.083.

**History:** s. 5, ch. 3020, 1877; RS 2584; GS 3504; RGS 5390; CGL 7529; s. 1038, ch. 71-136; s. 1337, ch. 97-102.
