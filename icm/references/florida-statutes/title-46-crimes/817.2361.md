# Fla. Stat. § 817.2361 — False or fraudulent proof of motor vehicle insurance.

**Title:** XLVI — CRIMES
**Chapter:** 817 — FRAUDULENT PRACTICES
**Part:** I — FALSE PRETENSES AND FRAUDS, GENERALLY
**Section:** 817.2361
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Any person who, with intent to deceive any other person, creates, markets, or presents a false or fraudulent proof of motor vehicle insurance commits a felony of the third degree, punishable as provided in s. 775.082, s. 775.083, or s. 775.084.

**History:** s. 12, ch. 2003-411; s. 17, ch. 2006-305.
