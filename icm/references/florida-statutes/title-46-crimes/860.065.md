# Fla. Stat. § 860.065 — Commercial transportation; penalty for use in commission of a felony.

**Title:** XLVI — CRIMES

**Part:** IV — CREDIT COUNSELING SERVICES

**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

Unauthorized person interfering with railroad train, cars, or engines.
——

Any person, other than an employee or authorized agent of the railroad company acting within the line of duty, who shall knowingly or willfully detach or uncouple any train; put on, apply, or tamper with any brake, bell cord, or emergency valve; or otherwise interfere with any train, engine, car, or part thereof is guilty of a felony of the third degree, punishable as provided in s. 775.082, s. 775.083, or s. 775.084.

**History:** ss. 1, 2, ch. 4704, 1899; GS 3654; RGS 5591; CGL 7777; s. 1089, ch. 71-136; s. 1, ch. 79-360; s. 1391, ch. 97-102.
