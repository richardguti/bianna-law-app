# Fla. Stat. § 817.411 — False information; advertising.

**Title:** XLVI — CRIMES
**Chapter:** 817 — FRAUDULENT PRACTICES
**Part:** I — FALSE PRETENSES AND FRAUDS, GENERALLY
**Section:** 817.411
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

——

A person or business entity may not knowingly publish, disseminate, circulate, or place before the public, or cause directly or indirectly, to be made, published, disseminated, circulated, or placed before the public, in a newspaper,dvertisement or unlawful sale.
