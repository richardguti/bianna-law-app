# Fla. Stat. § 893.015 — Statutory references.

**Title:** XLVI — CRIMES
**Chapter:** 877 — MISCELLANEOUS CRIMES
**Part:** IV — CREDIT COUNSELING SERVICES
**Section:** 893.015
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The purpose of this chapter is to comprehensively address drug abuse prevention and control in this state. To this end, unless expressly provided otherwise, a reference in any section of the Florida Statutes to chapter 893 or to any section or portion of a section of chapter 893 includes all subsequent amendments to chapter 893 or to the referenced section or portion of a section.

**History:** s. 3, ch. 2017-107; s. 2, ch. 2017-110.
