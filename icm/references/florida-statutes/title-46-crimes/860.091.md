# Fla. Stat. § 860.091 — Violations of s. 860.05, s. 860.08, or s. 860.09 resulting in death; penalty.

**Title:** XLVI — CRIMES
**Chapter:** 860 — OFFENSES CONCERNING AIRCRAFT, MOTOR VEHICLES, VESSELS, AND RAILROADS
**Part:** IV — CREDIT COUNSELING SERVICES
**Section:** 860.091
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Any person who violates the provisions of s. 860.05, s. 860.08, or s. 860.09 when such violation results in the death of another person is guilty of homicide as defined in chapter 782, punishable as provided in s. 775.082.

**History:** s. 2, ch. 79-360.
