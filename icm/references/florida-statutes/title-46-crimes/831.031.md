# Fla. Stat. § 831.031 — Evidence.

**Title:** XLVI — CRIMES
**Chapter:** 831 — FORGERY AND COUNTERFEITING
**Part:** IV — CREDIT COUNSELING SERVICES
**Section:** 831.031
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

——

In any proceeding under or related to ss. 831.03-831.034:
(1)  
Proof that a person is in possession of more than 25 goods, labels, patches, stickers, wrappers, badges, emblems, medallions, charms, boxes, containers, cans, cases, hangtags, documentation, or packaging or any other components of any type or nature bearing a counterfeit mark, unless satisfactorily explained, gives rise to an inference that such property is being possessed with intent to offer it for sale or distribution.

(2)  
(b)  
In the case of labels, patches, stickers, wrappers, badges, emblems, medallions, charms, boxes, containers, cans, cases, hangtags, documentation, or packaging or any other components of any type or nature that are designed, marketed, or otherwise intended to be used on or in connection with any goods or services, the retail value shall be treated as if each component was a finished good and valued as described in paragraph (a).
