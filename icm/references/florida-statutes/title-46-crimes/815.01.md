# Fla. Stat. § 815.01 — Short title.

**Title:** XLVI — CRIMES
**Chapter:** 815 — COMPUTER-RELATED CRIMES
**Part:** II — ASBESTOS AND SILICA COMPENSATION FAIRNESS ACT
**Section:** 815.01
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The provisions of this act shall be known and may be cited as the “Florida Computer Crimes Act.”

**History:** s. 1, ch. 78-92.
