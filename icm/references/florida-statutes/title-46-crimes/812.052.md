# Fla. Stat. § 812.052 — Certain purchases prohibited.

**Title:** XLVI — CRIMES
**Chapter:** 812 — THEFT, ROBBERY, AND RELATED CRIMES
**Part:** II — ASBESTOS AND SILICA COMPENSATION FAIRNESS ACT
**Section:** 812.052
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

It shall be unlawful for any person to purchase any object used to commemorate a deceased person or placed in memory of a deceased person, or any part of such object, unless the same is sold by an authorized representative of the deceased person or of the cemetery in which such object was placed.
