# Fla. Stat. § 828.26 — Penalties.

**Title:** XLVI — CRIMES
**Chapter:** 828 — ANIMALS: CRUELTY; SALES; ANIMAL ENTERPRISE PROTECTION
**Part:** IV — CREDIT COUNSELING SERVICES
**Section:** 828.26
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
Any person who violates the provisions of ss. 828.22-828.26 and any rule associated with these sections shall be subject to an administrative fine of up to $10,000 for each violation.

(2)  
Unless otherwise provided, any person who violates any provision of ss. 828.22-828.26 commits a misdemeanor of the second degree, punishable as provided in s. 775.082 or s. 775.083.

(3)  
Nothing in this section precludes the enforcement of s. 828.12, relating to cruelty to animals.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 61-254; ss. 14, 35, ch. 69-106; s. 958, ch. 71-136; s. 43, ch. 2001-279; s. 2, ch. 2002-51.
