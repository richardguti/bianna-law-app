# Fla. Stat. § 796.05 — Deriving support from the proceeds of prostitution.

**Title:** XLVI — CRIMES
**Chapter:** 796 — PROSTITUTION
**Part:** II — ASBESTOS AND SILICA COMPENSATION FAIRNESS ACT
**Section:** 796.05
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
It shall be unlawful for any person with reasonable belief or knowing another person is engaged in prostitution to live or derive support or maintenance in whole or in F.S. 796.046796.04  
Forcing, compelling, or coercing another to become a prostitute.
——

(1)  
It shall be unlawful for anyone to force, compel, or coerce another to become a prostitute.

(2)  
Anyone violating this section shall be guilty of a felony of the third degree, punishable as provided in s. 775.082, s. 775.083, or s. 775.084.
