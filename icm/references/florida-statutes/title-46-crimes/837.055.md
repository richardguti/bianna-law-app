# Fla. Stat. § 837.055 — False information to law enforcement during investigation.

**Title:** XLVI — CRIMES
**Chapter:** 837 — PERJURY
**Part:** IV — CREDIT COUNSELING SERVICES
**Section:** 837.055
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
Whoever knowingly and willfully gives false information to a law enforcement officer who is conducting a missing person investigation or a felony criminal investigation with the intent to mislead the officer or impede the investigation commits a misdemeanor of the first degree, punishable as provided in s. 775.082 or s. 775.083.

(2)  
(a)  
The costs of prosecution and investigation under s. 938.27.

(b)  
Restitution to any victim who suffers damage or injury as a proximate result of lawful conduct arising out of a law enforcement response.

**History:** s. 57, ch. 74-383; s. 34, ch. 75-298; s. 206, ch. 91-224; s. 5, ch. 97-90; s. 1, ch. 2013-117; s. 2, ch. 2025-60.
