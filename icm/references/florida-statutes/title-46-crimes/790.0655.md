# Fla. Stat. § 790.0655 — Purchase and delivery of firearms; mandatory waiting period; exceptions; penalties.

**Title:** XLVI — CRIMES
**Chapter:** 790 — WEAPONS AND FIREARMS
**Part:** II — ASBESTOS AND SILICA COMPENSATION FAIRNESS ACT
**Section:** 790.0655
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)(a)  
A mandatory waiting period is imposed between the purchase and delivery of a firearm. The mandatory waiting period is 3 days, excluding weekends and legal holidays, or expires upon the completion of the records checks required under s. 790.065, whichever occurs later.

(b)  
Records of firearm sales must be available for inspection by any law enforcement agency, as defined in s. 934.02, during normal business hours.

(2)  
The waiting period does not apply in the following circumstances:
(a)  
When a firearm is being purchased by a holder of a concealed weapons or concealed firearms license.

(b)  
To a trade-in of another firearm.

(c)  
To the purchase of a rifle or shotgun, upon a person's successfully completing a minimum of a 16-hour hunter safety course and possessing a hunter safety certification card issued under s. 379.3581. A person who is exempt from the hunter safety course requirements under s. 379.3581 and holds a valid Florida hunting license is exempt from the mandatory waiting period under this section for the purchase of a rifle or shotgun.

(3)

(a)  
For any retailer, or any employee or agent of a retailer, to deliver a firearm before the expiration of the waiting period, subject to the exceptions provided in subsection (2).
