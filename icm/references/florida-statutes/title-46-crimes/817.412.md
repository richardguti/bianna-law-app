# Fla. Stat. § 817.412 — Sale of used goods as new; penalty.

**Title:** XLVI — CRIMES
**Chapter:** 817 — FRAUDULENT PRACTICES
**Part:** I — FALSE PRETENSES AND FRAUDS, GENERALLY
**Section:** 817.412
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

——

(1)  
It is unlawful for a seller in a transaction where the purchase price of goods exceeds $100 to misrepresent orally, in writing, electronically, or by failure to speak that the goods are new or original when they are used or repossessed or where they have been used for sales demonstration.

(e)  
The performance is expressly authorized in the advertising by the recording person or group.
