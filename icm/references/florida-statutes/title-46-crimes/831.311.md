# Fla. Stat. § 831.311 — Unlawful sale, manufacture, alteration, delivery, uttering, or possession of counterfeit-resistant prescription blanks for controlled substances.

**Title:** XLVI — CRIMES
**Chapter:** 831 — FORGERY AND COUNTERFEITING
**Part:** IV — CREDIT COUNSELING SERVICES
**Section:** 831.311
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

——

(1)  
It is unlawful for any person having the intent to injure or defraud any person or to facilitate any violation of s. 893.13 to sell, manufacture, alter, deliver, utter, or possess with intent to injure or defraud any person, or to facilitate any violation of s. 893.13, any counterfeit-resistant prescription blanks for controlled substances, the form and content of which are adopted by rule of the Department of Health pursuant to s. 893.065.

(2)  
Any person who violates this section commits a felony of the third degree, punishable as provid320; s. 8, ch. 2002-78; s. 27, ch. 2016-105; s. 4, ch. 2019-166.
