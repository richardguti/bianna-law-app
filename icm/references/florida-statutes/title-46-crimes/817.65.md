# Fla. Stat. § 817.65 — Defenses not available.

**Title:** XLVI — CRIMES
**Chapter:** 817 — FRAUDULENT PRACTICES
**Part:** II — CREDIT CARD CRIMES
**Section:** 817.65
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

It shall not constitute a defense to a prosecution for any violation of this part that:
(1)  
A credit card that is not a counterfeit credit card is offered for use or sale as a counterfeit credit card.

(2)  
A person, other than the defendant, who violated this part has not been convicted, apprehended, or identified.

**History:** s. 8, ch. 67-340; s. 7, ch. 85-43.
