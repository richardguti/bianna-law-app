# Fla. Stat. § 817.29 — Cheating.

**Title:** XLVI — CRIMES
**Chapter:** 817 — FRAUDULENT PRACTICES
**Part:** I — FALSE PRETENSES AND FRAUDS, GENERALLY
**Section:** 817.29
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Whoever is convicted of any gross fraud or cheat at common law shall be guilty of a felony of the third degree, punishable as provided in s. 775.082, s. 775.083, or s. 775.084.

**History:** s. 54, ch. 1637, 1868; RS 2475; GS 3344; RGS 5187; CGL 7290; s. 860, ch. 71-136.
