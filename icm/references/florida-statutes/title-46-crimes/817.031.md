# Fla. Stat. § 817.031 — Making false statements; venue of prosecution.

**Title:** XLVI — CRIMES
**Chapter:** 817 — FRAUDULENT PRACTICES
**Part:** I — FALSE PRETENSES AND FRAUDS, GENERALLY
**Section:** 817.031
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Prosecutions under s. 817.03 may be begun in the county where the statement was written, or purports to have been written.

**History:** s. 2, ch. 5134, 1903; GS 3323; RGS 5161; CGL 7264.

**Note:** Former s. 817.04.
