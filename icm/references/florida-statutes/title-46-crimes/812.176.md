# Fla. Stat. § 812.176 — Rulemaking authority.

**Title:** XLVI — CRIMES
**Chapter:** 815 — COMPUTER-RELATED CRIMES
**Part:** II — ASBESTOS AND SILICA COMPENSATION FAIRNESS ACT
**Section:** 812.176
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The Division of Alcoholic Beverages and Tobacco shall have the power to adopt rules pursuant to chapter 120 as necessary to implement the Convenience Business Security Act. The security measures and training provisions of ss. 812.173 and 812.174 shall meet the requirements of the department as set forth by rule.

**History:** s. 8, ch. 92-103; s. 24, ch. 2021-131.
