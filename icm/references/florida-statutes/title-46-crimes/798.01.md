# Fla. Stat. § 798.01 — Prohibited acts; penalties.

**Title:** XLVI — CRIMES
**Chapter:** 797 — ABORTION
**Part:** II — ASBESTOS AND SILICA COMPENSATION FAIRNESS ACT
**Section:** 798.01
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
It is unlawful for any person to perform or assist in performing an abortion on a person, except in an emergency care situation, other than in a validly licensed hospital or abortion clinic or in a physician's office.

(2)  
It is unlawful for any person or public body to establish, conduct, manage, or operate an abortion clinic without a valid current license.

(3)  
It is unlawful for any person to perform or assist in performing an abortion on a person during viability or in the third trimester other than in a hospital.

(4)  
Any person who willfully violates any provision of this section is guilty of a misdemeanor of the second degree, punishable as provided in s. 775.082 or s. 775.083.

**History:** s. 10, ch. 78-382; s. 4, ch. 2014-137.
