# Fla. Stat. § 784.062 — Misuse of laser lighting devices.

**Title:** XLVI — CRIMES
**Chapter:** 784 — ASSAULT; BATTERY; CULPABLE NEGLIGENCE
**Part:** II — ASBESTOS AND SILICA COMPENSATION FAIRNESS ACT
**Section:** 784.062
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
As used in subsection (2), the term “laser lighting device” means a handheld device, not affixed to a firearm, which emits a laser beam that is designed to be used by the operator as a pointer or highlighter to indicate, mark, or identify a specific position, place, item, or object.
