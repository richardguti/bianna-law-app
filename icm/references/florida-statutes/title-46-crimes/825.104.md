# Fla. Stat. § 825.104 — Knowledge of victim's age.

**Title:** XLVI — CRIMES
**Chapter:** 825 — ABUSE, NEGLECT, AND EXPLOITATION OF ELDERLY PERSONS AND DISABLED ADULTS
**Part:** IV — CREDIT COUNSELING SERVICES
**Section:** 825.104
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

It does not constitute a defense to a prosecution for any violation of this chapter that the accused did not know the age of the victim.

**History:** s. 5, ch. 95-158.
