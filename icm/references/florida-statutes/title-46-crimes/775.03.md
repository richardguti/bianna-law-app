# Fla. Stat. § 775.03 — Benefit of clergy.

**Title:** XLVI — CRIMES
**Chapter:** 775 — GENERAL PENALTIES; REGISTRATION OF CRIMINALS
**Part:** II — ASBESTOS AND SILICA COMPENSATION FAIRNESS ACT
**Section:** 775.03
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The doctrine of benefit of clergy shall have no operation in this state.

**History:** s. 75, Feb. 10, 1832; RS 2371; GS 3196; RGS 5026; CGL 7128.
