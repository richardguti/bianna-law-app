# Fla. Stat. § 790.151 — Using firearm while under the influence of alcoholic beverages, chemical substances, or controlled substances; penalties.

**Title:** XLVI — CRIMES
**Chapter:** 790 — WEAPONS AND FIREARMS
**Part:** II — ASBESTOS AND SILICA COMPENSATION FAIRNESS ACT
**Section:** 790.151
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

——

(1)  
As used in ss. 790.151-790.157, to “use a firearm” means to discharge a firearm or to have a firearm readily accessible for immediate discharge.

(2)  
To a person lawfully defending life or property or performing official duties requiring the discharge of a firearm;

(b)  
If, under the circumstances, the discharge does not pose a reasonably foreseeable risk to life, safety, or property; or

(c)  
To a person who accidentally discharges a firearm.
