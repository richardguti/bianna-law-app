# Fla. Stat. § 817.43 — Exemption.

**Title:** XLVI — CRIMES
**Chapter:** 817 — FRAUDULENT PRACTICES
**Part:** I — FALSE PRETENSES AND FRAUDS, GENERALLY
**Section:** 817.43
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The provisions of s. 817.40 or s. 817.41 shall not apply to any publisher of a newspaper, magazine or other publication, or the owner or operator of a radio or television station, or any other owner or operator of a media primarily devoted to advertising, who publishes, broadcasts, or otherwise disseminates an advertisement in good faith without knowledge of its false, deceptive or misleading character.

**History:** s. 4, ch. 59-301; s. 178, ch. 79-164.
