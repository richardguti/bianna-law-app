# Fla. Stat. § 295.124 — State approving agency for veterans' education and training.

**Title:** XX — VETERANS
**Chapter:** 295 — LAWS RELATING TO VETERANS: GENERAL PROVISIONS
**Part:** XII — FLORIDA OFFICE OF BROADBAND
**Section:** 295.124
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The Department of Veterans' Affairs shall act as the state approving agency for purposes of veterans' education and training, in accordance with 38 U.S.C. s. 3671 and the applicable annual contract between the state and the Federal Government.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 80-140; s. 16, ch. 84-114; s. 24, ch. 88-290; s. 4, ch. 2025-146.
