# Fla. Stat. § 292.16 — Construction of ss. 292.10-292.15.

**Title:** XX — VETERANS
**Chapter:** 292 — VETERANS’ AFFAIRS; SERVICE OFFICERS
**Part:** XII — FLORIDA OFFICE OF BROADBAND
**Section:** 292.16
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Sections 292.10 to 292.15 shall not be construed to be exclusive, but shall be cumulative and supplemental to other acts relating to the same general purposes of this law.

**History:** s. 7, ch. 23017, 1945.
