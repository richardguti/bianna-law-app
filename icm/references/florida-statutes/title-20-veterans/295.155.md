# Fla. Stat. § 295.155 — Military retirement based on longevity.

**Title:** XX — VETERANS
**Chapter:** 295 — LAWS RELATING TO VETERANS: GENERAL PROVISIONS
**Part:** XII — FLORIDA OFFICE OF BROADBAND
**Section:** 295.155
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Military retirement on the basis of longevity does not disqualify a person eligible under s. 295.07 from veterans' employment preference.

**History:** s. 9, ch. 98-33.
