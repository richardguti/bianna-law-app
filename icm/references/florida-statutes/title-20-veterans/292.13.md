# Fla. Stat. § 292.13 — Services to be without charge.

**Title:** XX — VETERANS
**Chapter:** 292 — VETERANS’ AFFAIRS; SERVICE OFFICERS
**Part:** XII — FLORIDA OFFICE OF BROADBAND
**Section:** 292.13
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

All services performed by any county or city veteran service officer employed hereunder for any veteran or his or her dependents shall be rendered without charge to said veteran or said dependents.

**History:** s. 3, ch. 23017, 1945; s. 6, ch. 77-330; s. 122, ch. 79-400.
