# Fla. Stat. § 292.14 — Construction of law.

**Title:** XX — VETERANS
**Chapter:** 292 — VETERANS’ AFFAIRS; SERVICE OFFICERS
**Part:** XII — FLORIDA OFFICE OF BROADBAND
**Section:** 292.14
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

It is the intent and purpose of the Legislature that in construing this law the broadest interpretation be given to the same, in order to carry out and effectuate the purposes of this law.

**History:** s. 5, ch. 23017, 1945.
