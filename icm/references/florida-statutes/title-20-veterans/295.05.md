# Fla. Stat. § 295.05 — Admission; enrollment.

**Title:** XX — VETERANS
**Chapter:** 295 — LAWS RELATING TO VETERANS: GENERAL PROVISIONS
**Part:** XII — FLORIDA OFFICE OF BROADBAND
**Section:** 295.05
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Eligibility for admission is not affected by this chapter, but all students receiving benefits under this chapter shall be enrolled according to the customary rules and requirements of the institution attended.

**History:** s. 5, ch. 20966, 1941; s. 3, ch. 82-93; s. 6, ch. 86-177; s. 4, ch. 2006-244.
