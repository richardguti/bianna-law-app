# Fla. Stat. § 310.171 — Pilots may incorporate themselves.

**Title:** XXII — PORTS AND HARBORS
**Chapter:** 310 — PILOTS, PILOTING, AND PILOTAGE
**Part:** III — VETERANS’ ADULT DAY HEALTH CARE OF FLORIDA ACT
**Section:** 310.171
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Any one or more licensed state pilots may incorporate in the manner provided under part I of chapter 607 or chapter 621.

**History:** s. 2, ch. 75-201; s. 3, ch. 76-168; s. 1, ch. 77-457; s. 16, ch. 78-140; s. 3, ch. 79-9; s. 2, ch. 81-318; ss. 2, 3, ch. 84-185; ss. 10, 11, ch. 86-280; s. 2, ch. 89-262; s. 4, ch. 91-429; s. 39, ch. 2014-209.
