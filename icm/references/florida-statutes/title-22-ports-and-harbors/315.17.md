# Fla. Stat. § 315.17 — Waiver of term limitation for leases entered into by county with another governmental entity for benefit of a seaport.

**Title:** XXII — PORTS AND HARBORS
**Chapter:** 315 — PORT FACILITIES FINANCING
**Part:** III — VETERANS’ ADULT DAY HEALTH CARE OF FLORIDA ACT
**Section:** 315.17
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Notwithstanding any provision of law to the contrary, a lease entered into by a county with another governmental entity for the benefit of a seaport created or authorized under the provisions of s. 125.01 shall not be subject to the 30-year lease term limitation of s. 125.031.

**History:** s. 11, ch. 95-257.
