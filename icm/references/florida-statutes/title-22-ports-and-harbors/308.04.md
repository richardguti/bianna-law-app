# Fla. Stat. § 308.04 — Acting as shipping master without license; penalties.

**Title:** XXII — PORTS AND HARBORS
**Chapter:** 309 — PROTECTION OF PORTS AND HARBORS
**Part:** III — VETERANS’ ADULT DAY HEALTH CARE OF FLORIDA ACT
**Section:** 308.04
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Whoever attempts to exercise the calling of a shipping master, or falsely represents himself or herself as a shipping master, in this state, not having been licensed or appointed by law, shall be guilty of a misdemeanor of the second degree, punishable as provided in s. 775.082 or s. 775.083.

**History:** s. 4, ch. 1750, 1870; RS 2750; GS 3745; RGS 5770; CGL 8000; s. 167, ch. 71-136; s. 277, ch. 95-148.
