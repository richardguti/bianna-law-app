# Fla. Stat. § 310.121 — Application, examination, and biennial fees.

**Title:** XXII — PORTS AND HARBORS
**Chapter:** 310 — PILOTS, PILOTING, AND PILOTAGE
**Part:** III — VETERANS’ ADULT DAY HEALTH CARE OF FLORIDA ACT
**Section:** 310.121
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
The department shall, in accordance with rules set by the board, assess and collect the following fees:
(a)  
A fee not to exceed $300 for each application for licensure as a state pilot or certification as a deputy pilot. This fee shall be nonrefundable.

(b)  
A fee not to exceed $300 for each examination for licensure as a state pilot or certification as a deputy pilot.

(c)  
A fee not to exceed $300 for each examination review.

(2)  
The department shall assess and collect biennially from each licensed state pilot and each certificated deputy pilot a fee, not to exceed $200 in the case of a licensed state pilot or $100 in the case of a certificated deputy pilot, such fees to be set by the board.
