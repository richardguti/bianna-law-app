# Fla. Stat. § 314.04 — Deputies.

**Title:** XXII — PORTS AND HARBORS
**Chapter:** 314 — HARBORMASTERS FOR CERTAIN PORTS
**Part:** III — VETERANS’ ADULT DAY HEALTH CARE OF FLORIDA ACT
**Section:** 314.04
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Any harbormaster so appointed may appoint deputies to assist him or her in the performance of his or her duties, the harbormaster paying them for their services and being responsible for their acts.

**History:** s. 4, ch. 3752, 1887; RS 960; GS 1330; RGS 2500; CGL 3910; s. 289, ch. 95-148.
