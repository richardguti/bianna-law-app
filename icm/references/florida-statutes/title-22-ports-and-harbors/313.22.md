# Fla. Stat. § 313.22 — Definition.

**Title:** XXII — PORTS AND HARBORS
**Chapter:** 313 — HARBORMASTERS FOR PORTS IN GENERAL
**Part:** III — VETERANS’ ADULT DAY HEALTH CARE OF FLORIDA ACT
**Section:** 313.22
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

As used in ss. 313.21-313.24, “port” means a port authority or port district, as defined in s. 315.02, or a port operation as provided in s. 125.012.

**History:** s. 9, ch. 90-54.
