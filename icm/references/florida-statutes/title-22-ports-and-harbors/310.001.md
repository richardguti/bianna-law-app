# Fla. Stat. § 310.001 — Purpose.

**Title:** XXII — PORTS AND HARBORS
**Chapter:** 309 — PROTECTION OF PORTS AND HARBORS
**Part:** III — VETERANS’ ADULT DAY HEALTH CARE OF FLORIDA ACT
**Section:** 310.001
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

——

The Legislature recognizes that the waters, harbors, and ports of the state are important resources, and it is deemed necessary in the interest of public health, safety, and welfare to provide laws regulating the piloting of vessels utilizing the navigable waters of the state in order that such resources, the environment, life, and property may be protected to the fullest extent possible. To that end, it is the legislative intent to regulate pilots, piloting, and pilotage to the full extent of an*Assessment of percentage of gross pilotage.
