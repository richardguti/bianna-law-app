# Fla. Stat. § 315.16 — Liberal construction.

**Title:** XXII — PORTS AND HARBORS
**Chapter:** 315 — PORT FACILITIES FINANCING
**Part:** III — VETERANS’ ADULT DAY HEALTH CARE OF FLORIDA ACT
**Section:** 315.16
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

This law, being necessary for the welfare of the inhabitants of the state, shall be liberally construed to effect the purposes thereof.

**History:** s. 16, ch. 59-411.
