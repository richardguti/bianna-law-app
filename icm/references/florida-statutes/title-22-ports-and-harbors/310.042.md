# Fla. Stat. § 310.042 — Organization of board; meetings.

**Title:** XXII — PORTS AND HARBORS
**Chapter:** 310 — PILOTS, PILOTING, AND PILOTAGE
**Part:** III — VETERANS’ ADULT DAY HEALTH CARE OF FLORIDA ACT
**Section:** 310.042
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
Immediately after the appointment and qualification of its members, the board shall meet and organize. Said board shall annually elect a chair and a vice chair from its membership. Members, while attending official board meetings, shall receive per diem and mileage, as specified in s. 112.061, from their place of residence to the place of meeting and return.

(2)  
Said board shall hold one or more regular meetings each year at some convenient place in the state on such date or dates as the board may select. Special meetingsF.S. 310.0326310.032  
Oath of members of the board.
——

Immediately, and before entering upon the duties of said office, the members of the board shall take the constitutional oath of office and shall file the same with the Department of State; there shall thereupon issue to said member a certificate of his or her appointment.
