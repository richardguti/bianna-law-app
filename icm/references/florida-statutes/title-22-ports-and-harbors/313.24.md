# Fla. Stat. § 313.24 — Construction.

**Title:** XXII — PORTS AND HARBORS
**Chapter:** 314 — HARBORMASTERS FOR CERTAIN PORTS
**Part:** III — VETERANS’ ADULT DAY HEALTH CARE OF FLORIDA ACT
**Section:** 313.24
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Nothing in ss. 313.21-313.24 shall be construed to derogate the duties and responsibilities of a state pilot licensed under chapter 310.

**History:** s. 9, ch. 90-54.
