# Fla. Stat. § 252.9335 — Expense reimbursement under compact.

**Title:** XVII — MILITARY AFFAIRS AND RELATED MATTERS
**Chapter:** 252 — EMERGENCY MANAGEMENT
**Part:** IV — ACCIDENTAL RELEASE PREVENTION AND RISK MANAGEMENT PLANNING
**Section:** 252.9335
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Travel expense reimbursement limits provided in s. 112.061(6) do not apply to an employee of the state or of a political subdivision of the state traveling under the Emergency Management Assistance Compact when such expenses are reimbursed based on the amount agreed upon in an interstate mutual aid request for assistance.

**History:** s. 2, ch. 2015-55.
