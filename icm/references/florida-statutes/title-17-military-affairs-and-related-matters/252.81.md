# Fla. Stat. § 252.81 — Short title.

**Title:** XVII — MILITARY AFFAIRS AND RELATED MATTERS
**Chapter:** 252 — EMERGENCY MANAGEMENT
**Part:** II — FLORIDA EMERGENCY PLANNING AND COMMUNITY RIGHT-TO-KNOW ACT
**Section:** 252.81
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Part II of this chapter, consisting of ss. 252.81-252.90, may be cited as the “Florida Emergency Planning and Community Right-to-Know Act.”

**History:** s. 1, ch. 88-200; s. 1, ch. 92-150; s. 1, ch. 2005-5.
