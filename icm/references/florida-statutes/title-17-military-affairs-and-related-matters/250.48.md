# Fla. Stat. § 250.48 — Leaves of absence.

**Title:** XVII — MILITARY AFFAIRS AND RELATED MATTERS
**Chapter:** 250 — MILITARY AFFAIRS
**Part:** I — MILITARY CODE
**Section:** 250.48
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

A unit of the Florida National Guard may not leave the state without permission of the Governor.

**History:** s. 24, ch. 8502, 1921; CGL 2036; s. 1, ch. 25112, 1949; s. 43, ch. 2003-68.

**Note:** Former s. 250.26.
