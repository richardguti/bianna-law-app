# Fla. Stat. § 250.548 — Injury or death while going to or returning from duty.

**Title:** XVII — MILITARY AFFAIRS AND RELATED MATTERS
**Chapter:** 250 — MILITARY AFFAIRS
**Part:** III — EMERGENCY RELIEF
**Section:** 250.548
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

All benefits to be paid under the foregoing compact shall include any injury or death sustained while going to or returning from such duty.

**History:** s. 3, ch. 93-283.
