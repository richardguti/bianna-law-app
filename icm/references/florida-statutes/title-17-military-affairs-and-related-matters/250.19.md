# Fla. Stat. § 250.19 — Expenses for travel on military business.

**Title:** XVII — MILITARY AFFAIRS AND RELATED MATTERS
**Chapter:** 250 — MILITARY AFFAIRS
**Part:** I — MILITARY CODE
**Section:** 250.19
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Any officer or enlisted person of the Florida National Guard, traveling on military business not with troops, in obedience to the orders of the Governor, must be reimbursed for expenses incurred in the performance of such duties as prescribed by law for state officers and employees.

**History:** s. 28, ch. 8502, 1921; CGL 2040; s. 1, ch. 25112, 1949; s. 103, ch. 95-148; s. 17, ch. 2003-68.

**Note:** Former s. 250.31.
