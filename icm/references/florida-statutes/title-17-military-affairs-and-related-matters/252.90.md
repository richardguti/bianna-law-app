# Fla. Stat. § 252.90 — Commission and committee duties.

**Title:** XVII — MILITARY AFFAIRS AND RELATED MATTERS
**Chapter:** 252 — EMERGENCY MANAGEMENT
**Part:** II — FLORIDA EMERGENCY PLANNING AND COMMUNITY RIGHT-TO-KNOW ACT
**Section:** 252.90
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The commission and the committees shall be state agencies, and the members of the commission and committees shall be officers, employees, or agents of the state for the purposes of s. 768.28.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 88-200.
