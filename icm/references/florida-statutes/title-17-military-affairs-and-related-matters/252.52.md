# Fla. Stat. § 252.52 — Liberality of construction.

**Title:** XVII — MILITARY AFFAIRS AND RELATED MATTERS
**Chapter:** 252 — EMERGENCY MANAGEMENT
**Part:** I — GENERAL PROVISIONS
**Section:** 252.52
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Sections 252.31-252.90 shall be construed liberally in order to effectuate their purposes.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 74-285; s. 33, ch. 83-334; s. 21, ch. 93-211; s. 38, ch. 2001-61.
