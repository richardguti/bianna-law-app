# Fla. Stat. § 250.16 — Authority to incur charge against state.

**Title:** XVII — MILITARY AFFAIRS AND RELATED MATTERS
**Chapter:** 250 — MILITARY AFFAIRS
**Part:** I — MILITARY CODE
**Section:** 250.16
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

An officer of the militia or Florida National Guard may not make any purchases or enter into any contract or agreement for purchases or services as a charge against the state without the authority of the Adjutant General.

**History:** s. 29, ch. 8502, 1921; s. 3, ch. 10185, 1925; CGL 2041; s. 1, ch. 25112, 1949; s. 13, ch. 2003-68.

**Note:** Former s. 250.32.
