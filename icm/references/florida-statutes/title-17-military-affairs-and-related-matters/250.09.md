# Fla. Stat. § 250.09 — Appropriations, property, and equipment.

**Title:** XVII — MILITARY AFFAIRS AND RELATED MATTERS
**Chapter:** 250 — MILITARY AFFAIRS
**Part:** I — MILITARY CODE
**Section:** 250.09
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The Governor of Florida may take all necessary steps to obtain all appropriations, property, and equipment provided by the United States or authorized by law for the use, aid, equipment, benefit, or instruction of the Florida National Guard.

**History:** s. 2, ch. 8502, 1921; CGL 2013; s. 1, ch. 25112, 1949; s. 9, ch. 2003-68.

**Note:** Former s. 250.02.
