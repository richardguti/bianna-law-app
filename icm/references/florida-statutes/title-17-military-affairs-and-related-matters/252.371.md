# Fla. Stat. § 252.371 — Emergency Management, Preparedness, and Assistance Trust Fund.

**Title:** XVII — MILITARY AFFAIRS AND RELATED MATTERS
**Chapter:** 252 — EMERGENCY MANAGEMENT
**Part:** I — GENERAL PROVISIONS
**Section:** 252.371
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

There is created the Emergency Management, Preparedness, and Assistance Trust Fund to be administered by the division.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 93-128; s. 101, ch. 2011-142.
