# Fla. Stat. § 250.481 — Reserve components; employment discrimination prohibited.

**Title:** XVII — MILITARY AFFAIRS AND RELATED MATTERS
**Chapter:** 250 — MILITARY AFFAIRS
**Part:** I — MILITARY CODE
**Section:** 250.481
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Any person who seeks or holds an employment position may not be denied employment or retention in employment, or any promotion or advantage of employment, because of any obligation as a member of a reserve component of the Armed Forces.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 87-381; s. 45, ch. 2003-68.
