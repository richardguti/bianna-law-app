# Fla. Stat. § 90.603 — Disqualification of witness.

**Title:** VII — EVIDENCE
**Chapter:** 90 — EVIDENCE CODE
**Part:** IX — MISCELLANEOUS PROVISIONS
**Section:** 90.603
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

A person is disqualified to testify as a witness when the court determines that the person is:
(1)  
Incapable of expressing himself or herself concerning the matter in such a manner as to be understood, either directly or through interpretation by one who can understand him or her.

(2)  
Incapable of understanding the duty of a witness to tell the truth.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 76-237; s. 1, ch. 77-77; s. 22, ch. 78-361; s. 1, ch. 78-379; s. 482, ch. 95-147.
