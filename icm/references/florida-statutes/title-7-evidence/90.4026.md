# Fla. Stat. § 90.4026 — Statements expressing sympathy; admissibility; definitions.

**Title:** VII — EVIDENCE
**Chapter:** 90 — EVIDENCE CODE
**Part:** IX — MISCELLANEOUS PROVISIONS
**Section:** 90.4026
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
As used in this section:
(a)  
“Accident” means an occurrence resulting in injury or death to one or more persons which is not the result of willful action by a party.

(b)  
“Benevolent gestures” means actions that convey a sense of compassion or commiseration emanating from human impulses.

(c)  
F.S. 90.4025690.4025  
Admissibility of paternity determination in certain criminal prosecutions.
——

If a person less than 18 years of age gives birth to a child and the paternity of that child is established under chapter 742, such evidence of paternity is admissible in a criminal prosecution under ss. 794.011, 794.05, 800.04, and 827.04(3).

**History:** s. 8, ch. 96-215; s. 2, ch. 96-409; s. 27, ch. 99-2.
