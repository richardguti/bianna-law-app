# Fla. Stat. § 92.39 — Evidence of individual's claim against the state in suits between them.

**Title:** VII — EVIDENCE
**Chapter:** 92 — WITNESSES, RECORDS, AND DOCUMENTS
**Part:** IX — MISCELLANEOUS PROVISIONS
**Section:** 92.39
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Comparison of a disputed writing with any writing proved to the satisfaction of the judge to be genuine, shall be permitted to be made by the witnesses; and such writings, and the evidence of witnesses respecting the same, may be submitted to the jury, or to the court in case of a trial by the court, as evidence of the genuineness, or otherwise, of the writing in dispute.

**History:** s. 55, ch. 1096, 1861; RS 1121; GS 1539; RGS 2739; CGL 4411.

**Note:** Former s. 90.20.
