# Fla. Stat. § 90.616 — Exclusion of witnesses.

**Title:** VII — EVIDENCE
**Chapter:** 90 — EVIDENCE CODE
**Part:** IX — MISCELLANEOUS PROVISIONS
**Section:** 90.616
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
At the request of a party the court shall order, or upon its own motion the court may order, witnesses excluded from a proceeding so that they cannot hear the testimony of other witnesses except as provided in subsection (2).

(2)  
A witness may not be excluded if the witness is:
(a)  
A party who is a natural person.

(b)  
In a civil case, an officer or employee of a party that is notF.S. 90.615690.615  
Calling witnesses by the court.
——

(1)  
The court may call witnesses whom all parties may cross-examine.

(2)  
When required by the interests of justice, the court may interrogate witnesses, whether called by the court or by a party.
