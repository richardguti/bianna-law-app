# Fla. Stat. § 90.958 — Functions of court and jury.

**Title:** VII — EVIDENCE
**Chapter:** 90 — EVIDENCE CODE
**Part:** IX — MISCELLANEOUS PROVISIONS
**Section:** 90.958
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
Except as provided in subsection (2), when the admissibility under this chapter of other evidence of the contents of writings, recordings, or photographs depends upon the existence of a preliminary fact, the question as to whether the preliminary fact exists is for the court to determine.

(2)  
The trier of fact shall determine whether:
(a)  
The asserted writing ever existed.

(b)  
Another writing, recording, or photograph produced at the trial is the original.

(c)  
Other evidence of the contents correctly reflects the contents.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 76-237; s. 1, ch. 77-77; s. 22, ch. 78-361; s. 1, ch. 78-379.
