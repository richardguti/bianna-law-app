# Fla. Stat. § 90.605 — Oath or affirmation of witness.

**Title:** VII — EVIDENCE
**Chapter:** 90 — EVIDENCE CODE
**Part:** IX — MISCELLANEOUS PROVISIONS
**Section:** 90.605
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
Before testifying, each witness shall declare that he or she will testify truthfully, by taking an oath or affirmation in substantially the following form: “Do you swear or affirm that the evidence you are about to give will be the truth, the whole truth, and nothing but the truth?” The witness's answer shall be noted in the record.

(2)  
In the court's discretion, a child may testify without taking the oath if the court determines the child understands the duty to tell the truth or the duty not to lie.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 76-237; s. 1, ch. 77-77; s. 22, ch. 78-361; s. 1, ch. 78-379; s. 3, ch. 85-53; s. 484, ch. 95-147.
