# Fla. Stat. § 90.609 — Character of witness as impeachment.

**Title:** VII — EVIDENCE
**Chapter:** 90 — EVIDENCE CODE
**Part:** IX — MISCELLANEOUS PROVISIONS
**Section:** 90.609
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

A party may attack or support the credibility of a witness, including an accused, by evidence in the form of reputation, except that:
(1)  
The evidence may refer only to character relating to truthfulness.

(2)  
Evidence of a truthful character is admissible only after the character of the witness for truthfulness has been attacked by reputation evidence.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 76-237; s. 1, ch. 77-77; ss. 15, 22, ch. 78-361; ss. 1, 2, ch. 78-379.
