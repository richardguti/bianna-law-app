# Fla. Stat. § 90.402 — Admissibility of relevant evidence.

**Title:** VII — EVIDENCE
**Chapter:** 90 — EVIDENCE CODE
**Part:** IX — MISCELLANEOUS PROVISIONS
**Section:** 90.402
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

All relevant evidence is admissible, except as provided bF.S. 90.401690.401  
Definition of relevant evidence.
——

Relevant evidence is evidence tending to prove or disprove a material fact.
