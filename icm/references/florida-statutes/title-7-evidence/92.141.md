# Fla. Stat. § 92.141 — Law enforcement employees; travel expenses; compensation as witness.

**Title:** VII — EVIDENCE
**Chapter:** 92 — WITNESSES, RECORDS, AND DOCUMENTS
**Part:** IX — MISCELLANEOUS PROVISIONS
**Section:** 92.141
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---
