# Fla. Stat. § 121.181 — Effective date.

**Title:** X — PUBLIC OFFICERS, EMPLOYEES, AND RECORDS
**Chapter:** 121 — FLORIDA RETIREMENT SYSTEM
**Part:** I — GENERAL PROVISIONS
**Section:** 121.181
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

For the purpose of activating and implementing the Florida Retirement System this chapter shall take effect on December 1, 1970, but for all other purposes it shall take effect July 1, 1970.

**History:** s. 19, ch. 70-112.
