# Fla. Stat. § 116.08 — County commissioners to furnish books.

**Title:** X — PUBLIC OFFICERS, EMPLOYEES, AND RECORDS
**Chapter:** 116 — POWERS AND DUTIES OF OFFICERS
**Part:** VIII — FIREFIGHTERS
**Section:** 116.08
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The county commissioners shall furnish the books provided for in s. 116.07.

**History:** s. 2, ch. 5176, 1903; GS 815; RGS 411; CGL 476.
