# Fla. Stat. § 119.092 — Registration by federal employer's registration number.

**Title:** X — PUBLIC OFFICERS, EMPLOYEES, AND RECORDS
**Chapter:** 119 — PUBLIC RECORDS
**Part:** II — ONLINE NOTARIZATIONS
**Section:** 119.092
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

——

Each state agency which registers or licenses corporations, partnerships, or other business entities shall include, within its numbering system, the federal employer's identification number of each corporation, partnership, or other business entity registered or licensed by it.
