# Fla. Stat. § 112.42 — Period during which grounds may have occurred.

**Title:** X — PUBLIC OFFICERS, EMPLOYEES, AND RECORDS
**Chapter:** 112 — PUBLIC OFFICERS AND EMPLOYEES: GENERAL PROVISIONS
**Part:** V — SUSPENSION, REMOVAL, OR RETIREMENT OF PUBLIC OFFICERS
**Section:** 112.42
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The Governor may suspend any officer on any constitutional ground for such suspension that occurred during the existing term of the officer or during the next preceding 4 years.

**History:** s. 3, ch. 69-277; s. 1, ch. 71-333.
