# Fla. Stat. § 121.5911 — Disability retirement program; qualified status; rulemaking authority.

**Title:** X — PUBLIC OFFICERS, EMPLOYEES, AND RECORDS
**Chapter:** 121 — FLORIDA RETIREMENT SYSTEM
**Part:** II — FLORIDA RETIREMENT SYSTEM INVESTMENT PLAN
**Section:** 121.5911
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

It is the intent of the Legislature that the disability retirement program for members of the Florida Retirement System Investment Plan meet all applicable requirements of federal law for a qualified plan. The department shall seek a private letter ruling from the Internal Revenue Service on the disability retirement program.
