# Fla. Stat. § 121.192 — State retirement actuary.

**Title:** X — PUBLIC OFFICERS, EMPLOYEES, AND RECORDS
**Chapter:** 121 — FLORIDA RETIREMENT SYSTEM
**Part:** I — GENERAL PROVISIONS
**Section:** 121.192
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The department may employ an actuary. Such actuary shall, together with such other duties as the secretary may assign, be responsible for:
(1)  
Advising the secretary on actuarial matters of the state retirement systems.

(2)  
Making periodic valuations of the retirement systems.

(3)  
Providing actuarial analyses to the Legislature concerning proposed changes in the retirement systems.

(4)  
Assisting the secretary in developing a sound and modern retirement system.

Special acts prohibited.
——

After July 1, 1972, there shall not be enacted any special act or general law of local application which proposes to amend, alter, or contravene the provisions of any state-administered retirement system or any state-supported retirement system established by general law.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 72-388.
