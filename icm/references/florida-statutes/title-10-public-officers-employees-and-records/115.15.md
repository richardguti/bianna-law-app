# Fla. Stat. § 115.15 — Adoption of federal law for employees.

**Title:** X — PUBLIC OFFICERS, EMPLOYEES, AND RECORDS
**Chapter:** 115 — LEAVES OF ABSENCE TO OFFICIALS AND EMPLOYEES
**Part:** VIII — FIREFIGHTERS
**Section:** 115.15
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The provisions of the Uniformed Services Employment and Reemployment Rights Act, chapter 43 of Title 38 U.S.C., shall be applicable in this state, and the refusal of any state, county, or municipal official to comply therewith shall subject him or her to removal from office.

**History:** s. 8, ch. 20718, 1941; s. 740, ch. F.S. 115.146115.14
