# Fla. Stat. § 112.0805 — Employer notice of insurance eligibility to employees who retire.

**Title:** X — PUBLIC OFFICERS, EMPLOYEES, AND RECORDS
**Chapter:** 112 — PUBLIC OFFICERS AND EMPLOYEES: GENERAL PROVISIONS
**Part:** I — CONDITIONS OF EMPLOYMENT; RETIREMENT; TRAVEL EXPENSES
**Section:** 112.0805
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Any employer who provides insurance coverage under s. 110.123 or s. 112.0801 shall notify those employees who retire of their eligibility to participate in either the same group insurance plan or self-insurance plan as provided in ss. 110.123 and 112.0801, or the insurance coverage as provided by this law.

**History:** s. 2, ch. 85-305.
