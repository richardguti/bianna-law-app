# Fla. Stat. § 115.04 — Applicability of ss. 115.01-115.06 to certain officers.

**Title:** X — PUBLIC OFFICERS, EMPLOYEES, AND RECORDS
**Chapter:** 115 — LEAVES OF ABSENCE TO OFFICIALS AND EMPLOYEES
**Part:** VIII — FIREFIGHTERS
**Section:** 115.04
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The provisions of ss. 115.01-115.06 shall only apply to such officers as are now authorized by law to appoint deputies.

**History:** s. 3, ch. 7393, 1917; RGS 402; CGL 467; s. 734, ch. 95-147; s. 19, ch. 98-34.
