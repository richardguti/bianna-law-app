# Fla. Stat. § 121.135 — Annual report to Legislature concerning state-administered retirement systems.

**Title:** X — PUBLIC OFFICERS, EMPLOYEES, AND RECORDS
**Chapter:** 121 — FLORIDA RETIREMENT SYSTEM
**Part:** I — GENERAL PROVISIONS
**Section:** 121.135
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The department shall make to each regular session of the Legislature a written report on the operation and condition of the state-administered retirement systems.

**History:** s. 3, ch. 72-345; s. 2, ch. 77-286; s. 21, ch. 79-183; s. 35, ch. 84-254; s. 58, ch. 92-279; s. 55, ch. 92-326; s. 27, ch. 94-249; s. 41, ch. 99-255.
