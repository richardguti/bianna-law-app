# Fla. Stat. § 116.14 — Receipts required from purchasers of state property.

**Title:** X — PUBLIC OFFICERS, EMPLOYEES, AND RECORDS
**Chapter:** 116 — POWERS AND DUTIES OF OFFICERS
**Part:** VIII — FIREFIGHTERS
**Section:** 116.14
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Upon the sale of any state property by the superintendent and presidents of state institutions as provided by law, they shall take receipt for the same from the purchaser, which receipt shall be forwarded, together with the proceeds of the sale, to the Chief Financial Officer.

**History:** s. 2, ch. 4181, 1893; GS 3494; RGS 5374; CGL 7508; s. 137, ch. 2003-261.
