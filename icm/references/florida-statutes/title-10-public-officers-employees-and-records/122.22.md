# Fla. Stat. § 122.22 — Applicable law.

**Title:** X — PUBLIC OFFICERS, EMPLOYEES, AND RECORDS
**Chapter:** 122 — STATE AND COUNTY OFFICERS AND EMPLOYEES RETIREMENT SYSTEM
**Part:** III — FLORIDA RETIREMENT SYSTEM CONTRIBUTION RATES
**Section:** 122.22
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Sections 122.01-122.12, 122.15, 122.16, 122.18 to 122.20, inclusive, in relation to administration of division B and to duties, rights, privileges and benefits of members of this division under this system, shall apply to said division B and membership therein, except to the extent that the provisions of ss. 122.21-122.24, 122.26 to 122.321, inclusive, may be at variance or in conflict therewith.

**History:** s. 2, ch. 57-382; s. 23, ch. 2014-17.
