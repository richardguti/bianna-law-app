# Fla. Stat. § 120.50 — Exception to application of chapter.

**Title:** X — PUBLIC OFFICERS, EMPLOYEES, AND RECORDS
**Chapter:** 120 — ADMINISTRATIVE PROCEDURE ACT
**Part:** II — ONLINE NOTARIZATIONS
**Section:** 120.50
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

This chapter shall not apply to:
(1)  
The Legislature.

(2)  
The courts.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 74-310; s. 3, ch. 77-468; s. 1, ch. 78-162.
