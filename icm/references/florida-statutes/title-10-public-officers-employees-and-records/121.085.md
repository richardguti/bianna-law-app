# Fla. Stat. § 121.085 — Creditable service.

**Title:** X — PUBLIC OFFICERS, EMPLOYEES, AND RECORDS
**Chapter:** 121 — FLORIDA RETIREMENT SYSTEM
**Part:** I — GENERAL PROVISIONS
**Section:** 121.085
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The following provision shall apply to creditable service as defined in s. 121.021(17): no creditable service which remained unclaimed at retirement may be claimed or purchased after a retirement benefit payment has been cashed or deposited.

**History:** s. 6, ch. 2000-32000-347; s. 8, ch. 2004-347; s. 10, ch. 2007-196; s. 8, ch. 2009-209; s. 14, ch. 2010-5; s. 14, ch. 2011-68.
