# Fla. Stat. § 110.1055 — Rules and rulemaking authority.

**Title:** X — PUBLIC OFFICERS, EMPLOYEES, AND RECORDS
**Chapter:** 110 — STATE EMPLOYMENT
**Part:** I — GENERAL STATE EMPLOYMENT PROVISIONS
**Section:** 110.1055
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The Department of Management Services shall have authority to adopt rules as necessary to effectuate the provisions of this chapter.

**History:** s. 42, ch. 2001-43; s. 30, ch. 2012-116.
