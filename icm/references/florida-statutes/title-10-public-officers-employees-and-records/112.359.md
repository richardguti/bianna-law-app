# Fla. Stat. § 112.359 — Benefits exempt from taxes and execution.

**Title:** X — PUBLIC OFFICERS, EMPLOYEES, AND RECORDS
**Chapter:** 112 — PUBLIC OFFICERS AND EMPLOYEES: GENERAL PROVISIONS
**Part:** IV — SUPPLEMENTAL RETIREMENT ACT FOR RETIRED MEMBERS OF STATE RETIREMENT SYSTEMS
**Section:** 112.359
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The benefits provided for any person under the provisions of this act are exempt from any state, county or municipal tax of the state and shall not be subject to assignment, execution or attachment or to any legal process whatsoever.

**History:** s. 9, ch. 67-276.
