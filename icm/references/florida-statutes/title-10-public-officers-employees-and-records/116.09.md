# Fla. Stat. § 116.09 — Penalty for failure.

**Title:** X — PUBLIC OFFICERS, EMPLOYEES, AND RECORDS
**Chapter:** 116 — POWERS AND DUTIES OF OFFICERS
**Part:** VIII — FIREFIGHTERS
**Section:** 116.09
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Any officer who shall neglect or refuse to comply with the duties imposed by s. 116.07 shall be subject to suspension from office by the Governor.

**History:** s. 4, ch. 5176, 1903; GS 817; RGS 412; CGL 477.
