# Fla. Stat. § 117.051 — Civil cause of action.

**Title:** X — PUBLIC OFFICERS, EMPLOYEES, AND RECORDS
**Chapter:** 117 — NOTARIES PUBLIC
**Part:** I — GENERAL PROVISIONS
**Section:** 117.051
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

——

A person aggrieved by a violation of s. 117.05(10) or (11) has a civil cause of action against the entity violating such provision for, where appropriate:
(1)  
Declaratory or injunctive relief.

(2)  
Actual damages.
