# Fla. Stat. § 122.21 — Activation of division B.

**Title:** X — PUBLIC OFFICERS, EMPLOYEES, AND RECORDS
**Chapter:** 122 — STATE AND COUNTY OFFICERS AND EMPLOYEES RETIREMENT SYSTEM
**Part:** III — FLORIDA RETIREMENT SYSTEM CONTRIBUTION RATES
**Section:** 122.21
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Sections 122.21-122.24, 122.26 to 122.321, inclusive, shall control with respect to division B of this system and membership therein, and shall prescribe the method for activating such division.

**History:** s. 2, ch. 57-382.
