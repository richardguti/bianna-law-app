# Fla. Stat. § 117.04 — Acknowledgments.

**Title:** X — PUBLIC OFFICERS, EMPLOYEES, AND RECORDS
**Chapter:** 117 — NOTARIES PUBLIC
**Part:** I — GENERAL PROVISIONS
**Section:** 117.04
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

A notary public is authorized to take the acknowledgments of deeds and other instruments of writing for record, as fully as other officers of this state.

**History:** s. 2, ch. 1127, 1860; RS 220; GS 305; RGS 416; CGL 482; s. 20, ch. 73-334; s. 8, ch. 81-260; s. 3, ch. 91-291; s. 3, ch. 93-62; s. 3, ch. 98-246.
