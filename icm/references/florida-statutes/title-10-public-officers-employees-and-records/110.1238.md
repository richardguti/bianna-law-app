# Fla. Stat. § 110.1238 — State group health insurance plans; refunds with respect to overcharges by providers.

**Title:** X — PUBLIC OFFICERS, EMPLOYEES, AND RECORDS
**Chapter:** 110 — STATE EMPLOYMENT
**Part:** I — GENERAL STATE EMPLOYMENT PROVISIONS
**Section:** 110.1238
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

A participant in a state group health insurance plan who discovers that he or she was overcharged by a health care provider shall receive a refund of 50 percent of any amount recovered as a result of such overcharge, up to a maximum of $1,000.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 83-148; s. 1, ch. 83-292; s. 662, ch. 95-147; s. 4, ch. 99-255.

**Note:** Former s. 110.1233.
