# Fla. Stat. § 121.012 — Inclusive provisions.

**Title:** X — PUBLIC OFFICERS, EMPLOYEES, AND RECORDS
**Chapter:** 121 — FLORIDA RETIREMENT SYSTEM
**Part:** I — GENERAL PROVISIONS
**Section:** 121.012
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The provisions of part I of this chapter shall be applicable to parts II and III to the extent such provisions are not inconsistent with, or duplicative of, the provisions of parts II and III.
