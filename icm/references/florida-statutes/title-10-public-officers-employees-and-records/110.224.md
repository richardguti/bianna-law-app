# Fla. Stat. § 110.224 — Public employee performance evaluation system.

**Title:** X — PUBLIC OFFICERS, EMPLOYEES, AND RECORDS
**Chapter:** 110 — STATE EMPLOYMENT
**Part:** II — CAREER SERVICE SYSTEM
**Section:** 110.224
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

——

A public employee performance evaluation system shall be established as a basis for evaluating and improving the performance of the state's workforce, to inform employees of strong and weak points in the employee's performance, to identify training needs, athe employee shall be entitled to accumulate all benefits granted under paid leave status.
