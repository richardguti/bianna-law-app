# Fla. Stat. § 111.075 — Elected officials; prohibition concerning certain committees.

**Title:** X — PUBLIC OFFICERS, EMPLOYEES, AND RECORDS
**Chapter:** 111 — PUBLIC OFFICERS: GENERAL PROVISIONS
**Part:** V — SELECTED EXEMPT SERVICE SYSTEM
**Section:** 111.075
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Elected officials are prohibited from being employed by, or acting as a consultant for compensation to, a political committee.

**History:** s. 26, ch. 89-256; s. 28, ch. 2013-37.
