# Fla. Stat. § 110.1128 — Selective service registration.

**Title:** X — PUBLIC OFFICERS, EMPLOYEES, AND RECORDS
**Chapter:** 110 — STATE EMPLOYMENT
**Part:** I — GENERAL STATE EMPLOYMENT PROVISIONS
**Section:** 110.1128
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

——

(1)  
No person who is required to register with the Selective Service System under the Military Selective Service Act, 50 U.S.C. App. 453, may be offered employment by this state in an authorized position, as defined in s. 216.011, without proof of such registration.

(2)  
No person who has failed to register as required by the Military Selective Service Act, 50 U.S.C. /ch. 93-156; s. 16, ch. 94-134; s. 16, ch. 94-135; s. 1398, ch. 95-147; s. 10, ch. 95-158; s. 33, ch. 95-228; s. 120, ch. 95-418; s. 4, ch. 96-268; s. 3, ch. 96-399; ss. 28, 29, ch. 96-406; s. 6, ch. 97-296; s. 52, ch. 2000-349; s. 6, ch. 2001-43; s. 112, ch. 2003-261; s. 36, ch. 2004-267; s. 2, ch. 2012-215; s. 5, ch. 2016-78; s. 2, ch. 2016-162.
