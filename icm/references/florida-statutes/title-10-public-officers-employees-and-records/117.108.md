# Fla. Stat. § 117.108 — Validity of acts, seals, and certificates prior to January 1, 1995.

**Title:** X — PUBLIC OFFICERS, EMPLOYEES, AND RECORDS
**Chapter:** 117 — NOTARIES PUBLIC
**Part:** II — ONLINE NOTARIZATIONS
**Section:** 117.108
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

A notarial act performed, a notarial certificate signed, or a notarial seal used by any notary public before January 1, 1995, which would have been valid under the laws in effect in this state on January 1, 1991, is valid.

**History:** s. 5, ch. 93-62.
