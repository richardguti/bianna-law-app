# Fla. Stat. § 122.31 — Future amendments.

**Title:** X — PUBLIC OFFICERS, EMPLOYEES, AND RECORDS
**Chapter:** 122 — STATE AND COUNTY OFFICERS AND EMPLOYEES RETIREMENT SYSTEM
**Part:** III — FLORIDA RETIREMENT SYSTEM CONTRIBUTION RATES
**Section:** 122.31
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Where in this law reference is made to state and federal laws, it shall be understood that such references are intended to include such laws as they now exist or may hereafter be amended.

**History:** s. 2, ch. 57-382.
