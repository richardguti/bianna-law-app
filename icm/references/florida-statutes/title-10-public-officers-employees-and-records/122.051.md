# Fla. Stat. § 122.051 — Eligibility of retired state employee for Legislature; compensation.

**Title:** X — PUBLIC OFFICERS, EMPLOYEES, AND RECORDS
**Chapter:** 122 — STATE AND COUNTY OFFICERS AND EMPLOYEES RETIREMENT SYSTEM
**Part:** III — FLORIDA RETIREMENT SYSTEM CONTRIBUTION RATES
**Section:** 122.051
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

——

Any retired state employee who is presently drawing retirement benefits under any state retirement system may, as any other citizen, serve in the Legislature without affecting in any way his or her retirement status or the receipt of retirement funds while a member of the Legislature. Such person may be paid the salary ofNher total service, $100 per month. In computing the retirement benefit for such members of the Legislature, the average final compensation shall be the amount that a 6-percent contribution would have applied to produce the contribution required above.
