# Fla. Stat. § 110.211 — Recruitment.

**Title:** X — PUBLIC OFFICERS, EMPLOYEES, AND RECORDS
**Chapter:** 110 — STATE EMPLOYMENT
**Part:** II — CAREER SERVICE SYSTEM
**Section:** 110.211
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
Recruiting shall be planned and carried out in a manner that assures open competition based upon current and projected employing agency needs, taking into consideration the number and types of positions to be filled and the labor market conditions, with special emphasis placed on recruiting efforts to attract minorities, women, or other groups that are underrepresented in the workforce of the employing agency.

(2)  
(4)  
The employing agency shall be responsible for the day-to-day administration of the shared-employment program under the rules promulgated by the department.

(5)  
The department shall adopt any rules necessary to implement the provisions of this section; however, such rules shall be approved by the Administration Commission prior to their adoption by the department.
