# Fla. Stat. § 116.02 — Payment of commissions on unremitted funds prohibited; penalty.

**Title:** X — PUBLIC OFFICERS, EMPLOYEES, AND RECORDS
**Chapter:** 116 — POWERS AND DUTIES OF OFFICERS
**Part:** VIII — FIREFIGHTERS
**Section:** 116.02
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

It is unlawful for any state or county officer or any board of county officers, required to audit the accounts of officers under the laws of this state, to approve or pay any commissions on funds collected and not paid over as required by s. 116.01 and any officer violating the provisions of this section, shall be guilty of a misdemeanor of the first degree, punishable as provided in s. 775.082 or s. 775.083.

**History:** s. 3, ch. 6205, 1911; RGS 5328; CGL 7461; s. 66, ch. 71-136.
