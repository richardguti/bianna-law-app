# Fla. Stat. § 112.26 — Definitions.

**Title:** X — PUBLIC OFFICERS, EMPLOYEES, AND RECORDS
**Chapter:** 112 — PUBLIC OFFICERS AND EMPLOYEES: GENERAL PROVISIONS
**Part:** II — INTERCHANGE OF PERSONNEL BETWEEN GOVERNMENTS
**Section:** 112.26
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

For the purposes of this part, the following words and phrases have the meanings ascribed to them in this section:
(1)  
“Receiving agency” means any department or agency of the Federal Government or a state government which receives an employee of another government under this part.

(2)  
“Sending agency” means any department or agency of the Federal Government or a state government which sends any employee thereof to another government agency under this part.

**History:** s. 2, ch. 65-524; s. 34, ch. 2023-8.
