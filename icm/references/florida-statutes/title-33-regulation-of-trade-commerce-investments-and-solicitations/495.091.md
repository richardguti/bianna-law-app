# Fla. Stat. § 495.091 — Records.

**Title:** XXXIII — REGULATION OF TRADE, COMMERCE, INVESTMENTS, AND SOLICITATIONS
**Chapter:** 495 — REGISTRATION AND PROTECTION OF TRADEMARKS
**Part:** III — MORTGAGE LENDERS
**Section:** 495.091
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The department shall keep for public examination a record of all marks registered or renewed under this chapter, including all documents recorded under s. 495.081.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 67-58; ss. 10, 35, ch. 69-106; s. 11, ch. 2006-191.
