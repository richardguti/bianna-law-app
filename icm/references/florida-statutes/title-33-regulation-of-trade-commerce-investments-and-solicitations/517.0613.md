# Fla. Stat. § 517.0613 — Failure to comply with a securities registration exemption.

**Title:** XXXIII — REGULATION OF TRADE, COMMERCE, INVESTMENTS, AND SOLICITATIONS
**Chapter:** 517 — SECURITIES TRANSACTIONS
**Part:** II — MEMBERSHIP CAMPGROUNDS
**Section:** 517.0613
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
Failure to meet the requirements for any exemption from securities registration does not preclude the issuer from claiming the availability of any other applicable state or federal exemption.

(2)  
The exemptions created under ss. 517.061, 517.0611, and 517.0612 are not available to an issuer for any transaction or series of transactions that, although in technical compliance with the applicable provisions, is part of a plan or scheme to evade the registration provisions of s. 517.07, and registration under s. 517.07 is required in connection with such transactions.
