# Fla. Stat. § 526.50 — Definition of terms.

As used in this part:
(1)  
“Brake fluid” means the fluid intended for use as the liquid medium through which force is transmitted in the hydraulic brake system of a vehicle operated upon the highways.

(2)  
“Department” means the Department of Agriculture and Consumer Services.
