# Fla. Stat. § 499.032 — Phenylalanine; prescription required.

**Title:** XXXIII — REGULATION OF TRADE, COMMERCE, INVESTMENTS, AND SOLICITATIONS
**Chapter:** 499 — FLORIDA DRUG AND COSMETIC ACT
**Part:** I — DRUGS; DEVICES; COSMETICS; HOUSEHOLD PRODUCTS
**Section:** 499.032
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Phenylalanine restricted formula is declared to be a prescription drug and may be dispensed only upon the prescription of a practitioner authorized by law to prescribe prescription drugs.

**History:** s. 34, ch. 82-225; s. 1, ch. 83-265; ss. 31, 52, ch. 92-69; s. 23, ch. 2008-207.
