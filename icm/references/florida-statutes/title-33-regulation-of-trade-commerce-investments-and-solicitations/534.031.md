# Fla. Stat. § 534.031 — Certified copies of marks and brands.

**Title:** XXXIII — REGULATION OF TRADE, COMMERCE, INVESTMENTS, AND SOLICITATIONS
**Chapter:** 534 — LIVESTOCK; MARKS AND BRANDS
**Part:** II — SALE OF BRAKE FLUID
**Section:** 534.031
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Certified copies of recorded marks and brands shall be furnished by the department when and as requested and it shall charge and collect $2 for each certificate. Such certificates shall be admissible in evidence in all courts.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 65-357; ss. 14, 35, ch. 69-106; s. 1, ch. 75-37.
