# Fla. Stat. § 504.013 — Penalties.

**Title:** XXXIII — REGULATION OF TRADE, COMMERCE, INVESTMENTS, AND SOLICITATIONS
**Chapter:** 504 — SPECIALIZED AGRICULTURAL PRODUCT LABELING
**Part:** VIII — PATENT TROLL PREVENTION ACT
**Section:** 504.013
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Any person, firm, or corporation engaged in the business of the retail vending of fresh fruits, fresh vegetables, bee pollen, or honey who willfully and knowingly removes any labels or identifying marks from fruits, vegetables, bee pollen, or honey so labeled is guilty of a noncriminal violation as defined in s. 775.08(3) and upon conviction shall be punished as provided in s. 775.082(5) by a civil fine of not more than $500.

**History:** s. 4, ch. 79-121; s. 3, ch. 83-14.
