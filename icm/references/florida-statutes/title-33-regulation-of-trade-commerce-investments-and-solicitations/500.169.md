# Fla. Stat. § 500.169 — Enforcement of federal act.

**Title:** XXXIII — REGULATION OF TRADE, COMMERCE, INVESTMENTS, AND SOLICITATIONS
**Chapter:** 500 — FOOD PRODUCTS
**Part:** III — MEDICAL GAS
**Section:** 500.169
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The department may exercise the enforcement powers granted by, and subject to the limitations of, 21 U.S.C. s. 337(b).

**History:** s. 12, ch. 59-302; s. 23, ch. 82-225; s. 15, ch. 87-388; s. 16, ch. 92-180.

**Note:** Former s. 500.40.
