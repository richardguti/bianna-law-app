# Fla. Stat. § 548.07 — SF.S. 548.0696548.069

**Title:** XXXIII — REGULATION OF TRADE, COMMERCE, INVESTMENTS, AND SOLICITATIONS
**Chapter:** 548 — PUGILISTIC EXHIBITIONS
**Part:** II — FLORIDA CHOICE ACT
**Section:** 548.07
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

A person under 16 years of age may not attend a match unless such person is accompanied by an adult or unless the exhibition is of amateur status.

**History:** ss. 2, 4, ch. 84-246; s. 4, ch. 91-429.
