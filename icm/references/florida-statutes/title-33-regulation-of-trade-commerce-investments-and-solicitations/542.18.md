# Fla. Stat. § 542.18 — Restraint of trade or commerce.

**Title:** XXXIII — REGULATION OF TRADE, COMMERCE, INVESTMENTS, AND SOLICITATIONS
**Chapter:** 542 — COMBINATIONS RESTRICTING TRADE OR COMMERCE
**Part:** I — FLORIDA ANTITRUST ACT OF 1980
**Section:** 542.18
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Every contract, combination, or conspiracy in restraint of trade or commerce in this state is unlawful.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 80-28.
