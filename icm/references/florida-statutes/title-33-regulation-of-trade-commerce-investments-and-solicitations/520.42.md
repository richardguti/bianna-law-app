# Fla. Stat. § 520.42 — Construction.

**Title:** XXXIII — REGULATION OF TRADE, COMMERCE, INVESTMENTS, AND SOLICITATIONS
**Chapter:** 520 — RETAIL INSTALLMENT SALES
**Part:** III — RETAIL INSTALLMENT SALES
**Section:** 520.42
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Nothing in this act shall be construed to affect any transaction covered by chapter 516 and part I of this chapter or any transaction by any banking institution, state or federal savings and loan association, or credit union.

**History:** s. 14, ch. 59-414; s. 3, ch. 76-168; s. 1, ch. 77-457; ss. 11, 21, ch. 80-256; s. 2, ch. 81-318; ss. 35, 36, ch. 90-103; s. 4, ch. 91-429.
