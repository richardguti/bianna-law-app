# Fla. Stat. § 539.002 — Applicability.

**Title:** XXXIII — REGULATION OF TRADE, COMMERCE, INVESTMENTS, AND SOLICITATIONS
**Chapter:** 539 — PAWNBROKING
**Part:** III — MAIL-IN SECONDHAND PRECIOUS METALS DEALERS
**Section:** 539.002
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Chapter 538 does not apply to pawnbrokers licensed under the Florida Pawnbroking Act. This act does not abrogate any provision of chapters 671-680.

**History:** s. 2, ch. 96-242.
