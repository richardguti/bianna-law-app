# Fla. Stat. § 501.052 — Home solicitation sale; enforcement authority; injunctive relief.

**Title:** XXXIII — REGULATION OF TRADE, COMMERCE, INVESTMENTS, AND SOLICITATIONS
**Chapter:** 501 — CONSUMER PROTECTION
**Part:** I — GENERAL PROVISIONS
**Section:** 501.052
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The Attorney General or state attorney may institute proceedings to enjoin any person found to be violating the provisions of ss. 501.021-501.055.

**History:** s. 2, ch. 77-350; s. 12, ch. 97-250.
