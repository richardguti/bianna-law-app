# Fla. Stat. § 520.155 — Commercial transactions exempt.

**Title:** XXXIII — REGULATION OF TRADE, COMMERCE, INVESTMENTS, AND SOLICITATIONS
**Chapter:** 520 — RETAIL INSTALLMENT SALES
**Part:** I — MOTOR VEHICLE SALES FINANCE; VEHICLE VALUATION PROTECTION AGREEMENTS
**Section:** 520.155
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Sections 520.154 and 520.156 do not apply to vehicle value protection agreements offered in connection with a commercial transaction.

**History:** s. 7, ch. 2024-142.
