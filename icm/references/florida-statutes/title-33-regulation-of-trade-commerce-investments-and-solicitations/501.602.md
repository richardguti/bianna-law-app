# Fla. Stat. § 501.602 — Purpose.

**Title:** XXXIII — REGULATION OF TRADE, COMMERCE, INVESTMENTS, AND SOLICITATIONS
**Chapter:** 501 — CONSUMER PROTECTION
**Part:** IV — FLORIDA TELEMARKETING ACT
**Section:** 501.602
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The provisions of this part shall be construed liberally to promote the general welfare of the public and the integrity of the telemarketing industry.

**History:** ss. 1, 2, ch. 91-237; s. 4, ch. 91-429.
