# Fla. Stat. § 538.15 — Certain acts and practices prohibited.

**Title:** XXXIII — REGULATION OF TRADE, COMMERCE, INVESTMENTS, AND SOLICITATIONS
**Chapter:** 538 — SECONDHAND DEALERS AND SECONDARY METALS RECYCLERS
**Part:** I — SECONDHAND DEALERS
**Section:** 538.15
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

It is unlawful for a secondhand dealer or any employee thereof to do or allow any of the following acts:
(1)  
Knowingly make a transaction with:
(a)  
Any person who is under the influence of drugs or alcohol when such condition is visible or apparent;

(b)  
Any person under the age of 18 years; or

(c)  
Any person using a name other than her or his own name or the registered name of her or his business.
