# Fla. Stat. § 496.4255 — Public airport not required to grant permit or access.

**Title:** XXXIII — REGULATION OF TRADE, COMMERCE, INVESTMENTS, AND SOLICITATIONS
**Chapter:** 496 — SOLICITATION OF FUNDS
**Part:** III — MORTGAGE LENDERS
**Section:** 496.4255
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Notwithstanding s. 496.425, a governmental entity or authority that owns or operates an airport, as defined in s. 332.01, may not be required to issue a permit or grant any person access to airport property for the purpose of soliciting funds.

**History:** s. 60, ch. 96-323.
