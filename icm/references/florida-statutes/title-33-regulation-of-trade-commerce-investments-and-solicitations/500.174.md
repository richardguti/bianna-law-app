# Fla. Stat. § 500.174 — Seizure;F.S. 500.1736500.173

**Title:** XXXIII — REGULATION OF TRADE, COMMERCE, INVESTMENTS, AND SOLICITATIONS
**Chapter:** 500 — FOOD PRODUCTS
**Part:** III — MEDICAL GAS
**Section:** 500.174
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Any article of food that is adulterated or misbranded under the provisions of this chapter is subject to seizure and condemnation by the department or by its duly authorized agents designated for that purpose in regard to foods.

**History:** s. 13, ch. 59-302; s. 3, ch. 61-456; ss. 14, 19, 35, ch. 69-106; s. 434, ch. 77-147; s. 24, ch. 82-225; s. 17, ch. 87-388.

**Note:** Former s. 500.41.
