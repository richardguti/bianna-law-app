# Fla. Stat. § 501.626 — Rulemaking power.

**Title:** XXXIII — REGULATION OF TRADE, COMMERCE, INVESTMENTS, AND SOLICITATIONS
**Chapter:** 501 — CONSUMER PROTECTION
**Part:** IV — FLORIDA TELEMARKETING ACT
**Section:** 501.626
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The department has the authority to adopt rules pursuant to ss. 120.536(1) and 120.54 to implement this part.

**History:** ss. 1, 2, ch. 91-237; s. 4, ch. 91-429; s. 17, ch. 97-250; s. 173, ch. 98-200.
