# Fla. Stat. § 553.508 — Architectural barrier removal.

**Title:** XXXIII — REGULATION OF TRADE, COMMERCE, INVESTMENTS, AND SOLICITATIONS
**Chapter:** 553 — BUILDING CONSTRUCTION STANDARDS
**Part:** II — ACCESSIBILITY BY HANDICAPPED PERSONS
**Section:** 553.508
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Removal of architectural barriers, pursuant to 28 C.F.R. s. 36.304, from buildings, structures, or facilities to which this act applies shall comply with ss. 553.501-553.513 unless compliance would render the removal not readily achievable. In no instance shall the removal of an architectural barrier create a significant risk to the health or safety of an individual with a disability or others.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 93-183.
