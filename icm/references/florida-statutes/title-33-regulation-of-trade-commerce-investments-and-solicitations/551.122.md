# Fla. Stat. § 551.122 — Rulemaking.

**Title:** XXXIII — REGULATION OF TRADE, COMMERCE, INVESTMENTS, AND SOLICITATIONS
**Chapter:** 551 — SLOT MACHINES
**Part:** II — FLORIDA CHOICE ACT
**Section:** 551.122
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The commission may adopt rules pursuant to ss. 120.536(1) and 120.54 to administer the provisions of this chapter.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 2005-362; s. 61, ch. 2022-7.
