# Fla. Stat. § 550.6315 — Applicability of s. 565.02(5) to guest tracks.

**Title:** XXXIII — REGULATION OF TRADE, COMMERCE, INVESTMENTS, AND SOLICITATIONS
**Chapter:** 550 — PARI-MUTUEL WAGERING
**Part:** II — FLORIDA CHOICE ACT
**Section:** 550.6315
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The provisions of s. 565.02(5) apply to any guest track.

**History:** s. 50, ch. 92-348.
