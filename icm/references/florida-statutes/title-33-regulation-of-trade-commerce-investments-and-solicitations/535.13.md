# Fla. Stat. § 535.13 — Inapplicability to horseracing.

**Title:** XXXIII — REGULATION OF TRADE, COMMERCE, INVESTMENTS, AND SOLICITATIONS
**Chapter:** 535 — HORSE SALES, SHOWS, AND EXHIBITIONS
**Part:** II — SALE OF BRAKE FLUID
**Section:** 535.13
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

No provision contained in s. 535.11 or s. 535.12 affects in any way existing statutes governing horseracing.

**History:** s. 4, ch. 71-166; s. 2, ch. 81-318; ss. 5, 10, 11, ch. 83-13; s. 2, ch. 93-28.
