# Fla. Stat. § 551.119 — Caterer's license.

**Title:** XXXIII — REGULATION OF TRADE, COMMERCE, INVESTMENTS, AND SOLICITATIONS
**Chapter:** 551 — SLOT MACHINES
**Part:** II — FLORIDA CHOICE ACT
**Section:** 551.119
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

A slot machine licensee is entitled to a caterer's license pursuant to s. 565.02 on days on which the pari-mutuel facility is open to the public for slot machine game play as authorized by this chapter.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 2005-362.
