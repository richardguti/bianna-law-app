# Fla. Stat. § 552.181 — Conduct of hearings.

**Title:** XXXIII — REGULATION OF TRADE, COMMERCE, INVESTMENTS, AND SOLICITATIONS
**Chapter:** 552 — MANUFACTURE, DISTRIBUTION, AND USE OF EXPLOSIVES
**Part:** II — FLORIDA CHOICE ACT
**Section:** 552.181
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

All hearings shall be conducted in accordance with the provisions of chapter 120.

**History:** s. 12, ch. 65-59; ss. 13, 35, ch. 69-106; s. 16, ch. 77-84.
