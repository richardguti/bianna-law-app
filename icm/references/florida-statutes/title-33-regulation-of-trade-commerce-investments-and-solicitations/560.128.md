# Fla. Stat. § 560.128 — Customer contacts; license display.

**Title:** XXXIII — REGULATION OF TRADE, COMMERCE, INVESTMENTS, AND SOLICITATIONS
**Chapter:** 560 — MONEY SERVICES BUSINESSES
**Part:** I — GENERAL PROVISIONS
**Section:** 560.128
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
A money services business and authorized vendor must provide each customer with a toll-free telephone number for the purpose of contacting the money services business or authorized vendor or, in lieu of a toll-free telephone number, the address and telephone number of the office may be provided.

(2)  
The commission may by rule require a licensee to display its license at each location where the licensee engages in the activities autatml xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml">F.S. 560.1276560.127  
Control of a money services business.
——

A person has a controlling interest in a money services business if the person:
(1)  
Possesses the power, directly or indirectly, to direct the management or policies of the money services business, whether through ownership, by contract, or otherwise;

(2)  
Directly or indirectly may vote 25 percent or more of a class of a voting security or sell or direct the sale of 25 percent or more of a class of voting securities; or

(3)  
In the case of a partnership, may receive upon dissolution or has contributed 25 percent or more of the capital.
