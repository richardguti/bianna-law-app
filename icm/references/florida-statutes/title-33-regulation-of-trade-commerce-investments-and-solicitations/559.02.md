# Fla. Stat. § 559.02 — Fraud; false representation and lotteries prohibited.

**Title:** XXXIII — REGULATION OF TRADE, COMMERCE, INVESTMENTS, AND SOLICITATIONS
**Chapter:** 559 — REGULATION OF TRADE, COMMERCE, AND INVESTMENTS, GENERALLY
**Part:** I — TRADING STAMPS
**Section:** 559.02
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

No trading stamp company shall commit any fraud or shall make any false representation or shall resort to any lottery, in distributing or redeeming trading stamps in this state.

**History:** s. 2, ch. 59-311.
