# Fla. Stat. § 559.3906 — Violations of act.

**Title:** XXXIII — REGULATION OF TRADE, COMMERCE, INVESTMENTS, AND SOLICITATIONS
**Chapter:** 559 — REGULATION OF TRADE, COMMERCE, AND INVESTMENTS, GENERALLY
**Part:** IV — BUYING SERVICES
**Section:** 559.3906
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

A violation of this act shall be deemed an unfair or deceptive trade practice within the meaning of part II of chapter 501. Violators shall be subject to the penalties and remedies provided therein.
