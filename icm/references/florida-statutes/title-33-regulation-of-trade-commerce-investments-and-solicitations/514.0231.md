# Fla. Stat. § 514.0231 — Advisory committee to oversee sampling of beach waters.

**Title:** XXXIII — REGULATION OF TRADE, COMMERCE, INVESTMENTS, AND SOLICITATIONS
**Chapter:** 514 — PUBLIC SWIMMING AND BATHING FACILITIES
**Part:** II — MEMBERSHIP CAMPGROUNDS
**Section:** 514.0231
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The Department of Health shall form an interagency technical advisory committee to oversee the performance of the study required in s. 514.023 and to advise it in rulemaking pertaining to standards for public bathing places along the coastal and intracoastal beaches and shores of the state. Membership on the committee shall consist of equal numbers of staff of the Department of Health and the Department of Environmental Protection with expertise in the subject matter of the study. Members shall be appointed by the State Surgeon General and the Secretary of Environmental Protection. The committee shall be chaired by a representative from the Department of Health.
