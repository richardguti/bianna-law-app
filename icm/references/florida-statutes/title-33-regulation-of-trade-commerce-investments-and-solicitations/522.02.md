# Fla. Stat. § 522.02 — Persons presumed to be doing business in state.

**Title:** XXXIII — REGULATION OF TRADE, COMMERCE, INVESTMENTS, AND SOLICITATIONS
**Chapter:** 522 — COMMISSION MERCHANTS
**Part:** VI — DEPARTMENT REGULATION OF SALES AND FINANCE
**Section:** 522.02
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Any person maintaining an office or soliciting personally or by agent such business in this state shall be presumed to be doing business in this state.

**History:** s. 2, ch. 6235, 1911; RGS 4939; CGL 7026.
