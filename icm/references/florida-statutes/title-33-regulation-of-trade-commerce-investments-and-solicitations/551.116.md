# Fla. Stat. § 551.116 — Days and hours of operation.

**Title:** XXXIII — REGULATION OF TRADE, COMMERCE, INVESTMENTS, AND SOLICITATIONS
**Chapter:** 551 — SLOT MACHINES
**Part:** II — FLORIDA CHOICE ACT
**Section:** 551.116
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Slot machine gaming areas may be open 24 hours per day throughout the year.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 2005-362; s. 9, ch. 2007-252; s. 32, ch. 2021-271.
