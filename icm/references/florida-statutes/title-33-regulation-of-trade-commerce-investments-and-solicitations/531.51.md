# Fla. Stat. § 531.51 — Injunction.

**Title:** XXXIII — REGULATION OF TRADE, COMMERCE, INVESTMENTS, AND SOLICITATIONS
**Chapter:** 531 — WEIGHTS, MEASURES, AND STANDARDS
**Part:** II — SALE OF BRAKE FLUID
**Section:** 531.51
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The department is authorized, without bond, to apply to any court of competent jurisdiction for a temporary or permanent injunction restraining any person from violating any provision of this chapter.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 72-101.
