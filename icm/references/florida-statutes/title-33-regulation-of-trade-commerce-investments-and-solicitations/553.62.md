# Fla. Stat. § 553.62 — State standard.

**Title:** XXXIII — REGULATION OF TRADE, COMMERCE, INVESTMENTS, AND SOLICITATIONS
**Chapter:** 553 — BUILDING CONSTRUCTION STANDARDS
**Part:** II — ACCESSIBILITY BY HANDICAPPED PERSONS
**Section:** 553.62
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The Occupational Safety and Health Administration's excavation safety standards, 29 C.F.R. s. 1926.650 subpart P, are hereby incorporated as the state standard.

**History:** s. 3, ch. 90-96; s. 31, ch. 2011-213.
