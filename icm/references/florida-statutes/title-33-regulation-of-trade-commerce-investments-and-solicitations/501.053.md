# Fla. Stat. § 501.053 — Home solicitation sale; judicial review.

**Title:** XXXIII — REGULATION OF TRADE, COMMERCE, INVESTMENTS, AND SOLICITATIONS
**Chapter:** 501 — CONSUMER PROTECTION
**Part:** I — GENERAL PROVISIONS
**Section:** 501.053
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Any person, firm, corporation, or agency aggrieved by any decision of a clerk of the circuit court under authority granted in ss. 501.021-501.055 may appeal to the courts, as provided by general law, within 90 days from the date of the decision sought to be reviewed.

**History:** s. 3, ch. 87-344.
