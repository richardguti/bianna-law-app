# Fla. Stat. § 553.994 — Applicability.

**Title:** XXXIII — REGULATION OF TRADE, COMMERCE, INVESTMENTS, AND SOLICITATIONS
**Chapter:** 553 — BUILDING CONSTRUCTION STANDARDS
**Part:** VIII — BUILDING ENERGY-EFFICIENCY RATING SYSTEM
**Section:** 553.994
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Building energy-efficiency rating systems apply to all public, commercial, and residential buildings in the state.

**History:** s. 12, ch. 93-249; s. 4, ch. 94-284; s. 42, ch. 98-250; s. 28, ch. 2013-193.
