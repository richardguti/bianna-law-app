# Fla. Stat. § 504.014 — Enforcement.

**Title:** XXXIII — REGULATION OF TRADE, COMMERCE, INVESTMENTS, AND SOLICITATIONS
**Chapter:** 504 — SPECIALIZED AGRICULTURAL PRODUCT LABELING
**Part:** VIII — PATENT TROLL PREVENTION ACT
**Section:** 504.014
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The Department of Agriculture and Consumer Services shall be responsible for enforcing the provisions of this chapter.

**History:** s. 5, ch. 79-121; s. 3, ch. 90-322; s. 92, ch. 2004-5.
