# Fla. Stat. § 520.998 — Regulatory Trust Fund.

**Title:** XXXIII — REGULATION OF TRADE, COMMERCE, INVESTMENTS, AND SOLICITATIONS
**Chapter:** 521 — MOTOR VEHICLE LEASE DISCLOSURE
**Part:** VI — DEPARTMENT REGULATION OF SALES AND FINANCE
**Section:** 520.998
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

All fees, charges, and fines collected by the office pursuant to this chapter shall be deposited in the State Treasury to the credit of the Regulatory Trust Fund under the office.

**History:** ss. 34, 36, ch. 90-103; s. 4, ch. 91-429; s. 30, ch. 99-155; s. 638, ch. 2003-261.
