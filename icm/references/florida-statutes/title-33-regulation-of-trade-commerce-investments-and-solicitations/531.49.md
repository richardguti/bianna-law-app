# Fla. Stat. § 531.49 — Advertising packages for sale.

**Title:** XXXIII — REGULATION OF TRADE, COMMERCE, INVESTMENTS, AND SOLICITATIONS
**Chapter:** 531 — WEIGHTS, MEASURES, AND STANDARDS
**Part:** II — SALE OF BRAKE FLUID
**Section:** 531.49
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

A packaged commodity closely and conspicuously associated with the retail price must have a declaration of quantity as is required by law or rule to appear on the package.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 72-101; s. 13, ch. 2005-210; s. 58, ch. 2025-22.
