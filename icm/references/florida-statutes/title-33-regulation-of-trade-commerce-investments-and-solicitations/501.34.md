# Fla. Stat. § 501.34 — Enforcement.

**Title:** XXXIII — REGULATION OF TRADE, COMMERCE, INVESTMENTS, AND SOLICITATIONS
**Chapter:** 501 — CONSUMER PROTECTION
**Part:** III — AFTERMARKET CRASH PARTS ACT
**Section:** 501.34
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
Any violation of this part by an insurer shall be deemed a violation of the Unfair Insurance Trade Practices Act, part IX, chapter 626.

(2)  
Any violation of this part by a repair facility shall be deemed a violation of the Florida Deceptive and Unfair Trade Practices Act, part II, this chapter.

**History:** s. 5, ch. 89-241; s. 21, ch. 2001-63.
