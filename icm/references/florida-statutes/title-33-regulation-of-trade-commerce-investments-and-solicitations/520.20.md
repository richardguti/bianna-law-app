# Fla. Stat. § 520.20 — Definitions.

**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

As used in this part, the term:
(1)  
“Agreement” means a contract executed between a buyer or lessee and a seller that leases or sells a distributed energy generation system. For purposes of this part, the term includes retail installment contracts.

(2)  
“Buyer” means a person that enters into an agreement to buy a distributed energy generation system from a seller.
