# Fla. Stat. § 522.05 — Penalty for failure of commission merchant to make returns.

**Title:** XXXIII — REGULATION OF TRADE, COMMERCE, INVESTMENTS, AND SOLICITATIONS
**Chapter:** 522 — COMMISSION MERCHANTS
**Part:** VI — DEPARTMENT REGULATION OF SALES AND FINANCE
**Section:** 522.05
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Any person, or agent or servant of such person failing to comply with the provisions of s. 522.01 shall be guilty of a misdemeanor of the second degree, punishable as provided in s. 775.083.

**History:** s. 3, ch. 6235, 1911; RGS 5666; CGL 7869; s. 496, ch. 71-136.
