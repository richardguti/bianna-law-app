# Fla. Stat. § 560.204 — License required.

**Title:** XXXIII — REGULATION OF TRADE, COMMERCE, INVESTMENTS, AND SOLICITATIONS
**Chapter:** 560 — MONEY SERVICES BUSINESSES
**Part:** II — PAYMENT INSTRUMENTS, FUNDS TRANSMISSION, AND CUSTODIANS OF GOLD AND SILVER COIN
**Section:** 560.204
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
Unless exempted, a person may not engage in, or in any manner advertise that they engage in, the activity of a payment instrument seller or money transmitter, for compensation, without first obtaining a license under this part. For purposes of this subsection, the term “compensation” includes profit or loss on the exchange of currency, monetary value, or virtual currency.

(2)  
A licensee under this part may also engage in the activities authorized under part III of this chapter without the imposition of any additional licensing fees.

**History:** s. 2, ch. 94-238; s. 2, ch. 94-354; s. 6, ch. 2001-119; s. 30, ch. 2008-177; s. 5, ch. 2022-113.
