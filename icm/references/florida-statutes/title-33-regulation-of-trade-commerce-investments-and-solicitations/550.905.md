# Fla. Stat. § 550.905 — States eligible to join compact.

**Title:** XXXIII — REGULATION OF TRADE, COMMERCE, INVESTMENTS, AND SOLICITATIONS
**Chapter:** 550 — PARI-MUTUEL WAGERING
**Part:** II — FLORIDA CHOICE ACT
**Section:** 550.905
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Any state that has adopted or authorized pari-mutuel wagering shall be eligible to become a party to this compact.

**History:** s. 35, ch. 2000-354.
