# Fla. Stat. § 559.9240 — Waiver.

**Title:** XXXIII — REGULATION OF TRADE, COMMERCE, INVESTMENTS, AND SOLICITATIONS
**Chapter:** 559 — REGULATION OF TRADE, COMMERCE, AND INVESTMENTS, GENERALLY
**Part:** X — RENTAL-PURCHASE AGREEMENTS
**Section:** 559.9240
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Any waiver by the lessee of any provisions of this act or of any remedies granted to the lessee by this act is unenforceable and void.

**History:** s. 10, ch. 88-69.F.S. 559.92396559.9239
