# Fla. Stat. § 553.61 — Intent.

**Title:** XXXIII — REGULATION OF TRADE, COMMERCE, INVESTMENTS, AND SOLICITATIONS
**Chapter:** 553 — BUILDING CONSTRUCTION STANDARDS
**Part:** II — ACCESSIBILITY BY HANDICAPPED PERSONS
**Section:** 553.61
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The purpose and intent of this act is to provide for increased worker safety by requiring compliance with sufficient standards for trench safety.

**History:** s. 2, ch. 90-96.
