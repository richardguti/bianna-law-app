# Fla. Stat. § 553.35 — Short title.

**Title:** XXXIII — REGULATION OF TRADE, COMMERCE, INVESTMENTS, AND SOLICITATIONS
**Chapter:** 553 — BUILDING CONSTRUCTION STANDARDS
**Part:** I — MANUFACTURED BUILDINGS
**Section:** 553.35
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

This part shall be known and may be cited as the “Florida Manufactured Building Act of 1979.”
