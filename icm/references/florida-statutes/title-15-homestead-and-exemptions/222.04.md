# Fla. Stat. § 222.04 — Sale after survey.

**Title:** XV — HOMESTEAD AND EXEMPTIONS
**Chapter:** 222 — METHOD OF SETTING APART HOMESTEAD AND EXEMPTIONS
**Part:** X — TAX CRIMES
**Section:** 222.04
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

After such survey has been made, the officer making the levy may sell the property levied upon not included in such property set off in such manner.

**History:** s. 4, ch. 1715, 1869; RS 2001; GS 2523; RGS 3878; CGL 5785.
