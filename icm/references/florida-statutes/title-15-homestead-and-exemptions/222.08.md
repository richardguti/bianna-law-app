# Fla. Stat. § 222.08 — Jurisdiction to set apart homestead and exemption.

**Title:** XV — HOMESTEAD AND EXEMPTIONS
**Chapter:** 222 — METHOD OF SETTING APART HOMESTEAD AND EXEMPTIONS
**Part:** X — TAX CRIMES
**Section:** 222.08
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The circuit courts have equity jurisdiction to order and decree the setting apart of homesteads and of exemptions of personal property from forced sales.

**History:** s. 2, ch. 3246, 1881; RS 2005; GS 2527; RGS 3882; CGL 5789.
