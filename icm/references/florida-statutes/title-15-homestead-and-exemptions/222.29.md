# Fla. Stat. § 222.29 — No exemption for fraudulent transfers.

**Title:** XV — HOMESTEAD AND EXEMPTIONS
**Chapter:** 222 — METHOD OF SETTING APART HOMESTEAD AND EXEMPTIONS
**Part:** X — TAX CRIMES
**Section:** 222.29
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

An exemption from attachment, garnishment, or legal process provided by this chapter is not effective if it results from a fraudulent transfer or conveyance as provided in chapter 726.

**History:** s. 4, ch. 93-256.
