# Fla. Stat. § 402.41 — Educational materials and training concerning HIV infections and AIDS.

**Title:** XXIX — PUBLIC HEALTH
**Chapter:** 402 — HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES: MISCELLANEOUS PROVISIONS
**Part:** III — MEDICAL TRANSPORTATION SERVICES
**Section:** 402.41
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The Department of Health shall develop educational materials and training about the transmission, control, and prevention of human immunodeficiency virus infections and acquired immune deficiency syndrome and other communicable diseases relevant for use in those facilities licensed under the provisions of this chapter.

**History:** s. 9, ch. 88-380; s. 50, ch. 97-237.
