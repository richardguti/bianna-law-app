# Fla. Stat. § 391.065 — Health care provider agreements.

**Title:** XXIX — PUBLIC HEALTH
**Chapter:** 391 — CHILDREN’S MEDICAL SERVICES
**Part:** I — GENERAL PROVISIONS
**Section:** 391.065
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The department is authorized to establish health care provider agreements for participation in the Children's Medical Services program.

**History:** s. 14, ch. 98-288.
