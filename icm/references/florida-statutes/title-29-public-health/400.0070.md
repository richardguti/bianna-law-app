# Fla. Stat. § 400.0070 — Conflicts of interest.

**Title:** XXIX — PUBLIC HEALTH
**Chapter:** 400 — NURSING HOMES AND RELATED HEALTH CARE FACILITIES
**Part:** I — LONG-TERM CARE FACILITIES: OMBUDSMAN PROGRAM
**Section:** 400.0070
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
A representative of the State Long-Term Care Ombudsman Program may not:
(a)  
Have a direct involvement in the licensing or certification of, or an ownership or investment interest in, a long-term care facility or a provider of a long-term care service.

(b)  
Be employed by, or participate in the management of, a long-term care facility.
