# Fla. Stat. § 394.4995 — Conversion of specified facilities to children's behavioral crisis units; not required.

**Title:** XXIX — PUBLIC HEALTH
**Chapter:** 394 — MENTAL HEALTH
**Part:** III — COMPREHENSIVE CHILD AND ADOLESCENT MENTAL HEALTH SERVICES
**Section:** 394.4995
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Nothing in [1]s. 394.499 shall be construed to require an existing crisis stabilization unit or juvenile addictions receiving facility to convert to a children's behavioral crisis unit.

**History:** s. 7, ch. 2001-171; s. 2, ch. 2001-191.

**Note:** [1]Note.
—
As enacted by s. 2, ch. 2001-191. Section 394.4995 was also enacted by s. 7, ch. 2001-171, and that version references “this act” instead of specifying s. 394.499.
