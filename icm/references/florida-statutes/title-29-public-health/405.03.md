# Fla. Stat. § 405.03 — Confidentiality.

**Title:** XXIX — PUBLIC HEALTH
**Chapter:** 406 — MEDICAL EXAMINERS; DISPOSITION OF HUMAN REMAINS
**Part:** IX — EXPEDITED PERMITTING
**Section:** 405.03
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

In all events the identity of any person whose condition or treatment has been studied shall be confidential and exempt from the provisions of s. 119.07(1).

**History:** s. 3, ch. 65-533; s. 21, ch. 90-344; s. 245, ch. 96-406.
