# Fla. Stat. § 383.014 — Perinatal mental health care.

**Title:** XXIX — PUBLIC HEALTH
**Chapter:** 383 — MATERNAL AND INFANT HEALTH CARE
**Part:** III — FLORIDA COMMUNITIES TRUST
**Section:** 383.014
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

By January 1, 2019, the Department of Health shall offer perinatal mental health care information through the Family Health Line toll-free hotline, accessible to the general public, which:
(1)  
Provides basic information on postpartum depression;

(2)  
May recommend that a caller be further evaluated by a qualified health care provider; and

(3)  
May refer a caller to an appropriate health care provider in the caller's local area.

**History:** s. 2, ch. 2018-98.
