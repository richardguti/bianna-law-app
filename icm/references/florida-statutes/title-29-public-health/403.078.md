# Fla. Stat. § 403.078 — Effect on other law.

**Title:** XXIX — PUBLIC HEALTH
**Chapter:** 403 — ENVIRONMENTAL CONTROL
**Part:** I — POLLUTION CONTROL
**Section:** 403.078
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The Public Notice of Pollution Act does not alter or affect the emergency management responsibilities of the Governor, the Division of Emergency Management, or the governing body of any political subdivision of the state pursuant to chapter 252.

**History:** s. 3, ch. 2017-95.
