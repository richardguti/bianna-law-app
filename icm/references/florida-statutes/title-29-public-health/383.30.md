# Fla. Stat. § 383.30 — Birth Center Licensure Act; short title.

**Title:** XXIX — PUBLIC HEALTH
**Chapter:** 383 — MATERNAL AND INFANT HEALTH CARE
**Part:** III — FLORIDA COMMUNITIES TRUST
**Section:** 383.30
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Sections 383.30-383.332 shall be known and may be cited as the “Birth Center Licensure Act.”

**History:** ss. 1, 27, ch. 84-283; s. 4, ch. 91-429; s. 12, ch. 2018-24.
