# Fla. Stat. § 381.91 — Jessie Trice Cancer Prevention Program.

**Title:** XXIX — PUBLIC HEALTH
**Chapter:** 381 — PUBLIC HEALTH: GENERAL PROVISIONS
**Part:** III — FLORIDA COMMUNITIES TRUST
**Section:** 381.91
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
It is the intent of the Legislature to create a community faith-based disease-prevention program in conjunction with the Health Choice Network and other community health centers to build upon the natural referral and education networks in place within minority communities and to increase access to health service delivery in Florida and establish a funding source to build upon local private participation to sustain the operation of the program.

(2)(a)  
There is created the Jessie Trice Cancer Prevention Program, to be located, for administrative purposes, within the Department of Health, and operated from the community health centers within the Health Choice Network in Florida.

(b)  
Funding may be provided to develop contracts with community health centers and local community faith-based education programs to provide cancer screening, diagnosis, education, and treatment services to low-income populations throughout the state.

**History:** s. 31, ch. 2000-367; s. 1, ch. 2004-329; s. 69, ch. 2012-184.
