# Fla. Stat. § 395.0091 — Alternate-site testing.

**Title:** XXIX — PUBLIC HEALTH
**Chapter:** 395 — HOSPITAL LICENSING AND REGULATION
**Part:** I — HOSPITALS AND OTHER LICENSED FACILITIES
**Section:** 395.0091
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

——

The agency, in consultation with the Board of Clinical Laboratory Personnel, shall adopt by rule the criteria for alternate-site testing to be performed under the supervision of a clinical laboratory director. At a minimum, the criteria must address hospital internal needs assessment; a protocol for implementation, including the identification of tests to be performed and who will perform them; selection of the method of testing to be used for alternate-site testing; minimum training and education requirements for those who will perform alternate-site testing, such as documented training, licensure, certification, or other medical professional background not limited to laboratory professionals; documented inservice training and initial and ongoing competency validation; an appropriate internal and external quality control protocol; an internal mechanism for the central laboratory to identify and track alternate-site testing; and record-keeping requirements. Alternate-site testing locations must register when the hospital applies to renew its license.
