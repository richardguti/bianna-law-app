# Fla. Stat. § 403.7531 — Notice by retail dealer.

**Title:** XXIX — PUBLIC HEALTH
**Chapter:** 403 — ENVIRONMENTAL CONTROL
**Part:** IV — RESOURCE RECOVERY AND MANAGEMENT
**Section:** 403.7531
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

A retail dealer who annually sells directly to the public more than 500 gallons of oil in containers for use off-premises shall post in a prominent place the toll-free telephone number the public can call to learn the location of a public used oil collection center.

**History:** s. 47, ch. 93-207.
