# Fla. Stat. § 388.381 — Cooperation by programs.

**Title:** XXIX — PUBLIC HEALTH
**Chapter:** 388 — MOSQUITO CONTROL
**Part:** II — SMOKING AND VAPING
**Section:** 388.381
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Any program conducting arthropod control may cooperate with another county, district, or municipality in carrying out work for the control of mosquitoes and other arthropods, by agreement as to the program and reimbursement thereof, when approved by the department.

**History:** s. 2, ch. 59-195; s. 1, ch. 63-236; ss. 19, 35, ch. 69-106; s. 171, ch. 77-147; s. 17, ch. 92-203; s. 26, ch. 2025-22.
