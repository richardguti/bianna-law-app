# Fla. Stat. § 400.9985 — Receivership proceedings.

**Title:** XXIX — PUBLIC HEALTH
**Chapter:** 400 — NURSING HOMES AND RELATED HEALTH CARE FACILITIES
**Part:** XI — TRANSITIONAL LIVING FACILITIES
**Section:** 400.9985
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The agency may apply s. 429.22 with regard to receivership proceedings for transitional living facilities.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 2015-25.
