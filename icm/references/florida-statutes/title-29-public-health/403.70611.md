# Fla. Stat. § 403.70611 — Requirements relating to solid waste disposal facility permitting.

**Title:** XXIX — PUBLIC HEALTH
**Chapter:** 403 — ENVIRONMENTAL CONTROL
**Part:** IV — RESOURCE RECOVERY AND MANAGEMENT
**Section:** 403.70611
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Local government applicants for a permit to construct or expand a Class I landfill are encouraged to consider construction of a waste-to-energy facility as an alternative to additional landfill space.

**History:** s. 4, ch. 2005-259.
