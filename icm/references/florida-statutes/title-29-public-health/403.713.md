# Fla. Stat. § 403.713 — Ownership and control of solid waste and recovered materials.

**Title:** XXIX — PUBLIC HEALTH
**Chapter:** 403 — ENVIRONMENTAL CONTROL
**Part:** IV — RESOURCE RECOVERY AND MANAGEMENT
**Section:** 403.713
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

——

(1)  
Nothing in this act or in any local act or ordinance shall be construed to limit the free flow of solid waste across municipal or county boundaries provided such solid waste is transported C by a local or state government or the Federal Government.
