# Fla. Stat. § 390.01116 — Public records exemptions; minors seeking waiver of notice requirements.

**Title:** XXIX — PUBLIC HEALTH
**Chapter:** 390 — TERMINATION OF PREGNANCIES
**Part:** II — SMOKING AND VAPING
**Section:** 390.01116
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Any information that can be used to identify a minor petitioning a circuit court for a judicial waiver, as provided in s. 390.01114, of the notice requirements under the Parental Notice of Abortion Act is:
(1)  
Confidential and exempt from s. 24(a), Art. I of the State Constitution if held by a circuit court or an appellate court.

(2)  
Confidential and exempt from s. 119.07(1) and s. 24(a), Art. I of the State Constitution if held by the office of criminal conflict and civil regional counsel or the Justice Administrative Commission.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 99-321; ss. 1, 2, ch. 2005-104; ss. 1, 3, ch. 2010-41; s. 1, ch. 2015-74.
