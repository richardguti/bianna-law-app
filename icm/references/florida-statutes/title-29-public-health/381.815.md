# Fla. Stat. § 381.815 — Sickle-cell program.

**Title:** XXIX — PUBLIC HEALTH
**Chapter:** 381 — PUBLIC HEALTH: GENERAL PROVISIONS
**Part:** III — FLORIDA COMMUNITIES TRUST
**Section:** 381.815
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The Department of Health shall, to the extent that resources are available:
(1)  
Provide education to the citizens of Florida about sickle-cell disease.

(2)  
Work cooperatively with not-for-profit centers to provide community-based education, patient teaching, and counseling and to encourage diagnostic screening.

(3)  
Make grants or enter into contracts with not-for-profit centers.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 96-292; s. 186, ch. 97-101.
