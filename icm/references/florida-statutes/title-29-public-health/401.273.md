# Fla. Stat. § 401.273 — Emergency medical technician and paramedic registry for disasters and emergencies.

**Title:** XXIX — PUBLIC HEALTH
**Chapter:** 401 — MEDICAL TELECOMMUNICATIONS AND TRANSPORTATION
**Part:** III — MEDICAL TRANSPORTATION SERVICES
**Section:** 401.273
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

——

459.025. A physician who supervises or provides medical direction to a paramedic who provides basic life support services or advanced life support services to patients receiving acute and postacute hospital care at home pursuant to a formal supervisory relationship or standing orders is liable for any act or omission of the paramedic acting under the physician's supervision or medical direction when providing such services. The department may adopt and enforce rules necessary to implement this paragraph.
