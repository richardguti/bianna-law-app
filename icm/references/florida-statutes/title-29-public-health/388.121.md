# Fla. Stat. § 388.121 — District boards of commissioners; organization.

**Title:** XXIX — PUBLIC HEALTH
**Chapter:** 388 — MOSQUITO CONTROL
**Part:** II — SMOKING AND VAPING
**Section:** 388.121
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

As soon as practicable after such commissioners have been elected and qualified, they shall meet and organize by the election from among their number of a chair, a secretary and a treasurer. Two members of the board shall constitute a quorum. The vote of two members shall be necessary to transact business.

**History:** s. 2, ch. 59-195; s. 691, ch. 95-148.
