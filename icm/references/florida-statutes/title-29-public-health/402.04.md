# Fla. Stat. § 402.04 — Award of scholarships and stipends; disbursement of funds; administration.

**Title:** XXIX — PUBLIC HEALTH
**Chapter:** 401 — MEDICAL TELECOMMUNICATIONS AND TRANSPORTATION
**Part:** III — MEDICAL TRANSPORTATION SERVICES
**Section:** 402.04
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

The award of scholarships or stipends provided for herein shall be made by the Department of Children and Families, hereinafter referred to as the department. The department shall handle the administration of the scholarship or stipend and the Department of Education shall, for and on behalf of the department, handle the notes issued for the payment of the scholarships or stipends provided for herein and the collection of same.
