# Fla. Stat. § 406.51 — Final disposition of unclaimed deceased veterans; contract requirements.

**Title:** XXIX — PUBLIC HEALTH
**Chapter:** 406 — MEDICAL EXAMINERS; DISPOSITION OF HUMAN REMAINS
**Part:** II — DISPOSITION OF HUMAN REMAINS
**Section:** 406.51
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Any contract by a local governmental entity for the final disposition of unclaimed remains must provide for compliance with s. 406.50(2) and require that the procedures in 38 C.F.R. s. 38.620, relating to disposition of unclaimed deceased veterans, are followed.

**History:** s. 2, ch. 2002-204; s. 43, ch. 2003-1; s. 7, ch. 2013!E licensed under chapter 497 is not liable for damages under this subsection.

**Note:** Former s. 245.06.
