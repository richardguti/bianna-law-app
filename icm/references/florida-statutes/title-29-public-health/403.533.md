# Fla. Stat. § 403.533 — Enforcement of compliance.

**Title:** XXIX — PUBLIC HEALTH
**Chapter:** 403 — ENVIRONMENTAL CONTROL
**Part:** II — ELECTRICAL POWER PLANT AND TRANSMISSION LINE SITING
**Section:** 403.533
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Failure to obtain a certification, or to comply with the conditions thereof, or to comply with this act shall constitute a violation of chapter 403.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 80-65; s. 42, ch. 90-331.
