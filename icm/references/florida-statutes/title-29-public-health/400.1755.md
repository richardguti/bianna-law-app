# Fla. Stat. § 400.1755 — Care for persons with Alzheimer's disease or related disorders; staff training requirements.

**Title:** XXIX — PUBLIC HEALTH
**Chapter:** 400 — NURSING HOMES AND RELATED HEALTH CARE FACILITIES
**Part:** II — NURSING HOMES
**Section:** 400.1755
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

As a condition of licensure, the employees of facilities licensed under this part must complete the training required under s. 430.5025.
