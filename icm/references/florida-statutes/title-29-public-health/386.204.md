# Fla. Stat. § 386.204 — Prohibition.

**Title:** XXIX — PUBLIC HEALTH
**Chapter:** 386 — SANITARY NUISANCES; FLORIDA CLEAN AIR ACT
**Part:** II — SMOKING AND VAPING
**Section:** 386.204
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

A person may not smoke or vape in an enclosed indoor workplace, except as otherwise provided in s. 386.2045.

**History:** s. 4, ch. 85-257; s. 4, ch. 92-185; s. 4, ch. 2003-398; s. 4, ch. 2019-14.
