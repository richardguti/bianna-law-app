# Fla. Stat. § 400.490 — Nurse-delegated tasks.

**Title:** XXIX — PUBLIC HEALTH
**Chapter:** 400 — NURSING HOMES AND RELATED HEALTH CARE FACILITIES
**Part:** III — HOME HEALTH SERVICES
**Section:** 400.490
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

A certified nursing assistant, home health aide for medically fragile children, or home health aide may perform any task delegated by a registered nurse as authorized in this part and in chapter 464, including, but not limited to, medication administration.

**History:** s. 8, ch. 2020-9; s. 6, ch. 2023-183.
