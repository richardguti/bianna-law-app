# Fla. Stat. § 403.5116 — County and municipal authority unaffected by ch. 75-22.

**Title:** XXIX — PUBLIC HEALTH
**Chapter:** 403 — ENVIRONMENTAL CONTROL
**Part:** II — ELECTRICAL POWER PLANT AND TRANSMISSION LINE SITING
**Section:** 403.5116
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Except as provided in ss. 403.510 and 403.511, nothing in chapter 75-22, Laws of Florida, shall be construed to have altered the authority of county and municipal governments as provided by law.

**History:** s. 22, ch. 75-22; s. 17, ch. 90-331.

**Note:** Former s. 403.5111.
