# Fla. Stat. § 406.03 — Organization and meetings of commission.

**Title:** XXIX — PUBLIC HEALTH
**Chapter:** 406 — MEDICAL EXAMINERS; DISPOSITION OF HUMAN REMAINS
**Part:** I — MEDICAL EXAMINERS
**Section:** 406.03
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The commission shall annually select a chair from among its own membership and shall meet at least four times each year and on the call of the chair.

**History:** s. 3, ch. 70-232; s. 4, ch. 78-323; ss. 6, 7, ch. 81-89; ss. 1, 4, ch. 82-46; s. 2, ch. 83-265; ss. 7, 8, ch. 87-359; s. 5, ch. 91-429; s. 26, ch. 97-103.
