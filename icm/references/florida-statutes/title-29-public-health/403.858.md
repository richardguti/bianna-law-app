# Fla. Stat. § 403.858 — Inspections.

**Title:** XXIX — PUBLIC HEALTH
**Chapter:** 403 — ENVIRONMENTAL CONTROL
**Part:** VI — WATER SUPPLY; WATER TREATMENT PLANTS
**Section:** 403.858
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Any duly authorized representative of the department or of the Department of Health may enter, take water samples from, and inspect any property, premises, or place, except a building which is used exclusively for a private residence, on or at which a public water system is located or is being constructed or installed, at any reasonable time, for the purpose of ascertaining the state of compliance with the law or with rules or orders of the department.

**History:** s. 9, ch. 77-337; s. 168, ch. 99-8.
