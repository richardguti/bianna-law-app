# Fla. Stat. § 388.301 — Payment of state funds; supplies and services.

**Title:** XXIX — PUBLIC HEALTH
**Chapter:** 388 — MOSQUITO CONTROL
**Part:** II — SMOKING AND VAPING
**Section:** 388.301
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

State funds shall be payable, in accordance with the rules of the department, upon requisition by the department to the Chief Financial Officer. The department is authorized to furnish insecticides, chemicals, materials, equipment, vehicles, and personnel in lieu of state funds where mass purchasing may save funds for the state, or where it would be more practical and economical to use equipment, supplies, and services between two or more programs.

The department may at its discretion discontinue any state aid provided hereunder in the event it finds the jointly agreed-upon program is not being followed or is not efficiently and effectively administered.
