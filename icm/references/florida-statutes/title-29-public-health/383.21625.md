EVALUATIONS AND REPORTS.——
The department shall conduct an annual evaluation of the implementation of the initiative describing which areas are participating in the initiative, the number of reviews conducted by each participating coalition, grant balances, and recommendations for modifying the initiative.

(8)  
IMMUNITY.——
Each participating coalition, its case review team members, and professional staff are immune from liability pursuant to s. 766.101.

(9)  
RULEMAKING.——
The department shall adopt rules, pursuant to ss. 120.536(1) and 120.54, necessary to implement this section.
