# Fla. Stat. § 403.855 — Imminent hazards.

**Title:** XXIX — PUBLIC HEALTH
**Chapter:** 403 — ENVIRONMENTAL CONTROL
**Part:** VI — WATER SUPPLY; WATER TREATMENT PLANTS
**Section:** 403.855
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

——

In coordination with the Department of Health, the department, upon receipt of information that a contaminant which is present in, or is likely to enter, public or private water supplies may present an imminent and substantial danger to the public health, may take such actions as it may deem necessary in order to protect the public health. Department actions shall include, but are noVDany injury, sickness, or death sustained by any person as a result of drinking water from any transient noncommunity water system granted a waiver under subsection (4) or subsection (5).
