# Fla. Stat. § 386.051 — Nuisances injurious to health, penalty.

**Title:** XXIX — PUBLIC HEALTH
**Chapter:** 386 — SANITARY NUISANCES; FLORIDA CLEAN AIR ACT
**Part:** I — SANITARY NUISANCES
**Section:** 386.051
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Any person found guilty of creating, keeping, or maintaining a nuisance injurious to health shall be guilty of a misdemeanor of the second degree, punishable as provided in s. 775.082 or s. 775.083.

**History:** s. 2, ch. 63-64; s. 337, ch. 71-136.
