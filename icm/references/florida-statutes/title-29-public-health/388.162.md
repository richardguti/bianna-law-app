# Fla. Stat. § 388.162 — Direction of the program.

**Title:** XXIX — PUBLIC HEALTH
**Chapter:** 388 — MOSQUITO CONTROL
**Part:** II — SMOKING AND VAPING
**Section:** 388.162
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The program shall be administered for the board of commissioners by a qualified person. The department shall establish minimum qualifications for employment of a director in accordance with the responsibilities attached to the position.

**History:** 388.161
