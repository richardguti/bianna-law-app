# Fla. Stat. § 388.241 — Board of county commissioners vested with powers and duties of board of commissioners in certain counties.

**Title:** XXIX — PUBLIC HEALTH
**Chapter:** 388 — MOSQUITO CONTROL
**Part:** II — SMOKING AND VAPING
**Section:** 388.241
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

In those counties or municipalities where there has been no formation of a separate or special board of commissioners, all the rights, powers, and duties of a board of commissioners as conferred in this chapter shall be vested in the board of county commissioners or similar governing body of said county or municipality.

**History:** s. 2, ch. 59-195; s. 8, ch. 86-203; s. 12, ch. 2025-22.
