# Fla. Stat. § 403.757 — Coordination with other state agencies.

**Title:** XXIX — PUBLIC HEALTH
**Chapter:** 403 — ENVIRONMENTAL CONTROL
**Part:** IV — RESOURCE RECOVERY AND MANAGEMENT
**Section:** 403.757
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
The department shall coordinate its activities and functions under ss. 403.75-403.769 and s. 526.01, as amended by chapter 84-338, Laws of Florida, with the Department of Commerce and other state agencies to avoid duplication in reporting and information gathering.
