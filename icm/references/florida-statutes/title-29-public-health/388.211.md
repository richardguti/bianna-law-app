# Fla. Stat. § 388.211 — Change in district boundaries.

**Title:** XXIX — PUBLIC HEALTH
**Chapter:** 388 — MOSQUITO CONTROL
**Part:** II — SMOKING AND VAPING
**Section:** 388.211
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The boundaries of each district may only be changed by a special act of the Legislature.

**History:** s. 2, ch. 59-195; s. 1, ch. 63-236; s. 3, ch. 71-323; s. 11, ch. 80-281; s. 11, ch. 2024-136.
