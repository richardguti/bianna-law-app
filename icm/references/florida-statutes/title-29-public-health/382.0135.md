# Fla. Stat. § 382.0135 — Social security numbers; enumeration-at-birth program.

**Title:** XXIX — PUBLIC HEALTH
**Chapter:** 382 — VITAL STATISTICS
**Part:** III — FLORIDA COMMUNITIES TRUST
**Section:** 382.0135
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The department shall make arrangements with the United States Social Security Administration for electronic notification of deaths that occur in the state and to participate in the voluntary enumeration-at-birth program. The State Registrar is authorized to take any actions necessary to administer the program in this state, including modifying the procedures and forms used in the birth registration process.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 88-348; s. 53, ch. 97-101; s. 97, ch. 97-237; s. 8, ch. 2015-105.
