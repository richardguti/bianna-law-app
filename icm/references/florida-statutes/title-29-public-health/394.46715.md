# Fla. Stat. § 394.46715 — Rulemaking authority.

**Title:** XXIX — PUBLIC HEALTH
**Chapter:** 394 — MENTAL HEALTH
**Part:** I — FLORIDA MENTAL HEALTH ACT
**Section:** 394.46715
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The department may adopt rules to administer this part.

**History:** s. 10, ch. 2004-385; s. 84, ch. 2014-19; s. 14, ch. 2016-241.
