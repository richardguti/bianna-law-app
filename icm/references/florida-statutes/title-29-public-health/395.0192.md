# Fla. Stat. § 395.0192 — Duty to notify physicians.

**Title:** XXIX — PUBLIC HEALTH
**Chapter:** 395 — HOSPITAL LICENSING AND REGULATION
**Part:** I — HOSPITALS AND OTHER LICENSED FACILITIES
**Section:** 395.0192
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

A hospital shall notify each obstetrical physician who has privileges at the hospital at least 120 days before the hospital closes its obstetrical department or ceases to provide obstetrical services.

**History:** s. 2, ch. 2016-113.
