# Fla. Stat. § 388.171 — Power to perform work.

**Title:** XXIX — PUBLIC HEALTH
**Chapter:** 388 — MOSQUITO CONTROL
**Part:** II — SMOKING AND VAPING
**Section:** 388.171
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The board of commissioners may have any and all work performed by contract with or without advertisement, or without contract, by machinery, equipment, and labor employed directly by the board of commissioners.

**History:** s. 2, ch. 59-195.
