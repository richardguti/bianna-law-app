# Fla. Stat. § 400.512 — Screening of home health agency personnel; nurse registry personnel and contractors; and companions and homemakers.

**Title:** XXIX — PUBLIC HEALTH
**Chapter:** 400 — NURSING HOMES AND RELATED HEALTH CARE FACILITIES
**Part:** III — HOME HEALTH SERVICES
**Section:** 400.512
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The agency, registry, or service shall require level 2 background screening for employees or contractors as required in s. 408.809(1)(e) pursuant to chapter 435 and s. 408.809.
