# Fla. Stat. § 397.341 — Transportation of individuals by law enforcement officers.

**Title:** XXIX — PUBLIC HEALTH
**Chapter:** 397 — SUBSTANCE ABUSE SERVICES
**Part:** I — GENERAL PROVISIONS
**Section:** 397.341
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

A law enforcement officer transporting an individual pursuant to this chapter shall restrain that individual in the least restrictive manner available and appropriate under the circumstances.

**History:** s. 3, ch. 2022-41.
