# Fla. Stat. § 395.3037 — Definitions.

**Title:** XXIX — PUBLIC HEALTH
**Chapter:** 395 — HOSPITAL LICENSING AND REGULATION
**Part:** I — HOSPITALS AND OTHER LICENSED FACILITIES
**Section:** 395.3037
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

As used in this act, the term:
(1)  
“Department” means the Department of Health.

(2)  
“Agency” means the Agency for Health Care Administration.

**History:** s. 2, ch. 2004-325.
