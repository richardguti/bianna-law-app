# Fla. Stat. § 406.55 — Contracts for delivery of human remains after death prohibited.

**Title:** XXIX — PUBLIC HEALTH
**Chapter:** 406 — MEDICAL EXAMINERS; DISPOSITION OF HUMAN REMAINS
**Part:** II — DISPOSITION OF HUMAN REMAINS
**Section:** 406.55
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The anatomical board may not enter into any contract, oral or written, that provides for any sum of money to be paid to any living person in exchange for the delivery of that person's remains to the anatomical board when the person dies.

**History:** s. 9, ch. 28163, 1953; ss. 15, 35, ch. 69-106; s. 5, ch. 96-251; s. 10, ch. 2013-138.

**Note:** Former s. 245.10.
