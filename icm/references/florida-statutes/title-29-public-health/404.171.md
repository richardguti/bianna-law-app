# Fla. Stat. § 404.171 — Construction.

**Title:** XXIX — PUBLIC HEALTH
**Chapter:** 404 — RADIATION
**Part:** IX — EXPEDITED PERMITTING
**Section:** 404.171
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

This chapter is cumulative and is intended to supplement existing laws, and no part shall be construed to repeal any existing law, specifically enacted for the protection of public health and safety, with the exception of those sections included in this chapter.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 78-373; s. 2, ch. 81-318; ss. 18, 22, ch. 82-186; ss. 17, 18, ch. 84-190; s. 4, ch. 91-429.

**Note:** Former s. 290.171.
