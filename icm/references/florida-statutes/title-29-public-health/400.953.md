# Fla. Stat. § 400.953 — Background screening of home medical equipment provider personnel.

**Title:** XXIX — PUBLIC HEALTH
**Chapter:** 400 — NURSING HOMES AND RELATED HEALTH CARE FACILITIES
**Part:** VII — HOME MEDICAL EQUIPMENT PROVIDERS
**Section:** 400.953
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The agency shall require level 2 background screening for personnel as required in s. 408.809(1)(e) pursuant to chapter 435 and s. 408.809.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 99-189; s. 90, ch. 2000-349; s. 27, ch. 2004-267; s. 109, ch. 2006-120; s. 14, ch. 2010-114.
