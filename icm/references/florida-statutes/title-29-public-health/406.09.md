# Fla. Stat. § 406.09 — Expert witness fees.

**Title:** XXIX — PUBLIC HEALTH
**Chapter:** 406 — MEDICAL EXAMINERS; DISPOSITION OF HUMAN REMAINS
**Part:** I — MEDICAL EXAMINERS
**Section:** 406.09
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

District medical examiners or associate medical examiners shall be entitled to expert witness fees as provided by law.

**History:** s. 5, ch. 70-232; s. 4, ch. 81-89.
