# Fla. Stat. § 403.869 — Authority to adopt rules.

**Title:** XXIX — PUBLIC HEALTH
**Chapter:** 403 — ENVIRONMENTAL CONTROL
**Part:** VI — WATER SUPPLY; WATER TREATMENT PLANTS
**Section:** 403.869
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The department may adopt rules pursuant to ss. 120.536(1) and 120.54 to implement the provisions of ss. 403.865-403.876.

**History:** s. 8, ch. 97-236.
