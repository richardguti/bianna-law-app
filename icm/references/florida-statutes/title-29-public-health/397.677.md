# Fla. Stat. § 397.677 — Protective custody; circumstances justifying.

**Title:** XXIX — PUBLIC HEALTH
**Chapter:** 397 — SUBSTANCE ABUSE SERVICES
**Part:** V — INVOLUNTARY ADMISSIONS PROCEDURES
**Section:** 397.677
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

A law enforcement officer may implement protective custody measures as specified in this part when a minor or an adult who appears to meet the involuntary admission criteria in s. 397.675 is:
(1)  
Brought to the attention of law enforcement; or

(2)  
In a public place.

**History:** s. 6, ch. 93-39.
