# Fla. Stat. § 399.10 — Enforcement of law.

**Title:** XXIX — PUBLIC HEALTH
**Chapter:** 399 — ELEVATOR SAFETY
**Part:** IX — CHILDREN’S SUBSTANCE ABUSE SERVICES
**Section:** 399.10
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

It shall be the duty of the department to enforce the provisions of this chapter. The department shall have rulemaking authority to carry out the provisions of this chapter.

**History:** s. 10, ch. 24096, 1947; ss. 16, 35, ch. 69-106; s. 12, ch. 81-120; s. 2, ch. 81-318; ss. 9, 16, 17, ch. 83-145; s. 8, ch. 93-16; s. 12, ch. 2001-186.
