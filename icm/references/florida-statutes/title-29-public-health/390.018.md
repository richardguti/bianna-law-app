# Fla. Stat. § 390.018 — Administrative fine.

**Title:** XXIX — PUBLIC HEALTH
**Chapter:** 390 — TERMINATION OF PREGNANCIES
**Part:** II — SMOKING AND VAPING
**Section:** 390.018
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

In addition to the requirements of part II of chapter 408, the agency may impose a fine upon the clinic in an amount not to exceed $1,000 for each violation of any provision of this chapter, part II of chapter 408, or applicable rules.
