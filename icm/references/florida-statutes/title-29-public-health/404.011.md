# Fla. Stat. § 404.011 — Short title.

**Title:** XXIX — PUBLIC HEALTH
**Chapter:** 403 — ENVIRONMENTAL CONTROL
**Part:** IX — EXPEDITED PERMITTING
**Section:** 404.011
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)
