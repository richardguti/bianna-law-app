# Fla. Stat. § 390.015 — Application for license.

**Title:** XXIX — PUBLIC HEALTH
**Chapter:** 390 — TERMINATION OF PREGNANCIES
**Part:** II — SMOKING AND VAPING
**Section:** 390.015
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

In addition to the requirements of part II of chapter 408, an application for a license to operate an abortion clinic shall be made to the agency and must include the location of the clinic for which application is made and a statement that local zoning ordinances permit such location.
