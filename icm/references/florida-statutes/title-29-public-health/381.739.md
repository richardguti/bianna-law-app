# Fla. Stat. § 381.739 — Short title; Charlie Mack Overstreet Brain or Spinal Cord Injuries Act.

**Title:** XXIX — PUBLIC HEALTH
**Chapter:** 381 — PUBLIC HEALTH: GENERAL PROVISIONS
**Part:** III — FLORIDA COMMUNITIES TRUST
**Section:** 381.739
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Sections 381.739-381.79 may be cited as the “Charlie Mack Overstreet Brain or Spinal Cord Injuries Act.”

**History:** s. 32, ch. 94-324; s. 17, ch. 99-240.

**Note:** Former s. 413.465.
