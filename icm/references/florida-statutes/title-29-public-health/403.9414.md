# Fla. Stat. § 403.9414 — Alteration of time limits.

**Title:** XXIX — PUBLIC HEALTH
**Chapter:** 403 — ENVIRONMENTAL CONTROL
**Part:** VIII — NATURAL GAS TRANSMISSION PIPELINE SITING
**Section:** 403.9414
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Any time limitation in ss. 403.9401-403.9425 may be altered by the administrative law judge upon stipulation between the department and the applicant unless objected to by any party within 5 days after notice or for good cause shown.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 92-284; s. 179, ch. 96-410.
