# Fla. Stat. § 392.61 — Community tuberculosis control programs.

**Title:** XXIX — PUBLIC HEALTH
**Chapter:** 392 — TUBERCULOSIS CONTROL
**Part:** II — EARLY STEPS PROGRAM
**Section:** 392.61
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
The department shall operate, directly or by contract, community tuberculosis control programs in each county in the state.

(2)  
Community tuberculosis control programs shall have the following functions:
(a)  
Promotion of community and professional education about the causes and dangers of tuberculosis and methods of its control and treatment to cure;

(b)  
Community and individual screening for the presence of tuberculosis;
