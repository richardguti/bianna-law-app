# Fla. Stat. § 403.0873 — Florida Air-Operation License Fee Account.

**Title:** XXIX — PUBLIC HEALTH
**Chapter:** 403 — ENVIRONMENTAL CONTROL
**Part:** I — POLLUTION CONTROL
**Section:** 403.0873
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

——

The “Florida Air-Operation License Fee Account” is established as a nonlapsing account within the Department of Environmental Protection's Air Pollution Control Trust Fund. All license fees paid pursuant to s. 403.0872(11) shall be deposited in such account and must be used solely by the department and approved local programs under the advice and consent of the Legislature to pay the direct and indirect costs required to develop and administer the major stationary source air-operation permit program.
