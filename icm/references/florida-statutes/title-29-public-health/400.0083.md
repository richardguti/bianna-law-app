# Fla. Stat. § 400.0083 — Interference; retaliation; penalties.

**Title:** XXIX — PUBLIC HEALTH
**Chapter:** 400 — NURSING HOMES AND RELATED HEALTH CARE FACILITIES
**Part:** I — LONG-TERM CARE FACILITIES: OMBUDSMAN PROGRAM
**Section:** 400.0083
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
A person, long-term care facility, or other entity may not willfully interfere with a representative of the State Long-Term Care Ombudsman Program in the performance of official duties.

(2)  
A person, long-term care facility, or other entity may not knowingly or willfully take action or retaliate against any resident, employee, or other person for filing a complaint with, providing information to, or otherwise cooperating with any representative of the State Long-Term Care Ombudsman Program.

(3)  
A person, long-term care facility, or other entity that violates this section:
(a)  
Is liable for damages and equitable relief as determined by law.

(b)  
Commits a misdemeanor of the second degree, punishable as provided in s. 775.083.

**History:** ss. 12, 30, ch. 93-177; s. 132, ch. 2000-349; s. 52, ch. 2000-367; s. 16, ch. 2006-121; s. 15, ch. 2015-31.
