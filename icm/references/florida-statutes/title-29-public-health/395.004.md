# Fla. Stat. § 395.004 — Application for license; fees.

**Title:** XXIX — PUBLIC HEALTH
**Chapter:** 395 — HOSPITAL LICENSING AND REGULATION
**Part:** I — HOSPITALS AND OTHER LICENSED FACILITIES
**Section:** 395.004
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

——

In accordance with s. 408.805, an applicant or licensee shall pay a fee for each license application submitted under this part, part II of chapter 408, and applicable rules. The amount of the fee shall be established by rule. The license fee required of a facility licensed under this part shall be established by rule at the rate of not less than $9.50 per hospital bed, nor more than $>(8) based upon a showing that an established program will be substantially affected by the issuance or renewal of a license to a hospital within the same district or service area.
