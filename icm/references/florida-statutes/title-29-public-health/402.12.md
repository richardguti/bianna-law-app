# Fla. Stat. § 402.12 — National Community Mental Health Centers Act.

**Title:** XXIX — PUBLIC HEALTH
**Chapter:** 402 — HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES: MISCELLANEOUS PROVISIONS
**Part:** III — MEDICAL TRANSPORTATION SERVICES
**Section:** 402.12
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Any federal funds accruing to the state for the purposes of carrying out the national Community Mental Health Centers Act of 1963 shall be paid to the Department of Children and Families for expenditure as directed by said department.
