# Fla. Stat. § 394.77 — Uniform management information, accounting, and reporting systems for providers.

**Title:** XXIX — PUBLIC HEALTH
**Chapter:** 394 — MENTAL HEALTH
**Part:** IV — COMMUNITY SUBSTANCE ABUSE AND MENTAL HEALTH SERVICES
**Section:** 394.77
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The department shall establish, for the purposes of control of costs:
(1)  
A uniform management information system and fiscal accounting system for use by providers of community substance abuse and mental health services.

(2)  
A uniform reporting system with uniform definitions and reporting categories.

**History:** s. 13, ch. 70-109; s. 1, ch. 70-439; s. 12, ch. 76-221; s. 6, ch. 82-223; s. 19, ch. 84-285; s. 14, ch. 2000-349.
