# Fla. Stat. § 388.111 — District boards of commissioners; vacancies.

**Title:** XXIX — PUBLIC HEALTH
**Chapter:** 388 — MOSQUITO CONTROL
**Part:** II — SMOKING AND VAPING
**Section:** 388.111
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

In the event of a vacancy due to any cause in any board of commissioners, the same shall be filled by appointment by the Commissioner of Agriculture for the unexpired term.

**History:** s. 2, ch. 59-195; s. 3, ch. 92-203.
