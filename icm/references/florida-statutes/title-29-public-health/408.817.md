# Fla. Stat. § 408.817 — Administrative proceedings.

**Title:** XXIX — PUBLIC HEALTH
**Chapter:** 408 — HEALTH CARE ADMINISTRATION
**Part:** II — HEALTH CARE LICENSING: GENERAL PROVISIONS
**Section:** 408.817
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Administrative proceedings challenging agency licensure enforcement action shall be reviewed on the basis of the facts and conditions that resulted in the agency action.

**History:** s. 5, ch. 2006-192.
