# Fla. Stat. § 404.042 — Designation of state radiation protection agency.

**Title:** XXIX — PUBLIC HEALTH
**Chapter:** 404 — RADIATION
**Part:** IX — EXPEDITED PERMITTING
**Section:** 404.042
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The department is hereby designated the state agency to administer a statewide radiation protection program consistent with the provisions of this chapter.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 78-373; s. 2, ch. 81-318; ss. 4, 22, ch. 82-186; ss. 17, 18, ch. 84-190; s. 4, ch. 91-429.

**Note:** Former s. 290.041.
