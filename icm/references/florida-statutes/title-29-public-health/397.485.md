# Fla. Stat. § 397.485 — Lawyer assistance programs; information subject to privilege.

**Title:** XXIX — PUBLIC HEALTH
**Chapter:** 397 — SUBSTANCE ABUSE SERVICES
**Part:** II — SERVICE PROVIDERS
**Section:** 397.485
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

All privileged information, whether attorney-client, work product, or otherwise, in any form, furnished to the lawyer assistance program shall remain privileged.

**History:** s. 4, ch. 2001-240.
