# Fla. Stat. § 403.9423 — Certification admissible in eminent domain proceedings; attorney's fees and costs.

**Title:** XXIX — PUBLIC HEALTH
**Chapter:** 403 — ENVIRONMENTAL CONTROL
**Part:** VIII — NATURAL GAS TRANSMISSION PIPELINE SITING
**Section:** 403.9423
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
Certification pursuant to ss. 403.9401-403.9425 shall be admissible as evidence of public need and necessity in proceedings under chapter 73 or chapter 74.

(2)  
No party may rely on this section or any provision of chapter 73 or chapter 74 to request the award of attorney's fees or costs incurred as a result of participation in the certification proceeding.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 92-284.
