# Fla. Stat. § 397.752 — Scope of part.

**Title:** XXIX — PUBLIC HEALTH
**Chapter:** 397 — SUBSTANCE ABUSE SERVICES
**Part:** VII — OFFENDER REFERRALS
**Section:** 397.752
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

An inmate's substance abuse service records are confidential in accordance with s. 397.501(7). No other provision of parts I-VII of this chapter applies to inmates except as indicated by the context or specified.

**History:** s. 9, ch. 93-39.
