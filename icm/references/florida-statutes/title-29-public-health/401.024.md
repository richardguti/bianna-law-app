# Fla. Stat. § 401.024 — System approval.

**Title:** XXIX — PUBLIC HEALTH
**Chapter:** 401 — MEDICAL TELECOMMUNICATIONS AND TRANSPORTATION
**Part:** I — EMERGENCY TELECOMMUNICATION SYSTEMS
**Section:** 401.024
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

No emergency medical telecommunications system shall be established or present systems expanded without prior approval of the Department of Management Services.

**History:** s. 5, ch. 73-254; s. 103, ch. 98-279; s. 67, ch. 2019-3.
