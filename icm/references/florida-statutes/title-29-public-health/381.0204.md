# Fla. Stat. § 381.0204 — Vital statistics.

**Title:** XXIX — PUBLIC HEALTH
**Chapter:** 381 — PUBLIC HEALTH: GENERAL PROVISIONS
**Part:** III — FLORIDA COMMUNITIES TRUST
**Section:** 381.0204
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The department shall provide for a statewide vital statistics program pursuant to chapter 382.

**History:** s. 47, ch. 91-297.
