# Fla. Stat. § 403.5312 — Filing of notice of certified corridor route.

**Title:** XXIX — PUBLIC HEALTH
**Chapter:** 403 — ENVIRONMENTAL CONTROL
**Part:** II — ELECTRICAL POWER PLANT AND TRANSMISSION LINE SITING
**Section:** 403.5312
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

——

(1)  
Within 60 days after certification of a transmission line corridor under ss. 403.52-403.5365, the applicant shall file with the department and, in accordance with s. 28.222, with the clerk of the circuit court for each county through which the corridor will pass, a notice of the certified route.

(2)  
The notice must consist of maps or aerial photographs in the scale of 1:24,000 which clearly show the location of the certified route and must state that the certification of the corridor will result in the acquisition of rights-of-way within the corridor.
