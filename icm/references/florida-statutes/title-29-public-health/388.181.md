# Fla. Stat. § 388.181 — Power to do all things necessary.

**Title:** XXIX — PUBLIC HEALTH
**Chapter:** 388 — MOSQUITO CONTROL
**Part:** II — SMOKING AND VAPING
**Section:** 388.181
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The respective programs of the state are hereby fully authorized to do and perform all things necessary to carry out the intent and purposes of this law.

**History:** s. 2, ch. 59-195; s. 10, ch. 2025-22.
