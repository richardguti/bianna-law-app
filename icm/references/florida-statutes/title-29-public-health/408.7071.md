# Fla. Stat. § 408.7071 — Standardized claim form.

**Title:** XXIX — PUBLIC HEALTH
**Chapter:** 408 — HEALTH CARE ADMINISTRATION
**Part:** I — HEALTH FACILITY AND SERVICES PLANNING
**Section:** 408.7071
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The Agency for Health Care Administration shall develop a standardized claim form to be used by insurers and health care providers licensed in this state.

**History:** s. 81, ch. 93-129; s. 41, ch. 97-103; s. 60, ch. 2000-158; s. 18, ch. 2000-209.
