# Fla. Stat. § 403.9419 — Enforcement of compliance.

**Title:** XXIX — PUBLIC HEALTH
**Chapter:** 403 — ENVIRONMENTAL CONTROL
**Part:** VIII — NATURAL GAS TRANSMISSION PIPELINE SITING
**Section:** 403.9419
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Failure to obtain a certification, to comply with the conditions of certification, or to comply with ss. 403.9401-403.9425 shall constitute a violation of this chapter. The department shall enforce compliance with the conditions of certification issued under ss. 403.9401-403.9425, in accordance with the provisions of ss. 403.061 and 403.121.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 92-284.
