# Fla. Stat. § 397.481 — Applicability of Community Alcohol, Drug Abuse, and Mental Health Services Act.

**Title:** XXIX — PUBLIC HEALTH
**Chapter:** 397 — SUBSTANCE ABUSE SERVICES
**Part:** II — SERVICE PROVIDERS
**Section:** 397.481
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

All service providers as defined in and governed by this chapter are also subject to part IV of chapter 394, the Community Alcohol, Drug Abuse, and Mental Health Services Act.

**History:** s. 3, ch. 93-39.
