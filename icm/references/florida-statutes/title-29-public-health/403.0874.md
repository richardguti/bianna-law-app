# Fla. Stat. § 403.0874 — Air Pollution Control Trust Fund.

**Title:** XXIX — PUBLIC HEALTH
**Chapter:** 403 — ENVIRONMENTAL CONTROL
**Part:** I — POLLUTION CONTROL
**Section:** 403.0874
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
The Air Pollution Control Trust Fund is established in and administered by the Department of Environmental Protection.

(2)  
Funds to be credited to and uses of the trust fund shall be administered in accordance with ss. 320.03, 376.60, 403.0872, and 403.0873.

(3)  
Notwithstanding s. 216.301 and pursuant to s. 216.351, any balance in the trust fund at the end of a fiscal year shall remain in the trust fund and shall be available for carrying out the purposes of the trust fund.

**History:** s. 3, ch. 2015-8.
