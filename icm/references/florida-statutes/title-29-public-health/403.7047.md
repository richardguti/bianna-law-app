# Fla. Stat. § 403.7047 — Regulation of fossil fuel combustion products.

**Title:** XXIX — PUBLIC HEALTH
**Chapter:** 403 — ENVIRONMENTAL CONTROL
**Part:** IV — RESOURCE RECOVERY AND MANAGEMENT
**Section:** 403.7047
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

——

(1)  
Each county is encouraged to form multicounty regional solutions to the capture and reuse or sale of methane gas from landfills and wastewater treatment facilities.

; s. 6, ch. 2010-143.

C class="Text Intro Justify">In order to assist in achieving the municipal solid waste reduction goal and the recycling provisions of s. 403.706(2), a county or a municipality which owns or operates a solid waste management facility is hereby authorized to charge solid waste disposal fees which may vary based on a number of factors, including, but not limited to, the amount, characteristics, and form of recyclable materials present in the solid waste that is brought to the county's or the municipality's facility for processing or disposal.
