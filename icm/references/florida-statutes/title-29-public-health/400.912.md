# Fla. Stat. § 400.912 — Closing of a PPEC center.

**Title:** XXIX — PUBLIC HEALTH
**Chapter:** 400 — NURSING HOMES AND RELATED HEALTH CARE FACILITIES
**Part:** VI — PRESCRIBED PEDIATRIC EXTENDED CARE CENTERS
**Section:** 400.912
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Whenever a PPEC center voluntarily discontinues operation, it shall, at least 30 days before the discontinuance of operation, inform each child's legal guardian of the fact and the proposed time of such discontinuance.

**History:** ss. 12, 17, ch. 87-290; ss. 9, 14, ch. 93-66; s. 19, ch. 98-288; s. 100, ch. 2007-230.

**Note:** Former s. 391.212.
