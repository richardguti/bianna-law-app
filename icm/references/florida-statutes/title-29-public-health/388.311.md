# Fla. Stat. § 388.311 — Carry over of state funds and local funds.

**Title:** XXIX — PUBLIC HEALTH
**Chapter:** 388 — MOSQUITO CONTROL
**Part:** II — SMOKING AND VAPING
**Section:** 388.311
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

State and local funds budgeted for the control of mosquitoes and other arthropods shall be carried over at the end of the program's fiscal year, and rebudgeted for such control measures the following fiscal year.

**History:** s. 2, ch. 59-195; s. 18, ch. 2025-22.
