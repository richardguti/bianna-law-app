# Fla. Stat. § 288.71025 — Prohibited acts; penalties.

**Title:** XIX — PUBLIC BUSINESS
**Chapter:** 288 — COMMERCIAL DEVELOPMENT AND CAPITAL IMPROVEMENTS
**Part:** IV — SMALL AND MINORITY BUSINESS
**Section:** 288.71025
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
It is unlawful for any person to hold itself out as a black business investment corporation without being certified as eligible to participate in the Florida Black Business Loan Program.

(2)  
In addition to any other penalties or remedies provided under law, the department may bring a civil action in any court of competent jurisdiction against any person for a knowing or willful violation of this section. Upon an adverse adjudication, the court may impose a civil penalty of up to $500 and payment of court costs and reasonable attorney's fees incurred by the plaintiff.

**History:** s. 12, ch. 2007-157; s. 5, ch. 2010-39; s. 39, ch. 2013-15.
