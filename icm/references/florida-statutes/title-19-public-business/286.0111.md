# Fla. Stat. § 286.0111 — Legislative review of certain exemptions from requirements for public meetings and recordkeeping by governmental entities.

**Title:** XIX — PUBLIC BUSINESS
**Chapter:** 286 — PUBLIC BUSINESS: MISCELLANEOUS PROVISIONS
**Part:** II — GAMING COMPACT
**Section:** 286.0111
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The provisions of s. 119.15, the Open Government Sunset Review Act, apply to the provisions of law which provide exemptions to s. 286.011, as provided in s. 119.15.

**History:** s. 9, ch. 84-298; s. 2, ch. 85-301; s. 3, ch. 95-217; s. 53, ch. 2008-4.
