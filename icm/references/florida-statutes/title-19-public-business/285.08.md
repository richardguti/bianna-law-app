# Fla. Stat. § 285.08 — Definitions.

**Title:** XIX — PUBLIC BUSINESS
**Chapter:** 285 — INDIAN RESERVATIONS AND AFFAIRS
**Part:** I — INDIAN AFFAIRS
**Section:** 285.08
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

For the purpose of ss. 285.09-285.13:
(1)  
“Tribe” means the Seminole Tribe in the state composed of bands of Indians known and referred to as Miccosukee and Muskogee or Cow Creek.

(2)  
“Indian” or “Indians” means one or more members of a tribe.

(3)  
“Trustee” means the Board of Trustees of the Internal Improvement Trust Fund.

(4)  
“Reservation” means that tract of land of approximately 104,800 acres located in Palm Beach and Broward Counties set aside for the perpetual use and benefit of Seminole Indians by Legislative Acts of 1917 and 1935, known as the Seminole Indian Reservation.

Purpose of law.
——

The purpose of ss. 285.07-285.13 is to protect the Seminole Indians of Florida against undue and unnecessary hardships during these difficult years of transition from their ancestral culture to the culture of the white person's civilization and to aid said Indians to obtain economic independence as a tribe and as individuals.
