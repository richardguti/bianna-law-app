# Fla. Stat. § 279.19 — Covenant of state.

**Title:** XIX — PUBLIC BUSINESS
**Chapter:** 279 — REGISTERED PUBLIC OBLIGATIONS
**Part:** III — WILD AND SCENIC RIVERS
**Section:** 279.19
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The State of Florida hereby covenants with the owners of any registered public obligations that it will not amend or repeal this act if the effect may be to impair the exemption from income taxation of interest on registered public obligations.

**History:** s. 14, ch. 83-271.
