# Fla. Stat. § 287.131 — Assistance of Department of Financial Services.

**Title:** XIX — PUBLIC BUSINESS
**Chapter:** 287 — PROCUREMENT OF PERSONAL PROPERTY AND SERVICES
**Part:** I — COMMODITIES, INSURANCE, AND CONTRACTUAL SERVICES
**Section:** 287.131
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The Department of Financial Services shall provide the Department of Management Services with technical assistance in all matters pertaining to the purchase of insurance for all agencies, and shall make surveys of the insurance needs of the state and all departments thereof, including the benefits, if any, of self-insurance.

**History:** ss. 13, 22, 35, ch. 69-106; s. 87, ch. 98-279; s. 338, ch. 2003-261.
