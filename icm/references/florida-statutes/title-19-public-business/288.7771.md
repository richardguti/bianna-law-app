# Fla. Stat. § 288.7771 — Annual report of Florida Export Finance Corporation.

**Title:** XIX — PUBLIC BUSINESS
**Chapter:** 288 — COMMERCIAL DEVELOPMENT AND CAPITAL IMPROVEMENTS
**Part:** IV — SMALL AND MINORITY BUSINESS
**Section:** 288.7771
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The corporation shall annually prepare and submit to the department for inclusion in its annual report required under s. 20.60 a complete and detailed report setting forth:
(1)  
The report required in s. 288.776(3).

(2)  
Its assets and liabilities at the end of its most recent fiscal year.
