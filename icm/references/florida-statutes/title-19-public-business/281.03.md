# Fla. Stat. § 281.03 — Incident reports and record retention.

**Title:** XIX — PUBLIC BUSINESS
**Chapter:** 281 — SAFETY AND SECURITY SERVICES
**Part:** III — WILD AND SCENIC RIVERS
**Section:** 281.03
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The Department of Management Services shall make provision for the collection and retention of copies of reports relating to criminal activity or other safety-related and security-related incidents occurring on state-owned property for use in ongoing security planning and to fulfill its responsibilities under s. 281.02.

**History:** s. 2, ch. 76-247; s. 7, ch. 84-143; s. 5, ch. 84-274; s. 65, ch. 85-80; s. 3, ch. 91-54; s. 59, ch. 98-279; s. 30, ch. 99-399; s. 6, ch. 2002-21.

**Note:** Former s. 287.36.
