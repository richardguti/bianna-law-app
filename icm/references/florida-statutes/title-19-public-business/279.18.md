# Fla. Stat. § 279.18 — Laws in conflict with act superseded.

**Title:** XIX — PUBLIC BUSINESS
**Chapter:** 279 — REGISTERED PUBLIC OBLIGATIONS
**Part:** III — WILD AND SCENIC RIVERS
**Section:** 279.18
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Any special or general law in conflict with the provisions of this act authorizing the issuance of bonds in registered form is hereby superseded to the extent of such conflict.

**History:** s. 16, ch. 83-271.
