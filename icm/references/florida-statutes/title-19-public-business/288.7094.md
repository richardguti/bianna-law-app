# Fla. Stat. § 288.7094 — Black business investment corporations.

**Title:** XIX — PUBLIC BUSINESS
**Chapter:** 288 — COMMERCIAL DEVELOPMENT AND CAPITAL IMPROVEMENTS
**Part:** IV — SMALL AND MINORITY BUSINESS
**Section:** 288.7094
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
The term “black business investment corporation” means a corporation that provides loans, loan guarantees, or investments to black business enterprises under s. 288.7102.

(2)  
A black business investment corporation that meets the requirements of s. 288.7102(4) is eligible to participate in the Black Business Loan Program and shall receive priority consideration by the department for participation in the program.

**History:** s. 10, ch. 2007-157; s. 3, ch. 2008-140; s. 175, ch. 2011-142.
