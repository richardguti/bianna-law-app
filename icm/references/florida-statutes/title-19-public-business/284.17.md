# Fla. Stat. § 284.17 — Rules.

**Title:** XIX — PUBLIC BUSINESS
**Chapter:** 284 — STATE RISK MANAGEMENT AND SAFETY PROGRAMS
**Part:** II — STATE CASUALTY CLAIMS
**Section:** 284.17
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The Department of Financial Services has authority to adopt rules pursuant to ss. 120.536(1) and 120.54 to implement the provisions of this chapter.

**History:** s. 8, ch. 70-272; s. 56, ch. 98-200; s. 315, ch. 2003-261.
