# Fla. Stat. § 284.39 — Adoption of rules.

**Title:** XIX — PUBLIC BUSINESS
**Chapter:** 284 — STATE RISK MANAGEMENT AND SAFETY PROGRAMS
**Part:** II — STATE CASUALTY CLAIMS
**Section:** 284.39
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The Department of Financial Services may adopt rules for the proper management and maintenance of the fund.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 72-206; s. 324, ch. 2003-261.
