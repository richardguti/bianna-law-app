# Fla. Stat. § 290.00710 — Enterprise zone designation for the City of Lakeland.

**Title:** XIX — PUBLIC BUSINESS
**Chapter:** 290 — URBAN REDEVELOPMENT
**Part:** XII — FLORIDA OFFICE OF BROADBAND
**Section:** 290.00710
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

——

The City of Lakeland may apply to the department for designation of one enterprise zone for an area within the City of Lakeland, which zone shall encompass an area up to 10 square miles.
