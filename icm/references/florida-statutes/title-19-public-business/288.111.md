# Fla. Stat. § 288.111 — Information concerning local manufacturing development programs.

**Title:** XIX — PUBLIC BUSINESS
**Chapter:** 288 — COMMERCIAL DEVELOPMENT AND CAPITAL IMPROVEMENTS
**Part:** I — GENERAL PROVISIONS
**Section:** 288.111
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The department shall develop materials that identify each local government that establishes a local manufacturing development program under s. 163.3252. The materials, which the department may elect to develop and maintain in electronic format or in any other format deemed by the department to provide public access, must be updated at least annually. State agencies may distribute the materials to prospective, new, expanding, and relocating businesses seeking to conduct business in this state.

**History:** s. 5, ch. 2013-224; s. 54, ch. 2023-173.
