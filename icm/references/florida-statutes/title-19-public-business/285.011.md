# Fla. Stat. § 285.011 — Seminole Indian lands; trustee.

**Title:** XIX — PUBLIC BUSINESS
**Chapter:** 285 — INDIAN RESERVATIONS AND AFFAIRS
**Part:** I — INDIAN AFFAIRS
**Section:** 285.011
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The Board of Trustees of the Internal Improvement Trust Fund shall hereafter serve as the trustee of all Seminole Indian lands, and title to all such lands shall be vested in said board to be held in trust for the perpetual use and benefit of the Seminole Indians and as a reservation for them.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 71-286.
