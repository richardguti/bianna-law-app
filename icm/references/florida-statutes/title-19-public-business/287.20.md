# Fla. Stat. § 287.20 — Applicability of this part.

**Title:** XIX — PUBLIC BUSINESS
**Chapter:** 288 — COMMERCIAL DEVELOPMENT AND CAPITAL IMPROVEMENTS
**Part:** II — MEANS OF TRANSPORT
**Section:** 287.20
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The provisions of this part apply to motor vehicles, watercraft, and aircraft owned, leased, or acquired in any manner by any state agency, or the judicial branch, as defined in s. 216.011.

**History:** s. 22, ch. 69-106; s. 7, ch. 83-304; s. 81, ch. 92-142.
