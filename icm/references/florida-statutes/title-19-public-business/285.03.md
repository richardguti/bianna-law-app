# Fla. Stat. § 285.03 — Grant of Florida lands to Seminole Indians.

**Title:** XIX — PUBLIC BUSINESS
**Chapter:** 285 — INDIAN RESERVATIONS AND AFFAIRS
**Part:** I — INDIAN AFFAIRS
**Section:** 285.03
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)(a)  
A grant is made, for use of the Seminole Indians of Florida, of a tract of land situated in Broward County, described as follows:

(b)  
Beginning 330 feet west of the northeast corner of lot fourteen, of section thirty-six, township fifty south, range forty-one east; thence west 495 feet; thence south 1,320 feet, thence east 495 feet, thence north 1,320 feet to point of beginning, being 15 acres, more or less.

(2)  
The said described lands shall become a part of the Seminole Indian Reservation, reserved by Act of Legislature, 1931, to use of the Seminole Indians of Florida.

(3)  
If, at any time, said F.S. 285.0116285.011  
Seminole Indian lands; trustee.
——

The Board of Trustees of the Internal Improvement Trust Fund shall hereafter serve as the trustee of all Seminole Indian lands, and title to all such lands shall be vested in said board to be held in trust for the perpetual use and benefit of the Seminole Indians and as a reservation for them.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 71-286.
