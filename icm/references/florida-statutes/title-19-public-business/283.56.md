# Fla. Stat. § 283.56 — Preparation of agency publications.

**Title:** XIX — PUBLIC BUSINESS
**Chapter:** 283 — PUBLIC PRINTING
**Part:** III — COMMUNICATION INFORMATION TECHNOLOGY SERVICES
**Section:** 283.56
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

When economical and practicable, any publication produced for public distribution shall display the name of such publication on its spine, where such spine is glued to the cover and measures at least one-quarter inch in width.

**History:** s. 11, ch. 90-335.
