# Fla. Stat. § 285.07 — Purpose of law.

**Title:** XIX — PUBLIC BUSINESS
**Chapter:** 285 — INDIAN RESERVATIONS AND AFFAIRS
**Part:** I — INDIAN AFFAIRS
**Section:** 285.07
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The purpose of ss. 285.07-285.13 is to protect the Seminole Indians of Florida against undue and unnecessary hardships during these difficult years of transition from their ancestral culture to the culture of the white person's civilization and to aid said Indians to obtain economic independence as a tribe and as individuals.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 29908, 1955; s. 11, ch. 91-221; s. 206, ch. 95-148.
