# Fla. Stat. § 282.3186 — Ransomware incident compliance.

**Title:** XIX — PUBLIC BUSINESS
**Chapter:** 282 — COMMUNICATIONS AND DATA PROCESSING
**Part:** III — WILD AND SCENIC RIVERS
**Section:** 282.3186
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

A state agency as defined in s. 282.318(2), a county, or a municipality experiencing a ransomware incident may not pay or otherwise comply with a ransom demand.

**History:** s. 4, ch. 2022-220.
