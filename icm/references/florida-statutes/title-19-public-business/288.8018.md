# Fla. Stat. § 288.8018 — Gulf Coast audits.

**Title:** XIX — PUBLIC BUSINESS
**Chapter:** 288 — COMMERCIAL DEVELOPMENT AND CAPITAL IMPROVEMENTS
**Part:** VI — GULF COAST ECONOMIC CORRIDOR
**Section:** 288.8018
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
The scope of a financial audit conducted pursuant to s. A contract executed by Triumph Gulf Coast, Inc., with an awardee must include provisions requiring a performance report on the contracted activities, must account for the proper use of funds provided under the contract, and must include provisions for recovery of awards in the event the award was based upon fraudulent information or the awardee is not meeting the performance requirements of the award. Awardees must regularly report to Triumph Gulf Coast, Inc., the expenditure of funds and the status of the project or program on a schedule determined by the corporation.

**History:** s. 58, ch. 2013-39; s. 7, ch. 2017-63.
