# Fla. Stat. § 282.708 — Emergency assumption of control.

**Title:** XIX — PUBLIC BUSINESS
**Chapter:** 282 — COMMUNICATIONS AND DATA PROCESSING
**Part:** III — COMMUNICATION INFORMATION TECHNOLOGY SERVICES
**Section:** 282.708
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

In the event of an emergency, the Governor may direct emergency management assumption of control over all or part of the state communications system.

**History:** s. 22, ch. 69-106; s. 37, ch. 83-334; s. 23, ch. 2009-80.

**Note:** Former s. 287.28; s. 282.109.
