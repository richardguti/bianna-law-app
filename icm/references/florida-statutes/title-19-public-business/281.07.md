# Fla. Stat. § 281.07 — Rules; Facilities Program; traffic regulation.

**Title:** XIX — PUBLIC BUSINESS
**Chapter:** 281 — SAFETY AND SECURITY SERVICES
**Part:** III — WILD AND SCENIC RIVERS
**Section:** 281.07
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
The Department of Management Services shall adopt and promulgate rules to govern the administration, operation, and management of the Facilities Program and to regulate traffic and parking on state-owned property, including the Capitol Complex, which rules are not in conflict with any state law or county or municipal ordinance, and to carry out the provisions of ss. 281.02-281.08.

(2)  
Political subdivisions and municipalities may enact and enforce ordinances on the violation of traffic and parking rules provided in subsection (1).
