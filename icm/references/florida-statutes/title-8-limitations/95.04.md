# Fla. Stat. § 95.04 — Promise to pay barred debt.

**Title:** VIII — LIMITATIONS
**Chapter:** 95 — LIMITATIONS OF ACTIONS; ADVERSE POSSESSION
**Part:** IX — MISCELLANEOUS PROVISIONS
**Section:** 95.04
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

An acknowledgment of, or promise to pay, a debt barred by a statute of limitations must be in writing and signed by the person sought to be charged.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 4375, 1895; GS 1717; RGS 2930; CGL 4650; s. 6, ch. 74-382.
