# Fla. Stat. § 95.371 — Actions against real estate appraisers and appraisal management companies.

**Title:** VIII — LIMITATIONS
**Chapter:** 95 — LIMITATIONS OF ACTIONS; ADVERSE POSSESSION
**Part:** IX — MISCELLANEOUS PROVISIONS
**Section:** 95.371
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

(1)  
For purposes of this section, the terms opan class="Number">(a)  
The secretary of the Department of Transportation, or the secretary's designee, if the road is a road in the State Highway System or State Park Road System;

(b)  
The chair and clerk of the board of county commissioners of the county, if the road is a county road; or

(c)  
The mayor and clerk of the municipality, if the road is a municipal road or street,

shall be prima facie evidence of ownership of the land by the state, county, or municipality, as the case may be.
