# Fla. Stat. § 177.121 — Misdemeanor to molest monument or deface or destroy map or plat.

**Title:** XII — MUNICIPALITIES
**Chapter:** 177 — LAND BOUNDARIES
**Part:** I — PLATTING
**Section:** 177.121
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

It is a misdemeanor of the second degree, punishable as provided in s. 775.082 or s. 775.083, for any person to molest any monuments established according to this part or to deface or destroy any map or plat placed on public record.

**History:** s. 1A, ch. 71-339; s. 10, ch. 98-20.
