# Fla. Stat. § 177.508 — Private practice not affected.

**Title:** XII — MUNICIPALITIES
**Chapter:** 177 — LAND BOUNDARIES
**Part:** III — RESTORATION OF CORNERS
**Section:** 177.508
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Nothing in this part restricts or limits the actions or practice of surveyors and mappers as provided in chapter 472.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 77-361; s. 111, ch. 94-119.
