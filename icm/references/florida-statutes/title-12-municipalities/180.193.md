# Fla. Stat. § 180.193 — Regional utilities authority created by Legislature through charter amendment; preempted to state.

**Title:** XII — MUNICIPALITIES
**Chapter:** 180 — MUNICIPAL PUBLIC WORKS
**Part:** III — RESTORATION OF CORNERS
**Section:** 180.193
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
The subject of a regional utilities authority created by the Legislature through charter amendment after January 1, 2023, is expressly preempted to the state.

(2)  
This section shall take effect upon this act becoming a law.

**History:** s. 4, ch. 2026-135.
