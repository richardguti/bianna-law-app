# Fla. Stat. § 166.141 — Full authority for issuance of bonds.

**Title:** XII — MUNICIPALITIES
**Chapter:** 166 — MUNICIPALITIES
**Part:** III — MUNICIPAL FINANCE AND TAXATION
**Section:** 166.141
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

This part shall be full authority for the issuance of bonds authorized herein.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 73-129.
