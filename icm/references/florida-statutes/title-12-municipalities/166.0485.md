# Fla. Stat. § 166.0485 — Establishment of neighborhood crime watch programs.

**Title:** XII — MUNICIPALITIES
**Chapter:** 166 — MUNICIPALITIES
**Part:** I — GENERAL PROVISIONS
**Section:** 166.0485
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

A county sheriff or municipal police department may establish neighborhood crime watch programs within the county or municipality. The participants of a neighborhood crime watch program shall include, but need not be limited to, residents of the county or municipality and owners of businesses located within the county or municipality.

**History:** s. 2, ch. 2026-55.
