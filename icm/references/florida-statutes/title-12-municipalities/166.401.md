# Fla. Stat. § 166.401 — Right of eminent domain; procedure; compliance with limitations.

**Title:** XII — MUNICIPALITIES
**Chapter:** 166 — MUNICIPALITIES
**Part:** III — MUNICIPAL FINANCE AND TAXATION
**Section:** 166.401
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
All municipalities in the state may exercise the right and power of eminent domain; that is, the right to appropriate property within the state, except state or federal property, for the uses or purposes authorized pursuant to this part. The absolute fee simple title to all property so taken and acquired shall vest in such municipal corporation unless the municipality seeks to condemn a particular right or estate in such property.
