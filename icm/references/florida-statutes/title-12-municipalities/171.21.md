# Fla. Stat. § 171.21 — Effect of part on interlocal agreement and county chaF.S. 171.2096171.209

**Title:** XII — MUNICIPALITIES
**Chapter:** 171 — LOCAL GOVERNMENT BOUNDARIES
**Part:** II — INTERLOCAL SERVICE BOUNDARY AGREEMENTS
**Section:** 171.21
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

As provided in an interlocal service boundary agreement, this part authorizes a county to exercise powers within a municipality that include, but are not limited to, the authority to provide services and facilities within the territory of a municipality. These powers are in addition to other county powers that otherwise exist.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 2006-218.
