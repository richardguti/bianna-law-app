# Fla. Stat. § 166.021until

**Title:** XII — MUNICIPALITIES
**Chapter:** 166 — MUNICIPALITIES
**Part:** I — GENERAL PROVISIONS
**Section:** 166.021until
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

166.021until otherwise amended. A municipality or agency thereof that provides any per diem and travel expense policy pursuant to this subsection shall be deemed to be exempt from all provisions of s. 112.061. A municipality or agency thereof that does not provide a per diem and travel expense policy pursuant to this subsection remains subject to all provisions of s. 112.061.
