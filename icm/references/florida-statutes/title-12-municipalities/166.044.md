# Fla. Stat. § 166.044 — Ordinances relating to possession or sale of ammunition.

**Title:** XII — MUNICIPALITIES
**Chapter:** 166 — MUNICIPALITIES
**Part:** I — GENERAL PROVISIONS
**Section:** 166.044
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

No municipality may adopt any ordinance relating to the possession or sale of ammunition. Any such ordinance in effect on June 24, 1983, is void.

**History:** s. 2, ch. 83-253.
