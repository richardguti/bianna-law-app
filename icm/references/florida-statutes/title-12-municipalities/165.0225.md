# Fla. Stat. § 165.0225 — Counties prohibited from requiring consideration for allowing incorporation.

**Title:** XII — MUNICIPALITIES
**Chapter:** 165 — FORMATION OF LOCAL GOVERNMENTS
**Part:** VI — COLLABORATIVE CLIENT INFORMATION SYSTEMS
**Section:** 165.0225
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Notwithstanding s. 165.022, any municipality formed after January 1, 2000, and any municipality formed on or after July 1, 2007, shall not be required to pay any charge, assessment, tax, fee, or other consideration as a condition for allowing the citizens of an area to incorporate and govern themselves.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 2007-26.
