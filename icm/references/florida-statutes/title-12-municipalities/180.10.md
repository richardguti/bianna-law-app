# Fla. Stat. § 180.10 — When election necessary.

**Title:** XII — MUNICIPALITIES
**Chapter:** 180 — MUNICIPAL PUBLIC WORKS
**Part:** III — RESTORATION OF CORNERS
**Section:** 180.10
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Where any mortgage revenue certificates, debentures, or other evidences of indebtedness shall come within the purview of s. 12, Art. VII of the State Constitution, the same shall be issued only after having been approved by a majority of the votes cast in an election in which a majority of the owners of freeholds not wholly exempt from taxation who are qualified electors residing in such municipality shall participate, pursuant to the provisions of ss. 100.201-100.221, 100.241, 100.261-100.341, and 100.351.
