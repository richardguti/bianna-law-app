# Fla. Stat. § 166.122 — Establishment of sinking funds.

**Title:** XII — MUNICIPALITIES
**Chapter:** 166 — MUNICIPALITIES
**Part:** II — MUNICIPAL BORROWING
**Section:** 166.122
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The governing body of a municipality may establish and administer such sinking funds as it deems necessary or convenient for the payment, purchase, or redemption of any outstanding bonded indebtedness of the municipality.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 73-129.
