# Fla. Stat. § 339.04 — Disposition of proceeds of sale or lease of realty by the department.

**Title:** XXVI — PUBLIC TRANSPORTATION
**Chapter:** 339 — TRANSPORTATION FINANCE AND PLANNING
**Part:** III — SPACEFLIGHT
**Section:** 339.04
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Any money derived from the sale, lease, or conveyance of any property by the department shall be deposited into the State Treasury and placed in the State Transportation Trust Fund or other appropriate department trust fund as required by law or when needed to comply with bond covenants.

**History:** s. 135, ch. 29965, 1955; s. 2, ch. 61-119; ss. 23, 35, ch. 69-106; s. 1, ch. 71-39; ss. 2, 3, ch. 73-57; s. 203, ch. 84-309; s. 47, ch. 93-164.
