# Fla. Stat. § 338.225 — Taking of public road for feeder road.

**Title:** XXVI — PUBLIC TRANSPORTATION
**Chapter:** 338 — LIMITED ACCESS AND TOLL FACILITIES
**Part:** III — SPACEFLIGHT
**Section:** 338.225
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Before taking over any existing public road for maintenance and operation as a feeder road, the department shall obtain the consent of the governmental entity then exercising jurisdiction over the road, which governmental entity is authorized to give such consent by resolution. Each feeder road or portion of a feeder road acquired, constructed, or taken over under this section for maintenance and operation shall, for all purposes of ss. 338.22-338.241, be deemed to constitute a part of the turnpike system, except that no toll shall be charged for transit between points on such feeder road.
