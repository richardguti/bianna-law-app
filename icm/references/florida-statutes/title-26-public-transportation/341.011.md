# Fla. Stat. § 341.011 — Florida Public Transit Act; short title.

**Title:** XXVI — PUBLIC TRANSPORTATION
**Chapter:** 341 — PUBLIC TRANSIT
**Part:** III — SPACEFLIGHT
**Section:** 341.011
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)
