# Fla. Stat. § 334.615 — Parking authority operations; interlocal agreements.

**Title:** XXVI — PUBLIC TRANSPORTATION
**Chapter:** 334 — TRANSPORTATION ADMINISTRATION
**Part:** III — SPACEFLIGHT
**Section:** 334.615
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

A parking authority created by special act may operate, manage, and control parking facilities in contiguous counties, municipalities, or other local governmental entities upon entering into interlocal agreements with the governing bodies of the appropriate contiguous counties, municipalities, or local governmental entities.

**History:** s. 24, ch. 2025-155.
