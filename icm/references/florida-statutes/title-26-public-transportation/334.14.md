# Fla. Stat. § 334.14 — Employees of department who are required to be engineers.

**Title:** XXVI — PUBLIC TRANSPORTATION
**Chapter:** 334 — TRANSPORTATION ADMINISTRATION
**Part:** III — SPACEFLIGHT
**Section:** 334.14
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Each employee performing engineering as defined in chapter 471 shall be registered in accordance with the provisions of chapter 471.
