# Fla. Stat. § 341.842 — Liberal construction.

**Title:** XXVI — PUBLIC TRANSPORTATION
**Chapter:** 341 — PUBLIC TRANSIT
**Part:** III — SPACEFLIGHT
**Section:** 341.842
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

This act, being necessary for the welfare of the state and its inhabitants, shall be liberally construed to effect the purposes hereof.

**History:** s. 50, ch. 2002-20.
