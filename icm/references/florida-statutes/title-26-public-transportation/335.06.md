# Fla. Stat. § 335.06 — Access roads to the state park system.

**Title:** XXVI — PUBLIC TRANSPORTATION
**Chapter:** 335 — STATE HIGHWAY SYSTEM
**Part:** III — SPACEFLIGHT
**Section:** 335.06
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Any road that provides access to property within the state park system shall be maintained by the department if the road is a part of the State Highway System and may be improved and maintained by the department if the road is part of a county road system or city street system. If the department does not maintain a county or city road that provides access to the state park system, the road shall be maintained by the appropriate county or municipality.

**History:** s. 30, ch. 29965, 1955; ss. 23, 25, 35, ch. 69-106; s. 37, ch. 84-309; s. 3, ch. 2014-169; s. 7, ch. 2014-223.
