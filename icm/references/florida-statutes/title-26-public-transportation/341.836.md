# Fla. Stat. § 341.836 — Associated development.

**Title:** XXVI — PUBLIC TRANSPORTATION
**Chapter:** 341 — PUBLIC TRANSIT
**Part:** III — SPACEFLIGHT
**Section:** 341.836
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

——

(1)  
The enterprise, alone or as part of a joint development, may undertake associated developments to be a source of revenue for the establishment, construction, operation, or maintenance of the high-speed rail system. Such associated developments must be consistent, to the extent feasible, with applicable local government comprehensive plans and local land development regulations and otherwise be in compliance with ss. 341.8201-341.842.
