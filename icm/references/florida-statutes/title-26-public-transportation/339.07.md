# Fla. Stat. § 339.07 — National aid expended under supervision of the department.

**Title:** XXVI — PUBLIC TRANSPORTATION
**Chapter:** 339 — TRANSPORTATION FINANCE AND PLANNING
**Part:** III — SPACEFLIGHT
**Section:** 339.07
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

All funds and all road building equipment, supplies, and materials that have heretofore or may hereafter be apportioned to this state by Congress to aid and assist in road building shall be expended and used under the control and supervision of the department, and any and all expenses which are necessary to secure such equipment, supplies, and materials for the use of the state to be used on the roadF.S. 339.066339.06  
Authority of department to amortize advancements from United States.
——

The department may set aside, from any revenues allocated to it by law, such sums as are necessary and sufficient to properly amortize any amount advanced under Act of Congress and to make suitable provision from year to year in its annual budget for such amortization.

**History:** s. 137, ch. 29965, 1955; ss. 23, 35, ch. 69-106; s. 205, ch. 84-309.
