# Fla. Stat. § 338.241 — Cash reserve requirement.

**Title:** XXVI — PUBLIC TRANSPORTATION
**Chapter:** 338 — LIMITED ACCESS AND TOLL FACILITIES
**Part:** III — SPACEFLIGHT
**Section:** 338.241
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The budget for the turnpike system shall be so planned as to provide for a cash reserve at the end of each fiscal year of not less than 5 percent of the unpaid balance of all turnpike system contractual obligations, excluding bond obligations, to be paid from revenues.

**History:** s. 3, ch. 63-257; s. 1, ch. 65-158; ss. 2, 3, ch. 67-371; ss. 23, 31, 35, ch. 69-106; s. 119, ch. 79-190; s. 1, ch. 82-162; s. 201, ch. 84-309; s. 16, ch. 88-286; s. 24, ch. 2002-20.

**Note:** Former s. 340.35.
