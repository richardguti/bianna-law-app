# Fla. Stat. § 337.108 — Hazardous materials and pollutants; indemnification.

**Title:** XXVI — PUBLIC TRANSPORTATION
**Chapter:** 337 — CONTRACTING; ACQUISITION, DISPOSAL, AND USE OF PROPERTY
**Part:** III — SPACEFLIGHT
**Section:** 337.108
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
For purposes of this section:
(a)  
The term “hazardous materials” shall have the same meaning as provided in s. 768.128(1)(a).

(b)  
The term “pollutants” shall have the same meaning as provided in s. 376.031.
