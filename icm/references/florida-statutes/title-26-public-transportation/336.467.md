# Fla. Stat. § 336.467 — County-state right-of-way acquisition agreements.

**Title:** XXVI — PUBLIC TRANSPORTATION
**Chapter:** 336 — COUNTY ROAD SYSTEM
**Part:** III — SPACEFLIGHT
**Section:** 336.467
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

A county or other governmental entity may enter into an agreement with the department to provide for the department to acquire rights-of-way for the county or other governmental entity.

**History:** s. 110, ch. 84-309; s. 42, ch. 2003-286.
