# Fla. Stat. § 343.63 — Central Florida Regional Transportation Authority.

**Title:** XXVI — PUBLIC TRANSPORTATION
**Chapter:** 343 — REGIONAL TRANSPORTATION
**Part:** II — CENTRAL FLORIDA REGIONAL TRANSPORTATION AUTHORITY
**Section:** 343.63
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
There is created and established a body politic and corporate, an agency of the state, to be known as the “Central Florida Regional Transportation Authority,” hereinafter referred to as the “authority.”

(2)  
The governing board of the authority shall consist of five voting members as follows:
(a)  
The chairs of the county commissions of Seminole, Orange, and Osceola Counties, or another member of the commission designated by the county chair, shall each serve as a representative on the board for the full extent of his or her term.
