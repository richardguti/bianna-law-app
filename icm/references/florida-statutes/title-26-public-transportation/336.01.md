# Fla. Stat. § 336.01 — Designation of county road system.

**Title:** XXVI — PUBLIC TRANSPORTATION
**Chapter:** 336 — COUNTY ROAD SYSTEM
**Part:** III — SPACEFLIGHT
**Section:** 336.01
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The county road system shall be as defined in s. 334.03(8).

**History:** s. 41, ch. 29965, 1955; s. 10, ch. 77-165; s. 37, ch. 91-221; s. 120, ch. 99-13; s. 81, ch. 99-385.
