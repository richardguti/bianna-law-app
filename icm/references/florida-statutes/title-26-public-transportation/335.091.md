# Fla. Stat. § 335.091 — Blue Star Memorial Highway designation.

**Title:** XXVI — PUBLIC TRANSPORTATION
**Chapter:** 335 — STATE HIGHWAY SYSTEM
**Part:** III — SPACEFLIGHT
**Section:** 335.091
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
The head of the department, in cooperation with the Florida Federation of Garden Clubs, Inc., is authorized to designate certain roads in this state as Blue Star Memorial highways in honor of those individuals who have served or are serving in the Armed Forces of the United States.

Uniform erection and maintenance of traffic control devices.
——

The department shall erect and maintain a uniform system of signs, signals, markings, and other traffic control devices for the regulation, control, guidance, and protection of traffic on the State Highway System. Such system shall conform to the department's uniform system of traffic control devices adopted pursuant to s. 316.0745.
