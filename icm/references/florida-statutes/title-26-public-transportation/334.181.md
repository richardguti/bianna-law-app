# Fla. Stat. § 334.181 — Electronic proof of delivery.

**Title:** XXVI — PUBLIC TRANSPORTATION
**Chapter:** 334 — TRANSPORTATION ADMINISTRATION
**Part:** III — SPACEFLIGHT
**Section:** 334.181
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Notwithstanding any law, rule, or ordinance to the contrary, a local governmental entity must accept an electronic proof of delivery as an official record for a material delivery on the local governmental entity's transportation project.

**History:** s. 8, ch. 2023-197.
