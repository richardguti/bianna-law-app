# Fla. Stat. § 347.20 — Vested rights not impaired.

**Title:** XXVI — PUBLIC TRANSPORTATION
**Chapter:** 347 — FERRIES, TOLL BRIDGES, DAMS, AND LOG DITCHES
**Part:** III — COASTAL LINK COMMUTER RAIL SERVICE ACT
**Section:** 347.20
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Nothing in this chapter shall affect or impair any right or privilege belonging to any individual or corporation by virtue of any law of this state.

**History:** s. 10, ch. 3039, 1877; RS 645; GS 918; RGS 1702; CGL 2753.
