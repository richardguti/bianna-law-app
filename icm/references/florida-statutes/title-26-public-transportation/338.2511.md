# Fla. Stat. § 338.2511 — Deposit and use of funds in Toll Facilities Revolving Trust Fund.

**Title:** XXVI — PUBLIC TRANSPORTATION
**Chapter:** 338 — LIMITED ACCESS AND TOLL FACILITIES
**Part:** III — SPACEFLIGHT
**Section:** 338.2511
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The funds in the Toll Facilities Revolving Trust Fund and all future payments of obligated funds shall be deposited into the State Transportation Trust Fund to be expended for the purposes specified in s. 339.08.

**History:** s. 41, ch. 2012-128.
