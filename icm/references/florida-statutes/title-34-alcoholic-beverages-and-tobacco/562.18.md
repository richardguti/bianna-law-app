# Fla. Stat. § 562.18 — Possession of beverage upon which federal tax unpaid.

**Title:** XXXIV — ALCOHOLIC BEVERAGES AND TOBACCO
**Chapter:** 562 — BEVERAGE LAW: ENFORCEMENT
**Part:** VI — VIRTUAL CURRENCY KIOSK BUSINESSES
**Section:** 562.18
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

It is unlawful for any person to have in her or his possession within this state any alcoholic beverage on which a federal excise tax is required to be paid, unless such federal excise tax has been paid as to such beverage.

**History:** s. 4, ch. 18015, 1937; CGL 1940 Supp. 4151(271d); s. 2, ch. 72-230; s. 864, ch. 97-103.
