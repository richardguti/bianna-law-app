# Fla. Stat. § 561.18 — License investigation.

**Title:** XXXIV — ALCOHOLIC BEVERAGES AND TOBACCO
**Chapter:** 561 — BEVERAGE LAW: ADMINISTRATION
**Part:** VI — VIRTUAL CURRENCY KIOSK BUSINESSES
**Section:** 561.18
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

After the application has been filed with the local district office supervisor, the district supervisor shall cause the application to be fully investigated, both as to qualifications of the applicants and a manager or person to be in charge and the premises and location sought to be licensed, except that, in the event of any licenses issued pursuant to s. 561.19(2), the division shall cause only those applications selected by public drawing in the order selected to be fully investigated.

**History:** s. 2, ch. 16774, 1935; CGL 1936 Supp. 4151(228); s. 5, ch. 25359, 1949; s. 15, ch. 57-420; s. 2, ch. 59-316; s. 1, ch. 72-230; s. 2, ch. 81-158.
