# Fla. Stat. § 562.24 — Administration of oaths by director or authorized employees.

**Title:** XXXIV — ALCOHOLIC BEVERAGES AND TOBACCO
**Chapter:** 562 — BEVERAGE LAW: ENFORCEMENT
**Part:** VI — VIRTUAL CURRENCY KIOSK BUSINESSES
**Section:** 562.24
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The director and authorized employees of the division may administer oaths or affirmations on statements of defendants charged with the violation of the Beverage Law and other things directly connected with the enforcement of said law.

**History:** s. 7, ch. 19301, 1939; CGL 1940 Supp. 4151(271-o); ss. 16, 35, ch. 69-106; s. 2, ch. 72-230; s. 23, ch. 79-11.
