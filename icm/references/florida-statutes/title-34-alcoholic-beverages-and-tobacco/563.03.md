# Fla. Stat. § 563.03 — Regulation concerning draft beer.

**Title:** XXXIV — ALCOHOLIC BEVERAGES AND TOBACCO
**Chapter:** 563 — BEER AND MALT BEVERAGES
**Part:** VI — VIRTUAL CURRENCY KIOSK BUSINESSES
**Section:** 563.03
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Each and every tap or spigot through which draft beer is served shall, on the handle of such tap or spigot in plain view of the consuming public, display the name of the beer being presently served through such tap or spigot.

**History:** s. 9, ch. 20830, 1941; s. 18, ch. 57-1; s. 3, ch. 72-230.

**Note:** Former s. 561.60.
