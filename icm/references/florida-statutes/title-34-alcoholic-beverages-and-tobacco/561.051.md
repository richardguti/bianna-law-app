# Fla. Stat. § 561.051 — Reporting requirements of director.

**Title:** XXXIV — ALCOHOLIC BEVERAGES AND TOBACCO
**Chapter:** 561 — BEVERAGE LAW: ADMINISTRATION
**Part:** VI — VIRTUAL CURRENCY KIOSK BUSINESSES
**Section:** 561.051
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The director of the division shall promptly report and remit to the Chief Financial Officer all taxes and fees collected by him or her hereunder.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 72-230; s. 15, ch. 79-11; s. 839, ch. 97-103; s. 40, ch. 98-34; s. 731, ch. 2003-261.
