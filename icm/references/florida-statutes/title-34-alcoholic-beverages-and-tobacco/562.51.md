# Fla. Stat. § 562.51 — Retail alcoholic beverage establishments; rights as private enterprise.

**Title:** XXXIV — ALCOHOLIC BEVERAGES AND TOBACCO
**Chapter:** 562 — BEVERAGE LAW: ENFORCEMENT
**Part:** VI — VIRTUAL CURRENCY KIOSK BUSINESSES
**Section:** 562.51
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

A licensed retail alcoholic beverage establishment open to the public is a private enterprise and:
(1)  
May refuse service to any person who is objectionable or undesirable to the licensee, but such refusal of service shall not be on the basis of race, creed, color, religion, sex, national origin, marital status, or physical handicap.

(2)(a)  
May not refuse service to any person solely because the person is not purchasing alcoholic beverages if that person is the designated driver for one or more persons who are purchasing alF.S. 562.506562.50  
Habitual drunkards; furnishing intoxicants to, after notice.
——

Any person who shall sell, give away, dispose of, exchange, or barter any alcoholic beverage, or any essence, extract, bitters, preparation, compound, composition, or any article whatsoever under any name, label, or brand, which produces intoxication, to any person habitually addicted to the use of any or all such intoxicating liquors, after having been given written notice by wife, husband, father, mother, sister, brother, child, or nearest relative that said person so addicted is an habitual drunkard and that the use of intoxicating drink or drinks is working an injury to the person using said liquors, or to the person giving said written notice, shall be guilty of a misdemeanor of the second degree, punishable as provided in s. 775.082 or s. 775.083.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 22633, 1945; s. 576, ch. 71-136; s. 2, ch. 72-230.
