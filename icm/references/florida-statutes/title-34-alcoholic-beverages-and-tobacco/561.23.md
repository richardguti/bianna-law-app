# Fla. Stat. § 561.23 — License display.

**Title:** XXXIV — ALCOHOLIC BEVERAGES AND TOBACCO
**Chapter:** 561 — BEVERAGE LAW: ADMINISTRATION
**Part:** VI — VIRTUAL CURRENCY KIOSK BUSINESSES
**Section:** 561.23
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

All vendors licensed under the Beverage Law shall display their licenses in conspicuous places on their licensed premises.
