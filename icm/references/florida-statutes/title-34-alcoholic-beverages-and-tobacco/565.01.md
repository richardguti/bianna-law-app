# Fla. Stat. § 565.01 — Definition; liquor.

**Title:** XXXIV — ALCOHOLIC BEVERAGES AND TOBACCO
**Chapter:** 565 — LIQUOR
**Part:** VI — VIRTUAL CURRENCY KIOSK BUSINESSES
**Section:** 565.01
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The words “liquor,” “distilled spirits,” “spirituous liquors,” “spirituous beverages,” or “distilled spirituous liquors” mean that substance known as ethyl alcohol, ethanol, or spirits of wine in any form, including all dilutions and mixtures thereof from whatever source or by whatever process produced.

**History:** s. 5, ch. 72-230; s. 14, ch. 86-269.

**Note:** Former s. 561.01(6).
