# Fla. Stat. § 565.16 — Beverage lists furnished to vendors.

**Title:** XXXIV — ALCOHOLIC BEVERAGES AND TOBACCO
**Chapter:** 565 — LIQUOR
**Part:** VI — VIRTUAL CURRENCY KIOSK BUSINESSES
**Section:** 565.16
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

A distributor of spirituous beverages in this state may furnish, give, rent, loan, or sell to a vendor, and a vendor may accept, alcoholic beverage lists, otherwise referred to as “wine lists,” without the same being a violation of s. 561.42(1).

**History:** s. 2, ch. 81-159.
