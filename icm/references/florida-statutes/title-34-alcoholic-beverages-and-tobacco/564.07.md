# Fla. Stat. § 564.07 — Wine lists furnished to vendors.

**Title:** XXXIV — ALCOHOLIC BEVERAGES AND TOBACCO
**Chapter:** 564 — WINE
**Part:** VI — VIRTUAL CURRENCY KIOSK BUSINESSES
**Section:** 564.07
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

A distributor of vinous beverages in this state may furnish, give, rent, loan, or sell to a vendor, and a vendor may accept, alcoholic beverage lists, commonly referred to as “wine lists,” without the same being a violation of s. 561.42(1).

**History:** s. 1, ch. 81-159.
