# Fla. Stat. § 563.025 — Surtax on license fees.

**Title:** XXXIV — ALCOHOLIC BEVERAGES AND TOBACCO
**Chapter:** 563 — BEER AND MALT BEVERAGES
**Part:** VI — VIRTUAL CURRENCY KIOSK BUSINESSES
**Section:** 563.025
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Each vendor of malt beverages containing alcohol of 0.5 percent or more by volume shall pay an annual surtax in an amount equal to 40 percent of the license fee imposed by s. 563.02(1).

**History:** s. 23, ch. 86-269.
