# Fla. Stat. § 55.502 — Construction of act.

**Title:** VI — CIVIL PRACTICE AND PROCEDURE
**Chapter:** 55 — JUDGMENTS
**Part:** XII — DOMESTIC VIOLENCE
**Section:** 55.502
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
As used in ss. 55.501-55.509, the term “foreign judgment” means a judgment, decree, or order of a court of any other state, territory or commonwealth of the United States, or of the United States if such judgment, decree, or order is entitled to full faith and credit in this state.

(2)  
This act shall not be construed to impair the right of a judgment creditor to bring an action to enforce his or her judgment instead of proceeding under this act.

(3)  
F.S. 55.501655.501  
Florida Enforcement of Foreign Judgments Act; short title.
——

Sections 55.501-55.509 may be cited as the “Florida Enforcement of Foreign Judgments Act.”

**History:** s. 1, ch. 84-5.
