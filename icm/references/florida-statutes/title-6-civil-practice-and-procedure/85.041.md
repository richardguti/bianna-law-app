# Fla. Stat. § 85.041 — Joinder.

**Title:** VI — CIVIL PRACTICE AND PROCEDURE
**Chapter:** 85 — ENFORCEMENT OF STATUTORY LIENS
**Part:** III — SELF-SERVICE STORAGE SPACE
**Section:** 85.041
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

All persons who have liens under part I or part II of chapter 713, may join to enforce their respective liens.

**History:** s. 14, ch. 5143, 1903; GS 2224; RGS 3531; CGL 5394; s. 37, ch. 67-254.

**Note:** Former s. 86.10.
