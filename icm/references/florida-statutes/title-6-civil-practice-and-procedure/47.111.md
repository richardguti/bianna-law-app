# Fla. Stat. § 47.111 — Change of venue; denial of motion.

**Title:** VI — CIVIL PRACTICE AND PROCEDURE
**Chapter:** 47 — VENUE
**Part:** XII — DOMESTIC VIOLENCE
**Section:** 47.111
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The adverse party has the right to deny the allegations of the motion. The court shall hear the evidence on the motion.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 5372, 1905; GS 1474; s. 3, ch. 7852, 1919; RGS 2673; CGL 4340; s. 3, ch. 67-254.

**Note:** Former s. 53.04.
