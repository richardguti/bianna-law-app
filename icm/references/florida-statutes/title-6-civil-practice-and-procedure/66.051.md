# Fla. Stat. § 66.051 — Betterment, answer.

**Title:** VI — CIVIL PRACTICE AND PROCEDURE
**Chapter:** 66 — EJECTMENT
**Part:** II — UNIFORM PARTITION OF HEIRS PROPERTY ACT
**Section:** 66.051
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The plaintiff in the judgment of eviction may file written defenses to the petition within 20 days after service of the petition.

**History:** RS 1517; GS 1972; RGS 3240; CGL 5048; s. 14, ch. 29737, 1955; s. 21, ch. 67-254.

**Note:** Former s. 70.07.
