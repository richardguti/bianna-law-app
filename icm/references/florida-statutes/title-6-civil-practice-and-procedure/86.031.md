# Fla. Stat. § 86.031 — Before breach.

**Title:** VI — CIVIL PRACTICE AND PROCEDURE
**Chapter:** 86 — DECLARATORY JUDGMENTS
**Part:** III — SELF-SERVICE STORAGE SPACE
**Section:** 86.031
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

A contract may be construed either before or after there has been a breach of it.

**History:** s. 3, ch. 21820, 1943; s. 38, ch. 67-254.

**Note:** Former s. 87.03.
