# Fla. Stat. § 76.20 — Replevy of property taken by attachment.

**Title:** VI — CIVIL PRACTICE AND PROCEDURE
**Chapter:** 76 — ATTACHMENT
**Part:** II — UNIFORM PARTITION OF HEIRS PROPERTY ACT
**Section:** 76.20
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

If property taken under a writ of attachment is not subject to attachment, it may be replevied by defendant.

**History:** s. 4, Feb. 14, 1835; RS 1654; GS 2118; RGS 3419; CGL 5272; s. 26, ch. 67-254.
