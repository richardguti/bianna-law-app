# Fla. Stat. § 75.01 — Jurisdiction.

**Title:** VI — CIVIL PRACTICE AND PROCEDURE
**Chapter:** 75 — BOND VALIDATION
**Part:** II — UNIFORM PARTITION OF HEIRS PROPERTY ACT
**Section:** 75.01
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Circuit courts have jurisdiction to determine the validation of bonds and certificates of indebtedness and all matters connected therewith.

**History:** s. 25, ch. 67-254.
