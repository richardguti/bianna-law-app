# Fla. Stat. § 76.21 — Claims of third parties to attached property.

**Title:** VI — CIVIL PRACTICE AND PROCEDURE
**Chapter:** 76 — ATTACHMENT
**Part:** II — UNIFORM PARTITION OF HEIRS PROPERTY ACT
**Section:** 76.21
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

If any attachment is levied on property claimed by any person other than defendant, such person may replevy it or interpose a claim in the manner provided in case of execution.

**History:** s. 8, Feb. 15, 1834; s. 1, Mar. 15, 1843; RS 1665; GS 2129; RGS 3430; CGL 5283; s. 26, ch. 67-254.
