# Fla. Stat. § 83.44 — Obligation of good faith.

**Title:** VI — CIVIL PRACTICE AND PROCEDURE
**Chapter:** 83 — LANDLORD AND TENANT
**Part:** II — RESIDENTIAL TENANCIES
**Section:** 83.44
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Every rental agreement or duty within this part imposes an obligation of good faith in its performance or enforcement.

**History:** s. 2, ch. 73-330.
