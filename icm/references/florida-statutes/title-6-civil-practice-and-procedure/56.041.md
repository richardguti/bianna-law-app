# Fla. Stat. § 56.041 — Executions; collection and return.

**Title:** VI — CIVIL PRACTICE AND PROCEDURE
**Chapter:** 56 — FINAL PROCESS
**Part:** XII — DOMESTIC VIOLENCE
**Section:** 56.041
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
All executions shall be returnable when satisfied, and the officers to whom they are delivered shall collect the amounts thereof as soon as possible and shall furnish the judgment debtor with a satisfaction of judgment. All receipts shall be endorsed on the execution.

(2) &#x200F.S. 56.031656.031  
Executions; form.
——

All executions shall be dated on the day on which they are issued, shall be directed to all and singular the sheriffs of the state and shall be in full force throughout the state.

**History:** s. 1, Feb. 17, 1833; RS 1186; GS 1614; RGS 2818; CGL 4505; s. 11, ch. 67-254.

**Note:** Former s. 55.17.
