# Fla. Stat. § 61.505 — Application to Indian tribes.

**Title:** VI — CIVIL PRACTICE AND PROCEDURE
**Chapter:** 61 — DISSOLUTION OF MARRIAGE; SUPPORT; TIME-SHARING
**Part:** II — UNIFORM CHILD CUSTODY JURISDICTION AND ENFORCEMENT ACT
**Section:** 61.505
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
A child custody proceeding that pertains to an Indian child, as defined in the Indian Child Welfare Act, 25 U.S.C. ss. 1901 et seq., is not subject to this part to the extent that it is governed by the Indian Child Welfare Act.
