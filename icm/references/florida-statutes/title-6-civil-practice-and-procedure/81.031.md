# Fla. Stat. § 81.031 — Prohibition; procedure.

**Title:** VI — CIVIL PRACTICE AND PROCEDURE
**Chapter:** 82 — FORCIBLE ENTRY AND UNLAWFUL DETAINER
**Part:** II — UNIFORM PARTITION OF HEIRS PROPERTY ACT
**Section:** 81.031
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

In the circuit court the petition shall be accompanied by a supporting brief in the same manner as required for mandamus in that court.

**History:** s. 32, ch. 67-254.
