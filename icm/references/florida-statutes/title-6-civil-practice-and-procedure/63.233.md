# Fla. Stat. § 63.233 — Rulemaking authority.

**Title:** VI — CIVIL PRACTICE AND PROCEDURE
**Chapter:** 63 — ADOPTION
**Part:** IV — UNIFORM DEPLOYED PARENTS CUSTODY AND VISITATION ACT
**Section:** 63.233
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The department shall adopt rules pursuant to ss. 120.536(1) and 120.54 to implement the provisions of this chapter.

**History:** s. 11, ch. 87-397; s. 10, ch. 98-200.
