# Fla. Stat. § 64.022 — Venue.

**Title:** VI — CIVIL PRACTICE AND PROCEDURE
**Chapter:** 64 — PARTITION OF PROPERTY
**Part:** I — GENERAL PROVISIONS
**Section:** 64.022
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Partition shall be brought in any county where the lands or any part thereof lie which are the subject matter of the action.

**History:** s. 1, Mar. 14, 1844; RS 1491; GS 1940; RGS 3203; CGL 4995; s. 19, ch. 67-254.

**Note:** Former s. 66.02.
