# Fla. Stat. § 78.18 — Judgment for plaintiff when goods not delivered to defendant.

**Title:** VI — CIVIL PRACTICE AND PROCEDURE
**Chapter:** 78 — REPLEVIN
**Part:** II — UNIFORM PARTITION OF HEIRS PROPERTY ACT
**Section:** 78.18
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

If it appears that the property described in the complaint was wrongfully taken or detained by defendant and the property has been delivered to plaintiff by the officer executing the writ, plaintiff shall have judgment for his or her damages caused by the taking and detention and costs.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 1099; 1861; RS 1719; GS 2183; RGS 3488; CGL 5341; s. 28, ch. 67-254; s. 1, ch. 73-20; s. 3, ch. 84-176; s. 408, ch. 95-147.
