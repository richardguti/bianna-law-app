# Fla. Stat. § 66.041 — Betterment, petition.

**Title:** VI — CIVIL PRACTICE AND PROCEDURE
**Chapter:** 66 — EJECTMENT
**Part:** II — UNIFORM PARTITION OF HEIRS PROPERTY ACT
**Section:** 66.041
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

If a judgment of eviction is rendered against defendant, within 60 days thereafter, or if he or she has appealed, within 20 days after filing the mandate affirming the judgment, defendant may file in the court in which the judgment was rendered a petition setting forth that:
(1)  
F.S. 66.031666.031  
Verdict and judgment.
——

(1)  
VERDICT.——
A verdict for plaintiff shall state the quantity of the estate of plaintiff, and describe the land by metes and bounds, lot number or other certain description.

(2)  
JUDGMENT.——
The judgment awarding possession shall state the quantity of the estate and give a description of the land recovered in like manner.
