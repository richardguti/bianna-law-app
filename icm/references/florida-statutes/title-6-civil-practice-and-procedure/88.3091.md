# Fla. Stat. § 88.3091 — Private counsel.

**Title:** VI — CIVIL PRACTICE AND PROCEDURE
**Chapter:** 88 — UNIFORM INTERSTATE FAMILY SUPPORT ACT
**Part:** III — CIVIL PROVISIONS OF GENERAL APPLICATION
**Section:** 88.3091
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

An individual may employ private counsel to represent the individual in proceedings authorized by this act.

**History:** s. 3, ch. 96-189.
