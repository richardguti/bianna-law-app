# Fla. Stat. § 64.011 — Parties.

**Title:** VI — CIVIL PRACTICE AND PROCEDURE
**Chapter:** 63 — ADOPTION
**Part:** IV — UNIFORM DEPLOYED PARENTS CUSTODY AND VISITATION ACT
**Section:** 64.011
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The action may be filed by any one or more of several joint tenants, tenants in common, or coparceners, against their cotenants, coparceners, or others interested in the lands to be divided.

**History:** s. 2, Mar. 14, 1844; RS 1492; GS 1941; RGS 3204; CGL 4996; s. 19, ch. 67-254.

**Note:** F.S. 64.022664.022  
Venue.
—

Partition shall be brought in any county where the lands or any part thereof lie which are the subject matter of the action.
