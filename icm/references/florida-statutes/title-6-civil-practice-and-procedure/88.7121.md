# Fla. Stat. § 88.7121 — Personal information; limit on use.

**Title:** VI — CIVIL PRACTICE AND PROCEDURE
**Chapter:** 88 — UNIFORM INTERSTATE FAMILY SUPPORT ACT
**Part:** VII — SUPPORT PROCEEDING UNDER CONVENTION
**Section:** 88.7121
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Personal information gathered or transmitted under this part may be used only for the purposes for which it was gathered or transmitted.

**History:** s. 70, ch. 2011-92.
