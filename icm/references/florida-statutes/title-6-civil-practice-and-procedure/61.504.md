# Fla. Stat. § 61.504 — Proceedings governed by other law.

**Title:** VI — CIVIL PRACTICE AND PROCEDURE
**Chapter:** 61 — DISSOLUTION OF MARRIAGE; SUPPORT; TIME-SHARING
**Part:** II — UNIFORM CHILD CUSTODY JURISDICTION AND ENFORCEMENT ACT
**Section:** 61.504
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

This part does not govern a proceeding pertaining to the authorization of emergency medical care for a child.

**History:** s. 5, ch. 2002-65.
