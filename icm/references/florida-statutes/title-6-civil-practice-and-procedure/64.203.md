# Fla. Stat. § 64.203 — Applicability; relation to other law.

**Title:** VI — CIVIL PRACTICE AND PROCEDURE
**Chapter:** 64 — PARTITION OF PROPERTY
**Part:** II — UNIFORM PARTITION OF HEIRS PROPERTY ACT
**Section:** 64.203
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

——

(1)  
This part applies to partition actions filed on or after July 1, 2020.

(2)  
Provided that a partition action is otherwise available under part I of this chapter, the court shall determine whether the property is heirs property. If the court determines that the property is heirs property, the property must be partitioned under this part unless all of the cotenants otherwise agree in a record.
