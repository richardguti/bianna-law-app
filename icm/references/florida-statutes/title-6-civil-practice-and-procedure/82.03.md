# Fla. Stat. § 82.03 — Remedies.

**Title:** VI — CIVIL PRACTICE AND PROCEDURE
**Chapter:** 82 — FORCIBLE ENTRY AND UNLAWFUL DETAINER
**Part:** II — UNIFORM PARTITION OF HEIRS PROPERTY ACT
**Section:** 82.03
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
A person entitled to possession of real property, including constructive possession by a record titleholder, has a cause of action against a person who obtained possession of that real property by forcible entry, unlawful entry, or unlawful detention and may recover possession and damages. Applicability.
——

(1)  
This chapter does not apply to residential tenancies under part II of chapter 83.

(2)  
This chapter does not apply to the possession of real property under chapter 513 or chapter 723.
