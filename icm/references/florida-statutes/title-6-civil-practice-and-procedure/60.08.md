# Fla. Stat. § 60.08 — Injunctions sought by the state pursuant to statute shall issue without bond.

**Title:** VI — CIVIL PRACTICE AND PROCEDURE
**Chapter:** 61 — DISSOLUTION OF MARRIAGE; SUPPORT; TIME-SHARING
**Part:** XII — DOMESTIC VIOLENCE
**Section:** 60.08
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

In any action for injunctive relief sought by the state or one of its agencies as provided in ss. 501.207(1)(b), 542.23, and 895.05(5), any injunction sought shall issue without bond or surety and no bond or surety shall be required during the term of the injunction.

**History:** s. 5, ch. 2001-266.
