# Fla. Stat. § 59.15 — Proceedings in pais; authentication.

**Title:** VI — CIVIL PRACTICE AND PROCEDURE
**Chapter:** 59 — APPELLATE PROCEEDINGS
**Part:** XII — DOMESTIC VIOLENCE
**Section:** 59.15
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Proceedings in pais, not stenographically reported, may be authenticated by recitals in orders, judgments, or decrees, of the trial court, or of the judge thereof, or by a stipulation by the interested parties.

**History:** s. 68, Nov. 23, 1828; s. 1, ch. 138, 1848; RS 1268; GS 1696; s. 10, ch. 7838, 1919; RGS 2906; CGL 4616; s. 15, ch. 22854, 1945; s. 5, ch. 71-316.
