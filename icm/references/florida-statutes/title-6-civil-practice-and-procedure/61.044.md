# Fla. Stat. § 61.044 — Certain existing defenses abolished.

**Title:** VI — CIVIL PRACTICE AND PROCEDURE
**Chapter:** 61 — DISSOLUTION OF MARRIAGE; SUPPORT; TIME-SHARING
**Part:** I — GENERAL PROVISIONS
**Section:** 61.044
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The defenses to divorce and legal separation of condonation, collusion, recrimination, and laches are abolished.

**History:** s. 6, ch. 71-241.
