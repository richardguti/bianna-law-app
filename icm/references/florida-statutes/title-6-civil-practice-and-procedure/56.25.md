# Fla. Stat. § 56.25 — Execution sale; bill of sale or deed.

**Title:** VI — CIVIL PRACTICE AND PROCEDURE
**Chapter:** 56 — FINAL PROCESS
**Part:** XII — DOMESTIC VIOLENCE
**Section:** 56.25
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

When a sale is made under an execution, the officer making the sale shall execute and deliver to the purchaser a deed or bill of sale to the property on payment of the purchase money and the cost of the deed or bill of sale.

**History:** s. 6, Feb. 17, 1833; RS 1205; GS 1634; RGS 2838; CGL 4525; s. 11, ch. 67-254.

**Note:** Former s. 55.48.
