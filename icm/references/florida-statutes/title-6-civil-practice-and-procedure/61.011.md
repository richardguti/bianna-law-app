# Fla. Stat. § 61.011 — Dissolution in chancery.

**Title:** VI — CIVIL PRACTICE AND PROCEDURE
**Chapter:** 61 — DISSOLUTION OF MARRIAGE; SUPPORT; TIME-SHARING
**Part:** I — GENERAL PROVISIONS
**Section:** 61.011
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Proceedings under this chapter are in chancery.

**History:** s. 1, Oct. 31, 1828; RS 1477; GS 1925; RGS 3188; CGL 4980; s. 2, ch. 29737, 1955; s. 16, ch. 67-254; s. 2, ch. 71-241.

**Note:** Former s. 65.01.
