# Fla. Stat. § 61.532 — Service of petition and order.

**Title:** VI — CIVIL PRACTICE AND PROCEDURE
**Chapter:** 61 — DISSOLUTION OF MARRIAGE; SUPPORT; TIME-SHARING
**Part:** II — UNIFORM CHILD CUSTODY JURISDICTION AND ENFORCEMENT ACT
**Section:** 61.532
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Except as otherwise provided in s. 61.534, the petition and order must be served by any method authorized by the laws of this state upon the respondent and any person who has physical custody of the child.

**History:** s. 5, ch. 2002-65.
