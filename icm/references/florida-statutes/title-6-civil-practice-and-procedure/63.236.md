# Fla. Stat. § 63.236 — Petitions filed before July 1, 2008; governing law.

**Title:** VI — CIVIL PRACTICE AND PROCEDURE
**Chapter:** 63 — ADOPTION
**Part:** IV — UNIFORM DEPLOYED PARENTS CUSTODY AND VISITATION ACT
**Section:** 63.236
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

A petition for termination of parental rights filed before July 1, 2008, is governed by the law in effect at the time the petition was filed.

**History:** s. 23, ch. 2008-151.
