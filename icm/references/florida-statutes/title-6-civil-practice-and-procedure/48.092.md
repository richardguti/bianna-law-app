# Fla. Stat. § 48.092 — Service on financial institutions.

**Title:** VI — CIVIL PRACTICE AND PROCEDURE
**Chapter:** 48 — PROCESS AND SERVICE OF PROCESS
**Part:** XII — DOMESTIC VIOLENCE
**Section:** 48.092
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Service on financial institutions must be made in accordance with s. 655.0201.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 2016-180.
