# Fla. Stat. § 83.22 — Removal of tenant; service.

**Title:** VI — CIVIL PRACTICE AND PROCEDURE
**Chapter:** 83 — LANDLORD AND TENANT
**Part:** I — NONRESIDENTIAL TENANCIES
**Section:** 83.22
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
After at least two attempts to obtain service as provided by law, if the defendant cannot be found in the county in which the action is pending and either the defendant has no usual place of abode in the county or there is no person 15 years of age or older residing at the defendant's usual place of abode in the county, the sheriff shall serve the summons by attaching it to some part of the premises involved in the proceeding. The minimum time delay between the two attempts to obtain service shall be 6 hours.
