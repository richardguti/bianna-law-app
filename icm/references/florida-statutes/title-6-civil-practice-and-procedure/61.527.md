# Fla. Stat. § 61.527 — Temporary visitation.

**Title:** VI — CIVIL PRACTICE AND PROCEDURE
**Chapter:** 61 — DISSOLUTION OF MARRIAGE; SUPPORT; TIME-SHARING
**Part:** II — UNIFORM CHILD CUSTODY JURISDICTION AND ENFORCEMENT ACT
**Section:** 61.527
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
A court of this state which does not have jurisdiction to modify a child custody determination may issue a temporary order enforcing:
(a)  
A visitation schedule made by a court of another state; or

(b)  
The visitation provisions of a child custody determination of another state which does not provide for a specific visitation schedule.

(2)  
If a court of this state makes an order under paragraph (1)(b), it shall specify in the order a period that it considers adequate to allow the petitioner to obtain an order from a court having jurisdiction under the criteria specified in ss. 61.514-61.523. The order remains in effect until an order is obtained from the other court or the period expires.

**History:** s. 5, ch. 2002-65.
