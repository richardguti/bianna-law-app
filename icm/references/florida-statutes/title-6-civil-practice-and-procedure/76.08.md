# Fla. Stat. § 76.08 — Procurement of attachment; generally.

**Title:** VI — CIVIL PRACTICE AND PROCEDURE
**Chapter:** 76 — ATTACHMENT
**Part:** II — UNIFORM PARTITION OF HEIRS PROPERTY ACT
**Section:** 76.08
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Upon motion by plaintiff, a writ of attachment may issue when the grounds relied on for the issuance of the writ clearly appear from specific facts shown by a verified complaint or a separate affidavit of the plaintiff, and all applicable requirements of s. 76.09, s. 76.10, or s. 76.11 are met.

**History:** RS 1641; GS 2105; RGS 3406; CGL 5259; s. 26, ch. 67-254; s. 2, ch. 78-38.
