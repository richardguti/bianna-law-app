# Fla. Stat. § 75.10 — Recording of judgment in other counties.

**Title:** VI — CIVIL PRACTICE AND PROCEDURE
**Chapter:** 75 — BOND VALIDATION
**Part:** II — UNIFORM PARTITION OF HEIRS PROPERTY ACT
**Section:** 75.10
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

If any judgment extends into more than one county it shall be recorded in each county in which the plaintiff municipality or district extends.

**History:** s. 4, ch. 10036, 1925; CGL 5116; s. 25, ch. 67-254.
