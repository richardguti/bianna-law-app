# Fla. Stat. § 77.02 — Garnishment in tort actions.

**Title:** VI — CIVIL PRACTICE AND PROCEDURE
**Chapter:** 77 — GARNISHMENT
**Part:** II — UNIFORM PARTITION OF HEIRS PROPERTY ACT
**Section:** 77.02
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Before judgment against a defendant no writ of garnishment shall issue in any action sounding in tort.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 7352, 1917; RGS 3432; CGL 5285; s. 27, ch. 67-254.
