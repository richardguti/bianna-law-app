# Fla. Stat. § 88.7131 — Record in original language; English translation.

**Title:** VI — CIVIL PRACTICE AND PROCEDURE
**Chapter:** 88 — UNIFORM INTERSTATE FAMILY SUPPORT ACT
**Part:** VII — SUPPORT PROCEEDING UNDER CONVENTION
**Section:** 88.7131
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

A record filed with a tribunal of this state under this part must be in the original language and, if not in English, must be accompanied by an English translation.

**History:** s. 71, ch. 2011-92.
