# Fla. Stat. § 88.6011 — Registration of order for enforcement.

**Title:** VI — CIVIL PRACTICE AND PROCEDURE
**Chapter:** 88 — UNIFORM INTERSTATE FAMILY SUPPORT ACT
**Part:** V — ENFORCEMENT OF SUPPORT ORDER OF ANOTHER STATE WITHOUT REGISTRATION
**Section:** 88.6011
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

A support order or an income-withholding order issued in another state or a foreign support order may be registered in this state for enforcement.

**History:** s. 6, ch. 96-189; s. 45, ch. 2011-92.
