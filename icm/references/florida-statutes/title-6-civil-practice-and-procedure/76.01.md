# Fla. Stat. § 76.01 — Right to attachment.

**Title:** VI — CIVIL PRACTICE AND PROCEDURE
**Chapter:** 75 — BOND VALIDATION
**Part:** II — UNIFORM PARTITION OF HEIRS PROPERTY ACT
**Section:** 76.01
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Any creditor may have an attachment at law against the goods and chattels, lands, and tenements of his or her debtor under the circumstances and in the manner hereinafter provided.

**History:** RS 1635; GS 2099; RGS 3400; CGL 5253; s. 26, ch. 67-254; s. 370, ch. 95-147.
