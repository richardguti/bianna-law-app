# Fla. Stat. § 88.3021 — Proceeding by minor parent.

**Title:** VI — CIVIL PRACTICE AND PROCEDURE
**Chapter:** 88 — UNIFORM INTERSTATE FAMILY SUPPORT ACT
**Part:** III — CIVIL PROVISIONS OF GENERAL APPLICATION
**Section:** 88.3021
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

A minor parent, or a guardian or other legal representative of a minor parent, may maintain a proceeding on behalf of or for the benefit of the minor's child.

**History:** s. 3, ch. 96-189; s. 17, ch. 2011-92.
