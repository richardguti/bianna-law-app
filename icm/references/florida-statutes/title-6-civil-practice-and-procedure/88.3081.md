# Fla. Stat. § 88.3081 — Duty of Governor and Cabinet.

**Title:** VI — CIVIL PRACTICE AND PROCEDURE
**Chapter:** 88 — UNIFORM INTERSTATE FAMILY SUPPORT ACT
**Part:** III — CIVIL PROVISIONS OF GENERAL APPLICATION
**Section:** 88.3081
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

——
't agency of this state that requests registration of a child support order in this state for enforcement or for modification shall make reasonable efforts:
(a)  
To ensure that the order to be registered is the controlling order; or

(b)  
If two or more child support orders exist and the identity of the controlling order has not been determined, to ensure that a request for such a determination is made in a tribunal having jurisdiction to do so.
