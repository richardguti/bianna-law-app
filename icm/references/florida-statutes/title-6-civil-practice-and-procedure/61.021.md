# Fla. Stat. § 61.021 — Residence requirements.

**Title:** VI — CIVIL PRACTICE AND PROCEDURE
**Chapter:** 61 — DISSOLUTION OF MARRIAGE; SUPPORT; TIME-SHARING
**Part:** I — GENERAL PROVISIONS
**Section:** 61.021
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

To obtain a dissolution of marriage, one of the parties to the marriage must reside 6 months in the state before the filing of the petition.
