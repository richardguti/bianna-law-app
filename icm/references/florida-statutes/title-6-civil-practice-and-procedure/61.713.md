# Fla. Stat. § 61.713 — General consideration in custody proceeding of parent's service.

**Title:** VI — CIVIL PRACTICE AND PROCEDURE
**Chapter:** 61 — DISSOLUTION OF MARRIAGE; SUPPORT; TIME-SHARING
**Part:** IV — UNIFORM DEPLOYED PARENTS CUSTODY AND VISITATION ACT
**Section:** 61.713
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

In a proceeding for custodial responsibility of a child of a servicemember, a court may not consider a parent's past deployment or possible future deployment in determining the best interest of the child.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 2018-69.
