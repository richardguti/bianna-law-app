# Fla. Stat. § 63.043 — Mandatory screening or testing for sickle-cell trait prohibited.

**Title:** VI — CIVIL PRACTICE AND PROCEDURE
**Chapter:** 63 — ADOPTION
**Part:** IV — UNIFORM DEPLOYED PARENTS CUSTODY AND VISITATION ACT
**Section:** 63.043
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

——

No person, firm, corporation, unincorporated association, state agency, unit of local government, or any public or private entity shall require screening or testin[ , or other relatives without the consent of the adoptive parent or parents. As part of the review process, the court may order the parties to engage in mediation. The department shall not be required to be a party to such review.
