# Fla. Stat. § 88.9031 — Severability.

**Title:** VI — CIVIL PRACTICE AND PROCEDURE
**Chapter:** 88 — UNIFORM INTERSTATE FAMILY SUPPORT ACT
**Part:** IX — MISCELLANEOUS PROVISIONS
**Section:** 88.9031
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

This act applies to proceedings begun on or after the effective date of this act to establish a support order or determine parentage of a child or to register, recognize, enforce, or modify a prior support order, determination, or agreement, whenever issued or entered.

**History:** s. 74, ch. 2011-92.
