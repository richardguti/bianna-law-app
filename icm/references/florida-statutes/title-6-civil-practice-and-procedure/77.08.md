# Fla. Stat. § 77.08 — Writ; jury trial.

**Title:** VI — CIVIL PRACTICE AND PROCEDURE
**Chapter:** 77 — GARNISHMENT
**Part:** II — UNIFORM PARTITION OF HEIRS PROPERTY ACT
**Section:** 77.08
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

On demand of either party a jury summoned from the body of the county shall be impaneled to try the issues.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 7353, 1917; RGS 3455; CGL 5308; s. 27, ch. 67-254.
