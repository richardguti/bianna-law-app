# Fla. Stat. § 60.03 — Injunction against removal of mortgaged personal property.

**Title:** VI — CIVIL PRACTICE AND PROCEDURE
**Chapter:** 60 — INJUNCTIONS
**Part:** XII — DOMESTIC VIOLENCE
**Section:** 60.03
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The removal from the state of any personal property mortgaged to secure a debt which has not matured at the time of the removal may be enjoined by any chancery court within whose territorial jurisdiction the property is located.

**History:** RS 1472; GS 1920; RGS 3182; CGL 4974; s. 15, ch. 67-254.

**Note:** Former s. 64.09.
