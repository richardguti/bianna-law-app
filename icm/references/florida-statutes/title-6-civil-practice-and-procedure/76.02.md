# Fla. Stat. § 76.02 — Attachment of corporate stock.

**Title:** VI — CIVIL PRACTICE AND PROCEDURE
**Chapter:** 75 — BOND VALIDATION
**Part:** II — UNIFORM PARTITION OF HEIRS PROPERTY ACT
**Section:** 76.02
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Shares of stock in any corporation incorporated by the laws of this state are subject to attachment under the circumstances hereinafter provided and in the manner prescribed for levy of execution thereon.

**History:** RS 1635; GS 2099; RGS 3400; CGL 5253; s. 26, ch. 67-254; s. 370, ch. 95-147.
