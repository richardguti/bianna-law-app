# Fla. Stat. § 80.032 — Judgment of ouster.

**Title:** VI — CIVIL PRACTICE AND PROCEDURE
**Chapter:** 80 — QUO WARRANTO
**Part:** II — UNIFORM PARTITION OF HEIRS PROPERTY ACT
**Section:** 80.032
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

When any petition is well-founded, a judgment of ouster may issue without further amendments to the extent that the petition is well-founded.

**History:** s. 30, ch. 67-254.
