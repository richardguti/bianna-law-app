# Fla. Stat. § 75.13 — Certain prior proceedings validated.

**Title:** VI — CIVIL PRACTICE AND PROCEDURE
**Chapter:** 75 — BOND VALIDATION
**Part:** II — UNIFORM PARTITION OF HEIRS PROPERTY ACT
**Section:** 75.13
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Any action for validation heretofore brought by any municipality, special taxing district or political district or subdivision which extends into more than one county or judicial circuit, whereby bonds or certificates of debt have been validated in which the proceedings have been brought in one county and a decree has been entered, said decree shall be binding on all of the citizens, property owners, or taxpayers of each municipality, district or subdivision.

**History:** s. 5, ch. 10036, 1925; CGL 5117; s. 25, ch. 67-254.
