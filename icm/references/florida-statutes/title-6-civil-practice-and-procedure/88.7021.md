# Fla. Stat. § 88.7021 — Applicability.

**Title:** VI — CIVIL PRACTICE AND PROCEDURE
**Chapter:** 88 — UNIFORM INTERSTATE FAMILY SUPPORT ACT
**Part:** VII — SUPPORT PROCEEDING UNDER CONVENTION
**Section:** 88.7021
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

This part applies only to a support proceeding under the convention. In such a proceeding, if a provision of this part is inconsistent with parts I through VI, this part controls.

**History:** s. 60, ch. 2011-92.
