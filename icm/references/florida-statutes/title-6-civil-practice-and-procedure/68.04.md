# Fla. Stat. § 68.04 — Chancery jurisdiction over liens.

**Title:** VI — CIVIL PRACTICE AND PROCEDURE
**Chapter:** 68 — MISCELLANEOUS PROCEEDINGS
**Part:** II — UNIFORM PARTITION OF HEIRS PROPERTY ACT
**Section:** 68.04
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

All liens of any kind, whether created by statute or the common law, and whether heretofore regarded as merely possessory or not, may be enforced in chancery.

**History:** ss. 1, 2, Feb. 12, 1832; RS 1499; GS 1948; RGS 3211; CGL 5003; s. 10, ch. 29737, 1955; s. 22, ch. 67-254; s. 355, ch. 95-147.

**Note:** Former s. 62.22.
