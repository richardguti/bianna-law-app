# Fla. Stat. § 64.214 — Access for all residents.

**Title:** VI — CIVIL PRACTICE AND PROCEDURE
**Chapter:** 64 — PARTITION OF PROPERTY
**Part:** II — UNIFORM PARTITION OF HEIRS PROPERTY ACT
**Section:** 64.214
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Notwithstanding any provision to the contrary in this part, cotenants owning real property that is not heirs property may agree to partition such real property under this part. All of the cotenants must jointly notify the court of such agreement.

**History:** s. 2, ch. 2020-55.

**Note:** Former s. 66.11.
