# Fla. Stat. § 77.27 — No appeal until fees are paid.

**Title:** VI — CIVIL PRACTICE AND PROCEDURE
**Chapter:** 77 — GARNISHMENT
**Part:** II — UNIFORM PARTITION OF HEIRS PROPERTY ACT
**Section:** 77.27
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

If the writ is dismissed or plaintiff fails to sustain his or her claim, an appeal from the judgment is not permitted until the attorney fee provided in s. 77.28 has been paid.

**History:** s. 2, ch. 4030, 1891; GS 1357; RGS 2553; CGL 4171; s. 27, ch. 67-254; s. 400, ch. 95-147; s. 2, ch. 2014-211.
