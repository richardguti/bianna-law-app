# Fla. Stat. § 61.1811 — Clerk of the Court Child Support Enforcement Collection System Trust Fund.

**Title:** VI — CIVIL PRACTICE AND PROCEDURE
**Chapter:** 61 — DISSOLUTION OF MARRIAGE; SUPPORT; TIME-SHARING
**Part:** I — GENERAL PROVISIONS
**Section:** 61.1811
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

There is hereby created the Clerk of the Court Child Support Enforcement Collection System Trust Fund to be used to deposit the department's share of the fees generated in s. 61.181(2)(b)1.

**History:** s. 8, ch. 92-138; s. 8, ch. 2023-152.
