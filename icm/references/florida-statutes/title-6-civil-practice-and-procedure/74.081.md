# Fla. Stat. § 74.081 — Proceedings as evidence.

**Title:** VI — CIVIL PRACTICE AND PROCEDURE
**Chapter:** 74 — PROCEEDINGS SUPPLEMENTAL TO EMINENT DOMAIN
**Part:** II — UNIFORM PARTITION OF HEIRS PROPERTY ACT
**Section:** 74.081
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Neither the declaration of taking, nor the amount of the deposit, shall be admissible in evidence in any action.

**History:** s. 4, ch. 65-369.
