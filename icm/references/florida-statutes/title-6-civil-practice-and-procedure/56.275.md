# Fla. Stat. § 56.275 — Disposition of unclaimed money collected.

**Title:** VI — CIVIL PRACTICE AND PROCEDURE
**Chapter:** 56 — FINAL PROCESS
**Part:** XII — DOMESTIC VIOLENCE
**Section:** 56.275
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

All unclaimed proceeds from sheriff's sales or money collected under execution shall be disposed of as provided in s. 116.21.

**History:** s. 9, ch. 79-396; s. 7, ch. 82-118.
