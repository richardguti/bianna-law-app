# Fla. Stat. § 83.18 — Claims by third persons.

**Title:** VI — CIVIL PRACTICE AND PROCEDURE
**Chapter:** 83 — LANDLORD AND TENANT
**Part:** I — NONRESIDENTIAL TENANCIES
**Section:** 83.18
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Any third person claiming any property so distrained may interpose and prosecute his or her claim for it in the same manner as is provided in similar cases of claim to property levied on under execution.

**History:** s. 7, ch. 3131, 1879; RS 1770; GS 2246; RGS 3565; CGL 5429; s. 34, ch. 67-254; s. 17, ch. 82-66; s. 435, ch. 95-147.
