# Fla. Stat. § 86.081 — Costs.

**Title:** VI — CIVIL PRACTICE AND PROCEDURE
**Chapter:** 86 — DECLARATORY JUDGMENTS
**Part:** III — SELF-SERVICE STORAGE SPACE
**Section:** 86.081
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The court may award costs as are equitable.

**History:** s. 9, ch. 21820, 1943; s. 38, ch. 67-254.

**Note:** Former s. 87.09.
