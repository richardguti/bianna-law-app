# Fla. Stat. § 68.104 — Construction.

**Title:** VI — CIVIL PRACTICE AND PROCEDURE
**Chapter:** 68 — MISCELLANEOUS PROCEEDINGS
**Part:** II — UNIFORM PARTITION OF HEIRS PROPERTY ACT
**Section:** 68.104
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

This act shall not be construed to create a statutory right to counsel in any proceeding. This act shall not be construed to create any statutory right accruing to any attorney.

**History:** s. 11, ch. 2002-288.
