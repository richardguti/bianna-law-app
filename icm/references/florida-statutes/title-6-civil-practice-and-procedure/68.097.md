# Fla. Stat. § 68.097 — Authority and duties of the department.

**Title:** VI — CIVIL PRACTICE AND PROCEDURE
**Chapter:** 68 — MISCELLANEOUS PROCEEDINGS
**Part:** II — UNIFORM PARTITION OF HEIRS PROPERTY ACT
**Section:** 68.097
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The department shall have the powers necessary or appropriate to carry out the purposes and provisions of this act, including, but not limited to, the power to contract with a statewide not-for-profit organization that provides funding for civil legal assistance to the poor in this state to allocate funds to not-for-profit legal aid organizations consistent with the provisions of this act.

**History:** s. 4, ch. 2002-288.
