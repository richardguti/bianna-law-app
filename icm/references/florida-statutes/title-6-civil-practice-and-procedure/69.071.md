# Fla. Stat. § 69.071 — Number of jurors.

**Title:** VI — CIVIL PRACTICE AND PROCEDURE
**Chapter:** 69 — MISCELLANEOUS PROCEDURAL MATTERS
**Part:** II — UNIFORM PARTITION OF HEIRS PROPERTY ACT
**Section:** 69.071
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

In all civil actions when a jury is impaneled, a jury of six qualified jurors is sufficient.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 4717, 1899; GS 1494; RGS 2694; CGL 4361; s. 8, ch. 67-254; s. 25, ch. 73-333.

**Note:** Former ss. 54.14, 53.041.
