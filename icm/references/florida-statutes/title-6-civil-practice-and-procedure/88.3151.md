# Fla. Stat. § 88.3151 — Nonparentage as defense.

**Title:** VI — CIVIL PRACTICE AND PROCEDURE
**Chapter:** 88 — UNIFORM INTERSTATE FAMILY SUPPORT ACT
**Part:** III — CIVIL PROVISIONS OF GENERAL APPLICATION
**Section:** 88.3151
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

A party whose parentage of a child has been previously determined by or pursuant to law may not plead nonparentage as a defense to a proceeding under this act.

**History:** s. 3, ch. 96-189.
