# Fla. Stat. § 61.773 — Applicability.

**Title:** VI — CIVIL PRACTICE AND PROCEDURE
**Chapter:** 61 — DISSOLUTION OF MARRIAGE; SUPPORT; TIME-SHARING
**Part:** IV — UNIFORM DEPLOYED PARENTS CUSTODY AND VISITATION ACT
**Section:** 61.773
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

This act does not:
(1)  
Affect the validity of a temporary agreement or court order concerning custodial responsibility during deployment entered before July 1, 2018.

(2)  
Apply to a permanent change of station move by a servicemember, which shall be governed by s. 61.13001.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 2018-69.
