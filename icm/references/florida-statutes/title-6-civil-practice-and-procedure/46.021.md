# Fla. Stat. § 46.021 — Actions; surviving death of party.

**Title:** VI — CIVIL PRACTICE AND PROCEDURE
**Chapter:** 46 — PARTIES
**Part:** XII — DOMESTIC VIOLENCE
**Section:** 46.021
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

No cause of action dies with the person. All causes of action survive and may be commenced, prosecuted, and defended in the name of the person prescribed by law.

**History:** s. 30, Nov. 23, F.S. 46.015646.015
