# Fla. Stat. § 601.80 — Unlawful to use uncertified coloring matter.

**Title:** XXXV — AGRICULTURE, HORTICULTURE, AND ANIMAL INDUSTRY
**Chapter:** 601 — FLORIDA CITRUS CODE
**Part:** II — DISEASE INSPECTION, CONTROL, AND ERADICATION
**Section:** 601.80
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

It is unlawful for any person to use on oranges or citrus hybrids any coloring matter which has not first received the approval of the Department of Agriculture.

**History:** s. 80, ch. 25149, 1949; s. 2, ch. 61-89; ss. 14, 35, ch. 69-106; s. 7, ch. 71-185; s. 17, ch. 71-186; s. 10, ch. 87-44; s. 61, ch. 2012-182; s. 128, ch. 2013-18; s. 39, ch. 2022-5.
