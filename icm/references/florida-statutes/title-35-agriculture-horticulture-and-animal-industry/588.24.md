# Fla. Stat. § 588.24 — Penalty.

**Title:** XXXV — AGRICULTURE, HORTICULTURE, AND ANIMAL INDUSTRY
**Chapter:** 588 — LEGAL FENCES AND LIVESTOCK AT LARGE
**Part:** II — DISEASE INSPECTION, CONTROL, AND ERADICATION
**Section:** 588.24
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Any owner of livestock who unlawfully, intentionally, knowingly or negligently permits the same to run at large or stray upon the public roads of this state or any person who shall release livestock, after being impounded, without authority of the impounder, shall be guilty of a misdemeanor of the second degree, punishable as provided in s. 775.082 or s. 775.083.

**History:** s. 13, ch. 25236, 1949; s. 612, ch. 71-136.
