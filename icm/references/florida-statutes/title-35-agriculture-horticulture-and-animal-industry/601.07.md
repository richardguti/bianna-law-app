# Fla. Stat. § 601.07 — Location of executive offices.

**Title:** XXXV — AGRICULTURE, HORTICULTURE, AND ANIMAL INDUSTRY
**Chapter:** 601 — FLORIDA CITRUS CODE
**Part:** II — DISEASE INSPECTION, CONTROL, AND ERADICATION
**Section:** 601.07
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The department's executive offices shall be established and maintained at Bartow.

**History:** s. 7, ch. 25149, 1949; s. 22, ch. 71-186; s. 1, ch. 2010-145; ss. 9, 11, ch. 2010-277; HJR 5-A, 2010 Special Session A; s. 10, ch. 2012-182.
