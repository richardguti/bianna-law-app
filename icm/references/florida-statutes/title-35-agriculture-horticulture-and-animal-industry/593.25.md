# Fla. Stat. § 593.25 — Definition.

**Title:** XXXV — AGRICULTURE, HORTICULTURE, AND ANIMAL INDUSTRY
**Chapter:** 595 — SCHOOL FOOD AND NUTRITION SERVICES
**Part:** II — DISEASE INSPECTION, CONTROL, AND ERADICATION
**Section:** 593.25
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

As used in this act, the term “urbanized area” means land that is zoned for purposes other than agricultural purposes and that has a residential density of two or more units per acre.

**History:** s. 6, ch. 98-108.
