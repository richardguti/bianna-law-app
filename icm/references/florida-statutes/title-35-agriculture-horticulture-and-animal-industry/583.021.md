# Fla. Stat. § 583.021 — Nest run eggs; limitation on sale.

**Title:** XXXV — AGRICULTURE, HORTICULTURE, AND ANIMAL INDUSTRY
**Chapter:** 583 — CLASSIFICATION AND SALE OF EGGS AND POULTRY
**Part:** II — FLORIDA AGRICULTURAL PROMOTIONAL CAMPAIGN ACT
**Section:** 583.021
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Nest run eggs shall be sold only by producers or processors to other producers or processors and shall not be sold directly to retail outlets, consumers, or public eating places.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 84-102.
