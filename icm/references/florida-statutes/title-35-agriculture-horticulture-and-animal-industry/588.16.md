# Fla. Stat. § 588.16 — Authority to impound livestock running at large or strays.

**Title:** XXXV — AGRICULTURE, HORTICULTURE, AND ANIMAL INDUSTRY
**Chapter:** 588 — LEGAL FENCES AND LIVESTOCK AT LARGE
**Part:** II — DISEASE INSPECTION, CONTROL, AND ERADICATION
**Section:** 588.16
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

It shall be the duty of the sheriff or her or his deputies or designees, or any other law enforcement officer of the county, the county animal control center, or state highway patrol officers, where livestock is found to be running at large or straying, to take up, confine, hold, and impound any such livestock, to be disposed of as hereinafter provided.

**History:** s. 5, ch. 25236, 1949; s. 2, ch. 77-200; s. 944, ch. 97-103; s. 33, ch. 99-391.
