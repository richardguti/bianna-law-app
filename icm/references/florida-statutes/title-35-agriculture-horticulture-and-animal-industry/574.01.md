# Fla. Stat. § 574.01 — Administration of law.

**Title:** XXXV — AGRICULTURE, HORTICULTURE, AND ANIMAL INDUSTRY
**Chapter:** 574 — SALE OF LEAF TOBACCO
**Part:** II — FLORIDA AGRICULTURAL PROMOTIONAL CAMPAIGN ACT
**Section:** 574.01
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

This act shall be administered within the Division of Marketing and Development of the Department of Agriculture and Consumer Services.

**History:** s. 14, ch. 59-154; ss. 14, 35, ch. 69-106; s. 2, ch. 81-318; ss. 3, 4, ch. 83-9; s. 46, ch. 92-291; s. 1, ch. 93-6.
