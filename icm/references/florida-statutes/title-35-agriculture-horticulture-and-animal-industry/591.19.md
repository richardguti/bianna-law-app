# Fla. Stat. § 591.19 — Community forests; tax delinquent lands.

**Title:** XXXV — AGRICULTURE, HORTICULTURE, AND ANIMAL INDUSTRY
**Chapter:** 591 — FOREST DEVELOPMENT
**Part:** II — DISEASE INSPECTION, CONTROL, AND ERADICATION
**Section:** 591.19
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The Department of Revenue, the Board of Trustees of the Internal Improvement Trust Fund, counties, cities, towns, school districts, or any other public agency holding fee simple or tax certificate lands are hereby empowered to, and may, upon application to them, transfer title of fee simple lands not in other public use to any county, city, town, or school district for forest purposes as described under this law, provided such lands are approved by the Florida Forest Service for this purpose.

**History:** s. 5, ch. 20902, 1941; ss. 14, 21, 27, 35, ch. 69-106; s. 155, ch. 71-355; s. 71, ch. 2012-7.
