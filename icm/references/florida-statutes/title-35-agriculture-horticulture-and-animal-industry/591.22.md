# Fla. Stat. § 591.22 — Community forests; appropriations.

**Title:** XXXV — AGRICULTURE, HORTICULTURE, AND ANIMAL INDUSTRY
**Chapter:** 591 — FOREST DEVELOPMENT
**Part:** II — DISEASE INSPECTION, CONTROL, AND ERADICATION
**Section:** 591.22
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Counties, cities, towns, or school districts in which forestry committees have been appointed may appropriate money from available funds to be used by said committee to carry out the purposes of this law. The forestry committee shall each year make a budget of recommendation for acquisition and operation and management of the forest for approval by the governing board.

**History:** s. 8, ch. 20902, 1941.
