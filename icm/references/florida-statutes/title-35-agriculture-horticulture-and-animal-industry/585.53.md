# Fla. Stat. § 585.53 — Permit revocation.

**Title:** XXXV — AGRICULTURE, HORTICULTURE, AND ANIMAL INDUSTRY
**Chapter:** 585 — ANIMAL INDUSTRY
**Part:** II — DISEASE INSPECTION, CONTROL, AND ERADICATION
**Section:** 585.53
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Every permitted feeder of garbage who shall violate this chapter or the rules promulgated by the department pursuant thereto shall have her or his permit revoked, canceled, or suspended.

**History:** s. 7, ch. 28313, 1953; s. 44, ch. 59-457; s. 6, ch. 78-95; s. 38, ch. 90-321; s. 935, ch. 97-103.
