# Fla. Stat. § 601.79 — To color grapefruit and tangerines prohibited.

**Title:** XXXV — AGRICULTURE, HORTICULTURE, AND ANIMAL INDUSTRY
**Chapter:** 601 — FLORIDA CITRUS CODE
**Part:** II — DISEASE INSPECTION, CONTROL, AND ERADICATION
**Section:** 601.79
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

It is unlawful for any person to use on grapefruit or tangerines or apply thereto any coloring matter.

**History:** s. 79, ch. 25149, 1949; ss. 1-3, ch. 29808, 1955; ss. 1-3, ch. 57-27; ss. 1-3, ch. 59-32; s. 1, ch. 61-89.
