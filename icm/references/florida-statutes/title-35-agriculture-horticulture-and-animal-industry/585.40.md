# Fla. Stat. § 585.40 — Violation of quarantine regulations.

**Title:** XXXV — AGRICULTURE, HORTICULTURE, AND ANIMAL INDUSTRY
**Chapter:** 585 — ANIMAL INDUSTRY
**Part:** II — DISEASE INSPECTION, CONTROL, AND ERADICATION
**Section:** 585.40
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Whenever the department issues a quarantine, it shall be unlawful for any person, while such quarantine exists, to take, drive, or transport any animal into or out of such quarantined premise without permission of the State Veterinarian.

**History:** F.S. 585.386585.38
