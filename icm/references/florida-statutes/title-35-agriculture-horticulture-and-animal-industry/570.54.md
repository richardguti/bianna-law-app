# Fla. Stat. § 570.54 — Director; duties.

**Title:** XXXV — AGRICULTURE, HORTICULTURE, AND ANIMAL INDUSTRY
**Chapter:** 570 — DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE AND CONSUMER SERVICES
**Part:** II — PROGRAM SERVICES
**Section:** 570.54
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
The director of the Division of Marketing and Development shall be appointed by the commissioner and shall serve at the commissioner's pleasure.

(2)  
It shall be the duty of the director of this division to supervise, direct, and coordinate the activities authorized by ss. 570.07(4), (7), (8), (10), (11), (12), (17), (18), and (20), 570.21, 534.47-534.53, and 604.15-604.34 and chapters 504, 571, 573, and 574 and to exercise other powers and authority as authorized by the department.
