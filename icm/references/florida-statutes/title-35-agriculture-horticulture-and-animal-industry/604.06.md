# Fla. Stat. § 604.06 — Determination of soils to be surveyed.

**Title:** XXXV — AGRICULTURE, HORTICULTURE, AND ANIMAL INDUSTRY
**Chapter:** 604 — GENERAL AGRICULTURAL LAWS
**Part:** II — FLORIDA TROPICAL FRUIT POLICY ACT
**Section:** 604.06
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The successive selection of units to be surveyed shall be by type areas well distributed over the state, just as far as possible or practicable, especially during the early stages of the program, though determination shall naturally depend, too, on the feeling of need by the people in the area and the willingness of county or other local officials to cooperate.

**History:** s. 6, ch. 20454, 1941.
