# Fla. Stat. § 601.68 — Investigation of violations.

**Title:** XXXV — AGRICULTURE, HORTICULTURE, AND ANIMAL INDUSTRY
**Chapter:** 601 — FLORIDA CITRUS CODE
**Part:** II — DISEASE INSPECTION, CONTROL, AND ERADICATION
**Section:** 601.68
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

All fines imposed and collected by the Department of Agriculture under the provisions of this chapter are hereby appropriated in the manner provided by s. 601.28(3)(b).

**History:** s. 5, ch. 71-185; s. 2, ch. 81-318; ss. 1, 3, ch. 85-129; s. 4, ch. 91-429.
