# Fla. Stat. § 586.055 — Location of apiaries.

**Title:** XXXV — AGRICULTURE, HORTICULTURE, AND ANIMAL INDUSTRY
**Chapter:** 586 — HONEY CERTIFICATION AND HONEYBEES
**Part:** II — DISEASE INSPECTION, CONTROL, AND ERADICATION
**Section:** 586.055
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

An apiary may be located on land classified as agricultural under s. 193.461 or on land that is integral to a beekeeping operation.

**History:** s. 2, ch. 89-56; s. 8, ch. 92-147; s. 69, ch. 93-169; s. 10, ch. 95-317; s. 28, ch. 2023-154.
