# Fla. Stat. § 601.85 — Standard shipping box for fresh fruit.

**Title:** XXXV — AGRICULTURE, HORTICULTURE, AND ANIMAL INDUSTRY
**Chapter:** 601 — FLORIDA CITRUS CODE
**Part:** II — DISEASE INSPECTION, CONTROL, AND ERADICATION
**Section:** 601.85
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The specifications for the standard shipping box, when used as a unit of trade or for reporting purposes, shall be as established by the department, but the unit of a standard-packed box, commonly called 13
/5

bushels, shall contain an inside cubical measurement of 3,456 cubic inches.

**History:** s. 85, ch. 25149, 1949; s. 22, ch. 71-186; s. 62, ch. 2012-182.
