# Fla. Stat. § 583.19 — Sale of fowl unfit for human consumption prohibited.

**Title:** XXXV — AGRICULTURE, HORTICULTURE, AND ANIMAL INDUSTRY
**Chapter:** 583 — CLASSIFICATION AND SALE OF EGGS AND POULTRY
**Part:** II — FLORIDA AGRICULTURAL PROMOTIONAL CAMPAIGN ACT
**Section:** 583.19
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

It is unlawful for any person to sell live or dressed fowl suspected to be unsound, unhealthful, unwholesome, diseased, adulterated, or otherwise unfit for human consumption.

**History:** s. 11, ch. 17170, 1935; CGL 1936 Supp. 4151(389); s. 16, ch. 84-102.
