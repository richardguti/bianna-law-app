# Fla. Stat. § 585.51 — Permitting of feeders of garbage.

**Title:** XXXV — AGRICULTURE, HORTICULTURE, AND ANIMAL INDUSTRY
**Chapter:** 585 — ANIMAL INDUSTRY
**Part:** II — DISEASE INSPECTION, CONTROL, AND ERADICATION
**Section:** 585.51
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

No person shall feed garbage without first having applied for and obtained a permit from the department. Each permit shall expire as of July 1 of each year.

**History:** s. 5, ch. 28313, 1953; s. 42, ch. 59-457; s. 36, ch. 90-321.
