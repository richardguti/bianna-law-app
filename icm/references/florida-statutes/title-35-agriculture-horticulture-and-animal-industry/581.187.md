# Fla. Stat. § 581.187 — Exemptions from s. 581.185.

**Title:** XXXV — AGRICULTURE, HORTICULTURE, AND ANIMAL INDUSTRY
**Chapter:** 581 — PLANT INDUSTRY
**Part:** II — FLORIDA AGRICULTURAL PROMOTIONAL CAMPAIGN ACT
**Section:** 581.187
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

All Florida Indians, as defined in s. 285.11, shall be exempt from the prohibitions and penalties of s. 581.185.

**History:** s. 3, ch. 78-72.
