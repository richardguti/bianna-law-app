# Fla. Stat. § 595.501 — Corrective action plans; penalties.

**Title:** XXXV — AGRICULTURE, HORTICULTURE, AND ANIMAL INDUSTRY
**Chapter:** 595 — SCHOOL FOOD AND NUTRITION SERVICES
**Part:** II — DISEASE INSPECTION, CONTROL, AND ERADICATION
**Section:** 595.501
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
When a corrective action plan is issued by the department or a federal agency, each sponsor is required to complete the corrective action plan to be in compliance with the program.

(2)  
Any person or sponsor that violates any provision of this chapter or any rule adopted thereunder or otherwise does not comply with the program is subject to a suspension or revocation of their agreement, loss of reimbursement, or a financial penalty in accordance with federal or state law, or both. This section does not restrict the applicability of any other law.

**History:** s. 36, ch. 2013-226; s. 41, ch. 2016-61.
