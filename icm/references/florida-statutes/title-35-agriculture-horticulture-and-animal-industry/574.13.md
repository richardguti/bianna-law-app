# Fla. Stat. § 574.13 — Penalty.

**Title:** XXXV — AGRICULTURE, HORTICULTURE, AND ANIMAL INDUSTRY
**Chapter:** 574 — SALE OF LEAF TOBACCO
**Part:** II — FLORIDA AGRICULTURAL PROMOTIONAL CAMPAIGN ACT
**Section:** 574.13
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Any person violating the provisions of this act shall be deemed guilty of a misdemeanor and upon conviction thereof shall be punished as provided by law.

**History:** s. 11, ch. 59-154; s. 1, ch. 65-415; ss. 14, 35, ch. 69-106; s. 592, ch. 71-136; s. 2, ch. 81-318; ss. 1, 3, 4, ch. 83-9; s. 1, ch. 93-6; s. 909, ch. 97-103.
