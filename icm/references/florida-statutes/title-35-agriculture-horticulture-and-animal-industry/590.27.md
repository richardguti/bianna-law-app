# Fla. Stat. § 590.27 — Penalty for mutilating or destroying forestry or fire control signs and posters.

**Title:** XXXV — AGRICULTURE, HORTICULTURE, AND ANIMAL INDUSTRY
**Chapter:** 590 — FOREST PROTECTION
**Part:** II — DISEASE INSPECTION, CONTROL, AND ERADICATION
**Section:** 590.27
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Whoever intentionally breaks down, mutilates, removes, or destroys any fire control or forestry sign or poster commits a misdemeanor of the second degree, punishable as provided in s. 775.082 or s. 775.083.

**History:** s. 3, ch. 26833, 1951; ss. 14, 35, ch. 69-106; s. 617, ch. 71-136; s. 14, ch. 99-292.
