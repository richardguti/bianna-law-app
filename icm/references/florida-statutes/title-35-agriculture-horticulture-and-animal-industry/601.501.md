# Fla. Stat. § 601.501 — Charitable shipments exempt from assessments.

**Title:** XXXV — AGRICULTURE, HORTICULTURE, AND ANIMAL INDUSTRY
**Chapter:** 601 — FLORIDA CITRUS CODE
**Part:** II — DISEASE INSPECTION, CONTROL, AND ERADICATION
**Section:** 601.501
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Shipments of citrus fruit when permitted under s. 601.50 for charitable purposes are exempt from all advertising assessments.

**History:** s. 3, ch. 28197, 1953; s. 38, ch. 2012-182.
