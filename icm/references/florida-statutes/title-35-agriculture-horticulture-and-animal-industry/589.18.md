# Fla. Stat. § 589.18 — Application of general laws.

**Title:** XXXV — AGRICULTURE, HORTICULTURE, AND ANIMAL INDUSTRY
**Chapter:** 589 — FORESTRY
**Part:** II — DISEASE INSPECTION, CONTROL, AND ERADICATION
**Section:** 589.18
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The general laws of this state with reference to the acquisition and enforcement of statutory liens shall be applicable to the lien created by ss. 589.13-589.16 insofar as the same may be consistent with and pertinent hereto.

**History:** s. 5, ch. 17026, 1935; CGL 1936 Supp. 4151(10-x).
