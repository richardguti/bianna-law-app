# Fla. Stat. § 573.111 — Notice of effective date of marketing order.

**Title:** XXXV — AGRICULTURE, HORTICULTURE, AND ANIMAL INDUSTRY
**Chapter:** 573 — MARKETING OF AGRICULTURAL COMMODITIES
**Part:** II — FLORIDA AGRICULTURAL PROMOTIONAL CAMPAIGN ACT
**Section:** 573.111
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Before the issuance of any marketing order, or any suspension, amendment, or termination thereof, a notice must be posted on the department website. A marketing order, or any suspension, amendment, or termination thereof, may not become effective until 5 days after the date of posting and publication.

**History:** ss. 11, 26, ch. 87-171; s. 4, ch. 91-429; s. 4, ch. 92-4; ss. 44, 45, ch. 92-291; s. 19, ch. 2012-212; s. 38, ch. 2018-84.
