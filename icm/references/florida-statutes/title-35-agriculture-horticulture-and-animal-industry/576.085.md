# Fla. Stat. § 576.085 — Minimum plant nutrient content.

**Title:** XXXV — AGRICULTURE, HORTICULTURE, AND ANIMAL INDUSTRY
**Chapter:** 576 — AGRICULTURAL FERTILIZERS
**Part:** II — FLORIDA AGRICULTURAL PROMOTIONAL CAMPAIGN ACT
**Section:** 576.085
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The department shall establish, by rule, a minimum plant nutrient content.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 65-348; ss. 14, 35, ch. 69-106; s. 5, ch. 76-35; ss. 8, 19, ch. 92-143; s. 11, ch. 97-6.
