# Fla. Stat. § 603.14 — Cooperative certificates as evidence.

**Title:** XXXV — AGRICULTURE, HORTICULTURE, AND ANIMAL INDUSTRY
**Chapter:** 603 — FRUITS AND VEGETABLES
**Part:** I — INSPECTION
**Section:** 603.14
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

All such cooperative government certificates shall be accepted as prima facie evidence in the courts of Florida.

**History:** s. 4, ch. 12292, 1927; CGL 2009.
