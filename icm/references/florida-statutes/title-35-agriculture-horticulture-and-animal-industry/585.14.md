# Fla. Stat. § 585.14 — Information concerning animal diseases.

**Title:** XXXV — AGRICULTURE, HORTICULTURE, AND ANIMAL INDUSTRY
**Chapter:** 585 — ANIMAL INDUSTRY
**Part:** II — DISEASE INSPECTION, CONTROL, AND ERADICATION
**Section:** 585.14
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The department shall collect, preserve, and disseminate information concerning infectious, contagious, communicable, and other diseases of animals, their origin, locality, nature, appearance, manner of dissemination or contagion, and method of treatment required for the successful eradication and control thereof.

**History:** s. 4, ch. 7345, 1917; RGS 2104; s. 4, ch. 9201, 1923; CGL 3320; s. 7, ch. 59-457; s. 3, ch. 61-408; ss. 14, 35, ch. 69-106; s. 16, ch. 90-321.
