# Fla. Stat. § 601.05 — Department of Citrus a body corporate.

**Title:** XXXV — AGRICULTURE, HORTICULTURE, AND ANIMAL INDUSTRY
**Chapter:** 601 — FLORIDA CITRUS CODE
**Part:** II — DISEASE INSPECTION, CONTROL, AND ERADICATION
**Section:** 601.05
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The department shall be a body corporate, shall have power to contract and be contracted with, and shall have and possess all the powers of a body corporate for all purposes necessary for fully carrying out the provisions and requirements of this chapter. The department shall adopt a corporate seal with which it shall authenticate its proceedings.

**History:** s. 5, ch. 25149, 1949; s. 22, ch. 71-186; s. 1, ch. 2009-112; s. 8, ch. 2012-182.
