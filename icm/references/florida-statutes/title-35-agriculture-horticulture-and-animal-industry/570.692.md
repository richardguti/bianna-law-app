# Fla. Stat. § 570.692 — Florida Agricultural Legacy Learning Center.

**Title:** XXXV — AGRICULTURE, HORTICULTURE, AND ANIMAL INDUSTRY
**Chapter:** 570 — DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE AND CONSUMER SERVICES
**Part:** II — PROGRAM SERVICES
**Section:** 570.692
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The Florida Agricultural Legacy Learning Center is designated as the legacy learning center for agriculture and rural history of this state and is established within the department.

**History:** s. 11, ch. 92-151; s. 101, ch. 2014-150; s. 33, ch. 2024-137.

**Note:** Former s. 570.901.
