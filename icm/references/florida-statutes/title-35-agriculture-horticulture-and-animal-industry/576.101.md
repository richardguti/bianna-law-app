# Fla. Stat. § 576.101 — License denial, revocation, or suspension.

**Title:** XXXV — AGRICULTURE, HORTICULTURE, AND ANIMAL INDUSTRY
**Chapter:** 576 — AGRICULTURAL FERTILIZERS
**Part:** II — FLORIDA AGRICULTURAL PROMOTIONAL CAMPAIGN ACT
**Section:** 576.101
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The department may deny, suspend, or revoke any license issued by the department for any violation of this chapter, the rules adopted under this chapter, or any lawful order of the department.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 65-348; ss. 14, 35, ch. 69-106; s. 6, ch. 78-95; s. 5, ch. 83-202; ss. 11, 19, ch. 92-143; ss. 8, 11, ch. 97-6; s. 127, ch. 2014-150.
