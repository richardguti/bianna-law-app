# Fla. Stat. § 589.34 — Revocation of agreement.

**Title:** XXXV — AGRICULTURE, HORTICULTURE, AND ANIMAL INDUSTRY
**Chapter:** 589 — FORESTRY
**Part:** II — DISEASE INSPECTION, CONTROL, AND ERADICATION
**Section:** 589.34
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Any agreement or revision thereof entered into by the Florida Forest Service and a county or municipality under the provisions of this law shall continue from year to year, unless written notice is given to the other party 30 days prior to July 1 of any year of the intention to discontinue the work and cancel the agreement.

**History:** s. 7, ch. 20899, 1941; ss. 14, 35, ch. 69-106; s. 7, ch. 71-183; s. 55, ch. 2012-7.
