# Fla. Stat. § 578.25 — Use of disclaimer clause.

**Title:** XXXV — AGRICULTURE, HORTICULTURE, AND ANIMAL INDUSTRY
**Chapter:** 578 — SEED
**Part:** II — FLORIDA AGRICULTURAL PROMOTIONAL CAMPAIGN ACT
**Section:** 578.25
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The use of a disclaimer or nonwarranty clause in any invoice, advertising, labeling, or written, printed, or graphic matter pertaining to any seed shall not relieve or exempt any person from any provisions of the Florida Seed Law.

**History:** s. 14, ch. 22694, 1945.
