# Fla. Stat. § 604.17 — License required.

**Title:** XXXV — AGRICULTURE, HORTICULTURE, AND ANIMAL INDUSTRY
**Chapter:** 604 — GENERAL AGRICULTURAL LAWS
**Part:** II — FLORIDA TROPICAL FRUIT POLICY ACT
**Section:** 604.17
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

It shall be unlawful for any dealer in agricultural products who comes within the terms of this law to engage in such business in this state without a state license issued by the department.

**History:** s. 3, ch. 20678, 1941; ss. 14, 35, ch. 69-106; s. 3, ch. 76-168; s. 1, ch. 77-457; ss. 4, 12, 14, ch. 79-238; ss. 2, 3, ch. 81-318; ss. 9, 10, ch. 90-161; s. 4, ch. 91-429.
