# Fla. Stat. § 597.0042 — Public records exemptions; aquaculture records.

**Title:** XXXV — AGRICULTURE, HORTICULTURE, AND ANIMAL INDUSTRY
**Chapter:** 597 — AQUACULTURE
**Part:** II — DISEASE INSPECTION, CONTROL, AND ERADICATION
**Section:** 597.0042
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
The following records held by the department are confidential and exempt from s. 119.07(1) and s. 24(a), Art. I of the State Constitution:
(a)  
Shellfish receiving and production records generated by shellfish processing facilities licensed pursuant to s. 597.020.

(b)  
Audit records and supporting documentation required for submerged land leases issued in accordance with chapter 253 or former chapter 370.

(c)  
Aquaculture production records and receipts generated by aquaculture facilities certified pursuant to s. 597.004.

(2)  
A record made confidential and exempt under subsection (1) may be disclosed to another governmental entity in the performance of its duties and responsibilities and may be disclosed pursuant to s. 474.2165.

(3)  
The exemption from public records requirements under subsectemeanor of the first degree, punishable as provided in s. 775.082 or s. 775.083.

**History:** s. 28, ch. 96-247; s. 12, ch. 99-390; s. 28, ch. 2000-364; s. 39, ch. 2001-63; s. 155, ch. 2014-150.
