# Fla. Stat. § 582.295 — Automatic dissolution of districts.

**Title:** XXXV — AGRICULTURE, HORTICULTURE, AND ANIMAL INDUSTRY
**Chapter:** 582 — SOIL AND WATER CONSERVATION
**Part:** II — FLORIDA AGRICULTURAL PROMOTIONAL CAMPAIGN ACT
**Section:** 582.295
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
If the governing body of a district fails to meet as required under s. 582.195, the district shall be automatically dissolved as of January 1 of the year immediately following the year in which the governing body failed to meet. All assets and liabilities of the district shall be transferred to the Department of Agriculture and Consumer Services.

(2)  
Sections 189.076 and 582.30-582.32 do not apply to dissolution of a district under this section.

**History:** s. 5, ch. 2022-191.
