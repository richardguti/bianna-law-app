# Fla. Stat. § 274.09 — Construction.

**Title:** XVIII — PUBLIC LANDS AND PROPERTY
**Chapter:** 274 — TANGIBLE PERSONAL PROPERTY OWNED BY LOCAL GOVERNMENTS
**Part:** III — WILD AND SCENIC RIVERS
**Section:** 274.09
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The provisions of this act shall be liberally interpreted to be cumulative and supplementary to any general, special or local law, heretofore or hereafter enacted.

**History:** s. 10, ch. 59-163.
