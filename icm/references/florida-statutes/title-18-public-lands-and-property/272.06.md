# Fla. Stat. § 272.06 — Authority to contract for utility services.

**Title:** XVIII — PUBLIC LANDS AND PROPERTY
**Chapter:** 272 — CAPITOL CENTER
**Part:** III — WILD AND SCENIC RIVERS
**Section:** 272.06
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The Department of Management Services may provide or enter into contracts to provide heating, power, lighting, cooling systems, and other necessary services or facilities for any or all of said buildings.

**History:** s. 6, ch. 25032, 1949; ss. 22, 35, ch. 69-106; s. 74, ch. 71-377; s. 2, ch. 75-70; s. 46, ch. 85-349; s. 202, ch. 92-279; s. 55, ch. 92-326; s. 47, ch. 98-279.
