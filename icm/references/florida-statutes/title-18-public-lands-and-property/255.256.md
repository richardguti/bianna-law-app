# Fla. Stat. § 255.256 — Energy performance index.

**Title:** XVIII — PUBLIC LANDS AND PROPERTY
**Chapter:** 255 — PUBLIC PROPERTY AND PUBLICLY OWNED BUILDINGS
**Part:** IV — ACCIDENTAL RELEASE PREVENTION AND RISK MANAGEMENT PLANNING
**Section:** 255.256
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The department shall promulgate rules for energy performance indices as defined in s. 255.253(3) to audit and evaluate competing design proposals submitted to the state.

**History:** s. 6, ch. 74-187.
