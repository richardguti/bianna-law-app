# Fla. Stat. § 258.158 — Exemption from s. 588.15.

**Title:** XVIII — PUBLIC LANDS AND PROPERTY
**Chapter:** 258 — STATE PARKS AND PRESERVES
**Part:** I — PARKS
**Section:** 258.158
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The state is exempt from the provisions of s. 588.15 with respect to any property within the state park system that has free-roaming animal populations.

**History:** s. 3, ch. 2011-110.
