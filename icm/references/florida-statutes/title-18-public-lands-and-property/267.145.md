# Fla. Stat. § 267.145 — Florida network of public archaeology centers.

**Title:** XVIII — PUBLIC LANDS AND PROPERTY
**Chapter:** 267 — HISTORICAL RESOURCES
**Part:** III — WILD AND SCENIC RIVERS
**Section:** 267.145
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
The Department of State shall create, through a memorandum of agreement, a Florida network of public archaeology centers to help stem the rapid deterioration of this state's buried past and to expand public interest in archaeology. The network of public archaeology centers shall work in cooperation with the State Historic Preservation Officer and the division through the cooperative memorandum of agreement.

(2)  
The network of public archaeology centers shall be administered through a public archaeology center at the University of West Florida. Additional centers shall be established throughout the state with each center located in an existing facility, free of charge, of a state university with a local archaeological program, a regional historic preservation office, the facility of a nonprofit organization that is involved in the archaeology of the region, or other locations as set forth in the memorandum of agreement.

**History:** s. 3, ch. 2004-91.
