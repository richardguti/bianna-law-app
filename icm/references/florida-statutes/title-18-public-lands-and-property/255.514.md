# Fla. Stat. § 255.514 — Division of Bond Finance; revenue bonds.

**Title:** XVIII — PUBLIC LANDS AND PROPERTY
**Chapter:** 255 — PUBLIC PROPERTY AND PUBLICLY OWNED BUILDINGS
**Part:** IV — ACCIDENTAL RELEASE PREVENTION AND RISK MANAGEMENT PLANNING
**Section:** 255.514
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The division is authorized to issue obligations under this act on behalf of and at the request of the Department of Management Services.

**History:** s. 5, ch. 85-349; s. 33, ch. 98-279.
