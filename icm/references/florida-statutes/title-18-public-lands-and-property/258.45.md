# Fla. Stat. § 258.45 — Provisions not superseded.

**Title:** XVIII — PUBLIC LANDS AND PROPERTY
**Chapter:** 258 — STATE PARKS AND PRESERVES
**Part:** II — AQUATIC PRESERVES
**Section:** 258.45
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The provisions of this act shall not supersede, but shall be subject to, the provisions of ss. 403.501-403.518.

**History:** ss. 3, 6, ch. 75-172; s. 48, ch. 90-331.
