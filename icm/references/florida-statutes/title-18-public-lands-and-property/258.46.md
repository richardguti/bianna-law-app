# Fla. Stat. § 258.46 — Enforcement; violations; penalty.

**Title:** XVIII — PUBLIC LANDS AND PROPERTY
**Chapter:** 258 — STATE PARKS AND PRESERVES
**Part:** II — AQUATIC PRESERVES
**Section:** 258.46
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

This act may be enforced by the Board of Trustees of the Internal Improvement Trust Fund or in accordance with s. 403.412. However, any violation by any person, natural or corporate, of this act or any rule or regulation issued hereunder is further punishable by a civil penalty of not less than $750 per day or more than $7,500 per day of such violation. Each day during any portion of which such violation occurs constitutes a separate offense.

**History:** s. 5, ch. 75-172; s. 6, ch. 2020-158.
