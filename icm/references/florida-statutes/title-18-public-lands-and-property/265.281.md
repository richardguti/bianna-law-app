# Fla. Stat. § 265.281 — Florida Arts and Culture Act; short title.

**Title:** XVIII — PUBLIC LANDS AND PROPERTY
**Chapter:** 265 — MEMORIALS, MUSEUMS, AND ARTS AND CULTURE
**Part:** III — WILD AND SCENIC RIVERS
**Section:** 265.281
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Sections 265.281-265.703 may be cited as the “Florida Arts and Culture Act.”

**History:** s. 1, ch. 80-319; s. 3, ch. 2009-72; s. 4, ch. 2021-71.
