# Fla. Stat. § 256.06 — Mutilation or disrespect of state or United States flag.

**Title:** XVIII — PUBLIC LANDS AND PROPERTY
**Chapter:** 256 — FLAGS
**Part:** IV — ACCIDENTAL RELEASE PREVENTION AND RISK MANAGEMENT PLANNING
**Section:** 256.06
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

No person shall publicly mutilate, deface, defile, defy, trample upon, or by word or act cast contempt upon any such flag, standard, color, ensign or shield.

**History:** ss. 1, 2, 3, ch. 7819, 1919; s. 1, ch. 9325, 1923; CGL 8117, 8118.
