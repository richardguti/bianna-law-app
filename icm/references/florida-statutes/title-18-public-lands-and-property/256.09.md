# Fla. Stat. § 256.09 — Penalty.

**Title:** XVIII — PUBLIC LANDS AND PROPERTY
**Chapter:** 256 — FLAGS
**Part:** IV — ACCIDENTAL RELEASE PREVENTION AND RISK MANAGEMENT PLANNING
**Section:** 256.09
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Any person violating the provisions of s. 256.05 or s. 256.06 shall be guilty of a misdemeanor of the second degree, punishable as provided in s. 775.082 or s. 775.083.

**History:** ss. 1-3, ch. 7819, 1919; s. 1, ch. 9325, 1923; CGL 8117, 8118; s. 155, ch. 71-136.
