# Fla. Stat. § 274.12 — Special districts subject to chapter.

**Title:** XVIII — PUBLIC LANDS AND PROPERTY
**Chapter:** 274 — TANGIBLE PERSONAL PROPERTY OWNED BY LOCAL GOVERNMENTS
**Part:** III — WILD AND SCENIC RIVERS
**Section:** 274.12
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Every special district governed by the provisions of this act shall comply with the provisions of this chapter.

**History:** s. 12, ch. 79-183; s. 3, ch. 2004-296.
