# Fla. Stat. § 258.601 — Enforcement of prohibited activities.

**Title:** XVIII — PUBLIC LANDS AND PROPERTY
**Chapter:** 258 — STATE PARKS AND PRESERVES
**Part:** II — AQUATIC PRESERVES
**Section:** 258.601
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Prohibited activities under this chapter shall be enforced by the Department of Environmental Protection and the Division of Law Enforcement of the Fish and Wildlife Conservation Commission and its officers.
