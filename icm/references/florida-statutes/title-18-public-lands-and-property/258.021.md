# Fla. Stat. § 258.021 — Power of eminent domain; procedure.

**Title:** XVIII — PUBLIC LANDS AND PROPERTY
**Chapter:** 258 — STATE PARKS AND PRESERVES
**Part:** I — PARKS
**Section:** 258.021
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Whenever the Division of Recreation and Parks shall find it necessary to acquire private property for state parks, or rights-of-way for state parks, or for exercising any of the powers and duties authorized and prescribed by law to be exercised and performed by the division, the division is hereby empowered and authorized to exercise the right of eminent domain and to proceed to condemn said property in the same manner as provided by law for the condemnation of private property by counties.

**History:** s. 28, ch. 29615, 1955; ss. 25, 35, ch. 69-106.

**Note:** F.S. 258.0176258.017  
Dedication of state park lands for public use.
—

The Division of Recreation and Parks is authorized and empowered, from time to time, by resolution, to dedicate and reserve for the use of the public all or any part of the lands acquired by the division for park purposes; provided however, that said dedication and reservation shall be subject to such rules and regulations, as to reasonable use by the public, as may be adopted by the division.
