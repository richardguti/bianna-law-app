# Fla. Stat. § 258.603 — Andrew “Red” Harris Spoil Island designated.

**Title:** XVIII — PUBLIC LANDS AND PROPERTY
**Chapter:** 258 — STATE PARKS AND PRESERVES
**Part:** III — WILD AND SCENIC RIVERS
**Section:** 258.603
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
SI3, a mangrove island adjacent to the Intracoastal Waterway within Jupiter Sound, is designated as the “Andrew ‘Red' Harris Spoil Island.”

(2)  
The Department of Environmental Protection is directed to erect suitable markers designating the “Andrew ‘Red' Harris Spoil Island” as described in subsection (1).

**History:** s. 1, ch. 2026-42.
