# Fla. Stat. § 256.031 — Department of State custodian of state flag.

**Title:** XVIII — PUBLIC LANDS AND PROPERTY
**Chapter:** 256 — FLAGS
**Part:** IV — ACCIDENTAL RELEASE PREVENTION AND RISK MANAGEMENT PLANNING
**Section:** 256.031
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)(a)  
The Department of State is the custodian of the official state flag, and, for the purpose of assisting schools, governmental agencies, and other groups and organizations in the care, handling, and history of the state flag, including all flags that have flown over any part of the State of Florida by those sovereigns to which Florida has belonged, the department is hereby authorized to buy F.S. 256.026256.02  
Certain officers to provide flag.
——

The officer charged with the maintenance or upkeep of the buildings mentioned in s. 256.01 shall provide suitable flags and cause them to be displayed, the expense to be borne out of the funds provided for the upkeep and maintenance of said buildings.

**History:** s. 2, ch. 7369, 1917; RGS 1207; CGL 1684.
