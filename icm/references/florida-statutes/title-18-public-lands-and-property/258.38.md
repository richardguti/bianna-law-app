# Fla. Stat. § 258.38 — Types of aquatic preserves.

**Title:** XVIII — PUBLIC LANDS AND PROPERTY
**Chapter:** 258 — STATE PARKS AND PRESERVES
**Part:** II — AQUATIC PRESERVES
**Section:** 258.38
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Each aquatic preserve shall be characterized as being one or more of the following principal types:
(1)  
Biological.

(2)  
Aesthetic.

(3)  
Scientific.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 75-172.
