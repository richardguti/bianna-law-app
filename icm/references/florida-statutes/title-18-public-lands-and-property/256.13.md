# Fla. Stat. § 256.13 — POW-MIA flag; display at rest areas.

**Title:** XVIII — PUBLIC LANDS AND PROPERTY
**Chapter:** 256 — FLAGS
**Part:** IV — ACCIDENTAL RELEASE PREVENTION AND RISK MANAGEMENT PLANNING
**Section:** 256.13
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The Florida Department of Transportation shall fly the POW-MIA flag year-round at each of the rest areas along interstate highways in this state.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 2000-329.
