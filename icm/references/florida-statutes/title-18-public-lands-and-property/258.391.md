# Fla. Stat. § 258.391 — Cockroach Bay Aquatic Preserve.

**Title:** XVIII — PUBLIC LANDS AND PROPERTY
**Chapter:** 258 — STATE PARKS AND PRESERVES
**Part:** II — AQUATIC PRESERVES
**Section:** 258.391
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

——

The following described area in Hillsborough County is hereby designated by the Legislature for inclusion in the aquatic preserve system under the Florida Aquatic Preserve Act of 1975. Such area shall be known as the Cockroach Bay Aquatic Preserve.
