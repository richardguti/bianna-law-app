# Fla. Stat. § 260.0161 — Coordination with Department of Transportation.

**Title:** XVIII — PUBLIC LANDS AND PROPERTY
**Chapter:** 260 — FLORIDA GREENWAYS AND TRAILS ACT
**Part:** III — WILD AND SCENIC RIVERS
**Section:** 260.0161
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

——

(1)  
Upon the request of the department, the Department of Transportation shall provide information to the department on abandoned and to-be-abandoned railroad rights-of-way.

(2) &%s="Number">(d)  
At the option of the landowner, acceleration of the acquisition process or higher consideration in the ranking process when any lands owned by the landowner are under consideration for acquisition by the state or other unit of government.

(e)  
At the option of the landowner, removal of any lands owned by the landowner from consideration for acquisition by the state or other unit of government.

(f)  
Execution of patrol and protection agreements.

(g)  
Where applicable and appropriate, providing lease fees, not to exceed fair market value of the leasehold interest.
