# Fla. Stat. § 257.01 — State Library; creation; administration.

**Title:** XVIII — PUBLIC LANDS AND PROPERTY
**Chapter:** 257 — PUBLIC LIBRARIES AND STATE ARCHIVES
**Part:** IV — ACCIDENTAL RELEASE PREVENTION AND RISK MANAGEMENT PLANNING
**Section:** 257.01
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

There is created and established the State Library which shall be located at the capital. The State Library shall be administered by the Division of Library and Information Services of the Department of State.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 10278, 1925; CGL 1687; s. 1, ch. 63-39; ss. 10, 35, ch. 69-106; s. 21, ch. 69-353; s. 1, ch. 70-250; s. 1, ch. 71-279; s. 86, ch. 73-333; s. 1, ch. 74-228; s. 1, ch. 81-68; s. 13, ch. 86-163.
