# Fla. Stat. § 255.501 — Building and Facilities Act; short title.

**Title:** XVIII — PUBLIC LANDS AND PROPERTY
**Chapter:** 255 — PUBLIC PROPERTY AND PUBLICLY OWNED BUILDINGS
**Part:** IV — ACCIDENTAL RELEASE PREVENTION AND RISK MANAGEMENT PLANNING
**Section:** 255.501
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Sections 255.501-255.525 shall be known and may be cited as the “Florida Building and Facilities Act.”

**History:** s. 1, ch. 85-349.
