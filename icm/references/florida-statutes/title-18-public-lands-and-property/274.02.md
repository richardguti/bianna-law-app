# Fla. Stat. § 274.02 — Record and inventory of certain property.

**Title:** XVIII — PUBLIC LANDS AND PROPERTY
**Chapter:** 274 — TANGIBLE PERSONAL PROPERTY OWNED BY LOCAL GOVERNMENTS
**Part:** III — WILD AND SCENIC RIVERS
**Section:** 274.02
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
The word “property” as used in this section means fixtures and other tangible personal property of a nonconsumable nature.

(2)  
The Chief Financial Officer shall establish by rule the requirements for the recording of property and for the periodic review of property for inventory purposes.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 59-163; s. 1, ch. 61-102; s. 4, ch. 2023-144.
