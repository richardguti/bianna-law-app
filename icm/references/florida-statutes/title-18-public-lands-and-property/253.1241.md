# Fla. Stat. § 253.1241 — Studies.

**Title:** XVIII — PUBLIC LANDS AND PROPERTY
**Chapter:** 253 — STATE LANDS
**Part:** IV — ACCIDENTAL RELEASE PREVENTION AND RISK MANAGEMENT PLANNING
**Section:** 253.1241
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The Department of Environmental Protection shall have a period of 90 days, after application therefor, in which to make the studies and surveys required by s. 253.12. The Board of Trustees of the Internal Improvement Trust Fund and others required by those sections to obtain such studies and surveys shall request them within 30 days after the receipt of an application for sale or for a dredge or fill permit, as the case may be.

**History:** s. 6, ch. 67-393; ss. 25, 27, 35, ch. 69-106; s. 1, ch. 69-337; s. 79, ch. 77-104; s. 3, ch. 82-27; s. 3, ch. 91-221; s. 73, ch. 94-356.
