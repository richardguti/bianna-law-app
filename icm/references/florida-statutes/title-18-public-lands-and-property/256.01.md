# Fla. Stat. § 256.01 — Flag of United States to be displayed.

**Title:** XVIII — PUBLIC LANDS AND PROPERTY
**Chapter:** 255 — PUBLIC PROPERTY AND PUBLICLY OWNED BUILDINGS
**Part:** IV — ACCIDENTAL RELEASE PREVENTION AND RISK MANAGEMENT PLANNING
**Section:** 256.01
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The flag of the United States shall be displayed daily when the weather permits, from a staff upon the state capitol and upon each county courthouse.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 7369, 1917; RGS 1206; CGL 1683.
