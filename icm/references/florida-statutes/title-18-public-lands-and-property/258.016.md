# Fla. Stat. § 258.016 — Senior/disabled citizen camping permit.

**Title:** XVIII — PUBLIC LANDS AND PROPERTY
**Chapter:** 258 — STATE PARKS AND PRESERVES
**Part:** I — PARKS
**Section:** 258.016
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The Division of Recreation and Parks is authorized to issue a senior/disabled citizen camping permit. This permit shall be available free of charge to Florida citizens who are at least 65 years of age or older or Florida citizens possessing a current social security disability award certificate or a 100 percent disability award certificate from the Federal Government. The permit shall allow the person named and one companion to receive a 50 percent discount on current base campsite fees at Florida state parks.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 87-336.
