# Fla. Stat. § 253.128 — Enforcement; board or agency under special law.

**Title:** XVIII — PUBLIC LANDS AND PROPERTY
**Chapter:** 253 — STATE LANDS
**Part:** IV — ACCIDENTAL RELEASE PREVENTION AND RISK MANAGEMENT PLANNING
**Section:** 253.128
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

In any county where the Legislature by special law or general law with local application has heretofore or hereafter transferred or delegated to any county board or agency other than the board of county commissioners or the governing body of any municipality powers and duties over the establishment of bulkhead line or lines, dredging permits, fill permits, seawall construction or any other powers of a like nature such agency shall have jurisdiction under this law in lieu of the board of county commissioners or the governing body of any municipality as the case may be.

**History:** s. 8, ch. 57-362.
