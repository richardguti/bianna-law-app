# Fla. Stat. § 255.0515 — Bids for state contracts; substitution of subcontractors.

**Title:** XVIII — PUBLIC LANDS AND PROPERTY
**Chapter:** 255 — PUBLIC PROPERTY AND PUBLICLY OWNED BUILDINGS
**Part:** IV — ACCIDENTAL RELEASE PREVENTION AND RISK MANAGEMENT PLANNING
**Section:** 255.0515
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

With respect to state contracts let pursuant to competitive bidding, whether under chapter 1013, relating to educational facilities, or this chapter, relating to public buildings, the contractor shall not remove or replace subcontractors listed in the bid subsequent to the lists being made public at the bid opening, except upon good cause shown.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 78-389; s. 928, ch. 2002-387.
