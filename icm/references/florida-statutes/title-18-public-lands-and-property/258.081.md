# Fla. Stat. § 258.081 — Stephen Foster State Folk Culture Center.

**Title:** XVIII — PUBLIC LANDS AND PROPERTY
**Chapter:** 258 — STATE PARKS AND PRESERVES
**Part:** I — PARKS
**Section:** 258.081
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The division shall maintain and operate the Stephen Foster State Folk Culture Center facility in such manner that the performing arts component of the Florida Folklife Programs provided in s. 265.802 shall have priority use of the facility.

**History:** s. 7, ch. 79-322; s. 126, ch. 83-217; s. 29, ch. 86-163; s. 19, ch. 2021-71.
