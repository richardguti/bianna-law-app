# Fla. Stat. § 258.0165 — Defibrillators in state parks.

**Title:** XVIII — PUBLIC LANDS AND PROPERTY
**Chapter:** 258 — STATE PARKS AND PRESERVES
**Part:** I — PARKS
**Section:** 258.0165
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
Each state park is encouraged to have on the premises at all times a functioning automated external defibrillator.

(2)  
State parks that provide automated external defibrillators shall ensure that employees and volunteers are properly trained in accordance with s. 401.2915.

(3)  
The location of each automated external defibrillator shall be registered with a local emergency medical services medical director.

(4)  
The use of automated external defibrillators by employees and volunteers shall be covered under the provisions of ss. 768.13 and 768.1325.

Dedication of state park lands for public use.
—

The Division of Recreation and Parks is authorized and empowered, from time to time, by resolution, to dedicate and reserve for the use of the public all or any part of the lands acquired by the division for park purposes; provided however, that said dedication and reservation shall be subject to such rules and regulations, as to reasonable use by the public, as may be adopted by the division.
