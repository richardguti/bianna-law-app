# Fla. Stat. § 257.14 — Division of Library and Information Services; rules.

**Title:** XVIII — PUBLIC LANDS AND PROPERTY
**Chapter:** 257 — PUBLIC LIBRARIES AND STATE ARCHIVES
**Part:** IV — ACCIDENTAL RELEASE PREVENTION AND RISK MANAGEMENT PLANNING
**Section:** 257.14
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The Division of Library and Information Services has authority to adopt rules pursuant to ss. 120.536(1) and 120.54 to implement the provisions of this chapter.

**History:** s. 2, ch. 61-402; s. 4, ch. 63-39; ss. 10, 35, ch. 69-106; s. 21, ch. 69-353; s. 3, ch. 83-24; s. 19, ch. 86-163; s. 46, ch. 98-200.
