# Fla. Stat. § 274.07 — Authorizing and recording the disposal of property.

**Title:** XVIII — PUBLIC LANDS AND PROPERTY
**Chapter:** 274 — TANGIBLE PERSONAL PROPERTY OWNED BY LOCAL GOVERNMENTS
**Part:** III — WILD AND SCENIC RIVERS
**Section:** 274.07
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Authority for the disposal of property shall be recorded in the minutes of the governmental unit. The disposal of property within the purview of s. 274.02 shall be recorded in the records required by that section.

**History:** s. 7, ch. 59-163.274.06
