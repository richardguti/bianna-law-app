# Fla. Stat. § 257.33 — Notices.

**Title:** XVIII — PUBLIC LANDS AND PROPERTY
**Chapter:** 257 — PUBLIC LIBRARIES AND STATE ARCHIVES
**Part:** IV — ACCIDENTAL RELEASE PREVENTION AND RISK MANAGEMENT PLANNING
**Section:** 257.33
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

In the event of withdrawal from the compact, the Secretary of State shall send and receive any notices required by Art. XI (b) of the compact.

**History:** s. 6, ch. 72-157.
