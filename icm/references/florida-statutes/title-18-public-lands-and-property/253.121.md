# Fla. Stat. § 253.121 — Conveyances of such lands heretofore made, ratified, confirmed, and validated.

**Title:** XVIII — PUBLIC LANDS AND PROPERTY
**Chapter:** 253 — STATE LANDS
**Part:** IV — ACCIDENTAL RELEASE PREVENTION AND RISK MANAGEMENT PLANNING
**Section:** 253.121
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

All conveyances of sovereignty lands heretofore made by the Board of Trustees of the Internal Improvement Trust Fund subsequent to the enactment of chapters 6451 (June 5, 1913), 6960 (June 2, 1915), and 7304 (May 21, 1917), Acts of 1913, 1915, and 1917, respectively, where advertisement therefor was published in the county of sale but not in the county seat, are hereby ratified, confirmed, and validated in all respects, including all defects subject to ratification, confirmance, and validation by the Legislature.
