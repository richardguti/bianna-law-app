# Fla. Stat. § 257.25 — Free library service.

**Title:** XVIII — PUBLIC LANDS AND PROPERTY
**Chapter:** 257 — PUBLIC LIBRARIES AND STATE ARCHIVES
**Part:** IV — ACCIDENTAL RELEASE PREVENTION AND RISK MANAGEMENT PLANNING
**Section:** 257.25
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Free library service shall constitute as a minimum the free lending of library materials that are made available for circulation and the free provision of reference and information services.

**History:** s. 13, ch. 61-402; s. 7, ch. 83-24.
