# Fla. Stat. § 267.135 — Location of archaeological sites.

**Title:** XVIII — PUBLIC LANDS AND PROPERTY
**Chapter:** 267 — HISTORICAL RESOURCES
**Part:** III — WILD AND SCENIC RIVERS
**Section:** 267.135
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Any information identifying the location of an archaeological site held by the Division of Historical Resources of the Department of State is exempt from s. 119.07(1) and s. 24(a), Art. I of the State Constitution, if the Division of Historical Resources finds that disclosure of such information will create a substantial risk of harm, theft, or destruction at such site.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 2001-162; s. 1, ch. 2006-106.
