# Fla. Stat. § 255.251 — Energy Conservation and Sustainable Buildings Act; short title.

**Title:** XVIII — PUBLIC LANDS AND PROPERTY
**Chapter:** 255 — PUBLIC PROPERTY AND PUBLICLY OWNED BUILDINGS
**Part:** IV — ACCIDENTAL RELEASE PREVENTION AND RISK MANAGEMENT PLANNING
**Section:** 255.251
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

This act shall be cited as the “Florida Energy Conservation and Sustainable Buildings Act.”

**History:** s. 1, ch. 74-187; s. 16, ch. 2008-227.
