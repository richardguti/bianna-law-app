# Fla. Stat. § 257.21 — Maximum grants allowable.

**Title:** XVIII — PUBLIC LANDS AND PROPERTY
**Chapter:** 257 — PUBLIC LIBRARIES AND STATE ARCHIVES
**Part:** IV — ACCIDENTAL RELEASE PREVENTION AND RISK MANAGEMENT PLANNING
**Section:** 257.21
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Any reduction in grants because of insufficient funds shall be prorated on the basis of maximum grants allowable.

**History:** s. 9, ch. 61-402.
