# Fla. Stat. § 257.32 — Compact administrator.

**Title:** XVIII — PUBLIC LANDS AND PROPERTY
**Chapter:** 257 — PUBLIC LIBRARIES AND STATE ARCHIVES
**Part:** IV — ACCIDENTAL RELEASE PREVENTION AND RISK MANAGEMENT PLANNING
**Section:** 257.32
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The Secretary of State shall be the compact administrator pursuant to Art. X of the compact. The Secretary of State may appoint a deputy compact administrator pursuant to said article.

**History:** s. 5, ch. 72-157.
