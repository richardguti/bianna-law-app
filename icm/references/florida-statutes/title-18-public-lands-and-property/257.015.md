# Fla. Stat. § 257.015 — Definitions.

**Title:** XVIII — PUBLIC LANDS AND PROPERTY
**Chapter:** 257 — PUBLIC LIBRARIES AND STATE ARCHIVES
**Part:** IV — ACCIDENTAL RELEASE PREVENTION AND RISK MANAGEMENT PLANNING
**Section:** 257.015
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

As used in this chapter, the term:
(1)  
“Department” means the Department of State.

(2)  
“Depository library” means a library that has been designated as a depository for receiving state publications in accordance with s. 257.05(3).

(3)  
“Division” means the Division of Library and Information Services of the Department of State.

(4)  
“Secretary” means the Secretary of State.

(5)  
“State Librarian” means the person appointed by the secretary as the director of the Division of Library and Information Services pursuant to s. 257.031.

(6)  
F.S. 257.016257.01  
State Library; creation; administration.
——

There is created and established the State Library which shall be located at the capital. The State Library shall be administered by the Division of Library and Information Services of the Department of State.
