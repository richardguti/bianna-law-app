# Fla. Stat. § 260.021 — Recreational opportunities on mined lands.

**Title:** XVIII — PUBLIC LANDS AND PROPERTY
**Chapter:** 260 — FLORIDA GREENWAYS AND TRAILS ACT
**Part:** III — WILD AND SCENIC RIVERS
**Section:** 260.021
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

——

The Florida Wildlife Federation, Audubon Florida, and Rails-to-Trails Conservancy, in partnership with the phosphate industry and other mining companies, are encouraged to continue the operation of the nonprofit corporation, Florida Mining-Recreation, Inc., for the purpose of working with industry, government, and private landowners to create plans and assist in the development of recreational opportunities on mined lands in the state. These opportunities should include walking, hiking, use of off-highway vehicles, canoeing, bicycling, equestrian activities, wildlife viewing, and other trail areas along w%ustify">Segment 15: Florida Keys.
