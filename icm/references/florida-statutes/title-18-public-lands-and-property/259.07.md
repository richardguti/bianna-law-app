# Fla. Stat. § 259.07 — Public meetings.

**Title:** XVIII — PUBLIC LANDS AND PROPERTY
**Chapter:** 259 — LAND ACQUISITIONS FOR CONSERVATION OR RECREATION
**Part:** III — WILD AND SCENIC RIVERS
**Section:** 259.07
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The council, before making recommendations to the board for the purchase of any land pursuant to s. 259.035, shall hold one or more public meetings on the proposed purchase of such land in areas of the state where major portions of such land are situated.
