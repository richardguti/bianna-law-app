# Fla. Stat. § 253.129 — Confirmation of title in upland owners.

**Title:** XVIII — PUBLIC LANDS AND PROPERTY
**Chapter:** 253 — STATE LANDS
**Part:** IV — ACCIDENTAL RELEASE PREVENTION AND RISK MANAGEMENT PLANNING
**Section:** 253.129
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The title to all lands heretofore filled or developed is herewith confirmed in the upland owners and the trustees shall on request issue a disclaimer to each such owner.

**History:** s. 9, ch. 57-362; s. 13, ch. 59-1.
