# Fla. Stat. § 270.08 — Notice of sale of public lands.

**Title:** XVIII — PUBLIC LANDS AND PROPERTY
**Chapter:** 270 — PUBLIC LANDS
**Part:** III — WILD AND SCENIC RIVERS
**Section:** 270.08
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

When the Board of Trustees of the Internal Improvement Trust Fund considers it expedient to sell any of the lands that are vested in the Board of Trustees of the Internal Improvement Trust Fund of the state, it must give notice of the sale as provided in s. 253.115. This section does not limit the applicability of s. 253.111.

**History:** s. 2, ch. 5943, 1909; s. 1, ch. 6452, 1913; RGS 1223; CGL 1768; s. 1092, ch. 19355, 1939; CGL 1940 Supp. 892(411); s. 2, ch. 61-119; s. 3, ch. 65-324; ss. 27, 35, ch. 69-106; s. 76, ch. 71-355; s. 499, ch. 94-356.
