# Fla. Stat. § 253.70 — Public notice.

**Title:** XVIII — PUBLIC LANDS AND PROPERTY
**Chapter:** 253 — STATE LANDS
**Part:** IV — ACCIDENTAL RELEASE PREVENTION AND RISK MANAGEMENT PLANNING
**Section:** 253.70
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Upon receiving an application under this act that satisfactorily sets forth the information required by s. 253.69, the board shall give notice of the application as provided by s. 253.115.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 69-46; ss. 27, 35, ch. 69-106; s. 23, ch. 78-95; s. 494, ch. 94-356.
