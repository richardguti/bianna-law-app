# Fla. Stat. § 272.11 — Capitol information center.

**Title:** XVIII — PUBLIC LANDS AND PROPERTY
**Chapter:** 272 — CAPITOL CENTER
**Part:** III — WILD AND SCENIC RIVERS
**Section:** 272.11
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The Florida Tourism Industry Marketing Corporation shall establish, maintain, and operate a Capitol information center somewhere within the area of the Capitol Center and employ personnel or enter into contracts to maintain same.

**History:** s. 3, ch. 29843, 1955; ss. 22, 35, ch. 69-106; s. 1, ch. 73-274; s. 6, ch. 91-218; s. 23, ch. 96-320; s. 123, ch. 2011-142; s. 28, ch. 2023-173.
