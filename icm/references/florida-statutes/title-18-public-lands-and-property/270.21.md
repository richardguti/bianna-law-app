# Fla. Stat. § 270.21 — Transactions to which sections applicable.

**Title:** XVIII — PUBLIC LANDS AND PROPERTY
**Chapter:** 270 — PUBLIC LANDS
**Part:** III — WILD AND SCENIC RIVERS
**Section:** 270.21
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The provisions of ss. 270.16-270.18 shall apply to all transactions of the state, or any agency thereof, of the kind described in said sections in which the state or its agency has an equity.

**History:** s. 6, ch. 15641, 1931; CGL 1936 Supp. 1771(11).
