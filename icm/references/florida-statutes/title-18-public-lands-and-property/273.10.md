# Fla. Stat. § 273.10 — Repeal.

**Title:** XVIII — PUBLIC LANDS AND PROPERTY
**Chapter:** 274 — TANGIBLE PERSONAL PROPERTY OWNED BY LOCAL GOVERNMENTS
**Part:** III — WILD AND SCENIC RIVERS
**Section:** 273.10
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

This act shall not repeal existing law relating to property but shall be interpreted to be supplementary in nature and shall be applicable to the extent that existing law is not in conflict.

**History:** s. 11, ch. 57-277.
