# Fla. Stat. § 255.523 — Exemption from taxes.

**Title:** XVIII — PUBLIC LANDS AND PROPERTY
**Chapter:** 255 — PUBLIC PROPERTY AND PUBLICLY OWNED BUILDINGS
**Part:** IV — ACCIDENTAL RELEASE PREVENTION AND RISK MANAGEMENT PLANNING
**Section:** 255.523
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The property of the Department of Management Services, the transactions and operations thereof, and the income therefrom shall be exempt from taxation by the state and political subdivisions.

**History:** s. 23, ch. 85-349; s. 40, ch. 98-279.
