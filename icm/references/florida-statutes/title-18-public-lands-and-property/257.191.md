# Fla. Stat. § 257.191 — Construction grants.

**Title:** XVIII — PUBLIC LANDS AND PROPERTY
**Chapter:** 257 — PUBLIC LIBRARIES AND STATE ARCHIVES
**Part:** IV — ACCIDENTAL RELEASE PREVENTION AND RISK MANAGEMENT PLANNING
**Section:** 257.191
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The Division of Library and Information Services may accept and administer library construction moneys appropriated to it and shall allocate such appropriation to municipal, county, and regional libraries in the form of library construction grants on a matching basis. The local matching portion shall be no less than the grant amount, on a dollar-for-dollar basis, up to the maximum grant amount, unless the matching requirement is waived by s. 288.06561. Initiation of a library construction project 12 months or less prior to the grant award under this section shall not affect the eligibility of an applicant to receive a library construction grant. The division shall adopt rules for the administration of library construction grants. For the purposes of this section, s. 257.21 does not apply.

**History:** s. 4, ch. 73-138; s. 5, ch. 83-24; s. 23, ch. 86-163; s. 2, ch. 2003-126.
