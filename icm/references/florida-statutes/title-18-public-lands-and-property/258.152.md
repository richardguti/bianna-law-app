# Fla. Stat. § 258.152 — Ney Landrum State Park designation.

**Title:** XVIII — PUBLIC LANDS AND PROPERTY
**Chapter:** 258 — STATE PARKS AND PRESERVES
**Part:** I — PARKS
**Section:** 258.152
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The St. Marks River Preserve State Park, located in Leon and Jefferson Counties, is renamed and designated as Ney Landrum State Park.

**History:** s. 5, ch. 2025-76.
