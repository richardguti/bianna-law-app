# Fla. Stat. § 255.508 — Participation in pool.

**Title:** XVIII — PUBLIC LANDS AND PROPERTY
**Chapter:** 255 — PUBLIC PROPERTY AND PUBLICLY OWNED BUILDINGS
**Part:** IV — ACCIDENTAL RELEASE PREVENTION AND RISK MANAGEMENT PLANNING
**Section:** 255.508
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

To participate in the pool, an agency head shall submit a request to the Department of Management Services and to the division pursuant to rules adopted by the Department of Management Services pursuant to this act.

**History:** s. 9, ch. 85-349; s. 73, ch. 87-224; s. 18, ch. 97-296; s. 27, ch. 98-279.
