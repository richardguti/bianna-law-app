# Fla. Stat. § 259.06 — Construction.

**Title:** XVIII — PUBLIC LANDS AND PROPERTY
**Chapter:** 259 — LAND ACQUISITIONS FOR CONSERVATION OR RECREATION
**Part:** III — WILD AND SCENIC RIVERS
**Section:** 259.06
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The provisions of ss. 259.01-259.06 shall be liberally construed in a manner to accomplish the purposes thereof.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 72-300.
