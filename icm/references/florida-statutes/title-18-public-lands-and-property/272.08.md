# Fla. Stat. § 272.08 — Duty of repair, maintenance, and supervision.

**Title:** XVIII — PUBLIC LANDS AND PROPERTY
**Chapter:** 272 — CAPITOL CENTER
**Part:** III — WILD AND SCENIC RIVERS
**Section:** 272.08
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Except when otherwise directed by the Department of Management Services, the official or officials now having the duty of repair, care, maintenance, and supervision of any of said buildings shall continue to exercise such authority.

**History:** s. 8, ch. 25032, 1949; ss. 22, 35, ch. 69-106; s. 2, ch. 75-70; s. 48, ch. 85-349; s. 204, ch. 92-279; s. 55, ch. 92-326; s. 49, ch. 98-279.
