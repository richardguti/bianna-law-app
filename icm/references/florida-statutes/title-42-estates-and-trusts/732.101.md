# Fla. Stat. § 732.101 — Intestate estate.

**Title:** XLII — ESTATES AND TRUSTS
**Chapter:** 732 — PROBATE CODE: INTESTATE SUCCESSION AND WILLS
**Part:** I — INTESTATE SUCCESSION
**Section:** 732.101
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
Any part of the estate of a decedent not effectively disposed of by will passes to the decedent's heirs as prescribed in the following sections of this code.

(2)  
The decedent's death is the event that vests the heirs' right to the decedent's intestate property.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 74-106; s. 8, ch. 75-220; s. 14, ch. 2001-226.

**Note:** Created from former s. 731.23.
