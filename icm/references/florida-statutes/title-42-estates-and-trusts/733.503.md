# Fla. Stat. § 733.503 — Appointment of successor upon resignation.

**Title:** XLII — ESTATES AND TRUSTS
**Chapter:** 733 — PROBATE CODE: ADMINISTRATION OF ESTATES
**Part:** V — CURATORS; RESIGNATION AND REMOVAL OF PERSONAL REPRESENTATIVES
**Section:** 733.503
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

When the personal representative's resignation is accepted, the court shall appoint a personal representative or shall appoint a curator to serve until a successor personal representative is appointed.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 74-106; s. 69, ch. 75-220; s. 997, ch. 97-102; s. 114, ch. 2001-226.

**Note:** Created from former s. 734.10.
