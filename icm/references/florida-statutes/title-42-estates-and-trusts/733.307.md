# Fla. Stat. § 733.307 — Effect of appointment of debtor.

**Title:** XLII — ESTATES AND TRUSTS
**Chapter:** 733 — PROBATE CODE: ADMINISTRATION OF ESTATES
**Part:** III — PREFERENCE IN APPOINTMENT AND QUALIFICATIONS OF PERSONAL REPRESENTATIVE
**Section:** 733.307
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The appointment of a debtor as personal representative shall not extinguish the debt due the decedent.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 74-106; s. 63, ch. 75-220; s. 101, ch. 2001-226.

**Note:** Created from former s. 732.51.
