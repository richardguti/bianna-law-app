# Fla. Stat. § 736.0204 — Venue.

**Title:** XLII — ESTATES AND TRUSTS
**Chapter:** 736 — FLORIDA TRUST CODE
**Part:** II — JUDICIAL PROCEEDINGS
**Section:** 736.0204
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Venue for actions and proceedings concerning trusts, including those under s. 736.0201, may be laid in:
(1)  
Any county where the venue is proper under chapter 47;

(2)  
Any county where the beneficiary suing or being sued resides or has its principal place of business; or

(3)  
The county where the trust has its principal place of administration.

**History:** s. 2, ch. 2006-217.
