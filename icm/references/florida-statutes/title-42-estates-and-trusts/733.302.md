# Fla. Stat. § 733.302 — Who may be appointed personal representative.

**Title:** XLII — ESTATES AND TRUSTS
**Chapter:** 733 — PROBATE CODE: ADMINISTRATION OF ESTATES
**Part:** III — PREFERENCE IN APPOINTMENT AND QUALIFICATIONS OF PERSONAL REPRESENTATIVE
**Section:** 733.302
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

After letters have been granted in either a testate or an intestate estate, if a person who was entitled to, and has not waived, preference over the person appointed at the time of the appointment and on whom formal notice was not served seeks the appointment, the letters granted may be revoked and the person entitled to preference may have letters granted after formal notice and hearing.
