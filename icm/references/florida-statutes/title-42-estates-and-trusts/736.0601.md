# Fla. Stat. § 736.0601 — Capacity of settlor of revocable trust.

**Title:** XLII — ESTATES AND TRUSTS
**Chapter:** 736 — FLORIDA TRUST CODE
**Part:** V — CREDITORS’ CLAIMS; SPENDTHRIFT AND DISCRETIONARY TRUSTS
**Section:** 736.0601
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The capacity required to create, amend, revoke, or add property to a revocable trust, or to direct the actions of the trustee of a revocable trust, is the same as that required to make a will.

**History:** s. 6, ch. 2006-217.
