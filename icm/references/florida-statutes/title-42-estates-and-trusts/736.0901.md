# Fla. Stat. § 736.0901 — Applicability of chapter 518.

**Title:** XLII — ESTATES AND TRUSTS
**Chapter:** 736 — FLORIDA TRUST CODE
**Part:** IX — TRUST INVESTMENTS
**Section:** 736.0901
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

A trustee shall invest trust property in accordance with chapter 518.

**History:** s. 9, ch. 2006-217.
