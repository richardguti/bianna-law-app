# Fla. Stat. § 736.0811 — Enforcement and defense of claims.

**Title:** XLII — ESTATES AND TRUSTS
**Chapter:** 736 — FLORIDA TRUST CODE
**Part:** VIII — DUTIES AND POWERS OF TRUSTEE
**Section:** 736.0811
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

A trustee shall take reasonable steps to enforce claims of the trust and to defend claims against the trust.

**History:** s. 8, ch. 2006-217.
