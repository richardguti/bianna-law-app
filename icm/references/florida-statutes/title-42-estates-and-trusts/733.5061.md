# Fla. Stat. § 733.5061 — Appointment of successor upon removal.

**Title:** XLII — ESTATES AND TRUSTS
**Chapter:** 733 — PROBATE CODE: ADMINISTRATION OF ESTATES
**Part:** V — CURATORS; RESIGNATION AND REMOVAL OF PERSONAL REPRESENTATIVES
**Section:** 733.5061
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

When a personal representative is removed, the court shall appoint a personal representative or shall appoint a curator to serve until a successor personal representative is appointed.

**History:** s. 120, ch. 2001-226.
