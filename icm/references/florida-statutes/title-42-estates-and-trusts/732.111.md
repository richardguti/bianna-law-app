# Fla. Stat. § 732.111 — Dower and curtesy abolished.

**Title:** XLII — ESTATES AND TRUSTS
**Chapter:** 732 — PROBATE CODE: INTESTATE SUCCESSION AND WILLS
**Part:** I — INTESTATE SUCCESSION
**Section:** 732.111
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Dower and curtesy are abolished.
