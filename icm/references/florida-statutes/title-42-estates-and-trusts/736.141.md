# Fla. Stat. § 736.141 — Duty to provide information.

**Title:** XLII — ESTATES AND TRUSTS
**Chapter:** 736 — FLORIDA TRUST CODE
**Part:** XIV — FLORIDA UNIFORM DIRECTED TRUST ACT
**Section:** 736.141
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
Subject to s. 736.1411, relating to limitations on the duties of trustees or trust directors to monitor, inform, or advise on matters involving the other, a trustee shall provide information to a trust director to the extent the information is reasonably related to the powers or duties of the trust director.
