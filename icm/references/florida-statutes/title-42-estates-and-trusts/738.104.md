# Fla. Stat. § 738.104 — Governing law.

**Title:** XLII — ESTATES AND TRUSTS
**Chapter:** 738 — UNIFORM FIDUCIARY INCOME AND PRINCIPAL ACT
**Part:** XV — COMMUNITY PROPERTY TRUST ACT
**Section:** 738.104
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Except as otherwise provided in the terms of a trust or this chapter, this chapter applies when this state is the principal place of administration of a trust or estate or the situs of property that is not held in a trust or estate and is subject to a life estate or other term interest described in s. 738.103(2). By accepting the trusteeship of a trust having its principal place of administration in this state or by moving the principal place of administration of a trust to this state, the trustee submits to the application of this chapter to any matter within the scope of this chapter involving the trust.
