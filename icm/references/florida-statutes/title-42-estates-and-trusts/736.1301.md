# Fla. Stat. § 736.1301 — Electronic records and signatures.

**Title:** XLII — ESTATES AND TRUSTS
**Chapter:** 736 — FLORIDA TRUST CODE
**Part:** XII — CHARITABLE TRUSTS
**Section:** 736.1301
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Any provisions of this code governing the legal effect, validity, or enforceability of electronic records or electronic signatures, and of contracts formed or performed with the use of such records or signatures, are deemed to conform to the requirements of s. 102 of the Electronic Signatures in Global and National Commerce Act, 15 U.S.C. s. 7002, and supersede, modify, and limit the requirements of the Electronic Signatures in Global and National Commerce Act.

**History:** s. 13, ch. 2006-217.
