# Fla. Stat. § 739.501 — Tax-qualified disclaimer.

**Title:** XLII — ESTATES AND TRUSTS
**Chapter:** 739 — FLORIDA UNIFORM DISCLAIMER OF PROPERTY INTERESTS ACT
**Part:** XV — COMMUNITY PROPERTY TRUST ACT
**Section:** 739.501
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Notwithstanding any provision of this chapter other than s. 739.402, if, as a result of a disclaimer or transfer, the disclaimed or transferred interest is treated pursuant to the provisions of s. 2518 of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 as never having been transferred to the disclaimant, the disclaimer or transfer is effective as a disclaimer under this chapter.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 2005-108; s. 18, ch. 2009-115.
