# Fla. Stat. § 732.503 — Self-proof of will.

**Title:** XLII — ESTATES AND TRUSTS
**Chapter:** 732 — PROBATE CODE: INTESTATE SUCCESSION AND WILLS

(2)  
A will or codicil made self-proved under former law, or executed in another state and made self-proved under the laws of that state, shall be considered as self-proved under this section.

**Note:** 732.505  
Revocation by writing.
—

A will or codicil, or any part of either, is revoked:
(1)  
By a subsequent inconsistent will or codicil, even though the subsequent inconsistent will or codicil does not expressly revoke all previous wills or codicils, but the revocation extends only so far as the inconsistency.
