# Fla. Stat. § 731.1035 — Applicable rules of evidence.

**Title:** XLII — ESTATES AND TRUSTS
**Chapter:** 731 — PROBATE CODE: GENERAL PROVISIONS
**Part:** I — SHORT TITLE; CONSTRUCTION
**Section:** 731.1035
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

In proceedings under this code, the rules of evidence in civil actions are applicable unless specifically changed by the code.

**History:** s. 28, ch. 2006-217.
