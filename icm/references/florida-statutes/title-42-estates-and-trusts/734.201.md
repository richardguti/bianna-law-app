# Fla. Stat. § 734.201 — Jurisdiction by act of foreign personal representative.

**Title:** XLII — ESTATES AND TRUSTS
**Chapter:** 734 — PROBATE CODE: FOREIGN PERSONAL REPRESENTATIVES; ANCILLARY ADMINISTRATION
**Part:** I — GENERAL PROVISIONS
**Section:** 734.201
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

A foreign personal representative submits personally to the jurisdiction of the courts of this state in any proceeding concerning the estate by:
(1)  
Filing authenticated copies of the domiciliary proceedings under s. 734.104;

(2)  
Receiving payment of money or taking delivery of personal property, under s. 734.101; or

(3)  
Doing any act as a personal representative in this state that would have given the state jurisdiction over that person as an individual.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 74-106; s. 99, ch. 75-220; s. 1031, ch. 97-102; s. 174, ch. 2001-226.
