# Fla. Stat. § 733.704 — Amendment of claims.

**Title:** XLII — ESTATES AND TRUSTS
**Chapter:** 733 — PROBATE CODE: ADMINISTRATION OF ESTATES
**Part:** VII — CREDITORS’ CLAIMS
**Section:** 733.704
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

If a bona fide attempt to file a claim is made but the claim is defective as to form, the court may permit the amendment of the claim at any time.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 74-106; s. 1, ch. 77-174; s. 148, ch. 2001-226.

**Note:** Created from former s. 733.17.
