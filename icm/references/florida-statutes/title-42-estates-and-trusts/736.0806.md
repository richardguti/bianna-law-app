# Fla. Stat. § 736.0806 — Trustee's skills.

**Title:** XLII — ESTATES AND TRUSTS
**Chapter:** 736 — FLORIDA TRUST CODE
**Part:** VIII — DUTIES AND POWERS OF TRUSTEE
**Section:** 736.0806
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

A trustee who has special skills or expertise, or is named trustee in reliance on the trustee's representation that the trustee has special skills or expertise, shall use those special skills or expertise.

**History:** s. 8, ch. 2006-217.
