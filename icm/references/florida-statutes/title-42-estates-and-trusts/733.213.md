# Fla. Stat. § 733.213 — Probate as prerequisite to judicial construction of will.

**Title:** XLII — ESTATES AND TRUSTS
**Chapter:** 733 — PROBATE CODE: ADMINISTRATION OF ESTATES
**Part:** III — PREFERENCE IN APPOINTMENT AND QUALIFICATIONS OF PERSONAL REPRESENTATIVE
**Section:** 733.213
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

A will may not be construed until it has been admitted to probate.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 74-106; s. 61, ch. 75-220; s. 97, ch. 2001-226.

**Note:** Created from former s. 732.42.
