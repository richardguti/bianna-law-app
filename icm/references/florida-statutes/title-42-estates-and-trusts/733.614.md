# Fla. Stat. § 733.614 — Powers and duties of successor personal representative.

**Title:** XLII — ESTATES AND TRUSTS
**Chapter:** 733 — PROBATE CODE: ADMINISTRATION OF ESTATES
**Part:** VI — DUTIES AND POWERS OF PERSONAL REPRESENTATIVE
**Section:** 733.614
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

A successor personal representative has the same power and duty as the original personal representative to complete the administration and distribution of the estate as expeditiously as possible, but shall not exercise any power made personal to the personal representative named in the will without court approval.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 74-106; s. 78, ch. 75-220; s. 1012, ch. 97-102; s. 138, ch. 2001-226.

**Note:** Created from former s. 734.10.
