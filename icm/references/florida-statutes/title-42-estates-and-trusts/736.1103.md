# Fla. Stat. § 736.1103 — Gifts to multigeneration classes to be per stirpes.

**Title:** XLII — ESTATES AND TRUSTS
**Chapter:** 736 — FLORIDA TRUST CODE
**Part:** XI — RULES OF CONSTRUCTION
**Section:** 736.1103
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Class gifts to descendants, issue, and other multigeneration classes shall be per stirpes.

**History:** s. 11, ch. 2006-217; s. 17, ch. 2010-132.
