# Fla. Stat. § 733.406 — Bond premium allowable as expense of administration.

**Title:** XLII — ESTATES AND TRUSTS
**Chapter:** 733 — PROBATE CODE: ADMINISTRATION OF ESTATES
**Part:** V — CURATORS; RESIGNATION AND REMOVAL OF PERSONAL REPRESENTATIVES
**Section:** 733.406
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

A personal representative or other fiduciary required to give bond shall pay the reasonable premium as an expense of administration.

**History:** s. 613, ch. 59-205; s. 3, ch. 76-168; s. 1, ch. 77-457; ss. 2, 3, ch. 81-318; ss. 253, 566, ch. 82-243; s. 994, ch. 97-102; s. 111, ch. 2001-226.

**Note:** Former s. 627.753.
