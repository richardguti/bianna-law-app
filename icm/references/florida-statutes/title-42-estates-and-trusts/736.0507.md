# Fla. Stat. § 736.0507 — Personal obligations of trustee.

**Title:** XLII — ESTATES AND TRUSTS
**Chapter:** 736 — FLORIDA TRUST CODE
**Part:** V — CREDITORS’ CLAIMS; SPENDTHRIFT AND DISCRETIONARY TRUSTS
**Section:** 736.0507
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Except to the extent of the trustee's interest in the trust other than as a trustee, trust property is not subject to personal obligations of the trustee, even if the trustee becomes insolvent or bankrupt.
