# Fla. Stat. § 736.1009 — Reliance on trust instrument.

**Title:** XLII — ESTATES AND TRUSTS
**Chapter:** 736 — FLORIDA TRUST CODE
**Part:** X — LIABILITY OF TRUSTEE AND RIGHTS OF PERSONS DEALING WITH TRUSTEE
**Section:** 736.1009
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

A trustee who acts in reasonable reliance on the terms of the trust as expressed in the trust instrument is not liable to a beneficiary for a breach of trust to the extent the breach resulted from the reliance.

**History:** s. 10, ch. 2006-217.
