# Fla. Stat. § 731.1055 — Disposition of real property.

**Title:** XLII — ESTATES AND TRUSTS
**Chapter:** 731 — PROBATE CODE: GENERAL PROVISIONS
**Part:** I — SHORT TITLE; CONSTRUCTION
**Section:** 731.1055
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The validity and effect of a disposition, whether intestate or testate, of real property in this state shall be determined by Florida law.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 2016-189.
