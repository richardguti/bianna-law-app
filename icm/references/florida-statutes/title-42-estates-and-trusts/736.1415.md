# Fla. Stat. § 736.1415 — Jurisdiction over trust director.

**Title:** XLII — ESTATES AND TRUSTS
**Chapter:** 736 — FLORIDA TRUST CODE
**Part:** XIV — FLORIDA UNIFORM DIRECTED TRUST ACT
**Section:** 736.1415
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
By accepting appointment as a trust director of a trust subject to this part, the trust director submits to the personal jurisdiction of the courts of the state regarding any matter related to a power or duty of the trust director.

(2)  
This section does not preclude other methods of obtaining jurisdiction over a trust director.

**History:** s. 26, ch. 2021-183.
