# Fla. Stat. § 732.226 — Limitations on testamentary disposition.

**Title:** XLII — ESTATES AND TRUSTS
**Chapter:** 732 — PROBATE CODE: INTESTATE SUCCESSION AND WILLS
**Part:** II — ELECTIVE SHARE OF SURVIVING SPOUSE; RIGHTS IN COMMUNITY PROPERTY
**Section:** 732.226
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Sections 732.216-732.228 do not authorize a person to dispose of property by will if it is held under limitations imposed by law preventing testamentary disposition by that person.

**History:** s. 13, ch. 92-200.
