# Fla. Stat. § 736.0102 — Scope.

**Title:** XLII — ESTATES AND TRUSTS
**Chapter:** 736 — FLORIDA TRUST CODE
**Part:** I — GENERAL PROVISIONS AND DEFINITIONS
**Section:** 736.0102
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
Except as otherwise provided in this section, this code applies to express trusts, charitable or noncharitable, and trusts created pursuant to a law, judgment, or decree that requires the trust to be administered in the manner of an express trust.

(2)
