# Fla. Stat. § 731.103 — Evidence as to death or status.

**Title:** XLII — ESTATES AND TRUSTS
**Chapter:** 731 — PROBATE CODE: GENERAL PROVISIONS
**Part:** I — SHORT TITLE; CONSTRUCTION
**Section:** 731.103
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

In proceedings under this code and under chapter 736, the following additional rules relating to determination of death and status are applicable:
(1)  
An authenticated copy of a death certificate issued by an official or agency of the place where the death purportedly occurred is prima facie proof of the fact, place, date, and time of death and the identity of the decedent.

(2)  
A copy of any record or report of a governmental agency, domestic or foreign, that a person is alive, missing, detained, or, from the facts related, presumed dead is prima facie evidence F.S. 731.1026731.102
