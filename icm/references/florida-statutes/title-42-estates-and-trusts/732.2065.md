# Fla. Stat. § 732.2065 — Amount of the elective share.

**Title:** XLII — ESTATES AND TRUSTS
**Chapter:** 732 — PROBATE CODE: INTESTATE SUCCESSION AND WILLS
**Part:** II — ELECTIVE SHARE OF SURVIVING SPOUSE; RIGHTS IN COMMUNITY PROPERTY
**Section:** 732.2065
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The elective share is an amount equal to 30 percent of the elective estate.

**History:** s. 15, ch. 75-220; s. 1, ch. 81-27; s. 6, ch. 99-343.

**Note:** Former s. 732.207.
