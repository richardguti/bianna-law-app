# Fla. Stat. § 740.07 — Relation to Electronic Signatures in Global and National Commerce Act.

**Title:** XLII — ESTATES AND TRUSTS
**Chapter:** 740 — FIDUCIARY ACCESS TO DIGITAL ASSETS
**Part:** XV — COMMUNITY PROPERTY TRUST ACT
**Section:** 740.07
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

This chapter modifies, limits, and supersedes the Electronic Signatures in Global and National Commerce Act, 15 U.S.C. ss. 7001 et seq., but does not modify, limit, or supersede s. 101(c) of that act, 15 U.S.C. s. 7001(c), or authorize electronic delivery of any of the notices described in s. 103(b) of that act, 15 U.S.C. s. 7003(b).

**History:** s. 17, ch. 2016-46.
