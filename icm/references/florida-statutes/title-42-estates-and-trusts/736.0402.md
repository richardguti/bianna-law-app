# Fla. Stat. § 736.0402 — Requirements for creation.

**Title:** XLII — ESTATES AND TRUSTS
**Chapter:** 736 — FLORIDA TRUST CODE
**Part:** IV — CREATION, VALIDITY, MODIFICATION, AND TERMINATION
**Section:** 736.0402
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
A trust is created only if:
(a)  
The settlor has capacity to create a trust.

(b)  
The settlor indicates an intent to create the trust.

(c)  
The trust has a definite beneficiary or is:
1.  
A charitable trust;

2.  
A trust for the care of an animal, as provided in s. 736.0408; or

3.  
A trust for a noncharitable purpose, as provided in s. 736.0409.

(d)  
The trustee has duties to perform.

736.0401  
Methods of creating trust.
——

A trust may be created by:
(1)  
Transfer of property to another person as trustee during the settlor's lifetime or by will or other disposition taking effect on the settlor's death;

(2)  
Declaration by the owner of property that the owner holds identifiable property as trustee; or

(3)  
Exercise of a power of appointment in favor of a trustee.

**History:** s. 4, ch. 2006-217.
