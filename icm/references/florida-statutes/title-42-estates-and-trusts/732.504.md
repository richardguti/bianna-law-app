# Fla. Stat. § 732.504 — Who may witness.

**Title:** XLII — ESTATES AND TRUSTS
**Chapter:** 732 — PROBATE CODE: INTESTATE SUCCESSION AND WILLS
**Part:** V — WILLS
**Section:** 732.504
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
Any person competent to be a witness may act as a witness to a will.

(2)  
A will or codicil, or any part of either, is not invalid because the will or codicil is signed by an interested witness.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 74-106; s. 22, ch. 75-220; s. 1, ch. 77-174; s. 268, ch. 79-400.
