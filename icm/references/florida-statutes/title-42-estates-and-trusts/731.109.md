# Fla. Stat. § 731.109 — Seal of the court.

**Title:** XLII — ESTATES AND TRUSTS
**Chapter:** 731 — PROBATE CODE: GENERAL PROVISIONS
**Part:** I — SHORT TITLE; CONSTRUCTION
**Section:** 731.109
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

For the purposes of this code, the seal of the clerk of the circuit court is the seal of the court.
