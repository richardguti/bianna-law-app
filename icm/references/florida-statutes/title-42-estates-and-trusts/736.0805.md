# Fla. Stat. § 736.0805 — Expenses of administration.

**Title:** XLII — ESTATES AND TRUSTS
**Chapter:** 736 — FLORIDA TRUST CODE
**Part:** VIII — DUTIES AND POWERS OF TRUSTEE
**Section:** 736.0805
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

In administering a trust, the trustee shall only incur expenses that are reasonable in relation to the trust property, the purposes of the trust, and the skills of the trustee.

**History:** s. 8, ch. 2006-217.
