# Fla. Stat. § 733.209 — Estates of missing persons.

**Title:** XLII — ESTATES AND TRUSTS
**Chapter:** 733 — PROBATE CODE: ADMINISTRATION OF ESTATES
**Part:** II — COMMENCING ADMINISTRATION
**Section:** 733.209
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Any interested person may petition to administer the estate of a missing person; however, no personal representative shall be appointed until the court determines the missing person is dead.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 74-106; s. 93, ch. 2001-226.

**Note:** Created from former s. 732.53.
