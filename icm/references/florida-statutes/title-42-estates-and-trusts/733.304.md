# Fla. Stat. § 733.304 — Nonresidents.

**Title:** XLII — ESTATES AND TRUSTS
**Chapter:** 733 — PROBATE CODE: ADMINISTRATION OF ESTATES
**Part:** III — PREFERENCE IN APPOINTMENT AND QUALIFICATIONS OF PERSONAL REPRESENTATIVE
**Section:** 733.304
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

A person who is not domiciled in the state cannot qualify as personal representative unless the person is:
(1)  
A legally adopted child or adoptive parent of the decedent;

(2)  
Related by lineal consanguinity to the decedent;

(3)  
A spouse or a brother, sister, uncle, aunt, nephew, or niece of the decedent, or someone related by lineal consanguinity to any such person; or

(4)  
The spouse of a person otherwise qualified under this section.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 74-106; s. 63, ch. 75-220; s. 6, ch. 79-343.

**Note:** Created from former s. 732.47.
