# Fla. Stat. § 733.701 — Notifying creditors.

**Title:** XLII — ESTATES AND TRUSTS
**Chapter:** 733 — PROBATE CODE: ADMINISTRATION OF ESTATES
**Part:** VII — CREDITORS’ CLAIMS
**Section:** 733.701
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Unless creditors' claims are otherwise barred by s. 733.710, every personal representative shall cause notice to creditors to be published and served under s. 733.2121.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 74-106; s. 83, ch. 75-220; s. 33, ch. 77-87; s. 4, ch. 89-340; s. 145, ch. 2001-226; s. 31, ch. 2003-154.

**Note:** Created from former s. 733.15.
