# Fla. Stat. § 733.505 — Jurisdiction in removal proceedings.

**Title:** XLII — ESTATES AND TRUSTS
**Chapter:** 733 — PROBATE CODE: ADMINISTRATION OF ESTATES
**Part:** V — CURATORS; RESIGNATION AND REMOVAL OF PERSONAL REPRESENTATIVES
**Section:** 733.505
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

A petition for removal shall be filed in the court having jurisdiction of the administration.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 74-106; s. 118, ch. <a hr

**Note:** Created from former s. 734.11.
