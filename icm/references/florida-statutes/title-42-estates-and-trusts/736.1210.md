# Fla. Stat. § 736.1210 — Interpretation.

**Title:** XLII — ESTATES AND TRUSTS
**Chapter:** 736 — FLORIDA TRUST CODE
**Part:** XII — CHARITABLE TRUSTS
**Section:** 736.1210
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

This part shall be interpreted to effectuate the intent of the state to preserve, foster, and encourage gifts to, or for the benefit of, charitable organizations.

**History:** s. 12, ch. 2006-217.
