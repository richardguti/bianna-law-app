# Fla. Stat. § 736.0417 — Combination and division of trusts.

**Title:** XLII — ESTATES AND TRUSTS
**Chapter:** 736 — FLORIDA TRUST CODE
**Part:** IV — CREATION, VALIDITY, MODIFICATION, AND TERMINATION
**Section:** 736.0417
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
After notice to the qualified beneficiaries, a trustee may combine two or more trusts into a single trust or divide a trust into two or more separate trusts, if the result does not impair rights of any beneficiary or adversely affect achievement of the purposes of the trusts or trust, respectively.

Modification to achieve settlor's tax objectives.
——

Upon application of any interested person, to achieve the settlor's tax objectives the court may modify the terms of a trust in a manner that is not contrary to the settlor's probable intent. The court may provide that the modification has retroactive effect.
