# Fla. Stat. § 736.0203 — Subject matter jurisdiction.

**Title:** XLII — ESTATES AND TRUSTS
**Chapter:** 736 — FLORIDA TRUST CODE
**Part:** II — JUDICIAL PROCEEDINGS
**Section:** 736.0203
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The circuit court has original jurisdiction in this state of all proceedings arising under this code.

**History:** s. 2, / upon any person may be made as provided in chapter 48.
