# Fla. Stat. § 732.227 — Homestead defined.

**Title:** XLII — ESTATES AND TRUSTS
**Chapter:** 732 — PROBATE CODE: INTESTATE SUCCESSION AND WILLS
**Part:** II — ELECTIVE SHARE OF SURVIVING SPOUSE; RIGHTS IN COMMUNITY PROPERTY
**Section:** 732.227
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

For purposes of ss. 732.216-732.228, the term “homestead” refers only to property the descent and devise of which is restricted by s. 4(c), Art. X of the State Constitution.

**History:** s. 14, ch. 92-200.
