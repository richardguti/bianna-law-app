# Fla. Stat. § 328.25 — Supplemental principles of law and equity.

**Title:** XXIV — VESSELS
**Chapter:** 328 — VESSELS: TITLE CERTIFICATES; LIENS; REGISTRATION
**Part:** I — UNIFORM CERTIFICATE OF TITLE FOR VESSELS ACT
**Section:** 328.25
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Unless displaced by a provision of this part, the principles of law and equity supplement its provisions.

**History:** s. 26, ch. 2019-76.
