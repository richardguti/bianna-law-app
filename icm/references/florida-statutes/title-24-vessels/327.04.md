# Fla. Stat. § 327.04 — Rules.

**Title:** XXIV — VESSELS
**Chapter:** 327 — VESSEL SAFETY
**Part:** III — VETERANS’ ADULT DAY HEALTH CARE OF FLORIDA ACT
**Section:** 327.04
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The commission may adopt rules pursuant to ss. 120.536(1) and 120.54 to implement this chapter, the provisions of chapter 705 relating to vessels, and s. 823.11 conferring powers or duties upon it.
