# Fla. Stat. § 327.43 — Silver Glen Run and Silver Glen Springs; navigation channel.

**Title:** XXIV — VESSELS
**Chapter:** 327 — VESSEL SAFETY
**Part:** III — VETERANS’ ADULT DAY HEALTH CARE OF FLORIDA ACT
**Section:** 327.43
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The Fish and Wildlife Conservation Commission is directed to mark a navigation channel within Silver Glen Run and Silver Glen Springs, located on the western shore of Lake George on the St. Johns River.

**History:** ss. 1, 2, 3, 4, ch. 94-341; s. 26, ch. 99-245; s. 3, ch. 2003-143.
