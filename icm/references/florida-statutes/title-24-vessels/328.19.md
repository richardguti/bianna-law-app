# Fla. Stat. § 328.19 — Penalty.

**Title:** XXIV — VESSELS
**Chapter:** 328 — VESSELS: TITLE CERTIFICATES; LIENS; REGISTRATION
**Part:** I — UNIFORM CERTIFICATE OF TITLE FOR VESSELS ACT
**Section:** 328.19
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Except as otherwise provided in this chapter, any person convicted of violating any of the provisions of this chapter is guilty of a misdemeanor of the second degree, punishable as provided in s. 775.082 or s. 775.083.

**History:** s. 4, ch. 67-586; s. 305, ch. 71-136; s. 8, ch. 80-266; s. 50, ch. 91-224.

**Note:** Former s. 371.82.
