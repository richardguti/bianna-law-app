# Fla. Stat. § 327.804 — Compilation of statistics on boating accidents and violations.

**Title:** XXIV — VESSELS
**Chapter:** 328 — VESSELS: TITLE CERTIFICATES; LIENS; REGISTRATION
**Part:** III — VETERANS’ ADULT DAY HEALTH CARE OF FLORIDA ACT
**Section:** 327.804
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The Fish and Wildlife Conservation Commission shall compile statistics on boating accidents and boating violations of the age groups of persons affected by chapter 96-187, Laws of Florida.

**History:** s. 5, ch. 96-187; s. 35, ch. 99-245.
