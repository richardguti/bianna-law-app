# Fla. Stat. § 327.74 — Uniform boating citations.

**Title:** XXIV — VESSELS

(1)  
The commission shall prepare and supply to every law enforcement agency and code enforcement officer authorized under s. 327.4107(5) or s. 327.4111(5) in this state that enforce the laws of this state regulating the operation of vessels, an appropriate form boating citation containing a notice to appear, which must be issued in prenumbered books with citations in quintuplicate and meet the requirements of this chapter or any laws of this state regulating boating. The form must be consistent with the state's county court rules and the procedures established by the commission.

(2)  
Courts, enforcement agencies, and the commission are jointly responsible to account for all uniform boating citations in accordance with the procedures promulgated by the commission.

(3)  
Every law enforcement officer and code enforcement officer authorized under s. 327.4107(5) or s. 327.4111(5), upon issuing a boating citation to an alleged violator of any provision of the boating laws of this state or any boating ordinance of any municipality, shall deposit the original and one copy of such boating citation with a court having jurisdiction over the alleged offense or with its traffic violations bureau within 5 days after issuance to the violator.
