# Fla. Stat. § 326.003 — Administration.

**Title:** XXIV — VESSELS
**Chapter:** 326 — YACHT AND SHIP BROKERS
**Part:** III — VETERANS’ ADULT DAY HEALTH CARE OF FLORIDA ACT
**Section:** 326.003
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The division shall:
(1)  
Administer ss. 326.001-326.006 and collect fees sufficient to administer ss. 326.001-326.006.

(2)  
Adopt rules pursuant to ss. 120.536(1) and 120.54 to implement ss. 326.001-326.006 and to classify brokers and salespersons and regulate their activities.

(3)  
Enforce the provisions of ss. 326.001-326.006 against any person who operates as a broker or salesperson without a license.

**History:** ss. 3, 7, ch. 88-282; s. 3, ch. 89-128; s. 4, ch. 91-429; s. 1, ch. 93-55; s. 446, ch. 95-148; s. 66, ch. 98-200.
