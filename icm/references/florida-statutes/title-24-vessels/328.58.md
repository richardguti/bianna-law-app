# Fla. Stat. § 328.58 — Reciprocity of nonresident or alien vessels.

**Title:** XXIV — VESSELS
**Chapter:** 328 — VESSELS: TITLE CERTIFICATES; LIENS; REGISTRATION
**Part:** II — VESSEL REGISTRATION
**Section:** 328.58
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

——

The owner of any vessel already covered by a registration number in full force and effect which has been awarded by:
(1)  
Another state pursuant to a federally approved numbering system of another state;

(2)  
The United States Coast Guard in a state without a federally approved numbering system; or

(3)  
The United States Coast Guard for a federally documented vessel with a valid registration in full force and effect from another state,

shall record the number with the Department of Highway Safety and Motor Vehicles prior to operating, using, or storing the vessel on the waters of this state in excess of the 90-day reciprocity period provided for in this chapter. Such recordation shall be pursuant tos0.
