# Fla. Stat. § 327.32 — Vessel declared dangerous instrumentality; civil liability.

**Title:** XXIV — VESSELS
**Chapter:** 327 — VESSEL SAFETY
**Part:** III — VETERANS’ ADULT DAY HEALTH CARE OF FLORIDA ACT
**Section:** 327.32
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

All vessels, of whatever classification, shall be considered dangerous instrumentalities in this state, and any operator of a vessel shall, during any utilization of the vessel, exercise the highest degree of care in order to prevent injuries to others.
