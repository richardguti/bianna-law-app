# Fla. Stat. § 328.30 — Transactions by electronic or telephonic means.

**Title:** XXIV — VESSELS
**Chapter:** 328 — VESSELS: TITLE CERTIFICATES; LIENS; REGISTRATION
**Part:** I — UNIFORM CERTIFICATE OF TITLE FOR VESSELS ACT
**Section:** 328.30
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
The department may accept any application provided for under this chapter by electronic or telephonic means.

(2)  
The department may issue an electronic certificate of title in lieu of printing a paper title.

(3)  
The department may collect e-mail addresses and use e-mail in lieu of the United States Postal Service as a method of notification.

**History:** s. 25, ch. 97-300; s. 69, ch. 2012-181; s. 44, ch. 2026-39.
