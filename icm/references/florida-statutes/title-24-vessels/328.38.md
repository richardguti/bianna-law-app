# Fla. Stat. § 328.38 — Applicability.

**Title:** XXIV — VESSELS
**Chapter:** 328 — VESSELS: TITLE CERTIFICATES; LIENS; REGISTRATION
**Part:** I — UNIFORM CERTIFICATE OF TITLE FOR VESSELS ACT
**Section:** 328.38
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Subject to s. 328.37, this act applies to any transaction, certificate of title, or record relating to a vessel, even if the transaction, certificate of title, or record was entered into or created before the effective date of this act.

**History:** s. 32, ch. 2019-76.
