# Fla. Stat. § 327.42 — Mooring to or damaging of uniform waterway markers prohibited.

**Title:** XXIV — VESSELS
**Chapter:** 327 — VESSEL SAFETY
**Part:** III — VETERANS’ ADULT DAY HEALTH CARE OF FLORIDA ACT
**Section:** 327.42
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

——

(1)  
No person shall moor or fasten a vessel to a lawfully placed uniform waterway marker, except in case of emergency or with the written consent of the marker's owner.

(2)  
(d)  
Vessels engaged in recreational fishing if the persons onboard are actively tending hook and line fishing gear or nets.
