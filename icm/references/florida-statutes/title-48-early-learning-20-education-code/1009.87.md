# Fla. Stat. § 1009.87 — Provisions of ss. 1009.78-1009.88 cumulative.

**Title:** XLVIII — EARLY LEARNING-20 EDUCATION CODE
**Chapter:** 1009 — EDUCATIONAL SCHOLARSHIPS, FEES, AND FINANCIAL ASSISTANCE
**Part:** II — POSTSECONDARY STUDENT FEES
**Section:** 1009.87
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The provisions of ss. 1009.78-1009.88 shall be in addition to the other provisions of this chapter and shall not be construed to be in derogation thereof, except as otherwise expressly provided hereby.

**History:** s. 469, ch. 2002-387.
