# Fla. Stat. § 1000.40 — Future repeal of the Interstate Compact on Educational Opportunity for Military Children.

**Title:** XLVIII — EARLY LEARNING-20 EDUCATION CODE
**Chapter:** 1001 — EARLY LEARNING-20 GOVERNANCE
**Part:** III — EDUCATIONAL COMPACTS
**Section:** 1000.40
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Sections 1000.36, 1000.361, 1000.38, and 1000.39 and this section shall stand repealed on July 1, 2028, unless reviewed and saved from repeal through reenactment by the Legislature.

**History:** s. 2, ch. 2016-34; s. 1, ch. 2019-7; s. 2, ch. 2022-182; s. 9, ch. 2025-110.
