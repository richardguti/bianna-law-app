# Fla. Stat. § 1011.43 — Investment of university agency and activity funds; earnings used for scholarships.

**Title:** XLVIII — EARLY LEARNING-20 EDUCATION CODE
**Chapter:** 1011 — PLANNING AND BUDGETING
**Part:** I — PREPARATION, ADOPTION, AND IMPLEMENTATION OF BUDGETS
**Section:** 1011.43
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Each university is authorized to invest available agency and activity funds and to use the earnings from such investments for student scholarships and loans. The university board of trustees shall provide procedures for the administration of these scholarships and loans by regulations.

**History:** s. 639, ch. 2002-387; s. 25, ch. 2010-78.
