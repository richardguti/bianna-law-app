# Fla. Stat. § 1009.82 — Terms of loans.

**Title:** XLVIII — EARLY LEARNING-20 EDUCATION CODE
**Chapter:** 1009 — EDUCATIONAL SCHOLARSHIPS, FEES, AND FINANCIAL ASSISTANCE
**Part:** II — POSTSECONDARY STUDENT FEES
**Section:** 1009.82
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The term of all authorized loans shall be fixed by rules adopted by the state board and the loan agreements to be entered into with the student borrowers.

**History:** s. 464, ch. 2002-387.
