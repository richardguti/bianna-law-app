# Fla. Stat. § 1001.462 — Oath of district school superintendent.

**Title:** XLVIII — EARLY LEARNING-20 EDUCATION CODE
**Chapter:** 1001 — EARLY LEARNING-20 GOVERNANCE
**Part:** II — SCHOOL DISTRICT GOVERNANCE
**Section:** 1001.462
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Before entering upon the duties of his or her office, the district school superintendent shall take the oath of office prescribed by the State Constitution.

**History:** s. 64, ch. 2002-387.
