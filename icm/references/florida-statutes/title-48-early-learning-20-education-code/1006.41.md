# Fla. Stat. § 1006.41 — Disposal of instructional materials.

**Title:** XLVIII — EARLY LEARNING-20 EDUCATION CODE
**Chapter:** 1006 — SUPPORT FOR LEARNING
**Part:** I — PUBLIC K-12 EDUCATION SUPPORT FOR LEARNING AND STUDENT SERVICES
**Section:** 1006.41
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

——

(1)  
Instructional materials that have become unserviceable or surplus or are no longer on state contract may be disposed of, under adopted rule of the district school board, by:
(a)  
Giving or lending the materials to other public education programs within the district or state, to the teachers to use in developing supplementary teaching materials, to students or others, or to any charitable organization, governmental agency, home education students, private school, or state.

(b)  
Selling the materials to used book dealers, recycling plants, pulp mills, or other persons, firms, or corporations upon such terms as are most economically advantageous to the district school board.
