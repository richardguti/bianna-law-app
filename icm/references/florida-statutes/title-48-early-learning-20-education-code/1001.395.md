# Fla. Stat. § 1001.395 — District school board members; compensation.

**Title:** XLVIII — EARLY LEARNING-20 EDUCATION CODE
**Chapter:** 1001 — EARLY LEARNING-20 GOVERNANCE
**Part:** II — SCHOOL DISTRICT GOVERNANCE
**Section:** 1001.395
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

——

An individual member of a district school board may, on any day and at any time at his or her pleasure, visit any district school in his or her school district. An individual charter school governing board member may, on any day and at any time at his or her pleasure, visit any charter school governed by the charter school's governing board. A member of the Legislature may visit any public school in the legislative district of the member.
