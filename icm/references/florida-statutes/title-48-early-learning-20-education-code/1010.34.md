# Fla. Stat. § 1010.34 — Audits of direct-support organizations.

**Title:** XLVIII — EARLY LEARNING-20 EDUCATION CODE
**Chapter:** 1010 — FINANCIAL MATTERS
**Part:** III — AUDIT REQUIREMENTS AND PROCEDURES
**Section:** 1010.34
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Audits of school district, Florida College System institution, and state university direct-support organizations are subject to the audit provisions of ss. 1001.453(4), 1004.28(5), and 1004.70(6), as applicable.

**History:** s. 547, ch. 2002-387; s. 162, ch. 2011-5.
