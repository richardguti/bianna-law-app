# Fla. Stat. § 1012.992 — Copies of contracts with other states; depository.

**Title:** XLVIII — EARLY LEARNING-20 EDUCATION CODE
**Chapter:** 1012 — PERSONNEL
**Part:** VI — INTERSTATE COMPACT ON QUALIFICATIONS OF EDUCATIONAL PERSONNEL
**Section:** 1012.992
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Two copies of all contracts made on behalf of this state pursuant to the agreement set forth in Article IX shall be kept on file in the office of the Commissioner of Education and in the office of the Department of State. The Department of Education shall publish all such contracts in convenient form.

**History:** s. 794, ch. 2002-387.
