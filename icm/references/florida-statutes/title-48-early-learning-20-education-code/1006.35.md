# Fla. Stat. § 1006.35 — Accuracy of instructional materials.

**Title:** XLVIII — EARLY LEARNING-20 EDUCATION CODE
**Chapter:** 1006 — SUPPORT FOR LEARNING
**Part:** I — PUBLIC K-12 EDUCATION SUPPORT FOR LEARNING AND STUDENT SERVICES
**Section:** 1006.35
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
In addition to relying on statements of publishers or manufacturers of instructional materials, the commissioner may conduct or cause to be conducted an independent investigation to determine the accuracy of state-adopted instructional materials.

(2)  
When errors in state-adopted materials are confirmed, the publisher of the materials shall provide to each district school board that has purchased the materials the corrections in a format approved by the department.

(3)  
The commissioner may remove materials from the list of state-adopted materials if he or she finds that the content is in error and the publisher refuses to correct the error when notified by the department.
