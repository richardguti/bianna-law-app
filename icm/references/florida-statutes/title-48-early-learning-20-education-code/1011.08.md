# Fla. Stat. § 1011.08 — Expenditures between July 1 and date budget becomes official.

**Title:** XLVIII — EARLY LEARNING-20 EDUCATION CODE
**Chapter:** 1011 — PLANNING AND BUDGETING
**Part:** I — PREPARATION, ADOPTION, AND IMPLEMENTATION OF BUDGETS
**Section:** 1011.08
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

During the period from July 1 to the date the tentative budget becomes official, district school boards are authorized to approve ordinary expenditures, including salary payments, which are necessary for the approved school program.

**History:** s. 611, ch. 2002-387.
