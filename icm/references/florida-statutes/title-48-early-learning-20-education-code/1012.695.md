# Fla. Stat. § 1012.695 — Local civil service system laws not superseded.

**Title:** XLVIII — EARLY LEARNING-20 EDUCATION CODE
**Chapter:** 1012 — PERSONNEL
**Part:** III — PUBLIC SCHOOLS; PERSONNEL
**Section:** 1012.695
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Sections 1012.61, 1012.63, and 1012.66 shall not be construed to supersede or modify any local law establishing a civil service system covering employees of any school district.
