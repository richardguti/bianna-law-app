# Fla. Stat. § 1004.384 — University of Central Florida College of Medicine.

**Title:** XLVIII — EARLY LEARNING-20 EDUCATION CODE
**Chapter:** 1004 — PUBLIC POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION
**Part:** II — STATE UNIVERSITIES
**Section:** 1004.384
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

A college of medicine, as approved by the Board of Governors on March 23, 2006, is authorized at the University of Central Florida.

**History:** s. 7, ch. 2006-58.
