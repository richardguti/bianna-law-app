# Fla. Stat. § 1010.75 — Teacher Certification Examination Trust Fund.

**Title:** XLVIII — EARLY LEARNING-20 EDUCATION CODE
**Chapter:** 1010 — FINANCIAL MATTERS
**Part:** V — TRUST FUNDS
**Section:** 1010.75
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The proceeds for the certification examination fee levied pursuant to s. 1012.59 shall be remitted by the Department of Education to the Chief Financial Officer for deposit into and disbursed from the “Teacher Certification Examination Trust Fund” as re-created by chapter 99-28, Laws of Florida.

**History:** s. 587, ch. 2002-387; s. 1968, ch. 2003-261; s. 64, ch. 2004-41.
