# Fla. Stat. § 1013.76 — Multiyear capital improvement contracts.

**Title:** XLVIII — EARLY LEARNING-20 EDUCATION CODE
**Chapter:** 1013 — EDUCATIONAL FACILITIES
**Part:** IV — FUNDING FOR EDUCATIONAL FACILITIES
**Section:** 1013.76
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Any provision of chapters 1010 and 1011 to the contrary notwithstanding, school districts are authorized to award capital improvement contracts involving expenditures to be incurred for a period of more than 1 year on the basis of voter-authorized and unissued general obligation bonding authority, provided that sufficient funds are available to, and budgeted by, the school district to pay actual disbursements during any fiscal year.

**History:** s. 873, ch. 2002-387.
