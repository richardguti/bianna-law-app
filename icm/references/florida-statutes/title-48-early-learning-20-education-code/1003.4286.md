# Fla. Stat. § 1003.4286 — Award of standard high school diplomas to honorably discharged veterans.

**Title:** XLVIII — EARLY LEARNING-20 EDUCATION CODE
**Chapter:** 1003 — PUBLIC K-12 EDUCATION
**Part:** IV — PUBLIC K-12 EDUCATIONAL INSTRUCTION
**Section:** 1003.4286
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Pursuant to rules adopted by the State Board of Education in consultation with the Department of Military Affairs, the Commissioner of Education may award a standard high school diploma to an honorably discharged veteran who has not completed high school graduation requirements.

**History:** s. 19, ch. 2013-27.
