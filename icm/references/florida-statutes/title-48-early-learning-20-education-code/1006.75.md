# Fla. Stat. § 1006.75 — Student career services.

**Title:** XLVIII — EARLY LEARNING-20 EDUCATION CODE
**Chapter:** 1006 — SUPPORT FOR LEARNING
**Part:** II — PUBLIC POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION SUPPORT FOR LEARNING AND STUDENT SERVICES
**Section:** 1006.75
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

——

(1)  
Each career center, charter technical center, Florida College System institution, and state university shall ensure that their student career service centers and job placement resources prepare students for employment upon completion of their academic work.

(2)  
Student career service centers shall, to the extent possible, use state career planning resources to assist students with all of the following:
(a)  
Exploring and identifying car. 1, ch. 2020-28; s. 20, ch. 2021-35; ss. 1, 2, ch. 2021-217; s. 2, ch. 2023-4.

the Board of Governors shall provide oversight for successful delivery by the host entity of the services described in this section.
