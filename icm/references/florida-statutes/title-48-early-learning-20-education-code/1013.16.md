# Fla. Stat. § 1013.16 — Construction of facilities on leased property; conditions.

**Title:** XLVIII — EARLY LEARNING-20 EDUCATION CODE
**Chapter:** 1013 — EDUCATIONAL FACILITIES
**Part:** II — USE AND MANAGEMENT OF EDUCATIONAL FACILITIES
**Section:** 1013.16
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
A board may construct or place educational facilities and ancillary facilities on land that is owned by any person after the board has acquired from the owner of the land a long-term lease for the use of this land for a period of not less than the life expectancy of the permanent facilities constructed thereon.

(2)  
A board may enter into a short-term lease for the use of land owned by any person on which temporary or relocatable facilities are to be utilized.

(3)  
Leases executed by a university board of trustees pursuant to this section are subject to s. 1010.62.

**History:** s. 809, ch. 2002-387; s. 172, ch. 2007-217; s. 32, ch. 2024-159.
