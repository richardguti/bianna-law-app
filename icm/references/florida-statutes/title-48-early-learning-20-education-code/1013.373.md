# Fla. Stat. § 1013.373 — Educational facilities used for agricultural education.

**Title:** XLVIII — EARLY LEARNING-20 EDUCATION CODE
**Chapter:** 1013 — EDUCATIONAL FACILITIES
**Part:** III — PLANNING AND CONSTRUCTION OF EDUCATIONAL FACILITIES
**Section:** 1013.373
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

——

(1)  
Notwithstanding any other provision of law, a local government may not adopt any ordinance, regulation, rule, or policy to prohibit, restrict, regulate, or otherwise limit any activities of public educational facilities and auxiliary facilities constructed by a board for agricultural education, for Future Farmers of America or 4-H activities, or the storage of any animal or equipment therein.
