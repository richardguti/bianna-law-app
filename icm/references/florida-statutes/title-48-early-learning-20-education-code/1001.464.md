# Fla. Stat. § 1001.464 — District school superintendent to devote full time to office.

**Title:** XLVIII — EARLY LEARNING-20 EDUCATION CODE
**Chapter:** 1001 — EARLY LEARNING-20 GOVERNANCE
**Part:** II — SCHOOL DISTRICT GOVERNANCE
**Section:** 1001.464
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The position of district school superintendent shall be considered a full-time position.

**History:** s. 66, ch. 2002-387.
