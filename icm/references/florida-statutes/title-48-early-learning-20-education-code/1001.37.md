# Fla. Stat. § 1001.37 — District school board members shall qualify.

**Title:** XLVIII — EARLY LEARNING-20 EDUCATION CODE
**Chapter:** 1001 — EARLY LEARNING-20 GOVERNANCE
**Part:** II — SCHOOL DISTRICT GOVERNANCE
**Section:** 1001.37
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Before entering upon the duties of office after being elected, or, if appointed, within 10 days after receiving notice of appointment, each member of the district school board shall take the prescribed oath of office.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 2026-73.
