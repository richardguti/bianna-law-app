# Fla. Stat. § 1007.36 — Inclusive Transition and Employment Management Program.

**Title:** XLVIII — EARLY LEARNING-20 EDUCATION CODE
**Chapter:** 1007 — ARTICULATION AND ACCESS
**Part:** II — ARTICULATION
**Section:** 1007.36
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

——

As authorized by and consistent with funding appropriated in the General Appropriations Act, the Inclusive Transition and Employment Management Program is created within the Department of Education for the purpose of providing young adults with disabilities who are between the ages of 16 years and 28 years with transitional skills, education, and on-the-job experience to allow thBnto college and the workforce, if available, in order to support longitudinal studies. The partnership shall analyze and report student performance data in a manner that protects the rights of students and parents as required in 20 U.S.C. s. 1232g and s. 1002.22.
