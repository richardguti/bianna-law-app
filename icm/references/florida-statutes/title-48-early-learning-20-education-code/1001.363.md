# Fla. Stat. § 1001.363 — District school board members to represent entire district.

**Title:** XLVIII — EARLY LEARNING-20 EDUCATION CODE
**Chapter:** 1001 — EARLY LEARNING-20 GOVERNANCE
**Part:** II — SCHOOL DISTRICT GOVERNANCE
**Section:** 1001.363
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Each district school board of each district shall represent the entire district. Each member of the district school board shall serve as the representative of the entire district, rather than as the representative of a district school board member residence area.

**History:** s. 46, ch. 2002-387.
