# Fla. Stat. § 1006.145 — Disturbing school functions; penalty.

**Title:** XLVIII — EARLY LEARNING-20 EDUCATION CODE
**Chapter:** 1006 — SUPPORT FOR LEARNING
**Part:** I — PUBLIC K-12 EDUCATION SUPPORT FOR LEARNING AND STUDENT SERVICES
**Section:** 1006.145
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Any person not subject to the rules of a school who creates a disturbance on the property or grounds of any school, who commits any act that interrupts the orderly conduct of a school or any activity thereof commits a misdemeanor of the second degree, punishable as provided in s. 775.082 or s. 775.083.

**History:** s. 286, ch. 2002-387.
