# Fla. Stat. § 1003.576 — Individual education plans for exceptional students.

**Title:** XLVIII — EARLY LEARNING-20 EDUCATION CODE
**Chapter:** 1003 — PUBLIC K-12 EDUCATION
**Part:** V — SPECIALIZED INSTRUCTION FOR CERTAIN PUBLIC K-12 STUDENTS
**Section:** 1003.576
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The Department of Education must develop and have an operating electronic IEP system in place for statewide use. The statewide system shall be developed collaboratively with school districts and must include input from school districts currently developing or operating electronic IEP systems.

**History:** s. 31, ch. 2006-74; s. 24, ch. 2018-6.
