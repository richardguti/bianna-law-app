# Fla. Stat. § 1010.44 — Conduct of election; form of ballot; appointment of inspectors; canvassing returns.

**Title:** XLVIII — EARLY LEARNING-20 EDUCATION CODE
**Chapter:** 1010 — FINANCIAL MATTERS
**Part:** IV — PROVISIONS RELATING TO BONDING
**Section:** 1010.44
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The election, provided for in s. 1010.43, shall be held at the place or several places in the district where the last general election was held throughout the district, unless the district school board orders otherwise; and the district school board shall appoint inspectors for the election and cause to be prepared and furnished to the inspectors the ballots to be used at the election; the form of ballots for such election shall be: “For bonds” or “Against bonds.” The inspectors shall make returns to the district school board immediately after the election, and the school board shall hold a special meeting as soon thereafter as practicable for the purpose of canvassing the election returns and shall determine and certify its result.

**History:** s. 553, ch. 2002-387.
