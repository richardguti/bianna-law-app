# Fla. Stat. § 1010.24 — Cost accounting and reporting for universities.

**Title:** XLVIII — EARLY LEARNING-20 EDUCATION CODE
**Chapter:** 1010 — FINANCIAL MATTERS
**Part:** II — FINANCIAL REPORTING
**Section:** 1010.24
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Universities shall provide an annual expenditure analysis report as provided in s. 1011.90.
