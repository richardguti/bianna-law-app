# Fla. Stat. § 1011.66 — Distribution of FEFP funds.

**Title:** XLVIII — EARLY LEARNING-20 EDUCATION CODE
**Chapter:** 1011 — PLANNING AND BUDGETING
**Part:** II — FUNDING FOR SCHOOL DISTRICTS
**Section:** 1011.66
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The distribution of FEFP funds shall be made in payments on or about the 10th and 26th of each month.

**History:** s. 657, ch. 2002-387; s. 18, ch. 2025-203.
