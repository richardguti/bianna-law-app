# Fla. Stat. § 1001.463 — Vacancy in office of district school superintendent.

**Title:** XLVIII — EARLY LEARNING-20 EDUCATION CODE
**Chapter:** 1001 — EARLY LEARNING-20 GOVERNANCE
**Part:** II — SCHOOL DISTRICT GOVERNANCE
**Section:** 1001.463
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The office of district school superintendent in any district shall be vacant when the district school superintendent removes his or her residence from the district.

**History:** s. 65, ch. 2002-387.
