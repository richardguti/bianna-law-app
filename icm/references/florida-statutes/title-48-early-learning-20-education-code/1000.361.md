# Fla. Stat. § 1000.361 — Dues for the Interstate Compact on Educational Opportunity for Military Children.

**Title:** XLVIII — EARLY LEARNING-20 EDUCATION CODE
**Chapter:** 1000 — EARLY LEARNING-20 GENERAL PROVISIONS
**Part:** III — EDUCATIONAL COMPACTS
**Section:** 1000.361
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The annual dues assessment for the Interstate Compact on Educational Opportunity for Military Children shall be paid within existing resources by the Department of Education.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 2016-34; s. 1, ch. 2019-7; s. 2, ch. 2022-182; s. 9, ch. 2025-110.

**Note:** [1]Note.
—
Section 9, ch. 2025-110, amended s. 1000.40 to provide that s. 1000.361 shall stand repealed on July 1, 2028, unless reviewed and saved from repeal through reenactment by the Legislature.
