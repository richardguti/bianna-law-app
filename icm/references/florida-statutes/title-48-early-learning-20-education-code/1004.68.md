# Fla. Stat. § 1004.68 — Florida College System institution; degrees and certificates.

**Title:** XLVIII — EARLY LEARNING-20 EDUCATION CODE
**Chapter:** 1004 — PUBLIC POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION
**Part:** III — FLORIDA COLLEGE SYSTEM INSTITUTIONS
**Section:** 1004.68
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Each Florida College System institution board of trustees shall adopt rules establishing student performance standards for the award of degrees and certificates.

**History:** s. 218, ch. 2002-387; s. 30, ch. 2010-70; s. 53, ch. 2011-5; s. 6, ch. 2011-177.
