# Fla. Stat. § 1001.461 — District school superintendent; election and term of office.

**Title:** XLVIII — EARLY LEARNING-20 EDUCATION CODE
**Chapter:** 1001 — EARLY LEARNING-20 GOVERNANCE
**Part:** II — SCHOOL DISTRICT GOVERNANCE
**Section:** 1001.461
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The district school superintendent shall be elected for a term of 4 years or until the election or appointment and qualification of his or her successor.

**History:** s. 62, ch. 2002-387.
