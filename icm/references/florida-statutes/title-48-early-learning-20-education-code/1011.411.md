# Fla. Stat. § 1011.411 — Budgets for sponsored research at universities.

**Title:** XLVIII — EARLY LEARNING-20 EDUCATION CODE
**Chapter:** 1011 — PLANNING AND BUDGETING
**Part:** I — PREPARATION, ADOPTION, AND IMPLEMENTATION OF BUDGETS
**Section:** 1011.411
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Funds for sponsored research at each university shall be budgeted and expended pursuant to ss. 1004.22 and 1011.42.

**History:** s. 637, ch. 2002-387; s. 154, ch. 2007-217.
