# Fla. Stat. § 1010.82 — Textbook Bid Trust Fund.

**Title:** XLVIII — EARLY LEARNING-20 EDUCATION CODE
**Chapter:** 1010 — FINANCIAL MATTERS
**Part:** V — TRUST FUNDS
**Section:** 1010.82
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Chapter 99-36, Laws of Florida, re-created the Textbook Bid Trust Fund to record the revenue and disbursements of textbook bid performance deposits submitted to the Department of Education as required in s. 1006.33.

**History:** s. 594, ch. 2002-387; s. 97, ch. 2012-5.
