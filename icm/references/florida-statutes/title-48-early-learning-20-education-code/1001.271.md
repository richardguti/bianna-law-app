# Fla. Stat. § 1001.271 — Florida Information Resource Network.

**Title:** XLVIII — EARLY LEARNING-20 EDUCATION CODE
**Chapter:** 1001 — EARLY LEARNING-20 GOVERNANCE
**Part:** I — STATE-LEVEL GOVERNANCE
**Section:** 1001.271
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The Commissioner of Education shall facilitate and coordinate the use of the Florida Information Resource Network by school districts, educational institutions in the Florida College System, universities, and other eligible users.

**History:** s. 3, ch. 2009-59; s. 14, ch. 2011-5; s. 5, ch. 2011-55.
