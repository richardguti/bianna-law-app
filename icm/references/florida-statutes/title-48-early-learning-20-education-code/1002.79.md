# Fla. Stat. § 1002.79 — Rulemaking authority.

**Title:** XLVIII — EARLY LEARNING-20 EDUCATION CODE
**Chapter:** 1002 — STUDENT AND PARENTAL RIGHTS AND EDUCATIONAL CHOICES
**Part:** V — VOLUNTARY PREKINDERGARTEN EDUCATION PROGRAM
**Section:** 1002.79
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The State Board of Education shall adopt rules under ss. 120.536(1) and 120.54 to administer the provisions of this part conferring duties upon the department.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 2004-484; s. 458, ch. 2011-142; s. 79, ch. 2012-96; s. 16, ch. 2013-252; s. 47, ch. 2021-10.
