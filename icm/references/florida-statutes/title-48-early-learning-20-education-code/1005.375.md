# Fla. Stat. § 1005.375 — Violations; penalties.

**Title:** XLVIII — EARLY LEARNING-20 EDUCATION CODE
**Chapter:** 1005 — NONPUBLIC POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION
**Part:** III — LICENSURE OF NONPUBLIC POSTSECONDARY EDUCATIONAL INSTITUTIONS
**Section:** 1005.375
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

——

(1)  
Each of the following acts constitutes a felony of the third degree, punishable as provided in s. 775.082, s. 775.083, or s. 775.084:
(a)  
Operating an independent postsecondary educational institution without a valid, active license issued pursuant to this chapter.

(b)  
Obtaining or attempting to obtain a license to operate an institution by fraudulent misrepresentation.
