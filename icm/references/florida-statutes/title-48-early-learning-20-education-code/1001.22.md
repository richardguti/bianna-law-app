# Fla. Stat. § 1001.22 — Commission for Independent Education.

**Title:** XLVIII — EARLY LEARNING-20 EDUCATION CODE
**Chapter:** 1001 — EARLY LEARNING-20 GOVERNANCE
**Part:** I — STATE-LEVEL GOVERNANCE
**Section:** 1001.22
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The Commission for Independent Education shall authorize granting of certificates, diplomas, and degrees for independent postsecondary educational institutions pursuant to chapter 1005.

**History:** s. 28, ch. 2002-387.
