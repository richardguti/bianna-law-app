# Fla. Stat. § 1012.8551 — Employee background screening and investigations for Florida College System personnel.

**Title:** XLVIII — EARLY LEARNING-20 EDUCATION CODE
**Chapter:** 1012 — PERSONNEL
**Part:** IV — PUBLIC POSTSECONDARY EDUCATIONAL INSTITUTIONS; PERSONNEL
**Section:** 1012.8551
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Section 110.1127 applies to each institution in the Florida College System. Each institution must designate the positions subject to background screening and investigation pursuant to that section.

**History:** s. 21, ch. 2019-15.
