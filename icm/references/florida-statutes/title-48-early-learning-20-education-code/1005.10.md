# Fla. Stat. § 1005.10 — Sexual predator and sexual offender notification; nonpublic colleges, universities, and schools.

**Title:** XLVIII — EARLY LEARNING-20 EDUCATION CODE
**Chapter:** 1005 — NONPUBLIC POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION
**Part:** IV — WORKFORCE DEVELOPMENT EDUCATION
**Section:** 1005.10
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Each nonpublic college, university, and school shall inform students and employees at orientation and on its website of the existence of the Department of Law Enforcement sexual predator and sexual offender registry website and the toll-free telephone number that gives access to sexual predator and sexual offender public information pursuant to s. 943.043.

**History:** s. 3, ch. 2014-3.
