# Fla. Stat. § 320.6975 — Procedures and remedies applicable to distributor agreements.

**Title:** XXIII — MOTOR VEHICLES
**Chapter:** 320 — MOTOR VEHICLE LICENSES
**Part:** III — VETERANS’ ADULT DAY HEALTH CARE OF FLORIDA ACT
**Section:** 320.6975
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

All procedures and remedies specified in ss. 320.641 and 320.697 shall be available to a distributor whose distributor agreement is discontinued, canceled, not renewed, modified, or replaced by an importer.

**History:** s. 2, ch. 90-78; s. 2, ch. 91-201; s. 4, ch. 91-429; s. 3, ch. 2017-187.
