# Fla. Stat. § 316.1926 — Additional offenses.

**Title:** XXIII — MOTOR VEHICLES
**Chapter:** 316 — STATE UNIFORM TRAFFIC CONTROL
**Part:** III — VETERANS’ ADULT DAY HEALTH CARE OF FLORIDA ACT
**Section:** 316.1926
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
A person who violates the provisions of s. 316.2085(2) or (3) shall be cited for a moving violation, punishable as provided in chapter 318.

(2)  
A person who exceeds the speed limit in excess of 50 miles per hour or more in violation of s. 316.183(2), s. F.S. 316.19256316.1925  
Careless driving.
——

(1)  
Any person operating a vehicle upon the streets or highways within the state shall drive the same in a careful and prudent manner, having regard for the width, grade, curves, corners, traffic, and all other attendant circumstances, so as not to endanger the life, limb, or property of any person. Failure to drive in such manner shall constitute careless driving and a violation of this section.

(2)  
Any person who violates this section shall be cited for a moving violation, punishable as provided in chapter 318.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 71-135; ss. 1, 6, ch. 76-31; s. 24, ch. 96-350.

**Note:** Former s. 316.030.
