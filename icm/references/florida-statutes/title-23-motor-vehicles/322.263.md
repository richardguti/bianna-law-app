# Fla. Stat. § 322.263 — Legislative intent.

**Title:** XXIII — MOTOR VEHICLES
**Chapter:** 322 — DRIVER LICENSES
**Part:** III — VETERANS’ ADULT DAY HEALTH CARE OF FLORIDA ACT
**Section:** 322.263
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

——

It is declared to be the legislative intent to:
(1)  
Provide maximum safety for all persons who travel or otherwise use the public highways of the state.

(2)  
Deny the privilege of operating motor vehicles on public highways to persons who, by their conduct and record, have demonstrated their indifference for the safety and welfare of others and their disrespect for the laws of the state and the orders of the state courts and administrative agencies.
