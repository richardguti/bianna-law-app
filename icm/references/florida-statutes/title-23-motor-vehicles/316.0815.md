# Fla. Stat. § 316.0815 — Duty to yield to public transit vehicles.

**Title:** XXIII — MOTOR VEHICLES
**Chapter:** 316 — STATE UNIFORM TRAFFIC CONTROL
**Part:** III — VETERANS’ ADULT DAY HEALTH CARE OF FLORIDA ACT
**Section:** 316.0815
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
The driver of a vehicle shall yield the right-of-way to a publicly owned transit bus traveling in the same direction which has signaled and is reentering the traffic flow from a specifically designated pullout bay.

(2)  
This section does not relieve the driver of a public transit bus from the duty to drive with due regard for the safety of all persons using the r*nter of the roadway for use by traffic not otherwise permitted to use such lanes, or except as permitted under paragraph (1)(b). However, this subsection shall not be construed as prohibiting the crossing of the centerline in making a left turn into or from an alley, private road, or driveway.

(5)  
A violation of this section is a noncriminal traffic infraction, punishable as a moving violation as provided in chapter 318.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 71-135; s. 104, ch. 99-248; s. 11, ch. 2013-160; s. 5, ch. 2014-216.
