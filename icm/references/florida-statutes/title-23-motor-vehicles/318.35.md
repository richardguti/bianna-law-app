# Fla. Stat. § 318.35 — Term of office.

**Title:** XXIII — MOTOR VEHICLES
**Chapter:** 318 — DISPOSITION OF TRAFFIC INFRACTIONS
**Part:** III — VETERANS’ ADULT DAY HEALTH CARE OF FLORIDA ACT
**Section:** 318.35
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Hearing officers shall be independent contractors and may serve either full time or part time as determined by the chief judge. In either case, they shall serve at the pleasure of the chief judge of the county and circuit in which they are to hear cases and shall have no definite term of office.

**History:** s. 6, ch. 89-337; s. 7, ch. 94-202.
