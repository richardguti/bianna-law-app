# Fla. Stat. § 322.201 — Records as evidence.

**Title:** XXIII — MOTOR VEHICLES
**Chapter:** 322 — DRIVER LICENSES
**Part:** III — VETERANS’ ADULT DAY HEALTH CARE OF FLORIDA ACT
**Section:** 322.201
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

——

A copy, computer copy, or transcript of all abstracts of crash reports and all abstracts of court records of convictions. 
The department is authorized in accordance with chapter 257 to destroy reports, records, documents, papers, and correspondence which are considered obsolete.
