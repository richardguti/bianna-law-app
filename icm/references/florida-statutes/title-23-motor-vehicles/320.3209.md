# Fla. Stat. § 320.3209 — Coercion of dealer prohibited.

**Title:** XXIII — MOTOR VEHICLES
**Chapter:** 320 — MOTOR VEHICLE LICENSES
**Part:** III — VETERANS’ ADULT DAY HEALTH CARE OF FLORIDA ACT
**Section:** 320.3209
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
A manufacturer or distributor may not coerce or attempt to coerce a dealer to:
(a)  
Purchase a product that the dealer did not order;

(b)  
Enter into an agreement with the manufacturer or distributor;

(c)  
Take any action that is unfair or unreasonable to the dealer; or

(d)  
Enter into an agreement that requires the dealer to submit its disputes to binding arbitration or otherwise waive rights or responsibilities provided under ss. 320.3201-320.3211.

(2)  
As used in this section, the term “coerce” includes, but is not limited to, threatening to terminate, cancel, or not renew a manufacturer/dealer agreement without good cause or threatening to withhold product lines or delay product delivery as an inducement to amending the manufacturer/dealer agreement.

**History:** s. 8, ch. 2007-258.
