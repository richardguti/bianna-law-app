# Fla. Stat. § 316.89 — Creation of a wake on streets or highways.

**Title:** XXIII — MOTOR VEHICLES
**Chapter:** 316 — STATE UNIFORM TRAFFIC CONTROL
**Part:** III — VETERANS’ ADULT DAY HEALTH CARE OF FLORIDA ACT
**Section:** 316.89
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

A person may not operate a motor vehicle, vessel, or any other conveyance at a speed that creates an excessive wake on a flooded or inundated street or highway.

**History:** s. 8, ch. 2025-149.
