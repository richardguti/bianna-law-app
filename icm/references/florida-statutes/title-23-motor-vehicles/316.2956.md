# Fla. Stat. § 316.2956 — Violation of provisions relating to windshields, windows, and sunscreening material; penalties.

**Title:** XXIII — MOTOR VEHICLES
**Chapter:** 316 — STATE UNIFORM TRAFFIC CONTROL
**Part:** III — VETERANS’ ADULT DAY HEALTH CARE OF FLORIDA ACT
**Section:** 316.2956
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
Any person who operates a motor vehicle on which, after June 20, 1984, material was installed in violation of ss. 316.2951-316.2954 commits a noncriminal traffic infraction, punishable as a nonmoving violation as provided in chapter 318.

(2)  
The replacement or repair of any material legally installed is not a violation of ss. 316.2951-316.2954.

Labeling is not required for factory glazing which complies with Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard No. 205.

(2)  
Every percentage measurement required by ss. 316.2951-316.2954 is subject to a tolerance of plus or minus 3 percent.

(3)  
The department shall adopt rules approving light transmittance measuring devices for use in making measurements required by ss. 316.2951-316.2954. A witness otherwise qualified to testify shall be competent to give testimony regarding the percentage of light transmission when the testimony is derived from the use of an approved device. The reading from an approved device is presumed accurate and shall be admissible into evidence in the trial of any infraction arising under ss. 316.2951-316.2954.
