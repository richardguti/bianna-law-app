# Fla. Stat. § 316.088 — One-way roadways and rotary traffic islands.

**Title:** XXIII — MOTOR VEHICLES
**Chapter:** 316 — STATE UNIFORM TRAFFIC CONTROL
**Part:** III — VETERANS’ ADULT DAY HEALTH CARE OF FLORIDA ACT
**Section:** 316.088
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
The Department of Transportation and local authorities, with respect to highways under their respective jurisdictions, may designate any highway, roadway, part of a roadway, or specific lanes upon which vehicular traffic shall proceed in one direction at such times as shall be indicated by official traffic control devices.

(2)  
Upon a roadway so designated for one-way traffic, a vehicle shall be driven only in the direction designated at such times as shall be indicated by official traffic control devices.

(3)  
A vehicle passing around a rotary traffic island shall be driven only to the right of such island.

(4)  
A violation of this section is a noncriminal traffic infraction, punishable as a moving violation as provided in chapter 318.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 71-135; s. 112, ch. 99-248.
