# Fla. Stat. § 319.40 — Transactions by electronic or telephonic means.

**Title:** XXIII — MOTOR VEHICLES
**Chapter:** 319 — TITLE CERTIFICATES
**Part:** III — VETERANS’ ADULT DAY HEALTH CARE OF FLORIDA ACT
**Section:** 319.40
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
The department may accept any application provided for under this chapter by electronic or telephonic means.

(2)  
The department may issue an electronic certificate of title in lieu of printing a paper title.

(3)  
The department may collect e-mail addresses and use e-mail in lieu of the United States Postal Service as a method of notification. However, any notice regarding the potential forfeiture or foreclosure of an interest in property must be sent via the United States Postal Service.

**History:** s. 18, ch. 97-300; s. 26, ch. 2012-181; s. 33, ch. 2026-39.
