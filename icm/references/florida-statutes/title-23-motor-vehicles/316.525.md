# Fla. Stat. § 316.525 — Requirements for vehicles hauling loads.

**Title:** XXIII — MOTOR VEHICLES
**Chapter:** 316 — STATE UNIFORM TRAFFIC CONTROL
**Part:** III — VETERANS’ ADULT DAY HEALTH CARE OF FLORIDA ACT
**Section:** 316.525
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

(1)  
It is the duty of every owner, licensee, and driver, severally, of any truck, trailer, semitrailer, or pole trailer to use such stanchions, standards, stays, supports, or other equipment, appliances, or contrivances, together with one or more lock chains, when lock chains are the most suitable means of ,0, 106, ch. 2002-20; s. 4, ch. 2002-235; s. 1, ch. 2020-135.
