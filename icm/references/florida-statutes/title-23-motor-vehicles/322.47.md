# Fla. Stat. § 322.47 — Executive head defined.

**Title:** XXIII — MOTOR VEHICLES
**Chapter:** 322 — DRIVER LICENSES
**Part:** III — VETERANS’ ADULT DAY HEALTH CARE OF FLORIDA ACT
**Section:** 322.47
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

As used in ss. 322.44 and 322.50, with reference to this state, the term “executive head” means the Governor.

**History:** s. 5, ch. 67-176; s. 4, ch. 77-373.
