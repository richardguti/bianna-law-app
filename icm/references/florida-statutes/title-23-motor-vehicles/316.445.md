# Fla. Stat. § 316.445 — Performance ability of motorcycle brakes.

**Title:** XXIII — MOTOR VEHICLES
**Chapter:** 316 — STATE UNIFORM TRAFFIC CONTROL
**Part:** III — VETERANS’ ADULT DAY HEALTH CARE OF FLORIDA ACT
**Section:** 316.445
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

Every motorcycle and motor-driven cycle, at all times and under all conditions of loading, upon application of the service brake, shall be capable of:
(a)  
Developing a braking force that is not less than 43.5 percent of its gross weight;

——

Every motor-driven cycle must comply with the provisions of s. 316.261, except that:
(1)  
Motorcycles and motor-driven cycles need not be equipped with parking brakes.

(2)  
The wheel of a sidecar attached to a motorcycle or to a motor-driven cycle, and the front wheel of a motor-driven cycle, need not be equipped with brakes, provided that such motorcycle or motor-driven cycle is capable of complying with the performance requirements of this chapter.

A violation of this section is a noncriminal traffic infraction, punishable as a nonmoving violation as provided in chapter 318.
