# Fla. Stat. § 317.0005 — Forms and notices.

**Title:** XXIII — MOTOR VEHICLES
**Chapter:** 317 — OFF-HIGHWAY VEHICLE TITLING
**Part:** III — VETERANS’ ADULT DAY HEALTH CARE OF FLORIDA ACT
**Section:** 317.0005
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The department shall prescribe and provide suitable forms for applications and other notices and forms necessary to administer the provisions of this chapter.

**History:** s. 59, ch. 2002-295; s. 45, ch. 2005-164; s. 26, ch. 2013-18.
