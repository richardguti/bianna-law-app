# Fla. Stat. § 320.95 — Transactions by electronic or telephonic means.

**Title:** XXIII — MOTOR VEHICLES
**Chapter:** 321 — HIGHWAY PATROL
**Part:** III — VETERANS’ ADULT DAY HEALTH CARE OF FLORIDA ACT
**Section:** 320.95
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
The department may accept an application provided for under this chapter by electronic or telephonic means.

(2)  
The department may collect e-mail addresses and use e-mail in lieu of the United States Postal Service as a method of notification.

**History:** s. 21, ch. 97-300; s. 44, ch. 2012-181; s. 15, ch. 2026-39.
