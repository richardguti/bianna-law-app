# Fla. Stat. § 322.23 — Suspending privileges of nonresidents and reporting convictions.

**Title:** XXIII — MOTOR VEHICLES
**Chapter:** 322 — DRIVER LICENSES
**Part:** III — VETERANS’ ADULT DAY HEALTH CARE OF FLORIDA ACT
**Section:** 322.23
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
The privilege of driving a motor vehicle on the highways of this state, given to a nonresident, shall be subject to suspension or revocation by the department in the same manner and for the same cause as a license issued by the department may be suspended or revoked.

(2)  
The department is authorized, upon receiving a record of the conviction in this state of a nonresident driver, of any offense under the motor vehicle laws of this state, to forward a certified copy of such record to the motor vehicle administrator in the state wherein the person so convicted is a resident.

**History:** s. 35, ch. 19551, 1939; CGL 1940 Supp. 4151(649); s. 35, ch. 20451, 1941.
