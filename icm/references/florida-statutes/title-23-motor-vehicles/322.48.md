# Fla. Stat. § 322.48 — Review of employee's acts.

**Title:** XXIII — MOTOR VEHICLES
**Chapter:** 322 — DRIVER LICENSES
**Part:** III — VETERANS’ ADULT DAY HEALTH CARE OF FLORIDA ACT
**Section:** 322.48
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Any act or omission of any official or employee of this state done or omitted pursuant to, or in enforcing, the provisions of s. 322.44 or s. 322.50 shall be subject to review in accordance with chapter 120 by the Department of Highway Safety and Motor Vehicles, but any review of the validity of any conviction or failure to answer summons reported pursuant to the compact in s. 322.44 or s. 322.50 shall be limited to establishing the identity of the person so convicted or failing to answer summons.

**History:** s. 6, ch. 67-176; ss. 24, 35, ch. 69-106; s. 1, ch. 77-117; s. 4, ch. 77-373.
