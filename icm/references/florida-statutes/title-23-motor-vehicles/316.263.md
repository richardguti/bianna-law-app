# Fla. Stat. § 316.263 — Maintenance of brakes.

**Title:** XXIII — MOTOR VEHICLES
**Chapter:** 316 — STATE UNIFORM TRAFFIC CONTROL
**Part:** III — VETERANS’ ADULT DAY HEALTH CARE OF FLORIDA ACT
**Section:** 316.263
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

All brakes shall be maintained in good working order and shall be so adjusted as to operate as equally as practicable with respect to the wheels on opposite sides of the vehicle. A violation of this section is a noncriminal traffic infraction, punishable as a nonmoving violation as provided in chapter 318.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 71-135; s. 201, ch. 99-248.
