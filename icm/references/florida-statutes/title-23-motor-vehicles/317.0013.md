# Fla. Stat. § 317.0013 — Nonmoving traffic violations.

**Title:** XXIII — MOTOR VEHICLES
**Chapter:** 317 — OFF-HIGHWAY VEHICLE TITLING
**Part:** III — VETERANS’ ADULT DAY HEALTH CARE OF FLORIDA ACT
**Section:** 317.0013
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Any person who fails to comply with any provision of this chapter for which a penalty is not otherwise provided commits a nonmoving traffic violation, punishable as provided in s. 318.18.

**History:** s. 67, ch. 2002-295; s. 51, ch. 2005-164.
