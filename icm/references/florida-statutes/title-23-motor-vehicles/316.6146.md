# Fla. Stat. § 316.6146 — Transportation of private school students on public school buses and public school students on private school buses; agreement.

**Title:** XXIII — MOTOR VEHICLES
**Chapter:** 316 — STATE UNIFORM TRAFFIC CONTROL
**Part:** III — VETERANS’ ADULT DAY HEALTH CARE OF FLORIDA ACT
**Section:** 316.6146
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

——

Private school students may be transported on public school buses and public school students may be transported on private school buses when there is mutual agreement between the local school board and the applicable private school.
