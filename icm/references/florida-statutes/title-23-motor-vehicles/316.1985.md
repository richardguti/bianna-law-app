# Fla. Stat. § 316.1985 — Limitations on backing.

**Title:** XXIII — MOTOR VEHICLES
**Chapter:** 316 — STATE UNIFORM TRAFFIC CONTROL
**Part:** III — VETERANS’ ADULT DAY HEALTH CARE OF FLORIDA ACT
**Section:** 316.1985
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
The driver of a vehicle shall not back the same unless such movement can be made with safety and without interfering with other traffic.

(2)  
The driver of a vehicle shall not back the same upon any shoulder or roadway of any limited access facility.

(3)  
A violation of this section is a noncriminal traffic infraction, punishable as a moving violation as provided in chapter 318.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 71-135; s. 1, ch. 76-31; s. 149, ch. 99-248.

**Note:** Former s. 316.098.
