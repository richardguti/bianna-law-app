# Fla. Stat. § 316.510 — Projecting loads on passenger vehicles.

**Title:** XXIII — MOTOR VEHICLES
**Chapter:** 316 — STATE UNIFORM TRAFFIC CONTROL
**Part:** III — VETERANS’ ADULT DAY HEALTH CARE OF FLORIDA ACT
**Section:** 316.510
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

No passenger type vehicle shall be operated on any highway with any load carried thereon extending beyond the fenders on the left side of the vehicle or extending more than 6 inches beyond the line of the fenders on the right side thereof. A violation of this section is a noncriminal traffic infraction, punishable as a nonmoving violation as provided in chapter 318.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 71-135; s. 1, ch. 76-31; s. 234, ch. 99-248.

**Note:** Former s. 316.197.
