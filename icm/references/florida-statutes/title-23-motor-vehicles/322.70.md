# Fla. Stat. § 322.70 — Transactions by electronic or telephonic means.

**Title:** XXIII — MOTOR VEHICLES
**Chapter:** 322 — DRIVER LICENSES
**Part:** III — VETERANS’ ADULT DAY HEALTH CARE OF FLORIDA ACT
**Section:** 322.70
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The department is authorized to accept any application provided for under this chapter by electronic or telephonic means.

**History:** s. 31, ch. 89-282; s. 88, ch. 94-306; s. 11, ch. 2025-125.
