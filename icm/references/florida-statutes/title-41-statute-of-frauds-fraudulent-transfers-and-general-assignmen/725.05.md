# Fla. Stat. § 725.05 — Satisfaction for less than amount due.

**Title:** XLI — STATUTE OF FRAUDS, FRAUDULENT TRANSFERS, AND GENERAL ASSIGNMENTS
**Chapter:** 725 — UNENFORCEABLE CONTRACTS
**Part:** IV — COMMISSIONER OF DEEDS
**Section:** 725.05
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

When the amount of any debt or obligation is liquidated, the parties may satisfy the debt by a written instrument other than by endorsement on a check for less than the full amount due.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 71-94.
