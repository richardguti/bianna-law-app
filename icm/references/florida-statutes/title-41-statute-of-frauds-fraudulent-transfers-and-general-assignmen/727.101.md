# Fla. Stat. § 727.101 — Intent of chapter.

**Title:** XLI — STATUTE OF FRAUDS, FRAUDULENT TRANSFERS, AND GENERAL ASSIGNMENTS
**Chapter:** 727 — GENERAL ASSIGNMENTS
**Part:** IV — COMMISSIONER OF DEEDS
**Section:** 727.101
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The intent of this chapter is to provide a uniform procedure for the administration and orderly liquidation of insolvent estates, and to ensure full reporting to creditors and equal distribution of assets according to priorities as established under this chapter.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 87-174; s. 1, ch. 2023-219.
