# Fla. Stat. § 636.055 — Levy upon deposit limited.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 636 — PREPAID LIMITED HEALTH SERVICE ORGANIZATIONS AND DISCOUNT PLAN ORGANIZATIONS
**Part:** I — PREPAID LIMITED HEALTH SERVICE ORGANIZATIONS
**Section:** 636.055
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

No judgment creditor or other claimant, other than the office or department, of a prepaid limited health service organization shall have the right to levy upon any of the assets or securities held in this state as a deposit under s. 636.046.

**History:** s. 44, ch. 93-148; s. 1546, ch. 2003-261.
