# Fla. Stat. § 642.0262 — Net worth required to maintain certificate of authority.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 642 — LEGAL EXPENSE INSURANCE
**Part:** III — HEALTH CARE SERVICES
**Section:** 642.0262
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

To maintain a certificate of authority to transact legal expense insurance, a legal expense insurance corporation must maintain a net worth of not less than the greater of:
(1)  
Ten thousand dollars; or

(2)  
Ten percent of the insurer's total liabilities.

**History:** ss. 4, 7, ch. 93-147; s. 83, ch. 2000-158.
