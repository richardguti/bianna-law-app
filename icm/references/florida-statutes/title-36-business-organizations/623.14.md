# Fla. Stat. § 623.14 — Construction.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 623 — PRIVATE SCHOOL CORPORATIONS
**Part:** II — REVISED UNIFORM PARTNERSHIP ACT
**Section:** 623.14
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The provisions of this act shall be deemed to be accumulative and supplemental to any other powers and authority for the creation of corporations not for profit as set out in chapter 617.
