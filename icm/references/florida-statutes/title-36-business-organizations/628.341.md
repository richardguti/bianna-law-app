# Fla. Stat. § 628.341 — Nonassessable policies; mutual insurers.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 628 — STOCK AND MUTUAL INSURERS; HOLDING COMPANIES
**Part:** I — STOCK AND MUTUAL INSURERS: ORGANIZATION AND CORPORATE PROCEDURES
**Section:** 628.341
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
While possessing surplus funds in amount not less than the paid-in capital stock required of a domestic stock insurer transacting like kinds of insurance, a domestic mutual insurer may, upon receipt of the order of the office so authorizing, extinguish the contingent liability of its members as to all its policies in force and may omit provisions imposing contingent liability in all its policies currently issued so long as such surplus funds meet such requirement as to amount.

(2)  
A foreign or alien mutual insurer may issue nonassessable policies to its members in this state pursuant to its articles of incorporation and the laws of its domicile.
