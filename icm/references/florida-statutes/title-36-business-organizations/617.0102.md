# Fla. Stat. § 617.0102 — Reservation of power to amend or repeal.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 617 — NONPROFIT CORPORATIONS
**Part:** III — FLORIDA STATE FAIR AUTHORITY
**Section:** 617.0102
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The Legislature has the power to amend or repeal all or part of this chapter at any time, and all domestic and foreign corporations subject to this chapter shall be governed by the amendment or repeal.

**History:** s. 2, ch. 90-179; s. 125, ch. 2026-168.
