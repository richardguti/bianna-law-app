# Fla. Stat. § 627.6742 — Permitted compensation arrangements.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 627 — INSURANCE RATES AND CONTRACTS
**Part:** VIII — MEDICARE SUPPLEMENT POLICIES
**Section:** 627.6742
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
The commission shall adopt rules governing the permitted compensation arrangements between insurers and agents with respect to Medicare supplement policies.

(2)  
The rules shall be based upon the format, interrelationships, and parameters relating to compensation arrangements as set forth in the NAIC Medicare Supplement Insurance Minimum Standards Model Act and Regulations adopted by the National Association of Insurance Commissioners on July 31, 1991.

**History:** s. 6, ch. 90-257; s. 18, ch. 91-201; ss. 143, 149, ch. 92-33; s. 114, ch. 92-318; s. 1175, ch. 2003-261.
