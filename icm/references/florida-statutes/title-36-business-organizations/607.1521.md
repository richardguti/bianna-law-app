# Fla. Stat. § 607.1521 — Withdrawal deemed on conversion to domestic filing entity.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 607 — FLORIDA BUSINESS CORPORATION ACT
**Part:** I — GENERAL PROVISIONS
**Section:** 607.1521
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

A foreign corporation authorized to transact business in this state that converts to a domestic corporation or another domestic eligible entity that is organized, incorporated, registered, or otherwise formed through the delivery of a record to the department for filing is deemed to have withdrawn its certificate of authority on the effective date of the conversion.

**History:** s. 211, ch. 2019-90.
