# Fla. Stat. § 635.081 — Administration and enforcement.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 635 — MORTGAGE GUARANTY INSURANCE
**Part:** III — SERVICE WARRANTY ASSOCIATIONS
**Section:** 635.081
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The commission may adopt rules pursuant to ss. 120.536(1) and 120.54 to implement the provisions of this chapter and shall have the same powers of administration and enforcement of the provisions of this chapter as it has with respect to casualty or surety insurers in general under the Florida Insurance Code.
