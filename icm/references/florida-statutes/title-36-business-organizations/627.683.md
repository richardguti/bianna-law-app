# Fla. Stat. § 627.683 — Licensed agent required.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 627 — INSURANCE RATES AND CONTRACTS
**Part:** IX — CREDIT LIFE AND DISABILITY INSURANCES
**Section:** 627.683
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

All policies to which this chapter applies shall be issued through a licensed agent.

**History:** s. 602, ch. 59-205; s. 3, ch. 76-168; s. 1, ch. 77-457; ss. 2, 3, ch. 81-318; ss. 534, 537, 809(2nd), ch. 82-243; s. 79, ch. 82-386; s. 114, ch. 92-318.
