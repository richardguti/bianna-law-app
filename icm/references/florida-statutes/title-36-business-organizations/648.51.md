# Fla. Stat. § 648.51 — Surrender of license.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 648 — BAIL BOND AGENTS
**Part:** III — HEALTH CARE SERVICES
**Section:** 648.51
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
Though issued to a licensee, all licenses issued under this chapter are at all times the property of the state, and upon notice of any suspension, revocation, refusal to renew, failure to renew, expiration, or other termination of the license, such license shall no longer be in force and effect.

(2)  
This section shall not be deemed to require the surrender to the department of any license unless such surrender has been requested by the department.

**History:** s. 21, ch. 61-406; ss. 13, 35, ch. 69-106; s. 177, ch. 70-339; s. 3, ch. 76-168; s. 1, ch. 77-457; ss. 2, 3, ch. 81-318; ss. 71, 72, ch. 82-175; ss. 32, 50, 51, ch. 84-103; s. 5, ch. 87-321; ss. 38, 46, 47, ch. 90-131; s. 4, ch. 91-429; s. 39, ch. 96-372.

**Note:** wass="Subsection">(3)  
A person whose license as a bail bond agent has been revoked or suspended may not be employed by any bail bond agent, have any ownership interest in any business involving bail bonds, or have any financial interest of any type in any bail bond business during the period of revocation or suspension.
