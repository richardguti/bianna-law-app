# Fla. Stat. § 627.786 — Transaction of title insurance and any other kind of insurance prohibited.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 627 — INSURANCE RATES AND CONTRACTS
**Part:** XIII — TITLE INSURANCE CONTRACTS
**Section:** 627.786
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
An insurer may not transact title insurance and any other kind of insurance in this state.

The regulation of title insurers and title insurance is preempted to the state.
