# Fla. Stat. § 607.08101 — Compensation of directors.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 607 — FLORIDA BUSINESS CORPORATION ACT
**Part:** I — GENERAL PROVISIONS
**Section:** 607.08101
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Unless the articles of incorporation or bylaws provide otherwise, the board of directors may fix the compensation of directors.

**History:** s. 77, ch. 89-154; s. 149, ch. 90-179.

**Note:** Former s. 607.0810.
