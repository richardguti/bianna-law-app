# Fla. Stat. § 610.113 — Nondiscrimination by municipality or county.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 610 — CABLE AND VIDEO SERVICES
**Part:** III — BENEFIT CORPORATIONS
**Section:** 610.113
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
A municipality or county shall allow a certificateholder to install, construct, and maintain a network within a public right-of-way and shall provide a certificateholder with comparable, nondiscriminatory, and competitively neutral access to the public right-of-way in accordance with the provisions of s. 337.401. All use of a public right-of-way by a certificateholder is nonexclusive.

(2)  
A municipality or county may not discriminate against a certificateholder regarding:
(a)  
The authorization or placement of a network in a public right-of-way;

(b)  
Access to a building or other property; or

(c)  
Utility pole attachment terms and conditions.

**History:** s. 7, ch. 2007-29.
