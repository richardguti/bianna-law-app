# Fla. Stat. § 616.24 — Enforcement.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 616 — PUBLIC FAIRS AND EXPOSITIONS
**Part:** I — GENERAL PROVISIONS
**Section:** 616.24
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
The department shall administer and enforce the provisions of this chapter and the rules adopted pursuant thereto.

(2)  
It is the duty of each state attorney, law enforcement officer as defined by chapter 943, and other appropriate county or municipal officer to enforce this chapter and the rules adopted pursuant thereto and to assist the department and its inspectors and agents in the enforcement of this chapter and the rules adopted pursuant thereto.

(3)  
The department may commence and maintain any necessary and proper action for the following purposes:
(a)  
To enforce this chapter and the rules adopted pursuant thereto.

(b)  
F.S. 616.236616.23  
Use of buildings.
——

The buildings authorized by ss. 616.21-616.23 may be used by the county, municipality, or fair association for public fair or exposition purposes. These buildings may be used as office space for agricultural agents; however, no more than 20 percent of the buildings may be so used.
