# Fla. Stat. § 634.4025 — Part exclusive; applicability of other laws.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 634 — WARRANTY ASSOCIATIONS
**Part:** III — SERVICE WARRANTY ASSOCIATIONS
**Section:** 634.4025
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
Except as provided in this part, service warranty associations shall be governed by this part and sF.S. 634.4026634.402  
Powers of department, commission, and office; rules.
——

The office shall administer this part, and the commission may adopt rules pursuant to ss. 120.536(1) and 120.54 to implement the provisions of this part related to service warranty associations and service warranties. The department shall administer this part and may adopt rules pursuant to ss. 120.536(1) and 120.54 to implement provisions of this part related to sales representatives. Such rules by the commission or department may identify specific methods of competition or acts or practices that are prohibited by s. 634.436, but shall not enlarge upon or extend the provisions of that section.
