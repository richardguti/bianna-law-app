# Fla. Stat. § 628.920 — Eligibility of licensed captive insurance company for certificate of authority to act as insurer.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 628 — STOCK AND MUTUAL INSURERS; HOLDING COMPANIES
**Part:** V — CAPTIVE INSURERS
**Section:** 628.920
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

A licensed captive insurance company that meets the necessary requirements of this part imposed upon an insurer must be considered for issuance of a certificate of authority to act as an insurer in this state.

**History:** s. 34, ch. 2012-151.
