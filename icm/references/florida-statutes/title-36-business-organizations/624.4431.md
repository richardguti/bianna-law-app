# Fla. Stat. § 624.4431 — Administration; rules.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 624 — INSURANCE CODE: ADMINISTRATION AND GENERAL PROVISIONS
**Part:** III — AUTHORIZATION OF INSURERS AND GENERAL REQUIREMENTS
**Section:** 624.4431
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The administration of ss. 624.436-624.446 is vested in the commission and office. The commission may adopt rules pursuant to ss. 120.536(1) and 120.54 to implement the provisions of ss. 624.436-624.446.

**History:** ss. 36, 188, ch. 91-108; s. 4, ch. 91-429; s. 202, ch. 98-200; s. 819, ch. 2003-261.
