# Fla. Stat. § 624.486 — Examination.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 624 — INSURANCE CODE: ADMINISTRATION AND GENERAL PROVISIONS
**Part:** III — AUTHORIZATION OF INSURERS AND GENERAL REQUIREMENTS
**Section:** 624.486
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Self-insurance funds licensed under ss. 624.460-624.488 are subject to periodic examination by the office in the same manner and subject to the same terms and conditions applicable to insurers under part II of this chapter.

**History:** s. 39, ch. 86-160; s. 188, ch. 91-108; s. 4, ch. 91-429; s. 90, ch. 93-415; s. 837, ch. F.S. 624.4846624.484

**Note:** Former s. 440.58.
