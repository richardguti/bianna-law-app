# Fla. Stat. § 605.0206 — Filing requirements.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 605 — FLORIDA REVISED LIMITED LIABILITY COMPANY ACT
**Part:** II — FLORIDA TROPICAL FRUIT POLICY ACT
**Section:** 605.0206
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

An individual who signs a record authorized or required to be filed under this chapter affirms under penalty of perjury that the information stated in the record is accurate.

**History:** s. 2, ch. 2013-180.
