# Fla. Stat. § 623.09 — Taxation exemption.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 623 — PRIVATE SCHOOL CORPORATIONS
**Part:** II — REVISED UNIFORM PARTNERSHIP ACT
**Section:** 623.09
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The property of any private school corporation incorporated under the provisions of this act shall be exempt from taxation as provided by law.

**History:** s. 9, ch. 59-113.
