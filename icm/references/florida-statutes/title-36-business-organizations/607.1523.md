# Fla. Stat. § 607.1523 — Action by Department of Legal Affairs.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 607 — FLORIDA BUSINESS CORPORATION ACT
**Part:** I — GENERAL PROVISIONS
**Section:** 607.1523
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The Department of Legal Affairs may maintain an action to enjoin a foreign corporation from transacting business in this state in violation of this chapter.

**History:** s. 213, ch. 2019-90.
