# Fla. Stat. § 617.1107 — (k)

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 617 — NONPROFIT CORPORATIONS
**Part:** III — FLORIDA STATE FAIR AUTHORITY
**Section:** 617.1107
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

617.1107  
(k)  
If the survivor exists before the merger:
1.  
All the property and contract and other rights of the survivor remain its property and contract and other rights without transfer, reversion, or impairment;

2.  
The survivor remains subject to all of its debts, obligations, and other liabilities; and

3.  
Except as provided by law or the plan of merger, the survivor continues to hold all of its rights, privileges, franchises, and immunities.
