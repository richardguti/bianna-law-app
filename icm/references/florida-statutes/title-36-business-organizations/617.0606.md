# Fla. Stat. § 617.0606 — Resignation of members.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 617 — NONPROFIT CORPORATIONS
**Part:** III — FLORIDA STATE FAIR AUTHORITY
**Section:** 617.0606
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
A member may resign at any time for any reason.

(2)  
The resignation of a member does not relieve the member from any obligations incurred or commitments made before resignation.

**History:** s. 18, ch. 2009-205; s. 29, ch. 2026-168.
