# Fla. Stat. § 634.429 — Fronting not permitted.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 634 — WARRANTY ASSOCIATIONS
**Part:** III — SERVICE WARRANTY ASSOCIATIONS
**Section:** 634.429
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

No authorized insurer or licensed service warranty association may act as a fronting company for any unauthorized insurer or unlicensed service warranty association. A “fronting company” is an authorized insurer or licensed service warranty association which, by reinsurance or otherwise, generally transfers to one or more unauthorized insurers or unlicensed service warranty associations the risk of loss under warranties written by it in this state.

**History:** s. 5, ch. 78-255; s. 3, ch. 81-148; s. 2, ch. 81-318; s. 3, ch. 83-265; ss. 23, 36, 37, 38, ch. 83-322; s. 20, ch. 93-195.
