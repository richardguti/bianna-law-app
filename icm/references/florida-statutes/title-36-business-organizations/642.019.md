# Fla. Stat. § 642.019 — Organization of legal expense insurance corporations.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 642 — LEGAL EXPENSE INSURANCE
**Part:** III — HEALTH CARE SERVICES
**Section:** 642.019
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
Any number of corporations or adult natural persons may organize a legal expense insurance corporation under the law of this state relating to corporations generally.

(2)  
The articles of incorporation shall conform to the requirements applicable to corporations, and, in addition:
(a)  
The name of the corporation shall indicate that legal services or indemnity for legal expenses is to be provided; and

(b)  
The purposes of the corporation shall be limited to providing legal services or indemnity for legal expenses and business reasonably related thereto.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 79-103; s. 2, ch. 81-318; ss. 3, 20, 21, ch. 83-278; ss. 6, 7, ch. 93-147.
