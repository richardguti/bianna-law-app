# Fla. Stat. § 636.224 — Notice of change of name or address of discount plan organization.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 636 — PREPAID LIMITED HEALTH SERVICE ORGANIZATIONS AND DISCOUNT PLAN ORGANIZATIONS
**Part:** II — DISCOUNT PLAN ORGANIZATIONS
**Section:** 636.224
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Each discount plan organization must provide the office at least 30 days' advance notice of any change in the discount plan organization's name, address, principal business address, or mailing address.

**History:** s. 12, ch. 2005-232; s. 97, ch. 2006-1; s. 23, ch. 2017-112.
