# Fla. Stat. § 625.307 — United States Government obligations.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 625 — ACCOUNTING, INVESTMENTS, AND DEPOSITS BY INSURERS
**Part:** II — INVESTMENTS
**Section:** 625.307
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

An insurer may invest in bonds, notes, warrants, and other evidences of indebtedness which are direct obligations of the Government of the United States or for which the full faith and credit of the Government of the United States is pledged for the payment of principal and interest.

**History:** s. 132, ch. 59-205; s. 3, ch. 76-168; s. 1, ch. 77-457; ss. 2, 3, ch. 81-318; ss. 122, 809(1st), ch. 82-243; ss. 187, 188, ch. 91-108; s. 4, ch. 91-429.
