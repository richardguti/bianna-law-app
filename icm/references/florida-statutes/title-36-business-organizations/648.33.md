# Fla. Stat. § 648.33 — Bail bond rates.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 648 — BAIL BOND AGENTS
**Part:** III — HEALTH CARE SERVICES
**Section:** 648.33
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
Bail bond rates are subject to the provisions of part I of chapter 627 of the insurance code.

(2)  
It is unlawful for a bail bond agent to execute a bail bond without charging a premium therefor, and the premium rate may not exceed or be less than the premium rate as filed with and approved by the office.

(3)F.S. 648.3156648.315  
Number of applications for licensure required.
——

After a license as a bail bond agent has been issued to an individual, the same individual is not required to file another application for a similar license unless:
(1)  
Specifically ordered by the department to complete a new application; or

(2)  
A period of 48 months transpires between the time the licensee's last limited surety agent or professional bail bond agent's appointment is terminated and the date an application for a similar appointment is received by the department.
