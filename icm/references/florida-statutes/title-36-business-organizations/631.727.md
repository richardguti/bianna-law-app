# Fla. Stat. § 631.727 — Immunity.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 631 — INSURER INSOLVENCY; GUARANTY OF PAYMENT
**Part:** III — LIFE AND HEALTH INSURANCE GUARANTY OF PAYMENTS
**Section:** 631.727
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

There shall be no liability on the part of, and no cause of action of any nature shall arise against, any member insurer or its agents or employees, the association or its agents or employees, members of the board of directors, the Chief Financial Officer, or the department or office or their representatives for any action taken by them in the performance of their powers and duties under thisF.S. 631.7266631.726  
Tax exemptions.
——

The association shall be exempt from payment of all fees and all taxes levied by this state or any of its subdivisions, except taxes levied on real property.

**History:** s. 15, ch. 79-189; s. 809(1st), ch. 82-243; ss. 187, 188, ch. 91-108; s. 4, ch. 91-429.
