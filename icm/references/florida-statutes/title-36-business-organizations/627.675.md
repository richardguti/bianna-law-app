# Fla. Stat. § 627.675 — Compliance with Omnibus Budget Reconciliation Acts.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 627 — INSURANCE RATES AND CONTRACTS
**Part:** VIII — MEDICARE SUPPLEMENT POLICIES
**Section:** 627.675
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Each entity that provides Medicare supplement policies or contracts must comply with all provisions of s. 4081 of the Omnibus Budget Reconciliation Act of 1987 (Pub. L. No. 100-203) (OBRA) and the Omnibus Budget Reconciliation Act of 1990 (Pub. L. No. 101-508).

**History:** s. 96, ch. 89-360; s. 1, ch. 90-192; s. 18, ch. 91-296; s. 114, ch. 92-318.
