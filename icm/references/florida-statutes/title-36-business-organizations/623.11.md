# Fla. Stat. § 623.11 — Corporation membership.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 623 — PRIVATE SCHOOL CORPORATIONS
**Part:** II — REVISED UNIFORM PARTNERSHIP ACT
**Section:** 623.11
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The membership of a corporation existing under the provisions of this act shall be composed of persons who have been approved for membership, as provided by the charter and bylaws of the corporation.

**History:** s. 11, ch. 59-113.
