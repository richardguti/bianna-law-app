# Fla. Stat. § 620.8917 — Action on plan of merger by constituent partnership.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 620 — PARTNERSHIP LAWS
**Part:** II — REVISED UNIFORM PARTNERSHIP ACT
**Section:** 620.8917
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
A plan of merger must be consented to by all of the partners of a constituent partnership. The consents required by this subsection must be in, or evidenced by, a record.

(2)  
Subject to s. 620.8920 and any contractual rights, after a merger is approved, and at any time before a filing is made under s. 620.8918, a constituent partnership may amend the plan or abandon the planned merger:
(a)  
As provided in the plan.

(b)  
Except as prohibited by the plan, with the same consent as was required to approve the plan.

**History:** s. 22, ch. 2005-267.
