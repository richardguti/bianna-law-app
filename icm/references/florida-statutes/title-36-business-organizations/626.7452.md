# Fla. Stat. § 626.7452 — Managing general agents; examination authority.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 626 — INSURANCE FIELD REPRESENTATIVES AND OPERATIONS
**Part:** II — GENERAL LINES AGENTS
**Section:** 626.7452
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The acts of the managing general agent are considered to be the acts of the insurer on whose behalf it is acting. A managing general agent may be examined as if it were the insurer.

**History:** ss. 85, 207, ch. 90-363; s. 4, ch. 91-429; s. 4, ch. 2021-77.
