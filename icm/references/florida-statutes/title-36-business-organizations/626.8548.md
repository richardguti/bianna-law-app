# Fla. Stat. § 626.8548 — “All-lines adjuster” defined.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 626 — INSURANCE FIELD REPRESENTATIVES AND OPERATIONS
**Part:** VI — INSURANCE ADJUSTERS
**Section:** 626.8548
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

——

An “all-lines adjuster” is a person who, for money, commission, or any other thing of value, directly or indirectly undertakes on behalf of a public adjuster or an insurer to ascertain and determine the amount of any claim, m document in the file the response or information provided.
