# Fla. Stat. § 634.302 — Powers of department, commission, and office; rules.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 634 — WARRANTY ASSOCIATIONS
**Part:** II — HOME WARRANTY ASSOCIATIONS
**Section:** 634.302
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The office shall administer this part, and the commission may adopt rules pursuant to ss. 120.536(1) and 120.54 to implement the provisions of this part related to home warranty associations and home warranties. The department shall administer this part and may adopt rules pursuant to ss. 120.536(1) and 120.54 to implement provisions of this part related to sales representatives. Such rules by the commission or department may identify specific methods of competition or acts or practices that are prohibited by s. 634.336 but shall not enlarge upon or extend the provisions of that section.
