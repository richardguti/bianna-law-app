# Fla. Stat. § 626.382 — Continuation, expiration of license; insurance agencies.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 626 — INSURANCE FIELD REPRESENTATIVES AND OPERATIONS
**Part:** I — INSURANCE REPRESENTATIVES: LICENSING PROCEDURES AND GENERAL REQUIREMENTS
**Section:** 626.382
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The license of an insurance agency shall continue in force until canceled, suspended, or revoked or until it is otherwise terminated or expires by operation of law.

**History:** ss. 182(2nd), 807, ch. 82-243; ss. 206, 207, ch. 90-363; s. 4, ch. 91-429l, any appointee to attend any training programs that are wholly or partially approved for general continuing education credit as provided in s. 626.2815.
