# Fla. Stat. § 634.4051 — Levy upon deposit limited.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 634 — WARRANTY ASSOCIATIONS
**Part:** III — SERVICE WARRANTY ASSOCIATIONS
**Section:** 634.4051
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

No judgment creditor or other claimant of a service warranty association shall have the right to levy upon any of the assets or securities held in this state as a deposit under s. 634.405.

**History:** ss. 38, 52, ch. 85-321; s. 20, ch. 93-195.
