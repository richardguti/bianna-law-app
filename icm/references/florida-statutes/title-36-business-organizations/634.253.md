# Fla. Stat. § 634.253 — Delinquency proceedings.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 634 — WARRANTY ASSOCIATIONS
**Part:** I — MOTOR VEHICLE SERVICE AGREEMENT COMPANIES
**Section:** 634.253
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
If any of the grounds for rehabilitation, liquidation, conservation, reorganization, seizure, or summary proceedings of an insurer as set forth in ss. 631.051, 631.061, and 631.071 exist as to a company, the office may petition for an appropriate court order or may pursue such other relief as is afforded in part I of chapter 631.

(2)  
In the event an order of rehabilitation, liquidation, conservation, reorganization, seizure, or summary proceedings has been entered against a company, the department and office shall be vested with all of the powers and duties they have under the provisions of part I of chapter 631 in regard to delinquency proceedings of insurance companies.

**History:** s. 12, ch. 78-231; s. 2, ch. 81-318; ss. 28, 32, 33, ch. 82-234; s. 68, ch. 91-106; s. 184, ch. 91-108; s. 20, ch. 93-195; s. 1441, ch. 2003-261.
