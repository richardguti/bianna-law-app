# Fla. Stat. § 620.2005 — Proceeds and expenses.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 620 — PARTNERSHIP LAWS
**Part:** I — FLORIDA REVISED UNIFORM LIMITED PARTNERSHIP ACT OF 2005
**Section:** 620.2005
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
Except as otherwise provided in subsection (2):
(a)  
Any proceeds or other benefits of a derivative action, whether by judgment, compromise, or settlement, belong to the limited partnership and not to the derivative plaintiff.

(b)  
If the derivative plaintiff receives any proceeds, the derivative plaintiff shall immediately remit such proceeds to the limited partnership.

(2)  
If a derivative action is successful in whole or in part, the court may award the plaintiff reasonable expenses, including reasonable attorney's fees, from the limited partnership.

**History:** s. 17, ch. 2005-267.
