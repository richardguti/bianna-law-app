# Fla. Stat. § 626.892 — Order of suspension or revocation of certificate of authority; notice.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 626 — INSURANCE FIELD REPRESENTATIVES AND OPERATIONS
**Part:** VII — INSURANCE ADMINISTRATORS
**Section:** 626.892
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
The suspension or revocation of a certificate of authority of an administrator shall be effected by order of the office mailed to the administrator by registered or certified mail.

(2)  
In its discretion, the office may cause notice of any such revocation or suspension to be published in one or more newspapers of general circulation published in this state.

**History:** s. 4, ch. 83-203; s. 3, ch. 84-94; s. 11, ch. 85-62; ss. 206, 207, ch. 90-363; s. 4, ch. 91-429; s. 1002, ch. 2003-261.
