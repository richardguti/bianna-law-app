# Fla. Stat. § 648.57 — Penalty.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 648 — BAIL BOND AGENTS
**Part:** III — HEALTH CARE SERVICES
**Section:** 648.57
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Any person or corporation, who is found guilty of violating any of the provisions of this chapter commits a misdemeanor of the second degree, punishable as provided in s. 775.082 or s. 775.083, unless a more severe criminal penalty is otherwise provided in this chapter with respect to the specific violation.

**History:** s. 22, ch. 29621, 1955; s. 177, ch. 70-339; s. 667, ch. 71-136; s. 3, ch. 76-168; s. 1, ch. 77-457; ss. 2, 3, ch. 81-318; ss. 71, 72, ch. 82-175; ss. 50, 51, ch. 84-103; s. 5, ch. 87-321; ss. 42, 46, 47, ch. 90-131; s. 4, ch. 91-429.

**Note:** Former s. 903.58.
