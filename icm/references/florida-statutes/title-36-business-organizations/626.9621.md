# Fla. Stat. § 626.9621 — Provisions of part additional to existing law.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 626 — INSURANCE FIELD REPRESENTATIVES AND OPERATIONS
**Part:** IX — UNFAIR INSURANCE TRADE PRACTICES
**Section:** 626.9621
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The powers vested in the department, commission, and office by this part shall be additional to any other powers to enforce any penalties, fines, or forfeitures authorized by law.

**History:** s. 9, ch. 76-260; s. 807, ch. 82-243; ss. 206, 207, ch. 90-363; s. 4, ch. 91-429; s. 1037, ch. 2003-261ules.
