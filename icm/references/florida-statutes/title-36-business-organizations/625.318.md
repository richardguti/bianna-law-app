# Fla. Stat. § 625.318 — Religious institution obligations.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 625 — ACCOUNTING, INVESTMENTS, AND DEPOSITS BY INSURERS
**Part:** II — INVESTMENTS
**Section:** 625.318
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

An insurer may invest in secured obligations of duly constituted churches and of church holding companies.

**History:** F.S. 625.3176625.317
