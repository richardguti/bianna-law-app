# Fla. Stat. § 616.255 — Duties of authority; Florida State Fairgrounds.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 616 — PUBLIC FAIRS AND EXPOSITIONS
**Part:** III — FLORIDA STATE FAIR AUTHORITY
**Section:** 616.255
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The authority shall:
(1)  
Designate a suitable location in Hillsborough County as the Florida State Fairgrounds.

(2)
