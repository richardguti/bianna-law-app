# Fla. Stat. § 625.60 — Levy upon deposit.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 625 — ACCOUNTING, INVESTMENTS, AND DEPOSITS BY INSURERS
**Part:** III — ADMINISTRATION OF DEPOSITS
**Section:** 625.60
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

So long as the insurer remains solvent and is in compliance with this code, it may:
(1)  
Demand, receive, sue for, and recover the income from the securities or assets deposited;

(2)

(3)
