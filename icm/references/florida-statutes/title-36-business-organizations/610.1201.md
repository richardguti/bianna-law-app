# Fla. Stat. § 610.1201 — Severability.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 610 — CABLE AND VIDEO SERVICES
**Part:** III — BENEFIT CORPORATIONS
**Section:** 610.1201
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

If any provision of ss. 610.102-610.118 or the application thereof to any person or circumstance is held invalid, such invalidity shall not affect other provisions or application of ss. 610.102-610.118 which can be given effect without the invalid provision or application, and to this end the provisions of ss. 610.102-610.118 are severable.

**History:** s. 7, ch. (c)
