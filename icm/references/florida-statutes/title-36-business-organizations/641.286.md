# Fla. Stat. § 641.286 — Levy upon deposit limited.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 641 — HEALTH CARE SERVICE PROGRAMS
**Part:** I — HEALTH MAINTENANCE ORGANIZATIONS
**Section:** 641.286
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

——

No judgment creditor or other claimant of a health maintevde a securities deposit may withdraw that deposit, or any part thereof, after making a substitute deposit of cash or eligible securities or any combination of these or other acceptable measures of equal amount and value.
