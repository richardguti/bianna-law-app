# Fla. Stat. § 620.2125 — Application of other laws to provisions governing conversions and mergers.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 620 — PARTNERSHIP LAWS
**Part:** I — FLORIDA REVISED UNIFORM LIMITED PARTNERSHIP ACT OF 2005
**Section:** 620.2125
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
The provisions of ss. 620.2101-620.2124 do not preclude an entity from being converted or merged under other law.

(2)  
The provisions of ss. 620.2101-620.2124 do not authorize any act prohibited by other applicable law or change the requirements of any law or rule regulating a specific organization or industry, such as a not-for-profit organization, insurance, banking or investment establishment, or other regulated business or activity.

**History:** s. 17, ch. 2005-267.
