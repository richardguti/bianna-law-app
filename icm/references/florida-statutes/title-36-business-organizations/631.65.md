# Fla. Stat. § 631.65 — Advertisement or solicitation.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 631 — INSURER INSOLVENCY; GUARANTY OF PAYMENT
**Part:** II — FLORIDA INSURANCE GUARANTY OF PAYMENTS
**Section:** 631.65
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

An advertisement or a solicitation that uses the existence of the association for the purpose of sales, solicitation, or inducement to purchase any form of insurance covered under this part must explain the coverage limits of the association set forth in s. 631.57(1) which apply to the type of insurance described in the advertisement or solicitation.

**History:** s. 16, ch. 70-20; s. 809(1st), ch. 82-243; ss. 187, 188, ch. 91-108; s. 4, ch. 91-429; s. 14, ch. 2009-87; s. 9, ch. 2015-135.
