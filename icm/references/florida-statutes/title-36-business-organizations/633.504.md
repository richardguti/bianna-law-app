# Fla. Stat. § 633.504 — Definitions.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 633 — FIRE PREVENTION AND CONTROL
**Part:** V — FLORIDA FIREFIGHTERS OCCUPATIONAL SAFETY AND HEALTH ACT
**Section:** 633.504
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

As used in this part, the term:
(1)  
“Firefighter employee” means a firefighter, volunteer firefighter, or individual providing support services who is engaged in any employment, public or private, under any appointment or contract of hire or apprenticeship, express or implied, oral or written, whether lawfully or unlawfully employed, responding to or assisting with fire or medical emergencies, regardless of whether on duty, except those appointed under s. 590.02(1)(d).
