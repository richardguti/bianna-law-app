# Fla. Stat. § 626.779 — “Life agent” defined.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 626 — INSURANCE FIELD REPRESENTATIVES AND OPERATIONS
**Part:** III — LIFE INSURANCE AGENTS
**Section:** 626.779
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

For the purposes of this part, a “life agent” is as defined in s. 626.015.
