# Fla. Stat. § 634.3073 — Acquisition.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 634 — WARRANTY ASSOCIATIONS
**Part:** II — HOME WARRANTY ASSOCIATIONS
**Section:** 634.3073
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Every home warranty association shall be subject to the provisions of s. 628.4615.
