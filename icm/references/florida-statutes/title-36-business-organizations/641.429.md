# Fla. Stat. § 641.429 — Acceptable payments to prepaid health clinic.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 641 — HEALTH CARE SERVICE PROGRAMS
**Part:** II — PREPAID HEALTH CLINICS
**Section:** 641.429
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Each prepaid health clinic may accept payments from governmental agencies, corporations, associations, groups, or individuals, which payments cover all or part of the cost of contracts entered into between the prepaid health clinic and its subscribers.

**History:** ss. 10, 11, ch. 84-313; ss. 187, 188, ch. 91-108; s. 4, ch. 91-429.
