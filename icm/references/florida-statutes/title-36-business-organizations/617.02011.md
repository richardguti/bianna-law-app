# Fla. Stat. § 617.02011 — Incorporators.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 617 — NONPROFIT CORPORATIONS
**Part:** III — FLORIDA STATE FAIR AUTHORITY
**Section:** 617.02011
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

One or more persons may act as the incorporator or incorporators of a corporation by delivering articles of incorporation to the department for filing.

**History:** s. 16, ch. 90-179; s. 129, ch. 2026-168.
