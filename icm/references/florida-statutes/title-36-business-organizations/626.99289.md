# Fla. Stat. § 626.99289 — Void and unenforceable contracts, agreements, arrangements, and transactions.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 626 — INSURANCE FIELD REPRESENTATIVES AND OPERATIONS
**Part:** X — VIATICAL SETTLEMENTS
**Section:** 626.99289
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Notwithstanding s. 627.455, a contract, agreement, arrangement, or transaction, including, but not limited to, a financing agreement or any other arrangement or understanding entered into, whether written or verbal, for the furtherance or aid of a stranger-originated life insurance practice is void and unenforceable.
