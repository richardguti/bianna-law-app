# Fla. Stat. § 607.0130 — Powers of department.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 607 — FLORIDA BUSINESS CORPORATION ACT
**Part:** I — GENERAL PROVISIONS
**Section:** 607.0130
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The department has the authority reasonably necessary to enable it to administer this chapter efficiently, to perform the duties imposed upon it, and to adopt reasonable rules necessary to carry out its duties and functions under this chapter.

**History:** s. 13, ch. 89-154; s. 4, ch. 91-214; s. 3, ch. 97-102; s. 5, ch. 2006-85; s. 131, ch. 2007-5; s. 12, ch. 2019-90.
