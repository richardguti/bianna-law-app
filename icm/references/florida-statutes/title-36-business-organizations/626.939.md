# Fla. Stat. § 626.939 — Records produced on order.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 626 — INSURANCE FIELD REPRESENTATIVES AND OPERATIONS
**Part:** VIII — UNAUTHORIZED INSURERS AND SURPLUS LINES
**Section:** 626.939
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
Every person by or as to whom insurance is procured or placed in an unauthorized insurer, upon the order of the department, shall produce for examination by the department, or by the authorized representative of the department, all policies and other documents evidencing the insurance and shall disclose to the department the amount of gross premiums paid or agreed to be paid for the insurance. For each refusal to obey such order, such person, upon conviction thereof, shall be liable to a fine of not more than $500.

(2)  
This section does not apply to life insurance or health insurance.
