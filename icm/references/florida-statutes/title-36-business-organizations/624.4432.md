# Fla. Stat. § 624.4432 — Assets, liabilities, and investments.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 624 — INSURANCE CODE: ADMINISTRATION AND GENERAL PROVISIONS
**Part:** III — AUTHORIZATION OF INSURERS AND GENERAL REQUIREMENTS
**Section:** 624.4432
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Each arrangement that obtains or maintains a certificate of authority is subject to parts I and II of chapter 625 as they apply to domestic insurers.

**History:** ss. 37, 188, ch. 91-108; s. 4, ch. 91-429.
