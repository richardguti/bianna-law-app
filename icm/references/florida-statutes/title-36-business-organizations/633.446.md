# Fla. Stat. § 633.446 — Fees.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 633 — FIRE PREVENTION AND CONTROL
**Part:** IV — FIRE STANDARDS AND TRAINING
**Section:** 633.446
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The division may fix and collect admission fees and other fees which it deems necessary to be charged for training given. All fees so collected shall be deposited in the Insurance Regulatory Trust Fund.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 90-189; s. 15, ch. 99-205; s. 1035, ch. 2002-387; s. 1410, ch. 2003-261; s. 73, ch. 2013-183; s. 48, ch. 2018-7; s. 44, ch. 2018-102.

**Note:** Former s. 633.50.
