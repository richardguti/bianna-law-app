# Fla. Stat. § 617.1510 — Serving process, giving notice, or making a demand on a foreign corporation.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 617 — NONPROFIT CORPORATIONS
**Part:** III — FLORIDA STATE FAIR AUTHORITY
**Section:** 617.1510
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
Process against a foreign corporation may be served in accordance with s. 48.081 and chapter 48 or chapter 49.

(2)  
Any notice to or demand on a foreign corporation made pursuant to this chapter may be made in accordance with the procedures for notice to or demand on domestic corporations under s. 617.0504.

**History:** s. 100, ch. 90-179; s. 28, ch. 2022-190; s. 145, ch. 2026-168.
