# Fla. Stat. § 627.911 — Scope of this part.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 627 — INSURANCE RATES AND CONTRACTS
**Part:** XVII — INSURER REPORTING
**Section:** 627.911
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Any insurer transacting insurance in this state shall report information as required by this part.

**History:** ss. 618, 809(2nd), ch. 82-243; s. 79, ch. 82-386; s. 114, ch. 92-318.
