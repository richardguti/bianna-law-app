# Fla. Stat. § 636.207 — Applicability of part.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 636 — PREPAID LIMITED HEALTH SERVICE ORGANIZATIONS AND DISCOUNT PLAN ORGANIZATIONS
**Part:** II — DISCOUNT PLAN ORGANIZATIONS
**Section:** 636.207
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Except as otherwise provided in this part, discount plan organizations are governed by this part and are exempt from the Florida Insurance Code unless specifically referenced.

**History:** s. 5, ch. 2005-232; s. 18, ch. 2017-112.
