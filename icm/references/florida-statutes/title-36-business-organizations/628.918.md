# Fla. Stat. § 628.918 — Management of assets of captive reinsurance company.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 628 — STOCK AND MUTUAL INSURERS; HOLDING COMPANIES
**Part:** V — CAPTIVE INSURERS
**Section:** 628.918
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

At least 35 percent of the assets of a captive reinsurance company must be managed by an asset manager domiciled in this state.

**History:** s. 32, ch. 2012-151.
