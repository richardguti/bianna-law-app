# Fla. Stat. § 624.6012 — “Lines of insurance” defined.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 624 — INSURANCE CODE: ADMINISTRATION AND GENERAL PROVISIONS
**Part:** V — KINDS OF INSURANCE; LIMITS OF RISK; REINSURANCE
**Section:** 624.6012
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Kinds of insurance shall be classified into “lines of insurance.” The commission shall adopt by rule the lines of insurance to be utilized. Such lines of insurance shall be consistent with the reporting requirements of the National Association of Insurance Commissioners.

**History:** ss. 39, 51, 70, ch. 89-360; ss. 187, 188, ch. 91-108; s. 4, ch. 91-429; s. 851, ch. 2003-261.
