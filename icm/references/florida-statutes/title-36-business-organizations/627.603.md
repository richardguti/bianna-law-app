# Fla. Stat. § 627.603 — Death benefits.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 627 — INSURANCE RATES AND CONTRACTS
**Part:** VI — HEALTH INSURANCE POLICIES
**Section:** 627.603
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Any health insurance policy may contain a provision for paying a benefit for death from any cause in an amount not exceeding $1,000, which benefit shall not relieve such policy from the requirements of this chapter. This provision shall not limit benefits for death by accident.

**History:** s. 546, ch. 59-205; s. 3, ch. 76-168; s. 1, ch. 77-457; ss. 2, 3, ch. 81-318; ss. 451, 497, 809(2nd), ch. 82-243; s. 79, ch. 82-386; s. 114, ch. 92-318.
