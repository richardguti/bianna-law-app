# Fla. Stat. § 631.395 — F.S. 631.3926631.392

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 631 — INSURER INSOLVENCY; GUARANTY OF PAYMENT
**Part:** I — INSURER INSOLVENCY: REHABILITATION AND LIQUIDATION
**Section:** 631.395
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

There shall be no liability on the part of, and no cause of action of any nature shall arise against, the Chief Financial Officer, the department, the office, or any of their employees or agents for any action taken by them in the performance of their powers and duties under this chapter.

**History:** ss. 42, 66, ch. 88-166; ss. 187, 188, ch. 91-108; s. 4, ch. 91-429; s. 1350, ch. 2003-261.
