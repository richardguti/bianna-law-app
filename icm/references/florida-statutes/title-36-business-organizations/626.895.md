# Fla. Stat. § 626.895 — Definition of “service company” or “service agent.”

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 626 — INSURANCE FIELD REPRESENTATIVES AND OPERATIONS
**Part:** VII — INSURANCE ADMINISTRATORS
**Section:** 626.895
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

——

For the purpose of this part, a “service company” is any business entity which has met all the requirements of ss. 626.895-626.899, which does not control funds, and which has obtained office approval to contract with self-insurers or multiple-employer welfare arrangements for the purpose of providing all or any part of the services necessary to establish and maintain a multiple-employer welfare arrangement as defined in s. 624.437(1).
