# Fla. Stat. § 607.0102 — Reservation of power to amend or repeal.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 607 — FLORIDA BUSINESS CORPORATION ACT
**Part:** I — GENERAL PROVISIONS
**Section:** 607.0102
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The Legislature has power to amend or repeal all or part of this chapter at any time, and all domestic and foreign corporations subject to this chapter shall be governed by the amendment or repeal.

**History:** s. 2, ch. 89-154; s. 2, ch. 2019-90.
