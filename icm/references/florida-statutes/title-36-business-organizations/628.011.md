# Fla. Stat. § 628.011 — Scope of part.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 627 — INSURANCE RATES AND CONTRACTS
**Part:** XXII — COLLATERAL PROTECTION INSURANCE
**Section:** 628.011
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

This part applies only to domestic insurers, mutual insurers, and captive insurers, except that s. 628.341(2) applies also as to foreign and alien insurers.
