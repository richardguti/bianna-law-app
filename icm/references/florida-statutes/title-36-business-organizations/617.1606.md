# Fla. Stat. § 617.1606 — Access to records.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 617 — NONPROFIT CORPORATIONS
**Part:** III — FLORIDA STATE FAIR AUTHORITY
**Section:** 617.1606
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Sections 617.1601-617.16051 do not apply to a corporation that is an association, as defined in s. 720.301, or a corporation regulated under chapter 718 or chapter 719.

**History:** s. 4, ch. 2010-174; s. 146, ch. 2026-168.
