# Fla. Stat. § 627.261 — Actuarial and technical services.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 627 — INSURANCE RATES AND CONTRACTS
**Part:** I — RATES AND RATING ORGANIZATIONS
**Section:** 627.261
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Any rating organization may subscribe for or purchase actuarial, technical, or other services; and such services shall be available to all members and subscribers without discrimination.

**History:** s. 436, ch. 59-205; s. 3, ch. 76-168; s. 1, ch. 77-457; ss. 2, 3, ch. 81-318; ss. 357, 809(2nd), ch. 82-243; ss. 49, 79, ch. 82-386; s. 114, ch. 92-318.
