# Fla. Stat. § 625.320 — Building and loan or savings and loan association accounts.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 625 — ACCOUNTING, INVESTMENTS, AND DEPOSITS BY INSURERS
**Part:** II — INVESTMENTS
**Section:** 625.320
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

An insurer may invest in share or saving accounts of savings and loan associations or building and loan associations.

**History:** s. 145, ch. 59-205; s. 3, ch. 76-168; s. 1, ch. 77-457; s. 1, ch. 78-64; ss. 2, 3, ch. 81-318; ss. 122, 809(1st), ch. 82-243; ss. 187, 188, ch. 91-108; s. 4, ch. 91-429.
