# Fla. Stat. § 621.051 — Limited liability company organization.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 621 — PROFESSIONAL SERVICE CORPORATIONS AND LIMITED LIABILITY COMPANIES
**Part:** II — REVISED UNIFORM PARTNERSHIP ACT
**Section:** 621.051
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

A group of professional service corporations, professional limited liability companies, or individuals, in any combination, duly licensed or otherwise legally authorized to render the same professional services may organize and become members of a professional limited liability company for pecuniary profit under the provisions of chapter 605 for the sole and specific purpose of rendering the same and specific professional service.

**History:** s. 6, ch. 93-110; s. 79, ch. 93-284; ss. 22, 23, ch. 2013-180.
