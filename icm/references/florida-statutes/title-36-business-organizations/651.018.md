# Fla. Stat. § 651.018 — Administrative supervision.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 651 — CONTINUING CARE CONTRACTS
**Part:** III — HEALTH CARE SERVICES
**Section:** 651.018
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The office may place a facility in administrative supervision pursuant to part VI of chapter 624.

**History:** ss. 2, 16, ch. 91-98; s. 12, ch. 93-22; s. 1667, ch. 2003-261.
