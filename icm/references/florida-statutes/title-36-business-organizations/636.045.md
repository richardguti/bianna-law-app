# Fla. Stat. § 636.045 — Minimum surplus requirements.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 636 — PREPAID LIMITED HEALTH SERVICE ORGANIZATIONS AND DISCOUNT PLAN ORGANIZATIONS
**Part:** I — PREPAID LIMITED HEALTH SERVICE ORGANIZATIONS
**Section:** 636.045
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
Each prepaid limited health service organization must at all times maintain a minimum surplus in an amount which is the greater of $150,000 or 10 percent of total liabilities.

(2)  
The office may not issue a certificate of authority unless the prepaid limited health service organization has a minimum surplus in an amount of $150,000 or 10 percent of liabilities, whichever is the greater amount.

(3)  
A prepaid limited health service organization that is authorized in this state and one or more other states, jurisdictions, or countries is subject to ss. 624.4085 and 624.40851.

**History:** s. 36, ch. 93-148; s. 21, ch. 2003vons of this chapter.
