# Fla. Stat. § 636.232 — Rules.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 636 — PREPAID LIMITED HEALTH SERVICE ORGANIZATIONS AND DISCOUNT PLAN ORGANIZATIONS
**Part:** II — DISCOUNT PLAN ORGANIZATIONS
**Section:** 636.232
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The commission may adopt rules to administer this part, including rules for the licensing of discount plan organizations, providing for the collection of data, relating to disclosures to plan members, and defining terms used in this part.

**History:** s. 31, ch. 2004-297; s. 12, ch. 2017-112.
