# Fla. Stat. § 627.7019 — Standardization of requirements applicable to insurers after natural disasters.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 627 — INSURANCE RATES AND CONTRACTS
**Part:** X — PROPERTY INSURANCE CONTRACTS
**Section:** 627.7019
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
The commission shall adopt by rule, pursuant to s. 120.54(1)-(3), standardized requirements that may be applied to insurers as a consequence of a hurricane or other natural disaster. The rules shall address the following areas:
(a)  
Claims reporting requirements.

(b)  
Grace periods for payment of premiums and performance of other duties by insureds.

(c)  
Temporary postponement of cancellations and nonrenewals.

(2)  
F.S. 627.70186627.7018  
Standards for determining risk of coverage.
——

In determining the risk of providing property insurance coverage, an insurer may not deny coverage solely on the basis of the age of the structure and shall consider the wind resistance of the structure and measures undertaken by the owner to protect the structure against hurricane loss.

**History:** s. 29, ch. 2007-1.
