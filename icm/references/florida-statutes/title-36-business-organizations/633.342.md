# Fla. Stat. § 633.342 — Violations subject to injunction.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 633 — FIRE PREVENTION AND CONTROL
**Part:** III — FIRE PROTECTION AND SUPPRESSION
**Section:** 633.342
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

——

A person who operates as a contractor without a current certificate or who violates any part of this chapter or any rule, decision, order, direction, demand, or requiu of a composition of creditors or the appointment of a receiver for the business of the certificateholder may be considered by the State Fire Marshal as just cause for suspension of a certificate.
