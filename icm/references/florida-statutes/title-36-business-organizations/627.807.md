# Fla. Stat. § 627.807 — Variable or indeterminate value contract reserve requirements.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 627 — INSURANCE RATES AND CONTRACTS
**Part:** XIII — TITLE INSURANCE CONTRACTS
**Section:** 627.807
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The reserve liability for variable or indeterminate value contracts shall be established in accordance with actuarial procedures that recognize the variable nature of the benefits, values, or premiums provided and any mortality guarantees.

**History:** s. 2, ch. 73-30; s. 3, ch. 76-168; s. 1, ch. 77-457; ss. 2, 3, ch. 81-318; ss. 592, 593, 809(2nd), ch. 82-243; s. 79, ch. 82-386; s. 114, ch. 92-318.
