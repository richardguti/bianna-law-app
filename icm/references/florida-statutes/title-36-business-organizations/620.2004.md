# Fla. Stat. § 620.2004 — Pleading.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 620 — PARTNERSHIP LAWS
**Part:** I — FLORIDA REVISED UNIFORM LIMITED PARTNERSHIP ACT OF 2005
**Section:** 620.2004
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

In a derivative action, the complaint must state with particularity:
(1)  
The date and content of plaintiff's demand and the general partners' response to the demand; or

(2)  
Why demand should be excused as futile.

**History:** s. 17, ch. 2005-267.
