# Fla. Stat. § 636.057 — Fees.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 636 — PREPAID LIMITED HEALTH SERVICE ORGANIZATIONS AND DISCOUNT PLAN ORGANIZATIONS
**Part:** I — PREPAID LIMITED HEALTH SERVICE ORGANIZATIONS
**Section:** 636.057
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Every prepaid limited health service organization subject to this act must pay to the office the following fees:
(1)  
For filing an application for a certificate of authority or amendment thereto: $500.

(2)  
For filing each annual report: $200.

(3)  
For each renewal of certificate of authority: $500.

**History:** s. 45, ch. 93-148; s. 1547, ch. 2003-261.
