# Fla. Stat. § 641.32 — Acceptable payments.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 641 — HEALTH CARE SERVICE PROGRAMS
**Part:** I — HEALTH MAINTENANCE ORGANIZATIONS
**Section:** 641.32
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Each health maintenance organization may accept from governmental agencies, corporations, associations, groups, or individuals payments covering all or part of the cost of contracts entered into between the health maintenance organization and its subscribers.

**History:** s. 16, ch. 72-264; s. 3, ch. 76-168; s. 1, ch. 77-457; ss. 2, 3, ch. 81-318; ss. 796, 804, 809(1st), ch. 82-243; ss. 187, 188, ch. 91-108; s. 4, ch. 91-429.
