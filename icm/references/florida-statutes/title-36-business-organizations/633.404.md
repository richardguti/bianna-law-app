# Fla. Stat. § 633.404 — Additional standards authorized.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 633 — FIRE PREVENTION AND CONTROL
**Part:** IV — FIRE STANDARDS AND TRAINING
**Section:** 633.404
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Nothing herein shall be construed to preclude a fire service provider from establishing qualifications and standards for hiring, training, or promoting firefighters that exceed the minimum set by the division.

**History:** ss. 16, 17, ch. 69-323; s. 1, ch. 75-151; s. 57, ch. 2013-183.

**Note:** Former s. 163.530; s. 633.42.
