# Fla. Stat. § 627.746 — Coverage for minors who have a learner's driver license; additional premium prohibited.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 627 — INSURANCE RATES AND CONTRACTS
**Part:** XI — MOTOR VEHICLE AND CASUALTY INSURANCE CONTRACTS
**Section:** 627.746
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

An insurer that issues an insurance policy on a private passenger motor vehicle to a named insured who is a caregiver of a minor who is under the age of 18 years and is in out-of-home care as defined in s. 39.01 may not charge an additional premium for coverage of the minor while the minor is operating the insured vehicle, for the period of time that the minor has a learner's driver license, until such time as the minor obtains a driver license.

**History:** s. 2, ch. 2001-83; s. 27, ch. 2018-103; s. 97, ch. 2019-3; s. 56, ch. 2024-70.
