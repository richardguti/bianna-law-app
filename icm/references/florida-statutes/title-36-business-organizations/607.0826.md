# Fla. Stat. § 607.0826 — Submission of matters for a shareholder vote.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 607 — FLORIDA BUSINESS CORPORATION ACT
**Part:** I — GENERAL PROVISIONS
**Section:** 607.0826
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

A corporation may agree to submit a matter to a vote of its shareholders even if, after approving the matter, the board of directors determines it no longer recommends the matter.

**History:** s. 98, ch. 2019-90.
