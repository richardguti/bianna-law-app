# Fla. Stat. § 631.903 — Construction.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 631 — INSURER INSOLVENCY; GUARANTY OF PAYMENT
**Part:** V — FLORIDA WORKERS’ COMPENSATION INSURANCE GUARANTY ASSOCIATION
**Section:** 631.903
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The statutes controlling the corporation shall be construed liberally to achieve the purposes stated in s. 631.902. The corporation shall perform its functions under a plan of operation established by its board of directors and approved by the department.

**History:** s. 20, ch. 97-262.
