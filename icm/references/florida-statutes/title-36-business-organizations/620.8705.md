# Fla. Stat. § 620.8705 — Continued use of partnership name.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 620 — PARTNERSHIP LAWS
**Part:** II — REVISED UNIFORM PARTNERSHIP ACT
**Section:** 620.8705
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Continued use of a partnership name, or a dissociated partner's name as part thereof, by partners continuing the business does not, by itself, make the dissociated partner liable for an obligation of the partners or the partnership continuing the business.

**History:** s. 13, ch. 95-242.
