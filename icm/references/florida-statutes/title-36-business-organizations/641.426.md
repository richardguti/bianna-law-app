# Fla. Stat. § 641.426 — Delivery of contract to subscriber.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 641 — HEALTH CARE SERVICE PROGRAMS
**Part:** II — PREPAID HEALTH CLINICS
**Section:** 641.426
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Unless delivered upon execution or issuance, every prepaid health clinic contract shall be mailed or delivered to the subscriber within 10 days of its execution or issuance, except when a condition required by the clinic has not been met by the applicant or subscriber.

**History:** ss. 10, 11, ch. 84-313; ss. 187, 188, ch. 91-108; s. 4, ch. 91-429.
