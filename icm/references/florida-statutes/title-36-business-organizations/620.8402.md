# Fla. Stat. § 620.8402 — Distributions in kind.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 620 — PARTNERSHIP LAWS
**Part:** II — REVISED UNIFORM PARTNERSHIP ACT
**Section:** 620.8402
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

A partner has no right to receive, and may not be required to accept, a distribution in kind.

**History:** s. 13, ch. 95-242.
