# Fla. Stat. § 617.0747 — Applicability to foreign corporations.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 617 — NONPROFIT CORPORATIONS
**Part:** III — FLORIDA STATE FAIR AUTHORITY
**Section:** 617.0747
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

In any derivative proceeding in the right of a foreign corporation brought in the courts of this state, the matters covered by ss. 617.0741-617.0747 are governed by the laws of the jurisdiction of incorporation of the foreign corporation, except for ss. 617.0743, 617.0745, and 617.0746.

**History:** s. 40, ch. 2026-168.
