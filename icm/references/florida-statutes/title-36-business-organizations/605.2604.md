# Fla. Stat. § 605.2604 — Restrictions on merger.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 605 — FLORIDA REVISED LIMITED LIABILITY COMPANY ACT
**Part:** II — FLORIDA TROPICAL FRUIT POLICY ACT
**Section:** 605.2604
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

A series limited liability company may be a party to a merger in accordance with ss. 605.1021-605.1026, this section, and ss. 605.2605-605.2608 only if both of the following apply:
(1)  
Each other party to the merger is a limited liability company.

(2)  
The surviving company is not created in the merger.

**History:** s. 34, ch. 2025-162.
