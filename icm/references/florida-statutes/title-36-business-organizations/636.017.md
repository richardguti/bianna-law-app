# Fla. Stat. § 636.017 — Rates and charges.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 636 — PREPAID LIMITED HEALTH SERVICE ORGANIZATIONS AND DISCOUNT PLAN ORGANIZATIONS
**Part:** I — PREPAID LIMITED HEALTH SERVICE ORGANIZATIONS
**Section:** 636.017
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
The rates charged by any prepaid limited health service organization to its subscribers shall not be excessive, inadequate, or unfairly discriminatory. The commission or office may require whatever information it deems necessary to determine that a rate or proposed rate meets the requirements of this section.

**History:** s. 13, ch. 93-148; s. 2, ch. 97-65; s. 485, ch. 97-102; s. 1, ch. 98-156; s. 1529, ch. 2003-261.
