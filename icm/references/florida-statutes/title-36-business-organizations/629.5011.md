# Fla. Stat. § 629.5011 — Tax on premiums.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 629 — RECIPROCAL INSURERS
**Part:** V — CAPTIVE INSURERS
**Section:** 629.5011
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Premiums and assessments received by reciprocal insurers are subject to any premium tax provided for in s. 624.509.

**History:** s. 7, ch. 88-206; s. 188, ch. 91-108; s. 4, ch. 91-429.
