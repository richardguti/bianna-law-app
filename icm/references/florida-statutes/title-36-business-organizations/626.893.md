# Fla. Stat. § 626.893 — Period of suspension; obligations during suspension; reinstatement.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 626 — INSURANCE FIELD REPRESENTATIVES AND OPERATIONS
**Part:** VII — INSURANCE ADMINISTRATORS
**Section:** 626.893
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
A certificate of authority of an administrator shall be suspended for such period, not to exceed 1 year, as is fixed in the order of suspension, unless such suspension or the order upon which the suspension is based is modified, rescinded, or reversed.

(2)  
During the period of suspension, the administrator shall file its annual statement and pay fees as required under this part as if the certificate had continued in full force.

(3)  
Upon expiration of the suspension period, if within such period the certificate has not otherwise terminated, the certificate shall automatically be reinstated, unless the causes of the suspension have not been removed or the administrator is otherwise not in compliance with the requirements of this part.

**History:** s. 4, ch. 83-203; s. 3, ch. 84-94; ss. 206, 207, ch. 90-363; s. 4, ch. 91-429.
