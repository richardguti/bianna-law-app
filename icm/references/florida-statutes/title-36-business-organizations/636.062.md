# Fla. Stat. § 636.062 — Appeals from the office or department.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 636 — PREPAID LIMITED HEALTH SERVICE ORGANIZATIONS AND DISCOUNT PLAN ORGANIZATIONS
**Part:** I — PREPAID LIMITED HEALTH SERVICE ORGANIZATIONS
**Section:** 636.062
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Any person, entity, or prepaid limited health service organization subject to an order of the office or department under s. 641.3909 or s. 641.3913 may obtain a review of the order by filing an appeal therefrom in accordance with the provisions and procedures for appeal under s. 120.68.

**History:** s. 49, ch. 93-148; s. 1550, ch. 2003-261.
