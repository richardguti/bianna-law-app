# Fla. Stat. § 636.216 — Written agreement.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 636 — PREPAID LIMITED HEALTH SERVICE ORGANIZATIONS AND DISCOUNT PLAN ORGANIZATIONS
**Part:** II — DISCOUNT PLAN ORGANIZATIONS
**Section:** 636.216
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

There must be a written agreement between the discount plan organization and the member specifying the benefits under the discount plan and complying with the disclosure requirements of this part.

**History:** s. 31, ch. 2004-297; s. 10, ch. 2005-232; s. 9, ch. 2017-112.
