# Fla. Stat. § 627.6844 — Replacement rules.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 627 — INSURANCE RATES AND CONTRACTS
**Part:** IX — CREDIT LIFE AND DISABILITY INSURANCES
**Section:** 627.6844
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Group-to-group consolidations are exempt from any rule of the commission relating to the replacement of existing life or health insurance. Sections 627.6841-627.6845 do not create an exemption from any such rule for consolidations that involve individual policies.

**History:** s. 76, ch. 92-318; s. 1181, ch. 2003-261.
