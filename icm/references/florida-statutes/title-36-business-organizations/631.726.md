# Fla. Stat. § 631.726 — Tax exemptions.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 631 — INSURER INSOLVENCY; GUARANTY OF PAYMENT
**Part:** III — LIFE AND HEALTH INSURANCE GUARANTY OF PAYMENTS
**Section:** 631.726
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The association shall be exempt from payment of all fees and all taxes levied by this state or any of its subdivisions, except taxes levied on real property.

**History:** s. 15, ch. 79-189; s. 809(1st), ch. 82-243; ss. 187, 188, ch. 91-108; s. 4, ch. 91-429.
