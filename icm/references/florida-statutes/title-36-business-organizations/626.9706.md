# Fla. Stat. § 626.9706 — Life insurance; discrimination on basis of sickle-cell trait prohibited.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 626 — INSURANCE FIELD REPRESENTATIVES AND OPERATIONS
**Part:** IX — UNFAIR INSURANCE TRADE PRACTICES
**Section:** 626.9706
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
No insurer authorized to transact insurance in this state shall refuse to issue and deliver any policy of life insurance solely because the person to be insured has the sickle-cell trait.

(2)  
No life insurance policy issued and delivered in this state shall carry a higher premium rate or charge solely because the person to be insured has the sickle-cell trait.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 78-35; s. 807, ch. 82-243; ss. 206, 207, ch. 90-363; s. 4, ch. 91-429.
