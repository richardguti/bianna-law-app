# Fla. Stat. § 636.059 — Unfair methods of competition, unfair or deceptive acts or practices defined.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 636 — PREPAID LIMITED HEALTH SERVICE ORGANIZATIONS AND DISCOUNT PLAN ORGANIZATIONS
**Part:** I — PREPAID LIMITED HEALTH SERVICE ORGANIZATIONS
**Section:** 636.059
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

For the purposes of defining unfair methods of competition, unfair or deceptive acts or practices, the provisions of s. 641.3903 apply to a prepaid limited health service organization.

**History:** s. 48, ch. 93-148.
