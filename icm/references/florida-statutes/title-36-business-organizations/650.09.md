# Fla. Stat. § 650.09 — Liberal construction.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 650 — SOCIAL SECURITY FOR PUBLIC EMPLOYEES
**Part:** III — HEALTH CARE SERVICES
**Section:** 650.09
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The provisions of this chapter shall be liberally construed in order to effectively carry out the purposes of the chapter as set forth in the declaration of public policy.

**History:** s. 9, ch. 26841, 1951.
