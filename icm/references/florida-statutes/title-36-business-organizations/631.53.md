# Fla. Stat. § 631.53 — Construction.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 631 — INSURER INSOLVENCY; GUARANTY OF PAYMENT
**Part:** II — FLORIDA INSURANCE GUARANTY OF PAYMENTS
**Section:** 631.53
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

This part shall be liberally construed to effect the purposes set forth in s. 631.51, which shall constitute an aid and guide to interpretation.

**History:** s. 4, ch. 70-20; s. 809(1st), ch. 82-243; ss. 187, 188, ch. 91-108; s. 4, ch. 91-429.
