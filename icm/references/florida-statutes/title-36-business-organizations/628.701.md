# Fla. Stat. § 628.701 — Scope.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 628 — STOCK AND MUTUAL INSURERS; HOLDING COMPANIES
**Part:** III — MUTUAL INSURANCE HOLDING COMPANIES
**Section:** 628.701
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

This part applies only to domestic mutual insurance holding companies.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 97-216.
