# Fla. Stat. § 607.503 — Incorporation.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 607 — FLORIDA BUSINESS CORPORATION ACT
**Part:** II — SOCIAL PURPOSE CORPORATIONS
**Section:** 607.503
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

To incorporate as a social purpose corporation, an incorporator must satisfy the requirements of this chapter, and the articles of incorporation must state that the corporation is a social purpose corporation under this part.

**History:** s. 9, ch. 2014-209.
