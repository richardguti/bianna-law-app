# Fla. Stat. § 617.1808 — Application of chapter to corporation converted to nonprofit corporation.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 617 — NONPROFIT CORPORATIONS
**Part:** III — FLORIDA STATE FAIR AUTHORITY
**Section:** 617.1808
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

This chapter relating to corporations, except insofar as they are inconsistent with ss. 617.1804-617.18046, applies to any for profit corporation whose character has been changed under ss. 617.1804-617.18046 and shall henceforth govern such corporation.

**History:** s. 126, ch. 90-179; s. 8, ch. 95-211; s. 152, ch. 2026-168.
