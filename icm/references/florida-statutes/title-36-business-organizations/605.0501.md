# Fla. Stat. § 605.0501 — Nature of transferable interest.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 605 — FLORIDA REVISED LIMITED LIABILITY COMPANY ACT
**Part:** II — FLORIDA TROPICAL FRUIT POLICY ACT
**Section:** 605.0501
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

A transferable interest is personal property.
