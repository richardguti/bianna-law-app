# Fla. Stat. § 627.941 — Purpose.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 627 — INSURANCE RATES AND CONTRACTS
**Part:** XIX — PURCHASING GROUPS AND RISK RETENTION GROUPS
**Section:** 627.941
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The purpose of this part is to regulate the formation or operation of risk retention groups and purchasing groups in this state.

**History:** ss. 2, 5, ch. 87-282; s. 114, ch. 92-318.
