# Fla. Stat. § 641.261 — Other reporting requirements.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 641 — HEALTH CARE SERVICE PROGRAMS
**Part:** I — HEALTH MAINTENANCE ORGANIZATIONS
**Section:** 641.261
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

——

(1)  
Each authorized health maintenance organization shall provide records and information to the Agency for Health Care Administration pursuant to s. 409.910(20) and (21) for the sole purpose of identifying potential coverage for claims filed with the agency and its fiscal agents for payment of medical services under the Medicaid program.
