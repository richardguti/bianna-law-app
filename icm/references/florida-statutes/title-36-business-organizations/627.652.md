# Fla. Stat. § 627.652 — Group health insurance; definitions.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 627 — INSURANCE RATES AND CONTRACTS
**Part:** VII — GROUP, BLANKET, AND FRANCHISE HEALTH INSURANCE POLICIES
**Section:** 627.652
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

——

As used in this part:
(1)  
Group health insurance is that form of health insurance covering groups of persons under a master group health insurance policy ip; s. 27, ch. 95-211; s. 348, ch. 97-102.
