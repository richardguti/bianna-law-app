# Fla. Stat. § 627.1615 — Workers' compensation applicant discrimination.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 627 — INSURANCE RATES AND CONTRACTS
**Part:** I — RATES AND RATING ORGANIZATIONS
**Section:** 627.1615
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Insurers shall not refuse to provide workers' compensation coverage on the basis of the applicant's premium volume.

**History:** s. 52, ch. 90-201; s. 50, ch. 91-1; s. 17, ch. 91-201; s. 4, ch. 91-429.
