# Fla. Stat. § 617.0204 — Liability for preincorporation transactions.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 617 — NONPROFIT CORPORATIONS
**Part:** III — FLORIDA STATE FAIR AUTHORITY
**Section:** 617.0204
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

All persons purporting to act as or on behalf of a corporation, knowing that there was no incorporation under this chapter, are jointly and severally liable for all liabilities created while so acting.
