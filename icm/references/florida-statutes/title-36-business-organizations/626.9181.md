# Fla. Stat. § 626.9181 — Levy upon deposit.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 626 — INSURANCE FIELD REPRESENTATIVES AND OPERATIONS
**Part:** VIII — UNAUTHORIZED INSURERS AND SURPLUS LINES
**Section:** 626.9181
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

No judgment creditor or other claimant of a surplus lines insurer shall have the right to levy upon any of the assets or securities held in this state as a deposit under s. 626.918.
