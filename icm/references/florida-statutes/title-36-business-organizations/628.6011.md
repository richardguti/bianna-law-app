# Fla. Stat. § 628.6011 — Assessable mutual insurers.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS

**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

——

(1)  
An “assessable mutual insurer” is an insurer incorporated in Florida without permanent capital stock which has only policyholders, insureds, or risks located in Florida and which transacts insurance only within Florida.
