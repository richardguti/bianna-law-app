# Fla. Stat. § 641.29 — Fees.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 641 — HEALTH CARE SERVICE PROGRAMS
**Part:** I — HEALTH MAINTENANCE ORGANIZATIONS
**Section:** 641.29
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Every health maintenance organization shall pay to the office the following fees:
(1)  
For filing a copy of its application for a certificate of authority or amendment thereto, a nonrefundable fee in the amount of $1,000.

(2)  
For filing each annual report, which must be filed by electronic means in a computer-readable form, $150.
