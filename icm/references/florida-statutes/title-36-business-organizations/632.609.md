# Fla. Stat. § 632.609 — Waiver prohibited.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 632 — FRATERNAL BENEFIT SOCIETIES
**Part:** V — FLORIDA WORKERS’ COMPENSATION INSURANCE GUARANTY ASSOCIATION
**Section:** 632.609
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The laws of the society shall provide that no subordinate body, nor any of its subordinate officers or members, shall have the power or authority to waive any of the provisions of the laws or rules of the society. Such provision shall be binding on the society and every member and beneficiary of a member.

**History:** ss. 4, 6, ch. 86-140; s. 4, ch. 91-429.
