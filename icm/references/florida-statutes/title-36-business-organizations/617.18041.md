# Fla. Stat. § 617.18041 — Limitation on conversion.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 617 — NONPROFIT CORPORATIONS
**Part:** III — FLORIDA STATE FAIR AUTHORITY
**Section:** 617.18041
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

A domestic corporation that holds property for a charitable purpose is prohibited from becoming a domestic eligible entity or a foreign eligible entity, except by domestication to become a foreign corporation.

**History:** s. 114, ch. 2026-168.
