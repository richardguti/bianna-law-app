# Fla. Stat. § 629.041 — Insuring powers of reciprocals.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 629 — RECIPROCAL INSURERS
**Part:** V — CAPTIVE INSURERS
**Section:** 629.041
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
A reciprocal insurer may transact any kind of insurance other than life or title.

(2)  
Such an insurer may purchase reinsurance, and may grant reinsurance as to any kind of insurance it is authorized to transact directly.

**History:** s. 674, ch. 59-205; s. 3, ch. 76-168; s. 1, ch. 77-457; ss. 2, 3, ch. 81-318; ss. 668, 681, 809(1st), ch. 82-243; ss. 187, 188, ch. 91-108; s. 4, ch. 91-429.
