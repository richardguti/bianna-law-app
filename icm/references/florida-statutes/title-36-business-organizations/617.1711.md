# Fla. Stat. § 617.1711 — Application to foreign and interstate commerce.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 617 — NONPROFIT CORPORATIONS
**Part:** III — FLORIDA STATE FAIR AUTHORITY
**Section:** 617.1711
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

This chapter applies to commerce with foreign nations and among the several states only insofar as such commerce may be permitted under the Constitution and laws of the United States.

**History:** s. 111, ch. 90-179; s. 151, ch. 2026-168.
