# Fla. Stat. § 609.051 — Shares, personal property.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 609 — COMMON-LAW DECLARATIONS OF TRUST
**Part:** III — BENEFIT CORPORATIONS
**Section:** 609.051
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Shares, however designated, in such trusts are declared for purposes of taxation, to be personal property, and not interest in land, notwithstanding the nature of the property of which the trust shall consist unless otherwise provided in the trust instrument.

**History:** s. 3, ch. 63-488.
