# Fla. Stat. § 636.026 — Construction of contracts.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 636 — PREPAID LIMITED HEALTH SERVICE ORGANIZATIONS AND DISCOUNT PLAN ORGANIZATIONS
**Part:** I — PREPAID LIMITED HEALTH SERVICE ORGANIZATIONS
**Section:** 636.026
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Every prepaid limited health services contract must be construed according to the entirety of its terms and conditions as set forth in the contract and as amplified, extended, or modified by any application, endorsement, attachment, or addendum.

**History:** s. 20, ch. 93-148; s. 487, ch. 97-102; s. 1532, ch. 2003-261.
