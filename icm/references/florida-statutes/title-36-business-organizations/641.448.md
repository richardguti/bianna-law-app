# Fla. Stat. § 641.448 — Penalty for violation of cease and desist order.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 641 — HEALTH CARE SERVICE PROGRAMS
**Part:** II — PREPAID HEALTH CLINICS
**Section:** 641.448
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Any person, entity, or prepaid health clinic that violates a cease and desist order of the office under s. 641.446 while such order is in effect, after notice and hearing as provided in s. 641.445, is subject, at the discretion of the office, to any one or more of the following:
(1)  
A monetary penalty of not more than $50,000 as to all matters determined in such hearing.

(2)  
The suspension or revocation of the certificate of authority of the prepaid health clinic.

**History:** ss. 10, 11, ch. 84-313; s. 54, ch. 87-226; ss. 187, 188, ch. 91-108; s. 4, ch. 91-429; s. 1618, ch. 2003-261.
