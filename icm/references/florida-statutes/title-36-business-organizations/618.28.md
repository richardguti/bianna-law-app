# Fla. Stat. § 618.28 — This chapter not to affect certain laws.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 619 — NONPROFIT COOPERATIVE ASSOCIATIONS
**Part:** III — FLORIDA STATE FAIR AUTHORITY
**Section:** 618.28
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The provisions of this chapter shall not be construed to affect, limit, or in anywise interfere with the rights, powers, or privileges of any corporation or association which exists or which may be hereafter organized under chapter 617 or chapter 619 and laws prior thereto.

**History:** s. 29, ch. 9300, 1923; CGL 6494; s. 30, ch. 14675, 1931; s. 6, ch. 16879, 1935; s. 2, ch. 80-192.
