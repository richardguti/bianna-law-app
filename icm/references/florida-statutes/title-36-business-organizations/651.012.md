# Fla. Stat. § 651.012 — Exempted facility; written disclosure of exemption.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 651 — CONTINUING CARE CONTRACTS
**Part:** III — HEALTH CARE SERVICES
**Section:** 651.012
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Any facility exempted under s. 632.637(1)(e) or excluded from the definition of the term “provider” in s. 651.011 must provide written disclosure of such exemption to each person admitted to the facility. This disclosure must be written using language likely to be understood by the person and must briefly explain the exemption.

**History:** s. 3, ch. 96-363; s. 1663, ch. 2003-261; s. 2, ch. 2010-202; s. 2, ch. 2011-193; s. 2, ch. 2019-160; s. 2, ch. 2023-295.
