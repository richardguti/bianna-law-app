# Fla. Stat. § 636.004 — Applicability of other laws.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 636 — PREPAID LIMITED HEALTH SERVICE ORGANIZATIONS AND DISCOUNT PLAN ORGANIZATIONS
**Part:** I — PREPAID LIMITED HEALTH SERVICE ORGANIZATIONS
**Section:** 636.004
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Except as provided in this act, prepaid limited health service organizations are governed by the provisions of this act and are exempt from the Florida Insurance Code unless specifically referenced.

**History:** s. 3, ch. 93-148.
