# Fla. Stat. § 631.64 — Recognition of assessments.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 631 — INSURER INSOLVENCY; GUARANTY OF PAYMENT
**Part:** II — FLORIDA INSURANCE GUARANTY OF PAYMENTS
**Section:** 631.64
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Charges or recoupments shall be separately displayed on premium statements to enable policyholders to determine the amount charged for association assessments but may not be included in rates filed and approved by the office.

**History:** s. 15, ch. 70-20; s. 809(1st), ch. 82-243; ss. 187, 188, ch. 91-108; s. 4, ch. 91-429; s. 3, ch. 2015-65.
