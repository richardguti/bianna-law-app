# Fla. Stat. § 629.525 — Rulemaking authority.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 630 — ALIEN INSURERS: TRUSTEED ASSETS; DOMESTICATION
**Part:** V — CAPTIVE INSURERS
**Section:** 629.525
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The commission shall adopt, amend, or repeal rules pursuant to chapter 120 which are necessary to implement this chapter.

**History:** s. 24, ch. 2024-182.
