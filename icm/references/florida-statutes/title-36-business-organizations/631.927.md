# Fla. Stat. § 631.927 — Assumption of liability.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 631 — INSURER INSOLVENCY; GUARANTY OF PAYMENT
**Part:** V — FLORIDA WORKERS’ COMPENSATION INSURANCE GUARANTY ASSOCIATION
**Section:** 631.927
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Notwithstanding s. 631.913, the corporation shall assume the liability for the payment of the workers' compensation indemnity and medical benefits that are due to claimants covered by the Certified Pulpwood Dealers Self-Insurers Fund. The corporation shall assess the former members of the Certified Pulpwood Dealers Self-Insurers Fund pursuant to the provisions of this act.

**History:** s. 20, ch. 97-262.
