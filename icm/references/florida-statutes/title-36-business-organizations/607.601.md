# Fla. Stat. § 607.601 — Application and effect of part.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 607 — FLORIDA BUSINESS CORPORATION ACT
**Part:** II — SOCIAL PURPOSE CORPORATIONS
**Section:** 607.601
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
This part applies to a benefit corporation and does not affect a corporation that is not a benefit corporation.

(2)  
Except as provided in this part, this chapter applies generally to all benefit corporations.
