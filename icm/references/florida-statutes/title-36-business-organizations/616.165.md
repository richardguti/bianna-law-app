# Fla. Stat. § 616.165 — Rules.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 616 — PUBLIC FAIRS AND EXPOSITIONS
**Part:** I — GENERAL PROVISIONS
**Section:** 616.165
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The department shall adopt rules pursuant to ss. 120.536(1) and 120.54 to implement the provisions of this chapter.

**History:** ss. 3, 26, ch. 83-239; ss. 41, 44, ch. 93-168; s. 196, ch. 98-200.

**Note:** Former s. 616.27.
