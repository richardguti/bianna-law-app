# Fla. Stat. § 626.99291 — Contestability of life insurance policies.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 626 — INSURANCE FIELD REPRESENTATIVES AND OPERATIONS
**Part:** X — VIATICAL SETTLEMENTS
**Section:** 626.99291
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Notwithstanding s. 627.455, a life insurer may contest a life insurance policy if the policy was obtained by a stranger-originated life insurance practice, as defined in s. 626.9911.

**History:** s. 11, ch. 2017-178.
