# Fla. Stat. § 620.1118 — Consent and proxies of partners.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 620 — PARTNERSHIP LAWS
**Part:** I — FLORIDA REVISED UNIFORM LIMITED PARTNERSHIP ACT OF 2005
**Section:** 620.1118
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Subject to the management and approval rights described in s. 620.1406, an action requiring the consent of partners under this act may be taken without a meeting, and a partner may appoint a proxy to consent or otherwise act for the partner by a record appointing the proxy that is signed, either personally or by the partner's attorney in fact.

**History:** s. 17, ch. 2005-267.
