# Fla. Stat. § 636.234 — Service of process on a discount plan organization.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 636 — PREPAID LIMITED HEALTH SERVICE ORGANIZATIONS AND DISCOUNT PLAN ORGANIZATIONS
**Part:** II — DISCOUNT PLAN ORGANIZATIONS
**Section:** 636.234
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Sections 624.422 and 624.423 apply to a discount plan organization as if the discount plan organization were an insurer.

**History:** s. 31, ch. 2004-297; s. 26, ch. 2017-112.
