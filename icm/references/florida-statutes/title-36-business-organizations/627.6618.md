# Fla. Stat. § 627.6618 — Payment of acupuncture benefits to certified acupuncturists.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 627 — INSURANCE RATES AND CONTRACTS
**Part:** VII — GROUP, BLANKET, AND FRANCHISE HEALTH INSURANCE POLICIES
**Section:** 627.6618
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Any policy of group health insurance that provides coverage for acupuncture shall cover the services of an acupuncturist certified pursuant to chapter 457 under the same conditions that apply to services of a licensed physician.

**History:** ss. 2, 3, ch. 87-176; s. 114, ch. 92-318.
