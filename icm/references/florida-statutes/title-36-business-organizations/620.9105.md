# Fla. Stat. § 620.9105 — Action by Attorney General.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 620 — PARTNERSHIP LAWS
**Part:** II — REVISED UNIFORM PARTNERSHIP ACT
**Section:** 620.9105
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The Attorney General may maintain an action to restrain a foreign limited liability partnership from transacting business in this state in violation of ss. 620.9101-620.9104.

**History:** s. 30, ch. 99-285.
