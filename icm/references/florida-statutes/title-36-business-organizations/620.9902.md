# Fla. Stat. § 620.9902 — Saving clause.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 621 — PROFESSIONAL SERVICE CORPORATIONS AND LIMITED LIABILITY COMPANIES
**Part:** II — REVISED UNIFORM PARTNERSHIP ACT
**Section:** 620.9902
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The Revised Uniform Partnership Act of 1995 does not affect any action or proceeding commenced or any right accrued before January 1, 1996.

**History:** s. 15, ch. 95-242; s. 35, ch. 99-285.

**Note:** Former s. 620.91.
