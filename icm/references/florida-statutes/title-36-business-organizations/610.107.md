# Fla. Stat. § 610.107 — Buildout.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 610 — CABLE AND VIDEO SERVICES
**Part:** III — BENEFIT CORPORATIONS
**Section:** 610.107
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

No franchising authority, state agency, or political subdivision may impose any buildout, system construction, or service deployment requirements on a certificateholder.

**History:** s. 7, ch. 2007-29.
