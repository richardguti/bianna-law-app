# Fla. Stat. § 625.306 — Cash and deposits.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 625 — ACCOUNTING, INVESTMENTS, AND DEPOSITS BY INSURERS
**Part:** II — INVESTMENTS
**Section:** 625.306
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

An insurer may have funds in coin or currency of the United States on hand or on deposit in any solvent national or state bank, savings and loan association, or trust company.

**History:** s. 131, ch. 59-205; s. 3, ch. 76-168; s. 1, ch. 77-457; ss. 2, 3, ch. 81-318; ss. 102, 122, 809(1st), ch. 82-243; ss. 187, 188, ch. 91-108; s. 4, ch. 91-429.
