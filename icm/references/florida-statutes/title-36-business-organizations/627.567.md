# Fla. Stat. § 627.567 — Conversion on termination of policy.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 627 — INSURANCE RATES AND CONTRACTS
**Part:** V — GROUP LIFE INSURANCE POLICIES
**Section:** 627.567
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

——

A group life insurance policy shall provide that, if the group policy terminates or is amended so as to terminate the insurance of any class of insured persons, every person insured thereunder at the date of such termination whose insurance terminates, including the insured dependent of a covered person, and who has been so insured for at least 5 years prior to such termination date is entitled to have issued to her or him by the insurer an individual policy of life insurance, subject to the same conditions and limitations as are provided by s. 627.566, except that the group policy may provide that the amount of such individual policy shall not exceed the smaller of:ip59-205; s. 3, ch. 76-168; s. 1, ch. 77-457; ss. 2, 3, ch. 81-318; ss. 437, 448, 809(2nd), ch. 82-243; s. 79, ch. 82-386; s. 114, ch. 92-318; s. 336, ch. 97-102.
