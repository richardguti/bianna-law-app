# Fla. Stat. § 627.4205 — Coverage identification number required.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 627 — INSURANCE RATES AND CONTRACTS
**Part:** II — THE INSURANCE CONTRACT
**Section:** 627.4205
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

An insurer shall provide to the named insured a coverage identification number no later than the time insurance coverage under a policy, binder, or other contract providing any insurance or surety coverage becomes effective. The coverage identification number shall be construed for regulatory purposes under this code as a policy number.

**History:** s. 17, ch. 86-160; s. 2, ch. 87-50; s. 114, ch. 92-318.
