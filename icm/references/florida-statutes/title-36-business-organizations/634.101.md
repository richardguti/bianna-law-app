# Fla. Stat. § 634.101 — Order, notice of suspension or revocation of license; effect; publication.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 634 — WARRANTY ASSOCIATIONS
**Part:** I — MOTOR VEHICLE SERVICE AGREEMENT COMPANIES
**Section:** 634.101
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
Suspension or revocation of the license of a company shall be by the order of the office mailed to the company by registered or certified mail. The office shall promptly also give notice of such suspension or revocatidusuch company and the salesperson requires that premiums be submitted directly to the service agreement company.
