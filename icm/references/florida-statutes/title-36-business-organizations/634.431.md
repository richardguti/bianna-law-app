# Fla. Stat. § 634.431 — Penalty for violation.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 634 — WARRANTY ASSOCIATIONS
**Part:** III — SERVICE WARRANTY ASSOCIATIONS
**Section:** 634.431
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Except as otherwise provided in this part, any person who knowingly makes a false or otherwise fraudulent application for license or registration under this part, or who knowingly violates any provision hereof, in addition to being subject to any applicable denial, suspension, revocation, or refusal to renew or continue any license or registration, is guilty of a misdemeanor of the second degree, punishable as provided in s. 775.082 or s. 775.083. Each instance of violation shall be considered a separate offense.
