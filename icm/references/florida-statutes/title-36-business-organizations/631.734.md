# Fla. Stat. § 631.734 — Reopening default judgments.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 631 — INSURER INSOLVENCY; GUARANTY OF PAYMENT
**Part:** III — LIFE AND HEALTH INSURANCE GUARANTY OF PAYMENTS
**Section:** 631.734
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

As to judgment under any decision, order, verdict, or finding based on default, the association may apply to have such judgment set aside by the same court that made such judgment and shall be permitted to defend againstF.S. 631.7336631.733  
Stay of proceedings.
——

All proceedings in which the insolvent insurer is a party in any court in this state shall be stayed 60 days from the date an order of liquidation, rehabilitation, or conservation is final to permit proper legal action by the association on any matters germane to its powers or duties.
