# Fla. Stat. § 630.011 — Scope of chapter.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 630 — ALIEN INSURERS: TRUSTEED ASSETS; DOMESTICATION
**Part:** V — CAPTIVE INSURERS
**Section:** 630.011
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

This chapter applies only to the trusteed assets of an alien insurer using Florida as a state of entry for transaction of insurance in the United States, and to the domestication of alien insurers in accordance with the procedures herein provided.

**History:** s. 701, ch. 59-205; s. 809(1st), ch. 82-243; ss. 187, 188, ch. 91-108; s. 4, ch. 91-429.
