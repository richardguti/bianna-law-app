# Fla. Stat. § 625.171 — Valuation of purchase money mortgages.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 625 — ACCOUNTING, INVESTMENTS, AND DEPOSITS BY INSURERS
**Part:** I — ASSETS AND LIABILITIES
**Section:** 625.171
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Purchase money mortgages on real property referred to in s. 625.161(2) shall be valued in an amount not exceeding the acquisition cost to the insurer of real property covered thereby or 90 percent of the fair value of such real property, whichever is less.

**History:** s. 125, ch. 59-205; s. 3, ch. 76-168; s. 1, ch. 77-457; ss. 2, 3, ch. 81-318; ss. 98, 809(1st), ch. 82-243; ss. 187, 188, ch. 91-108; s. 4, ch. 91-429; s. 90, ch. 2002-1.
