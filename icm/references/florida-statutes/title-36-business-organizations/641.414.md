# Fla. Stat. § 641.414 — Investment of funds of prepaid health clinic.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 641 — HEALTH CARE SERVICE PROGRAMS
**Part:** II — PREPAID HEALTH CLINICS
**Section:** 641.414
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The funds of any prepaid health clinic shall be invested only in securities permitted by the laws of this state for the investment of assets of life insurance companies.

**History:** ss. 10, 11, ch. 84-313; ss. 187, 188, ch. 91-108; s. 4, ch. 91-429.
