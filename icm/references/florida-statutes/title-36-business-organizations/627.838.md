# Fla. Stat. § 627.838 — Filing and approval of forms; service charges.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 627 — INSURANCE RATES AND CONTRACTS
**Part:** XV — PREMIUM FINANCE COMPANIES AND AGREEMENTS
**Section:** 627.838
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
No premium finance agreement form or related form shall be used in this state by a premium finance company unless it has been filed with and approved by the office. Every filing shall be made within 30 days of issuance or use.

(2)  
Each premium finance company shall file with the office the service charge and interest rate plan, including all modifications thereto, for informational purposes only. Every filing shall be made within 30 days of its effective date.
