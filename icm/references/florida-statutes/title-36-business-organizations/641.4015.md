# Fla. Stat. § 641.4015 — Part exclusive; applicability of other laws.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 641 — HEALTH CARE SERVICE PROGRAMS
**Part:** II — PREPAID HEALTH CLINICS
**Section:** 641.4015
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Except as provided in this part, prepaid health clinics shall be covered by the provisions of this part and shall be exempt from all other provisions of the Florida Insurance Code.
