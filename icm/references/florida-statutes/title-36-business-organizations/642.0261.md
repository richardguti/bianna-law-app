# Fla. Stat. § 642.0261 — Net worth required of applicants.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 642 — LEGAL EXPENSE INSURANCE
**Part:** III — HEALTH CARE SERVICES
**Section:** 642.0261
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

To receive authority to transact legal expense insurance, an applicant applying for a certificate of authority after October 1, 1993, must possess a net worth not less than the greater of:
(1)  
Ten thousand dollars; or

(2)  
Ten percent of the applicant's total liabilities.

**History:** ss. 3, 7, ch. 93-147.
