# Fla. Stat. § 624.1552 — Civil actions involving an insurance contract; applicability of offer of judgment provisions.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 624 — INSURANCE CODE: ADMINISTRATION AND GENERAL PROVISIONS
**Part:** I — SCOPE OF CODE
**Section:** 624.1552
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The provisions of s. 768.79 apply to any civil action involving an insurance contract.

**History:** s. 5, ch. 2023-15.
