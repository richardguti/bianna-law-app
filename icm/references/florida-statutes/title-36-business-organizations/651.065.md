# Fla. Stat. § 651.065 — Waiver of statutory protection.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 651 — CONTINUING CARE CONTRACTS
**Part:** III — HEALTH CARE SERVICES
**Section:** 651.065
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

No act, agreement, or statement of any resident, or of an individual purchasing care for a resident, under any contract to furnish care to the resident shall constitute a valid waiver of any provision of this chapter intended for the benefit or protection of the resident or the individual purchasing care for the resident.
