# Fla. Stat. § 627.318 — Records.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 627 — INSURANCE RATES AND CONTRACTS
**Part:** I — RATES AND RATING ORGANIZATIONS
**Section:** 627.318
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

——

Every insurer, rating organization, and advisory organization and every group, association, or other organization of insurers which engages in joint underwriting or joint reinsurance shall maintain reasonable records, of the type and kind reasonably adapted to its method of operation, of its experience or the experience of its membe.o 
This subsection does not apply as to workers' compensation and employer's liability insurances.
