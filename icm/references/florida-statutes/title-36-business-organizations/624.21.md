# Fla. Stat. § 624.21 — Prospective operation of amendments to code.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 624 — INSURANCE CODE: ADMINISTRATION AND GENERAL PROVISIONS
**Part:** I — SCOPE OF CODE
**Section:** 624.21
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Each amendment to this code shall be construed to operate prospectively, unless a contrary legislative intent is specified.

**History:** s. 4, ch. 82-386; s. 1, ch. 86-286; ss. 187, 188, ch. 91-108; s. 4, ch. 91-429.
