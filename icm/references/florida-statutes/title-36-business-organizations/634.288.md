# Fla. Stat. § 634.288 — Civil liability.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 634 — WARRANTY ASSOCIATIONS
**Part:** I — MOTOR VEHICLE SERVICE AGREEMENT COMPANIES
**Section:** 634.288
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The provisions of this part are cumulative to rights under the general civil and common law, and no action of the department or office will abrogate such rights to damages or other relief in any court.

**History:** s. 14, ch. 2001-281; s. 1449, ch. 2003-261.
