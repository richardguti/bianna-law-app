# Fla. Stat. § 618.17 — Marketing contracts.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 618 — AGRICULTURAL COOPERATIVE MARKETING ASSOCIATIONS
**Part:** III — FLORIDA STATE FAIR AUTHORITY
**Section:** 618.17
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The association and its members may make and execute marketing contracts requiring the members to sell, for any period of time, all or any specified part of their agricultural products or specified commodities exclusively to or through the association or any agencies designated by the association.
