# Fla. Stat. § 651.132 — Amendment or renewal of existing contracts.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 651 — CONTINUING CARE CONTRACTS
**Part:** III — HEALTH CARE SERVICES
**Section:** 651.132
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Any contract or agreement executed before October 1, 1983, which is amended or renewed subsequent to October 1, 1983, is subject to this act.

**History:** s. 6, ch. 77-323; ss. 21, 25, ch. 81-292; s. 2, ch. 81-318; s. 3, ch. 83-265; ss. 24, 31, 33, 35, ch. 83-328; s. 12, ch. 93-22.
