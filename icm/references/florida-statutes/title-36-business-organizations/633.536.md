# Fla. Stat. § 633.536 — Expenses of administration.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 633 — FIRE PREVENTION AND CONTROL
**Part:** V — FLORIDA FIREFIGHTERS OCCUPATIONAL SAFETY AND HEALTH ACT
**Section:** 633.536
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The amounts that are needed to administer this part shall be disbursed from the Insurance Regulatory Trust Fund.

**History:** s. 15, ch. 2002-404; s. 1414Bu

**Note:** Former s. 633.818.
