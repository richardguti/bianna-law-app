# Fla. Stat. § 626.9938 — Severability.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 626 — INSURANCE FIELD REPRESENTATIVES AND OPERATIONS
**Part:** XI — STRUCTURED SETTLEMENTS
**Section:** 626.9938
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Effective upon this act becoming a law, notwithstanding Article XV of the Interstate Insurance Product Regulation Compact, if any part of s. 626.9933 or s. 626.9934 is invalidated by the courts, such ruling renders the entire act invalid.

**History:** s. 7, ch. 2013-140.
