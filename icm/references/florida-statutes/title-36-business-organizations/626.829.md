# Fla. Stat. § 626.829 — “Health agent” defined.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS

**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

(1)  
A “health agent” is any person appointed as agent by an insurer to solicit applications for or to negotiate and effectuate contracts of health insurance, as such insurance is defined in s. 624.603.
