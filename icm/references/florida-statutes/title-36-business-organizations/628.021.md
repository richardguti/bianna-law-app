# Fla. Stat. § 628.021 — “Stock insurer” defined.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 628 — STOCK AND MUTUAL INSURERS; HOLDING COMPANIES
**Part:** I — STOCK AND MUTUAL INSURERS: ORGANIZATION AND CORPORATE PROCEDURES
**Section:** 628.021
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

A “stock insurer” is an incorporated insurer with its capital divided into shares and owned by its stockholders.

**History:** s. 622, ch. 59-205; s. 3, ch. 76-168; s. 1, ch. 77-457; ss. 2, 3, ch. 81-318; ss. 665, 809(1st), ch. 82-243; ss. 187, 188, ch. 91-108; s. 4, ch. 91-429.
