# Fla. Stat. § 620.8501 — Partner not co-owner of partnership property.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 620 — PARTNERSHIP LAWS
**Part:** II — REVISED UNIFORM PARTNERSHIP ACT
**Section:** 620.8501
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Partnership property is owned by the partnership as an entity, not by the partners as co-owners. A partner has no interest that can be transferred, either voluntarily or involuntarily, in specific partnership property.

**History:** s. 13, ch. 95-242.
