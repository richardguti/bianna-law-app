# Fla. Stat. § 627.021 — Scope of this part.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 627 — INSURANCE RATES AND CONTRACTS
**Part:** I — RATES AND RATING ORGANIZATIONS
**Section:** 627.021
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
This part of this chapter applies only to property, casualty, and surety insurances on subjects of insurance resident, located, or to be performed in this state.

(2)  
This part does not apply to:
(a)  
Reinsurance, except joint reinsurance as provided in s. 627.311.

(b)  
Insurance against loss of or damage to aircraft, their hulls, accessories, or equipment, or against liability, other than workers' compensation and employer's liability, arising out of the ownership, maintenance, or use of aiF.S. 627.0116627.011

This part of this chapter may be referred to as the “Rating Law.”
