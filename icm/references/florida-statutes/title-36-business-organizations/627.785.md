# Fla. Stat. § 627.785 — Preemption by state.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 627 — INSURANCE RATES AND CONTRACTS
**Part:** XIII — TITLE INSURANCE CONTRACTS
**Section:** 627.785
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The regulation of title insurers and title insurance is preempted to the state.

**History:** s. 11, ch. 65-359; s. 3, ch. 76-168; s. 1, ch. 77-457; ss. 2, 3, ch. 81-318; ss. 584, 809(2nd), ch. 82-243; s. 79, ch. 82-386; ss. 103, 114, ch. 92-318.
