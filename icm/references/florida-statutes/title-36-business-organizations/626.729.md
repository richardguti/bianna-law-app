# Fla. Stat. § 626.729 — “Industrial fire insurance” defined.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 626 — INSURANCE FIELD REPRESENTATIVES AND OPERATIONS
**Part:** II — GENERAL LINES AGENTS
**Section:** 626.729
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

As used in this code, the term “industrial fire insurance” means:
(1)  
Insurance against loss by fire of either buildings and other structures or contents, which may include extended coverage;

(2)  
Windstorm insurance;

(3)  
Basic limits owners, landlords, or tenants liability insurance with single limits of $25,000;

(4)  
Comprehensive personal liability insurance with a single limit of $25,000; or

(5)  
Burglary insurance, under which the premiums are collected quarterly or more often and the face amount of the insurance provided by the policy on one risk is not more than $50,000, including the contents of such buildings and other structures.
