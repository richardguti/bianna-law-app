# Fla. Stat. § 620.8916 — Merger.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 620 — PARTNERSHIP LAWS
**Part:** II — REVISED UNIFORM PARTNERSHIP ACT
**Section:** 620.8916
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
A partnership may merge with one or more other constituent organizations pursuant to this section and ss. 620.8917-620.8919 and a plan of merger, if:
(a)  
The governing law of each of the other organizations authorizes the merger.

(b)  
The merger is permitted by the law of each jurisdiction that enacted those governing laws.

(c)  
Each of the other organizations complies with its governing law in effecting the merger.

(2)  
A plan of merger must be in a record and must include:
(a)  
The name and form of each constituent organization.

(b)  
The name and form of the survivijnces as provided in s. 48.161.

(4)  
A copy of the certificate of conversion, certified by the Secretary of State, may be filed in any county of this state in which the converting organization holds an interest in real property.

**History:** s. 22, ch. 2005-267; s. 34, ch. 2022-190.
