# Fla. Stat. § 633.438 — Procedure for making expenditures.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 633 — FIRE PREVENTION AND CONTROL
**Part:** IV — FIRE STANDARDS AND TRAINING
**Section:** 633.438
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

No moneys shall be spent for and on behalf of the Florida State Fire College except upon a written voucher drawn by the division, stating the nature of the expenditures and the person to whom the same shall be made payable, which voucher shall be submitted to the Chief Financial Officer and audited for approval by her or him; upon such approval, the Chief Financial Officer shall draw a warrant for the payment thereof, filing the original voucher in her or his office.

**History:** s. 7, ch. 25097, 1949; ss. 18, 35, ch. 69-106; ss. 1, 10, ch. 75-151; s. 15, ch. 85-61; s. 439, ch. 97-102; s. 1409, ch. 2003-261; s. 71, ch. 2013-183.

**Note:** Former s. 163.536; s. 633.47.
