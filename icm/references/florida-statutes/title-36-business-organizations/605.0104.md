# Fla. Stat. § 605.0104 — Governing law.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 605 — FLORIDA REVISED LIMITED LIABILITY COMPANY ACT
**Part:** II — FLORIDA TROPICAL FRUIT POLICY ACT
**Section:** 605.0104
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The law of this state governs:
(1)  
The internal affairs of a limited liability company.

(2)  
The liability of a member as member, and a manager as manager, for the debts, obligations, or other liabilities of a limited liability company.

**History:** s. 2, ch. 2013-180.
