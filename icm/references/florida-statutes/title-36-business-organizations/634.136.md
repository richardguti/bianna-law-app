# Fla. Stat. § 634.136 — Office records required.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 634 — WARRANTY ASSOCIATIONS
**Part:** I — MOTOR VEHICLE SERVICE AGREEMENT COMPANIES
**Section:** 634.136
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Each licensed motor vehicle service contract company, as a minimum requirement for permanent office records, shall maintain:
(1)  
A complete set of accounting records, including, but not limited to, a general ledger, cash receipts and disbursements journals, accounts receivable registers, and accounts payable registers.

(2)  
AF.S. 634.1316634.131  
Tax on premiums and assessments.
——

Premiums and assessments received by insurers or service agreement companies and taxed under this section are not subject to any premium tax provided for in the Florida Insurance Code. However, the gross amount of such premiums and assessments is subject to the sales tax imposed by s. 212.0506.
