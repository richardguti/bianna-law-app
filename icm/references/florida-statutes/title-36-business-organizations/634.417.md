# Fla. Stat. § 634.417 — Service of process.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 634 — WARRANTY ASSOCIATIONS
**Part:** III — SERVICE WARRANTY ASSOCIATIONS
**Section:** 634.417
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Service warranty associations are subject to service of process in the same manner and subject to the same terms, conditions, and fees as apply to insurers under chapter 624.

**History:** s. 5, ch. 78-255; s. 3, ch. 81-148; s. 2, ch. 81-318; s. 3, ch. 83-265; ss. 14, 36, 37, 38, ch. 83-322; s. 20, ch. 93-195.
