# Fla. Stat. § 647.01 — Purpose and scope.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 642 — LEGAL EXPENSE INSURANCE
**Part:** III — HEALTH CARE SERVICES
**Section:** 647.01
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
The purpose of this chapter is to promote the public welfare by creating a comprehensive legal framework within which travel insurance may be sold in this state.
