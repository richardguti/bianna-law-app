# Fla. Stat. § 624.516 — State Fire Marshal regulatory assessment and surcharge; deposit and use of funds.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 624 — INSURANCE CODE: ADMINISTRATION AND GENERAL PROVISIONS
**Part:** IV — FEES, TAXES, AND FUNDS
**Section:** 624.516
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
The regulatory assessment imposed under s. 624.515(1) and the surcharge imposed under s. 624.515(2) shall be deposited by the Department of Revenue, when received and audited, into the Insurance Regulatory Trust Fund.

(2)  
624.509(1) relating to the premium receipts tax thereby imposed.
