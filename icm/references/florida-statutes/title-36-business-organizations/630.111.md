# Fla. Stat. § 630.111 — Canadian insurers.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 630 — ALIEN INSURERS: TRUSTEED ASSETS; DOMESTICATION
**Part:** V — CAPTIVE INSURERS
**Section:** 630.111
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The provisions of this chapter applicable to a United States manager shall, in the case of insurers domiciled in Canada, be deemed F.S. 630.1016630.101  
Examination of assets.
——

The office may from time to time examine trusteed assets of any insurer in accordance with the same conditions and procedures governing the examination of insurers in general under part II of chapter 624.
