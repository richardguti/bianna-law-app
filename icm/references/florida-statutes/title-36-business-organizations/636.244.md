# Fla. Stat. § 636.244 — Unlicensed discount plan organizations.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 636 — PREPAID LIMITED HEALTH SERVICE ORGANIZATIONS AND DISCOUNT PLAN ORGANIZATIONS
**Part:** II — DISCOUNT PLAN ORGANIZATIONS
**Section:** 636.244
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Sections 626.901-626.912 apply to the activities of an unlicensed discount plan organization as if the unlicensed discount plan organization were an unauthorized insurer.

**History:** s. 31, ch. 2004-297; s. 30, ch. 2017-112.
