# Fla. Stat. § 636.063 — Civil liability.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 636 — PREPAID LIMITED HEALTH SERVICE ORGANIZATIONS AND DISCOUNT PLAN ORGANIZATIONS
**Part:** I — PREPAID LIMITED HEALTH SERVICE ORGANIZATIONS
**Section:** 636.063
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The provisions of this act are cumulative to rights under the general civil and common law, and no action of the office or department abrogates such rights to damage or other relief in any court.

**History:** s. 50, ch. 93-148; s. 1551, ch. 2003-261.
