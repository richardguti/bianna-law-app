# Fla. Stat. § 607.1907 — Saving provision.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 607 — FLORIDA BUSINESS CORPORATION ACT
**Part:** I — GENERAL PROVISIONS
**Section:** 607.1907
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
Except as to procedural provisions, chapter 2019-90, Laws of Florida, does not affect a pending action or proceeding or a right accrued before January 1, 2020, and a pending civil action or proceeding may be completed, and a right accrued may be enforced, as if chapter 2019-90 had not become effective.

(2)  
If a penalty or punishment for violation of a statute or rule is reduced by chapter 2019-90, Laws of Florida, the penalty or punishment, if not already imposed, shall be imposed in accordance with chapter 2019-90.

**History:** s. 164, ch. 89-154; s. 170, ch. 90-179; s. 42, ch. 2003-283; s. 230, ch. 2019-90; s. 68, ch. 2020-32.
