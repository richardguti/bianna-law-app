# Fla. Stat. § 634.309 — Procedure to suspend or revoke license.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 634 — WARRANTY ASSOCIATIONS
**Part:** II — HOME WARRANTY ASSOCIATIONS
**Section:** 634.309
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Except when a hearing is expressly not required under s. 120.60 or s. 634.308, no order suspending or revoking a home warranty association's license shall be effective unless made after notice and hearing pursuant to chapter 120.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 77-339; s. 3, ch. 81-148; s. 2, ch. 81-318; s. 3, ch. 83-265; ss. 32, 33, 34, ch. 83-323; s. 1, ch. 84-94; s. 20, ch. 93-195.
