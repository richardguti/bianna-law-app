# Fla. Stat. § 620.1504 — Interim distributions.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 620 — PARTNERSHIP LAWS
**Part:** I — FLORIDA REVISED UNIFORM LIMITED PARTNERSHIP ACT OF 2005
**Section:** 620.1504
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

A partner does not have a right to any distribution before the dissolution and winding up of the limited partnership unless the limited partnership decides to make an interim distribution.

**History:** s. 17, ch. 2005-267.
