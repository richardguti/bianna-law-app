# Fla. Stat. § 624.09 — “Authorized,” “unauthorized” insurer defined.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 624 — INSURANCE CODE: ADMINISTRATION AND GENERAL PROVISIONS
**Part:** I — SCOPE OF CODE
**Section:** 624.09
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
An “authorized” insurer is one duly authorized by a subsisting certificate of authority issued by the office to transact insurance in this state.

(2)  
An “unauthorized” insurer is one not so authorized.

**History:** s. 9, ch. 59-205; s. 13, 35, ch. 69-106; s. 3, ch. 76-168; s. 1, ch. 77-457; ss. 2, 3, ch. 81-318; ss. 15, 809(1st), ch. 82-243; ss. 187, 188, ch. 91-108; s. 4, ch. 91-429; s. 753, ch. 2003-261.
