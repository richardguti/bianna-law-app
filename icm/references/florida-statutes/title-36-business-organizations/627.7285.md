# Fla. Stat. § 627.7285 — Experience while operating a train.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 627 — INSURANCE RATES AND CONTRACTS
**Part:** XI — MOTOR VEHICLE AND CASUALTY INSURANCE CONTRACTS
**Section:** 627.7285
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The experience of any person while operating any vehicle or train as a public conveyance or acting as a member of the crew of such train shall not be a factor in the setting of such person's motor vehicle liability insurance rates, nor shall such person's personal motor vehicle liability insurance policy be canceled or nonrenewed due to such experience. As used in this section, the term “public conveyance” means any vehicle or train operated over fixed rails.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 78-50; s. 2, ch. 81-318; ss. 563, 809(2nd), ch. 82-243; s. 79, ch. 82-386; s. 114, ch. 92-318.
