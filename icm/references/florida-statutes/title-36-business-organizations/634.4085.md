# Fla. Stat. § 634.4085 — Acquisition.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 634 — WARRANTY ASSOCIATIONS
**Part:** III — SERVICE WARRANTY ASSOCIATIONS
**Section:** 634.4085
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Except for manufacturers as defined in this part, every service warranty association shall be subject to the provisions of s. 628.4615.

**History:** ss. 24, 38, ch. 83-322; ss. 6, 17, ch. 86-250; s. 4, ch. 87-50; s. 45, ch. 90-119; s. 10, ch. 90-248; s. 184, ch. 91-108; s. 115, ch. 92-318; s. 20, ch. 93-195.
