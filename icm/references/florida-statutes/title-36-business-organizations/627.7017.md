# Fla. Stat. § 627.7017 — Hurricane loss mitigation projects.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 627 — INSURANCE RATES AND CONTRACTS
**Part:** X — PROPERTY INSURANCE CONTRACTS
**Section:** 627.7017
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

In addition to any other hurricane loss mitigation activities authorized or required by law, the office may contract with public or private entities for hurricane loss mitigation projects.
