# Fla. Stat. § 627.676 — Scope of this part.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 627 — INSURANCE RATES AND CONTRACTS
**Part:** IX — CREDIT LIFE AND DISABILITY INSURANCES
**Section:** 627.676
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

This part applies only to credit life and credit disability insurances as defined in s. 627.677.

**History:** s. 595, ch. 59-205; s. 3, ch. 76-168; s. 1, ch. 77-457; ss. 2, 3, ch. 81-318; ss. 537, 809(2nd), ch. 82-243; s. 79, ch. 82-386; s. 114, ch. 92-318.
