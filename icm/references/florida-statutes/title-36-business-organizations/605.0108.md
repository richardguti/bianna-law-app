# Fla. Stat. § 605.0108 — Nature, purpose, and duration of limited liability company.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 605 — FLORIDA REVISED LIMITED LIABILITY COMPANY ACT
**Part:** II — FLORIDA TROPICAL FRUIT POLICY ACT
**Section:** 605.0108
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

——

(1)  
A limited liability company is an entity distinct from its members.

(2)  
A limited liability company may have any lawful purpose, regardless of whether the company is a for-profit company.
