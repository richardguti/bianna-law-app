# Fla. Stat. § 624.865 — Rulemaking.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 624 — INSURANCE CODE: ADMINISTRATION AND GENERAL PROVISIONS
**Part:** VI — ADMINISTRATIVE SUPERVISION; CONFIDENTIALITY; REVIEW
**Section:** 624.865
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The commission may adopt rules to administer ss. 624.80-624.87. Such rules must protect the interests of insureds, claimants, insurers, and the public.

**History:** s. 10, ch. 2023-172.
