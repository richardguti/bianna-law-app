# Fla. Stat. § 628.535 — Authority to adopt rules.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 628 — STOCK AND MUTUAL INSURERS; HOLDING COMPANIES
**Part:** I — STOCK AND MUTUAL INSURERS: ORGANIZATION AND CORPORATE PROCEDURES
**Section:** 628.535
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The commission may adopt rules pursuant to ss. 120.536(1) and 120.54 to implement the provisions of this chapter.

**History:** s. 7, ch. 85-214; s. 1, ch. 86-286; s. 180, ch. 90-179; ss. 187, 188, ch. 91-108; s. 4, ch. 91-429; s. 1292, ch. 2003-261; s. 288, ch. 2019-90.
