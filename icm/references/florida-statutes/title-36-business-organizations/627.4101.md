# Fla. Stat. § 627.4101 — Credit insurance enrollment forms.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 627 — INSURANCE RATES AND CONTRACTS
**Part:** II — THE INSURANCE CONTRACT
**Section:** 627.4101
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

All credit insurance enrollment forms must be approved by the office pursuant to the provisions of s. 627.410 or s. 627.682.

**History:** s. 11, ch. 2002-57; s. 1113, ch. 2003-261.
