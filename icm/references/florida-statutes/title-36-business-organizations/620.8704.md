# Fla. Stat. § 620.8704 — Statement of dissociation.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 620 — PARTNERSHIP LAWS
**Part:** II — REVISED UNIFORM PARTNERSHIP ACT
**Section:** 620.8704
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

——

(1)  
A dissociated partner or the partnership may file a statement of dissociation stating:
(a)  
The name of the partnership as identified in the records of the Department of State.

(b)  
That the partner is dissociated from the partnership.

(2)  
A statement of dissociation may be filed without regard to the provisions of s. 620.8105(4) if it states that no partnership registration statement has been filed with the Department of State.

(3)  
A statement of dissociation is a limitation on the authority of a dissociated partner for purposes of s. 620.8303(4) and (5).
