# Fla. Stat. § 627.7018 — Standards for determining risk of coverage.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 627 — INSURANCE RATES AND CONTRACTS
**Part:** X — PROPERTY INSURANCE CONTRACTS
**Section:** 627.7018
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

In determining the risk of providing property insurance coverage, an insurer may not deny coverage solely on the basis of the age of the structure and shall consider the wind resistance of the structure and measures undertaken by the owner to protect the structure against hurricane loss.

**History:** s. 29, ch. 2007-1.
