# Fla. Stat. § 620.81001 — Uniformity of application and construction.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 620 — PARTNERSHIP LAWS
**Part:** I — FLORIDA REVISED UNIFORM LIMITED PARTNERSHIP ACT OF 2005
**Section:** 620.81001
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

This act shall be applied and construed to effectuate its general purpose to make uniform the law with respect to the subject of this act.

**History:** s. 13, ch. 95-242.
