# Fla. Stat. § 651.134 — Investigatory records.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 651 — CONTINUING CARE CONTRACTS
**Part:** III — HEALTH CARE SERVICES
**Section:** 651.134
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Any active investigatory record of the office made or received under this chapter, and any active examination record necessary to complete an active investigation, is confidential and exempt from s. 119.07(1) until such investigation is completed or ceases to be active.
