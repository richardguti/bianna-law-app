# Fla. Stat. § 617.1523 — Action against foreign corporation by Department of Legal Affairs.

**Title:** XXXVI — BUSINESS ORGANIZATIONS
**Chapter:** 617 — NONPROFIT CORPORATIONS
**Part:** III — FLORIDA STATE FAIR AUTHORITY
**Section:** 617.1523
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The Department of Legal Affairs may maintain an action to enjoin a foreign corporation from conducting affairs in this state in violation of this chapter.

**History:** s. 97, ch. 2026-168.
