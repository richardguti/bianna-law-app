# Fla. Stat. § 238.10 — Management of funds.

**Title:** XVI — TEACHERS’ RETIREMENT SYSTEM; HIGHER EDUCATIONAL FACILITIES BONDS
**Chapter:** 238 — TEACHERS’ RETIREMENT SYSTEM
**Part:** X — TAX CRIMES
**Section:** 238.10
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The Department of Management Services, annually, shall allow regular interest on the amount for the preceding year to the credit of each of the funds of the retirement system, and to the credit of the individual account therein, if any, with the exception of the expense fund, from the interest and dividends earned from investments.

**History:** s. 10, ch. 19014, 1939; CGL 1940 Supp. 892(165); s. 4, ch. 20749, 1941; s. 15, ch. 21989, 1943; s. 1, ch. 26963, 1951; s. 9, ch. 29942, 1955; s. 6, ch. 61-458; ss. 31, 35, ch. 69-106; s. 1, ch. 73-326; s. 80, ch. 99-255.
