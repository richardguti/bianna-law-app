# Fla. Stat. § 243.76 — Liberal construction.

**Title:** XVI — TEACHERS’ RETIREMENT SYSTEM; HIGHER EDUCATIONAL FACILITIES BONDS
**Chapter:** 243 — HIGHER EDUCATIONAL FACILITIES BONDS
**Part:** II — HIGHER EDUCATIONAL FACILITIES FINANCING
**Section:** 243.76
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Sections 243.50-243.77 shall be liberally construed to effectively carry out their purpose.
