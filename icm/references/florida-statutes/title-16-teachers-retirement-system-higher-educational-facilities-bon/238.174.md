# Fla. Stat. § 238.174 — Appropriation for monthly allowance to incapacitated teachers.

**Title:** XVI — TEACHERS’ RETIREMENT SYSTEM; HIGHER EDUCATIONAL FACILITIES BONDS
**Chapter:** 238 — TEACHERS’ RETIREMENT SYSTEM
**Part:** X — TAX CRIMES
**Section:** 238.174
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

There is appropriated out of any moneys in the State Treasury not otherwise appropriated, a sufficient sum of money to meet the requirements of s. 238.171.

**History:** s. 3, ch. 14782, 1931; s. 91, ch. 72-221.

**Note:** Former ss. 242.06, 231.53.
