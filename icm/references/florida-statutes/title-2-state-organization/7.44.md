# Fla. Stat. § 7.44 — Monroe County.

**Title:** II — STATE ORGANIZATION
**Chapter:** 7 — COUNTY BOUNDARIES
**Section:** 7.44
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

So much of the State of Florida as is situated south of the County of Collier and west or south of the County of Miami-Dade, constitutes the County of Monroe.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 10180, 1925; CGL 77; s. 3, ch. 63-200; s. 1, ch. 2012-45.
