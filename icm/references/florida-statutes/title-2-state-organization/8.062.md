# Fla. Stat. § 8.062 — Limitation on choice of venue for actions challenging congressional districts.

**Title:** II — STATE ORGANIZATION
**Chapter:** 8 — CONGRESSIONAL DISTRICTS
**Section:** 8.062
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, state court actions challenging the state's congressional districts shall be brought only in Leon County.

**History:** s. 11, ch. 2022-265.

**Note:** [1]Note.
—
Section 6, ch. 2026-229, provides that “[e]xcept as otherwise expressly provided in this act and except for this section, which shall take effect upon this act becoming a law, this act shall take effect upon the expiration of the terms of the representatives to the United States House of Representatives serving on the date that this act becomes a law.” Chapter 2026-229, became law on May 4, 2026. United States Constitution, amend. XX, s. 1, provides that the terms of representatives shall end at noon on the 3rd day of January of every second year.
