# Fla. Stat. § 7.221 — Glades County, extension of boundary.

**Title:** II — STATE ORGANIZATION
**Chapter:** 7 — COUNTY BOUNDARIES
**Section:** 7.221
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
The existing boundaries of Glades County, Florida, be, and the same are enlarged and extended so as to comprise and include the following described additional territory now described as follows:
All that portion of the S1
/2

of section 32, township 39 south, range 30 east lying east of Federal Highway No. 19 in Highlands County, Florida.

(2)  
Said territory herein and hereby added to Glades County, Florida, shall, after June 13, 1949, be as much a portion of said Glades County, as if originally incorporated therein.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 25612, 1949.
