# Fla. Stat. § 15.0455 — Official state history museum.

**Title:** IV — EXECUTIVE BRANCH
**Chapter:** 15 — SECRETARY OF STATE
**Section:** 15.0455
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The Museum of Florida History, located in Tallahassee, is hereby designated as the official state history museum.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 2021-71.
