# Fla. Stat. § 15.0465 — Official state flagship.

**Title:** IV — EXECUTIVE BRANCH
**Chapter:** 15 — SECRETARY OF STATE
**Section:** 15.0465
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The S.S. American Victory
, a 455-foot historic steamship, built in 1944, is designated the official state flagship.
