# Fla. Stat. § 24.1215 — Duty to inform public of lottery's significance to education.

**Title:** IV — EXECUTIVE BRANCH
**Chapter:** 24 — STATE LOTTERIES
**Part:** III — CUSTOMER SERVICE STANDARDS
**Section:** 24.1215
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The Department of the Lottery shall inform the public about the significance of lottery funding to the state's overall system of public education.

**History:** s. 9, ch. 89-208.
