# Fla. Stat. § 24.110 — Venue.

**Title:** IV — EXECUTIVE BRANCH
**Chapter:** 24 — STATE LOTTERIES
**Part:** III — CUSTOMER SERVICE STANDARDS
**Section:** 24.110
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The venue for all civil or administrative actions against the department shall be in Leon County.

**History:** s. 10, ch. 87-65.
