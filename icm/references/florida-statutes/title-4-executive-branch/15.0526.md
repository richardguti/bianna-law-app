# Fla. Stat. § 15.0526 — Official state horse.

**Title:** IV — EXECUTIVE BRANCH
**Chapter:** 15 — SECRETARY OF STATE
**Section:** 15.0526
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The Florida Cracker Horse (Marshtackie) is designated as the official Florida state horse.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 2008-34; s. 2, ch. 2018-43.
