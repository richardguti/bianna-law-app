# Fla. Stat. § 15.0382 — Official state butterfly.

**Title:** IV — EXECUTIVE BRANCH
**Chapter:** 15 — SECRETARY OF STATE
**Section:** 15.0382
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The Zebra Longwing (Heliconius charitonius
) is designated the official state butterfly.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 75-75.
