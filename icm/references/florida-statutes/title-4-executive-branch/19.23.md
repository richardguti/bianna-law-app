# Fla. Stat. § 19.23 — Residence and office.

**Title:** IV — EXECUTIVE BRANCH
**Chapter:** 19 — COMMISSIONER OF AGRICULTURE
**Section:** 19.23
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The Commissioner of Agriculture shall reside at the seat of government in this state, and shall keep his or her office in a room in the capitol.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 54, 1845; s. 2, ch. 1822, 1870; ch. 1845, 1871; RS 152; GS 162; RGS 174; CGL 206; s. 7, ch. 22858, 1945; s. 79, ch. 95-147.
