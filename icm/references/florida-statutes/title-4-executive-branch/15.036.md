# Fla. Stat. § 15.036 — Official state freshwater fish.

**Title:** IV — EXECUTIVE BRANCH
**Chapter:** 15 — SECRETARY OF STATE
**Section:** 15.036
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The Florida largemouth bass (Micropterus salmoides floridanus)
is hereby designated and declared as the official Florida state freshwater fish.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 75-1.
