# Fla. Stat. § 20.3311 — Additional powers of commission.

**Title:** IV — EXECUTIVE BRANCH
**Chapter:** 20 — ORGANIZATIONAL STRUCTURE
**Section:** 20.3311
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
The Fish and Wildlife Conservation Commission is assigned all powers, duties, responsibilities, functions, positions, and property necessary for enforcement of the laws and rules governing:
(a)  
Management, protection, conservation, improvement, and expansion of the state-owned lands managed  bond. In determining the amount of the bond, the commission may consider the amount of money or property likely to be in the custody of the officer or employee at any one time. The premium for the bond must be paid out of the funds of the commission, and the bond must be payable to the Governor of the state and the Governor's successors in office.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 99-245; s. 4, ch. 2000-151; s. 3, ch. 2000-197; s. 7, ch. 2001-60; s. 2, ch. 2003-156; s. 1, ch. 2004-264; s. 1, ch. 2007-223; s. 1, ch. 2008-106.
