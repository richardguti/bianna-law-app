# Fla. Stat. § 15.0522 — Official state dessert.

**Title:** IV — EXECUTIVE BRANCH
**Chapter:** 15 — SECRETARY OF STATE
**Section:** 15.0522
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Strawberry shortcake with natural Florida dairy topping is designated as the official Florida state dessert.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 2022-12.
