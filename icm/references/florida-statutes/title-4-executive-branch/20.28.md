# Fla. Stat. § 20.28 — State Board of Administration.

**Title:** IV — EXECUTIVE BRANCH
**Chapter:** 20 — ORGANIZATIONAL STRUCTURE
**Section:** 20.28
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The State Board of Administration, continued by s. 4, Art. IV of the State Constitution, retains all of its powers, duties, and functions as prescribed by law.

**History:** s. 28, ch. 69-106; s. 7, ch. 79-10; s. 3, ch. 2013-15.
