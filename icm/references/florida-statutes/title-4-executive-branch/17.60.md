# Fla. Stat. § 17.60 — Treasury Cash Deposit Trust Fund.

**Title:** IV — EXECUTIVE BRANCH
**Chapter:** 17 — CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER
**Section:** 17.60
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

(1)  
There is created in the State Treasury the Treasury Cash Deposit Trust Fund. Cash deposits made pursuant to s. 17.59 shall be deposited into this fund.

(2)  
Interest earned on cash deposited into this fund shall be prorated and paid to the depositing entities.

**History:** s. 3, ch. 90-357; s. 3, ch. 92-87; s. 59, ch. 2003-261.

**Note:** Former s. 18.104.
