# Fla. Stat. § 15.032 — State beverage.

**Title:** IV — EXECUTIVE BRANCH
**Chapter:** 15 — SECRETARY OF STATE
**Section:** 15.032
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The juice obtained from mature oranges of the species Citrus sinensis
and hybrids thereof is hereby adopted as the official beverage of Florida.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 67-4.
