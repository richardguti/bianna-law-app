# Fla. Stat. § 14.28 — Executive clemency.

**Title:** IV — EXECUTIVE BRANCH
**Chapter:** 14 — GOVERNOR
**Section:** 14.28
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

——

All records developed or received by any state entity pursuant to a Board of Executive Clemency investigation shall be confidential and exempt from the provisions of s. f Agriculture and Consumer Services.
