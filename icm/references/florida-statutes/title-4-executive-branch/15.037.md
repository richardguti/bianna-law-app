# Fla. Stat. § 15.037 — Official state saltwater fish.

**Title:** IV — EXECUTIVE BRANCH
**Chapter:** 15 — SECRETARY OF STATE
**Section:** 15.037
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The Atlantic sailfish (Istiophorus platypterus)
is hereby designated and declared as the official Florida state saltwater fish.

**History:** s. 2, ch. 75-1.
