# Fla. Stat. § 14.201 — Executive Office of the Governor.

**Title:** IV — EXECUTIVE BRANCH
**Chapter:** 14 — GOVERNOR
**Section:** 14.201
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

There is created the Executive Office of the Governor. The head of the Executive Office of the Governor is the Governor.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 79-190.
