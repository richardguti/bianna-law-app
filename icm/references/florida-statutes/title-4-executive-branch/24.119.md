# Fla. Stat. § 24.119 — Use of word “lottery” in corporate name.

**Title:** IV — EXECUTIVE BRANCH
**Chapter:** 24 — STATE LOTTERIES
**Part:** III — CUSTOMER SERVICE STANDARDS
**Section:** 24.119
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The corporate name of a corporation shall not contain the word “lottery” unless the Department of the Lottery approves such name in writing.

**History:** s. 19, ch. 87-65; s. 2, ch. 95-280.
