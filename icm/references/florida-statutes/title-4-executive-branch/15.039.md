# Fla. Stat. § 15.039 — Official state air fair.

**Title:** IV — EXECUTIVE BRANCH
**Chapter:** 15 — SECRETARY OF STATE
**Section:** 15.039
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The Central Florida Air Fair is hereby designated as the official Florida state air fair.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 76-45.
