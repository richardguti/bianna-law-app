# Fla. Stat. § 15.0396 — Official state moving image center and archive.

**Title:** IV — EXECUTIVE BRANCH
**Chapter:** 15 — SECRETARY OF STATE
**Section:** 15.0396
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The Louis Wolfson II Media History Center, Inc., in Miami, is hereby designated as an official state moving image center and archive of the State of Florida.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 89-251.
