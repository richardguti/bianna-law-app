# Fla. Stat. § 20.11 — Department of Legal Affairs.

**Title:** IV — EXECUTIVE BRANCH
**Chapter:** 20 — ORGANIZATIONAL STRUCTURE
**Section:** 20.11
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

There is created a Department of Legal Affairs. The head of the Department of Legal Affairs is the Attorney General.

**History:** s. 11, ch. 69-106; ss. 1, 2, ch. 77-105.
