# Fla. Stat. § 15.051 — Official Sports Hall of Fame.

**Title:** IV — EXECUTIVE BRANCH
**Chapter:** 15 — SECRETARY OF STATE
**Section:** 15.051
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The Florida Sports Hall of Fame in Lake City, Columbia County, is designated as the Official Sports Hall of Fame for the state.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 88-415.
