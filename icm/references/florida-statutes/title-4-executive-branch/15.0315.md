# Fla. Stat. § 15.0315 — State fruit.

**Title:** IV — EXECUTIVE BRANCH
**Chapter:** 15 — SECRETARY OF STATE
**Section:** 15.0315
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The orange (Citrus sinensis
and hybrids thereof) is hereby designated the official fruit of Florida.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 2005-38.
