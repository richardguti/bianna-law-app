# Fla. Stat. § 15.046 — Official state transportation museum.

**Title:** IV — EXECUTIVE BRANCH
**Chapter:** 15 — SECRETARY OF STATE
**Section:** 15.046
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The Florida Museum of Transportation and History, located in Fernandina Beach, is hereby designated as the official state transportation museum.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 85-358.
