# Fla. Stat. § 16.62 — Recognition and awards.

**Title:** IV — EXECUTIVE BRANCH
**Chapter:** 16 — ATTORNEY GENERAL
**Section:** 16.62
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

In addition to expenditures separately authorized by law, the Department of Legal Affairs may expend no more than $20,000 annually to support costs associated with the Law Enforcement Officer of the Year Recognition and Awards Program and the Victims Services Recognition and Awards Program.

**History:** s. 2, ch. 2015-92.
