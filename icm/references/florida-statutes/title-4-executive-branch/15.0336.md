# Fla. Stat. § 15.0336 — State stone.F.S. 15.033615.033

**Title:** IV — EXECUTIVE BRANCH
**Chapter:** 15 — SECRETARY OF STATE
**Section:** 15.0336
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The horse conch, which is also known as Pleuroploca gigantea
, and sometimes as the giant band shell, a shell native to the marine waters surrounding the State of Florida, is hereby designated as the Florida state shell.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 69-107.
