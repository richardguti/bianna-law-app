# Fla. Stat. § 17.63 — Chief Financial Officer not to issue evidences of indebtedness.

**Title:** IV — EXECUTIVE BRANCH
**Chapter:** 17 — CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER
**Section:** 17.63
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

It is not lawful for the Chief Financial Officer of this state to issue any treasury certificates, or any other evidences of indebtedness, for any purpose whatever, and the Chief Financial Officer is prohibited from issuing the same.

**History:** s. 2, ch. 1737, 1870; RS 130; GS 13F.S. 17.62617.62

**Note:** Former s. 18.15.
