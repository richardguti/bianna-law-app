# Fla. Stat. § 15.0385 — Official state reptile.

**Title:** IV — EXECUTIVE BRANCH
**Chapter:** 15 — SECRETARY OF STATE
**Section:** 15.0385
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

The American alligator is hereby designated and declared as the official Florida state reptile.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 87-18.
