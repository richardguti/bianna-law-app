# Fla. Stat. § 15.047 — Official state soil.

**Title:** IV — EXECUTIVE BRANCH
**Chapter:** 15 — SECRETARY OF STATE
**Section:** 15.047
**Source:** 2025 Florida Statutes (FLLawDL2026)

---

Myakka fine sand (sandy, siliceous, hyperthermic Aeric Haplaquods) is hereby designated and declared as the official Florida state soil.

**History:** s. 1, ch. 89-16.
